# Relocating to Helsinki for a Keva, VER or Finnish public-pension desk

Helsinki is the visiting-address city of [Keva](https://www.keva.fi/en/this-is-keva/contact/) and of the [State Pension Fund of Finland](https://www.ver.fi/en-US/Contacts). Keva’s official visiting address is **Unioninkatu 43, Helsinki**; investments sit at the same street. VER’s official street and mail address is **Mikonkatu 15 A, 6th floor, 00100 Helsinki**. VER was established in **1990**. Both are public-pension books, **not** sovereign wealth funds. A move here is often a move onto one of those files. Right to work sits with official Finnish sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit elsewhere

- Keva serves the public sector in pension matters. Official contact and the investments desk are Unioninkatu 43. Do not invent a Keva AUM on this page. Do not mint `/keva-explained/` or `/helsinki-keva/`.
- VER is a **different** legal person. It prepares for future state pension expenditure. VER’s own contacts page sends municipal-pension questions to Keva. Do not add the two books. Do not mint `/ver-explained/` or `/ver-finland/`.
- [ATP](/atp-denmark-pension-fund/) is Hillerød, not here. Read the [Copenhagen](/careers/cities/copenhagen/) city page. Swedish AP books sit in [Stockholm](/careers/cities/stockholm/) — and AP2 is Gothenburg. Do not mint `/careers/cities/gothenburg/`.

Do not treat this city page as a minted Keva or VER explainer. There is no UAO institution slug for either. Do not create one.

## Papers

Start at the [Finnish Immigration Service](https://migri.fi/en/home). Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Why people underestimate the job

Two Helsinki postcodes are not one investment committee. Finnish and Swedish are the languages of legitimacy even when the dealing language is English. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail.
