The crazy workflow

Lagarde asked Europe’s savings to come home. Carney asked the world’s savings to come to Canada. The investment-committee question

Which bodies outside this room can change the composition of this portfolio without our consent — and what is the most recent date on which we measured that?

Terms that can still save you

These are not vocabulary. They are instruments. Ignore one, and a press release becomes a pacing input. Learn one, and a brochure becomes a term sheet.

How to sort 167 lines without a total

Published capex totals for the prospectus do not agree. The Logic puts it above US$425 billion. Other tallies run higher. Do not publish a single sum. Sort by what is missing.

1 · Operating assets

The missing counterparty is usually the seller’s valuation. You can underwrite this. You can also overpay for a familiar cash-flow you already own through another sleeve.

2 · Permitted, unfunded

The missing counterparty is usually a customer or offtake, not another investor. More equity does not create a buyer of electrons, LNG, or nickel.

3 · Early concepts

The missing counterparty is an engineering milestone. Treat the extra return for development risk as visible, or do not take the risk.
