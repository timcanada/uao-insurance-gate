# Relocating to Dublin for an ISIF or NTMA desk

Dublin is the home file of the [Ireland Strategic Investment Fund](/isif-ireland-strategic-investment-fund-explained/). ISIF is managed and controlled by the [National Treasury Management Agency](https://www.ntma.ie/contact-us). Official contact is **Treasury Dock, North Wall Quay, Dublin 1, D01 A9T8**. ISIF is **two books**, not a single pile, and it is **not** Ireland’s Future Ireland Funds. A move here is often a move onto a sovereign development mandate — or into another NTMA business that shares the building. Right to work sits with official Irish sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here

- [ISIF](/isif-ireland-strategic-investment-fund-explained/) — Ireland’s sovereign **development** fund, successor to the National Pensions Reserve Fund (assets transferred December 2014). Discretionary and Directed are separate books. Do not add them. Do not invent an AUM on this page. Do not put leftover **€24 billion** back. Do not mint `/dublin-isif/` or `/ntma-explained/`. Do not 301 this city page onto leftover `/ireland-strategic-investment-fund-isif/`.
- Future Ireland Funds sit beside ISIF at the NTMA. They are a different legal book. Do not add them to ISIF.
- [NBIM](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/) and [GIC](https://www.gic.com.sg/) are not Irish houses. If the desk is theirs, read those city pages. Do not invent a GIC AUM.

Ask which NTMA business you are joining. A shared dock is not a shared mandate.

## Papers

Start at [Irish Immigration](https://www.irishimmigration.ie/). Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Why people underestimate the job

A development book is not a savings SWF. Directed assets sit on ministerial instruction. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail.
