# Smaller Gulf funds: ADIA is not a $157 billion second-tier house

The Abu Dhabi Investment Authority is not a “smaller Gulf sovereign wealth fund.” The live page, dated 7 July 2026, still files ADIA next to Qatar’s State General Reserve Fund, Oman’s State General Reserve Fund, and Mumtalakat, then assigns **$157 billion**, **$145 billion**, **$19 billion**, and **$11 billion**. That is a category error plus four invented AUMs.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## Official numbers (do not stack them)

| House | Official print | What is not official |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Abu Dhabi Investment Authority | Established **1976**. No official AUM. 20-year **6.3%** / 30-year **7.1%** at 31 Dec 2024 | **$157 billion**; founded **2007**; “smaller Gulf fund” |
| Qatar Investment Authority | No official AUM | Qatar SGRF **$145 billion as of 2025** |
| Oman Investment Authority | **Around OMR 23 billion** / OIA English **approximately USD 60 billion** at end-2025 | Oman SGRF **$19 billion** as current AUM |
| Mumtalakat | 2025 profit **BHD 592.7 million** on revenue **BHD 2.5 billion**. Established **2006**. No AUM in that release | **$11 billion** AUM |
| Combined “smaller Gulf” pile | No official combined AUM | “Tens of billions annually” as a deploy line |

ADIA is Abu Dhabi’s long-horizon reserve investor. It does **not**, as a matter of practice, invest in the UAE. Filing it as a second-tier peer of Mumtalakat teaches the wrong map. The Abu Dhabi cluster is [ADIA / Mubadala / L’IMAD](/abu-dhabi-three-sovereign-funds/).

Qatar’s current named sovereign investor on this desk is [QIA](/qatar-investment-authority-qia/). QIA does not publish AUM. Do not replace that fact with a leftover “State General Reserve Fund $145 billion” line.

Oman’s current named sovereign investor is [OIA](/oman-investment-authority-explained/), assembled in **2020** from the State General Reserve Fund and the Oman Investment Fund. The live **$19 billion** SGRF figure is not OIA’s 2025 print.

Mumtalakat is Bahrain’s strategic holding SWF. It prints profit, not AUM. See [Mumtalakat](/mumtalakat-bahrain-explained/).
