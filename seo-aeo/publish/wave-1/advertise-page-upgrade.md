# Advertise — labelled presence in the morning room

Universal Asset Owners sells labelled presence in a reviewed room of long-horizon allocators — sovereign funds, pensions, endowments, insurers and family offices — and to the talent market around them. Desk packages start at $20,000 a month. Category and Platform packages run to $30,000–$55,000 a month. Payment never buys editorial coverage. Request the readership audit under NDA.
