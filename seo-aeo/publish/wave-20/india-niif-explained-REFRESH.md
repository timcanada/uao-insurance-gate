# NIIF (National Investment and Infrastructure Fund), explained

The National Investment and Infrastructure Fund Limited is India’s sovereign-anchored alternative asset manager. It was founded in **2015**. NIIF’s About page prints **USD 4.9 billion+** AUM across funds. It is **not** a **$9 billion** savings SWF. The Government of India’s **29 June 2026** release is an additional **INR 30,000 crore** allocation (total allocation **INR 60,000 crore**), not a second AUM. Managing Director and CEO: **Sanjiv Aggarwal**. This is not investment advice.

**Last updated:** 26 August 2026 · **Desk:** UAO Research · **Canonical URL:** `/india-niif-explained/`

The live page this overwrites invents **AUM exceeding $9 billion as of 2024**, a DIPAM-as-manager story, and a **NIIF Defence and Aerospace Fund**. Official is a **fund manager** anchored by the Government of India, with four named strategies. There is no live short twin in `twins.csv`. Do not mint `/niif/` or `/national-investment-and-infrastructure-fund/`.

Official journey names on the About page include the **Sustainable Infrastructure fund (Master Fund I)** and the **Private Markets Fund**. Those are enough. Do not invent a Defence/Aerospace vintage.

## What is not official — do not put it back

- **AUM exceeding $9 billion as of 2024.**
- A homemade **NIIF Defence and Aerospace Fund (2021)**.
- Treating the **INR 60,000 crore** allocation as current AUM.
- A **$1.2 trillion** India infrastructure-gap lede as if it were NIIF’s book.
- Calling NIIF a savings SWF that publishes a dollars-in-the-ground NAV like [NBIM](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/).
- Minting `/niif/` or `/what-is-niif/`.

## How a peer should read it

NIIF is a **catalytic fund manager**, not India’s GPFG. The Government anchors it; global LPs sit alongside. The About widget is **USD 4.9 billion+**. The June 2026 cabinet story is **more rupees of allocation**, which is dry powder permission, not a NAV. Anyone writing **$9 billion** is reading a consultancy. Compare the job to [ISIF](/isif-ireland-strategic-investment-fund-explained/) or [PIF](/public-investment-fund-pif/)’s developmental sleeve, not to [GIC](/gic-singapore-explained/).
