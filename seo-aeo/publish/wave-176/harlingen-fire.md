# Harlingen Firemen’s Relief and Retirement Fund, explained

The Harlingen Firemen’s Relief and Retirement Fund is the City of Harlingen firefighter pension, a pension trust fund of the City of Harlingen under the Texas Local Fire Fighters’ Retirement Act, not leftover TMRS, leftover Texas ERS, leftover Houston Fire, leftover Texas TESRS, leftover Corpus Christi Fire, leftover McAllen Fire, leftover Denison Fire, leftover Longview Fire, leftover Galveston Fire, and not leftover MassPRIM. Investments **$48,226,612** are not that card. Notes and RSI money-weighted return was **10.90 percent**. MD&A **8.53 percent** is the fiduciary-net-position increase, not the notes card. GASB **70.52 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
