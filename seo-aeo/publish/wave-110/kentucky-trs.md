# Teachers' Retirement System of Kentucky, explained

The Teachers’ Retirement System of the State of Kentucky is Kentucky’s teacher pension, not leftover Ohio STRS and not [Texas TRS](/texas-teacher-retirement-system-explained/). The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://trs.ky.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/TRS-2025-ACFR-Final.pdf) prints Combined fiduciary net position of **$32,458,776,860** at **30 June 2025**. The Retirement Annuity Trust is **$28,394,724,584**. The Health Insurance Trust is **$3,951,267,870**. The Life Insurance Trust is **$111,331,590**. Those four lines are not interchangeable. The Retirement Annuity Trust time-weighted return was **12.34 percent** gross and **12.01 percent** net of investment fees. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Ohio STRS overwrite or a second Texas TRS page.** The Texas TRS keeper is `/texas-teacher-retirement-system-explained/`. Do not mint `/kentucky-trs-explained/`, `/trs-kentucky/`, or `/kytrs/`. Do not mint `/strs-ohio-explained/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combining statement is the sourced split:

1. **Combined fiduciary net position** **$32,458,776,860** at **30 June 2025**. That total covers the Retirement Annuity Trust, the Health Insurance Trust, the Life Insurance Trust, and Other Funds (**$1,452,816**). The MD&A rounded Trust Totals line of **$32,457.3 million** excludes Other Funds. Do not treat leftover Ohio STRS **$80 billion+**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books on the same report.** Retirement Annuity Trust **$28,394,724,584**. Health Insurance Trust **$3,951,267,870**. Life Insurance Trust **$111,331,590**. Those three trusts are not interchangeable with the combined line. Do not add the Health Insurance Trust to the Retirement Annuity Trust a second time and call the sum a second AUM.
3. **Returns.** Retirement Annuity Trust time-weighted return **12.34 percent** gross of fees. The same trust’s net-of-fees time-weighted line is **12.01 percent**. The letter of transmittal **12.34 percent** year-over-year line is the gross investment-section print. The required supplementary money-weighted line for the Retirement Annuity Trust is also **12.01 percent**. Those prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Kentucky TRS into [Texas TRS](/texas-teacher-retirement-system-explained/) or [Illinois TRS](/illinois-trs/). Texas TRS is Texas. Illinois TRS is Illinois. This is **one Kentucky teacher file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Kentucky teacher AUM” has to name the book. The **$32.46 billion** line is Combined fiduciary net position. The **$28.39 billion** line is the Retirement Annuity Trust. The **$3.95 billion** line is the Health Insurance Trust. Those are not the same mandate.
