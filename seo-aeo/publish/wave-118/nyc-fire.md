# New York City Fire Pension Fund, explained

The New York City Fire Pension Fund is New York City’s firefighter pension, not leftover New York City Police and not the Comptroller’s five-system combined card. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/fdny/downloads/pdf/about/fire-pension-fund-cafr.pdf) prints combined net position restricted for benefits of **$22,876,158 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. The Qualified Pension Plan book was **$22,057,201 thousand**. Combined investments at fair value were **$23,112,066 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The Qualified Pension Plan money-weighted return was **10.10 percent**. Combined fiduciary net position was **79.39 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combining statements of fiduciary net position are the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Combined net position restricted for benefits** **$22,876,158 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$21,059,119 thousand**). Qualified Pension Plan **$22,057,201 thousand**. Firefighters’ Variable Supplements Fund **$452,842 thousand**. Fire Officers’ Variable Supplements Fund **$366,115 thousand**. Those four lines are not interchangeable. Combined investments at fair value **$23,112,066 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Combined total assets **$25,205,311 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Combined GASB fiduciary net position **$22,902,921 thousand** is not the combining-statement book. Do not treat leftover New York City Police, leftover New York City Employees, leftover New York State Common **$226.8 billion**, or the Comptroller’s FY2026 five-system **$326.3 billion** card as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Combined fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **79.39 percent**. Qualified Pension Plan **78.51 percent**. Firefighters’ Variable Supplements Fund **103.68 percent**. Fire Officers’ Variable Supplements Fund **123.92 percent**. Those four cards are not interchangeable.
3. **Returns.** Qualified Pension Plan portfolio gain **10.10 percent**. Qualified Pension Plan money-weighted return **10.10 percent**. Firefighters’ Variable Supplements Fund money-weighted **13.17 percent**. Fire Officers’ Variable Supplements Fund money-weighted **13.08 percent**. Those four official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten New York City Fire into [New York City Police](/nyc-police/), [New York City Employees](/nyc-employees/), or [New York City Retirement Systems](/new-york-city-crs/). The Comptroller’s FY2026 **$326.3 billion** card is a five-system combined print at **30 June 2026**. This page is the house **30 June 2025** ACFR. Those vintages are not interchangeable.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “NYC Fire AUM” has to name the book. The **$22,876,158 thousand** line is the combined QPP and Variable Supplements book. The **$22,057,201 thousand** line is the Qualified Pension Plan. The **$23,112,066 thousand** line is combined investments. Those are not the same mandate.
