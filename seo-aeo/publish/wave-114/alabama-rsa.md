# Retirement Systems of Alabama, explained

The Retirement Systems of Alabama is Alabama’s combined teacher, public-employee, and judicial administrator, not leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion** and not leftover Georgia TRS. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.rsa-al.gov/uploads/files/2025_RSA_ACFR.pdf) prints combined net position restricted for pension benefits of **$52,924,886 thousand** at **30 September 2025**. Teachers’ Retirement System was **$34,631,519 thousand**. Employees’ Retirement System was **$17,894,384 thousand**. Judicial Retirement Fund was **$398,983 thousand**. Those four lines are not interchangeable. Investments at fair value were **$52,470,891 thousand**. Time-weighted returns, gross of fees, were **10.16 percent** for TRS, **10.40 percent** for ERS, and **10.37 percent** for JRF. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 September 2025)

The comparative combining statements are in **thousands**:

1. **Combined net position restricted for pension benefits** **$52,924,886 thousand** at **30 September 2025** (beginning **$49,476,296 thousand**; increase **$3,448,590 thousand**). Investments at fair value **$52,470,891 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. The rounded “**$52.9 billion** held in trust” line is the same combined book. Do not treat leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion**, leftover Georgia TRS **$116,767,912 thousand**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio STRS **$80 billion+** / **$90 billion** / **$350 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books.** Teachers’ Retirement System **$34,631,519 thousand**. Employees’ Retirement System **$17,894,384 thousand**. Judicial Retirement Fund **$398,983 thousand**. Those three books plus the combined total are not interchangeable. GASB 67 fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability was **73.60 percent** for TRS and **69.40 percent** for JRF. Aggregate ERS net-pension-liability information is not presented because ERS is an agent multiple-employer plan.
3. **Cash flows and returns.** Employee contributions **$1,024,067 thousand**. Employer contributions **$1,986,609 thousand**. Investment income **$4,891,112 thousand**. Retirement allowance payments **$4,263,902 thousand**. Time-weighted returns on investments, **gross of fees**, calculated by State Street Bank: TRS **10.16 percent**, ERS **10.40 percent**, JRF **10.37 percent**. Those three return lines are not interchangeable. This filing’s MD&A did not lock a separate money-weighted line on the pages used here — do not invent one.

Do not flatten Alabama RSA into [Georgia TRS](/georgia-trs/) or [Texas TRS](/texas-teacher-retirement-system-explained/). This is **one Alabama combined administrator file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Alabama RSA AUM” has to name the book. The **$52,924,886 thousand** line is the combined fiduciary book. The **$34,631,519 thousand** line is TRS. The **$17,894,384 thousand** line is ERS. The **$398,983 thousand** line is JRF. Those are not the same mandate.
