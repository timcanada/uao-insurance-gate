# Newest SWFs: INA is 2020, not a 2023 $15 billion ticket

Indonesia’s named sovereign wealth fund on ina.go.id is the **Indonesia Investment Authority (INA)**. INA’s own About page says it was established in **December 2020**. Its financial-statement page puts assets under management at **IDR 146.2 trillion** by the end of **2025**. The live page, dated 8 July 2026, still says Indonesia’s SWF “commenced operations on 2 February 2023 with $15 billion,” invents a manager called **IWFM** and **Law No. 22 of 2022**, and invents a 2024 Vietnam **State Capital Investment Fund** with **$2 billion / $10 billion**. Those tickets are not the filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

Print **IDR 146.2 trillion**. Do **not** dollarize that AUM. INA’s own financial-statement page separately prints cumulative deployment figures with its own USD roundings (with co-investors **approximately IDR 74.5 trillion / ~USD 4.7 billion**; INA’s own deployment **IDR 33.3 trillion / ~USD 2.1 billion**). Those are **deployment** lines, not a second AUM.

INA and [Danantara](/danantara-indonesia-sovereign-fund/) are **different legal persons**. Do not mash them into one “Indonesia SWF 2023” row. Do not mint `/indonesia-investment-authority/`, `/indonesia-ina-explained/`, or `/vietnam-scic/` (all 404).
