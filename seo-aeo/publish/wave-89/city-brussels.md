# Relocating to Brussels for an APG European or EU-window desk

Brussels is an APG office city, not ABP’s home file. [APG](https://apg.nl/en/contact/)’s official European offices include **Robert Schumanplein 9, 1040 Brussels**, alongside Heerlen and Amsterdam. APG administers — it is **not** a second AUM. A move here is often a move onto a European public-affairs or investment window whose investment committee sits in Heerlen or Amsterdam. Right to work sits with official Belgian sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit elsewhere

- [ABP](/abp-netherlands-pension-fund-explained/) is the Dutch government-and-education pension fund. Official prints are **euros**. Do not invent an APG client stack as ABP’s book. Read the [Amsterdam](/careers/cities/amsterdam/) and [The Hague](/careers/cities/the-hague/) city pages before you treat Brussels as the centre.
- APG official European offices: Heerlen, Amsterdam, Brussels. Do not mint `/brussels-apg/`, `/pggm/`, or `/pfzw-netherlands/`.
- [PFZW](/pfzw-netherlands-pension-fund-explained/) is care and welfare. Do not mash it into ABP.

A Schuman-roundabout postcode is not the Dutch investment book.

## Papers

Start at [Immigration Office — Belgium](https://dofi.ibz.be/en). Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Why people underestimate the job

An EU-quarter window is not automatically the IC. Dutch and French share the building; the mandate still sits with the Dutch fund. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail. Satellites fail when the IC is elsewhere — the same problem our [London note](/careers/intelligence/why-london-satellites-fail/) already names.
