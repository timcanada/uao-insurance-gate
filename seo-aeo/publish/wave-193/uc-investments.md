# UC Investments, explained

UC Investments is the Office of the Chief Investment Officer of the Regents, not leftover UCRP (**$110,808,448 thousand**), leftover CalPERS **$637.1 billion**. The official [2024–25 annual report](https://www.ucop.edu/investment-office/2024-25-annual-report.pdf) prints Combined UC Endowment of **$31.1 billion** at **30 June 2025**. General Endowment Pool **$24.1 billion** plus Blue and Gold Endowment Pool **$7 billion** are the named sleeves. Combined endowment net return was **13 percent**. The **$198 billion** house line includes Retirement and is not the endowment card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The official endowment spread is the sourced split.

1. **Combined UC Endowment** **$31.1 billion** at **30 June 2025**. General Endowment Pool **$24.1 billion** (6,981+ funds). Blue and Gold Endowment Pool **$7 billion**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The two sleeves sum to the combined card. Do not treat leftover UCRP Defined Benefit Plans **$110,808,448 thousand**, leftover CalPERS PERF **$637.1 billion**, leftover Harvard **$56.9 billion**, leftover MIT **$27,366.2 million**, leftover Notre Dame Endowment Pool **$25,434,616 thousand** as this filing.
2. **Named books are not interchangeable.** House assets **$198 billion** at **30 June 2025** include Retirement. Combined Working Capital is not endowment. Campus-foundation / FFE totals printed elsewhere are not the **$31.1 billion** GEP-plus-Blue-and-Gold card.
3. **Returns and payout.** Combined UC Endowment net return **13 percent**. GEP gross **13 percent**, net **12.1 percent**, 30-year annualized net **8.9 percent**. Blue and Gold gross **15.8 percent**, net **15.8 percent**. GEP 2025 payout to campuses **$743 million**. Blue and Gold 2025 payout to campuses **$204 million**.
