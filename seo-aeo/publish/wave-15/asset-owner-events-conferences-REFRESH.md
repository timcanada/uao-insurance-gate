# Asset-owner events and conferences

Asset-owner conferences are where CIOs, trustees, and the firms that sell to them compare notes. **This page is not a 2026 calendar.** The live URL invented events and printed dead peer sizes — [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/) at **$415 billion**, [CPP Investments](/cpp-investments-explained/) at **$475 billion**, [OTPP](/ontario-teachers-pension-plan-explained/) at **$246 billion**. Official prints: CalPERS PERF **$637.1 billion at 30 June 2026**; CPP **C$793.3 billion at 31 March 2026**; OTPP **C$303.2 billion at 30 June 2026**. Check the host’s own site for dates. This is not investment advice.

## What this page is for

Three uses, none of them a UAO-invented diary:

1. **Peer intelligence.** Who is in the room, what they will actually say, and which official size you should have in your briefing note — the table above.
2. **Host calendars.** The [IFSWF events page](https://www.ifswf.org/events) is the official SWF-forum list. As fetched **26 August 2026** it does **not** print a 2026 annual-meeting date. Do not invent one. PRI, ILPA, and other membership bodies keep their own calendars. Quote the host URL or do not list the event.
3. **How to spend the trip.** Three committee questions, two peer meetings booked before you fly, one session that matches the book you actually run — [TPA](/total-portfolio-approach/), [LDI](/liability-driven-investing-explained/), [private markets](/private-credit-direct-lending/). Attendance is not diligence.

UAO will cover meetings that happened. That is journalism. A forward calendar of unsourced summits is a farm.
