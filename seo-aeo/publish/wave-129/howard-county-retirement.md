# Howard County Retirement Plan, explained

Howard County Retirement Plan is Howard County, Maryland’s general-employee pension, not leftover Maryland SRPS, leftover Montgomery County Employee Retirement Plans, leftover Howard County Police and Fire, leftover Baltimore City ERS, and not leftover District of Columbia Retirement Board. The official [FY2025 financial statements](https://cc.howardcountymd.gov/sites/default/files/2025-12/Howard%20County%20General%20Pension%20Plan%20Final%20FY25.pdf) print fiduciary net position held in trust for pension benefits of **$720,035,177** at **30 June 2025**. Statement total investments were **$718,777,382**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. RSI money-weighted return was **9.9 percent** net. The GASB card uses fiduciary net position **$721,338,718**, not the statement line, and prints **90.02 percent** of total pension liability. Actuarial funded **91.9 percent** at **1 July 2024** is not the GASB card. Do not add this book to leftover Howard County Police and Fire. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Maryland SRPS overwrite or a leftover Montgomery County overwrite.** Do not mint `/howard-erp/` or `/hcrp/`. The GASB fiduciary-net-position **$721,338,718** card is not the statement **$720,035,177** line.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Fiduciary net position held in trust for pension benefits** **$720,035,177** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$658,202,293**). The change is **$61,832,884**. Statement total investments **$718,777,382** is not fiduciary net position. The Plan prints a proportionate share of a combined pension investment pool. It does not own an undivided interest in specific pool assets. Deferred Retirement Option Plan balance **$1,021,120** is not fiduciary net position. Do not add this book to leftover Howard County Police and Fire. Do not treat leftover Maryland SRPS, leftover Montgomery County Employee Retirement Plans, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards use a different fiduciary-net-position print.** Required-supplementary 2025 fiduciary net position **$721,339 thousand** / notes **$721,338,718**. Total pension liability **$801,345,105**. Net pension liability **$80,006,387**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **90.02 percent**. That GASB fiduciary-net-position print is not the statement **$720,035,177** line. Actuarial funded **91.9 percent** at **1 July 2024** is not the GASB card and is not a 30 June 2025 print.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **9.9 percent** for fiscal 2025, net of pension-plan investment expense (2024 **9.8 percent**). The filing prints pool time-weighted returns by asset class. It does not print one official house time-weighted total on the notes return line.

Do not flatten Howard County Retirement Plan into [Maryland SRPS](/maryland-srps/), [Montgomery County Employee Retirement Plans](/montgomery-erp/), [Howard County Police and Fire Employees’ Retirement Plan](/howard-county-police-fire/), or [Baltimore City Employees’ Retirement System](/baltimore-city-ers/). Maryland SRPS is the statewide file. Montgomery County is a different county house. Howard County Police and Fire is a different Howard legal person. This is **one Howard general-employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Howard County pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$720,035,177** line is statement fiduciary net position. The **$718,777,382** line is statement investments. The **$721,338,718** line is the GASB fiduciary-net-position print. The **90.02 percent** card is the GASB ratio. The **91.9 percent** card is the July 2024 actuarial ratio. The **9.9 percent** card is the money-weighted return. Those are not the same mandate.
