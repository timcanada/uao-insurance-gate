# Vermont Municipal Employees’ Retirement System, explained

Vermont Municipal Employees’ Retirement System is Vermont’s municipal-employee pension, not leftover Vermont State Retirement System, leftover Vermont Teachers, and not leftover Michigan MERS. The official [30 June 2025 State of Vermont Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://auditor.vermont.gov/sites/auditor/files/Fiscal%20Year%202025%C2%A0Annual%20Comprehensive%20Financial%20Report.pdf) prints defined-benefit fiduciary net position of **$1,121,818,646** at **30 June 2025**. Municipal defined-contribution **$33,259,280** is not this book. Money-weighted return was **10.69 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Plan-reporting net pension liability was **$332,348 thousand** at the **7.00 percent** discount. Plan-reporting **77.15 percent** is not leftover State Employees **74.92 percent** or leftover Teachers **65.86 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Vermont state-employee overwrite.** Do not mint `/vmers/`, `/mers/`, `/vermont-vmers-explained/`, or `/vermont-municipal/`. `/mers/` is already the leftover Michigan MERS twin. Do not invent a combined Vermont AUM.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The defined-benefit combining statements and the GASB 67 notes are the sourced lines:

1. **Defined-benefit fiduciary net position** **$1,121,818,646** at **30 June 2025** (beginning **$1,008,504,818**). Net investment income **$106,126,032**. Municipal defined-contribution **$33,259,280** is not this book. Single Deposit Investment Account **$21,140,055** is not this book. Leftover Vermont State Retirement System **$2,944,071,052** and leftover Teachers **$3,136,315,717** are not this book. Do not treat leftover Michigan MERS Defined Benefit **$14,870,864 thousand**, leftover ERSRI **$14,686,484,613**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, leftover MainePERS, leftover New Hampshire, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Plan-reporting net pension liability **$332,348 thousand** at the **7.00 percent** discount (**6.00 percent** sensitivity **$522,651 thousand**; **8.00 percent** **$176,328 thousand**). Plan-reporting fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **77.15 percent**. Leftover State Employees **74.92 percent** / **$985,495 thousand** and leftover Teachers **65.86 percent** / **$1,625,927 thousand** are not this card. Employer-reporting **70.99 percent** / **61.65 percent** at **30 June 2024** are leftover State Employees and Teachers cards, not this municipal book.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **10.69 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **10.22 percent**). State Employees **10.65 percent** and Teachers **10.76 percent** are not this card.

Do not flatten Vermont Municipal Employees’ Retirement System into [Vermont State Retirement System](/vermont-vsers/), [Vermont State Teachers’ Retirement System](/vermont-vstrs/), or [Municipal Employees’ Retirement System of Michigan](/michigan-mers/). State Employees is the Vermont state-employee file. Teachers is the Vermont educator file. Michigan MERS is a different legal person. This is **one Vermont municipal-employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Vermont municipal pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$1,121,818,646** line is Municipal Employees defined-benefit fiduciary net position. The **$33,259,280** line is municipal defined contribution. The **$332,348 thousand** line is plan-reporting net pension liability. The **77.15 percent** card is plan-reporting GASB. The **10.69 percent** card is money-weighted. Those are not the same mandate.
