# City of Philadelphia Municipal Pension Fund, explained

The City of Philadelphia Municipal Pension Fund is Philadelphia’s city pension, not leftover Pennsylvania PSERS, leftover Pennsylvania SERS, and not leftover Philadelphia Gas Works Retirement Reserve. The official [30 June 2025 City of Philadelphia Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.phila.gov/media/20260420112330/annual-comp-financial-report-FY-2025.pdf) prints Municipal Pension fiduciary net position of **$9,633,166 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Combined pension-trust net position was **$10,335,945 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return was **13.12 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **72.3 percent** of total pension liability. MD&A about-**65.4 percent** is not that card. House financial statements on the pensions board page remain **2024**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover PSERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/philadelphia-pension/`, `/philadelphia-municipal-explained/`, or `/phila-mpf/`. Statement prints are in thousands. Combined **$10,335,945 thousand** mixes this book with leftover [Philadelphia Gas Works Retirement Reserve](/philadelphia-pgw/). The city ACFR is the lock this wake; the separate house Fund statements are still the **2024** file.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

Footnote IV.1, the Municipal Pension sensitivity table, and Schedule III are the sourced lines:

1. **Municipal Pension fiduciary net position** **$9,633,166 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$8,566,208 thousand**). Combined pension-trust net position **$10,335,945 thousand** is not this card. Gas Works Retirement Reserve **$702,779 thousand** is not this book. Notes investments measured at fair value **$8,896,378 thousand** are not fiduciary net position. Equity in Treasurer’s Account **$9,484,797 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Securities-lending collateral **$478,084 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover Pennsylvania PSERS **$83,728,862 thousand**, leftover Pennsylvania SERS, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$13,328,167 thousand**. Collective net pension liability **$3,695,001 thousand**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **72.3 percent** at the **7.25 percent** discount. MD&A about-**65.4 percent** is not the GASB card. The **6.25 percent** sensitivity print **65.6 percent** is not the current-rate card.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **13.12 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025**. Gas Works **12.5 percent** is not this card. This filing does not print a separate official one-year time-weighted house card.

Do not flatten the Municipal Pension Fund into [Pennsylvania PSERS](/pennsylvania-psers/), [Pennsylvania SERS](/pennsylvania-sers/), or [Philadelphia Gas Works Retirement Reserve](/philadelphia-pgw/). PSERS is the Pennsylvania school file. SERS is the Pennsylvania state-employee file. Gas Works is the utility reserve. Later Philadelphia teachers sit in leftover PSERS if applicable. This is **one Philadelphia city file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Philadelphia pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$9,633,166 thousand** line is Municipal Pension fiduciary net position. The **$10,335,945 thousand** line is combined pension trusts. The **$8,896,378 thousand** line is notes investments. The **72.3 percent** card is the June GASB ratio. The about-**65.4 percent** card is the MD&A funding line. The **13.12 percent** card is money-weighted. Those are not the same mandate.
