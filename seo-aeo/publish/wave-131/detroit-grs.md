# General Retirement System of the City of Detroit, explained

General Retirement System of the City of Detroit is Detroit’s civilian employee pension, not leftover Police and Fire Retirement System of the City of Detroit and not leftover Michigan Public School Employees’ Retirement System. The official [30 June 2025 financial statements](https://cms2.revize.com/revize/rscdmi/General%20Employee%20Retirement%20System%20of%20the%20City%20of%20Detroit%20-%20June%2030%202025%20Financial%20Statements.pdf) print combined fiduciary net position of **$1,802,667,863**. Pension-restricted net position was **$1,770,424,254**. Death-benefit / OPEB **$32,243,609** is not the pension book. Component II Legacy defined-benefit net position was **$1,384,656,637**. Component I Hybrid defined-benefit net position was **$371,229,527**. Income Stabilization **$14,538,090** is not those pension columns. Time-weighted return was **10.3 percent** net of fees. Money-weighted return was **9.02 percent**. Component II fiduciary net position was **63.46 percent** of total pension liability. Component I was **107.76 percent**. Those two GASB cards are not a combined funded ratio. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Detroit Police and Fire overwrite or a leftover MPSERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/dgrs/` or `/rscd/`. Do not add Component I and Component II into a second AUM. Do not invent a combined funded ratio. Do not treat the GLWA employer share as the System. Do not draft from the **2024–2025 Draft** summary annual report.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Pension-restricted fiduciary net position** **$1,770,424,254** at **30 June 2025**. Combined fiduciary net position **$1,802,667,863** includes death-benefit / OPEB **$32,243,609** and is not the pension book. Component II Legacy defined-benefit **$1,384,656,637**. Income Stabilization **$14,538,090**. Component I Hybrid defined-benefit **$371,229,527**. Those columns are not interchangeable. Statement investment categories (global equities, global fixed income, real estate, private equity, and diversifying strategies) totaled **$1,723,065,315**. Cash and cash equivalents **$59,758,464** are not that investment total. Do not treat leftover Detroit Police and Fire **$2,822,919,237**, leftover MPSERS **$94,365,768 thousand**, leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Milwaukee ERS as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Component II total pension liability **$2,181,824,064**. Component II net pension liability **$797,167,427**. Component II fiduciary net position / total pension liability **63.46 percent**. Component I total pension liability **$344,507,889**. Component I fiduciary net position / total pension liability **107.76 percent**. Do not invent a combined funded ratio. Income Stabilization is not the Legacy GASB book. The GLWA share of Component II net pension liability is not the System.
3. **Returns.** Time-weighted total-fund composite **10.3 percent** net of fees and expenses for the year ended **30 June 2025**. Notes money-weighted return **9.02 percent**. Those official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Detroit General into [Police and Fire Retirement System of the City of Detroit](/detroit-police-fire/), [Michigan Public School Employees’ Retirement System](/michigan-mpsers/), [Ohio PERS](/ohio-opers/), [Milwaukee ERS](/milwaukee-ers/), or [Chicago LABF](/chicago-labf/). Detroit Police and Fire is the **sworn** city file. MPSERS is the Michigan **school** file. This is **one Detroit civilian city file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Detroit general retirement AUM” has to name the book. The **$1,770,424,254** line is pension-restricted net position. The **$1,802,667,863** line is combined fiduciary net position including death benefits. The **$1,384,656,637** line is Legacy defined benefit. The **$371,229,527** line is Hybrid defined benefit. The **$32,243,609** line is death-benefit / OPEB. The **63.46 percent** card is the Legacy GASB ratio. The **107.76 percent** card is the Hybrid GASB ratio. The **10.3 percent** card is the time-weighted return. The **9.02 percent** card is the money-weighted return. Those are not the same mandate.
