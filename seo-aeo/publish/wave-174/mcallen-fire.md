# McAllen Firemen’s Relief and Retirement Fund, explained

The McAllen Firemen’s Relief and Retirement Fund is the City of McAllen firefighter pension, authorized under the Texas Local Fire Fighters’ Retirement Act, not leftover TMRS, leftover Texas ERS, leftover Houston Fire, leftover Texas TESRS, leftover Corpus Christi Fire, leftover Denison Fire, and not leftover MassPRIM. The official [financial statements as of 30 September 2025](https://www.mcallen.net/docs/default-source/finance/other-statements/firemen-s-relief-and-retirement-fund/mcallen-firemen_s-fund-report---final-report.pdf) print fiduciary net position held in trust for pension benefits of **$70,136,002** at **30 September 2025**. Investments **$69,389,804** are not that card. Notes money-weighted return was **7.14 percent**. MD&A **about 5.9 percent** is the estimated print, not the notes card. GASB **65.6 percent** is the notes funded card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
