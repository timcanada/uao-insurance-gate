# What is a code of ethics for an investment office?

A code of ethics for an investment office is the written standard the desk enforces: fiduciary conduct, compliance with federal securities laws, personal-trade reporting, prompt violation reports to the chief compliance officer, and a written acknowledgment. SEC Rule 204A-1 already sets that minimum for registered advisers. It is not a newsroom ethics page and not a stewardship code. This page is not legal advice.

## What the rule already lists as the minimum

[17 CFR § 275.204A-1(a)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/275.204A-1) requires an adviser registered or required to be registered under Advisers Act § 203 to establish, maintain, and enforce a written code that, at a minimum, includes five things. The Commission adopted the rule on **2 July 2004** (69 FR 41708, **9 July 2004**):

1. A **standard of business conduct** that reflects the adviser’s **fiduciary obligations** and those of its supervised persons.
2. Provisions requiring supervised persons to **comply with applicable federal securities laws**.
3. Provisions that require **access persons to report**, and the adviser **to review**, personal securities transactions and holdings.
4. Provisions requiring supervised persons to **report violations promptly** to the **chief compliance officer** (or to another designated person, provided the CCO also receives every report).
5. A **copy of the code and any amendments** to each supervised person, and a **written acknowledgment** of receipt.

That is the document. How holdings and quarterly trades are timed — 10 days / 45 days / 12 months / 30 days after quarter-end, plus IPO and limited-offering pre-clearance — sits on [how owners handle personal trading](/how-do-owners-handle-personal-trading/). Do not reprint that calendar here as if it were a second page.

Public pensions and sovereign wealth funds are often **not** SEC advisers. Their written standard may be a statute, a board policy, or an employment contract. Name that file. Do not paste 204A-1 onto CalPERS or a ministry and call it the governing rule.

## What this page is not

Universal Asset Owners’ [ethics](/ethics/) page (version **1.0**, **28 May 2026**) is the newsroom code: no individual securities of issuers staff cover; holdings disclosure to the Standards Editor; gifts above a notional **US$100** declined, returned, or donated. **Do not overwrite it.**

A **stewardship code** is how an owner votes and engages issuers. That job already has keepers on the stewardship-codes cluster. It is not this page.
