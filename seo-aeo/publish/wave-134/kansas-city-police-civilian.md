# Civilian Employees’ Retirement System of the Police Department of Kansas City, explained

The Civilian Employees’ Retirement System of the Police Department of Kansas City is Kansas City’s police-department civilian pension, not leftover Police Retirement System of Kansas City and not leftover MOSERS. The official [30 April 2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://kcpers.org/media/jozdmqkk/civilianannreport25_fnl.pdf) prints net position restricted for pensions of **$183,870,569** at **30 April 2025**. Statement investments were **$183,617,195**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Time-weighted return was **6.9 percent**. Money-weighted return was **6.95 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **67.70 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Kansas City Police overwrite or a leftover MOSERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/kcpers-civilian/` or `/kansas-city-civilian/`. Statement investments **$183,617,195** are not fiduciary net position. Securities-lending collateral **$18,733,691** is not fiduciary net position. Time-weighted **6.9 percent** is not money-weighted **6.95 percent**. Year-end is **30 April**, not 30 June. Do not invent a combined Kansas City Police plus civilian AUM.

3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **6.95 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 April 2025**. Investment-section time-weighted total-fund **6.9 percent** is not the money-weighted card.
