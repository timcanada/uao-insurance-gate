# Libyan Investment Authority, explained

The Libyan Investment Authority is Libya’s sovereign wealth fund. It was established on **28 August 2006** by **Resolution No. 208** and reorganized by **Law No. 13 of 2010**. United Nations Security Council **Resolution 1970 of 2011** froze its assets. LIA’s own site dates **“more than $80 billion”** to a **2019** valuation. The IFSWF profile’s undated **“surpassing $67 billion”** is not a current filing. The live UAO page’s **2016** founding is wrong. This is not investment advice.

**Last updated:** 26 August 2026 · **Desk:** UAO Research · **Canonical URL:** `/libyan-investment-authority-explained/`

The live page this overwrites (10 July 2026) invents a **2016** merger founding (Legislative Decree No. 41; SGRF + LFIF) and treats **approximately $67 billion** as a current AUM. Official founding is **2006**. Official reorganization is **2010**. There is no live `/libyan-investment-authority/` (404). Do not mint it.

Treat the **2019 $80 billion** and the undated IFSWF **$67 billion** the way we treat tracker ADIA dollars: they are **not** a current official AUM. LIA told you the $80 billion is a **2019** valuation. Do not update the year. Do not average $67 and $80.

## What is not official — do not put it back

- **Established in 2016.** Official is **28 August 2006**.
- **Legislative Decree No. 41** as the founding instrument. Not on lia.ly.
- **$67 billion** as if it were a 2026 filing. IFSWF’s line is undated.
- **$80 billion** as if it were current. LIA dated it **2019**.
- A homemade **largest-in-Africa** rank that depends on those dead dollars.
- Sleeve weights built from the sector list (agriculture, real estate, oil and gas…). Labels, not weights.
- Minting `/libyan-investment-authority/` or `/lia-explained/`.

## How a peer should read it

LIA is a 2006 oil-surplus SWF, reorganized in 2010, frozen in 2011, still working through that freeze. The disclosure that matters is the **statute and the freeze**, not a consultancy AUM. Anyone writing **2016** has the founding year wrong by a decade. Anyone writing **$67 billion** as current is laundering an undated IFSWF sentence.
