# Family office vs sovereign wealth fund: a legal form, not a homemade AUM band

A family office is a private investment and administrative vehicle for one family or a small group of families. A sovereign wealth fund is a state-owned investment vehicle. Those are legal and operating forms. They are **not** official AUM bands. The live page, dated 4 July 2026, invents family-office tickets of **$100 million–$5 billion+**, SWF tickets of **$50 billion–$1 trillion+**, prints GPFG as **approximately $1.4 trillion as of end-2023**, and assigns ADIA **roughly $150 billion** disclosed plus estimates **exceeding $800 billion**. Those dollars are not filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## Official rule

| Claim | Official status |
| --- | --- |
| Family-office AUM band ($100M–$5B+) | Not an official category. Do not print it as a filing. |
| SWF AUM band ($50B–$1T+) | Not an official category. Quote the house or say no AUM. |
| NBIM / GPFG | **22,683 billion kroner** at **30 June 2026**. Not $1.4T. |
| ADIA | **Does not publish AUM**. Not $150bn, $171bn, $800bn, or $1.18T. |
| Named family offices | Do not publish official FO AUM. See `/largest-family-offices-in-the-world/`. |
| Mubadala as a family office | False. Mubadala is a sovereign investor (AED 1.4T / US$385bn at YE2025). |

## What this page can say

- **Ownership.** A family office answers to a family. A SWF answers to a state — a finance ministry, a central bank, or a statutory board.
- **Liabilities.** Most SWFs do not owe dated pensions. A public pension does. Do not file GPIF or CalPERS as SWFs.
- **Disclosure.** Some SWFs file an official size. Some refuse (ADIA, KIA, QIA, SAFE, GIC). Family offices generally file nothing.
- **How to print scale.** Quote the filing in the currency the house prints, or say the house does not publish AUM. Do not invent a band so the two forms can be ranked.

## What is not official

- **$100 million–$5 billion+** as a family-office size rule.
- **$50 billion–$1 trillion+** as a SWF size rule.
- **GPFG $1.4 trillion at end-2023.** Official print is **kroner**.
- **ADIA $150 billion / $800 billion.** ADIA does not publish AUM. It also did **not** “consolidate multiple entities in 2023.” Official founding is **1976**.
- A homemade “SWFs hold trillions” pile used as if it were one filing.
