# Employees’ Retirement System of Tulsa County, explained

The Employees’ Retirement System of Tulsa County is Tulsa County’s standalone civilian pension, administered by the Tulsa County Clerk, not leftover Oklahoma OPERS (**$13,351,556,235**), leftover Oklahoma Police, leftover Oklahoma Firefighters, leftover Oklahoma County Employees, leftover Ohio OPERS, leftover MassPRIM. The official [financial statements as of 30 June 2025](https://countyclerk.tulsacounty.org/Audit/FY%202024-2025%20Audit.pdf) print net position restricted for pensions of **$362,539,381** at **30 June 2025**. Investments **$361,126,483** are not that card. Notes money-weighted return was **7.63 percent**. MD&A **7.6 percent** is the same card rounded. MD&A **5.14 percent** is the fiduciary-net-position increase, not the notes card. GASB **67.55 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
