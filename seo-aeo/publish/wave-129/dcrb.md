# District of Columbia Retirement Board, explained

District of Columbia Retirement Board is the District’s teacher and police-and-firefighter pension board, not leftover Maryland SRPS, leftover Virginia Retirement System, leftover Montgomery County Employee Retirement Plans, leftover Baltimore City ERS, and not leftover Pennsylvania SERS. The official [FY2025 ACFR](https://dcrb.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/dcrb/DCRB%20ACFR%20FINAL%203.31.2026.pdf) prints combined net position restricted for pensions of **$13,463,925 thousand** at **30 September 2025**. Combined investments at fair value were **$13,389,944 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Teachers’ Retirement Fund was **$3,753,243 thousand**. Police Officers and Firefighters’ Retirement Fund was **$9,710,682 thousand**. Those columns are the combining books, not a second AUM. Money-weighted return was **10.250 percent** net of fees. Time-weighted return was **10.3 percent** net of fees. Teachers’ fiduciary net position was **101.96 percent** of total pension liability. Police-and-firefighter fiduciary net position was **116.36 percent** of total pension liability. Do not invent a combined funded ratio. Federal pre-1997 liabilities are not this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Maryland SRPS overwrite or a leftover VRS overwrite.** Do not mint `/dc-retirement-board/` or `/dcrb-explained/`. The MD&A **$13.5 billion** / **$3.8 billion** / **$9.7 billion** cards are not the statement thousands lines.

## What is official (30 September 2025)

The combining statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Combined net position restricted for pensions** **$13,463,925 thousand** at **30 September 2025** (2024 restated **$12,263,377 thousand**). The change is **$1,200,548 thousand**, or **9.79 percent**. Combined investments at fair value **$13,389,944 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Teachers’ Retirement Fund **$3,753,243 thousand** is not the combined book. Police Officers and Firefighters’ Retirement Fund **$9,710,682 thousand** is not the combined book. Those two columns sum to the combined line. Do not add them into a second AUM. The MD&A **$13.5 billion**, **$3.8 billion**, and **$9.7 billion** prints are rounded, not the statement. Do not treat leftover Maryland SRPS, leftover VRS, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards are per plan.** Teachers’ total pension liability **$3,681,144 thousand**. Teachers’ net pension **asset** **$72,098 thousand**. Teachers’ fiduciary net position / total pension liability **101.96 percent**. Police-and-firefighter total pension liability **$8,345,555 thousand**. Police-and-firefighter net pension **asset** **$1,365,128 thousand**. Police-and-firefighter fiduciary net position / total pension liability **116.36 percent**. MD&A funding-status prints **102.0 percent** and **116.4 percent** are those GASB cards rounded. Do not invent a combined funded ratio. Federal pre-1997 benefits assumed under the Revitalization Act are not this District book.
3. **Returns.** Required-supplementary money-weighted return **10.250 percent** for fiscal 2025, net of fees. Investment-section time-weighted return **10.3 percent** net of fees. Those official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten DCRB into [Maryland SRPS](/maryland-srps/), [Virginia Retirement System](/virginia-retirement-system/), [Montgomery County Employee Retirement Plans](/montgomery-erp/), or [Baltimore City Employees’ Retirement System](/baltimore-city-ers/). Maryland SRPS is the statewide Maryland file. VRS is the statewide Virginia file. This is **one District of Columbia board file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “DCRB AUM” has to name the book. The **$13,463,925 thousand** line is combined net position restricted for pensions. The **$13,389,944 thousand** line is combined investments. The **$3,753,243 thousand** line is the teachers’ column. The **$9,710,682 thousand** line is the police-and-firefighter column. The **101.96 percent** and **116.36 percent** cards are the per-plan GASB ratios. The **10.250 percent** card is the money-weighted return. The **10.3 percent** card is the time-weighted return. Those are not the same mandate.
