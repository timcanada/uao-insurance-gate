# Michigan Public School Employees' Retirement System, explained

The Michigan Public School Employees’ Retirement System is Michigan’s school-employee pension and OPEB file, not the Treasurer’s combined SISTF book and not leftover Ohio STRS **$80 billion-plus**. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://audgen.michigan.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/9-30-25-MPSERS-ACFR.pdf) prints net position restricted for pension benefits and OPEB of **$94,365,768 thousand** (**$94.4 billion**) at **30 September 2025**. The pension book is **$78,656,194 thousand**. The OPEB book is **$15,709,574 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The pension-plan money-weighted net return was **9.81 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second PSERS overwrite or a second OPERS overwrite.** Those keepers are `/pennsylvania-psers/` and `/ohio-opers/`. Do not mint `/mpsers/`, `/mpsers-explained/`, or `/michigan-public-school-employees-retirement-system/`. Do not mint `/michigan-sistf/` as a second MPSERS page. SISTF is the Treasurer’s combined investment book, and this desk did not lock that combined filing this wake.

## What is official (30 September 2025)

The combining statement is the sourced split (dollar amounts in thousands):

1. **Combined net position** **$94,365,768 thousand** at **30 September 2025** (2024: **$87,059,780 thousand**). Total assets **$97,310,083 thousand**. Investments **$93,735,172 thousand**. Do not treat leftover Ohio STRS **$80 billion-plus** / **$90 billion** / **$350 billion**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Oregon **$95 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books on the same statement.** Pension fiduciary net position **$78,656,194 thousand**. OPEB fiduciary net position **$15,709,574 thousand**. Do not add those lines a second time. Pension total pension liability **$98,549,294 thousand**; net pension liability **$19,893,100 thousand**; fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **79.81 percent**. OPEB is in surplus on this statement: fiduciary net position **$15,709,574 thousand** against total OPEB liability **$9,455,654 thousand**.
3. **Returns.** Pension-plan money-weighted net return **9.81 percent**. MD&A pension-plan rate of return **9.8 percent**; OPEB **9.3 percent**. Five-year annualized return **10.7 percent** for the pension and OPEB books named in that MD&A line. Net investment income **$8,399,762 thousand**. The State of Michigan Investment Board is the investment fiduciary. That fact is not a combined SISTF AUM.

Do not flatten MPSERS into [PSERS](/pennsylvania-psers/), [OPERS](/ohio-opers/), [Police and Fire Retirement System of the City of Detroit](/detroit-police-fire/), or [General Retirement System of the City of Detroit](/detroit-grs/). PSERS is Pennsylvania. OPERS is Ohio. Detroit Police and Fire and Detroit General are **city** files. This is **one Michigan school-employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Michigan pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$94.4 billion** line is MPSERS pension and OPEB. The **$78.7 billion** line is the pension book. The Treasurer’s combined SISTF stack is a different filing this desk has not locked.
