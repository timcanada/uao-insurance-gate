# Anne Arundel Detention Officers’ and Deputy Sheriffs’ Retirement Plan, explained

The Anne Arundel Detention Officers’ and Deputy Sheriffs’ Retirement Plan is Anne Arundel County’s detention-and-deputy-sheriff pension, not leftover Employees, leftover Police Service, and not leftover Fire Service. The official [31 December 2025 Anne Arundel County Retirement and Pension System combining statements](https://www.aacounty.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/acfr-pension-trust-funds-2025.pdf) print net position held in trust for pension benefits of **$239,065,785** at **31 December 2025**. Investments **$240,375,130** are not that card. DROP lump-sum value **$9,126,623** is not a second AUM. GASB **79.9 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Employees overwrite.** Do not mint `/anne-arundel/`, `/ers/`, or `/anne-arundel-detention-explained/`. Combined System **$2,934,944,458** is not a fifth book. Assets are commingled for investment; “each Plan’s assets may be used only for the payment of benefits to the members of that Plan.”

Combined System GASB **77.9 percent** is not this card.
3. **Returns.** Notes System money-weighted return **15.4 percent** net of System expenses for the year ended **31 December 2025** (2024 **10.5 percent**).
