# New York City Police Pension Fund, explained

The New York City Police Pension Fund is New York City’s police pension, not leftover New York City Employees and not the Comptroller’s five-system combined card. The official [2025 combining financial statements](https://comptroller.nyc.gov/wp-content/uploads/documents/2025-New-York-City-Police-Pension-Fund-FS-1.pdf) print combined net position restricted for benefits of **$62,166,647 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. The Qualified Pension Plan book was **$56,276,968 thousand**. Combined investments at fair value were **$62,457,088 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The Qualified Pension Plan money-weighted return was **9.71 percent**. Combined fiduciary net position was **92.19 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

Police Superior Officers’ Variable Supplements Fund **$3,834,891 thousand**. Police Officers’ Variable Supplements Fund **$2,054,788 thousand**. Those four lines are not interchangeable. Combined investments at fair value **$62,457,088 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Combined total assets **$68,658,407 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover New York City Employees, leftover New York City Teachers, leftover New York State Common **$226.8 billion**, or the Comptroller’s FY2026 five-system **$326.3 billion** card as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards are not a second combining-statement book.** Combined fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **92.19 percent**. Qualified Pension Plan **91.57 percent**. Combined total pension liability **$67,688,441 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable.
3. **Returns.** Qualified Pension Plan money-weighted return **9.71 percent**. Police Superior Officers’ Variable Supplements Fund money-weighted **13.15 percent**. Police Officers’ Variable Supplements Fund money-weighted **13.01 percent**. Three-year overall rates of return **9.86 percent**, **12.01 percent**, and **11.96 percent**. Those six official prints are not interchangeable. The three-year cards are not one-year cards.
