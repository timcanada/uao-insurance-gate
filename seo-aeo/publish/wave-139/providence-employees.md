# Employees’ Retirement System of the City of Providence, explained

The Employees’ Retirement System of the City of Providence is Providence’s city pension, not leftover Employees’ Retirement System of Rhode Island. The official [30 June 2025 City of Providence audited financial statements](https://municipalfinance.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur546/files/2026-04/Providence-Revised-FY-2025-Audit.pdf) print fiduciary net position restricted for pensions of **$609,610 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Investments were **$526,098 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return was **11.93 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **32.42 percent** of total pension liability. Later Providence teachers sit in leftover [Employees’ Retirement System of Rhode Island](/rhode-island-ersri/). The city does not issue a separate System report. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover ERSRI overwrite.** Do not mint `/providence/`, `/providence-ers/`, or `/providence-employees-explained/`. Statement prints are in thousands. Due from primary government **$58,111 thousand** is not fiduciary net position.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

Exhibit VIII and Note 12 are the sourced lines (in thousands):

1. **Fiduciary net position restricted for pensions** **$609,610 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (beginning **$525,882 thousand**). Investments at fair value **$526,098 thousand** are not fiduciary net position. Due from primary government **$58,111 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Loans receivable **$26,241 thousand** are not fiduciary net position. Cash and cash equivalents **$2,165 thousand** are not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover ERSRI combined **$14,686,484,613**, leftover MainePERS **$22,466,747,053**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$1,880,330 thousand**. Net pension liability **$1,270,720 thousand**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **32.42 percent**. Transmittal **32.42 percent** is that same GASB card, not a second method.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **11.93 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025**. This filing does not print a separate official one-year time-weighted house card. Leftover ERSRI pooled time-weighted **8.4 percent** is not this card.

Do not flatten the Providence Employees’ Retirement System into [Employees’ Retirement System of Rhode Island](/rhode-island-ersri/), [MainePERS](/maine-pers/), [New Hampshire Retirement System](/new-hampshire-retirement-system/), or [Massachusetts PRIM](/massachusetts-prim/). ERSRI is the statewide file. Later Providence teachers sit there if applicable. This is **one Providence city file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Providence pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$609,610 thousand** line is fiduciary net position. The **$526,098 thousand** line is investments. The **32.42 percent** card is the June GASB ratio. The **11.93 percent** card is money-weighted. Those are not the same mandate.
