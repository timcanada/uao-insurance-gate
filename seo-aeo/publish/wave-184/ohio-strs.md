# State Teachers Retirement System of Ohio, explained

The State Teachers Retirement System of Ohio is Ohio’s statewide teacher pension, defined-contribution, and post-employment health-care book, not leftover Ohio SERS (**$21,081,732,005**), leftover Ohio OPERS, leftover Ohio Police and Fire, leftover Ohio Highway Patrol, leftover Pennsylvania PSERS, leftover Ohio STRS **$80 billion+**, and not leftover MassPRIM. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://ohioauditor.gov/auditsearch/Reports/2026/State_Teachers_Retirement_System_Of_Ohio_2025_Franklin_FINAL.pdf) hosted by the Ohio Auditor of State prints defined-benefit fiduciary net position of **$92,707,886 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. The statements are in thousands. Combining total **$101,817,166 thousand** includes defined contribution **$3,621,209 thousand** and health care **$5,488,071 thousand** and is not the defined-benefit card. Notes money-weighted return was **10.41 percent**. Notes GASB **84.1 percent** is of the pension book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
