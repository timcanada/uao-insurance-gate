# Ohio Highway Patrol Retirement System, explained

The Ohio Highway Patrol Retirement System is Ohio’s statewide highway-patrol pension and post-employment health-care book, a component unit of the State of Ohio, not leftover Ohio OPERS (**$158,377,460,244**), leftover Ohio SERS, leftover Ohio Police and Fire, leftover STRS Ohio, leftover Pennsylvania SERS, leftover Cincinnati Retirement, and not leftover MassPRIM. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.ohprs.org/ohprs/downloads/Annual%20Reports/2025.pdf) prints pension net position restricted for pension benefits of **$1,095,716,911** at **31 December 2025**. Combining total **$1,230,649,666** and health-care **$134,932,755** are not that card. Notes and RSI money-weighted return was **11.95 percent**. Notes GASB **73.64 percent** is of this pension book.
