## What the page can say

- **The arithmetic.** A higher rate makes the same promise look smaller today. A lower rate makes it look larger. The cash to the member does not move when the rate moves.
- **Which rulebook.** ASC 715 uses high-quality corporate yields. Many US public plans use an assumed return. Name the rulebook before the ratio.
- **What a discount rate is not.** It is not a licence to reprint leftover Milliman **109%** as a UAO official.
