# Employees’ Retirement System of Rhode Island, explained

The Employees’ Retirement System of Rhode Island is Rhode Island’s statewide retirement system, not leftover City of Providence Employees’ Retirement System, leftover MainePERS, leftover New Hampshire, and not leftover Massachusetts PRIM. The official [30 June 2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.ersri.org/sites/default/files/2025-12/ACFR%20FY25_final.pdf) prints combined fiduciary net position restricted for pensions of **$14,686,484,613** at **30 June 2025**. That total includes the defined-contribution plan **$2,469,159,607**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The Employees’ Retirement System plan was **$8,834,782,139**. Pooled Investment Trust time-weighted return was **8.4 percent** net of fees. Employees’ Retirement System money-weighted return was **4.53 percent** net. Those two return cards are not interchangeable. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Providence overwrite or a leftover PRIM overwrite.** Do not mint `/ersri/`, `/rhode-island-retirement/`, or `/ersri-explained/`. Do not invent a combined money-weighted return or a combined GASB funded ratio. City of Providence sits in leftover [Employees’ Retirement System of the City of Providence](/providence-employees/).

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced split:

1. **Combined fiduciary net position restricted for pensions** **$14,686,484,613** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$13,564,905,703**). Defined-contribution plan **$2,469,159,607** is not the defined-benefit book. Employees’ Retirement System **$8,834,782,139**, Teachers’ Survivors Benefit **$484,433,092**, Municipal Employees’ Retirement System **$2,490,413,248**, State Police Retirement Benefits Trust **$245,291,810**, Judicial Retirement Benefits Trust **$123,028,635**, Rhode Island Judicial Retirement Fund Trust **$1,896,290**, and State Police Retirement Fund Trust **$37,479,792** are columns inside this System, not a second AUM. Pooled-trust investments **$12,141,168,976** and total investments **$14,610,328,583** are not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover Providence **$609,610 thousand**, leftover MainePERS **$22,466,747,053**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** This filing does not print one System-wide GASB funded ratio. Employees’ Retirement System state employees **66.5 percent** and teachers **70.1 percent** are not interchangeable. Teachers’ Survivors Benefit **200.6 percent** and Judicial Retirement Benefits Trust **108.7 percent** are overfunded cards, not the System. State Police Retirement Benefits Trust **93.1 percent**, State Police Retirement Fund Trust **26.2 percent**, and Rhode Island Judicial Retirement Fund Trust **11.5 percent** are not the System. Municipal Employees’ Retirement System general **90.7 percent** and municipal police and fire **84.9 percent** are not the System. Actuarial Municipal Employees’ Retirement System combined **85.2 percent** and legacy-unit **33.1 percent** are valuation cards, not GASB System cards. Do not invent a combined funded ratio.
3. **Returns.** Pooled Investment Trust time-weighted return **8.4 percent** net of fees for the year ended **30 June 2025**. That custodial-close print does not include later alternative-investment fair-value adjustments sitting in the financial statements. Money-weighted returns are plan-specific: Employees’ Retirement System **4.53 percent**, Teachers’ Survivors Benefit **3.16 percent**, Municipal Employees’ Retirement System **4.25 percent**, State Police Retirement Benefits Trust **8.17 percent**, Judicial Retirement Benefits Trust **5.89 percent**, Rhode Island Judicial Retirement Fund Trust **4.83 percent**, and State Police Retirement Fund Trust **8.27 percent**. Those official one-year prints are not interchangeable. Do not invent a combined money-weighted return.

Do not flatten the Employees’ Retirement System of Rhode Island into [Employees’ Retirement System of the City of Providence](/providence-employees/), [MainePERS](/maine-pers/), [New Hampshire Retirement System](/new-hampshire-retirement-system/), or [Massachusetts PRIM](/massachusetts-prim/). Providence is a city file. MainePERS and New Hampshire are different legal persons. PRIM is one Massachusetts PRIT file. This is **one Rhode Island System file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Rhode Island pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$14,686,484,613** line is combined fiduciary net position. The **$2,469,159,607** line is the defined-contribution plan. The **$8,834,782,139** line is the Employees’ Retirement System plan. The **8.4 percent** card is the pooled time-weighted return. The **4.53 percent** card is the Employees’ Retirement System money-weighted return. Those are not the same mandate.
