# Omaha School Employees Retirement Plan, explained

The Omaha School Employees Retirement Plan is Omaha Public Schools’ Class V pension, not leftover Nebraska School Employees and not leftover Iowa IPERS. The official [31 December 2025 Auditor of Public Accounts report](https://auditors.nebraska.gov/APA_Reports/2026/SA854-07222026-January_1_2025_through_December_31_2025_Audit_Report.pdf) prints fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits of **$1,970,571,740** at **31 December 2025**. Investments at fair value were **$1,969,172,309**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Calendar-year money-weighted return was **15.13 percent** net of investment expense. GASB fiduciary net position as of **31 August 2025** was **$1,908,174,094**, or **63.47 percent** of total pension liability. Those two year-ends are not interchangeable. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Nebraska School overwrite.** Do not mint `/osers/`, `/omaha-school/`, or `/omaha-osers-explained/`. Administration moved to the Nebraska Public Employees Retirement Board on **1 September 2024**. That transfer does not flatten this book onto leftover [Nebraska School Employees](/nebraska-school/). Calendar investments **$1,969,172,309** are not fiduciary net position. August GASB fiduciary net position **$1,908,174,094** is not the December statement card.

## What is official (31 December 2025 and 31 August 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced December line. The required-supplementary schedule is the sourced August GASB line:

1. **Calendar fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits** **$1,970,571,740** at **31 December 2025** (beginning **$1,716,538,326**). The change is **$254,033,414**. Total investments at fair value **$1,969,172,309** is not fiduciary net position. Securities-lending collateral **$124,673,705** is not fiduciary net position. Capital assets **$1,422,647** are not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover Nebraska School **$18,532,685,257**, leftover Iowa IPERS **$46,740,552,174**, leftover Missouri PSRS and PEERS **$63,124,090,036**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards as of 31 August 2025.** Total pension liability **$3,006,418,480**. Plan fiduciary net position **$1,908,174,094**. Net pension liability **$1,098,244,386**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **63.47 percent** (2024 **61.90 percent**). The required-supplementary schedule prints those cards in thousands. Statute still measures the GASB year-end at **31 August** even after LB 147 moved the audited statements to calendar year. Actuarial funded **59.69 percent** as of **1 January 2025** is not the GASB card.
3. **Returns.** Notes calendar money-weighted return **15.13 percent** net of investment expense for the year ended **31 December 2025**. Required-supplementary money-weighted return **11.44 percent** is the **31 August 2025** fiscal-year card. Those official prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten the Omaha School Employees Retirement Plan into [Nebraska School Employees Retirement Plan](/nebraska-school/), [Iowa IPERS](/iowa-ipers/), [Missouri PSRS and PEERS](/missouri-psrs-peers/), or [Minnesota TRA](/minnesota-tra/). Nebraska School is the statewide Class-other-than-V file. IPERS is Iowa. PSRS and PEERS are Missouri school. This is **one Omaha Class V school file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Omaha school AUM” has to name the book. The **$1,970,571,740** line is December fiduciary net position. The **$1,908,174,094** line is August GASB fiduciary net position. The **63.47 percent** card is the August GASB ratio. The **59.69 percent** card is the January actuarial ratio. The **15.13 percent** card is calendar money-weighted. The **11.44 percent** card is August money-weighted. Those are not the same mandate.
