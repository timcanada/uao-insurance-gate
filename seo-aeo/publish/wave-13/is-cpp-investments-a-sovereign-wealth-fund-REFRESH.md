# Is CPP Investments a sovereign wealth fund?

No. CPP Investments is the professional manager of the **Canada Pension Plan Fund**. It is **independent of the Canada Pension Plan** and **at arm’s length from governments**. It is **not** a sovereign wealth fund. The Fund ended fiscal 2026 with net assets of **C$793.3 billion at 31 March 2026**, a **7.8%** net return, and a **10-year** annualized net return of **8.8%**. This is not investment advice.

## The legal distinction

CPP Investments’ own about line, on the 20 May 2026 results: a professional investment management organization that manages the Canada Pension Plan Fund in the best interests of **more than 22 million** contributors and beneficiaries. Governed and managed **independently of the CPP** and **at arm’s length from governments**.

The [IFSWF Santiago definition](https://www.ifswf.org/what-is-a-sovereign-wealth-fund) **excludes public pension funds owned by the underlying members**. That is this house.

Two accounts, one manager:

| Account | Net assets | FY2026 net return | Longer print |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Base CPP | **C$712.9 billion** | **8.0%** | 10-year **8.8%** |
| Additional CPP (since 2019) | **C$80.4 billion** | **5.4%** | Since inception **6.0%** |
| **Fund (both)** | **C$793.3 billion** | **7.8%** | 10-year **8.8%** |

The additional account has a different legislative funding profile, contribution rate, and risk target. Do not treat 7.8% as if it were one policy portfolio.

The Office of the Chief Actuary’s December 2025 triennial review (as cited in the same release) reaffirmed long-term sustainability of both accounts at the legislated contribution rates, as at **31 December 2024**. That is an actuarial opinion, not an AUM.

## How a counterparty should use the label

- Pitching the *Canada Pension Plan’s investment manager* → **CPP Investments**. Not a Gulf SWF process.
- Putting CPPIB on a SWF slide → label it as a **tracker classification**. The legal sentence is arm’s-length pension manager.
- Asking “is it sovereign?” → the plan is public and mandatory. The **liabilities belong to contributors and beneficiaries**.
