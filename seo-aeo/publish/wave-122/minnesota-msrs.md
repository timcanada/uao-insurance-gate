# Minnesota State Retirement System, explained

The Minnesota State Retirement System is Minnesota’s statewide state-employee pension, not leftover Minnesota SBI, not leftover Minnesota TRA, and not leftover Minnesota PERA. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.mnretire.gov/sites/default/files/2025-12/FINAL%202025%20digital%20compressed.pdf) prints combined net position restricted for pensions of **$37,048,298 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Total investments were **$36,342,124 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The defined-benefit time-weighted return was **10.90 percent**. State Employees fiduciary net position was **98.42 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Minnesota SBI overwrite, a leftover Minnesota TRA overwrite, or a leftover Minnesota PERA overwrite.** Do not mint `/msrs/`, `/minnesota-msrs-explained/`, or `/minnesota-state-retirement/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Combined net position restricted for pensions** **$37,048,298 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$34,090,546 thousand**), up **8.68 percent**. Total investments **$36,342,124 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$37,943,365 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. The Minnesota State Board of Investment Combined Funds MSRS State Employees participation **$19,487.5 million** is a Combined Funds slice, not State Employees fiduciary net position. Those four prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover Minnesota SBI Total Funds Under Management **$155,859.3 million**, leftover Minnesota TRA, leftover Minnesota PERA, leftover Illinois SERS, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books on the same statement.** State Employees **$19,538,790 thousand**. State Patrol **$1,155,556 thousand**. Correctional **$1,985,814 thousand**. Judges **$319,329 thousand**. Legislators **$0**. Deferred Compensation **$11,072,714 thousand**. Health Care Savings **$2,351,473 thousand**. Unclassified **$441,147 thousand**. Hennepin Supplemental **$183,475 thousand**. The defined-benefit book is about **$22,999,489 thousand** / house “almost $23.0 billion.” Those lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat State Employees as the combined pension-trust book.
3. **Returns and funded cards.** Defined-benefit time-weighted return **10.90 percent** / house **10.9 percent**. Money-weighted returns: State Employees **10.94 percent**, State Patrol **10.94 percent**, Correctional **10.96 percent**, Judges **10.93 percent**. State Employees investments **$18,959,523 thousand** is not State Employees fiduciary net position. State Employees total pension liability **$19,851,695 thousand**. State Employees fiduciary net position / total pension liability **98.42 percent**. State Employees actuarial-value funded **95.01 percent**. Those official one-year prints and funded cards are not interchangeable.

The Minnesota State Board of Investment manages this book inside the Combined Funds with PERA and TRA. That Combined Funds line is not a second Minnesota State Retirement System AUM.

Do not flatten the Minnesota State Retirement System into [Minnesota SBI](/minnesota-sbi/), [Minnesota TRA](/minnesota-tra/), [Minnesota PERA](/minnesota-pera/), or [Illinois SERS](/illinois-sers/). Minnesota SBI is the statutory investor. TRA is the teacher plan. PERA is the public-employee plan. This is **one Minnesota state-employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Minnesota MSRS AUM” has to name the book. The **$37,048,298 thousand** line is combined fiduciary net position. The **$19,538,790 thousand** line is State Employees. The **$36,342,124 thousand** line is investments. The **$19,487.5 million** line is the Combined Funds State Employees participation. Those are not the same mandate.
