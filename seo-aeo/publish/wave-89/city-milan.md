# Relocating to Milan for a CDP or Italian owner desk

Milan is a CDP office city, not CDP’s headquarters. [Cassa depositi e prestiti](https://www.cdp.it/sitointernet/en/contatti_sedi.page) lists its Rome headquarters at **Piazza Giuseppe Verdi, 10 — 00198 Roma**. The Milan office is **via San Marco 21 A — 20121 Milano**. A move here is often a move onto an Italian promotional or regional file — or into a window whose investment committee sits in Rome. Right to work sits with official Italian sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit in Rome

- CDP is Italy’s national promotional institution. Official corporate headquarters is Rome. Do not invent an AUM on this page. Do not call CDP a sovereign wealth fund unless CDP’s own stationery does. Do not mint `/cdp-explained/`, `/milan-cdp/`, or `/careers/cities/rome/`.
- A Milan markets floor is not automatically the legal seat. Ask whether the investment committee sits in Rome. If it does, you are not at the centre.
- [FRR](https://www.fondsdereserve.fr/en/legal-notice/) sits in [Paris](/careers/cities/paris/). Do not invent an FRR AUM.

Do not treat this city page as a minted CDP explainer. There is no UAO CDP institution slug. Do not create one.

## Why people underestimate the job

A commercial capital is not automatically the statutory book. Italian is the language of legitimacy even when the dealing language is English. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail.
