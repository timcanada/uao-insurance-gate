# AustralianSuper, explained

AustralianSuper is Australia's largest superannuation fund. It manages members' retirement savings — more than A$410 billion on behalf of more than 3.6 million members from more than 485,000 businesses, as at 31 December 2025, per the fund's own newsroom. The Chief Investment Officer is Shaun Manuell, appointed 1 July 2026, succeeding Mark Delaney. This is a pension / super owner, not a sovereign wealth fund. Not investment advice.

**Desk:** UAO Research · **Owner type:** Public / industry superannuation · **HQ:** Australia (global investment seats include New York, London, Beijing)

## How a peer should read it

Australian super is a **mandatory savings system** with trustee governance, not a ministry SWF. AustralianSuper is the scale name in that system. The in-housing story (equities book grown internally from about A$1 billion to A$100 billion under Manuell’s previous seat, as the fund tells it) is why global managers and search firms already know the name. Compare it to [CPP](/cpp-investments-explained/) or a large US public plan only after you have respected the super rules.
