# South Carolina Retirement Systems, explained

The South Carolina Retirement Systems are South Carolina’s five defined-benefit pension trusts, not leftover Florida SBA and not a second CalPERS page. The official [fiscal 2025 financial statements](https://osa.sc.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/FY2025-SC-Retirement-Systems-Financial-Statements.pdf) print Combined fiduciary net position restricted for pensions of **$50,819,016 thousand** (**$50.8 billion**) at **30 June 2025**. The South Carolina Retirement System book is **$42,347,352 thousand**. The Police Officers Retirement System book is **$8,092,430 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. Time-weighted return was **11.34 percent** net of fees. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Florida SBA overwrite or a second CalPERS page.** The CalPERS keeper is `/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/`. Do not mint `/south-carolina-rsic/`, `/scrs/`, or `/peba-south-carolina/`. Do not mint `/florida-sba/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combining statement is the sourced split (dollar amounts in thousands):

1. **Combined fiduciary net position** **$50,819,016 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$45,439,660 thousand**). That total covers SCRS, PORS, GARS, JSRS, and SCNG. Do not treat leftover Florida SBA **$300 billion+**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books on the same report.** SCRS **$42,347,352 thousand**. PORS **$8,092,430 thousand**. GARS **$56,238 thousand**. JSRS **$269,739 thousand**. SCNG **$53,257 thousand**. Those five lines are not interchangeable with the combined line. Do not add SCRS to PORS a second time and call the sum a second AUM.
3. **Returns versus the RSIC market-value line.** Time-weighted return **11.34 percent** net of fees on the pooled investments of the consolidated pension trust funds. The Retirement System Investment Commission [30 June 2025 investment report](https://www.rsic.sc.gov/_documents/investment-reports/2025.06.30-investment-report.pdf) prints Total Plan market value **$50,339 million**. That market-value line is not the Systems’ fiduciary net position.

The Public Employee Benefit Authority administers the Systems. The Retirement System Investment Commission invests the assets. Those two legal persons are not interchangeable with the five trusts.

Do not flatten the South Carolina Retirement Systems into [Florida SBA](/florida-state-board-of-administration-explained/) or [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/). Florida SBA is Florida. CalPERS is California. This is **one South Carolina defined-benefit file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “South Carolina pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$50.8 billion** line is Combined fiduciary net position. The **$42.3 billion** line is SCRS. The **$50.3 billion** line is the RSIC Total Plan market value. Those are not the same mandate.
