# Austin Police Retirement System, explained

The Austin Police Retirement System is Austin’s sworn police pension, not leftover City of Austin Employees’ Retirement System. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.ausprs.org/resources/a9eabe2a-8375-435b-83db-f9e0a9ac22fe/aprs-2025-annual-report-final.pdf) prints fiduciary net position restricted for pensions of **$1,211,262,982** at **31 December 2025**. Investments at fair value were **$1,206,985,912**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted return was **13.21 percent** gross and **13.04 percent** net. The money-weighted return was **13.3 percent**. Fiduciary net position was **61.81 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

Do not treat GASB fiduciary net position **$1,211,288,721** as the statement **$1,211,262,982** line.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Fiduciary net position restricted for pensions** **$1,211,262,982** at **31 December 2025** (2024 **$1,082,777,584**), up **$128,485,399** / **11.9 percent**. Investments at fair value **$1,206,985,912** is not fiduciary net position. Those two prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover COAERS combined **$4,085,942,671**, leftover Texas ERS, leftover TMRS, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$1,959,648,278**. GASB fiduciary net position **$1,211,288,721** is not the statement **$1,211,262,982** line. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **61.81 percent**. Those prints are not interchangeable.
3. **Returns and actuarial card.** Time-weighted return **13.21 percent** gross and **13.04 percent** net (2024 **8.83 percent** / **8.59 percent**). Money-weighted return **13.3 percent** (2024 **8.7 percent**). Actuarial funded **59.7 percent** is not the GASB **61.81 percent** card.

Do not flatten Austin Police into [City of Austin Employees’ Retirement System](/coaers/), [Texas ERS](/texas-ers/), [Texas Municipal Retirement System](/texas-tmrs/), or [Houston Police](/houston-hpops/). COAERS is the civilian Austin file. This is **one Austin sworn police file**.
