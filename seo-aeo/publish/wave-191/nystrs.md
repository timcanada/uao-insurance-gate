# New York State Teachers’ Retirement System, explained

The New York State Teachers’ Retirement System is New York’s statewide teacher pension, not leftover New York City Teachers (**$134,906,818 thousand**), leftover NYSCRF (**$226.8 billion**), leftover Texas TRS. The official [30 June 2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.nystrs.org/getmedia/aa31d8ed-8708-4985-be81-8e124f48dad2/2025-ACFR.pdf) prints net position restricted for pensions of **$154,193,125 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Dollars in thousands. Investments **$151,536,570 thousand** are not that card. Notes money-weighted return was **10.38 percent**. GASB **104.0 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

Plante Moran, PC issued an unmodified opinion dated **30 October 2025** on the financial statements (other ACFR sections **25 November 2025**).

2. **GASB cards.** Notes total pension liability **$148,272,069 thousand**. School districts’ net pension **asset** **$(5,921,056) thousand**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of the total pension liability **104.0 percent**. RSI **104.0 percent** is the same card.
