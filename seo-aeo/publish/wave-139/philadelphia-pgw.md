# Philadelphia Gas Works Retirement Reserve Fund, explained

The Philadelphia Gas Works Retirement Reserve Fund is the Gas Works pension reserve, not leftover City of Philadelphia Municipal Pension Fund and not leftover Pennsylvania PSERS. The official [30 June 2025 City of Philadelphia Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.phila.gov/media/20260420112330/annual-comp-financial-report-FY-2025.pdf) prints Gas Works Retirement Reserve fiduciary net position of **$702,779 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Combined pension-trust net position was **$10,335,945 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return was **12.5 percent** net of investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **81.42 percent** of total pension liability. Municipal **13.12 percent** and **72.3 percent** are not this card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Municipal Pension overwrite.** Do not mint `/pgw/`, `/pgw-retirement/`, or `/philadelphia-gas-works/`. Combining-statement prints are in thousands. Combined **$10,335,945 thousand** mixes this book with leftover [City of Philadelphia Municipal Pension Fund](/philadelphia-municipal/). PGW’s operating company year-end is **31 August 2025**; the pension-reserve cards on this page are the **30 June 2025** city ACFR prints.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

Footnote IV.1 for the Gas Works Plan and Schedule III are the sourced lines:

1. **Gas Works Retirement Reserve fiduciary net position** **$702,779 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Combined pension-trust net position **$10,335,945 thousand** is not this card. Municipal Pension **$9,633,166 thousand** is not this book. Notes investments **$681,696,085** and the about-**$681.7 million** print are not fiduciary net position. Equity in Treasurer’s Account **$681,696 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Securities-lending collective investment pool **$36,282 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover Municipal Pension **$9,633,166 thousand**, leftover Pennsylvania PSERS **$83,728,862 thousand**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$863,104 thousand**. Net pension liability **$160,325 thousand**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **81.42 percent**. Municipal **72.3 percent** is not this card. The **6.00 percent** sensitivity print **73.59 percent** is not the current-rate card.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **12.5 percent** net of investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025**. Municipal **13.12 percent** is not this card. This filing does not print a separate official one-year time-weighted house card.

Do not flatten the Gas Works Retirement Reserve into [City of Philadelphia Municipal Pension Fund](/philadelphia-municipal/), [Pennsylvania PSERS](/pennsylvania-psers/), or [Pennsylvania SERS](/pennsylvania-sers/). The Municipal Pension Fund is the city file. PSERS is the Pennsylvania school file. This is **one Philadelphia Gas Works reserve file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Philadelphia Gas Works pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$702,779 thousand** line is Gas Works fiduciary net position. The **$10,335,945 thousand** line is combined pension trusts. The **$681,696,085** line is notes investments. The **81.42 percent** card is the June GASB ratio. The **12.5 percent** card is money-weighted. Those are not the same mandate.
