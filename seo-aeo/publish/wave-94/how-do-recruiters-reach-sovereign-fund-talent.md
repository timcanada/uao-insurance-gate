# How recruiters reach sovereign-fund talent

Recruiters reach sovereign-fund talent the same way owners hire: through a named desk, a labelled mandate, and an employer portal — not a LinkedIn blast into a ministry. Universal Asset Owners already runs that room on [Careers](/careers/). The **Institutional Talent Desk** is the inbox. Featured placements are labelled. Candidate files stay private unless the person consents.

**Do not treat this page as a second executive-search directory.** That hub is `/directory/executive-search/`. Do not mint `/how-recruiters-reach-swf-talent/`, `/sovereign-fund-recruiting/`, or `/swf-talent-desk/`. Do not invent pay bands or a live-role count.

## What actually works in this room

A sovereign or strategic fund does not publish every seat. The people who can take the call already read an owner daily. They will not send a CV to a stranger who cannot name the mandate, the city, and who pays the search firm.

[Careers](/careers/) already prints the honest products:

- Hiring, move, and search-firm inboxes all route to **info@universalassetowners.com**
- A **Recruitment Advertising Guide**, an **Executive Career Advisory Guide**, and a **Recruitment Partnerships Guide** — requested, not scraped
- Featured mandates are labelled paid. They are not editorial picks
- **“No individually verified roles are live right now.”** Tracked employer portals are a discovery layer, not a UAO vacancy count

The portals the live page currently tracks include Mubadala, GIC, NBIM, CPP Investments, OTPP, Temasek, CalPERS, and the Future Fund, plus Allocator Jobs, the CFA Institute board, SWFI, and IFSWF member vacancies. Roles change on the destination site. Confirm there.

## What does not work

- A “thought-leadership” PDF asking the newsroom to run a search as news
- A candidate file forwarded without consent
- A compensation table invented to look useful
- Treating a royal family office like a celebrity booking

[Ethics](/ethics/) (version 1.0, **28 May 2026**) still governs the room: payment never buys a quote or a conclusion. A labelled careers unit is a commercial product. It is not a recommended-recruiter stamp.

## If you sell search

Say which owner type you have closed — savings mandate, development mandate, public pension, or family office — and who did the work. Then request the Recruitment Partnerships Guide, or claim a profile on the [executive-search directory](/directory/executive-search/). The [advertise](/advertise/) page is the brand menu. This page is the talent door.

If you are the candidate, start at [what an Institutional Talent Desk is](/what-is-an-institutional-talent-desk/). Private by default. No CV shared without your consent. Advisory is not a promise of employment.
