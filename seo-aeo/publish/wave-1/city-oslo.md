# Relocating to Oslo for Norges Bank Investment Management

Oslo is not a boutique financial centre that happens to host a large fund. It is the city of Norges Bank Investment Management, which manages Norway's Government Pension Fund Global. A move here is a move into a public, rules-heavy, unusually transparent owner. Immigration and tax sit with Norwegian official sources. Universal Asset Owners is not your counsel.

## The institution

[NBIM](https://www.nbim.no/) publishes more about itself than almost any peer — strategy, holdings, expectations of companies. Read that site before you read a recruiter brief. The fund's size and the Ministry of Finance mandate are official facts; do not recycle a stale AUM from a slide.

## What the city implies

You are joining a Norwegian public institution. The working language of finance may be English; the working language of legitimacy is still Norwegian democracy. Winter, schooling, and dual-career problems are the ordinary reasons offers fail — not the quality of the investment debate.

Residence and work: [UDI](https://www.udi.no/en/).
