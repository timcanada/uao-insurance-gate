# City of Knoxville Pension System, explained

The City of Knoxville Pension System is Knoxville’s city pension, not leftover Tennessee Consolidated Retirement System. The official [30 June 2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://cokpension.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/FY-2025-audited-financial-statements-final.pdf) prints fiduciary net position of **$758,195,199** at **30 June 2025**. Statement investments were **$758,217,249**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Time-weighted return was **10.0 percent**. Money-weighted return was **10.23 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **73.30 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover TCRS overwrite.** Do not mint `/cokpension/` or `/knoxville-pension-explained/`. Statement investments **$758,217,249** are not fiduciary net position. Time-weighted **10.0 percent** is not money-weighted **10.23 percent**. Later Knoxville teachers sit in leftover TCRS. Divisions A, B, C, F, G, and H are columns inside this System, not a second AUM.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Fiduciary net position** **$758,195,199** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$708,311,788**). The change is **$49,883,411**. Statement investments **$758,217,249** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$760,732,527** is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover TCRS **$74,910,040,763**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion**, leftover Newport News Employees’ Retirement Fund **$1,150,643,142**, leftover Kansas City Police **$1,028,622,529**, or leftover St. Paul Teachers **$1,433,936,404** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$1,034,434,730**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **73.30 percent** (2024 **71.28 percent**). Net pension liability **$276,239,531**. Those official prints are not interchangeable with a later division-only card.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **10.23 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **9.37 percent**). Transmittal time-weighted **10.0 percent** is not the money-weighted card.
