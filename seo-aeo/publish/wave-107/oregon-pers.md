# Oregon Public Employees Retirement System, explained

The Oregon Public Employees Retirement System is Oregon’s public-employee pension, not leftover Oregon **$95 billion** and not a second CalPERS page. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.oregon.gov/pers/Documents/Financials/ACFR/2025-ACFR.pdf) prints fiduciary net position of **$108,349,083,336** at **30 June 2025**. The Defined Benefit Pension Plan is **$88,622,013,732**. The Regular Investment Portfolio returned **5.1 percent**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The Oregon Investment Council invests OPERF; PERS administers benefits. Calendar-year Treasury lines are a later date. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second CalPERS overwrite or a second PSERS overwrite.** Those keepers are `/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/` and `/pennsylvania-psers/`. Do not mint `/oregon-pers-investments/`, `/operf/`, `/oregon-investment-council/`, or `/oregon-pers-explained/`. Do not reprint leftover Oregon **$95 billion**.

## What is official (30 June 2025, and the later Treasury date)

The ACFR combining statement is the sourced fiscal-year split:

1. **Fiduciary net position** **$108,349,083,336** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$103,108,911,426**), restricted for pension, IAP, OPEB, and deferred compensation. Named books: Defined Benefit Pension Plan **$88,622,013,732**; Individual Account Program **$15,253,843,988**; RHIA **$766,666,727**; RHIPA **$97,700,889**; deferred compensation **$3,608,858,000**. Do not add those books a second time.
2. **Fiscal-year return.** Regular Investment Portfolio **5.1 percent** in fiscal year 2025 (2024: **6.0 percent**). Trailing ten-year return **7.6 percent**. That is the June fiscal-year line. It is not the December Treasury line.
3. **Later official Treasury lines (31 December 2025).** OPERF Regular Account **$101,338,832,752**. Oregon Treasury also prints total OPERF assets **$106.9 billion** and total assets under Oregon Investment Council oversight **$148.1 billion** at the same December date. Those December lines are not the June ACFR. The **$148.1 billion** line includes the Oregon Short Term Fund, SAIF, the Common School Fund, and other agency books. Do not treat that stack as PERS AUM.

Do not flatten Oregon PERS into [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/) or [PSERS](/pennsylvania-psers/). CalPERS is California. PSERS is one Pennsylvania school-employee file. This is **one Oregon public-employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Oregon pension AUM” has to name the date and the book. The **$108.3 billion** line is the June fiduciary total. The **$88.6 billion** line is the defined-benefit plan. The **$101.3 billion** line is the December Regular Account. The **$148.1 billion** line is the Investment Council stack. Those are not the same mandate.
