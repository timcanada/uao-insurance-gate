# PIF vs QIA: more than $900 billion vs no official AUM

Public Investment Fund’s official size line is **more than US$900 billion**. Qatar Investment Authority does **not** publish AUM. The live comparison, dated 4 July 2026, still stacks them into **combined assets exceeding $800 billion**, treats PIF as **$925 billion**, and assigns QIA **$550 billion** in the lede and **$650 billion** in the body. Those are tracker dollars. They are not the official filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

PIF’s **US$2 trillion** figure is a **2030 KPI**, not current AUM. The live lede’s “PIF targets $1 trillion AUM by 2030” is the wrong KPI and the wrong tense. Do not recycle either as a current size.

## What each house is

**PIF** is the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia’s public investment fund and a Vision 2030 vehicle. It publishes a “more than US$900 billion” size line. It does not publish a more precise current AUM that this desk can cite. A planned **$500 billion** NEOM ticket is not PIF AUM.

**QIA** is Qatar’s sovereign investor. It does not publish AUM. Putting QIA in a dollar column so the two houses can be ranked is a league-table invention.

They are not interchangeable. A “largest in the Gulf” frame that assigns both a press dollar is not a filing. Do not 301 this URL onto [PIF vs ADIA](/pif-vs-adia/) — different compare.

## What is not official

- **Combined assets exceeding $800 billion.**
- **PIF $925 billion** as current AUM, or a **$1 trillion by 2030** target as if it were the official KPI.
- **QIA $550 billion / $650 billion**.
- GPFG **$1.3 trillion**, CPP **$450 billion**, Temasek **$398 billion**.
- **NEOM $500+ billion** as if it were current PIF AUM.
