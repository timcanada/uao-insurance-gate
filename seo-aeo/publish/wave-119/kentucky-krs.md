# Kentucky Retirement Systems, explained

Kentucky Retirement Systems is Kentucky’s state-employee and state-police pension file, not leftover Kentucky TRS and not leftover CERS. It comprises the Kentucky Employees Retirement System and the State Police Retirement System. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/AgencyReports/IJC/AR/KPPA/KERS/2025%20Annual%20Comprehensive%20Financial%20Report.pdf) prints combined fiduciary net position restricted for benefits of **$9,882,254 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Combined investments at fair value were **$9,652,685 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The pension and insurance portfolios averaged **11.4 percent**. KERS nonhazardous pension money-weighted return was **11.24 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Kentucky TRS overwrite or a leftover CERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/kers/`, `/kentucky-krs-explained/`, or `/sprs/`. Do not mint `/kppa/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combining statements of fiduciary net position are the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Combined fiduciary net position restricted for benefits** **$9,882,254 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$8,612,810 thousand**). KERS nonhazardous pension **$5,143,284 thousand**. KERS hazardous pension **$1,148,670 thousand**. SPRS pension **$742,853 thousand**. KERS nonhazardous insurance **$1,833,872 thousand**. KERS hazardous insurance **$724,103 thousand**. SPRS insurance **$289,472 thousand**. Those seven lines are not interchangeable. Combined investments at fair value **$9,652,685 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Combined total assets **$10,347,829 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover CERS, leftover Kentucky TRS, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards are not the combining-statement book.** KERS nonhazardous pension GASB fiduciary net position **$5,061,425 thousand** is not the combining-statement **$5,143,284 thousand**. KERS nonhazardous pension fiduciary net position / total pension liability **30.40 percent**. KERS hazardous pension **77.06 percent**. Those three cards are not interchangeable with the combined $9.882 billion book.
3. **Returns.** Pension and insurance portfolios averaged **11.4 percent**. Pension time-weighted: KERS nonhazardous **11.2 percent**, KERS hazardous **11.9 percent**, SPRS **11.0 percent**. Money-weighted: KERS nonhazardous pension **11.24 percent**, KERS hazardous pension **11.88 percent**, SPRS pension **10.97 percent**. Those seven official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Kentucky Retirement Systems into [CERS](/kentucky-cers/) or [Kentucky TRS](/kentucky-trs/). CERS is the county-employee file. Kentucky TRS is the statewide teacher file. Kentucky Public Pensions Authority staffs both CERS and KRS; it is not a third AUM. This is **one KERS-plus-SPRS file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “KRS AUM” has to name the book. The **$9,882,254 thousand** line is the combined KERS-plus-SPRS pension-and-insurance book. The **$5,143,284 thousand** line is the KERS nonhazardous pension. The **$9,652,685 thousand** line is combined investments. Those are not the same mandate.
