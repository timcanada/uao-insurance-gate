# Regional Transportation Authority Pension Plan, explained

The Regional Transportation Authority Pension Plan is the RTA, Metra, and Pace non-union pension, a fiduciary component unit of the Regional Transportation Authority, not leftover MBTA (**$2,021,767,659**), leftover SacRT ATU, leftover VTA ATU, leftover University Health, leftover CTA. The official [financial statements as of 31 December 2025](https://www.rtachicago.org/uploads/files/general/Transit-Funding/Regional-Transportation-Authority-Pension-Plan_25-FS_Final.pdf) print net position restricted for pensions of **$517,819,947** at **31 December 2025**. Investments **$510,028,174** are not that card. Notes money-weighted return was **15.0 percent**. MD&A **14.6 percent** is the fiduciary-net-position increase, not the notes card. MD&A actuarial **85.0 percent** is not the GASB card. GASB **92.23 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
