# City of Atlanta Police Officers’ Pension Plan, explained

The City of Atlanta Police Officers’ Pension Plan is Atlanta’s sworn police pension, not leftover Georgia ERS and not leftover Atlanta Firefighters’ Pension Plan. The official [30 June 2025 financial statements](https://coa.sba-inc.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/FY2025-Police-Officers-Pension-Plan-Final-Report.pdf) print net position restricted for pensions of **$1,614,945 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Statement investments were **$1,598,731 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return was **11.20 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **84.54 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Georgia ERS overwrite or a leftover Atlanta Firefighters overwrite.** Do not mint `/atlanta-police-explained/` or `/coa-police/`. Statement prints are in thousands. Statement investments **$1,598,731 thousand** are not fiduciary net position. Management-discussion overall-portfolio **11.20 percent** is not a second method from money-weighted **11.20 percent**. Do not invent a combined Atlanta sworn-plus-civilian AUM.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position restricted for pensions** **$1,614,945 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$1,482,273 thousand**). The change is **$132,672 thousand**. Statement investments **$1,598,731 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$1,624,093 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover Georgia ERS combined **$26,469,880 thousand**, leftover Atlanta Firefighters **$948,512 thousand**, leftover Atlanta General Employees **$1,992,291 thousand**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$1,910,385 thousand**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **84.54 percent** (2024 **75.42 percent**). Net pension liability **$295,440 thousand**. Those official prints are not interchangeable with a later firefighter or general-employee card.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **11.20 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the years ended **30 June 2025** and **30 June 2024**. Management-discussion overall-portfolio **11.20 percent** is that money-weighted print, not a second method.

Do not flatten the City of Atlanta Police Officers’ Pension Plan into [Georgia ERS](/georgia-ers/), [Georgia TRS](/georgia-trs/), [Atlanta Firefighters’ Pension Plan](/atlanta-firefighters/), or [Atlanta General Employees’ Pension Plan](/atlanta-general-employees/). The firefighter and general-employee books are different legal persons on the same house site. This is **one Atlanta sworn-police file**.
