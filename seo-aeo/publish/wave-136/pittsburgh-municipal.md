# Pittsburgh Municipal Pension Plan, explained

The Municipal Pension Plan of the City of Pittsburgh is Pittsburgh’s civilian city pension, not leftover Pennsylvania PSERS and not leftover Pittsburgh Policemen’s Relief and Pension Plan. The official [31 December 2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.pittsburghpa.gov/files/assets/city/v/1/controller/documents/annual-comprehensive-financial-report/city-of-pittsburgh-acfr-final-2025-3.pdf) prints Act 205 allocated fiduciary net position of **$321,585,507** at **31 December 2025**. Total pension liability was **$459,279,190**. Fiduciary net position was **70.02 percent** of total pension liability. The residual Municipal Fund column of **$333,961** is not that allocated card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Pennsylvania PSERS overwrite or a leftover Comprehensive Trust overwrite.** Do not mint `/pittsburgh-municipal-explained/` or `/pgh-municipal/`. Residual Municipal Fund **$333,961** is not allocated fiduciary net position. Comprehensive Trust **$928,543,639** is not this book. Combined Total Pension Trust Fund **$931,067,584** is not a Municipal-only card. Combined GASB **56.73 percent** is not the Municipal **70.02 percent** card.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The notes net-pension-liability table is the sourced split:

1. **Allocated fiduciary net position** **$321,585,507** at **31 December 2025**. The allocation uses the method provided in Act 205. Residual Municipal Fund net position **$333,961** is not the allocated card. Do not treat leftover Comprehensive Trust **$928,543,639**, leftover Policemen allocated **$260,940,968**, leftover Firemen allocated **$348,541,109**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$459,279,190**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **70.02 percent**. Net pension liability **$137,693,683**. Combined city **56.73 percent** is not this card.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **11.6 percent** net of investment expense for the year ended **31 December 2025** on the Plan investments held by the leftover [Comprehensive Municipal Pension Trust Fund](/pittsburgh-cmptf/). That shared print is not a Municipal-only time-weighted card. This filing does not print a separate official one-year time-weighted house card for Municipal.

Do not flatten the Municipal Pension Plan into [Pennsylvania PSERS](/pennsylvania-psers/), [Pennsylvania SERS](/pennsylvania-sers/), [Comprehensive Municipal Pension Trust Fund](/pittsburgh-cmptf/), [Pittsburgh Policemen’s Relief and Pension Plan](/pittsburgh-police/), or [Pittsburgh Firemen’s Relief and Pension Plan](/pittsburgh-fire/). The Comprehensive Trust is the investment pool. Police and Fire are different legal persons. This is **one Pittsburgh civilian city file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Pittsburgh municipal pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$321,585,507** line is Act 205 allocated fiduciary net position. The **$333,961** line is residual Municipal Fund cash. The **70.02 percent** card is the Municipal GASB ratio. The **11.6 percent** card is the shared Plan money-weighted return. Those are not the same mandate.
