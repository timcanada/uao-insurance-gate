# PSP Investments, explained

PSP Investments (Public Sector Pension Investment Board) is one of Canada's largest pension investors. It invests the amounts transferred by the Government of Canada for the pension plans of the public service, the Canadian Forces, the Royal Canadian Mounted Police and the Reserve Force. As at 31 March 2026 it reported C$320.6 billion of net assets under management. Headquarters are in Montréal, with a global book. This is not investment advice.

## How a peer should read it

PSP is part of the Canadian pension-model cluster — in-house capability, global offices, a willingness to sit in infrastructure and private markets — but it is **not** CPP Investments and it is **not** Ontario Teachers'. The sponsor is the federal public service family of plans. The city is Montréal first, not Toronto. If you are hiring or selling, start there.
