# Austin Firefighters Retirement Fund, explained

Austin Firefighters Retirement Fund is Austin’s sworn firefighter pension, not leftover City of Austin Employees’ Retirement System and not leftover Austin Police Retirement System. The official [2025 Annual Report](https://www.afrfund.org/Resources/cbfb0067-436b-4e05-acb3-d33d739dd37d/2025%20Annual%20Report.pdf) prints fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits of **$1,260,063,638** at **31 December 2025**. Statement investments at fair value were **$1,243,840,553**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Time-weighted return was **13.7 percent** net of fees. Money-weighted return was **14.10 percent** net of investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **81.2 percent** of total pension liability. Actuarial funded **77.4 percent** is not the GASB card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

The house **$1.26 billion** card is not the statement **$1,260,063,638** line. The consultant portfolio card **$1,260,564,708** is not the statement. Do not draft this book from the City of Austin ACFR or from the 2025 valuation alone.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits** **$1,260,063,638** at **31 December 2025** (2024 **$1,165,347,238**). The change is **$94,716,400**. Statement investments at fair value **$1,243,840,553** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$1,260,131,572** is not fiduciary net position. The Meketa portfolio card **$1,260,564,708** is not the statement. Do not treat leftover COAERS **$4,085,942,671**, leftover Austin Police **$1,211,262,982**, leftover Houston Firefighters, leftover San Antonio Fire and Police, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$1,551,973,394**. Net pension liability **$291,909,756**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **81.2 percent**. Actuarial funded **77.4 percent** is not the GASB card. Market-value funded **81.2 percent** is that GASB card, not a second AUM.
3. **Returns.** Consultant time-weighted return **13.7 percent** net of fees for the year ended **31 December 2025**. Notes money-weighted return **14.10 percent** net of investment expense. Those official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Austin firefighters AUM” has to name the book. The **$1,260,063,638** line is fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits. The **$1,243,840,553** line is statement investments. The **$1.26 billion** card is house rounding. The **$1,260,564,708** card is a consultant portfolio print. The **81.2 percent** card is the GASB ratio. The **77.4 percent** card is the actuarial ratio. The **13.7 percent** card is the time-weighted return. The **14.10 percent** card is the money-weighted return. Those are not the same mandate.
