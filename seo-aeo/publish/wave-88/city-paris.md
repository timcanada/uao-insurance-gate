# Relocating to Paris for an FRR or French public-reserve desk

Paris is the registered-office city of the [Fonds de réserve pour les retraites](https://www.fondsdereserve.fr/en/legal-notice/). FRR is a public administrative institution created by **Law No. 2001-624 of 17 July 2001**, codified in the Social Security Code. Official siège: **56, Rue de Lille, 75007 Paris**. It is a pension-reserve book, **not** a sovereign wealth fund. A move here is often a move onto that public file — or into a window other owners staff. Right to work sits with official French sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here

- FRR manages the funds allocated to it to help ensure the sustainability of pension schemes. Official prints live on fondsdereserve.fr. Do not invent an AUM on this page. Do not mint `/frr-explained/`, `/paris-frr/`, or `/fonds-de-reserve/`.
- Confirm the legal person on the employer site. A Paris markets floor is not automatically the reserve book. Do not invent a house AUM.
- [NBIM](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/) sits in [Oslo](/careers/cities/oslo/). Do not euroize GPFG to decorate a Paris skyline.

Do not treat this city page as a minted FRR explainer. There is no UAO FRR institution slug. Do not create one.

## Why people underestimate the job

A reserve created by statute is not a product shelf. French is the language of legitimacy even when the dealing language is English. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail.
