# Press desk — quote Universal Asset Owners

Universal Asset Owners is an independent newsroom covering sovereign wealth funds, pensions, endowments, insurers and family offices. Journalists may attribute on-the-record comments to named correspondents or to a named desk (The Universal Owner, The Probability Desk, UAO Research). Embargoes are available. Payment never buys a quote. Contact the desk and mark the subject Press.

## How to attribute

- **Preferred:** “Universal Asset Owners’ Probability Desk …” or “Universal Asset Owners reported …”
- **Named:** correspondent as listed on the [masthead](/masthead/).
- **Not:** “analysts say” with our URL buried. If you use the number, use the name.

## What you can lift this month

Editors: replace this list every Monday with *sourced* findings from that week's desks. Do not leave stale weights on a press page.

Examples of the *form* (illustrative of format, not today's live weights):

1. A Probability Desk base / upside / tail with the date and the question.
2. A count from the careers tracker (“verified open roles we checked against employer systems”).
3. A Registry / UAO 100 change — only after the number is on our site with a method.
4. A leadership move we sourced to an official announcement.

If it is not on our site with a source, do not put it in this box.

## Embargoes and interviews

Email info@universalassetowners.com with **Press** in the subject. Say your outlet, deadline, and whether you need a general-desk line or a named correspondent (Gulf, Asia, Canada, Europe).

We will not comment on an unpublished people-move we cannot source. We will not preview a brief to a sponsor.

## About the organization

Independent editorial platform. Founder and publisher: Tim Peters. Standards: [Editorial standards](/editorial-standards/), [Ethics](/ethics/), [Methodology](/methodology/), [About](/about/).

## Commercial vs press

Sponsorship and advertising are a different inbox path ([Advertise](/advertise/)). If you are a reporter, do not use the sales form. If you are sales, do not promise a reporter.
