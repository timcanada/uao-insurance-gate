# Mongolia Future Heritage Fund: a Chinggis Khaan SWF sub-fund, not a standalone $1.5 billion pot

The Future Heritage Fund is no longer a standalone sovereign pot. Official Chinggis Khaan Sovereign Wealth Fund materials treat it as one of three sub-funds — Future Heritage, Savings, and Development — under the Chinggis Khaan SWF. The house's own FY2025 print for the Future Heritage Fund is **approximately USD 1.336 billion** at **31 December 2025**, with **83.44 percent** of those assets in short-term central-bank deposits and a **4.16 percent** USD return. The live page still prints **$1.5 billion as of 2023**. That is a stale standalone figure. It is not the current official FHF print.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

Do **not** add Future Heritage + Savings + Development into a homemade Chinggis pile. The house has not published an official combined Chinggis AUM that this desk can cite.

## What this is

The Future Heritage Fund is Mongolia's long-horizon savings sleeve. Government Resolution No. 72 of 5 February 2025 set the current FHF policy. Official pages now place that sleeve inside Chinggis Khaan Sovereign Wealth Fund, alongside a Savings Fund and a Development Fund. The official site is https://swf.gov.mn/en/funds. This overwrite stays on the live `/mongolia-future-heritage-fund/` URL. Do not mint `/chinggis-khaan-sovereign-wealth-fund/` — that slug 404s.

## What is not official

- **$1.5 billion as of 2023.** That is the live lede. It is a stale standalone print, not the FY2025 FHF figure.
- A homemade Chinggis Khaan combined AUM. The three sub-funds are not one official dollar pile.
- A US-dollar conversion of the Savings Fund's MNT 964 billion. Leave it in tugrik.
- A withdrawal figure. Official policy: no FHF withdrawals until 2030.
