# Abu Dhabi Investment Authority (ADIA), explained

The Abu Dhabi Investment Authority is the emirate of Abu Dhabi’s long-horizon sovereign investor, established in **1976**. It **does not publish assets under management**. The official score in the 2024 ADIA Review is the 20- and 30-year annualised return: **6.3%** and **7.1%** at 31 December 2024. The Review also prints allocation *ranges*, an internal/external split (**65 / 35**), and the line that ADIA does not, as a matter of practice, invest in the UAE. Tracker prints near **US$1.1 trillion** are not ADIA filings. This is not investment advice.

**Last updated:** 26 August 2026 · **Desk:** UAO Research · **Canonical URL:** `/adia-abu-dhabi-investment-authority/`

The live page this overwrites still said ADIA manages “roughly $1.1 trillion.” That number is a consultancy estimate (Global SWF is often the unnamed source in the Gulf press). It is **not** in the 2024 Review. Leave it off this URL.

| Internal / external | **65% / 35%** | 2024 Review | Same |

The 2024 Review was published **17 September 2025**. ADIA marked fifty years on **22 May 2026** with a release and still no AUM. Performance in the Review is time-weighted; 2024 figures were labelled provisional until non-listed assets were finalised.

## Long-term strategy ranges (not point weights)

The 2024 Review prints *ranges* for the long-term strategy portfolio. They are not a snapshot that adds to 100%.

| Sleeve | Range in the 2024 Review |
| --- | --- |
| Developed equities | 32–42% |
| Emerging-market equities | 7–15% |
| Small-cap equities | 1–5% |
| Government bonds | 7–15% |
| Credit | 2–7% |
| Financial alternatives | 5–10% |
| Real estate | 5–10% |
| Private equity | 12–17% |
| Infrastructure | 2–7% |
| Cash | 0–5% |

Geography ranges in the same document: North America 45–60%; Europe 15–30%; Developed Asia 10–20%; Emerging markets 5–10%.

Do not multiply those ranges by a tracker AUM and call the product an ADIA sleeve.

## What it is

ADIA’s own sentence: a globally diversified investment institution that prudently invests funds on behalf of the Government of Abu Dhabi, focused on long-term value creation. It is a [sovereign wealth fund](/sovereign-wealth-fund/) on the IFSWF / Santiago tests. It is **not** [Mubadala](/mubadala-investment-company/) (strategic / development) and **not** [PIF](/public-investment-fund-pif/).

Abu Dhabi runs more than one book. ADIA is the savings institution that, as a matter of practice, stays *out* of the UAE. That is the opposite of a domestic development fund.

## How a peer should read it

- **No AUM is the disclosure.** Anyone who needs a dollar size is reading a tracker, not ADIA.
- **The score is decades, not a year.** 20- and 30-year point-to-point returns are what ADIA chooses to print.
- **Internalisation is the modern fact.** 65% internal / 54% active is a different house from the 2009 Review (heavily external, heavily index).
- **Do not invent a 2025 or 2026 NAV** because a newspaper rounded Global SWF to $1.1 trillion.
