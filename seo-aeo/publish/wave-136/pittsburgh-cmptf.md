# Pittsburgh Comprehensive Municipal Pension Trust Fund, explained

The Comprehensive Municipal Pension Trust Fund is Pittsburgh’s pooled pension investment trust, not leftover Pennsylvania PSERS and not leftover Pennsylvania SERS. The official [31 December 2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.pittsburghpa.gov/files/assets/city/v/1/controller/documents/annual-comprehensive-financial-report/city-of-pittsburgh-acfr-final-2025-3.pdf) prints Comprehensive Trust net position restricted for pension of **$928,543,639** at **31 December 2025**. Total Pension Trust Fund net position was **$931,067,584**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return on the Plan investments was **11.6 percent** net of investment expense. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Pennsylvania PSERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/cmptf/` or `/pittsburgh-cmptf-explained/`. Comprehensive Trust **$928,543,639** is not Total Pension Trust Fund **$931,067,584**. OPEB Trust Funds **$42,877,283** are not the pension book. Combined pension-plus-OPEB **$973,944,867** is not pension AUM. Residual Municipal **$333,961**, Policemen **$2,119,460**, and Firemen **$70,524** columns are not the Act 205 allocated fiduciary net position cards. OPEB retiree money-weighted **12.4 percent** is not the pension card.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The combining statement of net position — pension and OPEB trust funds is the sourced line:

1. **Comprehensive Trust restricted for pension** **$928,543,639** at **31 December 2025** (2024 **$766,487,490**). The change is **$162,056,149**. Total Pension Trust Fund **$931,067,584** is not the Comprehensive Trust. Cash and short-term investments **$28,546,248** are not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover Pennsylvania PSERS, leftover Pennsylvania SERS, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB combined pension card.** The notes print a single-plan total pension liability **$1,641,131,652**, allocated fiduciary net position **$931,067,584**, net pension liability **$710,064,068**, and fiduciary net position / total pension liability **56.73 percent**. Those allocated Municipal, Policemen, and Firemen columns are different legal books. The allocations use the Act 205 method.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **11.6 percent** net of investment expense for the year ended **31 December 2025** on the Plan investments. OPEB retiree money-weighted **12.4 percent** is not this card. This filing does not print a separate official one-year time-weighted house card for the Comprehensive Trust.

Do not flatten the Comprehensive Municipal Pension Trust Fund into [Pennsylvania PSERS](/pennsylvania-psers/), [Pennsylvania SERS](/pennsylvania-sers/), [Pittsburgh Municipal Pension Plan](/pittsburgh-municipal/), [Pittsburgh Policemen’s Relief and Pension Plan](/pittsburgh-police/), or [Pittsburgh Firemen’s Relief and Pension Plan](/pittsburgh-fire/). PSERS is the statewide school file. SERS is the statewide employee file. The three Pittsburgh benefit plans are different legal persons. This is **one Pittsburgh investment-trust file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Pittsburgh pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$928,543,639** line is Comprehensive Trust net position restricted for pension. The **$931,067,584** line is Total Pension Trust Fund. The **56.73 percent** card is the combined GASB ratio. The **11.6 percent** card is money-weighted. Those are not the same mandate.
