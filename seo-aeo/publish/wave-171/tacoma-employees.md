# Tacoma Employees’ Retirement System, explained

The Tacoma Employees’ Retirement System is the City of Tacoma pension, a fiduciary fund of the City of Tacoma, not leftover Seattle City Employees, leftover Sacramento County SCERS, leftover MassPRIM, and not leftover San Diego City SDCERS. The official [31 December 2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://cms.tacoma.gov/retirement/reports/2026/2025%20ters%20acfr-%20final.pdf) prints net position restricted for pensions of **$2,423,438,168** at **31 December 2025**. Investments **$2,385,071,708** are not that card. RSI money-weighted return was **10.17 percent**. GASB **96.61 percent** is not actuarial-value **95.3 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Seattle City Employees overwrite.** Do not mint `/ters/`. `/scers/` remains Sacramento County. The City of Tacoma ACFR is not this PDF.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position, the GASB note, and the money-weighted print are the sourced lines:

Notes reconciliation also prints **$2,385,071,707**; that $1 is rounding, not a second AUM. Cash and short-term investments **$35,442,441** are not FNP. Total assets **$2,429,669,373**. Total liabilities **$6,231,205**. Do not treat leftover Seattle City Employees **$4,958,213,991**, leftover San Diego City SDCERS **$12,271,668 thousand**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion**, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, leftover Illinois IPOPIF **$13,743,292,730**, leftover Illinois FPIF **$10,286,871,117**, or leftover Boston Retirement **$12,269,220 thousand** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Notes total pension liability **$2,508,408,797**. Net pension liability **$84,970,629**. Fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **96.61 percent**. Do not treat **$2,508,408,797** as AUM. Actuarial-value funded ratio **95.3 percent** and fair-value funded ratio **96.6 percent** are not the GASB card. Assumed rate of return **6.75 percent**.
3. **Returns and cash flow.** RSI money-weighted return **10.17 percent** net of investment expenses for **2025** (2024 **7.05 percent**). Time-weighted **10.21 percent** is not money-weighted. Employer contributions **$44,279,230**. Plan-member contributions **$38,239,744**. Total contributions **$82,518,974**. Net investment income **$229,952,019**. Benefits **$118,553,681**. Refunds **$3,850,911**. Administrative expenses **$3,029,649**. Total deductions **$125,434,240**. The Washington State Auditor issued an unmodified opinion dated **30 June 2026**.
