# What is a regulated asset base?

A regulated asset base is a regulator’s capital account, not a second ports page and not a second listed-versus-unlisted infrastructure page. Ofgem calls the same idea the Regulatory Asset Value: the value ascribed to the capital employed in a licensee’s regulated network. It is built from an estimate of the initial market value at privatisation plus subsequent allowed additions at historical cost, minus regulatory depreciation and, in some cases, disposals, then indexed for inflation. Ofgem’s RIIO framework sets Revenue using Incentives to deliver Innovation and Outputs. The July 2018 RIIO-2 framework decision moved RAV indexation and allowed returns from RPI to CPIH. This page is not a ranking and not valuation advice.

## The account the regulator will pay on

Ofgem’s [RIIO-T1 / GD1 glossary](https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/sites/default/files/docs/2010/12/glossary_1.pdf) and the [RIIO-2 framework decision](https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/sites/default/files/docs/2018/07/riio-2_july_decision_document_final_300718.pdf) (**July 2018**) are the sourced definition:

1. **Regulatory Asset Value (RAV)** is the value Ofgem ascribes to the capital employed in the licensee’s regulated distribution or transmission business — the **regulated asset base**. It is calculated by summing an estimate of the **initial market value** of each licensee’s regulated asset base at privatisation and all subsequent **allowed additions** at historical cost, then **deducting annual depreciation** calculated under established regulatory methods. A deduction is also made in certain cases for **asset disposals**. The RAV is **indexed** to allow for inflation on the capital stock.
2. **RIIO** is Ofgem’s price-control framework: **Revenue = Incentives + Innovation + Outputs**. It was introduced for energy networks in **2013**. A return is paid on the RAV through the allowed cost of capital; the RAV is repaid through **depreciation allowances**.
3. The **July 2018** RIIO-2 framework decision moved away from the **Retail Prices Index (RPI)** to **CPIH** for inflation adjustment when calculating RAV and allowed returns. That switch applied from **April 2021** for gas distribution and gas and electricity transmission. Ofgem’s RIIO-ED2 finance annex adds a second fact an owner must not flatten: after totex, the RAV **no longer precisely corresponds to physical assets**. It is the balance of **unrecovered financial investment** plus the licensee’s share of incentivised out- or under-performance.

## What an owner is actually buying

A real-assets head underwriting a UK network is buying a **RAV-and-indexation read**, not a TEU vintage and not a listed-infra multiple.
