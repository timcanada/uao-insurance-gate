## What the comparison can say

- **Three jobs.** OTPP answers to Ontario teachers. CPP Investments invests the Canada Pension Plan. OMERS answers to Ontario municipal employers and members. Those are different statutes.
- **How to print scale.** Quote each filing in its own currency and date. A page that needs **$227 / $613 / $252** is a leftover page.
- **What they are not.** None of the three is a sovereign wealth fund.
