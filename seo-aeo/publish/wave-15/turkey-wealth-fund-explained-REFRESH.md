# Türkiye Wealth Fund (TVF), explained

Türkiye Wealth Fund is an **asset-backed development fund** that holds state companies, two licenses, and real estate. The audited consolidated statement at **31 December 2024** printed total assets of **TRY 12,713,728 million** and equity of **TRY 2,042,471 million**. The live UAO page’s **~US$195 billion as of 2023** is not that filing. Do not convert the lira lines into a homemade US-dollar AUM. This is not investment advice.

**Last updated:** 26 August 2026 · **Desk:** UAO Research · **Canonical URL:** `/turkey-wealth-fund-explained/`

The live page this overwrites still says **approximately $195 billion in assets under management as of 2023**, then ranks TVF against homemade PIF **$930 billion**, Samruk **$186 billion**, and Mumtalakat **$54 billion**. Official PIF language is **more than US$900 billion** — not $930bn. [Samruk-Kazyna](/samruk-kazyna-kazakhstan-explained/) official is **KZT 44.2 trillion**. [Mumtalakat](/mumtalakat-bahrain-explained/) prints **profit**, not AUM. The short twin `/turkey-wealth-fund/` is still 200. 301 that URL here after. Do not mint a third slug.

The homepage also displays a **TRY 2.7 tn equity** widget with **no as-of date** in the fetch. That is **not** a licence to replace the 31 December 2024 audited **TRY 2,042,471 million**. Wait for a dated statement.

Do not call **TRY 12.7 trillion** of *total assets* “AUM.” This is a consolidated holding-company balance sheet — banks sit inside it. Payables from financial-sector operations alone were **TRY 7,241,224 million** current. Equity **TRY 2.04 trillion** is the closer “net” line, and even that is not a savings-SWF NAV.

Their own July–December 2025 newsletter cites a GlobalSWF “10th largest” ranking. That is **TVF citing a tracker**. It is not a UAO ranking and not an official USD AUM. Do not put it in the lead.

## What it is

TVF’s own sentence: an **asset-backed development fund** established to manage publicly owned assets more effectively, enhance the value of those assets, provide equity to strategic investments in Türkiye, and deepen domestic financial markets. Compare it with [Samruk-Kazyna](/samruk-kazyna-kazakhstan-explained/) or [Temasek](/temasek-holdings-explained/), not with [NBIM](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/).

It is **not** a savings SWF that publishes a dollars-in-the-ground NAV. It is **not** [PIF](/public-investment-fund-pif/) with a “more than $900 billion” line you can paste.

## How a peer should read it

- **$195 billion is dead.** Quote **TRY 12,713,728 million** of assets and **TRY 2,042,471 million** of equity, 31 December 2024, or wait for the 2025 audit.
- **Do not dollarize.** Lira statements plus a homemade FX rate is how the live page got to $195bn.
- **Total assets ≠ liquid SWF.** The book is banks, airlines, energy, and licenses.
