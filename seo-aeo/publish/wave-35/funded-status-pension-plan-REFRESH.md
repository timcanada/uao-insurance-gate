## What the page can say

- **The ratio.** Assets divided by the present value of promised benefits. Above 100% is a surplus on that measure. Below 100% is a deficit on that measure.
- **Which measure.** Accounting, funding, and settlement can print three different ratios for the same book. Name the rulebook.
- **What funded status is not.** It is not a licence to reprint leftover CalPERS **$600 billion** or a leftover Milliman **109%** as if every pension used it.
