# Illinois State Board of Investment, explained

The Illinois State Board of Investment is the internal investment pool for Illinois SERS, GARS, and JRS — not Illinois TRS and not leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**. The official [FY2025 Annual Report](https://www.isbinvestment.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/ISBI-Report_2025_Mar26.pdf) prints fiduciary net position of **$28,581,051,604** at **30 June 2025**. Member Systems are **$28,534,549,893**. The Illinois Power Agency Trust Fund is **$46,501,711**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The investment portfolio returned **9.7 percent** net of fees. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combining statement is the sourced split (dollars, not thousands):

1. **Total fiduciary net position** **$28,581,051,604** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$26,166,688,840**). Member Systems **$28,534,549,893**. Illinois Power Agency Trust Fund **$46,501,711**. Do not add those two participant lines a second time. Do not treat leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** or leftover Oregon **$95 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named Member Systems on the same statement.** Illinois SERS **$26,987,731,426**; Judges’ Retirement System **$1,454,986,212**; General Assembly Retirement System **$91,832,255**. The investment portfolio at fair value is **$28,621,916,838**. Those lines are not Illinois TRS.
3. **Returns and statute.** Net-of-fees portfolio return **9.7 percent**. Three-year **8.4 percent**; five-year **8.5 percent**; ten-year **7.3 percent**. Net investment gains about **$2.5 billion**. Created **October 1969** by Article 22A of the Illinois Pension Code. The Illinois Power Agency Trust Fund was added **August 2007**. Office in Chicago.

Do not flatten ISBI into [Illinois TRS](/illinois-trs/), [SURS](/illinois-surs/), [Illinois SERS](/illinois-sers/), [Illinois JRS](/illinois-jrs/), [Illinois GARS](/illinois-gars/), or [IMRF](/illinois-imrf/). Illinois TRS, SURS, and IMRF invest their own books. Illinois SERS, JRS, and GARS are participants in this pool, not a second ISBI AUM. This is **one Illinois investment-pool file**.
