# Delaware Public Employees’ Retirement System, explained

The Delaware Public Employees’ Retirement System is Delaware’s combined pension administrator, not leftover Pennsylvania PSERS and not leftover Virginia VRS. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://open.omb.delaware.gov/FinancialReports/financials/FY25AnnualReport.pdf) prints combined net position restricted for pension benefits of **$14,760,489 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. The State Employees’ Plan was **$12,737,795 thousand**. Combined investments were **$14,123,427 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted return was **11.3 percent**. The State Employees’ money-weighted return was **9.6 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Pennsylvania PSERS overwrite or a second Virginia VRS page.** Do not mint `/dpers/`, `/delaware-dpers-explained/`, or `/delaware-public-employees/`. Do not mint `/delaware-pers/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combining statement of fiduciary net position is in **thousands**:

1. **Combined net position restricted for pension benefits** **$14,760,489 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (beginning **$13,605,646 thousand**; increase **$1,154,843 thousand**). Combined investments at fair value **$14,123,427 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover Pennsylvania PSERS, leftover Virginia VRS, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio STRS **$80 billion+** / **$90 billion** / **$350 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books.** State Employees’ Plan **$12,737,795 thousand**. New State Police **$809,254 thousand**. Judiciary **$116,455 thousand**. County & Municipal Police and Firefighters’ **$721,974 thousand**. County & Municipal Other Employees’ **$123,999 thousand**. Delaware Volunteer Firemen’s Fund **$82,075 thousand**. Closed Diamond State Port Corporation **$35,475 thousand**. County & Municipal Police and Firefighters’ COLA Fund **$34,623 thousand**. Post-Retirement Increase Fund **$87,793 thousand**. Delaware Local Government Retirement Investment Pool **$1,652 thousand**. Closed State Police **$9,268 thousand**. Special Fund **$126 thousand**. Those lines are not interchangeable. Do not add the named books into a second house AUM.
3. **Returns.** Time-weighted return **11.3 percent**. Annual money-weighted returns, net of pension plan investment costs: State Employees’ **9.6 percent**. New State Police **9.6 percent**. Judiciary **9.6 percent**. County & Municipal Police and Firefighters’ **9.6 percent**. Volunteer Firefighters **13.6 percent**. Closed State Police **8.3 percent**. Those return lines are not interchangeable. State Employees’ fiduciary net position was **92.7 percent** of total pension liability (**$12,737,795 thousand** over **$13,734,969 thousand**).
