# Southeast Asian SWFs: no official $1 trillion pile — and GIC has no AUM

There is no official combined AUM for Southeast Asian sovereign wealth funds. The live page, dated 3 July 2026, still leads with GIC **($671B AUM)** and Temasek **($403B)**, then says the region manages **combined assets exceeding $1 trillion**. Later it assigns GIC **approximately $870 billion**, invents Indonesia’s **State Investment Committee** at **approximately $47 billion**, and files SCIC at **roughly $35 billion**, Khazanah at **near $84 billion**, and Maharlika at **$10 billion from Philippine central bank reserves**. Those dollars are not the filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## Official numbers (do not stack them)

| House | Official print | What is not official |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GIC Private Limited | No official AUM. 20-year real **3.4%** / USD nominal **5.6%** to **31 March 2026**. Established **1981**. Legal name is **GIC Private Limited**, not “Government Investment Corporation” | **$671 billion**; **$870 billion** |
| Temasek Holdings | NPV **S$518 billion (US$401 billion)** at **31 March 2026**. Government is the **sole** equity shareholder. Incorporated **1974** | **$403 billion** as of March 2024 |
| Indonesia Investment Authority | Established **December 2020**. AUM **IDR 146.2 trillion** at end-2025. First cash injection **IDR 15 trillion** on **26 February 2021** | **State Investment Committee**; **$47 billion** |
| Danantara (BPI Danantara) | **Law No. 1 of 2025**. CEO **Rosan Roeslani**. No official AUM | Mashing Danantara into INA as one 2023 ticket |
| Khazanah Nasional | **RM105 billion NAV** / **RM156 billion** total assets at **31 December 2025**. **No USD AUM** | **~$84 billion** |
| Vietnam SCIC | State capital manager. This desk did not fetch an official SCIC AUM this wake | **$35 billion** |
| Maharlika Investment Fund | **Republic Act No. 11954** (2023). Authorized capital stock **PHP 500 billion**. Initial subscriptions: Landbank **P50 billion**, DBP **P25 billion**, national government **P50 billion** (BSP dividends up to **P50 billion** toward that NG line) | **$10 billion** AUM from BSP reserves |
| Combined “Southeast Asia SWFs” | No official combined AUM | **Exceeding $1 trillion** |

Print ringgit, rupiah, and Singapore dollars as the houses print them. Do **not** dollarize INA, Khazanah, or Maharlika’s peso capital into a homemade USD rank. Do not mint `/indonesia-investment-authority/` or `/vietnam-scic/` (both 404).

INA’s own financial-statement page separately prints deployment (with co-investors **approximately IDR 74.5 trillion / ~USD 4.7 billion**; INA’s own **IDR 33.3 trillion / ~USD 2.1 billion**). Those are **deployment** lines, not a second AUM.

## What this page can say

Singapore splits the jobs: [GIC](/gic-singapore-explained/) manages government assets and will not give you a size; [Temasek](/is-temasek-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/) owns a commercial book and **does** print NPV. Malaysia’s [Khazanah](/is-khazanah-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/) prints NAV in ringgit. Indonesia’s named SWF on ina.go.id is **INA**, not a “State Investment Committee.” [Danantara](/danantara-indonesia-sovereign-fund/) is a different legal person.

A “Southeast Asia exceeds $1 trillion” lede that needs GIC **$870 billion** and a **$47 billion** Indonesian committee is consultancy arithmetic.

## What is not official

- A combined Southeast Asian SWF pile of **more than $1 trillion**.
- GIC AUM in any dollar, including **$671 billion** and **$870 billion**.
- Temasek **$403 billion** as the current print.
- Indonesia **State Investment Committee $47 billion**.
- SCIC **$35 billion**.
- Khazanah **~$84 billion**.
- Maharlika **$10 billion** AUM, or GSIS **$46 billion** as if it were a SWF.
