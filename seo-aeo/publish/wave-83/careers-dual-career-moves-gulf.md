# Dual-career moves to the Gulf — what actually breaks the offer

A Gulf sovereign-investor offer often dies on the second career, not the first. Abu Dhabi and Riyadh are national-mandate cities. The spouse who cannot work, the school that is not in the pack, and the three-year tour are the closing conditions. This Careers Intelligence briefing sits beside Universal Asset Owners' [Abu Dhabi](/careers/cities/abu-dhabi/) and [Riyadh](/careers/cities/riyadh/) city pages and the [moving-into playbook](/careers/intelligence/moving-into-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/). It is not immigration, tax, or compensation advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second city page or a second moving-into playbook.** Do not invent a sovereign-fund AUM to decorate a family story.

## The second career is part of the mandate

[Asset owners](/what-is-an-asset-owner/) hold capital for their own account. A move onto [ADIA](https://www.adia.ae/), [Mubadala](/mubadala-investment-company/), [ADQ](/adq-abu-dhabi/), or [PIF](/public-investment-fund-pif/) is a move onto a national investment file — not a regional banking tour with desert weather.

The live moving-into playbook already says people “come for a few years but don't always stay.” Dual-career households fail faster. The [relocating umbrella](/careers/intelligence/relocating-for-an-asset-owner-role/) already names the reasons: schooling, spouses who cannot work, and parents who will not board the plane.

Right to work and dependent rules sit with official sources — start at the UAE portal [u.ae](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services) and [PIF’s own site](https://www.pif.gov.sa/) for the Riyadh house — or with licensed counsel. Do not copy a blog.

## What to pressure-test before either of you resigns

1. **Two work permissions.** Whose visa is the lead, and what can the other person actually do?
2. **School and housing.** What is in the offer, and what is a rumour?
3. **Which city is the desk.** Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Riyadh, or a deal team that lives on a plane?
4. **Year four.** How many people in the last incoming class are still there — with their household intact?
5. **Exit.** Where do dual-career alumni go when one seat ends?

## For employers and search firms

If the brief assumes a trailing spouse, say so. If it does not, budget for two careers or lose the hire in month six. Owners already use [Careers](/careers/) when they are hiring. Labelled recruitment products: [Careers](/careers/).
