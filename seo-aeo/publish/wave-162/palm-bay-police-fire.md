# City of Palm Bay Police and Firefighters’ Retirement System, explained

The City of Palm Bay Police and Firefighters’ Retirement System is Palm Bay’s combined sworn pension, not leftover Largo Police and Fire, leftover Pompano Beach Police and Fire, and not leftover Tampa Fire and Police. The official [30 September 2025 and 2024 City of Palm Bay Police and Firefighters’ Retirement System financial statements](https://www.pbpfpf.org/resources/759a1193-5d3f-47dd-b805-1ffd002958c9/2025%20final%20audit.pdf) print net position restricted for pensions of **$260,496,503** at **30 September 2025**. Investments **$251,476,725** are not that card. Police **$147,945,403**, Fire **$112,242,970**, and General Employees **$308,130** are statement columns of **one legal person**, not second AUM. DROP **$7,008,601** is not a second AUM. Money-weighted return was **8.36 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. GASB **78.11 percent** is of the combined book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
