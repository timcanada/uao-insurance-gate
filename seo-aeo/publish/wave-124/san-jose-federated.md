# San José Federated City Employees’ Retirement System, explained

The Federated City Employees’ Retirement System is San José’s civilian employee pension and healthcare book, not leftover San José Police and Fire and not leftover SFERS. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.sjretirement.com/Resources/7b19bfb5-0024-4529-9670-d259515f4410/2024-2025.pdf) prints combined plan net position of **$3,947,899 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Combined investments at fair value were **$3,957,079 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Pension fiduciary net position was **$3,486,313 thousand**. Pension fiduciary net position was **65.8 percent** of total pension liability. The pension time-weighted return was **9.6 percent** net of fees. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover San José Police and Fire overwrite or a leftover SFERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/sj-federated/`, `/federated-sj/`, or `/sjcers/`. Do not treat the house **$3.95 billion** card as a pension-only fiduciary line.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of plan net position is the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Combined plan net position restricted for pension benefits and postemployment healthcare benefits** **$3,947,899 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$3,567,779 thousand**), up **$380,120 thousand** / **10.7 percent**. Combined investments at fair value **$3,957,079 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Those two prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover San José Police and Fire combined **$5,966,077 thousand**, leftover SFERS, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books on the same statement.** Pension **$3,486,313 thousand**. Healthcare **$461,586 thousand**. Pension investments **$3,495,567 thousand** is not pension fiduciary net position. Healthcare investments **$461,512 thousand** is not healthcare fiduciary net position. Those lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat the pension book as the combined book.
3. **Returns and funded cards.** Pension time-weighted return **9.6 percent** net of fees against a policy benchmark **10.4 percent** and an assumed rate of **6.625 percent**. Healthcare time-weighted return **11.0 percent** is not the pension return. Pension total pension liability **$5,294,989 thousand**. Pension fiduciary net position / total pension liability **65.8 percent**. Those official one-year prints and funded cards are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Federated into [San José Police and Fire](/san-jose-police-fire/), [SFERS](/san-francisco-sfers/), [OCERS](/ocers/), or [SamCERA](/samcera/). Police and Fire is a different San José legal person. SFERS is the San Francisco **city** file. This is **one San José civilian federated file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “San José Federated AUM” has to name the book. The **$3,947,899 thousand** line is combined plan net position. The **$3,486,313 thousand** line is pension fiduciary net position. The **$3,957,079 thousand** line is combined investments. The house **$3.95 billion** card is the combined book rounded.
