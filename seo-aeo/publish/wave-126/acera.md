# ACERA, explained

Alameda County Employees’ Retirement Association is Alameda County’s CERL retirement system, not leftover LACERA, leftover CCCERA, leftover LACERS, and not a May 2026 draft packet. The official [2025 final ACFR](https://www.acera.org/sites/main/files/file-attachments/aceras_2025_acfr_-_final.pdf?1782760217) prints combined fiduciary net position restricted for benefits of **$14,052,478 thousand** at **31 December 2025**. Investments at fair value were **$14,015,718 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Time-weighted return was **15.9 percent** net of fees. Money-weighted return was **15.74 percent**. GASB pension fiduciary net position **$12,561,432 thousand** is not the combined book. Fiduciary net position was **96.36 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover LACERA overwrite, a leftover CCCERA overwrite, or a leftover LACERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/acera-explained/` or `/alameda-cera/`. Do not treat the house **$14.1 billion** card as a second AUM. That card is the combined book rounded. The County’s **$400 million** voluntary UAAL contribution is a contribution, not a second AUM.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line. The ACFR prints dollars in **thousands**:

1. **Combined fiduciary net position restricted for benefits** **$14,052,478 thousand** at **31 December 2025** (2024 **$12,034,355 thousand**). Pension Plan and Non-OPEB **$12,894,897 thousand**. Postemployment Medical **$1,157,581 thousand**. Investments at fair value **$14,015,718 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Those prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover LACERA, leftover LACERS combined **$25,375,319 thousand**, leftover CCCERA’s rounded listing card, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$13,036,367 thousand**. GASB pension fiduciary net position **$12,561,432 thousand**. Net pension liability **$474,935 thousand**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **96.36 percent**. The GASB fiduciary line is not the combined book **$14,052,478 thousand** and is not the pension-column **$12,894,897 thousand**. The house footnote says the GASB line is combined net assets minus OPEB-related SRBR assets.
3. **Returns.** Time-weighted total-fund return **15.9 percent** net of fees. Money-weighted return on total investments (pension plus OPEB) **15.74 percent**. Those two official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten ACERA into [LACERA](/lacera/), [LACERS](/lacers/), [SBCERA](/sbcera/), [VCERA](/vcera/), [KCERA](/kcera/), or [FCERA](/fcera/). LACERA is the Los Angeles **County** file. LACERS is the Los Angeles **civilian** city file. This is **one Alameda County file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “ACERA AUM” has to name the book. The **$14,052,478 thousand** line is combined fiduciary net position. The **$12,894,897 thousand** line is the pension and non-OPEB column. The **$1,157,581 thousand** line is postemployment medical. The **$14,015,718 thousand** line is investments. The **$12,561,432 thousand** line is the GASB pension fiduciary card. The **$400 million** line is a County voluntary UAAL contribution. Those are not the same mandate.
