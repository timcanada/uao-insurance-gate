# USC endowment, explained

The University of Southern California endowment is USC’s long-horizon book, not leftover Harvard **$56.9 billion**, leftover Notre Dame Endowment Pool **$25,434,616 thousand**. The official [2025 financial report](https://bpb-us-w1.wpmucdn.com/sites.usc.edu/dist/d/791/files/2026/02/2025-USC-Financial-Report.pdf) prints endowment net assets of **$8,774,280 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. The official [endowment brochure](https://giving.usc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/USC-Endowment-Brochure.pdf) prints **$9.03 billion** market value and a **14.0 percent** one-year gain. Those two value prints are different books. Do not flatten them. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

Non-pooled **$298,710 thousand**. Beginning **$8,092,846 thousand**. Those lines are not interchangeable. Brochure **$9.03 billion** market value is a different official print. Do not flatten the brochure into Note 7. Do not treat leftover Harvard **$56.9 billion**, leftover Yale **$44.1 billion**, leftover MIT **$27,366.2 million**, leftover Notre Dame Endowment Pool **$25,434,616 thousand**, leftover Penn **$24,808,028 thousand** as this filing.
2. **Named books are not interchangeable.** Note 7 total investment return, net **$1,059,153 thousand** is a dollar return, not **14.0 percent**. Brochure one-year gain **14.0 percent** and **$9.03 billion** market value are the brochure book. MD&A chart axis **14.0** is net assets in billions, not a return.
3. **Payout.** Spending-rule allocation **$395,437,000** (**$40.09** per unit). Appropriation of endowment assets for expenditure **$384,264 thousand** is a different line. Independent auditors’ report, Los Angeles, **18 December 2025**.
