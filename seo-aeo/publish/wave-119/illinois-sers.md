# State Employees’ Retirement System of Illinois, explained

The State Employees’ Retirement System of Illinois is Illinois’s state-employee pension, not leftover Illinois TRS, not leftover SURS, and not leftover Ohio SERS. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://ilsrs.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/srs/sers/resources/financial-report/annual-sers-2025.pdf) prints fiduciary net position restricted for pensions of **$27,638,458,059** at **30 June 2025**. Investments were **$26,987,731,428**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The Illinois State Board of Investment commingled-fund return was **9.7 percent**. The System’s money-weighted return on that commingled book was **9.8 percent**. Fiduciary net position was **45.9 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Fiduciary net position restricted for pensions** **$27,638,458,059** at **30 June 2025** (previously presented beginning **$25,303,027,219**; restated beginning after GASB 101 **$25,302,244,299**). Investments **$26,987,731,428** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$27,729,527,181** is not fiduciary net position. Those three lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover Illinois TRS, leftover SURS, leftover Ohio SERS, leftover Pennsylvania SERS, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$60,250,116,049**. Net pension liability **$32,611,657,990**. Fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **45.9 percent**. Those three cards are not a second investment book. The Illinois State Board of Investment commingled fund is the investment vehicle, not a second Illinois SERS AUM.
3. **Returns.** Illinois State Board of Investment commingled-fund overall rate of return **9.7 percent**. System money-weighted return on its investment in that commingled fund **9.8 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Those two official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Illinois SERS into [Illinois TRS](/illinois-trs/), [SURS](/illinois-surs/), [Illinois State Board of Investment](/illinois-isbi/), [Ohio SERS](/ohio-sers/), or [Pennsylvania SERS](/pennsylvania-sers/). ISBI manages this book. Illinois TRS is the statewide teacher file. SURS is the public-university file. Ohio SERS and Pennsylvania SERS are different legal persons. This is **one Illinois state-employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Illinois SERS AUM” has to name the book. The **$27,638,458,059** line is fiduciary net position restricted for pensions. The **$26,987,731,428** line is investments. Those are not the same mandate.
