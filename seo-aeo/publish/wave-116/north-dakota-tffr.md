# North Dakota Teachers’ Fund for Retirement, explained

The North Dakota Teachers’ Fund for Retirement is North Dakota’s teacher pension trust, not leftover North Dakota PERS and not leftover North Dakota Legacy Fund. The official [2025 Retirement and Investment Office Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.rio.nd.gov/sites/default/files/documents/PDFs/RIO/Reports/ACFR/annualreport2025.pdf) prints pension-trust fiduciary net position restricted for pensions of **$3,648,749,437** at **30 June 2025**. Pension-trust investments at fair value were **$3,568,869,008**. The State Investment Board investment-trust book was **$22,002,744,770**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted return on the Teachers’ Fund for Retirement client account was **11.42 percent**. The money-weighted return was **11.32 percent** net. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover North Dakota PERS overwrite or a second North Dakota Legacy Fund page.** Do not mint `/tffr/`, `/nd-tffr/`, or `/north-dakota-tffr-explained/`. Do not mint `/north-dakota-legacy/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of net position – fiduciary funds is the sourced line:

1. **Pension-trust fiduciary net position restricted for pensions** **$3,648,749,437** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$3,351,007,841**). Pension-trust investments at fair value **$3,568,869,008** is not fiduciary net position. Combined Retirement and Investment Office fiduciary net position **$25,651,494,207** includes the State Investment Board investment trust and is not the Teachers’ Fund for Retirement book. The State Investment Board investment-trust book **$22,002,744,770** is not the teacher pension. The State Investment Board client fair-value card **$3,582,303,323** is not fiduciary net position. Those five lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover North Dakota PERS, leftover North Dakota Legacy Fund, leftover Minnesota SBI, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books.** The Retirement and Investment Office administers a pension trust for the Teachers’ Fund for Retirement and an investment trust for the State Investment Board. The Teachers’ Fund for Retirement is one of the State Investment Board’s investment clients. Those roles are not interchangeable. [North Dakota PERS](/north-dakota-pers/) is a different administrator.
3. **Returns and funded status.** Time-weighted return on the Teachers’ Fund for Retirement client account **11.42 percent**. Annual money-weighted return on pension-plan investments, net of pension-plan investment expense, **11.32 percent**. Those two official one-year prints are not interchangeable. Total pension liability **$4,927,218 thousand**. Net pension liability **$1,278,469 thousand**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **74.05 percent**. Actuarial-value funded ratio **73.43 percent** at **1 July 2025**. Fair-value funded ratio **74.05 percent**. Those funded cards are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten the Teachers’ Fund for Retirement into [North Dakota PERS](/north-dakota-pers/) or [North Dakota Legacy Fund](/north-dakota-legacy-fund/). North Dakota PERS is the public-employee administrator. North Dakota Legacy Fund is a state oil-tax savings file. This is **one North Dakota teacher-pension file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “North Dakota TFFR AUM” has to name the book. The **$3,648,749,437** line is the pension-trust fiduciary book. The **$3,568,869,008** line is pension-trust investments. The **$25,651,494,207** line is the combined Retirement and Investment Office book. Those are not the same mandate.
