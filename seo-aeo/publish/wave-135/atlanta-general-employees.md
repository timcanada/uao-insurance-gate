# City of Atlanta General Employees’ Pension Plan, explained

The City of Atlanta General Employees’ Pension Plan is Atlanta’s civilian city pension, not leftover Georgia ERS and not leftover Atlanta Firefighters’ Pension Plan. The official [30 June 2025 financial statements](https://coa.sba-inc.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/FY2025-General-Employees-Pension-Plan-Final-Report.pdf) print net position restricted for pensions of **$1,992,291 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Statement investments were **$1,983,878 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return was **11.40 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. This filing does not print a GASB funded-ratio card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Georgia ERS overwrite or a leftover Atlanta Firefighters overwrite.** Do not mint `/atlanta-ge/` or `/atlanta-employees/`. Statement prints are in thousands. Statement investments **$1,983,878 thousand** are not fiduciary net position. Securities-lending liability **$88,408 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Management-discussion overall-portfolio **11.40 percent** is not a second method from money-weighted **11.40 percent**. Do not invent a combined Atlanta sworn-plus-civilian AUM. Do not invent a funded ratio.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position restricted for pensions** **$1,992,291 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$1,835,701 thousand**). The change is **$156,590 thousand**. Statement investments **$1,983,878 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$2,092,480 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover Georgia ERS combined **$26,469,880 thousand**, leftover Atlanta Firefighters **$948,512 thousand**, leftover Atlanta Police Officers **$1,614,945 thousand**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** This financial-statement package does not print a total-pension-liability or fiduciary-net-position / total-pension-liability card. Do not invent one from a later valuation or newsletter.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **11.40 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **10.00 percent**). Management-discussion overall-portfolio **11.40 percent** is that money-weighted print, not a second method.

Do not flatten the City of Atlanta General Employees’ Pension Plan into [Georgia ERS](/georgia-ers/), [Georgia TRS](/georgia-trs/), [Atlanta Firefighters’ Pension Plan](/atlanta-firefighters/), or [Atlanta Police Officers’ Pension Plan](/atlanta-police/). Georgia ERS is the statewide public-employee administrator. The firefighter and police books are different legal persons on the same house site. This is **one Atlanta civilian city file**.
