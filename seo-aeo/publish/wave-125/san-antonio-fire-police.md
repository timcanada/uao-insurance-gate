# Fire and Police Pension Fund, San Antonio, explained

The Fire and Police Pension Fund, San Antonio is San Antonio’s sworn fire-and-police pension, not leftover Houston fire or police and not leftover San José Police and Fire. The official [2025 audited financial statements](https://www.safppf.org/SSLFolder/pdf/2025_AnnualAudit.pdf) print fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits of **$4,493,776 thousand** at **31 December 2025**. Investments at fair value were **$4,452,419 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The money-weighted return was **11.9 percent** net of investment costs. Fiduciary net position was **87.39 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Net position restricted for pension benefits** **$4,493,776 thousand** at **31 December 2025** (2024 **$4,124,762 thousand**). Investments at fair value **$4,452,419 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. “Total Investments” including cash and lending collateral **$4,638,588 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Those prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover Houston Firefighters, leftover Houston Police, leftover San José Police and Fire combined **$5,966,077 thousand**, leftover LAFPP combined **$34,110,601,276**, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$5,142,169 thousand**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **87.39 percent**. Those two lines are not interchangeable.
3. **Returns.** Money-weighted return **11.9 percent** net of investment costs (2024 **8.5 percent**). The house audit does not print a time-weighted return. Do not invent one.
