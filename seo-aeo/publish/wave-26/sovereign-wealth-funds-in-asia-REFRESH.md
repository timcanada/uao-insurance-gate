# Asian SWFs: no official $4 trillion pile — and GPIF is not a SWF

There is no official combined AUM for Asian sovereign wealth funds. The live page, dated 8 July 2026, still prints **over $4 trillion**, files Japan’s Government Pension Investment Fund as a **$1.3 trillion SWF**, and assigns SAFE **$1.3 trillion**, CIC **$1.1 trillion**, GIC **$673 billion**, Temasek **$403 billion**, and NPS **$673 billion**. Those are tracker numbers. They are not the official filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## Official numbers (do not stack them)

| House | Official print | What is not official |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SAFE (China) | No official AUM. SAFE is a **reserve manager**, not a SWF AUM you can add | **$1.3 trillion**; **$800bn–$1T** Treasuries |
| China Investment Corporation | **US$1.57 trillion** total / **US$1.37 trillion** net at **31 December 2024**. Founded **29 September 2007** with registered capital **US$200 billion** | **$1.1 trillion**; leftover **$1.3 trillion as of Dec 2023** |
| GIC Private Limited | No official AUM. 20-year real **3.4%** / USD nominal **5.6%** to **31 March 2026** | **$673 billion** |
| Temasek Holdings | **S$518 billion (US$401 billion)** at **31 March 2026** | **$403 billion** |
| GPIF (Japan) | **¥293,643.7 billion** / FY2025 return **16.47%** at **31 March 2026**. GPIF is a **public pension**, not a SWF | **$1.3 trillion** as a SWF |
| National Pension Service (Korea) | **KRW 1,848.7 trillion** at end-May 2026. **No USD AUM** | **$673 billion** |
| Combined “Asia SWFs” | No official combined AUM | **$4 trillion** |

Do not mint `/safe-china/` (404). Do not add GPIF or NPS into a SWF pile. Do not dollarize yen or won.

## What this page can say

Name the houses. Quote the filing, or say the house does not publish AUM. [CIC](/china-investment-corporation-cic-explained/) is China’s SWF. [GIC](/gic-singapore-explained/) and [Temasek](/is-temasek-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/) are Singapore’s two different legal persons. [GPIF](/gpif-japan-explained/) and [NPS](/national-pension-service-korea-explained/) are pensions. SAFE is a reserve book.

An “Asia $4 trillion” lede that needs SAFE, GPIF, and GIC dollars is a consultancy pile, not a filing.

## What is not official

- **$4 trillion** as combined Asian SWF AUM.
- SAFE AUM in any dollar.
- GPIF as a SWF, or GPIF **$1.3 trillion**.
- GIC **$673 billion**.
- CIC **$1.1 trillion** when the house print is **US$1.57T / US$1.37T**.
