# Aware Super, explained

Aware Super is one of Australia’s largest **profit-for-member** superannuation funds. It opened as **First State Super** when compulsory super began in **1992** and is now **open to everyone**. Its own site prints **over $240 billion** in retirement savings at **30 June 2026** and **more than 1.2 million** members. The TelstraSuper successor-fund transfer completed on **30 April 2026**. The live UAO page’s **$16 billion** and **$130 billion** lines are dead. This is not investment advice.

**Last updated:** 26 August 2026 · **Desk:** UAO Research · **Canonical URL:** `/aware-super-explained/`

The live page this overwrites (6 July 2026) cannot decide what the fund is. The dek says **$16 billion** for “community and disability services.” The body says **AUD $130 billion** and “fourth-largest public sector pension scheme.” Both are wrong. Official is **over $240 billion** (Aware internal data, footnote M1, **30 June 2026**). Twin `/aware-super-australia-explained/` still returns 200 — 301 that URL here **after** this overwrite. Do not mint a third synonym.

## What is not official — do not put it back

- **$16 billion.** Dead. Off by more than **$200 billion**.
- **AUD $130 billion as of mid-2024.** Also dead.
- “Community and disability services” as the whole mandate. Official origin is **First State Super, 1992**, now open to everyone.
- A **2016** or **2019** three-scheme merger story (LGSS / EGRC / Multifund) as if it were the founding.
- **340,000** or **300,000** members.
- “Fourth-largest public sector pension scheme.”
- Minting `/aware-super/` or a 2027 twin.

## How a peer should read it

Aware is a scaled Australian industry fund — First State Super in 1992, Aware today, TelstraSuper absorbed in April 2026. The score they print is **over $240 billion** at 30 June 2026. Anyone still writing **$16 billion** is describing a different decade and a different institution.
