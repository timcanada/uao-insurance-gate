# Minnesota Teachers Retirement Association, explained

The Teachers Retirement Association of Minnesota is Minnesota’s statewide teacher pension, not leftover Minnesota SBI and not leftover Illinois TRS. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://minnesotatra.org/sites/default/files/2026-04/MN-TRA-2025-ACFR.pdf) prints net position restricted for pensions of **$31,261,375 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Cash and investments were **$32,422,884 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted return was **10.9 percent** net of investment-management fees. Fiduciary net position was **84.51 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Minnesota SBI overwrite or a leftover Illinois TRS overwrite.** Do not mint `/mntra/`, `/minnesota-tra-explained/`, or `/minnesota-teachers/`. Do not mint `/msbi/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Net position restricted for pensions** **$31,261,375 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$29,092,479 thousand**). Cash and investments **$32,422,884 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$32,452,394 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. The Minnesota State Board of Investment Combined Funds TRA participation **$31,248.6 million** is a Combined Funds slice, not this fiduciary line. Those four prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover Minnesota SBI Total Funds Under Management **$155,859.3 million**, leftover Illinois TRS, leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion**, leftover Ohio STRS **$80 billion+**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$36,992,230 thousand**. Net pension liability **$5,730,855 thousand**. Fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **84.51 percent**. The actuarial accrued-liability funding ratio **81.6 percent** uses actuarial assets **$30.2 billion** against actuarial accrued liability **$37 billion**. Market-value assets on that funding discussion **$31.3 billion** is the fiduciary book rounded, not a second AUM. Those funded cards are not interchangeable.
3. **Returns.** Minnesota State Board of Investment time-weighted Combined Funds return on TRA assets **10.9 percent** net of investment-management fees and profit-sharing, matching the composite benchmark. GASB money-weighted return **10.9 percent** net of the same fees. The required-supplementary-information money-weighted print **10.92 percent** is not the note’s **10.9 percent** card. Those official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

The Minnesota State Board of Investment manages this book inside the Combined Funds with MSRS and PERA. That Combined Funds line is not a second Teachers Retirement Association AUM.

Do not flatten the Teachers Retirement Association into [Minnesota SBI](/minnesota-sbi/), [Minnesota PERA](/minnesota-pera/), [Minnesota MSRS](/minnesota-msrs/), [Illinois TRS](/illinois-trs/), [Georgia TRS](/georgia-trs/), [Texas Teacher Retirement System](/texas-teacher-retirement-system-explained/), [St. Paul Teachers’ Retirement Fund Association](/st-paul-teachers/), [Nebraska School Employees Retirement Plan](/nebraska-school/), or [Omaha School Employees Retirement Plan](/omaha-osers/). Minnesota SBI is the statutory investor. PERA is the public-employee plan. MSRS is the state-employee plan. St. Paul Teachers is a different Minnesota teacher file. Nebraska School and OSERS are Nebraska files. This is **one Minnesota statewide teacher file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Minnesota TRA AUM” has to name the book. The **$31,261,375 thousand** line is fiduciary net position. The **$32,422,884 thousand** line is cash and investments. The **$31,248.6 million** line is the Combined Funds participation. Those are not the same mandate.
