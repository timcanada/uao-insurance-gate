# Relocating to Vienna for an ÖBAG or Austrian public-holding desk

Vienna is the official-address city of [Österreichische Beteiligungs AG](https://oebag.gv.at/impressum/). Official imprint: **Kolingasse 14-16, 1090 Vienna**. ÖBAG is the Republic of Austria’s strategic holding company. Its predecessor organisation was founded in **1967**; in its current form ÖBAG has existed since **2019**. It is a public holding, **not** a savings sovereign wealth fund. A move here is often a move onto that state-ownership file. Right to work sits with official Austrian sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit elsewhere

- ÖBAG’s own vision page: a public investor that manages companies partially or wholly owned by the Republic. Official prints live on oebag.gv.at. Do not invent an AUM on this page. Do not mint `/oebag-explained/` or `/vienna-oebag/`.
- Occupational Pensionskassen also sit in Vienna. They are **different** legal persons from ÖBAG. Do not mash them into one “Vienna SWF” desk. Do not mint `/apk-explained/`.
- [compenswiss](/careers/cities/geneva/) is Geneva. [PUBLICA](/careers/cities/zurich/) is Bern. Do not treat a Vienna postcode as a Swiss book.

Do not treat this city page as a minted ÖBAG explainer. There is no UAO ÖBAG institution slug. Do not create one.

## Papers

Start at the [Austrian federal migration platform](https://www.migration.gv.at/en/). Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Why people underestimate the job

A 2019 holding form is not a 1967 industrial conglomerate with a different logo. German is the language of legitimacy even when the dealing language is English. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail.
