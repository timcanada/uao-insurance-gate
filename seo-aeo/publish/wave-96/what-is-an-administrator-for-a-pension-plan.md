# What is an administrator for a pension plan?

Administrator is three different jobs that boards keep flattening. Under ERISA, the plan administrator is the person named in the instrument — or the plan sponsor if no one is named. A fund administrator is the operations vendor who produces a NAV and lands a capital call. A custodian safekeeps and reports. Universal Asset Owners maps custody and administration on one [directory hub](/directory/custody-administration/). This page is not operational advice.

## The legal person (ERISA)

29 U.S.C. § 1002(16)(A) is the public definition. The **administrator** is:

1. the person **specifically so designated** by the terms of the instrument under which the plan is operated;
2. if no one is designated, the **plan sponsor**; or
3. if neither an administrator nor a sponsor can be identified, such other person as the **Secretary of Labor** may prescribe by regulation.

§ 1002(16)(B) then names the sponsor: the employer (single-employer plan); the employee organization; a joint board or similar group (multiemployer); or the pooled plan provider.

That person is who the statute talks to about reports and notices. It is not, by itself, the bank that holds the securities.

**Governmental plans sit in a different file.** Many public pensions and sovereign-adjacent books are outside that Title I definition. Do not paste “ERISA administrator” onto CalPERS, ABP, or a ministry mandate as if it were a corporate DC plan. Ask which statute actually names the desk.

## The operations vendor

Owners also buy a **fund administrator**: NAV, capital-call mechanics, look-through, investor reporting. That vendor can be a third party even when the legal administrator is the sponsor. The failure mode is a number that does not match the board pack.

## The custodian (already defined)

The OCC’s public custody-services page says bank custody typically includes the **settlement, safekeeping, and reporting** of customers’ marketable securities and cash. How an owner picks that bank is on [how owners pick a custodian](/how-do-owners-pick-a-custodian/). Do not collapse safekeeping into “admin.”

[Asset owners](/what-is-an-asset-owner/) hold capital for their own account. The administrator and the custodian work for the owner. That is a [fiduciary](/fiduciary-duty-explained-institutional/) fact, not a sales line.
