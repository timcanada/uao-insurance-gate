# Danantara (Indonesia), explained

Danantara Indonesia is **Badan Pengelola Investasi Daya Anagata Nusantara**, a strategic investment-management institution established under **Law No. 1 of 2025**. The official about page names CEO **Rosan Roeslani**. It does **not** publish an official AUM. The live UAO page’s **IDR 150 trillion / ~US$9.5 billion** line is a homemade conversion. Directorate General of State Assets told Parliament in **June 2026** that **no state property (BMN)** had been transferred to BPI Danantara. This is not investment advice.

**Last updated:** 26 August 2026 · **Desk:** UAO Research · **Canonical URL:** `/danantara-indonesia-sovereign-fund/`

The live page this overwrites says the fund was “formally operationalized in 2024,” prints **preliminary capital deployments exceeding 150 trillion rupiah (approximately USD 9.5 billion)**, invents **40–50% / 50–60%** geographic weights, and ranks Danantara against homemade GIC **$1.3 trillion** and Khazanah **$50 billion**. [GIC](/gic-singapore-explained/) does **not** publish AUM. [Khazanah](/is-khazanah-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/) official is **RM105 billion NAV**. Do not mint `/danantara-explained/` or an INA synonym. Indonesia Investment Authority is a **different** legal person. There is no live short twin in `twins.csv`.

The about page’s vision line: “to become the best sovereign fund in the world” through job creation. That is **their** branding, not an AUM.

Two pillars named on the homepage: **Danantara Asset Management** (SOEs) and **Danantara Investment Management** (national-priority investing). Do not invent a NAV for either sleeve.

DJKN’s Plt Director General, answering whether BMN had been handed to BPI Danantara since formation on **24 February 2025**: **not through PMN, and no BMN transferred.** That is the June 2026 official fact that kills any “SOE assets already sit on Danantara’s balance sheet” sentence — including the live page’s Jasa Marga / Pelindo exhibit.

## What is not official — do not put it back

- **IDR 150 trillion / ~US$9.5 billion.** Homemade FX. Not on danantaraindonesia.co.id.
- **US$900 billion.** Wikipedia and press “ecosystem” talk. The official widget is **US$0B**. Refuse it the way we refuse tracker AUM for [ADIA](/adia-abu-dhabi-investment-authority/) and [GIC](/gic-singapore-explained/).
- **2024 launch.** The statute they cite is **Law 1/2025**.
- **40–50% domestic / 25–35% fixed income** “indicative allocations.”
- Homemade GIC, Khazanah, Brunei SGRF, or Mumtalakat dollar AUM.
- A 2030 “$25–30 billion” projection.

Until Danantara or the Ministry of Finance prints a dated total, the honest line is **no official AUM**.

## How a peer should read it

- **Not INA.** Indonesia Investment Authority is the other sovereign vehicle. Do not add the two.
- **Not a funded savings SWF until they print a book.** Law + CEO + “no BMN transferred” are the three facts.
- **$9.5bn is dead. $900bn is not a filing.**
