# SWF capital by city: no official $1.4 trillion Singapore stack

Sovereign desks sit in cities. That is real. A stacked city AUM is not. The live page, dated 4 July 2026, still says Singapore hosts Temasek and GIC with **combined AUM exceeding $1.4 trillion**, Oslo’s [GPFG](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/) at **$1.3 trillion** then **$1.4 trillion**, [ADIA](/adia-abu-dhabi-investment-authority/) at **roughly $950 billion**, Temasek at **near $575 billion**, and GIC at **approximately $880 billion**. Those are tracker leftovers. They are not the filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## Official numbers (do not stack them)

| House / city | Official print | What is not official |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Combined “Singapore SWF AUM” | GIC and Temasek are **different legal persons**. No official combined AUM | **$1.4 trillion** |
| GIC Private Limited (Singapore) | No official AUM. 20-year real **3.4%** / USD nominal **5.6%** to **31 March 2026** | **$880 billion** |
| Temasek Holdings (Singapore) | **S$518 billion (US$401 billion)** at **31 March 2026**. Government is the **sole** equity shareholder | **$575 billion** |
| Government Pension Fund Global (Oslo) | **22,683 billion kroner** at **30 June 2026** | **$1.3 trillion**; **$1.4 trillion** |
| Abu Dhabi Investment Authority (Abu Dhabi) | No official AUM. Founded **1976** | **$950 billion** |
| Combined “Abu Dhabi / Beijing / Wellington trillions” | Different legal persons | Homemade **trillions collectively** |

Print kroner for GPFG. Print Singapore dollars for Temasek. Print “no official AUM” for GIC and ADIA. The Singapore split is already the job of [`/singapore-investment-landscape-gic-temasek-mas/`](/singapore-investment-landscape-gic-temasek-mas/). This URL is the **city** job. **Do not 301 onto that keeper.**

MAS official foreign reserves are a **central-bank** book in Singapore, not a third SWF you can add to GIC and Temasek. Do not invent a city pile so Oslo, Abu Dhabi, and Singapore can be ranked.

## What the page can say

- **Where the desk sits.** NBIM is in Oslo. GIC and Temasek are in Singapore. ADIA is in Abu Dhabi. That is an address fact.
- **What the city does not publish.** No official “SWF capital by city” AUM table exists. A headquarters is not a stacked NAV.
- **How to print scale.** Quote the house or say no AUM. Do not add GIC + Temasek into **$1.4 trillion**.
