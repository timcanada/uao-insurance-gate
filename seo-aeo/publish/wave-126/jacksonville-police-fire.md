# Jacksonville Police and Fire Pension Fund, explained

The City of Jacksonville Police and Fire Pension Fund is Jacksonville’s sworn police-and-fire pension, not leftover Houston fire, leftover Houston police, leftover San José Police and Fire, leftover San Antonio Fire and Police, and not leftover LAFPP. The official [2025 combined audited financial statements](https://www.jacksonville.gov/getContentAsset/bc569a3a-75ee-4f3e-8569-7c566b94c04c/135b97c9-84fa-4e82-b956-0fbccec4aa1f/PFPF-FYE-2025-Audit-Final.pdf?language=en) print combined fiduciary net position of **$2,903,990,005** at **30 September 2025**. Investments at fair value were **$3,099,469,799**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return was **10.47 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. GASB fiduciary net position **$2,899,657,985** is not the combined statement. Fiduciary net position was **46.56 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Houston fire overwrite, a leftover Houston police overwrite, a leftover San José Police and Fire overwrite, a leftover San Antonio overwrite, or a leftover LAFPP overwrite.** Do not mint `/jacksonville-pfpf/` or `/jax-pfpf/`. Do not treat the combining Plan column as the combined book.

## What is official (30 September 2025)

The combined statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Combined fiduciary net position** **$2,903,990,005** at **30 September 2025** (2024 **$2,616,112,545**). Combining statements split that book as Plan **$2,899,968,999** plus Senior Staff Voluntary Retirement Plan (SSVRP) **$4,021,006**. Investments at fair value **$3,099,469,799** is not fiduciary net position. Those prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover Houston Firefighters, leftover Houston Police, leftover San José Police and Fire combined **$5,966,077 thousand**, leftover San Antonio Fire and Police **$4,493,776 thousand**, leftover LAFPP combined **$34,110,601,276**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$6,227,658,596**. GASB plan fiduciary net position **$2,899,657,985**. City net pension liability **$3,328,000,611**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **46.56 percent**. The GASB fiduciary line is not the combined statement **$2,903,990,005** and is not the combining Plan column **$2,899,968,999**.
3. **Returns.** Money-weighted return **10.47 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. The house audit does not print a time-weighted return. Do not invent one.

Do not flatten Jacksonville Police and Fire into [Houston Firefighters](/houston-hfrrf/), [Houston Police](/houston-hpops/), [San José Police and Fire](/san-jose-police-fire/), [San Antonio Fire and Police](/san-antonio-fire-police/), or [Los Angeles Fire and Police Pensions](/lafpp/). Those are different legal persons. This is **one Jacksonville sworn police-and-fire file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Jacksonville Police and Fire AUM” has to name the book. The **$2,903,990,005** line is combined fiduciary net position. The **$2,899,968,999** line is the Plan column. The **$4,021,006** line is SSVRP. The **$3,099,469,799** line is investments. The **$2,899,657,985** line is the GASB fiduciary card. Those are not the same mandate.
