# Qatar Investment Authority (QIA), explained

The Qatar Investment Authority is the State of Qatar’s sovereign wealth fund. It was established by **Amiri Decision No. 22 of 2005**. **Amiri Decision No. 34 of 16 May 2023** reorganized it. It is accountable to the **Supreme Council for Economic Affairs and Investment**, chaired by the Amir. The CEO named on QIA’s history page is **Mohammed Saif Al-Sowaidi**. QIA’s own site and the IFSWF profile **do not publish assets under management**. The live UAO page’s **$510–600 billion** line is a tracker estimate, not a QIA filing. This is not investment advice.

QIA’s history page also prints a **2020** line: no new investments in fossil fuels (announced at the World Economic Forum); a new asset-allocation approach and long-term investment plan. IFSWF repeats the hydrocarbons stop as **January 2020**, plus **50%** of power-generation investments at zero carbon and renewables at **46%** of infrastructure power-generation assets. Those are **not** an AUM. Do not add them to a homemade dollar size.

IFSWF lists sectors (TMT, healthcare, retail and consumer, real estate, infrastructure, financial institutions, industrials and materials, liquid securities and funds) and asset classes. QIA’s own portfolio nav matches the sector list. Those are **labels, not weights**. Do not invent sleeve percentages.

New York advisory office: **2015**, on the History “period of expansion” line. One Planet SWF Group: QIA names itself one of six founding members (**2015–2016** on the timeline).

## What is not official — do not put it back

- **$510–600 billion** on this URL, **US$500–600 billion** on `/qatar-investment-authority/`, and **$450 billion** plus a fake **2013** founding on `/qatar-investment-authority-portfolio/`. Tracker ranges. Not on qia.qa.
- Any other homemade dollar size, including a single-point “about $550 billion.”
- A global **rank** (“one of the most influential,” “top ten”). Rank requires a published size.
- Homemade sleeve weights built from the sector list.
- Adding QIA to [ADIA](/adia-abu-dhabi-investment-authority/) or [PIF](/public-investment-fund-pif/) on a homemade dollar table. PIF’s official line is “more than US$900 billion,” not a tracker $2 trillion. ADIA does not publish AUM.
- Minting `/qia-explained/` or `/qatar-investment-authority-qia-portfolio/`.

## How a peer should read it

QIA is a Gulf savings-and-stabilisation SWF with a 2005 statute, a 2023 constitutional update, and a board line that runs to the Amir. **No public AUM is the disclosure.** Anyone who needs a dollar size is reading a consultancy, not QIA. Internal accountability is the Supreme Council. That is not a licence for a journalist to invent **$510–600 billion**.
