# El Paso Policemen Division, explained

The Policemen Division of the El Paso Firemen and Policemen’s Pension Fund is El Paso’s sworn police book, not leftover City of El Paso Employees Retirement Trust and not leftover Firemen Division. The official [31 December 2025 audited financial statements](https://www.elpasofireandpolice.org/index.php/about/financial-reports-2/audits/1139-2025-audited-financial-statements/file) print Policemen Division fiduciary net position of **$1,214,553,790** at **31 December 2025**. Combining investments were **$1,286,019,867**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Combined Fund money-weighted return was **16.11 percent** net of investment expense. Policemen fiduciary net position was **83.55 percent** of total pension liability. Combined **83.69 percent** is not this card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
