# Largest family offices in the world: no official AUM league table

There is no official league table of family-office AUM. The houses most often named in press rankings — Walton, Albrecht, Thomson, and the rest — do **not** publish an official family-office AUM that this desk can cite. The live page, dated 4 July 2026, invents a **$4–8 trillion** industry pile, assigns **Walton $90 billion**, **Albrecht $50 billion**, and **Thomson $60 billion**, and treats **Mubadala** as a family office. Mubadala is a sovereign investor. Those dollars are not filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

A family office is a private investment and administrative vehicle for one family or a small group of families. That is a legal and operating form. It is not a public AUM category. Universal owners who *are* official — GPIF, CalPERS, NBIM, PIF, Mubadala — publish (or refuse) numbers under their own statutes. Family offices generally do not.

## What this page can say

- **What a family office is.** A private office that invests and administers wealth for a family. Single-family and multi-family are operating types, not official AUM bands.
- **Why league tables fail.** The offices most often ranked do not file a public AUM. Consultant and press dollars are not interchangeable with a GPIF yen print or a CalPERS PERF dollar.
- **What is not a family office.** Mubadala Investment Company is a sovereign wealth / development investor of the Government of Abu Dhabi. Putting it on a family-office list is a category error.
- **Where official numbers live instead.** For sovereign and pension houses, quote the filing. For family offices, say the office does not publish AUM.

## What is not official

- **$4–8 trillion** of family-office capital. Live lede. No official combined figure.
- **Walton $90 billion, Albrecht $50 billion, Thomson $60 billion.** Live table. Those families have not published those lines as official FO AUM.
- **Mubadala as a family office.** Category error. Official print: AED 1.4 trillion / US$385 billion at year-end 2025.
- Any "top 10 family offices by AUM" ranking built from press estimates.
