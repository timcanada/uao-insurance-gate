# Oman Investment Authority (OIA), explained

The Oman Investment Authority is Oman’s sovereign wealth fund. Official 2025 results, as carried by the Foreign Ministry on 19 May 2026: profit of **OMR 2.9 billion**, a **14.6%** return on investment, and total assets of **around OMR 23 billion**. OIA’s own English release of 18 May 2026 also prints **approximately USD 60 billion** of assets. The book is **three portfolios, not two**. This is not investment advice.

**Last updated:** 26 August 2026 · **Desk:** UAO Research · **Canonical URL:** `/oman-investment-authority-explained/`

Use **OMR 23 billion** as the official size. Use **USD 60 billion** only as OIA’s own English rounding of the same year, not as a second filing.

The 10.4% five-year average and the “third globally” rank are attributed by the Foreign Ministry to **SWF Global**. That is a named third-party ranking, not a UAO league table. Do not turn it into “UAO ranks OIA third.”

## Three books, not two

Official structure at end-2025:

1. **National Development Portfolio** — state-owned companies and domestic assets; more than **160** local companies; ~OMR 13.09 billion. Developmental *and* financial. This is not named “National Development Fund” on the Foreign Ministry page.
2. **Generations Portfolio** — investments **outside** Oman; OMR 8.57 billion; 210 funds. This is not named “Future Generations Fund.”
3. **Future Fund Oman** — domestic project / venture book with **OMR 2 billion** of capital; 186 approved projects (~OMR 1.7 billion estimated value); ~OMR 640 million invested; ~OMR 743 million of attracted foreign investment; 986 applications since launch.

Geography, as the Foreign Ministry prints it: Oman **nearly two-thirds**; North America **19%**; Europe **9%**; Asia and Pacific Rim **4%**; rest of world **7%**. Across **52** countries.

Divestment programme (launched 2022): **24** transactions by end-2025, returns **exceeding OMR 2.8 billion**. Six exits in 2025, including a **20%** IPO of Asyad Shipping.

Chairman: **Sultan bin Salem Al Habsi**, Minister of Finance. President: **Abdulsalam bin Mohammed Al Murshidi**. OIA staff **438** (Omanisation **91%**); subsidiaries **more than 41,000** staff (Omanisation **79.4%**).

OIA was assembled in **2020** from the State General Reserve Fund and the Oman Investment Fund. That part of the live page is fine. The invented fund *names* are not.

## How a peer should read it

- **Do not call Future Fund Oman the overseas book.** The overseas book is the **Generations Portfolio**. Future Fund Oman is a domestic project vehicle.
- **OMR first.** OIA’s USD 60 billion is their English rounding of “around OMR 23 billion.”
- **Dual mandate is the product.** Two-thirds at home is not a rounding error. It is the job.
- **14.6% is their ROI print**, not a UAO-calculated time-weighted return from a public NAV series.
