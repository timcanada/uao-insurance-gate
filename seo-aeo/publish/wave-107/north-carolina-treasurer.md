# North Carolina Department of State Treasurer, explained

The North Carolina Department of State Treasurer is the statutory investment office for the North Carolina Retirement Systems, not a second CalPERS page and not leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion**. The official [quarterly investment report for the period ending 30 June 2025](https://www.nctreasurer.gov/documents/files/imdinvestmentreports/quarterly-investment-report-qir-2025q2/open) prints Pension Plan assets of **$134,096,647 thousand** (**$134.1 billion**) at **30 June 2025**. The one-year Pension Plan return was **9.94 percent** net. The audited External Investment Pool net position held in trust is **$179,136,588 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second CalPERS overwrite or a second Oregon PERS overwrite.** Those keepers are `/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/` and `/oregon-pers/`. Do not mint `/ncrs/`, `/north-carolina-retirement-systems/`, or `/north-carolina-treasurer-explained/`. Do not add the External Investment Pool to the Pension Plan and call the sum North Carolina’s pension.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The Treasurer’s own reports are the sourced split (dollar amounts in thousands unless named as billions):

1. **Pension Plan market value** **$134,096,647 thousand** at **30 June 2025**, up from **$127.0 billion** at **31 March 2025**. The later official quarterly prints **$139.2 billion** at **30 September 2025**. That September line is a later date. Do not treat leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **Pension Plan returns, net, at 30 June 2025.** One year **9.94 percent**. Three years **7.79 percent**. Five years **6.71 percent**. Ten years **6.31 percent**. Implementation benchmark one year **9.48 percent**. Assumed actuarial return **6.5 percent** (lowered from **7.0 percent** in January 2021).
3. **What is not the Pension Plan.** Audited External Investment Pool net position held in trust **$179,136,588 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$166,678,013 thousand**). Pool investments at fair value **$177,963,918 thousand**. That pool holds retirement assets and other state books, including the Short-Term Investment Fund. Bond Index Investment Pool net position **$1,684,832 thousand**. Equity Index Investment Account net position **$1,079,844 thousand**. Do not add those three and call the sum the Pension Plan.

Do not flatten the Treasurer into [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/) or [Oregon PERS](/oregon-pers/). CalPERS is California. Oregon PERS is one Oregon public-employee file. This is **one North Carolina Treasurer file**.

Counsel selling “North Carolina pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$134.1 billion** line is the Pension Plan. The **$179.1 billion** line is the External Investment Pool. Those are not the same mandate.
