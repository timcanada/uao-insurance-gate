# New Hampshire Retirement System, explained

The New Hampshire Retirement System is New Hampshire’s statewide pension-and-OPEB administrator, not leftover Massachusetts PRIM and not leftover Iowa IPERS. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.nhrs.org/docs/default-source/cafr/2025-annual-comprehensive-financial-report.pdf) prints combined restricted net position of **$13,447,973 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. The Pension Plan is **$13,393,284 thousand**. The OPEB Plan is **$54,689 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted and money-weighted returns both printed **10.3 percent** net. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Massachusetts PRIM overwrite or a second Iowa IPERS page.** Do not mint `/nhrs/`, `/new-hampshire-nhrs/`, or `/new-hampshire-retirement-system-explained/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combining statements are in **thousands**. The sourced split is:

1. **Combined restricted net position** **$13,447,973 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$12,340,524 thousand**). Pension Plan **$13,393,284 thousand**. OPEB Plan **$54,689 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The MD&A’s **$13,448.0 million** print is the same combined book, rounded. Do not treat leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, leftover Iowa IPERS **$46,740,552,174**, or leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion** as this filing.
2. **Investments versus the fiduciary book.** Total investments **$12,947,572 thousand**. The chair letter’s “investments held in trust were **$13.3 billion**” line is not fiduciary net position. Do not add the OPEB book to the pension book a second time and call the sum a second AUM.
3. **Returns and cash flows.** Time-weighted total-fund return **10.3 percent** net of fees. Money-weighted return **10.3 percent** net of investment expenses. The filing printed the same **10.3 percent** on both lines — do not invent a difference. The performance chart’s **10.31 percent** print is the same one-year net time-weighted line, unrounded. Net income from investment activities **$1,244,735 thousand**. Benefits paid **$1,067,981 thousand**.

Do not flatten NHRS into [Massachusetts PRIM](/massachusetts-prim/), [Iowa IPERS](/iowa-ipers/), [Employees’ Retirement System of Rhode Island](/rhode-island-ersri/), or [Employees’ Retirement System of the City of Providence](/providence-employees/). Massachusetts PRIM is the Massachusetts board book. ERSRI is the Rhode Island statewide file. Providence is a Rhode Island city file. This is **one New Hampshire statewide file**.
