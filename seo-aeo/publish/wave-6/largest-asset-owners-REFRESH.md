# The Largest Asset Owners in the World

The largest asset owners are the institutions that **own the capital** — sovereign wealth funds and public pensions — not the asset managers hired to run sleeves. Norway’s Government Pension Fund Global is the largest book that publishes a current official value: **22,683 billion kroner at 30 June 2026**. Japan’s GPIF is the largest dedicated public pension (**¥293,427.6 billion at 31 December 2025**). China’s CIC last reported **US$1.57 trillion of total assets at 31 December 2024**. The Thinking Ahead Institute’s Asset Owner 100 put the top 100 at about **US$29.3 trillion at end-2024**. This is not investment advice.

**Last updated:** 26 August 2026 · **Desk:** UAO Research · **Canonical URL:** `/largest-asset-owners-in-the-world/`

SWF-only table: [largest sovereign wealth funds](/largest-sovereign-wealth-funds/). Pension-only table: [largest pension funds](/largest-pension-funds-in-the-world/). This page is the **owner** list — both families, plus the owner-versus-manager distinction.

## Asset owner vs asset manager

An **asset owner** holds the money and the obligation. A pension owes members. A sovereign fund holds national wealth. An insurer must pay claims. A family office holds a family’s book.

An **asset manager** invests other people’s money for a fee. It does not own the capital and does not carry the liability.

Owners hire managers. The owner still sets the mandate, lives with the risk, and answers to beneficiaries or a government. League tables of *managers* (BlackRock, Fidelity, a sovereign’s external sleeve) answer a different question. See [asset owner vs asset manager](/asset-owner-vs-asset-manager-explained/).

Every [universal owner](/universal-owner/) is an asset owner. Not every asset owner is a universal owner. Scale and diversification are the tests.

## Unpublished on purpose

ADIA, KIA, QIA, GIC and SAFE sit in every tracker’s top ten. They do not give UAO an official AUM. Tracker graphics that print ADIA at “~$1.1 trillion” are someone else’s model. We will not launder them into a filing. GIC’s official score is the rolling 20-year real return (**3.4%** to 31 March 2026), not a size.

## Two families, one owner list

**Sovereign wealth funds** are state-owned, usually funded by commodities or surpluses, with few named beneficiaries. They have been the fastest-growing owner type on the AO100 (the 2025 study: SWF assets +16.7% in 2024; pensions a smaller share of the top 100 than a decade earlier).

**Public pensions** owe members. GPIF, CPP Investments, NPS Korea, ABP, the US publics, and the Canadian provincial plans are this family. Norway’s GPFG has “pension” in the name and is still a SWF. That is why the [pension page](/largest-pension-funds-in-the-world/) and the [SWF page](/largest-sovereign-wealth-funds/) disagree about number one — they are supposed to.

Insurers, endowments, and large family offices are asset owners. They rarely match the sovereign/pension giants on a single-entity AUM print. Royal-family offices are a separate owner type; see [royal family investment offices](/royal-family-investment-offices/) once live.

## Why the dollar order moves

- **FX.** GPIF’s yen book can look smaller or larger than GPFG in dollars without a yen trade.
- **Dates.** NBIM’s June 2026 kroner figure is newer than CIC’s year-end 2024 annual report and newer than the AO100’s end-2024 cut.
- **Perimeter.** Include SAFE or GPIF and the top three change.
- **Inflows vs payouts.** CPP Investments grew C$78.9 billion in FY2026 (C$56.9 billion net income + C$22.0 billion net transfers). Many Western DB plans are now net payers. Gulf and Asian SWFs are still in accumulation.

## Why this list exists

These books are [universal owners](/universal-owner/) when they are large and diversified enough. They set the cost of capital in private markets and the voting maths in public ones. ## Frequently asked questions

**Who is the largest asset owner in the world?**  
On current official disclosures: **Norway’s GPFG** (22,683 billion kroner at 30 June 2026). On a beneficiary-pension definition, **GPIF** is the largest dedicated public pension.

**How much do the 100 largest control?**  
TAI AO100: about **US$29.3 trillion at end-2024**. Confirm the vintage before you put it in a slide.

**Is BlackRock an asset owner?**  
No. It is the largest *manager* of other people’s money. The owners are the pensions, sovereigns, insurers and individuals in the funds.

**Is CIC bigger than GPIF?**  
CIC’s last official total assets are US$1.57 trillion at 31 December 2024. GPIF’s official book is in yen. A dollar comparison without a dated FX rate is a magazine move.
