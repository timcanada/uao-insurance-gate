# Portland Fire and Police Disability and Retirement Funds, explained

The Portland Fire and Police Disability and Retirement Funds are Portland’s sworn pay-as-you-go disability and retirement trusts, not leftover Jacksonville Police and Fire, leftover Tampa Fire and Police, and not leftover Miami Fire and Police. The official [30 June 2025 Portland Fire and Police Disability and Retirement Funds financial statements](https://efiles.portlandoregon.gov/record/17529594/file/document) print combined plan net position of **$27,686,552** at **30 June 2025**. Cash and investments held by the City Treasurer **$36,964,795** are not that card. The Reserve Fund **$750,000** is a component, not a second AUM. Total pension liability **$3,363,287,824** is not AUM. GASB **0.82 percent** is of that liability because the Plan is pay-as-you-go. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
