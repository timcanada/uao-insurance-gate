# Denison Firemen’s Relief and Retirement Fund, explained

The Denison Firemen’s Relief and Retirement Fund is the City of Denison firefighter pension, a Texas Local Fire Fighters’ Retirement Act book, not leftover TMRS, leftover Texas ERS, leftover Houston Fire, leftover Texas TESRS, leftover Corpus Christi Fire, leftover McAllen Fire, and not leftover MassPRIM. The official [financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2025](https://www.cityofdenison.com/DocumentCenter/View/4111/Denison-FRRF-2025-Audit?bidId=) print plan net position restricted for pension benefits of **$26,731,832** at **31 December 2025**. Investments **$26,392,120** are not that card. RSI money-weighted return was **11.60 percent**. MD&A **12.69 percent** is not the RSI card. GASB **79.4 percent** is the notes funded card.
