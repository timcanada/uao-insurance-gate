# Notre Dame endowment, explained

The University of Notre Dame endowment is Notre Dame’s long-horizon book, not leftover Harvard **$56.9 billion**, leftover Yale **$44.1 billion**, leftover MIT **$27,366.2 million**. The official [30 June 2025 consolidated financial statements](https://treasury.nd.edu/assets/638748/fy25_financial_statements_11_14_25_for_upload_to_website_.pdf) print Notre Dame Endowment Pool assets of **$25,434,616 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. University endowment and funds functioning as endowment were **$20,072,670 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The official [fiscal 2025 annual report](https://finance.nd.edu/assets/645415/annual_report_2025_web.pdf) prints an Endowment Pool return of **13.6 percent**. Payout was **$655 million**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The financial statements are in **thousands**.

1. **Notre Dame Endowment Pool assets** **$25,434,616 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. University endowment and funds functioning as endowment **$20,072,670 thousand**. Working capital and other University designations **$2,476,962 thousand**. Funds invested on behalf of religious affiliates **$2,372,371 thousand**. Those four lines sum to the pool. Do not flatten the pool into the University FFE card. Do not treat leftover Harvard **$56.9 billion**, leftover Yale **$44.1 billion**, leftover Stanford endowment **$40.8 billion**, leftover Princeton **$36.4 billion**, leftover MIT **$27,366.2 million**, leftover UTIMCO Endowments **$67.6 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books are not interchangeable.** The Endowment Pool holds University endowment, working capital, and religious-affiliate capital. The **$20,072,670 thousand** line is the University endowment and funds functioning as endowment. That is not the whole pool.
3. **Returns and payout.** Official Endowment Pool return **13.6 percent** for fiscal 2025. Official payout distributed for spending **$655 million**. The financial-statement companion of that payout print is **$655,167 thousand**. Those two official prints are the same card, not a second AUM.
