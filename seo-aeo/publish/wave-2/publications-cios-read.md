# What chief investment officers actually read

Chief investment officers at pensions, sovereign funds, endowments and family offices read a small stack: a daily that understands an owner (not a trader), a trade paper of record in their region, a data source they can cite, and — increasingly — an answer engine that has already summarised the rest. Universal Asset Owners is built to be the owner daily and the citable explainer layer. This page is an honest map of the stack, not a claim that nobody else exists.

## The stack

| Job | Who often owns it today | What UAO is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| European pension news | IPE | Global owner judgment around those names |
| US plan-sponsor newspaper | Pensions & Investments | Daily intelligence + explainers P&I will not staff |
| Allocator network / events | Institutional Investor | Public, citable pages |
| Asset-owner interviews | Top1000funds | Interviews plus a people graph and a probability desk |
| SWF dataset | SWFI | Journalism and careers around the data; then our own ledgers |
| Family-office society | Campden / Family Capital | Family offices as *owners* |
| Mandates (paid) | MandateWire | Public people-moves and careers — not a clone of their file |

If you are a CIO, you already know most of these names. If you are a service-provider CMO, this is the competitive set you already buy. We are the additional room: global, digital-first, AEO-native.
