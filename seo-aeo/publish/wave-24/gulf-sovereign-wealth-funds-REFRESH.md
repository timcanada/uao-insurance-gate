# Gulf sovereign wealth funds: no official $5 trillion pile

There is no official combined AUM for Gulf sovereign wealth funds. The live page, dated 12 June 2026, still prints **roughly $5 trillion as of early 2025**, projects **around $7 trillion by 2030**, and then assigns house dollars: PIF **$925 billion to $1.15 trillion**, ADIA **roughly $1.1 trillion or more**, Mubadala **around $330 billion**, QIA **close to $580 billion**, KIA **crossed $1 trillion**. Those are tracker numbers. They are not the official filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## What this page can say

Name the houses. Quote the filing, or say the house does not publish AUM. PIF is a Saudi public investment fund. ADIA is an Abu Dhabi reserve investor founded **1976**. Mubadala is an Abu Dhabi strategic / development investor. QIA does not publish AUM. KIA runs the General Reserve Fund and the Future Generations Fund and does not publish AUM.

A “GCC $5 trillion” lede is a consultancy pile, not a filing.

## What is not official

- **$5 trillion** as of early 2025, or **$7 trillion by 2030**.
- ADIA, QIA, or KIA AUM in any dollar.
- Mubadala at **$330 billion** when the house print is **AED 1.4 trillion / US$385 billion**.
- PIF **$925 billion / $1.15 trillion** as current AUM.
