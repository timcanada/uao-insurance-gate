# City of Kansas City, Missouri Firefighters’ Pension System, explained

The City of Kansas City, Missouri Firefighters’ Pension System is Kansas City’s firefighter pension, not leftover Kansas City Employees (**$1,244,770,842**), leftover Kansas City Police (**$1,028,622,529**), leftover St. Louis Firemen. The official [financial statements as of 30 April 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20260827165011/https://www.kcmo.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/15686/638965488954000000) print net position restricted for pensions of **$678,265,671** at **30 April 2025**. Health-care subsidy **$5,872,199** and total net position **$684,137,870** are not that card. Notes and RSI money-weighted return was **7.49 percent**. Notes GASB **68.44 percent** is of the pension book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

2. **GASB cards.** Notes total pension liability **$991,020,901**. Net pension liability **$312,755,230**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **68.44 percent**.
