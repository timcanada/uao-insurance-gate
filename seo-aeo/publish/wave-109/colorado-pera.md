# Colorado Public Employees' Retirement Association, explained

The Colorado Public Employees’ Retirement Association is Colorado’s public-employee pension, not a second CalPERS page and not leftover PERA **$67 billion**. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://content.copera.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2025-Annual-Comprehensive-Financial-Report.pdf) prints Combined fiduciary net position of **$82,953,131 thousand** (**$83.0 billion**) at **31 December 2025**. The Combined Investment Fund is **$75.1 billion**. The Capital Accumulation Plans are **$7.6 billion**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The Combined Investment Fund time-weighted return was **14.1 percent** net of external management and custody fees. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The combining statement is the sourced split (dollar amounts in thousands unless named in billions by the house):

1. **Combined fiduciary net position** **$82,953,131 thousand** at **31 December 2025** (2024: **$73,677,448 thousand**). That total covers the five Division Trust Funds, the two Health Care Trust Funds, the Life Insurance Reserve, the Direct Distribution Reserve, and the Capital Accumulation Plans. Do not treat leftover Colorado PERA **$67 billion**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books on the same report.** Combined Investment Fund **$75.1 billion**. Capital Accumulation Plans **$7.6 billion**. GASB Division Trust Fund fiduciary net position **$73,775,340 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. Do not add the Combined Investment Fund to the Capital Accumulation Plans a second time and call the sum a second AUM.
3. **Returns, funding, and net pension liability.** Combined Investment Fund time-weighted return **14.1 percent** net of external management and custody fees. Three-, five-, ten-, and thirty-year net returns **12.7 percent**, **7.6 percent**, **9.5 percent**, and **8.1 percent**. Funding-valuation funded ratio for the five defined-benefit pension trust funds **69.1 percent**. Unfunded actuarial accrued liability **$30.1 billion**. GASB net pension liability **$24.2 billion**. Those funding and GASB lines are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Colorado PERA into [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/) or [Illinois ISBI](/illinois-isbi/). CalPERS is California. ISBI is an Illinois investment pool. This is **one Colorado public-employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Colorado pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$83.0 billion** line is Combined fiduciary net position. The **$75.1 billion** line is the Combined Investment Fund. The **$7.6 billion** line is the Capital Accumulation Plans. Those are not the same mandate.
