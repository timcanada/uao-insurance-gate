# Sacramento County Employees’ Retirement System, explained

The Sacramento County Employees’ Retirement System is Sacramento County’s employee pension, not leftover SDCERS and not leftover OCERS. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.scers.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/FY24-25_ACFR.pdf) prints net position restricted for pension benefits and program administration of **$14,577,459 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Investments were **$14,323,091 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted return was **10.8 percent** net of investment management fees. The money-weighted return was **11.10 percent**. Fiduciary net position was **93.5 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover SDCERS overwrite or a leftover OCERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/scers/`, `/sacramento-scers-explained/`, or `/sacramento-county-employees/`. Do not mint `/sdcers-explained/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Net position restricted for pension benefits and program administration** **$14,577,459 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (restated 2024 **$13,309,644 thousand**). The pension trust prints that entire book. The custodial fund prints a zero fiduciary net position. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Investments **$14,323,091 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$15,338,995 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. The MD&A investments card **$14,547.4 million** is not the statement investments line. The popular-report **$14.6 billion** card is the same fiduciary book, rounded. Do not treat leftover SDCERS, leftover OCERS, leftover SamCERA, leftover LACERA, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, or leftover SFERS as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$15,597,205 thousand**. Net pension liability **$1,019,746 thousand**. Fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **93.5 percent**. Those three cards are not a second investment book.
3. **Returns.** Time-weighted total-fund return **10.8 percent** net of investment management fees, against the house policy benchmark **9.9 percent** and the **6.75 percent** actuarial objective. Annual money-weighted return **11.10 percent** net of pension-plan investment expenses. Those two official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Sacramento County into [Sacramento City Employees](/sacramento-city-employees/), [SDCERS](/sdcers/), [OCERS](/ocers/), [SamCERA](/samcera/), [LACERA](/lacera/), [Sonoma SCERA](/sonoma-scera/), [ICERS](/icers/), or [MercedCERA](/mercedcera/). Sacramento City Employees is the **closed city** file. SDCERS is the San Diego **city** file. Sonoma SCERA is a different 1937-Act file — do not mint `/scera/` or reuse `/scers/` for Sonoma. This is **one Sacramento County employee file**. Seattle City Employees’ Retirement System is a different legal person — that is why `/scers/` stays unminted.
