# Uniform Retirement System for Justices and Judges, explained

The Uniform Retirement System for Justices and Judges is Oklahoma’s judicial pension, administered by Oklahoma OPERS and not leftover Oklahoma OPERS, leftover Oklahoma Firefighters, and not leftover Oklahoma Police. The official [2025 Uniform Retirement System for Justices and Judges Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.opers.ok.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2025_ACFR_URSJJ-Final.pdf) prints pension-plan fiduciary net position of **$447,697,292** at **30 June 2025**. Combined fiduciary net position **$452,582,675** mixes in the Health Insurance Subsidy Plan. Pension-plan investments **$463,535,215** are not the net-position card. Money-weighted return was **12.86 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. MD&A **12.9 percent** is not that card. GASB **112.34 percent** is a net pension asset card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statements of fiduciary net position, the GASB 67 note, and the money-weighted note are the sourced lines:

1. **Pension-plan fiduciary net position** **$447,697,292** at **30 June 2025** (beginning **$409,923,456**). Pension-plan investments **$463,535,215**. Combined investments **$468,585,817** include the Health Insurance Subsidy Plan and securities-lending collateral **$9,466,951**. Combined fiduciary net position **$452,582,675** (MD&A **$452.6 million**) is not the pension card. Health Insurance Subsidy Plan fiduciary net position **$4,885,383** is not the pension card. Do not treat leftover Oklahoma OPERS Combined **$13,351,556,235**, leftover Oklahoma Firefighters **$3,696,332,045**, leftover Oklahoma Police **$3,449,729 thousand**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$398,507,106**. Employer’s net pension asset **$49,190,186**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of the total pension liability **112.34 percent**. Health Insurance Subsidy Plan GASB **151.22 percent** is not the pension card.
3. **Returns.** Notes pension-plan money-weighted return **12.86 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **12.81 percent**). Health Insurance Subsidy Plan money-weighted return **9.50 percent** is not the pension card. MD&A **12.9 percent** is not the notes card.

Do not flatten the Uniform Retirement System for Justices and Judges into [Oklahoma OPERS](/oklahoma-opers/), [Oklahoma Firefighters Pension and Retirement System](/oklahoma-firefighters/), [Oklahoma Police Pension and Retirement System](/oklahoma-police/), or [Oklahoma TRS](/oklahoma-trs/). Oklahoma OPERS is the public-employee file. Oklahoma Firefighters and Oklahoma Police are different statewide sworn books. Oklahoma TRS is the teacher file. This is **one Oklahoma judicial file**.
