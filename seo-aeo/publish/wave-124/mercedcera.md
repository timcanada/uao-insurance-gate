# Merced County Employees’ Retirement Association, explained

The Merced County Employees’ Retirement Association is Merced County’s 1937-Act employee pension, not leftover StanCERA and not leftover Marin MCERA. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.mercedcera.com/sites/main/files/file-attachments/open_item_b2_-_mercedcera_acfr_2025_-_final.pdf?1769699823) prints fiduciary net position restricted for pensions of **$1,378,701,813** at **30 June 2025**. Investments at fair value were **$1,344,386,478**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The money-weighted return was **10.8 percent**. Fiduciary net position was **83.49 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Fiduciary net position restricted for pensions** **$1,378,701,813** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$1,253,752,672**), up **$124,949,141** / **10.0 percent**. Investments at fair value **$1,344,386,478** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$1,382,730,795** is not fiduciary net position. The house **$1.379 billion** card is the fiduciary line rounded. Those three prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover StanCERA, leftover SJCERA, leftover Marin MCERA, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$1,651,356,195**. Net pension liability **$272,654,382**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **83.49 percent**. Those three lines are not interchangeable.
3. **Returns and actuarial card.** Gross-of-fees investment return **11.6 percent** (Meketa) is not the money-weighted return. Money-weighted return **10.8 percent** (2024 **12.1 percent**). Actuarial funded **77.6 percent** as of the **30 June 2024** valuation is not the 2025 GASB **83.49 percent** card. Those official prints are not interchangeable.
