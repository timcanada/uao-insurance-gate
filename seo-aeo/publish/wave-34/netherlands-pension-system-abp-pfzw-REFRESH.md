# Dutch pensions: ABP is €530 billion, PFZW is €252 billion — not €550bn+ / €200bn+

The Netherlands has large sector funds and a 2027 contract change. That architecture is real. The live euro table is not. The live page, dated 3 July 2026, still assigns [ABP](/abp-netherlands-pension-fund-explained/) **€550bn+** and [PFZW](/pfzw-netherlands-pension-fund-explained/) **€200bn+**, then reprints PFZW at **roughly €100 billion**, stacks them at **over €500 billion**, and uses the wrong PFZW legal name (“Pensioenfonds Ziekenhuizen en Welzijnsinstellingen”). Those leftovers are not the current filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## What the page can say

- **Two houses.** ABP is the public-sector and education book. PFZW is healthcare and welfare. APG manages for ABP. PGGM administers for PFZW.
- **The 2027 change.** Dutch funds are moving onto a new contract. That is a statute fact. It is not a licence to invent **€550bn+**.
- **How to print scale.** Quote each filing. A page that needs **€550bn+ / €200bn+** is a leftover page.

## What is not official

- Combined Dutch occupational **€500 billion**.
- ABP **€550bn+**, or live institution **€532 billion** as the current print.
- PFZW **€200bn+** / **€100 billion** / leftover **€35 billion**.
- The legal name “Pensioenfonds Ziekenhuizen en Welzijnsinstellingen.”
