# Audience flywheel

Two rooms. One home. If we write only for CIOs, advertisers never feel invited. If we write only for vendors, CIOs leave.

## Room 1 — Prospective readers and members

### Who

- Chief investment officers and deputies
- Heads of asset allocation / total portfolio
- Heads of private markets, infrastructure, real assets, private credit
- Chief risk officers and heads of stewardship
- Investment committee chairs and staff
- Family-office principals, CIOs, and next-gen
- Sovereign investment officers and strategy staff
- Corporate pension and insurer CIOs
- A small number of serious academics and policymakers

### Why they come (jobs to be done)

| Job | Page type | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understand a force that hits the whole book | Daily brief / Probability Desk | Email habit |
| Look up an institution or a person | Registry / Allocator Class | Bookmark + signup |
| Learn a term without being patronized | AEO explainer | Internal links into desks |
| Hire or get hired | Careers | Alert / Talent Desk |
| Move city for a mandate | Relocation | Brief + careers |
| Prepare an IC pre-read | Research / membership sample | Request access |

### Conversion ladder

1. Work-email brief (already reviewed by the desk — keep that; it is the moat).
2. Open rate → careers snippet or Probability Desk click.
3. Sample research / procurement brief.
4. Institutional Core ($60k) or Intelligence ($100k).
5. Strategic Council / Sovereign for the desks that want private work.

SEO/AEO’s job is step 1 and the return visit. Membership copy already exists on `/membership/`. Do not invent a consumer freemium.

### Global nuance (do not flatten)

- **Gulf:** nation-building mandates, relocation, confidentiality, in-house deal teams.
- **Canada:** pension-model prestige, London/NY/HK offices, stewardship votes.
- **US public plans:** board politics, funded status, legislative risk.
- **Europe:** IORP, SFDR, buyout/PRT, real assets.
- **Asia:** GIC/Temasek/GPIF/NPS and the family-office buildout in Singapore/HK.
- **LatAm / Africa:** development funds, FX, political horizon — treat as first-class, not “frontier colour.”

## Room 2 — Prospective advertisers

### Who buys

Managing partners and CMOs at:

- Fiduciary, fund, sanctions, and employment law firms
- Big Four and specialist asset-owner tax/audit
- Family-office and institutional technology
- Investment banks, placement agents, secondaries advisers
- Investment consultants and OCIOs
- Executive search (already spoken to on `/careers/`)
- Custodians, administrators, data terminals

### Why they pay $20–50k/month

Because one introduction or one retained search pays for a year of IPE. They need:

1. Proof the room is real (`/readership/` audit under NDA).
2. A product they can brief their partnership on (packages, not “let’s hop on a call”).
3. Adjacent content their own clients already read (the Wave 1 hubs).
4. Separation from editorial so they do not fear a scandal.

### Conversion ladder

1. Land on a hub written in owner language (“How a pension actually retains counsel”).
2. See labelled examples of what a presence looks like.
3. Book 15-minute call / request media kit.
4. Desk or Category insertion order.
5. Expand to Platform (careers + directory + briefing).

## Shared utilities (the sticklers)

These serve both rooms and should never be buried:

- **Careers** — owners hire; search firms advertise; candidates become lifelong readers.
- **Leadership moves** — journalists, search, and peers all lurk here.
- **Directory** — owners discover firms; firms pay to be claimed.
- **Relocation** — the human story of a global industry; vendors (tax, immigration, schools, family office setup) sit beside it.

## Anti-goals

- Traffic from retail investors who will never be approved for the brief.
- Vendor-written “thought leadership” in the newsroom.
- A membership pitch on every paragraph.
