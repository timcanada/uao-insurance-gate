# El Paso Firemen and Policemen’s Pension Fund, explained

The El Paso Firemen and Policemen’s Pension Fund is El Paso’s sworn fire-and-police pension, not leftover City of El Paso Employees Retirement Trust and not leftover Houston fire or police. The official [31 December 2025 audited financial statements](https://www.elpasofireandpolice.org/index.php/about/financial-reports-2/audits/1139-2025-audited-financial-statements/file) print combined net position restricted for pension of **$2,070,958,626** at **31 December 2025**. Investments were **$2,177,021,668**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return was **16.11 percent** net of investment expense. Combined fiduciary net position was **83.69 percent** of total pension liability. MD&A about-**84 percent** is not that GASB card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

Civilian employees sit in leftover [City of El Paso Employees Retirement Trust](/el-paso-cert/). City of El Paso FY2025 ACFR Firemen and Policemen notes remain a **31 December 2024** measurement — this house audit is the later book.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The combined statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Combined net position restricted for pension** **$2,070,958,626** at **31 December 2025** (2024 **$1,862,177,603**). The change is **$208,781,023**. Combining Firemen Division **$854,452,647** plus Policemen Division **$1,214,553,790** plus Staff Plan **$1,952,189** equals that combined card. Total investments **$2,177,021,668** is not fiduciary net position. DROP payable **$101,981,398** is a liability, not a second AUM. Do not treat leftover City of El Paso Employees Retirement Trust **$1,060,641,134**, leftover Houston Firefighters **$5,799,107,368**, leftover San Antonio Fire and Police **$4,493,776 thousand**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Combined total pension liability **$2,474,585,986**. Combined net pension liability **$403,627,360**. Combined fiduciary net position / total pension liability **83.69 percent**. Firemen **83.84 percent** and Policemen **83.55 percent** are division cards. Staff Plan **113.05 percent** is a net-pension-asset print, not the sworn card. MD&A about-**84 percent** is not the **83.69 percent** card.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **16.11 percent** net of investment expense for the year ended **31 December 2025** (2024 notes **7.21 percent**). MD&A unlabeled 2024 return **7.42 percent** is not the notes money-weighted card. This filing does not print a separate official one-year time-weighted house card.
