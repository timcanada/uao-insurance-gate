# New Mexico Educational Retirement Board, explained

The New Mexico Educational Retirement Board is New Mexico’s educator pension, not leftover NMSIC and not leftover New Mexico PERA. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.erb.nm.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2025-Annual-Comprehensive-Financial-Report.pdf) prints net position restricted for pensions of **$18,960,350,984** at **30 June 2025**. Investments at fair value were **$18,506,229,186**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted return was **9.9 percent** net of fees. The money-weighted return was **9.88 percent**. Fiduciary net position was **67.8 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover NMSIC overwrite or a New Mexico PERA page.** The PERA house file is [New Mexico PERA](/new-mexico-pera/). Do not mint `/nmerb/`, `/new-mexico-erb-explained/`, or `/new-mexico-educational-retirement/`. Do not mint `/nmsic/`. Do not mint `/pera/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position restricted for pensions** **$18,960,350,984** at **30 June 2025** (MD&A beginning **$17,498 million**; increase **$1,462 million** after a GASB 101 prior-period restatement of **$(1) million**). Investments at fair value **$18,506,229,186** is not fiduciary net position. The MD&A **$18,960 million** card is the same book, rounded. Do not treat leftover NMSIC, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion**, or leftover Ohio STRS **$80 billion+** / **$90 billion** / **$350 billion** as this filing.
2. **Cash flows on the same report.** Employer contributions **$802,437,237**. Member contributions **$453,872,084**. Total contributions **$1,259,132,808**. Net investment income **$1,732 million** on the MD&A card. Benefits and withdrawals **$1,515 million**.
3. **Returns and funded status.** Time-weighted return **9.9 percent** net of fees (one-year table also prints **9.88 percent** against the policy index **8.97 percent**). Money-weighted return **9.88 percent** net of investment expenses. Those two official prints are not interchangeable. Three-year **8.0 percent**. Five-year **10.4 percent**. Ten-year **8.0 percent**. Since-inception **8.9 percent**. Fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **67.8 percent**. Net pension liability at the **7.00 percent** discount rate **$8,538,499,035**.

Do not flatten NMERB into [New Mexico State Investment Council](/new-mexico-state-investment-council/) or [New Mexico PERA](/new-mexico-pera/). NMSIC is the permanent-fund file. PERA is the public-employee file. This is **one New Mexico educator-pension file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “New Mexico teacher AUM” has to name the book. The **$18,960,350,984** line is net position restricted for pensions. The **$18,506,229,186** line is investments. Those are not the same mandate.
