# San Diego City Employees’ Retirement System, explained

The San Diego City Employees’ Retirement System is San Diego’s city-employee pension, not leftover LACERS and not leftover LACERA. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://content.sdcers.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/fy-2025-sdcers-acfr-final.pdf) prints combined fiduciary net position restricted for pension, healthcare and pension-obligation-bond benefits of **$12,271,668 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. The City book was **$11,309,802 thousand**. Combined investments at fair value were **$12,243,633 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted return was **9.4 percent** net of fees. The money-weighted return was **9.11 percent** net of investment expense. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover LACERS overwrite or a leftover LACERA overwrite.** Do not mint `/san-diego-sdcers/`, `/san-diego-city-employees/`, or `/sdcers-explained/`. Do not mint `/lacers-explained/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Combined fiduciary net position restricted for pension, healthcare and pension-obligation-bond benefits** **$12,271,668 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$11,288,795 thousand**). Combined investments at fair value **$12,243,633 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover LACERS, leftover LACERA, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, or leftover SFERS as this filing.
2. **Named books.** City **$11,309,802 thousand**. Port **$636,272 thousand**. Airport **$325,581 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The combined book is pension, healthcare, and pension-obligation-bond benefits. Healthcare and pension-obligation-bond books are not the pension books. SDCERS is an agent multiple-employer plan — each sponsor prints its own net pension liability card. Do not invent a single combined fiduciary-net-position / total-pension-liability ratio from this filing.
3. **Returns.** Time-weighted return **9.4 percent** net of fees. Money-weighted return **9.11 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. The MD&A card **9.1 percent** is that money-weighted print, rounded. Those two official one-year prints are not interchangeable. Three-year, five-year, and ten-year annualized time-weighted returns **7.3 percent**, **8.6 percent**, and **7.4 percent**.

Do not flatten SDCERS into [LACERS](/lacers/) or [LACERA](/lacera/). LACERS is the Los Angeles **City** file. LACERA is the Los Angeles **County** file. This is **one San Diego city-employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “SDCERS AUM” has to name the book. The **$12,271,668 thousand** line is the combined fiduciary book. The **$11,309,802 thousand** line is the City book. The **$12,243,633 thousand** line is combined investments. Those are not the same mandate.
