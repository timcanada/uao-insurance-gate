# LPPI: approximately £60 billion at 30 June 2026, not leftover £26.5 billion

Local Pensions Partnership Investments is an FCA-authorised LGPS pool. That job is real. The live pound table is not. The live page still assigns LPPI **roughly £26.5 billion as of 2025**, **more than 600,000** members, and **more than 1,900** employers. Those leftovers are the old three-fund book, not the current official print.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## Official numbers (do not reprint the three-fund year)

| Claim | Official print | What is not official |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LPPI AUM | **Approximately £60 billion** at **30 June 2026** ([LPPI About](https://www.lppi.co.uk/about/)) | **£26.5 billion as of 2025** as current |
| 2024–25 three-fund book | LPPI’s 2024–25 accounts printed **£26.5 billion** for **three** LGPS partner funds. That line is **not** the current pool | Treating **£26.5 billion** as 2026 AUM |
| Onboarding step | 12 May 2026 news: six new partner funds brought AUM to **approximately £55 billion** from **1 April 2026** | Homemade megafund leftovers |
| Partner funds | **Nine** named on the About page: Avon, Berkshire, Cornwall, Devon, Dorset, Environment Agency, Lancashire, LPFA, Somerset | Invented partner counts |
| Combined LGPS / “600,000 members” | Not fetched as official this wake | **600,000** members; **1,900** employers |
| Cost savings | LPPI’s own sentence: more than **£250 million** net cost savings for partner funds since **2016** | Homemade savings leftovers |

Print pounds. Quote the **30 June 2026** About-page line. The **£26.5 billion** accounts line is a **dated three-fund** print. It is not a licence to leave it as current.

The LGPS **pooling** job already sits on [`/uk-lgps-pooling-megafunds/`](/uk-lgps-pooling-megafunds/) (Wave 34). The UK sector page already sits on [`/uk-pension-funds-overview/`](/uk-pension-funds-overview/). **Do not 301 this keeper onto those pages.** Do not mint a Kent, Brunel, or LPFA institution slug.

## What the page can say

- **What LPPI is.** An FCA-authorised AIFM for named LGPS partner funds. Partner funds keep strategic asset allocation. LPPI is also AIFM for GLIL Infrastructure.
- **What changed.** Fit for the Future onboarded more partner funds. The official AUM line moved with them.
- **How to print scale.** Quote the About page, or quote the dated accounts line **as dated**. A page that needs leftover **£26.5 billion** as current is a leftover page.

## What is not official

- LPPI **£26.5 billion as of 2025** as the current print.
- **600,000** members; **1,900** employers.
- Combined LGPS **£350 billion** (already refused on the Wave 34 pooling page).
