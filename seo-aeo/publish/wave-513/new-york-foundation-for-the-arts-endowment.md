# New York Foundation for the Arts endowment, explained

The New York Foundation for the Arts endowment is the house FASB endowment book of New York Foundation for the Arts, Inc. (“NYFA”), not NYFA’s entity-wide book. The official [financial statements for 30 June 2025 and 2024](https://www.nyfa.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/NYFA-FS2025.pdf) print **Endowment net assests, end of year $1,004,034**, **Investment return $89,456**, and **Appropriation of endowment assets for expenditures $18,000** (printed as (18,000)) in one house change table. “Assests” is the filing’s printed spelling. The table has one **With Donor Restrictions** column; no columns were summed.

## What is official at 30 June 2025

1. **Endowment net assests, end of year: $1,004,034.** This is the ending house-table line. The beginning balance **$932,578** is not the card. The note says NYFA’s endowment consists of approximately two individual funds whose assets are to be held in perpetuity.
2. **Investment return: $89,456.** This is the one printed return line in the same table. Do not flatten entity-wide Investment income, net **$572,824**, Investments, at fair value **$9,319,928**, or 2024 Investment return **$72,822** onto the card. The table prints no one-year percentage; do not invent the spending-policy maximum of **7 percent** as one.
3. **Appropriation of endowment assets for expenditures: $18,000.** Do not flatten the 2024 appropriation **$2,000**, releases from restrictions, grants, or expenses onto this line.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel using “NYFA endowment AUM” must name the **$1,004,034** house endowment line, not entity-wide investments or net assets. The filing’s return and appropriation are **$89,456** and **$18,000**. Those labels make the three figures comparable without creating a homemade sum or rate.
