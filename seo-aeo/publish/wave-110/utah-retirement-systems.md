# Utah Retirement Systems, explained

Utah Retirement Systems is Utah’s public-employee pension-and-savings file, not leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** and not a second CalPERS page. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.urs.org/documents/byfilename/@Public%20Web%20Documents@URS@Reports@ACFR@2025@9@@application@pdf/) prints Total Pension Trust Funds fiduciary net position of **$65,271,016 thousand** (**$65.3 billion**) at **31 December 2025**. Defined Benefit Pension Plans are **$53,294,659 thousand**. Defined Contribution Plans are **$11,976,357 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The pension fund return was **13.0 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover MassPRIM overwrite or a second CalPERS page.** The CalPERS keeper is `/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/`. Do not mint `/utah-urs/`, `/urs/`, or `/utah-retirement-systems-explained/`. Do not mint `/strs-ohio-explained/`.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The combining statement is the sourced split (dollar amounts in thousands):

1. **Total Pension Trust Funds fiduciary net position** **$65,271,016 thousand** at **31 December 2025** (2024: **$58,488,506 thousand**). That total covers the defined-benefit systems and the defined-contribution savings plans. Do not treat leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, leftover Colorado PERA **$67 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books on the same report.** Defined Benefit Pension Plans **$53,294,659 thousand**. Defined Contribution Plans **$11,976,357 thousand**. Noncontributory System **$40,301,454 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. Do not add Defined Benefit to Defined Contribution a second time and call the sum a second AUM.
3. **Returns versus the pension-fund line.** The official [2025 Summary Annual Report](https://www.urs.org/documents/byfilename/@Public%20Web%20Documents@URS@Reports@SAR@2025_SAR@@application@pdf/) prints a pension-fund return of **13.0 percent** and a pension-fund size of **$53.3 billion**. That **$53.3 billion** line is the defined-benefit book, not Total Pension Trust Funds. Do not treat the **13.0 percent** line as a return on the **$65.3 billion** total.

Do not flatten Utah Retirement Systems into [Colorado PERA](/colorado-pera/) or [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/). Colorado PERA is Colorado. CalPERS is California. This is **one Utah public-employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Utah pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$65.3 billion** line is Total Pension Trust Funds. The **$53.3 billion** line is Defined Benefit Pension Plans. The **$12.0 billion** line is Defined Contribution Plans. Those are not the same mandate.
