# What is a strategic investment fund?

A strategic investment fund is a **state development book that must earn a commercial return and also deliver a named domestic-policy outcome** — not a synonym for every large pension or SWF. Ireland’s [ISIF](/isif-ireland-strategic-investment-fund-explained/) is the named exhibit: Discretionary Portfolio **€9.7 billion** and Directed Investments **€6.422 billion** at 31 December 2025. [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/) (**$637.1 billion**) and [CPP Investments](/cpp-investments-explained/) (**C$793.3 billion**) are **public pensions**. [Temasek](/temasek-holdings-explained/)’s official NPV is **S$518 billion (US$401 billion)**, and Temasek calls itself an **investment company**. This is not investment advice.

## What the label means

Three tests:

1. **A commercial return test.** The CIO can be scored. Grant-making and pure subsidy sit somewhere else.
2. **A named domestic-policy outcome** written into statute or a minister’s direction — employment, infrastructure, a sector the state wants built — not “maximize risk-adjusted return” alone.
3. **The state is the principal.** This is not a member-owned pension, even when the sponsor is a government.

That is why ISIF’s own line is a **“double bottom line”**: invest on a commercial basis **and** support economic activity and employment in Ireland. A [pension fund](/what-is-a-pension-fund/) that happens to own infrastructure is still a pension. A [sovereign wealth fund](/sovereign-wealth-fund/) that happens to own domestic champions is still a SWF until the statute says development is the job.

## What ISIF actually is (so the category has a filing)

Successor to the National Pensions Reserve Fund (assets transferred December 2014). Two books:

- **Discretionary.** The development mandate. 2025 return **8.1%**. Cumulative commitments to Ireland **€9.7 billion** across **272** investments — a flow since 2014, **not** the 2025 Discretionary stock, even though the euro figure matches.
- **Directed.** Held on Minister for Finance direction. The State’s directed AIB shareholding was **sold 17 June 2025**.

[NSIA](/nsia-nigeria-sovereign-investment-authority-explained/)’s Infrastructure Fund is a **sleeve**, not a second ISIF. Do not call $1.40 billion “Nigeria’s SIF AUM.”
