# How SWFs make money: quote the filing, not a $13 trillion pile

Sovereign wealth funds make money by investing state capital and compounding it. That mechanism is real. The live league table is not. The live page, dated 8 June 2026, still prints GPFG as **roughly $1.8 trillion** and later **more than $2 trillion**, a global **$13 trillion** pile, a Gulf **$5 trillion** pile, ADIA **~$1.1 trillion**, PIF **$925 billion / $1.15 trillion**, and KIA **over $1 trillion**. Those are tracker numbers. They are not official filings.

## What this page can say

A SWF is seeded from commodity revenue, excess reserves, or fiscal surplus. It invests that stock across public and private markets and compounds the return. Norway’s official proof is a **kroner** book, not a homemade dollar. Houses that refuse AUM still publish a return, a mandate, or a statute. Quote that. Do not invent a global pile so the mechanism looks larger.

## What is not official

- GPFG **$1.8 trillion**, **$2 trillion**, or **$1.76T**.
- A global **$13 trillion** SWF AUM, or **14%** growth as a UAO print.
- A Gulf **$5 trillion** pile, or three GCC houses each “over $1 trillion.”
- ADIA, KIA, QIA, or GIC AUM in any dollar.
- PIF **$925 billion / $1.15 trillion** as current AUM.
