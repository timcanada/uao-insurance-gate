# Public Employee Retirement System of Idaho, explained

The Public Employee Retirement System of Idaho is Idaho’s public-employee pension, not leftover Iowa IPERS and not leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**. The official [2025 financial statements](https://www.persi.idaho.gov/docs/employers/gasb/PERSI-2025-Financial-Statements_26.pdf) print combined net position restricted for pensions of **$24,797,083,283** at **30 June 2025**. Combined total net position is **$24,804,371,294**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The Base Plan fiduciary net position used for the GASB 67 comparison is **$24,138,888,873**. The Firefighters’ Retirement Fund is **$521,563,002**. The Judges’ Retirement Fund is **$127,464,623**. The money-weighted return was **10.81 percent** net. The gross return was **11.12 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The audited financial statements are the sourced split. The house 2025 ACFR PDF was not separately locked this wake; these statements are the official first-party filing.

1. **Combined net position restricted for pensions** **$24,797,083,283** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$22,744,178,043**). Combined total net position is **$24,804,371,294**. The difference is assets used in plan operation of **$7,288,011**. Those two totals are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion**, or leftover Iowa IPERS as this filing.
2. **Named books on the same report.** Base Plan fiduciary net position used for the GASB 67 comparison **$24,138,888,873**. FRF **$521,563,002**. JRF **$127,464,623**. Those three lines are not interchangeable with the combined pension-restricted total. Do not add FRF or JRF to the Base Plan a second time and call the sum the combined book.
3. **Returns.** Annual money-weighted return **10.81 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Gross return **11.12 percent**. Those two return lines are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Idaho PERSI into [Iowa IPERS](/iowa-ipers/) or [Minnesota SBI](/minnesota-sbi/). Iowa IPERS is the Iowa public-employee file. This is **one Idaho public-employee file**.
