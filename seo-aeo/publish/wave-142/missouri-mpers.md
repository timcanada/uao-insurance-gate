# Missouri MoDOT and Highway Patrol Employees’ Retirement System, explained

Missouri Department of Transportation and Highway Patrol Employees’ Retirement System is Missouri’s transportation-and-patrol pension, not leftover MOSERS, leftover Missouri PSRS and PEERS, leftover LAGERS, and not leftover MainePERS. The official [30 June 2025 MPERS Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.mpers.org/wp-content/uploads/FY-2025-Annual-Report.pdf) prints fiduciary net position of **$4,088,465,006** at **30 June 2025**. Investments **$4,101,436,130** are not that card. Money-weighted return was **12.71 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Transmittal **12.7 percent** is not that card. GASB **80.20 percent** is not actuarial **75.68 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover MOSERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/mpers/`, `/missouri-mpers-explained/`, or `/modot-mpers/`. `/mpers/` is already the leftover MainePERS twin.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position, the GASB 67 schedule, and the notes money-weighted print are the sourced lines:

1. **Fiduciary net position** **$4,088,465,006** at **30 June 2025** (beginning **$3,677,657,507**). Investments per the statement of fiduciary net position **$4,101,436,130**. Do not treat leftover MOSERS **$9,565,800,614**, leftover Missouri PSRS and PEERS combined **$63,124,090,036**, leftover LAGERS Trust **$11,341,575,751**, leftover MainePERS **$22,466,747,053**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$5,098,039,114**. Employers’ net pension liability **$1,009,574,108**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **80.20 percent**. Transmittal actuarial funded **75.68 percent** is not the GASB card.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **12.71 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025**. Transmittal **12.7 percent** is the rounded card.

Do not flatten this book into [MOSERS](/missouri-mosers/), [Missouri PSRS and PEERS](/missouri-psrs-peers/), [Missouri LAGERS](/missouri-lagers/), or [MainePERS](/maine-pers/). MOSERS is the Missouri state-employee file. PSRS and PEERS are the Missouri school files. LAGERS is the Missouri local-government file. MainePERS is a different legal person. This is **one Missouri transportation-and-patrol file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Missouri MPERS AUM” has to name the book. The **$4,088,465,006** line is fiduciary net position. The **$4,101,436,130** line is investments. The **12.71 percent** card is money-weighted. The **12.7 percent** card is the rounded transmittal print. The **80.20 percent** card is GASB. The **75.68 percent** card is actuarial. Those are not the same mandate.
