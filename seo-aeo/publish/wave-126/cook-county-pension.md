# Cook County Pension Fund, explained

The County Employees’ and Officers’ Annuity and Benefit Fund of Cook County is Cook County’s employee pension, not leftover IMRF, leftover Illinois FPIF, leftover Illinois IPOPIF, leftover Chicago Teachers, leftover Chicago Fire FABF, and not leftover MEABF. The official [2025 audited financial statements](https://www.cookcountypension.com/assets/1/6/CCPF_County_25_FS_Final.pdf) print fiduciary net position restricted for pensions of **$15,221,712,193** at **31 December 2025**. Investments were **$14,934,036,353**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return was **15.27 percent** net of investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **72.25 percent** of total pension liability. Healthcare net position prints **zero**. The Forest Preserve 2025 file is a different legal person. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Fiduciary net position restricted for pensions** **$15,221,712,193** at **31 December 2025** (2024 **$13,684,313,370**). The change is **$1,537,398,823**, or **11.2 percent**. Investments **$14,934,036,353** is not fiduciary net position. Combining healthcare net position prints a dash. Those prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover IMRF **$62,375,940,515**, leftover Illinois FPIF, leftover Illinois IPOPIF, leftover Chicago Teachers **$13,657,637,993**, leftover Chicago Fire FABF, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$21,066,944,967**. City / plan net pension liability **$5,845,232,774**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **72.25 percent** (2024 **67.81 percent**). Those two lines are not interchangeable with investments.
3. **Returns.** Money-weighted return **15.27 percent** net of investment expense (2024 **9.59 percent**). The house statements do not give this page a separate official time-weighted card to quote as the money-weighted substitute. Do not invent one.

Do not flatten Cook County Pension into [IMRF](/illinois-imrf/), [Illinois FPIF](/illinois-fpif/), [Illinois IPOPIF](/illinois-ipopif/), [Chicago Teachers](/chicago-teachers/), or [Chicago Fire FABF](/chicago-fire-fabf/). IMRF is the statewide municipal-employee file. FPIF and IPOPIF are consolidated investment trusts. Chicago Teachers is the Chicago teacher file. FABF is the Chicago fire file. This is **one Cook County employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Cook County AUM” has to name the legal person. The **$15,221,712,193** line is fiduciary net position restricted for pensions. The **$14,934,036,353** line is investments. The healthcare column is not a second AUM. The Forest Preserve 2025 financial statements are a different filing.
