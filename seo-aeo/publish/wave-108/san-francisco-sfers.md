# San Francisco Employees' Retirement System, explained

The San Francisco City and County Employees’ Retirement System is the City and County’s pension, not CalPERS and not LACERA. The official [2025 audited financial statements](https://cdn.mysfers.org/uploads/2026/02/SFERS-Audited-Financial-Statements-2025.pdf) print fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits of **$37,450,095 thousand** (**$37.45 billion**) at **30 June 2025**. The money-weighted net return was **8.47 percent**. The Trust Fund time-weighted net-of-fee return was **7.87 percent**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. Deferred compensation is not this pension line. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second CalPERS overwrite or a second LACERA overwrite.** Those keepers are `/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/` and `/lacera/`. Do not mint `/sfers/`, `/sfers-explained/`, or `/san-francisco-employees-retirement-system/`. Do not reprint leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statements are the sourced split (dollar amounts in thousands unless named as billions):

1. **Fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits** **$37,450,095 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$35,415,555 thousand**). Increase **$2,034,540 thousand**, or **5.7 percent**. Pension-plan total assets **$38.59 billion**. Do not treat leftover CalPERS **$460 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books that are not this line.** Deferred Compensation Plan net assets **$6.21 billion**. Do not add deferred compensation to the pension. Total pension liability **$41,836,074 thousand**. Net pension liability **$4,385,979 thousand**. Fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **89.5 percent**. The actuarial funded ratio is **96.6 percent**. Those two funded-status lines are not interchangeable.
3. **Returns.** Money-weighted net return **8.47 percent**. Trust Fund time-weighted net-of-fee return **7.87 percent**. Five-year net-of-fee **9.59 percent**; ten-year **8.44 percent**. Assumed actuarial rate **7.20 percent**. Net investment income **$2,961,176 thousand**.

Do not flatten SFERS into [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/) or [LACERA](/lacera/). CalPERS and CalSTRS sit in the Sacramento corridor. LACERA is Los Angeles County. This is **one San Francisco City and County file**.
