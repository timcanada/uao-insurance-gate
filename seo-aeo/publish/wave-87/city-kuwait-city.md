# Relocating to Kuwait City for a KIA desk

Kuwait City is the home file of the [Kuwait Investment Authority](/kuwait-investment-authority-kia/). KIA traces its roots to the Kuwait Investment Board of **1953** and was created by **Law No. 47 of 1982**. It manages the **General Reserve Fund** and the **Future Generations Fund**. It **does not publish assets under management**. The historic Kuwait Investment Office sits in [London](/careers/cities/london/). A move here is often a move onto a Kuwaiti reserve file, not a weekend in [Doha](/careers/cities/doha/) or [Abu Dhabi](/careers/cities/abu-dhabi/) with a different logo. Confirm immigration with official Kuwaiti sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here

- [KIA](/kuwait-investment-authority-kia/) — asset manager for the State of Kuwait, not the owner of the assets. IFSWF member. Official prints do **not** include an AUM. Do not recycle leftover **US$1.07 trillion**. Do not mint `/kia-explained/` or `/kuwait-swf/`.
- KIO is the London satellite with a 1953 lineage. If the desk is London, read the [London city page](/careers/cities/london/).
- [QIA](/qatar-investment-authority-qia/), [ADIA](https://www.adia.ae/) and [PIF](/public-investment-fund-pif/) sit in different capitals. If the desk is theirs, read those city pages.

Our [dual-career Gulf](/careers/intelligence/dual-career-moves-gulf/) note is the prior reading for the household. The [moving-into playbook](/careers/intelligence/moving-into-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/) already says people “come for a few years but don't always stay.”

## Papers

Kuwait’s official portal is [e.gov.kw](https://www.e.gov.kw/). Visa classes, dependent rules and tax guidance belong there — or with licensed counsel. Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Why people underestimate the job

You are closer to a government owner. Confidentiality is not a preference. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail.
