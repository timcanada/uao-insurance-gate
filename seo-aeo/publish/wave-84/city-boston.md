# Relocating to Boston for an endowment or public-plan desk

Boston is an endowment city that people flatten into Harvard. [Harvard Management Company](https://www.hmc.harvard.edu/), formed in **1974**, manages Harvard University’s endowment and related financial assets from this city. [MITIMCo](https://www.mitimco.org/) sits here too. Yale’s named model sits in New Haven, not Boston. A move here is a move into a mission file and a long horizon, not a banking tour with better weather. Right to work sits with official US sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here

- [Harvard Management Company](https://www.hmc.harvard.edu/about/) — a nonprofit subsidiary of Harvard University. Read that site before you read a recruiter brief. Do not mint `/harvard-endowment-explained/` or `/harvard-management-company/`. Do not recycle a stale endowment dollar from a slide; the official print lives on HMC’s site and on our endowment-model pages.
- [MITIMCo](https://www.mitimco.org/) — MIT’s investment office. Official site, not a rumour.
- Massachusetts public-plan capital sits in the same labour market. Do not invent a PRIM AUM on this page.

The [endowment model](/endowment-model-explained/) is the job description. The [Yale endowment model](/yale-endowment-model/) is a New Haven peer, not a Boston address.

## Why people underestimate the job

You are closer to a university’s operating budget than to a product shelf. Confidentiality and a public file both apply. Housing and dual-career problems are the ordinary reasons offers fail — not the quality of the investment debate.
