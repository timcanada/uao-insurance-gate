# What is a multi-custodian model?

A multi-custodian model is when an owner hires two or more custodian banks as principals — two books on purpose — not a master bank that appoints sub-custodians. The OCC already defines each bank’s job as settlement, safekeeping, and reporting. CalPERS’ official file is a master-custodian contract, not two competing primes. This page is not a ranking and not operational advice.

## Master plus sub is not multi

The OCC’s public [custody-services](https://www.occ.gov/topics/supervision-and-examination/capital-markets/asset-management/custody-services/index-custody-services.html) page says bank custody typically includes the **settlement, safekeeping, and reporting** of customers’ marketable securities and cash. That is what each appointed bank does.

CalPERS’ official file shows the common large-plan design — one **master** book, with **sub-custodians** underneath:

1. The Finance and Administration Committee item dated **20 September 2022** asked the board to extend the **Master Custodian** contract with State Street Bank for five years. CalPERS first contracted State Street after a **1991** RFP; successive awards began in **1992**. The same item lists four integrated services: Master Custody (custody and accounting), Investment Book of Record, Investment Performance Platform, and Alpha Data Services.
2. CalPERS’ investment-policy custody text (Investment Committee attachment, **2017**) says contracted custodians must have a system for **selecting and evaluating sub-custodians**, and that the custodian remains **fully liable** for the acts of its agents and sub-custodians.
3. That 2017 text also allows assets held by **additional agents or sub-custodians appointed by CalPERS or the Custodian**. Those are agents of the master book. They are not a second prime custodian.

A multi-custodian model is the other design: the owner splits the book across two or more **prime** banks — public markets at one, private markets at another, or two books for concentration or sanctions reasons. The failure mode is two numbers the board cannot add.

How to pick the first bank sits on [how owners pick a custodian](/how-do-owners-pick-a-custodian/). Do not flatten this page onto that how-to.

## What an owner is actually buying

A public pension that stays with one master is buying a single IBOR and a single performance file. CalPERS’ 2022 item is that file.

A family office or a sovereign desk that hires two primes is buying a split they can defend — not a second logo. Ask who reconciles the two cash accounts.

The legal administrator of a pension plan is a different job. That keeper is [what an administrator is for a pension plan](/what-is-an-administrator-for-a-pension-plan/).
