# Middle East family offices: no official $2–2.5 trillion pile

There is no official AUM table of Middle East family offices. The offices most often named in press rankings do **not** publish an official family-office AUM this desk can cite. The live page still commands **$2–2.5 trillion** of investable assets, a GCC pile of **$1.5–1.8 trillion**, Kingdom Holding **approaching $20 billion**, and QIA **approximately $550 billion**. The twin [`/middle-east-family-offices-investment/`](/middle-east-family-offices-investment/) is worse: **$2.5–4 trillion**, QIA **$445 billion**, PIF **$925 billion**. Those dollars are not filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

| Public Investment Fund | Official current language **more than US$900 billion**. Not **$925 billion** as current AUM |
| A ranked “largest Middle East family offices” dollar table | Not an official filing. Do not invent one |

A family office is a private investment and administrative vehicle for one family or a small group of families. A royal family office is still a **private** office. A [sovereign wealth fund](/sovereign-wealth-fund/) is a **state** book. Do not add QIA or PIF into a family-office pile so the region can be ranked.

## What this page can say

- **What the form is.** Single-family and multi-family are operating types, not official AUM bands. The same rule already sits on [`/largest-family-offices-in-the-world/`](/largest-family-offices-in-the-world/) and [`/family-office-vs-sovereign-wealth-fund/`](/family-office-vs-sovereign-wealth-fund/).
- **What is not a family office.** [QIA](/qatar-investment-authority-qia/) does not publish AUM. [PIF](/public-investment-fund-pif/) prints **more than US$900 billion**. [Mubadala](/mubadala-investment-company/) is an Abu Dhabi sovereign investor (AED 1.4 trillion / US$385 billion at YE2025). [ADIA](/adia-abu-dhabi-investment-authority/) publishes no AUM. Putting those houses on a family-office list is a category error.
- **Where official numbers live instead.** For sovereign houses, quote the filing or say no AUM. For family offices, say the office does not publish AUM.

Do not treat a listed holding-company market value as family-office AUM. Do not invent a **$50–100 billion** single-office deploy figure so a lede has scale.
