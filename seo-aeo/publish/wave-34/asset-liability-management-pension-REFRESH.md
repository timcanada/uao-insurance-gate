# Pension ALM: GPIF is yen, not a $2.1 trillion liability exhibit

Asset-liability management matches a pension book to named promises. That job is real. The live dollar table is not. The live page, dated 1 July 2026, still assigns [GPIF](/gpif-japan-explained/) **$2.1 trillion**, [CalSTRS](/calstrs-explained/) **$339 billion as of June 2024** and an unfunded gap of **approximately $73 billion as of June 2022**, and [GPFG](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/) **$1.32 trillion** as if it were a pension ALM exhibit. Those leftovers are not the current filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## Official numbers (do not mix the jobs)

| House | Official job | Official print | What is not official |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GPIF | Largest dedicated public pension | **¥293,643.7 billion** at **31 March 2026**. FY2025 return **16.47%** | **$2.1 trillion**; leftover **$2 trillion** |
| CalSTRS Teachers’ Retirement Fund | Educator-only public pension | **$415.4 billion** at **30 June 2026**. Funded status **79.3%** at **30 June 2025** — not a 2026 ratio | **$339 billion**; UAL **$73 billion** as current |
| Government Pension Fund Global | **SWF**, not a pension ALM book | **22,683 billion kroner** at **30 June 2026** | **$1.32 trillion** |

Print yen for GPIF. Print dollars for CalSTRS. Print kroner for GPFG. **Do not dollarize.** GPFG has no named-member liability. Do not file it here so an ALM page can have a third row.

This URL is the **ALM** job. Leveraged LDI and the 2022 gilt episode already sit on [`/liability-driven-investing-explained/`](/liability-driven-investing-explained/) (Wave 15). **Do not 301 this keeper onto that page.**

## What the page can say

- **The question.** A pension ALM book asks what it owes, and when, then builds assets that can pay it. An endowment and a SWF do not owe named members.
- **Quote the pair.** CalSTRS prints AUM at 30 June 2026 and funded status at **30 June 2025**. Those dates are part of the number. Do not invent a **$73 billion** UAL from 2022.
- **What ALM is not.** It is not a licence to reprint leftover GPIF dollars or to treat GPFG as a pension.

## What is not official

- GPIF **$2.1 trillion** / **$2 trillion**.
- CalSTRS **$339 billion**, or UAL **$73 billion** as current.
- GPFG **$1.32 trillion** as a pension ALM exhibit.
