# Oklahoma Law Enforcement Retirement System, explained

The Oklahoma Law Enforcement Retirement System is Oklahoma’s statewide law-enforcement pension, not leftover Oklahoma OPERS, leftover Oklahoma Firefighters, leftover Oklahoma Police, and not leftover Uniform Retirement System for Justices and Judges. The official [30 June 2025 Oklahoma Law Enforcement Retirement Plan financial statements](https://olers.ok.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2025-FS-6-30-OLERS-FINAL.pdf) print pension-plan fiduciary net position of **$1,224,654,601** at **30 June 2025**. Combined fiduciary net position **$1,228,986,597** mixes in the OPEB plan. Pension-plan investments **$1,213,258,051** are not the net-position card. Money-weighted return was **10.35 percent** net of investment expense. MD&A **11 percent** net yield is not that card. GASB **80.51 percent** is the pension card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statements of fiduciary net position, the GASB 67 schedules, and the money-weighted schedule are the sourced lines:

1. **Pension-plan fiduciary net position** **$1,224,654,601** at **30 June 2025** (beginning **$1,149,740,628**). Pension-plan investments **$1,213,258,051**. Combined investments **$1,217,556,657** include OPEB. Combined fiduciary net position **$1,228,986,597** is not the pension card. OPEB fiduciary net position **$4,331,996** is not the pension card. Pension securities-lending collateral **$66,210,289** is not extra AUM. Do not treat leftover Oklahoma OPERS Combined **$13,351,556,235**, leftover Oklahoma Firefighters **$3,696,332,045**, leftover Oklahoma Police **$3,449,729 thousand**, leftover Uniform Retirement System for Justices and Judges pension **$447,697,292**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** The required supplementary schedules print in thousands. Total pension liability **$1,521,118 thousand**. Employer’s net pension liability **$296,463 thousand**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of the total pension liability **80.51 percent**. OPEB GASB **40.02 percent** is not the pension card.
3. **Returns.** Schedule money-weighted return **10.35 percent** net of investment expense for the pension plan for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **8.23 percent**). OPEB money-weighted return **10.35 percent** is the allocated-asset card, not a reason to flatten OPEB into the pension book. MD&A **11 percent** net yield on average assets is not the notes card.

Do not flatten the Oklahoma Law Enforcement Retirement System into [Oklahoma OPERS](/oklahoma-opers/), [Oklahoma Firefighters Pension and Retirement System](/oklahoma-firefighters/), [Oklahoma Police Pension and Retirement System](/oklahoma-police/), or [Uniform Retirement System for Justices and Judges](/oklahoma-ursjj/). Oklahoma OPERS is the public-employee file. Oklahoma Firefighters and Oklahoma Police are different statewide sworn books. The Uniform Retirement System for Justices and Judges is the judicial file. This is **one Oklahoma law-enforcement file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Oklahoma law-enforcement AUM” has to name the book. The **$1,224,654,601** line is pension-plan fiduciary net position. The **$1,228,986,597** line is combined. The **$4,331,996** line is OPEB. The **$1,213,258,051** line is pension-plan investments. The **10.35 percent** card is money-weighted. The **11 percent** card is MD&A. The **80.51 percent** card is pension GASB. Those are not the same mandate.
