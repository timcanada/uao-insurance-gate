# GCC investment strategy: no official $2 trillion pile

There is no official combined AUM for Gulf Cooperation Council sovereign wealth funds. The live page, dated 3 July 2026, still prints **over $2 trillion**, then assigns PIF **$925 billion**, ADIA **$172.6 billion**, KIA **$700 billion**, and QIA **$450 billion**. Those are tracker numbers. They are not the official filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.
