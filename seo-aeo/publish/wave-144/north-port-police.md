# City of North Port Police Officers’ Pension–Local Option Trust Fund, explained

The City of North Port Police Officers’ Pension–Local Option Trust Fund is North Port’s police pension, not leftover Tampa Fire and Police, leftover Miami Fire and Police, leftover Jacksonville Police and Fire, and not leftover Sarasota Employees. The official [30 September 2025 City of North Port Police Officers’ Pension–Local Option Trust Fund financial statements](https://www.resourcecenters.com/Pension%20Funds/Select%20Your%20Fund/North%20Port%20Police%20Officers'%20Pension%20-%20Local%20Option%20Trust%20Fund/20Required%20Documents%20and%20Disclosures/2025%20Financial%20Statements%20-%20City%20of%20North%20Port%20Police%20Pension%20Plan.pdf) print net position restricted for pension benefits of **$81,628,223** at **30 September 2025**. Investments **$80,542,244** are not that card. Money-weighted return was **11.59 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense.
