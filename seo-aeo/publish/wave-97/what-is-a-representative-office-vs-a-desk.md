# What is a representative office vs a desk?

A U.S. representative office of a foreign bank is a legal status: it is not a branch, agency, or commercial lending company, and it may not take deposits, lend money, or do other banking for the bank. An owner satellite desk is a different job — origination or a time zone for a fund whose investment committee sits elsewhere. Those two rooms share a city. They do not share a statute. This page is not licensing advice.

## What Regulation K already prints

Federal Reserve Regulation K, [12 CFR § 211.21(x)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/12/211.21), defines a **representative office** as any office of a foreign bank located in a state that is **not** a Federal branch, Federal agency, State branch, State agency, or commercial lending company subsidiary.

[12 CFR § 211.24(d)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/12/211.24) then limits what that office may do. Three facts from that text:

1. It may perform **representational and administrative functions** in connection with the foreign bank’s banking activities — soliciting new business, research, liaison with U.S. customers, preliminary and servicing steps in connection with lending, or back-office functions.
2. It **shall not** contract for any deposit or deposit-like liability, **lend money**, or engage in any other banking activity for the foreign bank.
3. Credit decisions are allowed only in a narrow case: the foreign bank also operates a U.S. branch or agency, the loan is made by that U.S. office, and the proceeds are **not disbursed** in the representative office.

A **branch**, for a national bank, is already defined in [12 U.S.C. § 36(j)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/12/36): a place of business at which **deposits are received, checks are paid, or money is lent**. That is why a representative office is not a branch. Calling a deal floor a “rep office” does not make it one.

## What an owner desk actually is

A sovereign fund, a pension, or a family office that opens a London, New York, or Singapore seat is usually not applying under Regulation K. The seat is origination, public markets, or a client city. The book and the investment committee stay at headquarters.

That failure mode is already written on [why London satellites fail](/careers/intelligence/why-london-satellites-fail/): people take the job as if the committee lived on the same floor. The legal bank file and the owner satellite are easy to confuse in a lease. They are not the same licence.
