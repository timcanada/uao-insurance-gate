# Relocating to The Hague for a Dutch public-pension sponsor desk

The Hague is the political capital of the Dutch public-pension sponsors. It is not automatically ABP’s home file. [ABP](/abp-netherlands-pension-fund-explained/) was founded in **1922** as a civil-service fund that once sat under a ministry here; it was privatized in **1996** and split from [APG](https://apg.nl/en/contact/) in **2008**. APG’s official European offices are Heerlen, Amsterdam and Brussels — not this city. A move here is often a move onto a government or education sponsor file, or into a window whose investment committee sits in Heerlen or Amsterdam. Right to work sits with official Dutch sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit elsewhere

- [ABP](/abp-netherlands-pension-fund-explained/) — Stichting Pensioenfonds ABP, the pension fund for government and education. Official prints are **euros**. Do not invent an APG client stack as ABP’s book. Ask whether the board and the working teams sit in Heerlen. Read the [Amsterdam city page](/careers/cities/amsterdam/) for the markets floor.
- [The Dutch pension system](/netherlands-pension-system-abp-pfzw/) is the franchise page. **Do not treat this city page as a second system explainer.**
- [PFZW](/pfzw-netherlands-pension-fund-explained/) is care and welfare. Do not mash it into ABP. Do not mint `/pggm/`, `/pfzw-netherlands/`, or `/the-hague-abp/`.

APG administers — **not** a second AUM. Global owners staff this city as a *window* more often than as HQ.

## Papers

Start at the [Immigration and Naturalisation Service (IND)](https://www.ind.nl/en). Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Why people underestimate the job

A ministry postcode is not the investment book. The 2027 scheme change is a process, not a branding exercise. Dutch is the language of legitimacy even when the dealing language is English. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail.
