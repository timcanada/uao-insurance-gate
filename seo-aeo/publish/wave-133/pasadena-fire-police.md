# Pasadena Fire & Police Retirement System, explained

Pasadena Fire & Police Retirement System is Pasadena’s closed sworn fire-and-police pension, not leftover CalPERS and not leftover Los Angeles Fire and Police Pensions. The official [30 June 2025 financial statements](https://www.cityofpasadena.net/commissions/wp-content/uploads/sites/31/FY2025-FPRS-Financial-Statements.pdf) print net position restricted for pensions of **$107,075,040** at **30 June 2025**. Statement investments were **$106,318,445**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Time-weighted return was **9.8 percent** net of fees. Money-weighted return was **9.7 percent** net. Fiduciary net position was **98.2 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover CalPERS overwrite or a leftover Los Angeles Fire and Police Pensions overwrite.** Do not mint `/pasadena-fprs/` or `/fprs/`. Concord Property proceeds of about **$11.1 million** are not fiduciary net position. Actuarial-value funded **92.5 percent** is not the GASB card. The GASB 101 restated fiduciary-net-position print **$107,077 thousand** is not the statement **$107,075,040** line. Closed to new members in **June 2004**. Later Pasadena safety hires sit in CalPERS. There are no active employees in this book.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position restricted for pensions** **$107,075,040** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$98,629,595**). The change is **$8,446,846**. Statement investments **$106,318,445** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$111,573,424** is not fiduciary net position. Concord sale and class-action collections **$11,071 thousand** are additions, not AUM. Do not treat leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, leftover Los Angeles Fire and Police Pensions, leftover Fresno Fire and Police **$2,395,605,595**, leftover Oakland Police and Fire **$476,653,059**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$109,039 thousand**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **98.2 percent**. The GASB 101 restated fiduciary-net-position print **$107,077 thousand** is not the statement line. Actuarial-value funded **92.5 percent** is the contribution-agreement card, not the GASB ratio.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **9.7 percent** net of investment expenses for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **6.6 percent**). Management-discussion time-weighted **9.8 percent** net of fees is not the money-weighted card. Those official prints are not interchangeable.
