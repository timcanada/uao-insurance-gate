# How do Gulf funds invest in sports?

Gulf-fund sports investing is a named-vehicle job, not a second PIF AUM page and not a second Gulf-AI page. On 7 October 2021 an investment group led by Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund, with PCP Capital Partners and RB Sports & Media, completed the acquisition of 100 percent of Newcastle United. The Premier League said it had received legally binding assurances that the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia will not control the club. On 6 June 2023 the PGA TOUR, DP World Tour, and PIF announced a new collectively owned commercial entity combining PIF’s golf-related businesses, including LIV Golf, with the tours’ commercial businesses; the PGA TOUR would appoint a majority of the board and hold a majority voting interest. Qatar Sports Investments, founded in 2004 and not a QIA AUM print, marked 14 years of Paris Saint-Germain ownership on 30 June 2025 and dates the acquisition to 2011. ## Named vehicles, not a slogan

Official club, league, and investor statements are the sourced file:

1. Newcastle United’s [7 October 2021 club statement](https://www.nufc.co.uk/news/latest-news/pif-pcp-capital-partners-and-rb-sports-media-acquire-newcastle-united-football-club/) records that an investment group **led by PIF**, also comprising PCP Capital Partners and RB Sports & Media, completed the acquisition of **100 percent** of Newcastle United Limited and Newcastle United Football Club Limited from St. James Holdings Limited. Yasir Al-Rumayyan, Governor of PIF, would serve as **Non-Executive Chairman**. The [Premier League statement](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/2283712) says the league received **legally binding assurances** that the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia **will not control** the club.
2. PIF’s [6 June 2023 newswire](https://www.pif.gov.sa/en/news-and-insights/newswire/2023/pga-tour-dp-world-tour-and-pif-announce-newly-formed-commercial-entity-to-unify-golf/) with the PGA TOUR and DP World Tour announced a new **collectively owned, for-profit** entity combining **PIF’s golf-related commercial businesses and rights (including LIV Golf)** with the tours’ commercial businesses. PIF would initially be the **exclusive investor** alongside the tours. The PGA TOUR would appoint a **majority of the board** and hold a **majority voting interest**.
3. [Qatar Sports Investments](https://www.qsi.com.qa/qsi-marks-14-years-ownership-of-psg/), founded **2004**, marked **14 years** of Paris Saint-Germain ownership on **30 June 2025** and dates the acquisition to **2011**. QSI’s own about-line names PSG, Premier Padel, and Sporting Clube de Braga. **QSI is not a QIA AUM.** Do not invent one.
