# What a $20,000 institutional media buy actually is

On Universal Asset Owners’ public [advertise](/advertise/) page, **Establish authority** programmes start from **$20,000**. That buy is labelled proximity to an owner daily — not a guaranteed click, not a rented subscriber file, and not a newsroom conclusion. The **Briefing Partnership** starts from **$15,000**. Daily Brief sponsorship starts from **$2,500 per edition**. Read the firewall before you ask for an exception.

## What $20,000 is not

- A sentence in the daily brief sold as news
- A recommended-vendor stamp
- A rental of the subscriber file
- A promise that a CIO will take the meeting
- Progress toward a homemade **$1 million / year** advertising total

[Ethics](/ethics/) still governs the room. Sponsorship supports access and distribution. It does not buy a ranking, an award, or a favourable profile.
