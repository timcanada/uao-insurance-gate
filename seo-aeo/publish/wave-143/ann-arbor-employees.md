# City of Ann Arbor Employees’ Retirement System, explained

The City of Ann Arbor Employees’ Retirement System is Ann Arbor’s city pension, not leftover Michigan MERS, leftover Detroit GRS, and not leftover Detroit Police and Fire. The official [30 June 2025 City of Ann Arbor Employees’ Retirement System financial statements](https://www.a2gov.org/media/timhiaag/city-of-ann-arbor-ers-6-30-25-fs-final.pdf) print fiduciary net position of **$682,683,869** at **30 June 2025**. That statement includes defined-contribution plan assets of **$10,676,999**. Defined-benefit plan fiduciary net position **$672,006,870** is the GASB book. Investments **$684,914,488** are not the net-position card. Money-weighted return was **9.17 percent** net of investment expense. GASB **97.85 percent** is of the defined-benefit book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Michigan MERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/a2ers/`, `/ann-arbor-ers/`, or `/ann-arbor-employees-explained/`. Do not mint `/ers/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position, the GASB 67 note, and the money-weighted schedule are the sourced lines:

1. **Fiduciary net position** **$682,683,869** at **30 June 2025** (beginning **$647,691,349**). Total investments **$684,914,488**. Equity in City of Ann Arbor pooled cash **$355,088** is not that card. Defined-contribution plan assets **$10,676,999** are inside the statement and are not the defined-benefit book. Do not treat leftover Michigan MERS Defined Benefit **$14,870,864 thousand**, leftover Detroit GRS, leftover Detroit Police and Fire, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$686,800,011**. City’s net pension liability **$14,793,141**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **97.85 percent**. Those cards use defined-benefit plan fiduciary net position **$672,006,870**, not the combined statement **$682,683,869**.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **9.17 percent** net of investment expenses for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **10.16 percent**).

Do not flatten the City of Ann Arbor Employees’ Retirement System into [Municipal Employees’ Retirement System of Michigan](/michigan-mers/), [General Retirement System of the City of Detroit](/detroit-grs/), [Police and Fire Retirement System of the City of Detroit](/detroit-police-fire/), or [Michigan Public School Employees’ Retirement System](/michigan-mpsers/). MERS is a statewide local-government file. Detroit GRS and Detroit Police and Fire are Detroit city files. MPSERS is the Michigan school file. This is **one Ann Arbor city file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Ann Arbor pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$682,683,869** line is combined statement fiduciary net position. The **$672,006,870** line is the defined-benefit GASB book. The **$10,676,999** line is defined-contribution assets. The **$684,914,488** line is investments. The **9.17 percent** card is money-weighted. The **97.85 percent** card is GASB on the defined-benefit book. Those are not the same mandate.
