# Data and index providers for sovereign funds, pensions and family offices

Asset owners buy data and indexes for a number an investment committee can defend: benchmarks, look-through, private-markets tapes, and the difference between a vendor print and an official filing. This hub is Universal Asset Owners' map of that category. It is not a ranking and not a data licence.

The buying process for systems already sits on the Wave 1 [technology hub](/directory/technology/) (paste with Wave 1). **This page is the data-and-index job, not a second technology catalogue.** A listing here is not an endorsement. Featured placements are marked paid.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## What owners are actually buying

A public pension buys a policy benchmark the board can minute, and a look-through tape that matches the custodian.

A sovereign or strategic fund buys a market map that does not invent an AUM the fund has not published.

A family office buys private-markets and public-markets data that will not leak the family file.

[Asset owners](/what-is-an-asset-owner/) are judged on official filings, not on a vendor's estimated pile. If a dataset cannot say where a number came from, it does not belong in a board pack.

If you sell into this room, say which tape you actually maintain. If you buy, ask who paid for the last revision and what happens when the vendor and the filing disagree.

## The questions a CIO or data lead should ask

1. What is the official source behind each house AUM or return you display?
2. How do you treat funds that do not publish AUM?
3. Who can see the beneficiary or family file inside your platform?
4. What will you refuse to estimate?
