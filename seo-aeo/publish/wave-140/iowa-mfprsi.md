# Municipal Fire and Police Retirement System of Iowa, explained

Municipal Fire and Police Retirement System of Iowa is Iowa’s municipal sworn pension, not leftover IPERS, leftover Nebraska School, and not leftover Omaha OSERS. The official [30 June 2025 MFPRSI audit](https://www.mfprsi.org/media/publications/2025_aud_report.pdf) prints fiduciary net position of **$3,496,382,132** at **30 June 2025**. Investments were **$3,492,186,395**. Money-weighted return was **9.30 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **84.41 percent** of total pension liability. The annual-report cover actuarial about-**85 percent** card is not that card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover IPERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/mfprsi/`, `/iowa-mfprsi-explained/`, or `/iowa-fire-police/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position, the notes money-weighted line, and the GASB funded card are the sourced lines:

1. **Fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits** **$3,496,382,132** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$3,311,608,929**). Investments at fair value **$3,492,186,395**. Do not treat leftover IPERS **$46,740,552,174**, leftover Nebraska School **$18,532,685,257**, leftover Omaha OSERS, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$4,142,002,011**. Net pension liability **$645,619,879**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **84.41 percent** at the **7.5 percent** discount. The annual-report cover actuarial about-**85 percent** card is not the GASB card.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **9.30 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **7.93 percent**). This filing does not print a separate official one-year time-weighted house card used on this page.

Do not flatten MFPRSI into [Iowa IPERS](/iowa-ipers/), [Nebraska School Employees Retirement Plan](/nebraska-school/), or [Omaha School Employees Retirement Plan](/omaha-osers/). IPERS is Iowa’s statewide civilian file. Nebraska School is Nebraska statewide school. OSERS is Omaha Class V. This is **one Iowa municipal sworn file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Iowa fire and police AUM” has to name the book. The **$3,496,382,132** line is fiduciary net position. The **$3,492,186,395** line is investments. The **84.41 percent** card is the June GASB ratio. The about-**85 percent** card is the cover actuarial line. The **9.30 percent** card is money-weighted. Those are not the same mandate.
