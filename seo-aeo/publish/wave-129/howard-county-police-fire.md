# Howard County Police and Fire Employees’ Retirement Plan, explained

Howard County Police and Fire Employees’ Retirement Plan is Howard County, Maryland’s sworn police-and-fire pension, not leftover Maryland SRPS, leftover Montgomery County Employee Retirement Plans, leftover Howard County Retirement Plan, leftover Baltimore City ERS, leftover Ohio Police & Fire, and not leftover Fairfax PORS. The official [FY2025 financial statements](https://cc.howardcountymd.gov/sites/default/files/2025-12/Howard%20County%20Police%20%26%20Fire%20Pension%20Plan%20Final%20FY25.pdf) print fiduciary net position held in trust for pension benefits of **$993,303,977** at **30 June 2025**. Statement total investments were **$991,727,810**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. RSI money-weighted return was **9.9 percent** net. The GASB card uses fiduciary net position **$995,102,591**, not the statement line, and prints **89.99 percent** of total pension liability. Actuarial funded **88.6 percent** at **1 July 2024** is not the GASB card. Do not add this book to leftover Howard County Retirement Plan. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Howard County Retirement Plan overwrite or a leftover Maryland SRPS overwrite.** Do not mint `/howard-pf/` or `/hcpfrp/`. MD&A condensed investments **$991,303,977** is not the statement investments line.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Fiduciary net position held in trust for pension benefits** **$993,303,977** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$899,987,953**). The change is **$93,316,024**. Statement total investments **$991,727,810** is not fiduciary net position. MD&A condensed investments **$991,303,977** is not the statement investments line. The Plan prints a proportionate share of a combined pension investment pool. It does not own an undivided interest in specific pool assets. Deferred Retirement Option Plan balance **$14,846,518** is not fiduciary net position. Do not add this book to leftover Howard County Retirement Plan. Do not treat leftover Maryland SRPS, leftover Ohio Police & Fire, leftover Fairfax PORS, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards use a different fiduciary-net-position print.** Notes fiduciary net position **$995,102,591**. Total pension liability **$1,105,832,715**. Net pension liability **$110,730,124**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **89.99 percent**. That GASB fiduciary-net-position print is not the statement **$993,303,977** line. Actuarial funded **88.6 percent** at **1 July 2024** is not the GASB card and is not a 30 June 2025 print.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **9.9 percent** for fiscal 2025, net of pension-plan investment expense (2024 **9.8 percent**). The filing prints pool time-weighted returns by asset class. It does not print one official house time-weighted total on the notes return line.

Do not flatten Howard County Police and Fire into [Howard County Retirement Plan](/howard-county-retirement/), [Maryland SRPS](/maryland-srps/), [Montgomery County Employee Retirement Plans](/montgomery-erp/), [Ohio Police & Fire](/ohio-police-fire/), or [Fairfax PORS](/fairfax-pors/). Howard County Retirement Plan is the civilian file. Ohio Police & Fire is a statewide sworn file. This is **one Howard sworn police-and-fire file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Howard County police-and-fire AUM” has to name the book. The **$993,303,977** line is statement fiduciary net position. The **$991,727,810** line is statement investments. The **$995,102,591** line is the GASB fiduciary-net-position print. The **89.99 percent** card is the GASB ratio. The **88.6 percent** card is the July 2024 actuarial ratio. The **9.9 percent** card is the money-weighted return. Those are not the same mandate.
