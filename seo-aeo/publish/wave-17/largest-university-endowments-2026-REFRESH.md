# Largest university endowments, 2026

UAO lists only houses that have printed an official endowment or investment-pool value. Harvard Management Company valued the Harvard endowment at **$56.9 billion on 30 June 2025** (**11.9%** net). Yale News printed **$44.1 billion** and **11.1%** net at the same date. Stanford’s endowment was **$40.8 billion at 31 August 2025**; its Merged Pool was **$47.7 billion** and **14.3%** at **30 June 2025** — those are **different books**. PRINCO printed Princeton at **$36.4 billion** and **11.0%** at **30 June 2025**. The live UAO page’s **$50 billion** Harvard line, **$35–40 billion** peer band, and homemade MIT / pile totals are dead. This is not investment advice.

**Last updated:** 26 August 2026 · **Desk:** UAO Research · **Canonical URL:** `/largest-university-endowments-2026/`

The live page this overwrites (4 July 2026) still says Harvard “surpasses **$50 billion**,” groups Yale, Stanford, Princeton and MIT at **$35–40 billion**, and invents a combined pile. Official FY2025 filings are on the table below. This year-stamped URL is already live — overwrite it. Do **not** 301 this list onto [`/endowment-model-explained/`](/endowment-model-explained/) or [`/yale-endowment-model/`](/yale-endowment-model/). Those are different jobs.

Fiscal years are not aligned. Harvard, Yale and Princeton close the investment year on **30 June**. Stanford’s endowment print is **31 August**. Do not rank the four as if they sat on one date with one definition.

## Official prints we will reprint

| House | Official print | What the print is | Return | As-of | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Harvard | **$56.9 billion** | Endowment value | **11.9%** net; distributions **nearly 40%** of operating revenue; since-founding ~**11%** a year | 30 Jun 2025 | [HMC Partners & Performance](https://www.hmc.harvard.edu/partners-performance/) / [FY25 HMC annual report](https://www.hmc.harvard.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/FY25_HMC_Annual_Report.pdf) |
| Yale | **$44.1 billion** | Endowment value | **11.1%** net (**$4.5 billion** gains; **$2.1 billion** distributed); ten-year **9.4%**; target spend **5.25%** | 30 Jun 2025 | [Yale News, 24 Oct 2025](https://news.yale.edu/2025/10/24/yale-reports-investment-return-fiscal-2025) |
| Stanford | **$40.8 billion** | University endowment (Merged Pool **plus** other real-estate assets) | See Merged Pool row | 31 Aug 2025 (fiscal year-end) | [Stanford Report, 7 Oct 2025](https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2025/10/report-investment-portfolio-value-endowment) |
| Stanford Merged Pool | **$47.7 billion** | Principal investment vehicle — endowment funds **plus** Stanford Health Care and Stanford Medicine Children’s Health reserves and other long-term funds | **14.3%** net; five-year **11.7%**; ten-year **9.4%** | 30 Jun 2025 | Same |
| Princeton (PRINCO) | **$36.4 billion** | Endowment value printed by the investment office | **11.0%** | 30 Jun 2025 | [PRINCO, Supporting the University](https://princo.princeton.edu/impact/supporting-the-university/) |

Do **not** treat Stanford’s **$47.7 billion** Merged Pool as the endowment. The university said so: the pool also holds health-system capital reserves. The endowment disbursed **$1.9 billion** in fiscal 2025 (nearly double the **$1.1 billion** disbursed in 2015). Stanford expects **$2.0 billion** to flow from the investment portfolio to the operating budget in fiscal 2026. About **75%** of endowment payout is restricted. CEO of Stanford Management Company named on the release: **Robert Wallace**.

Yale’s CIO named on the 24 October 2025 release is **Matt Mendelsohn**. HMC’s own page and FY25 report are the **$56.9 billion / 11.9%** pair. Do not put the live page’s **$50 billion** back.

Princeton’s endowment provides **roughly two-thirds** of the university’s operating revenue and **53%** of the student financial-aid programme. PRINCO’s Class of 2028 lines: **71.5%** of students qualify for aid; **21.7%** are Pell-eligible.

## What is not official — do not put it back

- **Harvard “exceeding $50 billion” / $50.7 billion.** Dead. Official is **$56.9 billion**.
- **Yale $41.4 billion.** That is the FY2024 print. Official FY2025 is **$44.1 billion**.
- **Stanford $37.3 billion** as if it were current endowment. Official endowment is **$40.8 billion** at 31 August 2025.
- **MIT $35–40 billion.** UAO has no MITIMCo filing on this desk. Do not invent one to fill a “top five.”
- A homemade **$275 billion** (or any other) combined pile. Adding four different books, two dates, and a Merged Pool is not a statistic.
- **NACUBO 2026** as if UAO had the study. We do not. Stanford cited a **preliminary** Cambridge Associates median of **10.9%** for the year — that is Stanford’s citation, not a licence to invent everyone else’s NAV.
- Minting `/harvard-endowment-explained/`, `/stanford-endowment-explained/`, or a **2027** year-stamp. Category define stays on [`/endowment-model-explained/`](/endowment-model-explained/). Named Yale model stays on [`/yale-endowment-model/`](/yale-endowment-model/).

Houses we will add when they print an official figure we can fetch: MIT, Penn, Notre Dame, Michigan, Texas, Duke, Northwestern, Columbia, Chicago, Washington. Until then they are absent, not estimated.

## How a peer should read it

This is not a league table of “who won the endowment model.” It is four official stationery lines and a warning about Stanford’s two books. [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/) PERF is **$637.1 billion** at 30 June 2026. That is a public pension, not an endowment — do not drop it into this list.
