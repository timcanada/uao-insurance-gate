# How do Australian supers differ from US public plans?

Australian super is a compulsory employer contribution into an APRA- or ATO-regulated account. The Super Guarantee is 12 percent of ordinary time earnings from 1 July 2025, and from 1 July 2026 Payday Super calculates that contribution on each payday. A US public pension is typically a governmental defined-benefit plan that sits outside ERISA Title I. Those are different legal persons. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## Compulsory DC vs governmental DB

The Australian Taxation Office’s [super guarantee rates](https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/key-superannuation-rates-and-thresholds/super-guarantee) and [how much super to pay](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/super-for-employers/paying-super-contributions/how-much-super-to-pay) pages are the sourced contribution file:

1. From **1 July 2025** the Super Guarantee percentage is **12 percent** of an eligible employee’s **ordinary time earnings** for the quarter. The ATO calls this the final scheduled increase. Ordinary time earnings sit in the *Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992*.
2. From **1 July 2026**, with Payday Super, employers must pay Super Guarantee **for each payday**. The minimum is calculated as a percentage of each eligible employee’s qualifying earnings.
3. A US **governmental plan** is a different statute. [29 U.S.C. § 1003(b)(1)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/29/1003) excludes governmental plans from ERISA Title I. The legal administrator of that plan is the person named in the instrument, else the sponsor — already on [what an administrator is for a pension plan](/what-is-an-administrator-for-a-pension-plan/).

An APRA **RSE licensee** is the trustee of the Australian book. That person already sits on [what a superannuation trustee is](/what-is-a-superannuation-trustee/). A CalPERS-style public DB is a legislature and a board paying named members. Do not call Australian super “the Australian CalPERS.”

## What an owner is actually buying

A consultant selling into Australian super is selling to a **licensed trustee** of a compulsory DC account with a 12 percent contribution and a payday clock.

A consultant selling into a US public plan is selling to a **governmental DB** that does not file Form 5500 under Title I.
