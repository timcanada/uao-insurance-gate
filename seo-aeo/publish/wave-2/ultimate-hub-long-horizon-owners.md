# Long-horizon owners — sovereign funds, pensions, family offices and royal capital

Long-horizon owners are the institutions that hold residual investment risk for decades: sovereign wealth funds, public and corporate pensions, endowments, insurers, multi-generational family offices, and royal family investment offices. Universal Asset Owners is the newsroom and registry built for those desks — and for the counsel, banks, technologists and event hosts who need to stand in front of them. This hub is the door. It is not a ranking of managers and not investment advice.

Hedge funds are usually *agents*, not owners. We cover them when an owner is on the other side of the trade — not as the beat.
