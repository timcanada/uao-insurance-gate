# Sonoma County Employees’ Retirement Association, explained

The Sonoma County Employees’ Retirement Association is Sonoma County’s 1937-Act employee pension, not leftover Sacramento SCERS and not leftover SBCERS. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://scretire.org/Microsites/SCERA/Documents/Financial/ACFR%202025.pdf) prints fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits of **$4,330,832 thousand** at **31 December 2025**. Total investments were **$4,486,128 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted return was **18.22 percent**. The money-weighted return was **16.67 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **106.42 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Sacramento SCERS overwrite or a leftover SBCERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/scera/`, `/sonoma-scera-explained/`, or `/sonoma-county-employees/`. Do not mint `/scers/` (Sacramento already reserved that short). This book is a calendar year-end file, like OCERS and SJCERA, not a 30 June file.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits** **$4,330,832 thousand** at **31 December 2025** (2024 **$3,758,167 thousand**), up **$572,665 thousand** / **15.2 percent**. Total investments **$4,486,128 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$4,737,017 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Those three prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover Sacramento SCERS, leftover SBCERS, leftover SBCERA, leftover LACERA, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB versus actuarial funded cards.** Total pension liability **$4,069,702 thousand**. Fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **106.42 percent**. The actuarial funded ratio **94.6 percent** uses a different methodology. Those two funded cards are not interchangeable.
3. **Returns.** Time-weighted return **18.22 percent** against an assumed rate of **6.75 percent**. Money-weighted return **16.67 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Those two official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Sonoma SCERA into [Sacramento County](/sacramento-scers/), [SBCERS](/sbcers/), [SBCERA](/sbcera/), [SJCERA](/sjcera/), [ICERS](/icers/), or [MercedCERA](/mercedcera/). Sacramento County is a different 1937-Act file. SBCERS is Santa Barbara **County**. This is **one Sonoma County employee file**.
