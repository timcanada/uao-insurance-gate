# Louisiana Firefighters’ Retirement System, explained

Firefighters’ Retirement System is Louisiana’s municipal-firefighter pension, not leftover Louisiana TRSL, leftover LASERS, leftover Municipal Police Employees’ Retirement System, and not leftover Baton Rouge CPERS. The official [30 June 2025 Firefighters’ Retirement System financial report](https://lafrs.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/FRS-2025-Financial-Statement-Report-Signed-2025.pdf) prints fiduciary net position of **$2,808,100,816** at **30 June 2025**. Investments **$2,770,703,124** are not that card. Money-weighted return was **12.01 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. MD&A **11.5 percent** is not that card. GASB **86.96 percent** is not actuarial **86.75 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Municipal Police overwrite.** Do not mint `/frs/`, `/louisiana-frs-explained/`, or `/lafrs/`. The house report excludes Orleans Parish and the City of Baton Rouge.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statements of fiduciary net position, the GASB 67 note, and the money-weighted schedule are the sourced lines:

1. **Fiduciary net position** **$2,808,100,816** at **30 June 2025** (beginning **$2,510,150,455**). Total investments at fair value **$2,770,703,124**. Do not treat leftover Louisiana TRSL **$29,334,227,630**, leftover LASERS **$17,223,211,406**, leftover Municipal Police **$3,133,488,618**, leftover Clerks of Court **$906,589,423**, leftover Baton Rouge CPERS Trust **$1,439,791,602**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$3,229,309,328**. Employers’ net pension liability **$421,208,512**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **86.96 percent**. Transmittal actuarial **86.75 percent** is not the GASB card.
3. **Returns.** Required supplementary money-weighted return **12.01 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **10.52 percent**). MD&A **11.5 percent** net of fees is not the RSI card.

Do not flatten Firefighters’ Retirement System into [Municipal Police Employees’ Retirement System](/louisiana-mpers/), [Louisiana TRSL](/louisiana-trsl/), [LASERS](/louisiana-lasers/), or [Baton Rouge City-Parish Employees’ Retirement System](/baton-rouge-employees/). Municipal Police is the Louisiana municipal-police file. TRSL is the Louisiana educator file. LASERS is the Louisiana state-employee file. CPERS Trust is a Baton Rouge city-parish file. This is **one Louisiana municipal-firefighter file**.
