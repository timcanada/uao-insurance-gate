# Employees’ Retirement System of Baltimore County, explained

The Employees’ Retirement System of Baltimore County is Baltimore County’s pension, not leftover Maryland SRPS and not leftover Employees’ Retirement System of the City of Baltimore. The official [30 June 2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.baltimorecountymd.gov/files/departments/budget-and-finance/documents/fy2025-ers-acfr.pdf) prints combined net position restricted for pension benefits of **$3,317,788 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Investments were **$3,302,427 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return was **10.57 percent** net of investment expense. Time-weighted return was **10.6 percent** gross of fees. Combined fiduciary net position was **59.47 percent** of total pension liability. Actuarial funded **58.2 percent** is not the GASB card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Maryland SRPS overwrite or a leftover Baltimore City ERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/baltimore-county/` or `/baltimore-county-ers-explained/`. Do not mint `/bcers/` — that short is already reserved as the leftover [Baltimore City ERS](/baltimore-city-ers/) twin. Statement prints are in thousands. Combined investments **$3,302,427 thousand** are not fiduciary net position. MD&A about-**$3.318 billion** is the thousands line rounded. Plan A **$2,910,341 thousand** and Plan B **$407,447 thousand** are not a second combined AUM.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combined statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Combined net position restricted for pension benefits** **$3,317,788 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$3,097,608 thousand**). The change is **$220,180 thousand**. Combining Plan A **$2,910,341 thousand** plus Plan B **$407,447 thousand** equals that combined card. Total investments **$3,302,427 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Securities-lending collateral **$17,587 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. General-employee DROP allowance about-**$50.1 million** is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover Maryland SRPS **$73,240,774 thousand**, leftover Employees’ Retirement System of the City of Baltimore **$2,264,990,790**, leftover Baltimore Fire and Police **$3,467,209,097**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Combined total pension liability **$5,579,031 thousand**. Combined net pension liability **$2,261,243 thousand**. Combined fiduciary net position / total pension liability **59.47 percent** (2024 **56.59 percent**). Plan A **57.74 percent** and Plan B **75.65 percent** are plan cards. Actuarial funded **58.2 percent** as of **1 July 2024** is not the GASB card.
3. **Returns.** Required-supplementary money-weighted return **10.57 percent** net of investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **9.80 percent**). MD&A time-weighted return **10.6 percent** is **gross of fees**. Those official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten the Employees’ Retirement System of Baltimore County into [Maryland SRPS](/maryland-srps/), [Employees’ Retirement System of the City of Baltimore](/baltimore-city-ers/), [Baltimore Fire and Police](/baltimore-fire-police/), [Elected Officials’ Retirement System of the City of Baltimore](/baltimore-eos/), [Montgomery County Employee Retirement Plans](/montgomery-erp/), or [Howard County Retirement Plan](/howard-county-retirement/). Maryland SRPS is the statewide file. Baltimore City ERS, Baltimore Fire and Police, and Baltimore EOS are city files. This is **one Baltimore County file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Baltimore County pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$3,317,788 thousand** line is combined fiduciary net position. The **$2,910,341 thousand** line is Plan A. The **$407,447 thousand** line is Plan B. The **59.47 percent** card is the combined GASB ratio. The **58.2 percent** card is the actuarial ratio. The **10.57 percent** card is money-weighted. The **10.6 percent** card is time-weighted gross. Those are not the same mandate.
