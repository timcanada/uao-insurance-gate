# Public Investment Fund: more than US$900 billion, not $925 billion at end-2025

Public Investment Fund’s official size line is **more than US$900 billion**. The US$2 trillion figure is a **2030 KPI**, not current AUM. The live institution page still prints **roughly $925 billion at end-2025**, then raises it from **$913 billion at end-2024**, and stacks Gulf funds at **roughly $5 trillion**. Those are tracker and press dollars. They are not the official current print this desk can cite.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## Official numbers

| Line | Official print |

| Official size | **More than US$900 billion** |
| 2030 figure | **US$2 trillion** — a KPI, not current AUM |
| Combined Gulf AUM | **No official pile** |

Do not print **$925 billion**, **$1.15 trillion**, **$1.21 trillion**, or **$2.67 trillion** as current AUM. The $2T KPI already has its own keeper: `/saudi-pif-s-road-to-2-trillion-by-2030/`.

## What PIF is

PIF is the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia’s public investment fund and a Vision 2030 vehicle. It is a sovereign investor. It is **not** [GOSI](/saudi-gosi-explained/) (a social-insurance scheme). It is **not** [ADIA](/adia-abu-dhabi-investment-authority/) (an Abu Dhabi reserve investor that does not publish AUM). It is **not** [Mubadala](/mubadala-investment-company/) (AED 1.4 trillion / US$385 billion at YE2025).

A writer who needs a more precise current dollar than “more than US$900 billion” is not quoting PIF.

## What is not official

- **Roughly $925 billion at end-2025** as current AUM.
- **$913 billion at end-2024** sold as the current print.
- A homemade **$5 trillion** Gulf pile on this URL.
- Treating the **$2 trillion by 2030** KPI as current size.
