# Norway vs Canada model: GPFG is kroner; CPP and OTPP are Canadian dollars

Norway’s Government Pension Fund Global is a kroner book: **22,683 billion kroner** at **30 June 2026**. Canada’s two usual exhibits are pensions, not SWFs: CPP Investments printed **C$793.3 billion** at **31 March 2026**; Ontario Teachers’ printed **C$303.2 billion** on its IR page at **30 June 2026** (audited year-end **C$279.4 billion** at **31 December 2025**). The live page, dated July 2026, still assigns GPFG **$1.3 trillion**, CPPIB **$475 billion as of June 2023**, and OTPP **$233 billion as of December 2023**. Those dollars are not the current filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## Official numbers (do not invent a winner)

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| CPP Investments | Investor of Canada Pension Plan contributions. **Not a SWF** | **C$793.3 billion** | FY2026, **31 March 2026** |
| Ontario Teachers’ Pension Plan | Joint-sponsored DB plan for Ontario teachers. **Not a SWF** | **C$303.2 billion** (IR); audited **C$279.4 billion** | IR **30 June 2026**; AR **31 December 2025** |

Print kroner next to Canadian dollars. Do **not** convert 22,683 billion kroner into a homemade US-dollar AUM so Norway can be called larger. Do not convert C$793.3 billion into USD so Canada can be ranked against GPFG.

Of the GPFG stock at 30 June 2026, NBIM prints return **15,210 billion kroner**, net inflows **5,509 billion kroner**, and currency **1,965 billion kroner**.

## What the models actually are

The contrast is real. [GPFG](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/) is a savings vehicle with no named-member liability. It is benchmark-relative, listed-heavy, and constrained by a public ethics framework. [CPP Investments](/cpp-investments-explained/) and [OTPP](/ontario-teachers-pension-plan-explained/) sit against pension promises. They run more internal private markets and direct ownership. CPP is **not** a sovereign wealth fund. That Q&A already lives on [`/is-cpp-investments-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/`](/is-cpp-investments-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/).

A “Norway vs Canada” page that needs GPFG **$1.3 trillion** and CPP **$475 billion as of June 2023** to make the contrast is a stale tracker page, not a filing.
