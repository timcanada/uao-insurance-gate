# New York City Board of Education Retirement System, explained

The New York City Board of Education Retirement System is New York City’s non-pedagogical education pension, not leftover New York City Teachers and not the Comptroller’s five-system combined card. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.bers.nyc.gov/assets/bers/downloads/pdf/publications/bers_acfr_fy2025.pdf) prints combined net position restricted for benefits of **$11,173,970 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. The Qualified Pension Plan book was **$7,624,616 thousand**. The Tax-Deferred Annuity Program book was **$3,549,354 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The fixed-return Total Fund return was **10.54 percent** net of management fees. Qualified Pension Plan fiduciary net position was **102.56 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combining statements of fiduciary net position are the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Combined net position restricted for benefits** **$11,173,970 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Qualified Pension Plan **$7,624,616 thousand** (2024 **$6,848,121 thousand**). Tax-Deferred Annuity Program **$3,549,354 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. Qualified Pension Plan investments at fair value **$10,245,563 thousand** include the Tax-Deferred Annuity Program’s share of the fixed-return book and are not Qualified Pension Plan fiduciary net position. Amount due to the Tax-Deferred Annuity Program’s fixed-return fund **$2,944,811 thousand** is a Qualified Pension Plan liability, not a second Qualified Pension Plan book. The transmittal Total Fund card **$10.02 billion** is the fixed-return program, not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover New York City Teachers, leftover New York City Employees, leftover New York State Common **$226.8 billion**, or the Comptroller’s FY2026 five-system **$326.3 billion** card as this filing.
2. **Named books are not interchangeable.** The Qualified Pension Plan is a cost-sharing, multiple-employer public-employee retirement system for non-pedagogical Department of Education staff and named related employers. The Tax-Deferred Annuity Program is a voluntary Internal Revenue Code section 403(b) savings option. Qualified Pension Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **102.56 percent**. That funded card is the Qualified Pension Plan book, not the Tax-Deferred Annuity Program.
3. **Returns.** Fixed-return Total Fund **10.54 percent** net of management fees, against the house policy benchmark **11.45 percent**. Annual money-weighted return on the System’s fixed-return fund investments **10.54 percent**. Variable-return fund **12.85 percent**. Those three official one-year prints are not interchangeable. Variable-return assets held at the Teachers’ Retirement System **$1,016 million** are not fiduciary net position.

Do not flatten the Board of Education Retirement System into [New York City Teachers](/nyc-teachers/) or [New York City Retirement Systems](/new-york-city-crs/). New York City Teachers is the pedagogical file. This is **one non-pedagogical education file**. The Comptroller’s FY2026 **$326.3 billion** card is a five-system combined print at **30 June 2026**. This page is the house **30 June 2025** ACFR. Those vintages are not interchangeable.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “BERS AUM” has to name the book. The **$11,173,970 thousand** line is the combined Qualified Pension Plan and Tax-Deferred Annuity book. The **$7,624,616 thousand** line is the Qualified Pension Plan. The **$10,245,563 thousand** line is Qualified Pension Plan investments, including the Tax-Deferred Annuity share. Those are not the same mandate.
