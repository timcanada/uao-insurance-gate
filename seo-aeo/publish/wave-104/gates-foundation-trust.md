# Gates Foundation Trust, explained

The Gates Foundation Trust is the endowment legal person, not the grantmaking foundation and not a second Wellcome page. The Trust’s [audited financial statements](https://docs.gatesfoundation.org/documents/2025_gatesfoundationtrust_fs.pdf) print net assets without donor restrictions of **$89,249,227 thousand** (**$89.2 billion**) at **31 December 2025**. Investments were **$89,346,091 thousand**. Grants to the Gates Foundation were **$5,287,000 thousand** in 2025. The two-entity structure dates to **October 2006**; the names were changed in **2025**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (Trust FS, 31 December 2025)

The foundation’s own [financials page](https://www.gatesfoundation.org/about/financials) says the original trustees created a two-entity structure in **October 2006**: the grantmaking foundation and the Trust that holds the endowment. Both are tax-exempt private foundations. In **2025** the names became Gates Foundation and Gates Foundation Trust. The Trust statements (in thousands) are the sourced split:

1. **Net assets without donor restrictions** **$89,249,227 thousand** at **31 December 2025** (2024: **$77,237,720 thousand**). **Investments** **$89,346,091 thousand**. Total assets **$93,167,664 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable.
2. **2025 activity:** contributions of cash and other financial assets **$4,662,042 thousand**; net investment income **$12,220,500 thousand**; **grants to the Gates Foundation** **$5,287,000 thousand** (2024: **$8,096,000 thousand**). Change in net assets **$12,011,507 thousand**.
3. The Foundation’s own 2025 audited statements print a **beneficial interest** in the Trust’s net assets of **$89,249,227 thousand** at **31 December 2025** — the same Trust net-asset line — because the Foundation has the legal right to call on the Trust.

Do not flatten the Trust into [Wellcome](/wellcome-trust/). Wellcome is one UK charity with one portfolio. This is **two US legal persons**.
