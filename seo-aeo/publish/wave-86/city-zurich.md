# Relocating to Zurich for a Swiss owner or family-office desk

Zurich is Switzerland’s markets floor. It is not automatically the federal pension’s home file. The Swiss Federal Pension Fund [PUBLICA](https://www.publica.ch/en/contact) lists **Eigerstrasse 57, 3007 Bern**. A move here is often a move into a family office, an insurer investment office, or a window other owners staff — not a weekend in Bern with a different logo. Right to work sits with official Swiss sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit in Bern

- PUBLICA is the Swiss federal pension fund. Official contact is Bern. **Do not treat this city page as a second PUBLICA explainer.** Do not invent a PUBLICA AUM on this page.
- Family offices and multi-family offices cluster here. The job is still [a family office](/what-is-a-family-office/), not a product shelf. Do not invent a family-office AUM. Do not mint `/family-office/`.
- Insurer investment offices sit in this city. Those are insurance books, not a second public-pension AUM. Confirm the legal person on the employer site.

Global owners staff Zurich as a *window* more often than as HQ. Ask whether you are building a book or staffing a time zone. That is the same satellite problem our [London note](/careers/intelligence/why-london-satellites-fail/) already names.

## Papers

Start at the [State Secretariat for Migration — working in Switzerland](https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/en/home/overview-arbeit.html). Cantonal rules still apply. Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Why people underestimate the job

German is the language of legitimacy in this canton even when the dealing language is English. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail. Confidentiality is not a preference inside a family office.
