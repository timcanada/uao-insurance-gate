# Dallas Police and Fire Pension System, explained

Dallas Police and Fire Pension System is Dallas’s sworn police-and-fire pension, not leftover Houston fire, leftover Houston police, leftover San Antonio Fire and Police, leftover Jacksonville Police and Fire, leftover Austin Police, leftover LAFPP, leftover San José Police and Fire, leftover Texas ERS, leftover TMRS, and not leftover Dallas civilian ERF. The official [2025 ACFR](https://www.dpfp.org/Resources/7ad6f08a-2242-466b-bb4b-74405e461055/2025%20DPFP%20ACFR%20Final%20web.pdf) prints combined fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits of **$2,277,013,787** at **31 December 2025**. Combined Pension Plan was **$2,251,124,585**. Supplemental Plan was **$25,889,202**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. Group Trust money-weighted return was **16.47 percent** net of fees. Combined fiduciary net position was **39.0 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Houston fire overwrite, a leftover Houston police overwrite, a leftover San Antonio overwrite, a leftover Jacksonville overwrite, or a leftover Austin Police overwrite.** Do not mint `/dpfp/` or `/dallas-pf/`. The Combined Pension Plan and the Supplemental Plan are co-invested through a Group Master Trust. They are still two books.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The combining statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Combined fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits** **$2,277,013,787** at **31 December 2025** (2024 **$2,034,276,938**). Combined Pension Plan **$2,251,124,585**. Supplemental Plan **$25,889,202**. Total investments **$2,157,249,180**. Combined Pension Plan investments **$2,135,775,355**. Those prints are not interchangeable. MD&A net position **$2,277,014 thousand** is the combined book in thousands, not a second AUM. Do not treat leftover Houston Firefighters, leftover Houston Police, leftover San Antonio Fire and Police **$4,493,776 thousand**, leftover Jacksonville **$2,903,990,005**, leftover Austin Police **$1,211,262,982**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Combined Pension Plan total pension liability **$5,766,994 thousand**. Combined GASB fiduciary net position **$2,251,125 thousand**. Combined net pension liability **$3,515,869 thousand**. Combined fiduciary net position / total pension liability **39.0 percent** (2024 **36.1 percent**). Supplemental Plan fiduciary net position / total pension liability **52.9 percent** is not the Combined card. January 1, 2026 actuarial-value funded **35.2 percent** is not the GASB card.
3. **Returns.** Group Trust money-weighted return **16.47 percent** net of fees (2024 **8.30 percent**). The house consultant uses a one-quarter lag on private-asset fair-value adjustments. The house ACFR does not give this page a separate official time-weighted card to quote as the money-weighted substitute. Do not invent one.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Dallas Police and Fire AUM” has to name the book. The **$2,277,013,787** line is combined fiduciary net position. The **$2,251,124,585** line is the Combined Pension Plan. The **$25,889,202** line is the Supplemental Plan. The **$2,157,249,180** line is total investments. The **39.0 percent** card is the Combined GASB ratio. The **35.2 percent** card is the next-day actuarial-value ratio. Those are not the same mandate.
