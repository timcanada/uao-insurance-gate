# City of Boca Raton Executive Employees’ Retirement Plan, explained

The City of Boca Raton Executive Employees’ Retirement Plan is Boca Raton’s executive-employee pension, not leftover Boca General Employees, and not leftover Boca Police and Fire. The official [30 September 2025 and 2024 City of Boca Raton Executive Employees’ Retirement Plan financial statements](https://retirement.flcities.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/ADA-comp-Boca-Raton-EERP-Issued-FS-FY2025.pdf) print net position restricted for pension benefits of **$75,518,835** at **30 September 2025**. Investments **$74,703,594** are not that card. Notes money-weighted return was **8.84 percent** net of plan investment expense. GASB **85.49 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Boca civilian overwrite.** Do not mint `/boca-raton-eerp/`, `/boca-executive/`, `/eerp/`, or `/gepp/`. `/gepp/` remains Jacksonville.

## What is official (30 September 2025)

The statements of fiduciary net position, the GASB note, and the money-weighted schedule are the sourced lines:

1. **Net position restricted for pension benefits** **$75,518,835** at **30 September 2025**. Beginning **$70,495,791**. Change **$5,023,044**. Cash **$815,241**. Investments **$74,703,594**. Total cash and investments **$75,518,835** is not a second AUM. DROP account **$1,673,172** plus self-directed DROP **$1,406,037** equals **$3,079,209**, included in investments, not a second AUM. Do not treat leftover Boca General Employees **$256,729,999**, leftover Boca Police and Fire **$613,033,507**, leftover Fort Lauderdale Employees **$755,226,097**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$88,337,317**. Net pension liability **$12,818,482**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **85.49 percent** (2024 **82.24 percent**). Do not treat **$88,337,317** as AUM.
3. **Returns and cash flow.** Notes and required supplementary money-weighted return **8.84 percent** net of plan investment expense for the year ended **30 September 2025** (2024 **21.34 percent**). Time-weighted total fund return **8.93 percent** is not the money-weighted card. City contributions **$2,818,993**. Employee contributions **$932,150**. Transfers from other retirement plans **$843,624**. Net investment income **$6,152,337**. Retirement benefits **$3,744,837**. DROP distributions **$1,815,968**. Termination refunds **$31,297**. Administrative expenses **$131,958**. The independent auditors’ report is dated **24 February 2026**, CBIZ CPAs P.C.

Do not flatten the Executive Employees’ Retirement Plan into [City of Boca Raton General Employees’ Pension Plan](/boca-raton-employees/) or [City of Boca Raton Police and Firefighters’ Retirement System](/boca-raton-police-fire/). General Employees is the broader civilian file. Police and Fire is the sworn file. This is **one Boca Raton executive file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Boca Raton executive AUM” has to name the book. The **$75,518,835** line is fiduciary net position at **30 September 2025**. The **$74,703,594** line is investments. The **$3,079,209** line is DROP. The **8.84 percent** card is money-weighted. The **8.93 percent** card is time-weighted. The **85.49 percent** card is GASB. Those are not leftover Boca General Employees **$256,729,999**.
