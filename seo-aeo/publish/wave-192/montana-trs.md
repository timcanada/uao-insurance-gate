# Montana Teachers’ Retirement System, explained

The Teachers’ Retirement System of Montana is Montana’s statewide teacher pension, not leftover Montana MPERA (**$10,563,079 thousand**), leftover NYSTRS (**$154,193,125 thousand**), leftover Georgia TRS. The official [30 June 2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://trs.mt.gov/_docs/financial-reports/ACFR-2025.pdf) prints net position restricted for pension benefits of **$5,416,892,548** at **30 June 2025**. CAPP **$5,325,487,028** is not that card. Notes money-weighted return was **9.46 percent**. GASB **75.22 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

The Legislative Audit Division issued an unmodified opinion dated **23 December 2025**, Helena, Montana.
