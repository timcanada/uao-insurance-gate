This page overwrites a live UAO methodology stub that treats consultancy estimates as a data source. It assigns GPFG **$1.34 trillion** at Q3 2024, CIC **$1.02 trillion**, NSSF **$500+ billion**, and SAFE **$2+ trillion**. Those dollars are not the houses’ current official lines.

Twin `/how-swf-aum-is-estimated/` still returns **200**. 301 that URL here after paste. Do not mint a third methodology slug.

## Official method

| Step | What UAO reprints | What UAO refuses |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The house’s own dated filing, in the currency on the page | A homemade US-dollar conversion presented as AUM |
| 2 | “Does not publish AUM” when the official site has no size widget | Press $925bn / $1.21T / tracker $2T as current PIF |
| 3 | Separate legal persons kept separate (CIC total vs Huijin vs SAFE) | A consolidated “China SWF” pile |
| 4 | IFSWF membership as a **governance** fact, not a size fact | Treating the Santiago Principles as an AUM database |

IFSWF members commit to the Santiago Principles. That is a transparency pledge. It is not a licence to fill empty AUM cells.
