# Endowment model vs Canada model: Harvard is $56.9bn — not $50.9bn from 2023

## What the models actually are

The [endowment model](/endowment-model-explained/) — the Yale exhibit is on [`/yale-endowment-model/`](/yale-endowment-model/) — is a university spending rule plus a large alternatives book, usually with more outsourcing than a Canadian plan of the same ambition. The Canadian model is an arm’s-length pension with a professional board and large in-house teams. [CPP](/cpp-investments-explained/) and [OTPP](/ontario-teachers-pension-plan-explained/) sit against named-member liabilities. Harvard sits against a university budget. Those are different jobs.

A comparison that needs Harvard **$50.9 billion (June 2023)** and CPP **$600 billion (March 2024)** is a stale tracker page.
