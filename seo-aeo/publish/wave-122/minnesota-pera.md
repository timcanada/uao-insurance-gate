# Minnesota Public Employees Retirement Association, explained

The Public Employees Retirement Association of Minnesota is Minnesota’s statewide public-employee pension, not leftover Colorado PERA, not leftover Minnesota SBI, and not leftover Minnesota TRA. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.mnpera.org/sites/default/files/2025-annual-comprehensive-financial-report.pdf) prints combined net position restricted for pensions of **$47,438,420 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Total investments at fair value were **$46,076,325 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The Combined Funds time-weighted return was **10.90 percent**. General Employees fiduciary net position was **90.78 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Combined net position restricted for pensions** **$47,438,420 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. That combined book is defined-benefit plus defined-contribution. The MD&A defined-benefit net position **$47,322,534 thousand** / **$47.3 billion** is not the combined statement total. Total investments at fair value **$46,076,325 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$49,224,547 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. The Minnesota State Board of Investment Combined Funds PERA General Employees participation **$32,618.9 million** is a Combined Funds slice, not General Employees fiduciary net position. Those five prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover Colorado PERA **$67 billion**, leftover Minnesota SBI Total Funds Under Management **$155,859.3 million**, leftover Minnesota TRA, leftover Illinois IMRF, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books on the same statement.** General Employees **$32,638,737 thousand**. Police and Fire **$13,078,280 thousand**. Correctional **$1,344,302 thousand**. Statewide Volunteer Firefighter defined-benefit **$261,215 thousand**. Public Employees defined-contribution **$106,753 thousand**. Statewide Volunteer Firefighter defined-contribution **$9,133 thousand**. Those six lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat General Employees as the combined book.
3. **Returns and funded cards.** Combined Funds time-weighted return **10.90 percent** / MD&A **10.9 percent**. Money-weighted returns: General Employees **10.95 percent**, Police and Fire **10.95 percent**, Correctional **10.98 percent**. General Employees total pension liability **$35,952,646 thousand**. General Employees net pension liability **$3,313,909 thousand**. General Employees fiduciary net position / total pension liability **90.78 percent**. Police and Fire **91.78 percent**. Correctional **105.18 percent**. Those official one-year prints and funded cards are not interchangeable.

The Minnesota State Board of Investment manages this book inside the Combined Funds with MSRS and TRA. That Combined Funds line is not a second Public Employees Retirement Association AUM.

Do not flatten Minnesota PERA into [Colorado PERA](/colorado-pera/), [Minnesota SBI](/minnesota-sbi/), [Minnesota TRA](/minnesota-tra/), or [Illinois IMRF](/illinois-imrf/). Colorado PERA is a different legal person. Minnesota SBI is the statutory investor. TRA is the teacher plan. This is **one Minnesota public-employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Minnesota PERA AUM” has to name the book. The **$47,438,420 thousand** line is combined fiduciary net position. The **$47,322,534 thousand** line is defined-benefit fiduciary net position. The **$46,076,325 thousand** line is investments. The **$32,618.9 million** line is the Combined Funds General Employees participation. Those are not the same mandate.
