# What Is a Family Office?

A family office is a private organisation that manages the investments and affairs of one ultra-wealthy family — or, in a multi-family office, several families who share the infrastructure. It is an asset owner: it sits with the residual risk. It is not a private bank, and it is not an OCIO unless the family has rented the CIO seat. Universal Asset Owners covers the largest offices as peers of pensions and sovereign funds. This is not investment, tax or legal advice.

**Last updated:** 26 August 2026 · **Desk:** UAO Research

## Family office vs the things people confuse it with

| | Family office | Private bank / wealth manager | OCIO | Sovereign / pension |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Who is the owner | The family | Many clients | The family or institution that hired it | State or beneficiaries |
| Residual risk | Family | Client (the firm is an agent) | Still the family | State / members |
| Typical job | Whole book + tax, estate, family | Products and advice | Outsourced CIO seat | Mandate + public process |
| Privacy | High | Bank-grade | Contract-grade | Often public |

## What it actually does

Investing is the visible job. The office also usually runs tax and estate work, consolidated reporting, philanthropy, succession rules, and a long list of administration. The point of the structure is **one book, one set of incentives**, instead of a pile of accounts at different banks. The systems that keep that book — and a number that matches the custodian — sit on [what a family-office technology stack is](/what-is-a-family-office-technology-stack/).

A [family-office CIO](/what-does-a-family-office-cio-do/) is accountable to a principal or a family board for that whole book.

## How much wealth before a single-family office makes sense

There is no legal minimum. The constraint is cost. A staffed single-family office — investment, ops, compliance, technology — costs millions a year. Industry commentary commonly cites a practical band around **$100–250 million** of investable assets before a dedicated office is rational; many established offices are far larger. Below that, families share infrastructure in a multi-family office.

Treat any dollar threshold as directional. The real test is complexity, privacy, and whether the family wants to own the process.

## How large is the sector

Counts and AUM differ by who is counted. Deloitte’s family-office landscape work describes offices as structures that manage wealth and interests of ultra-high-net-worth families, typically across generations. Commercial trackers have put single-family-office counts in the thousands and assets in the **low trillions of dollars**. We will not pretend one vendor’s 2025 slide is a census. Read the methodology on any figure you reuse.

Geographically the weight sits in the Americas, Europe, the Gulf, and a fast-growing Asia book. Singapore and London are as much family-office cities as they are bank cities. See [relocating to Singapore](/careers/cities/singapore/) and [London](/careers/cities/london/).

## Why this newsroom covers them as owners

The largest multi-generational offices allocate like institutions: private markets, directs, co-invests, a spending rule in all but name. Individually they are usually smaller than NBIM or GPIF. Collectively they are a long-horizon book with more flexibility and less public process. That is why they sit in our [long-horizon owners](/long-horizon-owners/) map and in [family offices as universal owners](/family-offices-as-universal-owners/).

## FAQ

**What is a family office in one sentence?**  
A private firm whose client is one family (or a small group of families) and whose job is to run the capital and the affairs as a single enterprise.

**Is a family office an asset manager?**  
No. An asset manager is an agent. The family office is the owner, even when it hires managers.

**Do royal families have family offices?**  
Some private royal wealth is run as a family office. Some capital next to a court is state capital. Do not mix them. See [royal family investment offices](/royal-family-investment-offices/).
