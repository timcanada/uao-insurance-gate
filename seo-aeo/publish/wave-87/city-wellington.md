# Relocating to Wellington for a New Zealand owner desk

Wellington is New Zealand’s political capital. It is not NZ Super’s office city. The [Guardians of New Zealand Superannuation](/new-zealand-superannuation-fund-explained/) list the [Chief Post Office, Level 1, 12 Queen Street, Auckland Central](https://www.nzsuperfund.nz/contact-us/). A move here is often a move onto a Crown or agency file whose investment committee sits in Auckland — or into a window other owners staff. Right to work sits with official New Zealand sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit in Auckland

- [NZ Super](/new-zealand-superannuation-fund-explained/) — a Crown financial reserve that invests government capital contributions to help meet future superannuation costs. Official contact is Auckland. Official prints live on the keeper. Do not invent an AUM on this page. Do not mint `/wellington-nz-super/`, `/auckland-nz-super/`, or a third NZ Super slug. Do not 301 this city page onto `/nz-super-fund-explained/`.
- Ministers and sponsoring departments sit in this city. That is not the same as the investment committee sitting here. Ask where the IC actually sits.
- Do not mint `/careers/cities/auckland/`. If the desk is Auckland, say so in the brief.

[Australian](/careers/cities/melbourne/) and [Singapore](/careers/cities/singapore/) houses staff this time zone as a *window* more often than as HQ.

## Why people underestimate the job

A capital posting is not automatically the investment book. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail. Satellites fail when the IC is elsewhere — the same problem our [London note](/careers/intelligence/why-london-satellites-fail/) already names.
