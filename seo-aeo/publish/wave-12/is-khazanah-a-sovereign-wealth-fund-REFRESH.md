# Is Khazanah a sovereign wealth fund?

Yes. Khazanah Nasional Berhad **calls itself the sovereign wealth fund of Malaysia**. It is the government’s strategic investment holding company, not the manager of Bank Negara’s reserves. Khazanah Annual Review 2026 printed net assets of **RM105 billion** and total assets of **RM156 billion at 31 December 2025**, with a **5.2%** return for 2025 and a **6.1%** seven-year rolling return. It does **not** publish a USD AUM. This is not investment advice.

NAV is **total assets minus liabilities**. Do not call RM156 billion “AUM.” Do not invent a USD NAV from RM105 billion. [ADIA](/adia-abu-dhabi-investment-authority/) does not publish AUM; do not put a homemade ADIA dollar next to Khazanah. [Mumtalakat](/mumtalakat-bahrain-explained/) did not print AUM in its 2025 release either.

Managing Director named on the release: **Dato’ Amirul Feisal Wan Zahir**.

## Why the answer is yes — and what that still is not

Khazanah’s own “about” line on the KAR 2026 release: it is **the sovereign wealth fund of Malaysia**, entrusted to deliver sustainable value, investing in catalytic sectors, creating value through active stewardship, increasing its global presence, and building capacity and communities.

That is closer to [Temasek](/is-temasek-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/) or [Samruk-Kazyna](/samruk-kazyna-kazakhstan-explained/) — a **strategic holding SWF** — than to [NBIM](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/) or [GIC](/gic-singapore-explained/). Temasek’s own language is “investment company” and Singapore’s MOF splits Temasek from GIC. Khazanah does **not** make that dodge. They use the SWF label.

It is **not**:

- Bank Negara Malaysia’s reserve manager
- the Employees Provident Fund (a pension)
- a savings SWF that publishes a USD league-table AUM
- [PIF](/public-investment-fund-pif/) with a Vision 2030 KPI taped on

IFSWF’s Santiago definition (government-owned; financial objectives; foreign financial assets) can include a house like this. Membership and a self-assessment are the documents, not a $47 billion slide. See [Santiago Principles](/santiago-principles/).

## What KAR 2026 actually asked you to notice

The release’s Advancing Malaysia exhibits, not a dollar AUM:

- Malaysia Airports (MAHB) privatisation completed; **104.4 million** passenger movements, **+11.2%** year-on-year.
- Dana Warisan / Warisan KL conservation (Seri Negara; Bangunan Sultan Abdul Samad Block 1).
- Dana Impak / Jelawang Capital: first five fund managers, more than **10** startups, more than **RM30 million** crowded-in capital.
- **RM330 million** deployed in 2025 for capacity and community ( **RM2.7 billion** cumulative since 2006).
- K-Youth: **9,934** youths trained in 2025; more than **34,000** since 2023.

Those are nation-building lines. They are not a second NAV. Do not add them to RM105 billion.
