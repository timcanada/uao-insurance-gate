# Pennsylvania Municipal Retirement System, explained

Pennsylvania Municipal Retirement System is Pennsylvania’s municipal pension, not leftover PSERS and not leftover Pennsylvania SERS. The official [31 December 2025 PMRS Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://pmrs.pa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2025-Annual-Comprehensive-Financial-Report-FINAL.pdf) prints fiduciary net position of **$3,964,321,928** at **31 December 2025**. Investments **$3,967,924,591** are not that card. Money-weighted return was **14.12 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Highlights **14.1 percent** is not that card. January 1 2025 actuarial **101.7 percent** and fair-value **108.7 percent** are not a December GASB funded-ratio card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
