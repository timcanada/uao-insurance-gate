# Relocating to Seoul for a KIC or Korea owner desk

Seoul is the headquarters city of the [Korea Investment Corporation](/korea-investment-corporation-kic-explained/). Official [offices](https://kic.kr/en/about-kic/location) address: **15, 17–19F State Tower Namsan, 100 Toegye-ro, Jung-gu**. KIC was established in **2005** under the Korea Investment Corporation Act. It is **not** the [National Pension Service](/national-pension-service-korea-explained/). NPS lists its [headquarters in Jeonju](https://www.nps.or.kr/eng/ntnlpnssrvc/contus/getOHAI0007M0.do) — Kukmin-Yeonkum Building, 180 Giji-ro, Deokjin-gu. A move here is often a move onto the SWF file — or into a window whose public-pension book sits in Jeonju. Right to work sits with official Korean sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit in Jeonju

- [KIC](/korea-investment-corporation-kic-explained/) — the sovereign wealth fund of the Republic of Korea. It manages public funds entrusted by the government and the Bank of Korea. Official prints live on the keeper. Do not invent an AUM on this page. Do not mint `/korea-investment-corporation-kic/`, `/kic-explained/`, or `/seoul-kic/`.
- [NPS](/national-pension-service-korea-explained/) is a **public pension**, not a SWF, and not KIC. Official headquarters is Jeonju. Official prints are **won**. Do not dollarize. Do not add the two houses. Do not mint `/nps-korea/` or `/careers/cities/jeonju/`.
- A Seoul regional NPS office is not the fund-management headquarters. Ask where the investment committee sits.

[Tokyo](/careers/cities/tokyo/) and [Singapore](/careers/cities/singapore/) houses staff this time zone as a *window* more often than as HQ.

## Why people underestimate the job

A capital posting is not automatically the public-pension book. Korean is the language of legitimacy even when the dealing language is English. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail. Satellites fail when the IC is elsewhere — the same problem our [London note](/careers/intelligence/why-london-satellites-fail/) already names.
