# City of Lake Alfred General Employees’ Retirement System, explained

The City of Lake Alfred General Employees’ Retirement System is Lake Alfred’s civilian pension, not leftover Boca General Employees, and not leftover Lakeland Employees. The official [30 September 2025 and 2024 City of Lake Alfred General Employees’ Retirement System financial statements](https://retirement.flcities.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2025-audit.pdf) print net position restricted for pension benefits of **$7,514,883** at **30 September 2025**. Investments **$7,516,629** are not that card. Notes money-weighted return was **8.17 percent** net of investment expenses. GASB **90.46 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Boca civilian overwrite.** Do not mint `/lake-alfred/`, `/lagers/`, or `/gepp/`. `/lagers/` remains Missouri LAGERS. The house opinion presents only this retirement system, not the City of Lake Alfred.

## What is official (30 September 2025)

The statements of plan net position, the GASB note, and the money-weighted schedule are the sourced lines:

1. **Net position restricted for pension benefits** **$7,514,883** at **30 September 2025**. Beginning **$6,978,366**. Change **$536,517**. Investments at fair value **$7,516,629**. Cash and short-term money-market funds **$60,133** sit inside investments. Plan-member receivables **$5,063**. Accounts payable **$6,809**. DROP **$24,047** is inside net position, not a second AUM. Do not treat leftover Boca General Employees **$256,729,999**, leftover Boca Executive **$75,518,835**, leftover Lakeland Employees **$822,834,757**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$8,307,059**. Net pension liability **$792,176**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **90.46 percent** (2024 **87.33 percent**). Do not treat **$8,307,059** as AUM.
3. **Returns and cash flow.** Notes money-weighted return **8.17 percent** net of investment expenses for the year ended **30 September 2025**. City contributions **$308,971**. Plan-member contributions **$79,775**. Plan-member buy-back contributions **$122,044**. Net investment income **$572,931**. Benefit payments and refunds **$505,415**. Administrative expenses **$41,789**. The independent auditor’s report on internal control is dated **16 April 2026**, Brynjulfson CPA, P.A.
