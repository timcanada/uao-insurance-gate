# CAAT Pension Plan, explained

The Colleges of Applied Arts and Technology Pension Plan is a Canadian multi-employer defined-benefit pension, not leftover OTPP and not leftover OMERS. The official [2025 annual report](https://www.caatpension.ca/documents/annual-report/2025/caat-pension-plan-annual-report-2025-en.pdf) prints net assets available for benefits of **C$25,447 million** at **31 December 2025**. Investments were **C$26,513 million**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The one-year net return was **8.4 percent**. The ten-year annualized net return was **9.6 percent**. The house funded card was **124 percent**. Print Canadian dollars. Do not dollarize. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The statement of financial position is the sourced line (Canadian dollars in millions):

1. **Net assets available for benefits** **C$25,447 million** at **31 December 2025** (2024 **C$23,301 million**). The house **C$25.4 billion** card is that statement line rounded. Investments **C$26,513 million** is not net assets available for benefits. The 1 January 2026 funding table market value of net assets **C$25,420 million** is not the 31 December 2025 statement. Those three prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover OTPP, leftover OMERS **$145.2 billion**, leftover CPP, leftover OPTrust, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing. Do not dollarize.
2. **Surplus cards on the same statements.** Regulatory surplus **C$6,749 million**. Accounting surplus **C$7,379 million**. The house **C$6.7 billion surplus** card is the regulatory line rounded. Those surplus cards are not interchangeable with net assets available for benefits.
3. **Returns and funded cards.** One-year net return **8.4 percent**. Ten-year annualized net return **9.6 percent**. House funded card **124 percent**. Those official prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten CAAT into [Ontario Teachers’](/ontario-teachers-pension-plan-explained/), [OMERS](/omers-explained/), [CPP Investments](/cpp-investments-explained/), or [OPTrust](/optrust/). OTPP is the teacher plan. OMERS is the municipal plan. CPP is the Canada Pension Plan investment board. This is **one Colleges of Applied Arts and Technology file**.
