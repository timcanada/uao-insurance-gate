# City of Memphis Retirement System, explained

The City of Memphis Retirement System is Memphis’s city pension, not leftover Tennessee Consolidated Retirement System and not leftover City of Knoxville Pension System. The official [30 June 2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://memphistn.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Revised-FY25-COM-ACFR.pdf) prints GASB plan fiduciary net position of **$2,583,300 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Combining City Retirement System net position was **$2,676,751 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return was **10.71 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **83.8 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover TCRS overwrite.** Do not mint `/memphis-pension/` or `/memphis-retirement-explained/`. Statement prints are in thousands. Combining City Retirement System **$2,676,751 thousand** is not GASB plan fiduciary net position. Combining investments **$2,654,124 thousand** are not fiduciary net position. Library Retirement System **$54,594 thousand** is a different legal person. MLGW Retirement System **$1,591,676 thousand** is a different legal person and is not this book. Combined pension-and-OPEB trusts **$5,106,726 thousand** is not this book.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The notes City Retirement System schedule is the sourced line:

1. **GASB plan fiduciary net position** **$2,583,300 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$2,576,469 thousand**). Combining City Retirement System net position **$2,676,751 thousand** is not that GASB card. Combining investments **$2,654,124 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Securities-lending collateral is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover TCRS **$74,910,040,763**, leftover City of Knoxville Pension System **$758,195,199**, leftover Metropolitan Employees’ Benefit Trust **$4,360,675,155**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$3,081,465 thousand**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **83.8 percent**. Net pension liability **$498,165 thousand**. Memphis-Shelby County Airport Authority net pension liability **$20,271 thousand** is not a second City AUM.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **10.71 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025**, against the assumed **7.25 percent**. This filing does not print a separate official one-year time-weighted house card for City Retirement.

Do not flatten the City of Memphis Retirement System into [TCRS](/tennessee-tcrs/), [City of Knoxville Pension System](/knoxville-pension/), or [Metropolitan Employees’ Benefit Trust](/nashville-employees/). TCRS is the statewide file. Knoxville and Nashville are different Tennessee city files. Later Memphis teachers sit in leftover TCRS if applicable. This is **one Memphis city file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Memphis pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$2,583,300 thousand** line is GASB plan fiduciary net position. The **$2,676,751 thousand** line is combining City Retirement System net position. The **83.8 percent** card is the GASB ratio. The **10.71 percent** card is money-weighted. Those are not the same mandate.
