# CERN Pension Fund, explained

The CERN Pension Fund is the staff pension of CERN and associated organisations, not leftover USS and not a dollarized house AUM. The official [2024 Annual Report and Financial Statements](https://pensionfund.cern.ch/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Annual-Report-and-Financial-Statements-2024_compressed.pdf) print net assets available for benefits of **4,619,835 thousand Swiss francs** at **31 December 2024**. Total assets were **4,770,252 thousand Swiss francs**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted return was **5.02 percent** net of external management and custody fees. The accounting-measure funding ratio was **40.8 percent**. The updated-funding measure was **73.1 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (31 December 2024)

The statement of financial position is in **thousand Swiss francs**. Print house currency. **Do not dollarize.**

1. **Net assets available for benefits** **4,619,835 thousand Swiss francs** at **31 December 2024** (beginning **4,460,361 thousand Swiss francs**). The house card **4 620 MCHF** is the same book, rounded. Total assets **4,770,252 thousand Swiss francs**. Total liabilities **150,417 thousand Swiss francs**. Those four lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover USS **£76.8 billion**, leftover UNJSPF, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio STRS **$80 billion+** / **$90 billion** / **$350 billion** as this filing.
2. **Cash flows on the same report.** Net investment income **224,290 thousand Swiss francs**. Contributions received **262 million Swiss francs**. Benefits paid **318 million Swiss francs** (house card also prints **318 MCHF** to more than **3,270** beneficiaries). Net membership-activities cost was a negative **57 MCHF**.
3. **Returns and funding status.** Time-weighted performance **5.02 percent**, net of external management and custody fees, as reported by APEX. That print does not include governance and internal management costs of about **0.23 percentage points**. Geneva inflation **0.48 percent**. Accounting-measure funding ratio **40.8 percent** (net assets **4 620 MCHF** against actuarial liabilities **11 329 MCHF**). Updated Funding measure **73.1 percent** (liabilities **6 323 MCHF**). Periodic Actuarial Review at **1 January 2023** **77.1 percent**. Those three funding-ratio prints are not interchangeable. The 2025 Periodic Actuarial Review had not been printed as a finished ratio in this filing — do not invent one.

Do not flatten the CERN Pension Fund into [UNJSPF](/unjspf/) or leftover USS. UNJSPF is the United Nations staff file. This is **one CERN staff-pension file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “CERN pension AUM” has to name the book and the currency. The **4,619,835 thousand Swiss francs** line is net assets available for benefits. The **4,770,252 thousand Swiss francs** line is total assets. Those are not the same mandate. Do not convert either line into dollars.
