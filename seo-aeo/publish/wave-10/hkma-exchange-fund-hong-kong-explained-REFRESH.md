# The HKMA Exchange Fund (Hong Kong), explained

Hong Kong’s Exchange Fund is the official reserve portfolio managed by the Hong Kong Monetary Authority. It is a **currency-board and financial-stability book**, not a classic sovereign wealth fund. Total assets were **HK$4,463.6 billion at 30 June 2026**. First-half 2026 investment income was **HK$134.7 billion**. Accumulated surplus was **HK$862.7 billion**. This is not investment advice.

Do not convert those Hong Kong dollars into a fake-precise US dollar AUM. Do not call the Exchange Fund “Hong Kong’s SWF” in the lead. The job is the Linked Exchange Rate, then return.

## How the H1 income was made

HKMA’s 28 July 2026 components of the **HK$134.7 billion**:

| Component | HK$ billion |
| --- | --- |
| Gains on bonds | **49.1** |
| Losses on Hong Kong equities | **−11.8** |
| Gains on other equities | **53.7** |
| Positive currency-translation effect on non-HKD assets | **34.3** |
| Gains on other investments (subsidiaries; valuations to **end-March 2026**) | **9.4** |

Fees on placements by the Fiscal Reserves were **HK$10.9 billion** in H1 2026; fees on placements by HKSAR Government funds and statutory bodies were **HK$5.8 billion**. The 2026 fee-payment rate on those placements is **4.8%**. The 2026 Future Fund fee is **not** in that H1 print — HKMA says it waits on the 2026 composite rate.

Chief Executive of the HKMA, named on the release: **Eddie Yue**.

The Backing Portfolio / Investment Portfolio split is the right *architecture* (currency board first; return second; Long-Term Growth Portfolio inside the investment book). This H1 release does not reprint those sleeve NAVs. Do not invent them.

## How a peer should read it

- **Reserve fund, not PIF.** If your SWF league table needs an AUM, this is the wrong column. Put it with official reserves.
- **Income is not a “fund return %.”** HKMA printed **HK$134.7 billion** of H1 income, not a single official half-year percentage for the whole Fund. Do not invent one from 134.7 / 4,463.6.
- **Translation cuts both ways.** Yue’s own warning: the H1 FX translation gain may not repeat.
- **Future Fund is a placement, not a second HKMA.** HK$228.5 billion sat inside the Fund at 30 June 2026. That is not [Australia’s Future Fund](/future-fund-australia-explained/).
