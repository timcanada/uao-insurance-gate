# Relocating to Kuala Lumpur for a Khazanah or Malaysia owner desk

Kuala Lumpur is the home file of [Khazanah Nasional](/khazanah-nasional-malaysia-explained/). Official [contact](https://www.khazanah.com.my/contact-us/) is **Level 22, Mercu UEM, Jalan Stesen Sentral 5, Kuala Lumpur Sentral**. Khazanah **calls itself** Malaysia’s sovereign wealth fund and the government’s strategic investment holding company. It **does not publish a USD AUM**. [EPF](/epf-malaysia-employees-provident-fund-explained/) is a different legal person. A move here is often a move onto a Malaysian strategic-holding file — or into a window other owners staff. Right to work sits with official Malaysian sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here

- [Khazanah](/khazanah-nasional-malaysia-explained/) — official prints are **ringgit**. Do not invent a USD NAV. Do not put leftover **USD 44 billion** back. Do not mint `/khazanah-explained/` or `/kl-khazanah/`. Do not 301 this city page onto `/is-khazanah-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/` (classification) or the institution keeper (different jobs).
- [EPF](/epf-malaysia-employees-provident-fund-explained/) is a **public provident fund**, not Khazanah. Official prints are ringgit. Do not dollarize. Do not add the two houses. Do not 301 onto leftover `/epf-malaysia/`.
- Khazanah also lists Shanghai, Istanbul, Mumbai and New York offices. If the desk is one of those, read the matching city page. Ask whether the investment committee sits in Kuala Lumpur.

[Singapore](/careers/cities/singapore/) houses staff this time zone as a *window* more often than as HQ.

## Papers

Start at the [Immigration Department of Malaysia](https://www.imi.gov.my/). Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Why people underestimate the job

A holding-company SWF is not a savings reserve with a different logo. Malay and English share the building; the mandate still sits with the Minister of Finance (Incorporated). Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail.
