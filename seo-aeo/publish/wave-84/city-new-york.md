# Relocating to New York for an asset-owner desk

New York is both a headquarters city and everyone else's satellite: the City’s five public pension systems, State capital that is not this city, endowments and family offices, and the New York floors of Canadian and Gulf owners whose investment committee sits elsewhere. A move here is often a move into a time zone and a legal system, not into the institution’s political home. Right to work sits with official US sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which only keep a floor

Start with the legal person, not the postcode:

- The [New York City Retirement Systems](/new-york-city-crs/) are the City’s five public pension funds. The Comptroller is custodian, delegated investment advisor, and a trustee. That is a New York City file.
- The [New York State Common Retirement Fund](/new-york-state-common-retirement-fund-explained/) is a different house. Sole trustee, different fiscal year, different city of legitimacy. Do not mash the two books.
- [CPP Investments](/cpp-investments-explained/) and [Ontario Teachers'](/ontario-teachers-pension-plan-explained/) sit in Toronto and keep New York books because the book required it. [PSP](/psp-investments-canada-explained/) lists New York among its offices; headquarters is Ottawa, principal business office Montréal.

Ask whether the investment committee sits here. Often it does not. That is the same satellite problem our [London note](/careers/intelligence/why-london-satellites-fail/) already names. Do not invent a house AUM to decorate a skyline.

## Papers

Start at [USCIS — working in the United States](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states). Sponsorship, dependents, and the gap between a “global mobility promise” and a petition are why offers collapse. Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.
