# Fundo Soberano de Angola (FSDEA), explained

The Fundo Soberano de Angola is Angola’s sovereign wealth fund. IFSWF lists it under **that legal name**, not “Fundo Soberano de Angola para Ativos do Estado.” The 2016 Santiago self-assessment dates the fund to **Presidential Decree 48/11 of 9 March 2011** as the **Fundo Petrolífero**, renamed FSDEA by **Presidential Decree 89/13 of 19 June 2013**. The IFSWF member page **does not publish AUM**. The live UAO page’s **2012** founding and **$5–8 billion as of 2023** are not a current official filing. This is not investment advice.

The **US$5 billion** line is a **2013 funding rule** quoted in a **2016** self-assessment. It is not a current official AUM. Do not update the year. Do not stretch it into **$5–8 billion as of 2023**.

## What is not official — do not put it back

- **“Fundo Soberano de Angola para Ativos do Estado.”** Not the IFSWF legal name.
- **Established in 2012.** Official first decree is **9 March 2011**.
- **$5–8 billion as of 2023.** Tracker / leftover copy. Not on the IFSWF member page.
- Wikipedia / Global SWF / ANGOP **$3.99 billion** (or any other third-party dollar) until FSDEA’s own report PDF is fetched and read.
- A **Sonangol stake** or sector-weight table we have not seen on an official FSDEA filing.
- Minting `/angola-fsdea/` or `/what-is-the-angola-fsdea/`.

## How a peer should read it

FSDEA is Angola’s named sovereign wealth fund — 2011 oil-fund statute, 2013 rename, IFSWF member, Luanda address. **No public current AUM is the disclosure.** Anyone who needs a 2023 dollar band is reading a consultancy, not the Forum page. The 2013 **US$5 billion** endowment rule is history. It is not a licence to print **$5–8 billion** as if it were last year’s audit.
