# Relocating to Melbourne for Future Fund or AustralianSuper

Melbourne is the headquarters city of Australia’s sovereign wealth fund and of [AustralianSuper](/australiansuper-explained/). [Future Fund](/future-fund-australia-explained/) Management Agency lists **447 Collins Street, Melbourne**. AustralianSuper’s official member correspondence address is Melbourne. [Sydney](/careers/cities/sydney/) is the capital-markets floor, not automatically headquarters. A move here is often a move onto a Commonwealth file or a profit-for-member book. Right to work sits with official Australian sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit in Sydney

- [Future Fund](https://www.futurefund.gov.au/) — Australia’s sovereign wealth fund, as it describes itself. Not a superannuation fund. Official prints live on the keeper. Do not invent an AUM on this page. Do not treat the Future Fund Board’s combined FUM widget as the Future Fund sleeve.
- [AustralianSuper](/australiansuper-explained/) — profit-for-member super. Official help-and-support correspondence is Melbourne (GPO Box 1901). Do not mint `/australian-super/`. Do not invent a house AUM on this page.
- [Aware Super](/aware-super-explained/) opened as **First State Super** in **1992**. That file is [Sydney](/careers/cities/sydney/)-centred. Confirm the desk on the employer site.
- [UniSuper](/unisuper-explained/) is a different house. Confirm the city on unisuper.com.au. Do not mint `/unisuper/`.

Do not invent a house AUM to decorate a laneway. If the IC sits here, say so in the brief. If it sits in Sydney, you are staffing a window.

## Papers

Start at [Department of Home Affairs — immigration](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/). Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Why people underestimate the job

Super is a member file. Future Fund is a Commonwealth file. Those are different publicnesses and the same need for process. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail.

## Open roles

Use official portals via [Careers](/careers/). Confirm on futurefund.gov.au and australiansuper.com.
