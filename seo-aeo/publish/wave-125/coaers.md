# City of Austin Employees’ Retirement System, explained

The City of Austin Employees’ Retirement System is Austin’s civilian employee pension, not leftover Austin Police Retirement System. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.coaers.org/files/1e475242b/2025+ACFR_FINAL+6.3.26.pdf) prints combined fiduciary net position of **$4,085,942,671** at **31 December 2025**. Investments at fair value were **$4,044,287,715**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted return was **14.9 percent** net of fees. The money-weighted return was **15.02 percent** net of fees. Retirement Plan fiduciary net position was **65.78 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Austin Police Retirement System overwrite, a leftover Texas ERS overwrite, or a leftover TMRS overwrite.** Do not mint `/austin-employees/` or `/coaers-explained/`. Do not treat the house **$4.1 billion** card as a Retirement-Plan-only fiduciary line.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Combined fiduciary net position restricted for pensions** **$4,085,942,671** at **31 December 2025** (2024 **$3,570,745,498**). Retirement Plan **$4,085,892,671**. Restoration Plan **$50,000**. Investments at fair value **$4,044,287,715** is not fiduciary net position. Those prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover Austin Police statement **$1,211,262,982**, leftover Texas ERS, leftover TMRS, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Retirement Plan total pension liability **$6,211,037,365**. Net pension liability **$2,125,144,694**. Retirement Plan fiduciary net position / total pension liability **65.78 percent**. The GASB schedule uses Retirement Plan fiduciary net position **$4,085,892,671**, not the combined **$4,085,942,671** line. Those prints are not interchangeable.
3. **Returns and actuarial card.** Time-weighted return **14.9 percent** net of fees. Money-weighted return **15.02 percent** net of fees (2024 **9.86 percent**). Actuarial funded **63.1 percent** is not the GASB **65.78 percent** card.

Do not flatten COAERS into [Austin Police Retirement System](/austin-police-retirement/), [Austin Firefighters Retirement Fund](/austin-firefighters/), [Texas ERS](/texas-ers/), [Texas Municipal Retirement System](/texas-tmrs/), or [Houston Municipal Employees](/houston-hmeps/). Austin Police is a different legal person. Austin Firefighters is a different legal person. This is **one Austin civilian employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “COAERS AUM” has to name the book. The **$4,085,942,671** line is the combined fiduciary book. The **$4,085,892,671** line is the Retirement Plan used on the GASB card. The **$4,044,287,715** line is investments. The **63.1 percent** card is the actuarial funded print, not the GASB **65.78 percent** card. House **$4.1 billion** is the combined book rounded.
