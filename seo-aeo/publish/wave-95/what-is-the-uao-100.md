# What is the UAO 100?

The UAO 100 is Universal Asset Owners’ Top 100 ranking of long-horizon capital institutions — sovereign wealth funds, public pensions, reserves, superannuation funds and a small number of endowments — published on the [Registry](/registry/). It is a searchable ranking with confidence flags and an editorial Influence Index, not a recommended-manager list and not a substitute for an official filing.

**Do not treat this page as a second Registry overwrite.** Do not mint `/uao-100-explained/`, `/top-100-asset-owners/`, or `/uao-top-100/`. Do not reprint leftover dollar cells from the ranking table. Do not invent a subscriber total.

## What the live ranking already is

The live Registry heading is **The UAO Database · Top 100 + People**. On **26 August 2026** it printed **100 of 100** institutions on the ranking, plus a people directory beside it. Filters on that page include institution type (sovereign wealth fund, public pension, reserves, superannuation, endowment) and region.

Two scores sit on each row:

- **UAO Influence Index (0–100).** The live page says this is a **directional editorial score, not a rating**.
- **Transparency.** An editorial score on the same table. Not a Santiago-principles grade we invented for a sponsor.

Assets on the table carry a confidence flag. *Validated (official disclosure)* means the cell claims an official source. *Estimate (public ranking)* means it does not. Stub rows are a verified ranking entry with a fuller profile still in progress.

[Methodology](/methodology/) (version 1.0, **28 May 2026**) is the test for any ranking we publish: universe, data window, named sources, scoring rubric, exclusions, version, changelog. If a cell cannot meet that test, do not lift it.

## What the UAO 100 is not

- Official AUM for ADIA, GIC, KIA, QIA, or SAFE — those owners do not publish a figure we will treat as official
- A dollarization of NBIM (kroner) or GPIF (yen)
- A substitute for the [Readership](/readership/) audit
- A people biography. Leadership moves belong on the Registry with a source. We do not mint a CEO page from a row

How to use the product without flattening a filing is on [how to use the UAO Registry](/how-to-use-uao-registry/).

## How a newsroom should quote it

Name the product and the date: “Universal Asset Owners’ UAO 100 (Registry, 26 August 2026) …”. Lift a **count**, a **type**, or an **Influence Index** only if you also lift the disclaimer that it is editorial. Do not lift a USD cell as if it were the fund’s own accounts.
