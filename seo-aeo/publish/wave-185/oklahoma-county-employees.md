# Employees’ Retirement System of Oklahoma County, explained

The Employees’ Retirement System of Oklahoma County is Oklahoma County’s closed pre-1991 defined-benefit pension, not leftover Oklahoma OPERS (**$13,351,556,235**), leftover Tulsa County ERS (**$362,539,381**), leftover Oklahoma Police, leftover Oklahoma Firefighters, leftover Ohio OPERS, leftover MassPRIM. The official [financial statements as of 30 June 2025](https://www.oklahomacounty.org/Portals/0/Images/County%20Clerk/Employees%20Retirement%20System%20June%202025%20Audit.pdf) print net position restricted for pensions of **$6,472,964** at **30 June 2025**. Investments at cost **$6,375,225** are not that card. Notes money-weighted return was **6.31 percent**. Notes GASB **45.70 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statements of plan net position, the GASB note, and the money-weighted print are the sourced lines:

1. **Net position restricted for pensions** **$6,472,964** at **30 June 2025**. Beginning **$6,459,349**. Increase **$13,615**. Investments at cost **$6,375,225** (certificate of deposit **$250,000**, Treasury money-market funds **$5,170,000**, judgments **$955,225**). Cash and cash equivalents **$62,223** are not FNP. Interest receivable **$35,516** is not FNP. The statements print investments at cost, not fair value. The defined-contribution plan that later hires sit in is not this closed defined-benefit book. Do not treat leftover Oklahoma OPERS **$13,351,556,235**, leftover Tulsa County ERS **$362,539,381**, leftover Oklahoma Police **$3,449,729 thousand**, leftover Oklahoma Firefighters **$3,696,332,045**, leftover Ohio OPERS **$158,377,460,244**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion**, leftover USS **£76.8bn**, leftover PFZW **€35bn**, leftover CIC **$1.3T**, leftover Alaska **$89bn**, leftover GPIF **15.83%** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Notes total pension liability **$14,163,656**. Net pension liability **$7,690,692**. Plan net position as a percentage of the total pension liability **45.70 percent**. RSI **46.14 percent** is the 2024 companion, not the 2025 notes card. Do not treat **$14,163,656** or **$7,690,692** as AUM. Discount rate **2.75 percent**. The **2.75 percent** discount is not the money-weighted card.
3. **Returns and cash flow.** Notes annual money-weighted return **6.31 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025**. RSI annual money-weighted return **6.31 percent**. Those two official prints are the same card. Employer contributions **$811,480**. Interest **$397,830**. Benefits **$1,177,007**. Administrative expenses **$18,688**. Johnston & Ahlschwede, P.C. issued an unmodified opinion dated **22 September 2025**, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma.
