# Newport News Employees’ Retirement Fund, explained

The Newport News Employees’ Retirement Fund is Newport News’s closed city pension, not leftover Virginia Retirement System. The official [30 June 2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.nnva.gov/DocumentCenter/View/42510/FY25-NNERF-ACFR) prints net position restricted for pension benefits of **$1,150,643,142** at **30 June 2025**. Statement investments were **$1,133,721,587**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Time-weighted return was **10.9 percent** net of fees. Money-weighted return was **11.10 percent** net of investment fees. Fiduciary net position was **74.83 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Virginia Retirement System overwrite.** Do not mint `/nnerf/` or `/newport-news-employees-explained/`. Statement investments **$1,133,721,587** are not fiduciary net position. The OPEB Fund **$57,894,943** is not the pension book. Time-weighted net **10.9 percent** is not money-weighted **11.10 percent**. Time-weighted gross **11.1 percent** is not the money-weighted card even though the prints look close. Transmittal fair-value **11.0 percent** is not the investment-section card. Actuarial-value **9.85 percent** is not money-weighted. Closed to new City hires on **1 March 2010** and to new Schools hires on **1 July 2009**. Later hires sit in leftover VRS.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position restricted for pension benefits** **$1,150,643,142** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$1,087,519,504**). The change is **$63,123,638**. Statement investments **$1,133,721,587** is not fiduciary net position. Total pension-fund assets **$1,150,690,106** is not fiduciary net position. Do not add the OPEB Fund **$57,894,943** and call the sum a second AUM. Do not treat leftover VRS **$124,041,778 thousand**, leftover Fairfax PORS **$1,966,417,163**, leftover Richmond Retirement System **$883,849,545**, leftover Norfolk Employees **$1,412,980 thousand**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion**, leftover City of Knoxville Pension System **$758,195,199**, or leftover St. Paul Teachers **$1,433,936,404** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$1,537,620,795**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **74.83 percent**. MD&A **74.8 percent** is the rounded card, not a second ratio. Net pension liability **$386,977,653**. Those official prints are not interchangeable with the OPEB funded card.
3. **Returns.** Required-supplementary-information money-weighted return **11.10 percent** net of investment fees for the year ended **30 June 2025**. Investment-section time-weighted total-fund **10.9 percent** net / **11.1 percent** gross. Those official prints are not interchangeable. The RSI footnote **11.5 percent** is the prior-year net time-weighted card, not 2025.

Do not flatten Newport News Employees’ Retirement Fund into [Virginia Retirement System](/virginia-retirement-system/), [Fairfax ERS](/fairfax-ers/), [Fairfax PORS](/fairfax-pors/), [Fairfax URS](/fairfax-urs/), [Richmond Retirement System](/richmond-retirement/), [Norfolk Employees’ Retirement System](/norfolk-employees/), [City of Knoxville Pension System](/knoxville-pension/), or [City of Roanoke Pension Plan](/roanoke-pension/). VRS is the later Newport News hire book. Roanoke is an open Virginia city file. This is **one closed Newport News city file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Newport News pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$1,150,643,142** line is net position restricted for pension benefits. The **$1,133,721,587** line is statement investments. The **$57,894,943** line is the OPEB Fund. The **74.83 percent** card is the GASB ratio. The **10.9 percent** card is time-weighted net. The **11.10 percent** card is money-weighted. Those are not the same mandate.
