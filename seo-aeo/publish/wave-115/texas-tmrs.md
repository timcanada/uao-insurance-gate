# Texas Municipal Retirement System, explained

The Texas Municipal Retirement System is Texas’s municipal cash-balance pension, not leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion** and not leftover Texas ERS. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.tmrs.com/uploads/files/general/down/finance/TMRS-ACFR-2025-Final.pdf) prints net position restricted for pensions of **$49,405,467,577** at **31 December 2025**. Investments at fair value were **$49,126,197,726**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted net return was **13.75 percent**. The money-weighted return was **13.70 percent**. The system-wide funded ratio was **90.4 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position restricted for pensions** **$49,405,467,577** at **31 December 2025** (beginning **$43,406.5 million**; increase **$5,998,939,390**). Investments at fair value **$49,126,197,726** is not fiduciary net position. The highlights card **$49,405.5 million** is the same Pension Trust Fund book, rounded. Supplemental Death Benefits Fund **$23,566,196** is not the pension book. Combined fiduciary net position **$49,429,033,773**. Those four lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion**, leftover Texas ERS, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio STRS **$80 billion+** / **$90 billion** / **$350 billion** as this filing.
2. **Cash flows on the same report.** City contributions **$1,608.2 million**. Member contributions **$678.9 million**. Retirement benefits paid **$2,197.9 million**. Member account refunds **$90.7 million**.
3. **Returns and funded status.** Time-weighted net investment return **13.75 percent**. Three-year **12.01 percent**. Five-year **8.00 percent**. Ten-year **8.07 percent**. Money-weighted return on Pension Trust Fund assets, net of investment expenses, **13.70 percent**. Those two official one-year prints are not interchangeable. Actuarial investment-return assumption **6.75 percent**. System-wide funded ratio **90.4 percent**. Participating cities **943**.

Do not flatten TMRS into [Texas TRS](/texas-teacher-retirement-system-explained/) or [Texas ERS](/texas-ers/). Texas TRS is the teacher file. Texas ERS is the state-employee file. This is **one Texas municipal cash-balance file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Texas municipal AUM” has to name the book. The **$49,405,467,577** line is net position restricted for pensions. The **$49,126,197,726** line is investments. The **$23,566,196** line is the Supplemental Death Benefits Fund. Those are not the same mandate.
