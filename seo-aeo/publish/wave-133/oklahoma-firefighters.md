# Oklahoma Firefighters Pension and Retirement System, explained

The Oklahoma Firefighters Pension and Retirement System is Oklahoma’s statewide firefighter pension, not leftover Oklahoma OPERS and not leftover Oklahoma Police Pension and Retirement System. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://ofprs.ok.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2025-Combined-ACFR-OKFF.pdf) prints fiduciary net position restricted for pensions of **$3,696,332,045** at **30 June 2025**. Statement investments were **$4,046,657,378**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Time-weighted return was **12.13 percent** gross of explicit fees and **12.07 percent** net. Money-weighted return was **12.07 percent** net of investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **77.23 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

Net return on average assets **12.14 percent** is not the money-weighted card. Time-weighted net **12.07 percent** and money-weighted **12.07 percent** happen to match and are not interchangeable methods.
