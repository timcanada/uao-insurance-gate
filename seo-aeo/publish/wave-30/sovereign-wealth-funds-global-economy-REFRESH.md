# SWFs and the global economy: no official $10.7 trillion pile

Sovereign wealth funds move long-horizon capital across borders. That is real. A combined **$10 trillion / $10.7 trillion / $11 trillion** pile is not. The live page, dated 6 July 2026, still says SWFs **manage over $10 trillion**, then cites an IMF **$10.7 trillion as of 2024**, GPFG **$1.43 trillion**, ADIA **approximately $283 billion**, a Gulf **nearly $4 trillion**, PIF **$925 billion**, Future Fund **$237 billion**, and CPP **$597 billion**. The twin [`/sovereign-wealth-fund-role-in-global-economy/`](/sovereign-wealth-fund-role-in-global-economy/) invents **$10.7 trillion / $11 trillion**, GPFG **$1.3 trillion**, CPP **$616 billion**, GIC **$917 billion**, Temasek **$403 billion**, Mubadala **$284 billion**, SAFE **$1.35 trillion**, CIC **$1.9 trillion**, Angola **$69 billion**, and NSIA **$65 billion**. Those are tracker numbers. They are not the filings.

## What the page can say

- **What a SWF does in markets.** A [SWF](/sovereign-wealth-fund/) is a longer-horizon state investment mandate. Some vote, publish holdings, and set a stewardship line. That is a governance fact, not a licence to invent a global pile.
- **What is not a SWF.** [CPP Investments](/cpp-investments-explained/) is a pension. SAFE is a reserve manager. Do not add them so the pile can grow.
- **How to print scale.** Quote the house or say no AUM. Do not stack ADIA + PIF + “the Gulf” into **$4 trillion**.
