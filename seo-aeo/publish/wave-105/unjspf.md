# UNJSPF, explained

The United Nations Joint Staff Pension Fund is a multi-employer staff pension, not a sovereign wealth fund and not a second public-plan page. The official [2025 financial statements](https://www.unjspf.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/UNJSPF-2025-Financial-Statements-Aug-2026.pdf) print net assets available for benefits of **$107,533,052 thousand** (**$107.53 billion**) at **31 December 2025**. Investments were **$106,492,913 thousand**. Pension contributions were **$3,738,044 thousand**. Pension benefits were **$4,336,702 thousand**. Those lines are not interchangeable. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second CalPERS overwrite or a second GPIF overwrite.** Those keepers are `/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/` and `/gpif-japan-explained/`. Do not mint `/unjps-united-nations/` or `/united-nations-joint-staff-pension-fund/`. Do not reprint leftover CalPERS **$460bn**.

## What is official (year to 31 December 2025)

The statements are in thousands of United States dollars. The 2025 filing is the sourced split:

1. **Net assets available for benefits** **$107,533,052 thousand** at **31 December 2025** (2024: **$95,421,056 thousand**). **Investments** **$106,492,913 thousand** (2024: **$94,360,385 thousand**). Do not add the two lines.
2. **Pension contributions** **$3,738,044 thousand** (participants **$1,236,211 thousand**; member organizations **$2,457,958 thousand**; other **$43,875 thousand**). **Pension benefits** **$4,336,702 thousand**.
3. **Provision for the actuarial present value of promised retirement benefits** **$99,755,472 thousand**. Excess of net assets over that provision **$7,777,580 thousand**. Those are **not** a second AUM.

Do not flatten UNJSPF into [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/) or [GPIF](/gpif-japan-explained/). Those are domestic public plans with different statutes.
