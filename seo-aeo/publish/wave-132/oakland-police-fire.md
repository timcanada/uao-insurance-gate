# Oakland Police and Fire Retirement System, explained

Oakland Police and Fire Retirement System is Oakland’s closed sworn police-and-fire pension, not leftover CalPERS and not leftover City of Fresno Fire and Police Retirement System. Membership is limited to uniformed employees hired before **1 July 1976**. Later Oakland safety hires sit in **CalPERS**. The official [FY2025 annual report](https://www.oaklandca.gov/files/assets/city/v/2/boards-amp-commissions/documents/pfrs/fye-2025.06.30_pfrs-annual-report__final-e-format.pdf) prints net position restricted for pensions of **$476,653,059** at **30 June 2025**. Statement investments at fair value were **$522,965,087**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return was **10.12 percent**. Fiduciary net position was **101.2 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover CalPERS overwrite or a leftover Fresno Fire and Police overwrite.** Do not mint `/oakland-pfrs/` or `/pfrs/`. The house **$476.6 million** and **$476.7 million** cards are not the statement **$476,653,059** line. Statement investments include securities-lending collateral **$45,688,733**. That collateral is not fiduciary net position. There were **no active participants** at **30 June 2025**. July 1, 2024 actuarial market-value funded **90.6 percent** is not the GASB card.
