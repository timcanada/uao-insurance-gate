# Washington Department of Retirement Systems, explained

The Washington Department of Retirement Systems administers Washington’s retirement plans. It is not leftover WSIB and not a second CalPERS page. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.drs.wa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/2025-ACFR-Document-102325.pdf) prints combined fiduciary net position of **$197,122,522 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Pension-plan fiduciary net position was **$189,168,712 thousand**. Deferred Compensation was **$7,953,810 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. Equity in the Commingled Trust Fund was **$179,861,937 thousand**. The Commingled Trust Fund time-weighted return was **9.0 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover WSIB overwrite.** Do not mint `/drs/`, `/washington-drs-explained/`, or `/washington-retirement-systems/`. Do not treat leftover WSIB March 2026 assets under management **$233,882,154,639** or leftover WSIB Commingled Trust Fund **$187,429,848,897** as this June 2025 Department of Retirement Systems book.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line (in thousands unless the MD&A names millions):

1. **Combined fiduciary net position** **$197,122,522 thousand** / MD&A **$197,122.5 million** / **$197.1 billion** at **30 June 2025** (beginning combined **$182,246,880 thousand**). The increase is **$14,875,642 thousand** / **8.2 percent**. Pension-plan fiduciary net position **$189,168,712 thousand** is not the combined book. Deferred Compensation **$7,953,810 thousand** is not the combined book. Those three lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover WSIB **$233.9 billion**, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **Investments versus the book.** Equity in the Commingled Trust Fund **$179,861,937 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Total investments **$196,972,674 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$217,914,941 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Those three lines are not interchangeable with combined fiduciary net position.
3. **Named plan cards and returns.** Commingled Trust Fund time-weighted return **9.0 percent** (2024 **8.5 percent**). PERS Plan 2/3 statement fiduciary net position **$72,850,904 thousand**. PERS Plan 2/3 equity in the Commingled Trust Fund **$72,858,175 thousand**. GASB PERS Plan 2/3: total pension liability **$69,023,154 thousand**; fiduciary net position **$72,839,330 thousand**; funded **105.53 percent**. Do not mix that GASB fiduciary print with the statement fiduciary print. PERS Plan 1 fiduciary net position **$9,614,805 thousand**. Those official prints are not interchangeable.

The Washington State Investment Board invests the Commingled Trust Fund. The Department of Retirement Systems administers the plans. Those two legal persons are not the same file.

Do not flatten the Department of Retirement Systems into [Washington State Investment Board](/washington-state-investment-board/), [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/), or [OPERS](/ohio-opers/). WSIB is the statutory investor. This is **one Washington plan-administrator file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

The **$189,168,712 thousand** line is pension-plan fiduciary net position. The **$179,861,937 thousand** line is equity in the Commingled Trust Fund. Leftover WSIB March 2026 assets under management is a different date and a different legal person.
