# Louisiana State Employees' Retirement System, explained

The Louisiana State Employees' Retirement System is Louisiana’s state-employee pension, not leftover Louisiana TRSL **$29.33 billion** and not leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion**. The official [2025 audited financial statements](https://app.lla.state.la.us/PublicReports.nsf/0/CE77B74D5E0648BA86258D16005400DE/$FILE/00008CED.pdf) print net position restricted for pensions of **$17,223,211,406** at **30 June 2025**. Net investment income was **$1,796,819,283**. The gross-of-fees time-weighted return was **12.4 percent**. The money-weighted return was **11.9 percent** net. Those two return lines are not interchangeable. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second Louisiana TRSL overwrite or a leftover Texas TRS $295 billion overwrite.** The Louisiana TRSL keeper is `/louisiana-trsl/`. Do not mint `/lasers/`, `/louisiana-lasers-explained/`, or `/lasers-louisiana/`. Do not mint `/trsl/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position restricted for pensions** **$17,223,211,406** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$15,966,194,298**). Do not treat leftover Louisiana TRSL **$29,334,227,630**, leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **Investment income on the same statements.** Net investment income **$1,796,819,283**. Investments **$16,986,733,220**. Those two lines are not interchangeable with fiduciary net position. Do not add securities-lending collateral to investments and call the sum a second AUM.
3. **Returns.** Gross-of-fees time-weighted return **12.4 percent**. Three-year, five-year, and ten-year annualized lines **12.7 percent**, **12.5 percent**, and **8.4 percent**. Money-weighted return **11.9 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Those time-weighted and money-weighted prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten LASERS into [Louisiana TRSL](/louisiana-trsl/) or [Kentucky TRS](/kentucky-trs/). TRSL is the teacher file. This is **one Louisiana state-employee file**.
