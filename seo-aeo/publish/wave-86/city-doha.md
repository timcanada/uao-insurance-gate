# Relocating to Doha for a QIA or Qatar owner desk

Doha is Qatar’s sovereign-capital city. The [Qatar Investment Authority](/qatar-investment-authority-qia/) is the State of Qatar’s sovereign wealth fund — established by **Amiri Decision No. 22 of 2005** and reorganized by **Amiri Decision No. 34 of 16 May 2023**. QIA’s own site and the [IFSWF profile](https://ifswf.org/member-profiles/qatar-investment-authority) **do not publish assets under management**. A move here is often a move onto a national reserve file, not a weekend in [Abu Dhabi](/careers/cities/abu-dhabi/) or [Riyadh](/careers/cities/riyadh/) with a different logo. Confirm immigration with official Qatari sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here

- [QIA](/qatar-investment-authority-qia/) — accountable to the Supreme Council for Economic Affairs and Investment, chaired by the Amir. IFSWF founding member. Official prints do **not** include an AUM. Do not recycle leftover **$510–600 billion**. Do not mint `/qia-explained/` or `/doha-qia/`.
- Related Qatar vehicles appear and re-brand. Use the official site on the day you apply.
- Abu Dhabi’s [ADIA](https://www.adia.ae/), [Mubadala](/mubadala-investment-company/) and [ADQ](/adq-abu-dhabi/) sit in a different country. [PIF](/public-investment-fund-pif/) sits in Riyadh. If the desk is theirs, read those city pages.

Our [dual-career Gulf](/careers/intelligence/dual-career-moves-gulf/) note is the prior reading for the household. The [moving-into playbook](/careers/intelligence/moving-into-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/) already says people “come for a few years but don't always stay.”

## Papers

Qatar’s official portal is [Hukoomi](https://hukoomi.gov.qa/en). Visa classes, dependent rules and tax guidance belong there — or with licensed counsel. Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Why people underestimate the job

You are closer to a government owner. Confidentiality is not a preference. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail.
