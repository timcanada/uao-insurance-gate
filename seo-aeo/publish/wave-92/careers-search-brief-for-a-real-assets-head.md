# Writing a real-assets head search brief

A real-assets head search brief is a mandate split, not a job-ad rewrite. You must say whether the seat owns real estate, infrastructure, natural resources, or a combined book — and what the person may commit without a committee vote. This Careers Intelligence briefing sits beside Universal Asset Owners' [real-assets versus private equity](/real-assets-vs-private-equity-allocation/) and [executive-search directory](/directory/executive-search/) pages. It is not a vendor shortlist and not compensation advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second real-assets explainer.** Do not mint `/real-assets-head-search/` or `/search-brief-real-assets/`. Do not invent a house AUM to decorate a search.

## The brief is the book

[Infrastructure](/infrastructure-as-an-asset-class/) is not a synonym for every building, wire, or acre. A real-estate head who has never sat an infrastructure committee is a different hire from a combined real-assets seat. If the pack cannot say which book, the long list will be a mash.

A [pension fund](/what-is-a-pension-fund/) search is often a public file. A sovereign or strategic fund search is often quieter and still has a file. A family-office search is a confidentiality problem first. Do not send the wrong room the same paragraph.

[Executive search](/directory/executive-search/) firms already sit on the owner side of that table. Listing a firm here is not a recommendation. Three sourced facts or the firm does not get a child page.

## What the brief must settle

1. **The book.** Real estate, infrastructure, natural resources, or a combined sleeve — and whether listed and unlisted sit together.
2. **Decision rights.** What can the head commit without the investment committee, and what requires a minute?
3. **Internal versus external.** Are you hiring someone to run staff, a programme of managers, or both?
4. **Publicness.** Will the appointment appear in a board pack a newspaper can request?
5. **Exit.** How does the last head leave, and who still owns the assets under construction?

## For boards and search firms

Owners already use [Careers](/careers/) when they are hiring a real-assets head, a deputy, or an independent member. If the seat is a combined book, say so in the first sentence. Labelled recruitment products: [Careers](/careers/).
