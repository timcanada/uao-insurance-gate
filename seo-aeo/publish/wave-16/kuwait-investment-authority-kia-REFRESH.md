# Kuwait Investment Authority (KIA), explained

The Kuwait Investment Authority is the State of Kuwait’s sovereign investment manager. It traces its roots to the Kuwait Investment Board of **1953** and was created by **Law No. 47 of 1982**. It manages the **General Reserve Fund** and the **Future Generations Fund**. It **does not publish assets under management**. The live UAO page’s **US$1.07 trillion** line is a tracker estimate, not a KIA filing. This is not investment advice.

**Last updated:** 26 August 2026 · **Desk:** UAO Research · **Canonical URL:** `/kuwait-investment-authority-kia/`

The live page this overwrites (13 June 2026) leads with “more than a trillion dollars” and “estimated **US$1.07 trillion**,” then ranks KIA fourth or fifth on a tracker. KIA’s own site has **no AUM widget**. The IFSWF member profile has **no AUM**. Treat that absence the way we treat [ADIA](/adia-abu-dhabi-investment-authority/), [QIA](/qatar-investment-authority-qia/), and [GIC](/gic-singapore-explained/). ## What is official

| Item | Official print | As-of | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AUM / NAV | **Not published** | — | Absence on [kia.gov.kw](https://www.kia.gov.kw/) and the [IFSWF profile](https://ifswf.org/member-profiles/kuwait-investment-authority) |
| Lineage | Kuwait Investment Board, **1953** (eight years before independence) | — | KIA home; IFSWF |
| Statute | **Law No. 47 of 1982** — autonomous governmental body | 1982 | KIA home |
| Role | **Asset manager.** It does **not** own the assets; the **State of Kuwait** does | — | IFSWF |
| Books | **General Reserve Fund (GRF)** and **Future Generations Fund (FGF)**; plus any other fund the Minister of Finance entrusts | — | IFSWF |
| GRF | Repository of the State’s oil revenues and GRF investment income | — | IFSWF |
| FGF | Established **1976** with **50%** of the then GRF balance; **10%** of all state revenues, including GRF revenues, transferred each year | — | IFSWF |
| Mission (their words) | Long-term return on reserves entrusted by the State, “an alternative to oil reserves” | — | [KIA home](https://www.kia.gov.kw/) |
| Oversight named by IFSWF | Semi-annual AUM statements to the **State Audit Bureau**; annual statements to the **Council of Ministers** and the **National Assembly**; two external audit firms | — | IFSWF |
| Board | Independent public authority; **majority private-sector** directors; Managing Director appointed from the private-sector representatives | — | IFSWF |

KIA was in the room in **April 2009** in Kuwait City when the Kuwait Declaration led to IFSWF. That is history, not an AUM.

## What is not official — do not put it back

- **US$1.07 trillion.** Tracker. Not on kia.gov.kw.
- **“More than a trillion dollars.”** Same sin with the decimal dropped.
- A **FGF-alone** dollar estimate.
- A global **rank** (fourth, fifth, “one of the five largest”). Rank requires a published size.
- Homemade sleeve weights (**50% / 10%** as a *portfolio* mix). The **10%** IFSWF cites is an **annual revenue transfer into FGF**, not an asset-allocation weight.
- Adding KIA to [ADIA](/adia-abu-dhabi-investment-authority/) or [PIF](/public-investment-fund-pif/) on a homemade dollar table.

## How a peer should read it

KIA is the **oldest** sovereign wealth manager still standing — 1953 in London, 1982 as a Kuwaiti authority, two books (working treasury + locked savings). The FGF transfer rule is the intergenerational machine. **No public AUM is the disclosure.** Anyone who needs a dollar size is reading a consultancy, not KIA. Internal statements go to the Audit Bureau, the Cabinet, and Parliament. That is not a licence for a journalist to invent **$1.07 trillion**.
