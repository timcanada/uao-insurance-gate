# Oklahoma Police Pension and Retirement System, explained

The Oklahoma Police Pension and Retirement System is Oklahoma’s statewide police pension, not leftover Oklahoma OPERS and not leftover Oklahoma Firefighters Pension and Retirement System. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.opprs.ok.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2025-OPPRS-ACFR-Final-With-Opinion.pdf) prints fiduciary net position restricted for pensions of **$3,449,729 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Statement investments were **$3,357,365 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The statement prints in thousands. Time-weighted return was **9.83 percent** gross. Money-weighted return was **9.53 percent** net of investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **95.12 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Oklahoma OPERS overwrite or a leftover Oklahoma Firefighters overwrite.** Do not mint `/opprs/` or `/oklahoma-police-explained/`. The statement **$3,449,729 thousand** line is the same figure as the later **$3,449,729,000** expansion, not a second AUM. Time-weighted **9.83 percent** gross is not money-weighted **9.53 percent** net. Total-equity composite **9.53 percent** is not the total-fund card.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Fiduciary net position restricted for pensions** **$3,449,729 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$3,183,061 thousand**). The change is **$266,668 thousand**. Statement investments **$3,357,365 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Statement prints are in thousands. 2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$3,626,858 thousand**. Net pension liability **$177,129 thousand**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **95.12 percent**.
3. **Returns.** Required-supplementary money-weighted return **9.53 percent** net of investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025**. Investment-section time-weighted **9.83 percent** gross underperformed the policy benchmark **11.73 percent**. Those official prints are not interchangeable. Total-equity composite **9.53 percent** is not the total-fund return.
