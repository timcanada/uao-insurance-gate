# City of Hialeah Employees’ Retirement System, explained

The City of Hialeah Employees’ Retirement System is Hialeah’s city pension covering general employees, firefighters, and police officers, not leftover Miami Fire and Police, leftover Miami GESE, and not leftover Texas ERS. The official [30 September 2025 City of Hialeah Employees’ Retirement System financial statements](https://www.hialeahfl.gov/DocumentCenter/View/21742/Hialeah-ERS---Financial-Statements---FY-2025) print fiduciary net position of **$814,680,820** at **30 September 2025**. Investments **$816,022,606** are not the net-position card. DROP benefits **$9,574,588** are a restricted portion of that net position. Money-weighted return was **8.15 percent** net of Plan investment expense. MD&A **3.5 percent** higher than 2024 is growth, not that card. GASB **72.33 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 September 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position, the GASB 67 schedule, and the money-weighted note are the sourced lines:

1. **Fiduciary net position** **$814,680,820** at **30 September 2025** (beginning **$786,898,440**). Total investments at fair value **$816,022,606**. Cash and cash equivalents **$26,526,784** are not that card. Net position restricted for defined benefits **$805,106,232** plus DROP **$9,574,588** is the same statement, not a second AUM. Advanced contribution from employer **$36,000,000** is a liability. Loans to employees **$5,683,908** are not the investment line. Do not treat leftover Miami Fire and Police **$1,943,149,383**, leftover Miami GESE, leftover Gainesville Employees **$764,938,276**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$1,126,308,147**. Net pension liability **$311,627,327**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of the total pension liability **72.33 percent**.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **8.15 percent** net of Plan investment expense for the fiscal year ended **30 September 2025** (2024 **20.75 percent**). MD&A **3.5 percent** higher net position is not the money-weighted card.
