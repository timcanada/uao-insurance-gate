# What is an Afore?

An Afore is a Mexican retirement-fund administrator, not a public DB board and not the Fondo Mexicano del Petróleo. Under the Ley de los Sistemas de Ahorro para el Retiro, an Administradora de Fondos para el Retiro is a variable-capital company that must use the word AFORE in its name, opens workers’ individual accounts, and channels those balances into SIEFOREs — the investment companies it operates. CONSAR supervises the system. This page is not a ranking, not an Afore XXI Banorte institution page, and not advice.

## Administrator vs investment company

The official [Ley de los Sistemas de Ahorro para el Retiro](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LSAR.pdf) (published **23 May 1996**; CONSAR is the supervisor under Article 2) is the sourced definition:

1. **Article 18** — an administradora is a financial entity that professionally administers workers’ **cuentas individuales**, channels the sub-account balances, and administers **sociedades de inversión**. It must seek adequate return and security and attend **exclusively to the interest of the workers**.
2. **Article 20** — to operate it must be a **sociedad anónima de capital variable** and use in its name the expression “Administradora de Fondos para el Retiro” or the abbreviation **AFORE**.
3. **Articles 39 and 41** — the **SIEFORE** (Sociedad de Inversión Especializada de Fondos para el Retiro) is the investment company the Afore operates. Its object is to invest the resources from the individual accounts. It too must be a variable-capital company and use **SIEFORE** in its name.

CONSAR’s [glossary](https://www.consar.gob.mx/gobmx/aplicativo/factsheets/Docs/Factsheets_Glosario.pdf) draws the same line: the Afore administers the account; the Siefore is the vehicle that invests it. [gob.mx/consar](https://www.gob.mx/consar) is the supervisor’s public desk.

The Mexico City careers page is the **Fondo Mexicano del Petróleo** fiduciary window at Banxico. That is a petroleum-revenue fideicomiso, not an Afore. Do not mash them.

## What an owner is actually buying

A consultant selling into Mexican retirement is selling to a **licensed Afore** that must put the worker’s money in a Siefore — not to a CalPERS-style governmental DB.
