# Relocating to Vancouver for a BCI or Pacific owner desk

Vancouver is a BCI office city, not BCI’s head office. [British Columbia Investment Management Corporation](https://www.bci.ca/)’s official [head office is Victoria](https://www.bci.ca/contact/) — 750 Pandora Avenue. Vancouver is 666 Burrard Street. A move here is often a move onto a British Columbia public-client mandate — or into a satellite of that mandate. Do not mint `/bcimc/`. Immigration is federal. Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit on the island

- [BCI](/bci-british-columbia-investment-management/) — a Crown corporation that invests for British Columbia public-sector pension and institutional clients. Official offices on the contact page: Victoria (head office), Vancouver, New York, London. Established **1999**. Ask whether the investment committee sits in Victoria. If it does, you are not at the centre.
- Official prints live on the institution keeper and bci.ca. Do not invent a house AUM on this page. Do not collapse BCI’s gross and net prints into one number.

[CPP Investments](/cpp-investments-explained/) and [Ontario Teachers'](/ontario-teachers-pension-plan-explained/) sit in [Toronto](/careers/cities/toronto/). [PSP](/psp-investments-canada-explained/)’s principal business office is [Montréal](/careers/cities/montreal/). [AIMCo](/aimco-alberta-investment-management-corporation/) is Alberta-centred — Edmonton head office, [Calgary](/careers/cities/calgary/) satellite. Do not flatten Canada into Vancouver.

## Why people underestimate the job

A Pacific lifestyle is not the mandate. BCI’s clients are British Columbia public institutions. The [fiduciary](/fiduciary-duty-explained-institutional/) file is public. Housing and a household that will not move west are the ordinary reasons offers fail — not the quality of the investment debate.
