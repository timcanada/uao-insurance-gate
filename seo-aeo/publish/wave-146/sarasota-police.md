# City of Sarasota Police Officers’ Pension Fund, explained

The City of Sarasota Police Officers’ Pension Fund is Sarasota’s sworn police pension, not leftover Sarasota Employees, leftover Gainesville Employees, and not leftover Florida SBA. The official [30 September 2025 and 2024 City of Sarasota Police Officers’ Pension Fund financial statements](https://sarasota.granicus.com/MetaViewer.php?event_id=8135&meta_id=887573&view_id=84) print fiduciary net position of **$362,453,217** at **30 September 2025**. Investments **$360,135,401** are not that card. Money-weighted return was **11.3 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. GASB **101.24 percent** is not the MD&A fair-value funded **97.9 percent** card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

The statements of fiduciary net position, the GASB 67 note, and the money-weighted schedule are the sourced lines:

Total liabilities **$323,805**. DROP **$2,687,451** is a component of net position, not a second book. The share-plan balance **$530,077** is not a second AUM. Do not treat leftover Sarasota Employees **$192,591,188**, leftover Gainesville Employees **$764,938,276**, leftover Tampa Fire and Police **$3,407,822,026**, leftover Florida SBA **$300 billion+**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$358,007,373**. Net pension asset **$4,445,844**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **101.24 percent**. MD&A fair-value funded **97.9 percent** is not the GASB card.
3. **Returns.** RSI money-weighted return **11.3 percent** net of investment expense for the year ended **30 September 2025** (2024 **20.6 percent**).
