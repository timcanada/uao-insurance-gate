# Metropolitan Water Reclamation District Retirement Fund, explained

The Metropolitan Water Reclamation District Retirement Fund is the District’s employee pension, not leftover Cook County Pension, leftover IMRF, and not leftover Chicago LABF. The official [31 December 2025 Metropolitan Water Reclamation District Retirement Fund Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://mwrdrf.org/media/3360/2025-acfr-finalcompressed.pdf) prints fiduciary net position of **$1,788,522,242** at **31 December 2025**. Investments at fair value **$1,675,140,867** are not that card. Money-weighted return was **13.76 percent** net of Fund investment expense. Time-weighted **13.46 percent** is not that card. GASB **58.68 percent** is not actuarial **56.5 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Cook County Pension overwrite.** Do not mint `/mwrd/`, `/mwrd-retirement-explained/`, or `/mwrdrf/`. Do not mint `/ers/`.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The statements of fiduciary net position, the GASB 67 note, and the money-weighted schedule are the sourced lines:

1. **Fiduciary net position** **$1,788,522,242** at **31 December 2025** (beginning **$1,644,010,438**). Investments at fair value **$1,675,140,867**. Total investments including securities-lending collateral **$1,684,385,343**. MD&A **$1.789 billion** is the rounded card. The **30 April 2026** invested-assets line **$1.76 billion** is not the December statement. Do not treat leftover Cook County Pension **$15,221,712,193**, leftover Chicago LABF **$1,389,298,384**, leftover IMRF, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$3,047,858,423**. District’s net pension liability **$1,259,336,181**. Fund fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **58.68 percent**. Actuarial funded **56.5 percent** is not the GASB card.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **13.76 percent** net of Fund investment expense for the year ended **31 December 2025** (2024 **10.19 percent**). MD&A time-weighted **13.46 percent** versus policy **14.1 percent** is not the money-weighted card.

Do not flatten the Metropolitan Water Reclamation District Retirement Fund into [Cook County Pension](/cook-county-pension/), [IMRF](/illinois-imrf/), [Chicago LABF](/chicago-labf/), or [Cook County Forest Preserve](/cook-forest-preserve/). Cook County is the Cook County employee file. IMRF is the Illinois local-government file. LABF is the Chicago laborer file. The Forest Preserve is a Cook County forest-preserve file. This is **one Metropolitan Water Reclamation District file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “MWRD pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$1,788,522,242** line is fiduciary net position. The **$1,675,140,867** line is investments at fair value. The **$1,684,385,343** line includes securities-lending collateral. The **13.76 percent** card is money-weighted. The **13.46 percent** card is time-weighted. The **58.68 percent** card is GASB. The **56.5 percent** card is actuarial. Those are not the same mandate.
