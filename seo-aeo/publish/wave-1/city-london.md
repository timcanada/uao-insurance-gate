# Relocating to London for an asset-owner desk

London is the overseas floor of other people's headquarters: Canadian pensions, Gulf and Asian sovereigns, UK schemes, insurers, and the advisers who live off them. A move here is often a move into a time zone and a legal system, not into the institution's political home. Right to work sits with UK official sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## What “London” means on a business card

Ask whether the IC sits here. Often it does not. London can be origination, public markets, or a client city for a fund whose legitimacy is in Toronto, Abu Dhabi, Oslo or Singapore. That is a feature if you want deal flow. It is a bug if you thought you were joining the centre.

UK schemes and insurers *are* centred here. Do not write them out of a city that also hosts everyone else's satellite.

## Papers

Start at [GOV.UK visas and immigration](https://www.gov.uk/browse/visas-immigration). Sponsorship, dependents, and the gap between a “global mobility promise” and a licence are why offers collapse in week six.

## The service-provider city

London is also why [Directory](/directory/) categories exist: counsel, banks, search, and administrators. Allocators already know the postcodes. Vendors who want labelled presence should not hide inside this article.

## Open roles

Use official portals via [Careers](/careers/). OTPP, CPP and others list London seats when they have them — confirm on the employer site.
