# Relocating to Dubai for an ICD or family-office desk

Dubai is not Abu Dhabi. The [Investment Corporation of Dubai](/investment-corporation-of-dubai-icd-explained/) is the principal investment arm of the Government of Dubai — established **3 May 2006** under Law No. 11 of 2006, wholly owned by that government. Abu Dhabi’s [ADIA](https://www.adia.ae/), [Mubadala](/mubadala-investment-company/) and [ADQ](/adq-abu-dhabi/) sit in a different emirate. A move here is often a move onto a Dubai government file, a DIFC service-provider floor, or a family office — not a weekend in Abu Dhabi with a different logo. Confirm immigration with official UAE sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here

- [ICD](/investment-corporation-of-dubai-icd-explained/) — read the official financial-information page and our keeper. Official prints are **dirhams**. Do not recycle a homemade dollar AUM. Do not treat a homepage widget as the audit.
- Family offices and multi-family offices cluster here. The job is still [a family office](/what-is-a-family-office/), not a product shelf. Do not invent a family-office AUM. Do not mint `/family-office/`.
- Abu Dhabi owners staff Dubai as a *window* more often than as HQ. If the desk is ADIA, Mubadala or ADQ, read the [Abu Dhabi city page](/careers/cities/abu-dhabi/).

Our [dual-career Gulf](/careers/intelligence/dual-career-moves-gulf/) note is the prior reading for the household. The [moving-into playbook](/careers/intelligence/moving-into-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/) already says people “come for a few years but don't always stay.”

## Papers

The UAE’s official portal is [u.ae](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services). Visa classes, dependent rules and tax guidance belong there — or with licensed counsel. Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## The service-provider city

DIFC is why [Directory](/directory/) categories exist in this city: counsel, banks, search, and administrators. Vendors who want labelled presence should not hide inside this article. See [Advertise](/advertise/).
