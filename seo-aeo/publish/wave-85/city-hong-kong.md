# Relocating to Hong Kong for the Exchange Fund or an owner window

Hong Kong is the city of the Exchange Fund — the official reserve portfolio managed by the [Hong Kong Monetary Authority](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/) — and a window other owners staff. The Exchange Fund is a currency-board and financial-stability book, not a classic sovereign wealth fund. A move here is often a move into that public file, or into a time zone for a Canadian or Singapore house whose investment committee sits elsewhere. Right to work sits with official Hong Kong sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here

- [HKMA Exchange Fund](/hkma-exchange-fund-hong-kong-explained/) — read HKMA first. Official prints are **Hong Kong dollars**. Do not convert them into a homemade US-dollar AUM. Do not call the Exchange Fund “Hong Kong’s SWF” in the lead.
- [CPP Investments](/cpp-investments-explained/), [Ontario Teachers'](/ontario-teachers-pension-plan-explained/) and [PSP](/psp-investments-canada-explained/) keep Hong Kong books because the book required it. Headquarters is not here. Ask whether the investment committee sits in Toronto, Ottawa or Montréal.
- [GIC](https://www.gic.com.sg/) and [Temasek](https://www.temasek.com.sg/) are Singapore houses. If the desk is theirs, read the [Singapore city page](/careers/cities/singapore/). Do not invent a GIC AUM.

Do not invent a SAFE or CIC AUM to decorate a Hong Kong skyline. Those prints live on the institution keepers — or they do not exist.

## Papers

Start at the [Immigration Department](https://www.immd.gov.hk/eng/). Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Why people underestimate the job

The city is easy to land in and easy to fail in if you treat the institution like a regional coverage job. The Exchange Fund’s first job is the Linked Exchange Rate, then return. Satellites fail when the IC is elsewhere — the same problem our [London note](/careers/intelligence/why-london-satellites-fail/) already names.
