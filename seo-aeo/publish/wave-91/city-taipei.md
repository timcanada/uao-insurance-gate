# Relocating to Taipei for a Bureau of Labor Funds or Taiwan public-pension desk

Taipei is the official-address city of the [Bureau of Labor Funds](https://www.blf.gov.tw/8821/8844/8857/19663), Ministry of Labor. Official English contact: **10F., No. 6, Sec. 1, Roosevelt Rd., Zhongzheng Dist., Taipei City 100232**. BLF was established on **17 February 2014**. It invests labor funds under the Ministry of Labor. The Bureau of Labor Insurance at **No. 4, Sec. 1, Roosevelt Rd.** is a **different** legal person. BLF is a public-pension investor, **not** a sovereign wealth fund. Right to work sits with official Taiwan sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit elsewhere

- BLF coordinates investment of labor pension, labor insurance, employment insurance and related funds under the Ministry of Labor. Old-system labor-pension assets are invested by the Bank of Taiwan. Official prints live on blf.gov.tw. Do not invent an AUM on this page. Do not mint `/blf-explained/`, `/taiwan-labor-funds/`, or `/bli-explained/`.
- BLI at No. 4 is administration and claims, not the investment bureau. A Roosevelt Road postcode is not automatically the fund desk.
- [GPIF](/gpif-japan-explained/) sits in [Tokyo](/careers/cities/tokyo/). [KIC](/korea-investment-corporation-kic-explained/) sits in [Seoul](/careers/cities/seoul/). Do not dollarize yen or invent a KIC AUM to decorate a Taipei skyline.

Do not treat this city page as a minted BLF explainer. There is no UAO BLF institution slug. Do not create one.

## Papers

Start at the [National Immigration Agency](https://www.immigration.gov.tw/5475/). Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Why people underestimate the job

Two neighbouring Roosevelt Road numbers are not one investment committee. Mandarin is the language of legitimacy even when the dealing language is English. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail.
