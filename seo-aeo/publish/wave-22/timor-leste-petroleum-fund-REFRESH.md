# Timor-Leste Petroleum Fund: official Q2 2026 capital, not US$18.9 billion

The Banco Central de Timor-Leste Q2 2026 press release, dated 7 August 2026, put Petroleum Fund capital at **USD 18.43 billion** at **30 June 2026**. That is the latest official capital figure this desk can cite. The live page still prints **US$18.9 billion as of the end of 2025** in the lede and **US$18.91 billion as of 30 November 2025** in the table. Those are stale quarter-end prints. They are not the current official number.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

| Receipts since inception | Approximately USD 25.30 billion |
| Investment returns since inception | USD 12.65 billion; average annualised return 4.72% |
| Withdrawals since inception | USD 19.66 billion |

The quarterly report's closing book value of **USD 18,432,896 thousand** is the same USD 18.43 billion the press release rounded. Print either. Do not invent a newer number.

## What this is

The Petroleum Fund is Timor-Leste's sovereign petroleum-revenue fund. Law No. 9/2005 created it in August 2005. Banco Central de Timor-Leste publishes quarterly reports and a press release on each quarter. Those filings are the official capital, receipts, returns, and withdrawals. There is no later official capital print on the BCTL site that this desk can cite after 30 June 2026.

## What is not official

- **US$18.9 billion as of the end of 2025.** That is the live lede. It is a stale quarter-end print, not the Q2 2026 official capital.
- **US$18.91 billion as of 30 November 2025.** That is the live table. Same problem: an older monthly print sold as current AUM.
- **US$18.74 billion as of June 2025.** That is last year's mid-year print. It is not current.
- A homemade US-dollar conversion of a later unofficial estimate. The house already prints dollars. Use the BCTL dollar. Do not invent a newer one.
