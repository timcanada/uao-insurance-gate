# Farmington Hills Employees’ Retirement System, explained

The Farmington Hills Employees’ Retirement System is the City of Farmington Hills pension, covering general, court, police, and fire members, not leftover Michigan MERS, leftover Grand Rapids Employees, leftover Grand Rapids Police and Fire, leftover Battle Creek Police and Fire, leftover Ann Arbor Employees, leftover St. Joseph Employees, leftover Michigan MPSERS, and not leftover MassPRIM. The official [financial statements for the fiscal year ended 30 June 2025](https://www.fhgov.com/media/fr1b0xa2/audited-financial-report-pension-24-25-ada.pdf) print net position restricted for pension benefits of **$191,391,814** at **30 June 2025**. Investments **$188,839,315** are not that card. Notes money-weighted return was **9.62 percent**. MD&A **7.1 percent** is the fiduciary-net-position increase, not the notes card. GASB **71.45 percent** is the notes funded card. The Health Care book is not this card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
