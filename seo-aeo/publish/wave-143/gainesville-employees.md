# Employees’ Pension Plan of the City of Gainesville, explained

The Employees’ Pension Plan of the City of Gainesville is Gainesville’s civilian pension, not leftover Jacksonville Police and Fire, leftover Tampa General Employees, and not leftover Miami GESE. The official [30 September 2025 Employees’ Pension Plan of the City of Gainesville financial statements](https://www.gainesvillefl.gov/files/assets/public/v/1/budget-amp-finance/documents/fy25-general-employees-pension-fund-auditors-report.pdf) print fiduciary net position of **$764,938,276** at **30 September 2025**. Investments **$751,616,757** are not that card. Cash and investments **$762,160,099** is not fiduciary net position. Money-weighted return was **9.44 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. GASB **105.72 percent** uses schedule fiduciary net position **$764,938,277**, a **$1** rounding off the statement. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 September 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position, the GASB 67 note, and the money-weighted schedule are the sourced lines:

1. **Fiduciary net position** **$764,938,276** at **30 September 2025** (beginning **$731,780,898**). Investments at fair value **$751,616,757**. Cash and cash equivalents **$10,543,342**. Cash and investments **$762,160,099** is not the net-position card. Police officers and firefighters sit in the Consolidated Police Officers and Firefighters Retirement Plan, not this book. Do not treat leftover Jacksonville Police and Fire **$2,903,990,005**, leftover Tampa General Employees, leftover Miami GESE, leftover Florida SBA **$300 billion+**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$723,582,073**. Net pension asset **$41,356,204**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **105.72 percent**. The GASB schedule prints plan fiduciary net position **$764,938,277**. That is not the statement **$764,938,276**.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **9.44 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 September 2025** (2024 **25.10 percent**).

Do not flatten the Employees’ Pension Plan of the City of Gainesville into [Jacksonville Police and Fire](/jacksonville-police-fire/), [Tampa General Employees’ Retirement Fund](/tampa-general-employees/), [City of Miami General Employees’ and Sanitation Employees’ Retirement Trust](/miami-gese/), or [Florida State Board of Administration](/florida-state-board-of-administration-explained/).
