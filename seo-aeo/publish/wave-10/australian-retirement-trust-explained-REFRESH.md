# Australian Retirement Trust, explained

Australian Retirement Trust is an Australian superannuation fund formed on **28 February 2022** by the merger of **QSuper and Sunsuper**. The audited financial report put net assets available for member benefits at **A$350.694 billion at 30 June 2025**, for more than **2.4 million** members. The High Growth option returned **11.9%** in FY2024–25; that option is **not** the default. This is not a sovereign wealth fund. This is not investment advice.

**Last updated:** 26 August 2026 · **Desk:** UAO Research · **Canonical URL:** `/australian-retirement-trust-explained/`

The live page this overwrites is not just stale. It is **wrong**. It says ART “emerged in 2024” from a merger of QSuper and *Local Government Super* at **$180+ billion** for 2.2 million members. Official ART reports: QSuper merged with **Sunsuper** on **28 February 2022**. Audited member assets a year later than that invented 2024 birth are already **A$351 billion**. The short twin `/australian-retirement-trust/` is also 200 — 301 that URL here after this overwrite, not before.

## What it is — and what the live page invented

ART is a **MySuper / choice superannuation trustee**, not a Queensland ministry SWF and not “the largest superannuation consolidation in Australian history” as a 2024 event. The merger that created the legal person was **QSuper + Sunsuper on 28 February 2022**. Local Government Super is a different fund. Do not write that merger.

Default options, from ART’s own FY2025 report:

- Super Savings Accumulation: **Lifecycle Investment Strategy**
- QSuper Accumulation: **QSuper Lifetime**
- Income accounts: **Balanced Risk-Adjusted**

**High Growth is a choice option.** Most members under 50 in MySuper sit in strategies *consistent with* High Growth, which is not the same as “the default is High Growth.” Do not quote 11.9% as “the fund return.”

CEO from **1 October 2025**: **Katherine (Kathy) Vincent**, appointed after David Anderson’s 22 September 2025 resignation. CIO named in the same report: **Ian Patrick**.

[AustralianSuper](/australian-super/) is larger (the Wave 3 draft uses the fund’s own “more than A$410 billion / 3.6 million members” at 31 December 2025). ART is the other household name in that system. The live “ranks fourth… ahead of industry mega-funds” line is marketing, not a filing.

## How a peer should read it

- **Wrong parents, wrong year, wrong size.** QSuper + Sunsuper, 2022, A$351bn. Anything that says Local Government Super / 2024 / $180bn is not this fund.
- **Option returns ≠ fund return.** Ask which option. Then ask whether it is default.
- **Super, not SWF.** Mandatory contributions, trustee law, APRA. Compare with AustralianSuper and [NZ Super](/new-zealand-superannuation-fund-explained/) only after you have respected that.
- **CEO changed in 2025.** Vincent, not Anderson, is the current print from the FY2025 report.
