# Chicago Teachers’ Pension Fund, explained

The Chicago Teachers’ Pension Fund is Chicago’s teacher pension, not leftover Illinois TRS. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.ctpf.org/sites/files/2026-01/2025%20ACFR_FINAL_v7.pdf) prints net position restricted for pension benefits of **$13,657,637,993** at **30 June 2025**. Investments at fair value were **$13,464,363,637**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted return was **12.1 percent**. The money-weighted return was **11.63 percent** net of investment expenses. The actuarial-value funded ratio was **47.9 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position restricted for pension benefits** **$13,657,637,993** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$12,741,782,948**). Investments at fair value **$13,464,363,637** is not fiduciary net position. The MD&A card **$13,657.6 million** is the same pension book, rounded. The news-card **$13.7 billion** is that same book, rounded again. The Health Insurance Fund prints a zero fiduciary net position — it is a pass-through, not a second pension book. Those four lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover Illinois TRS, leftover Illinois State Board of Investment, leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **Cash flows on the same report.** Total additions **$2,688,846,910**. Net investment income **$1,407,616,612**. Pension benefits **$1,635,428,644**. Total pension liability **$28,626,660,359**.
3. **Returns and funded status.** Time-weighted return **12.1 percent**. Gross money-weighted return **12.05 percent**. Annual money-weighted return, net of investment expenses, **11.63 percent**. Those three official one-year prints are not interchangeable. Actuarial-value funded ratio **47.9 percent**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **47.71 percent**. Those two funded cards are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Chicago Teachers into [Illinois TRS](/illinois-trs/), [Illinois State Board of Investment](/illinois-isbi/), [IMRF](/illinois-imrf/), [Cook County Pension](/cook-county-pension/), [Chicago Fire FABF](/chicago-fire-fabf/), [Chicago LABF](/chicago-labf/), or [Cook County Forest Preserve](/cook-forest-preserve/). Illinois TRS is the statewide teacher file. IMRF is the local-government file. Cook County is the county employee file. FABF is the Chicago fire file. LABF is the Chicago laborers file. This is **one Chicago teacher-pension file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Chicago Teachers AUM” has to name the book. The **$13,657,637,993** line is net position restricted for pension benefits. The **$13,464,363,637** line is investments. The Health Insurance Fund is not the pension book.
