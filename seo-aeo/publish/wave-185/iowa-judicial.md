# Iowa Judicial Retirement System, explained

The Iowa Judicial Retirement System is Iowa’s statewide judge pension, not leftover IPERS (**$46,740,552,174**), leftover Iowa MFPRSI, leftover Iowa PORS (**$817,716,339**), leftover Illinois JRS, leftover MassPRIM. The official [financial statements as of 30 June 2025](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/publications/ADRPT/1594599.pdf) print net position restricted for pensions of **$300,885,184** at **30 June 2025**. Investments **$300,666,781** are not that card. Notes and RSI money-weighted return was **11.96 percent**. MD&A **11.82 percent** is the investment-section return, not the notes card. GASB **105.54 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
