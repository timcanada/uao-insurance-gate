# Relocating to Singapore for GIC, Temasek or a family office

Singapore is a state-capital city and a family-office city at once: GIC and Temasek are the official centres of gravity, and a growing private-wealth stack sits around them. A move here is often a move into process, confidentiality and a city-state's rules. Confirm Employment Pass and tax position on official Singapore sites. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here

- [GIC](https://www.gic.com.sg/) — Singapore's sovereign wealth fund, as it describes itself.
- [Temasek](https://www.temasek.com.sg/) — a commercial investment company owned by the Government of Singapore; not the same legal animal as GIC. If you need the distinction, read our explainer cluster (`temasek-vs-gic-difference`) rather than a second copy here.

Family offices and multi-family offices cluster here for reasons that are public policy as well as lifestyle. Do not flatten them into “UHNW in Asia.”

## Why people underestimate the job

Singapore rewards people who can write, wait, and still have a view. The city is easy to land in and easy to fail in if you treat the institution like a regional coverage job. Housing and schooling are the silent line items. The Ministry of Manpower and IRAS — not a Slack rumour — decide whether the offer is real.

- [Ministry of Manpower](https://www.mom.gov.sg/)
- [Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore](https://www.iras.gov.sg/)

## Allocator-adjacent roles

Global pensions and endowments staff Singapore as a *window*, not always as HQ. Ask whether you are building a book or staffing a time zone.
