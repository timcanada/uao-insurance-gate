# Kansas Public Employees Retirement System, explained

The Kansas Public Employees Retirement System is Kansas’s statewide cost-sharing pension, not leftover MOSERS (**$9,565,800,614**), leftover Oklahoma OPERS, leftover Nebraska School. The official [30 June 2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://web.archive.org/web/20251229015635id_/https://kspers.gov/annualreport2025.pdf) prints pension-plan net position restricted for pensions of **$29,449,836,163** at **30 June 2025**. Custodial funds **$311,176,930** are not that card. Investments **$29,260,439,065** are not that card. Notes money-weighted return was **9.94 percent**. MD&A time-weighted **9.9 percent** is not the money-weighted card. Introductory **74.0 percent** is the actuarial funded-ratio print. GASB **75.26 percent** is of this book.
