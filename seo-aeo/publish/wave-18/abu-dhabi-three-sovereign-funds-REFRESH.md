# Abu Dhabi’s sovereign funds: ADIA, Mubadala, L’IMAD

The Supreme Council for Financial and Economic Affairs names Abu Dhabi’s principal sovereign investment funds as **ADIA**, **Mubadala Investment Company** and **L’IMAD Holding**. ADIA **does not publish AUM**; its official 2024 score is **6.3% / 7.1%** over 20 / 30 years. Mubadala printed **AED 1.4 trillion (US$385 billion)** at year-end 2025. ADQ’s last audited consolidation is **AED 921.7 billion** of total assets at **31 December 2024**; SCFEA later put ADQ under L’IMAD. The live UAO page’s ADIA **$150 billion** and **$800–900 billion** pile are tracker inventions. This is not investment advice.

SCFEA also oversees **ADNOC**. That is an oil company, not a fourth SWF AUM.

## The 2026 consolidation, in official words

The Abu Dhabi Media Office: SCFEA “issued a resolution to consolidate the assets and investments of L’IMAD Holding Company (L’IMAD), and the Abu Dhabi Developmental Holding Group (ADQ) under the umbrella of L’IMAD.” The same release says SCFEA oversees **ADIA, Mubadala and L’IMAD**.

That is why this URL’s title changes. The live page’s three names were ADIA / Mubadala / ADQ. Official 2026 names are ADIA / Mubadala / L’IMAD, with ADQ the developmental group underneath. Do not invent a combined dollar. ADIA still has no AUM. You cannot add “no AUM + $385bn + AED 921.7bn.”

## What is not official — do not put it back

- **ADIA $150 billion.** Harmful understatement *and* an invented AUM.
- **$800–900 billion** combined. Rank-table arithmetic on numbers ADIA will not print.
- ADIA “restructured into its current form in **2018**.” Official founding year is **1976**.
- Homemade ADIA sleeve weights (**40–50% / 20–30% / 25–35%**) presented as current policy. ADIA prints *ranges* in the Review; Wave 9 already has them. Do not invent a new mix here.
- Adding [QIA](/qatar-investment-authority-qia/) or [KIA](/kuwait-investment-authority-kia/) dollars to the pile. Neither publishes AUM.
