# Competitor intelligence

Reviewed against public sites and historical media kits on 25 August 2026. Prices below are **directional** (many kits are years old). Use them to set UAO packages, not to quote as current.

## The field

### Newsrooms we are compared to

**Investment & Pensions Europe (IPE)**  
European pension paper of record. Awards, Real Assets, DACH, Quest (manager matching). Strength: density of European plan-sponsor names. Weakness: not built as a global universal-owner worldview; thin on family offices and Gulf nation-capital as *owners*. **Steal:** English-language AEO pages European CIOs actually search at 06:00.

**Pensions & Investments**  
US plan-sponsor newspaper. Print still matters to consultants. 2020-era kits showed cover wraps north of $90k and supplements in the $30–50k range. Strength: legitimacy in US public funds. Weakness: slow to entity-graph; definitions and people pages are not the product. **Steal:** the explainer + registry layer they will not staff.

**Institutional Investor**  
Allocator network, rankings, events. Strength: access. Weakness: the public web is a funnel to membership, not a citation machine. **Steal:** public citable pages that II keeps behind the rope.

**Top1000funds (Conexus)**  
Asset-owner interviews and a proprietary executive list. Older kit: newsletter to ~5,000 executives plus 5,000 service providers; leaderboard ~$120k; sponsored research ~$98k; custom research from ~$10k. Strength: peer voice. Weakness: not daily, not AEO, not careers. **Steal:** interview gravity *plus* a daily desk and a people graph.

**SWFI**  
The dataset other journalists already cite. Newsletter claimed 45k+. Advertising is “reach the $30T+ public-investor segment.” Strength: numbers that get lifted. Weakness: not a newsroom habit; not careers; not family offices as peers. **Steal:** be the *sentence around* the data — then publish UAO 100 so we are the data.

**MandateWire (FT)**  
Paid RFP/mandate intelligence, 70+ markets, investor profiles. Strength: budget from asset-manager sales teams. Weakness: not public, not journalism brand. **Steal:** the *public* people-move and careers adjacency. Do not try to clone their paid file.

### Family-office and CIO press

**Campden Wealth / Family Capital / Wealth Briefing**  
Membership, events, annual FO surveys (often with a bank). Strength: principals in the room. Weakness: lifestyle/society gravity. **Steal:** treat family offices as universal owners with books, not as attendees.

**Chief Investment Officer (ex-aiCIO) / With Intelligence / Preqin news**  
Trade moves and league tables. Strength: speed on appointments. Weakness: amnesia — yesterday’s move dies. **Steal:** Allocator Class pages that persist.

### Adjacent data firms (not media, but they take the citation)

Preqin, PitchBook, bfinance, CEM Benchmarking, Thinking Ahead Institute (Willis Towers Watson rankings), Global SWF, IFSWF. We partner or cite; we do not pretend to be their terminal. We *compile and judge*.

## What we will not copy

- Award-show industrial complexes (IPE style) as the core model.
- Print-rate-card thinking as the only product.
- Gated mandate databases we cannot legally or operationally run in Wave 1–2.
- Vendor native posts in the newsroom.
