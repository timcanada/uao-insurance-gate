# Cambridge University Endowment Fund, explained

The Cambridge University Endowment Fund is Cambridge’s pooled long-horizon book, not leftover Oxford, leftover USS (**£76.8bn**), leftover Harvard **$56.9 billion**. The official [financial statements for the year ended 31 July 2025](https://www.admin.cam.ac.uk/reporter/2025-26/weekly/6833/Annual_Reports_Financial_Statements-2025.pdf) print CUEF net asset value of **£4,606.8 million** at **31 July 2025**. The University Group share **£4,141.9 million** is not the whole fund. Print pounds. Do not dollarize. The CUEF returned **5.6 percent** net for the year ended **30 June 2025**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover USS overwrite or a leftover Harvard overwrite.** Do not mint `/cambridge-endowment-explained/` or `/cuef/`. **Never mint `/cambridge/` or `/ucim/`.** PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP, Cambridge, issued the auditors’ report dated **24 November 2025** (Andy Grimbly).

## What is official (31 July 2025)

The statements print the CUEF in **£ million**.

1. **CUEF net asset value** **£4,606.8 million** at **31 July 2025** (2024 **£4,285.9 million**). University Group share **£4,141.9 million** (about **90 percent**). Other investors **£464.9 million** (17 Colleges and 5 Trusts). Those three lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover USS **£76.8bn**, leftover PFZW **€35bn**, leftover Harvard **$56.9 billion**, leftover Notre Dame Endowment Pool **$25,434,616 thousand**, leftover GEPF **R2,651,120,439 thousand** as this filing. Do not convert these sterling lines into a homemade US-dollar AUM.
2. **Named books are not interchangeable.** Group net assets **£8,263 million** are not the CUEF card. Financial-statement values sit on a **31 July** basis. The performance year ends **30 June**.
3. **Returns and payout.** CUEF net return **+5.6 percent** for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **+9.1 percent**). Since 1 July 2020 annualized **+8.2 percent**. Distribution to investors **£173.6 million** (Group **£157.3 million**; other **£16.3 million**) for the year to **31 July 2025**. Policy around **4 percent** of NAV. PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP, Cambridge, **24 November 2025**.
