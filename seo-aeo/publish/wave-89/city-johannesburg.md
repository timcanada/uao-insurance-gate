# Relocating to Johannesburg for a South African owner desk

Johannesburg is South Africa’s capital-markets floor. It is not the Public Investment Corporation’s head office. [PIC](https://www.pic.gov.za/contact-us)’s official physical address is **Menlyn Maine Central Square, Corner Aramist Avenue and Corobay Avenue, Waterkloof Glen Extension 2, Pretoria, 0181**. A move here is often a move onto a markets window whose public-client book sits in Pretoria — or into a satellite whose investment committee sits there. Right to work sits with official South African sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit in Pretoria

- PIC is a state-owned investment manager (Financial Services Provider **FSP 19777**). Official prints live on pic.gov.za. Do not invent an AUM on this page. Do not mint `/pic-explained/`, `/johannesburg-pic/`, `/gepf/`, or `/careers/cities/pretoria/`.
- GEPF is a **client**, not a second PIC AUM. Do not add the two. There is no UAO PIC or GEPF institution slug. Do not mint one.
- Ask whether the investment committee sits in Pretoria. If it does, you are not at the centre.

Do not treat this city page as a minted PIC explainer.

## Papers

Start at the [Department of Home Affairs](https://www.dha.gov.za/). Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Why people underestimate the job

A Johannesburg postcode is not automatically the public-client book. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail. Satellites fail when the IC is elsewhere — the same problem our [London note](/careers/intelligence/why-london-satellites-fail/) already names.
