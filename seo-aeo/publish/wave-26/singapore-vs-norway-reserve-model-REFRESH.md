# Singapore vs Norway reserves: GIC has no AUM; GPFG is kroner

GIC Private Limited does **not** publish assets under management. Norway’s Government Pension Fund Global is a kroner book: **22,683 billion kroner** at **30 June 2026**. The live page, dated 7 July 2026, still assigns GIC **approximately $690 billion**, Temasek **approximately $403 billion as of end-2023**, and GPFG **approximately $1.368 trillion** as of Q3 2024. Those dollars are not the filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

Print kroner for GPFG. Do **not** convert 22,683 billion kroner into a homemade US-dollar AUM on this URL. The governance contrast is real. The dollar lede is not.

## What the models actually are

Singapore splits the jobs. [GIC](/gic-singapore-explained/) manages long-horizon government assets and will not give you a size. [Temasek](/is-temasek-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/) owns a commercial portfolio and **does** print NPV. MAS holds official foreign reserves for the exchange-rate regime. Those are three legal persons.

Norway’s [GPFG](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/) invests **only abroad**. NBIM executes; the Ministry of Finance sets the mandate. That separation is official.

A “Singapore vs Norway” page that needs GIC **$690 billion** and GPFG **$1.368 trillion** to make the contrast is consultancy arithmetic.

## What is not official

- GIC AUM in any dollar, including **$690 billion**.
- GPFG **$1.368 trillion** (or any other homemade dollar).
- Temasek **$403 billion** as the current print.
- Expanding GIC as “Government of Singapore Investment Corporation.”
