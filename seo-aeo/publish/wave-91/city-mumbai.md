# Relocating to Mumbai for an NIIF or India owner-manager desk

Mumbai is the corporate-headquarters city of the [National Investment and Infrastructure Fund Limited](/india-niif-explained/). Official [corporate HQ](https://niifindia.in/get-in-touch/): **UTI Tower, GN Block, 4th Floor, BKC, Mumbai 400 051**. The registered office is **Hindustan Times House, Kasturba Gandhi Marg, New Delhi**. NIIF was founded in **2015**. It is a sovereign-anchored alternative asset manager, **not** a savings SWF. Do not mint `/niif/` or `/careers/cities/new-delhi/`. Right to work sits with official Indian sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit elsewhere

- [NIIF](/india-niif-explained/) is a fund manager anchored by the Government of India. Official prints live on the institution keeper. Do not invent an AUM on this page. Do not reprint leftover **$9 billion** or leftover invented seed **$650 million**. Do not mint `/niif/`, `/mumbai-niif/`, or `/national-investment-and-infrastructure-fund/`.
- The registered office is New Delhi. A BKC floor is not automatically the Governing Council. Do not mint `/careers/cities/new-delhi/` (404).
- [Danantara](/danantara-indonesia-sovereign-fund/) sits in [Jakarta](/careers/cities/jakarta/). [Khazanah](/is-khazanah-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/) sits in [Kuala Lumpur](/careers/cities/kuala-lumpur/). Do not add those books to NIIF.
- Do not 301 this city page onto leftover `/niif-india-infrastructure-fund/`. Paste the Wave 20 keeper first.

## Why people underestimate the job

A 2015 manager with a Delhi registered office is not India’s GPFG. English and Hindi share the building; the mandate still sits with the Governing Council. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail.
