# San Diego County Employees Retirement Association, explained

The San Diego County Employees Retirement Association is the San Diego County pension, a component unit of the County of San Diego, not leftover San Diego City SDCERS, leftover LACERA, leftover SamCERA, leftover Sacramento County, and not leftover MassPRIM. The official [30 June 2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://content.sdcera.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2025-ACFR-FINAL-DRAFT-web.pdf) prints pension-plan net position restricted of **$19,661,907 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Total-fund fiduciary net position **$19,745,739 thousand** includes OPEB **$83,832 thousand** and is not the pension card. RSI pension-plan money-weighted return was **11.15 percent**. GASB **84.28 percent** is not actuarial-value **80.2 percent**. Statement lines are in **thousands**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
