# Public pension funded status: CalPERS is 85% on $637.1 billion, not 83% on $543 billion

Funded status is assets ÷ accrued liabilities on a stated basis. The live dollar table is stale. The live page, dated 6 July 2026, still assigns [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/) **$543 billion** and **83%** as of June 2025, invents a Public Plans Database pile of **$4.2 trillion** in assets and **$4.7 trillion** in liabilities, files TIAA at **$343 billion** as if it were a state plan, and reprints Illinois TRS at **$67 billion** of assets against **$178 billion** of liabilities (**38%**). Those leftovers are not the current filings.

## What the page can say

- **Quote the pair.** CalPERS prints AUM, return, and funded status on the same 30 June 2026 release. CalSTRS prints AUM at 30 June 2026 and funded status at **30 June 2025**. Those dates are part of the number.
- **Do not invent a sector.** Adding the fifteen largest plans into **$4.2 trillion / $4.7 trillion** is not a filing this desk fetched.
- **What funded status is not.** It is not a market-value NAV, and it is not a licence to reprint leftover AUM.
