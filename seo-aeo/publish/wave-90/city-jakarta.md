# Relocating to Jakarta for a Danantara or Indonesia owner desk

Jakarta is the headquarters city of [Danantara Indonesia](/danantara-indonesia-sovereign-fund/). Official [headquarter](https://www.danantaraindonesia.co.id/help-center): **Wisma Danantara Indonesia, Jalan Jend. Gatot Subroto Kav. 36-38, South Jakarta, 12190**. Danantara is **Badan Pengelola Investasi Daya Anagata Nusantara**, established under **Law No. 1 of 2025**. It does **not** publish an official AUM. Indonesia Investment Authority is a **different** legal person. A move here is often a move onto that new statutory file. Right to work sits with official Indonesian sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit elsewhere

- [Danantara](/danantara-indonesia-sovereign-fund/) is a strategic investment-management institution under the President. Official prints live on the institution keeper. Do not invent an AUM on this page. Do not reprint leftover **IDR 150 trillion / ~US$9.5 billion** or press **US$900 billion**. Do not mint `/danantara-explained/` or `/jakarta-danantara/`.
- Indonesia Investment Authority is a **different** legal person. Do not add the two. Do not mint `/indonesia-investment-authority/`.
- [Khazanah](/is-khazanah-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/) sits in [Kuala Lumpur](/careers/cities/kuala-lumpur/). [GIC](https://www.gic.com.sg/) and [Temasek](https://www.temasek.com.sg/) sit in [Singapore](/careers/cities/singapore/). Do not invent a GIC AUM to decorate a Jakarta skyline.
- Do not 301 this city page onto leftover `/indonesia-danantara-explained/` or `/danantara-indonesia/`. Paste the Wave 15 keeper first.

## Why people underestimate the job

A 2025 statute is not a funded savings book until the house prints one. Bahasa Indonesia is the language of legitimacy even when the dealing language is English. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail.
