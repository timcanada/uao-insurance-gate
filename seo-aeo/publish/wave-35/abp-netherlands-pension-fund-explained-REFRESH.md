# ABP: available assets are €530 billion, not leftover €532 billion

ABP — **Stichting Pensioenfonds ABP** — is the Dutch pension fund for government and education. That job is real. The live euro table is not. The live page still assigns **approximately €532 billion**, invents **€543 billion including assets for other APG clients**, files **2.8 million** members, and treats a **121.7%** Q3 2025 funding ratio as current. Those leftovers are not the current filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## Official numbers (do not mix available assets with APG clients)

| Available assets | **€530 billion** at **31 March 2026** (Q1 2026 press, 23 April 2026) | **€532 billion**; **€543 billion** “including APG clients” |
| YE2025 available assets | **€533.134 billion** at **31 December 2025** (Jaarverslag 2025 table: **533.134** in **€ million**). YE2024 **€542.223 billion** | Leftover **€532 billion** as a 2025 current line |

Print euros. Quote **available assets**, not an APG client stack. APG executes. It is **not** a second ABP AUM. The Q1 2026 press table also prints official weights (fixed income **41.0%**, equity **30.5%**, alternatives **18.8%**, real estate **9.5%**). Do not put leftover “33–35% listed equities” or a homemade **€18–20 billion** infrastructure ticket back on the page.

The Dutch **system** job already sits on [`/netherlands-pension-system-abp-pfzw/`](/netherlands-pension-system-abp-pfzw/) (Wave 34). **Do not 301 this institution keeper onto that page.** **Do not overwrite** [PFZW](/pfzw-netherlands-pension-fund-explained/) (Wave 16).

## What the page can say

- **Who ABP is.** Public-sector and education. Mandatory for eligible sectors. The board is a Dutch pension-fund board, not a corporate sponsor.
- **The 2027 change.** ABP’s own Q1 2026 press says it wants to move to the new scheme on **1 January 2027** with funding of at least **110%**. Current funding **119.1%** is the official line for that test. It is not a licence to reprint **€532 billion**.
- **How to print scale.** Quote the Q1 2026 available-assets line and the Jaarverslag 2025 year-end line. Dates are part of the number.

## What is not official

- Available assets **€532 billion**, or **€543 billion** including APG clients.
- **2.8 million** members.
- **121.7%** or **131%** as the current funding print.
- Homemade listed-equity **33–35%**, infrastructure **€18–20 billion**, or private-equity **€10 billion** tickets.
