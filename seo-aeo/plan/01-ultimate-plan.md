**Status:** Launch document. Written by running Super Prompt v4 against the live site on 25 August 2026.  
**North star (locked 26 Aug 2026):** `../GOALS.md` — #1 across search and LLMs; 1M subscribers (two lanes); $1M/year ads; 24/7 learning. This plan is how. The goal file is why.  
**Site today:** Ghost publication with two weekday desks, research, careers, registry, membership, and a large explainer cluster. Not a blank page.  
**Job:** Turn that into the default home of universal ownership — for readers, for people who move, for advertisers, and for other journalists.

## 1. What we are actually building

Universal Asset Owners already has the rare thing: a *concentrated* morning habit aimed at people who allocate permanent capital. The site says the list includes 98% of the world’s sovereign wealth funds, 20 of 20 of the largest pensions, 85% of the largest family offices, 100+ billionaires, and 45,000+ CIOs and investment leaders. Those figures are a research-desk audit, not a vanity metric — and they are the commercial product.

The missing piece is not “more blog posts.” It is a **public graph of the world those people live in**, so that:

- a deputy CIO in Oslo, Abu Dhabi, Toronto, or São Paulo finds us on a non-branded search or an AI answer;
- a partner at a law firm or a family-office technology vendor can justify $20k–$50k a month;
- a reporter at Bloomberg can lift a number and write “according to Universal Asset Owners”;
- a person leaving an asset manager for a sovereign fund uses our careers and city pages, then stays in the brief.

That graph is how we get to tens of thousands of pages without becoming junk.

## 2. Diagnosis of the live site (25 Aug 2026)

### What is already strong

- **Editorial identity.** About, editorial standards, ethics, methodology, masthead, responsible-AI language. This is a newsroom, not a newsletter farm.
- **Habit product.** Morning brief + afternoon Probability Desk, each in four formats.
- **AEO start.** `/llms/` exists. Dozens of institution explainers and what-is pages are already written in an answer-first voice.
- **Commercial stack.** Membership at $60k / $100k / $175k+ / $300k+. `/advertise/` and `/readership/` exist. Careers already sells recruitment campaigns.
- **Sticklers started.** Careers Intelligence (including *Moving into a sovereign wealth fund*), Registry, People / Allocator Class, Insider people-moves.

### What will stop us at scale

- **Cannibalization.** Sitemap already contains twin slugs (`santiago-principles` vs `santiago-principles-explained`; `total-portfolio-approach` vs `total-portfolio-approach-investing`; several USS / SWIB variants). Answer engines will pick one and ignore the rest — or trust none.
- **Explainer overweight.** The next 500 pages must not be more “what is” synonyms. The graph layers (people, cities, firms, moves) are thin.
- **Registry is a shell.** `/registry/` and `/registry/people/` currently resolve as a thin “Top 100” frame. This should become the most linked database in the category.
- **Advertise is a landing page, not a product.** No public packages at the $20–50k monthly level. A CMO cannot self-qualify.
- **Service-provider journalism is missing.** We write *as* owners. We rarely write the pages that make counsel, bankers, and vendors feel this is *their* trade press too — without capturing the newsroom.
- **Global holes.** Gulf and Canada show up in desks and careers. Family offices, Latin America, Africa, Japan/Korea, and European corporates are under-built as *hubs*.
- **Quotation engine is informal.** `/press-desk/` and `/newsroom/` exist; there is no weekly “here is the number you can attribute” machine.

## 3. Strategy in five lines

1. **Own the nouns.** Every consequential institution, person, city, and question gets one canonical URL.
2. **Own the mornings.** Desks stay the prestige layer. SEO/AEO pages feed the desks; desks link back and mint authority.
3. **Own the move.** Careers + relocation + leadership ledger is the personal flywheel. People change jobs more often than they change their philosophy.
4. **Own the room for sale.** Directory + labelled packages + recruitment, priced so a serious firm spends $20–50k/month.
5. **Own the citation.** Original ledgers and probability weights that other journalists and models have to source.

## 4. The flywheel (how traffic becomes money)

```text

        ↓

        ↓
Daily brief signup (work email, editorial review)
        ↓

        ↓
        ├── Allocator path → sample research → $60k–$300k membership

```

If a page cannot name which path it serves, it is a hobby.

## 7. AEO: become the sentence the model reads aloud

Google’s own docs still say there is no magic schema for AI Overviews. Citations come from pages that already rank, that a model can extract, and that other trusted pages repeat.

So AEO for UAO is:

1. **Answer block** (40–80 words) under the H1.
2. **Entity consistency** — same legal name, same AUM as-of date, same HQ, same `sameAs` (official site, Wikipedia, LinkedIn) on every mention.
3. **`/llms/` as the machine table of contents.** Update it when a core explainer is merged or a registry hub goes live.
4. **Original numbers** (Probability Desk weights, UAO 100, leadership counts) that models prefer over generic prose.
5. **Third-party mentions.** Being quoted in FT/Bloomberg is an AEO strategy, not just a vanity PR strategy. ChatGPT in particular follows established sources.
6. **Standing 50-query panel** scored weekly (see `08-monitoring-self-learning.md`).

## 8. Traffic, besides Google

- **Email** is the product. SEO’s job is to fill a *reviewed* list, not a raw one.
- **Podcast / video / chart** already exist — clip people-moves and city pages into the same story.
- **LinkedIn** of named correspondents, not a brand account screaming headlines.
- **X (@universalowners)** for desk alerts and press.
- **Careers alerts** as a second newsletter (talent + search firms).
- **Press pitches:** one attributable finding per week.
- **Associations:** IFSWF, ILPA, CFA Institute, local pension associations — not as SEO spam, as citation and event paths.
- **Do not:** retail social, paid link schemes, AI-spun guest posts.

## 9. Advertisers at $20k–$50k / month

The buyer is not “a company that wants impressions.” It is a firm whose *average mandate or placement* is six or seven figures. They already pay IPE, P&I, and conference organizers that much.

We sell **presence in a verified room**, not CPMs.

Packages (detail in `../media/advertiser-packages.md`):

- **Desk — $20–25k/mo.** Newsletter + site + one podcast billboard. Labelled.
- **Category — $30–40k/mo.** Own “fiduciary law” or “family-office technology” for a quarter. Includes a labelled briefing and a directory adjacency.
- **Platform — $45–55k/mo.** Desk + careers featured + directory + one private briefing series.

Proof they will ask for, and we already promised on `/readership/`: subscriber-level audit methodology under NDA, seniority survey, and “how we establish 98% / 20-of-20.” Sales cannot invent a new number.

**Content that creates inbound:** Wave 1 service-provider hubs (law, accounting, tech, banking) — written for the *allocator’s* decision, useful enough that the vendor’s BD lead forwards them internally and then calls us.

## 11. Being quoted — journalism, not “thought leadership”

Other reporters quote sources that (a) have a number, (b) answer the phone, (c) will not embarrass them.

Operating cadence:

- Keep `/press-desk/` current with 8–10 lift-ready findings and a human on the other end of info@ / press.
- Probability Desk already produces weights — package the week’s sharpest one as a four-line advisory.
- UAO 100 + leadership ledger + “roles we verified this week” are citeable recurrences.
- Correspondents (Gulf, Asia, Canada, Europe) byline the pages we want attributed.
- Masthead remains the proof we are a newsroom.
