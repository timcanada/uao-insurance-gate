# What is a superannuation trustee?

A superannuation trustee that runs an APRA-regulated fund is an RSE licensee — a constitutional corporation, body corporate, or group of individual trustees that holds a licence granted under Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993 section 29D. The fund itself is a registrable superannuation entity. An SMSF is not an RSE. From 1 July 2021 the SIS Act covenants require best financial interests. This page is not a ranking and not licensing advice.

## The licensee is not the fund

APRA’s public [register of superannuation institutions](https://www.apra.gov.au/register-of-superannuation-institutions) (updated **24 August 2026**) states the two legal persons:

1. A **registrable superannuation entity (RSE)** is a regulated superannuation fund, an approved deposit fund, or a pooled superannuation trust. It **does not include a self-managed superannuation fund**. Registration sits under Part 2B of the *Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993*.
2. An **RSE licensee** is a constitutional corporation, body corporate, or group of individual trustees that holds an RSE licence granted by APRA under **section 29D** of that Act.

APRA’s [licensing guidelines](https://www.apra.gov.au/licensing-guidelines-for-superannuation) say the same operating rule in one sentence: if you intend to operate an RSE, you **must hold an RSE licence** issued by APRA. Only an RSE licensee may apply to register the fund.

The trustee’s statutory covenant changed in 2021. The [Treasury Laws Amendment (Your Future, Your Super) Act 2021](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2021A00046/latest/text) (No. 46, 2021) amended SIS Act sections 52 and 52A so that each trustee, and each director of a corporate trustee, must perform duties and exercise powers in the **best financial interests** of the beneficiaries. Those amendments apply to duties performed and powers exercised on or after **1 July 2021**.

A US public-plan administrator is a different job. That keeper is [what an administrator is for a pension plan](/what-is-an-administrator-for-a-pension-plan/). An Australian SMSF trustee is regulated by the ATO, not by this APRA licence. Do not flatten those three persons.

## What an owner is actually buying

A member of a large APRA fund is buying a **licensed trustee** and a **registered RSE** — not a ministry SWF and not a US ERISA administrator.
