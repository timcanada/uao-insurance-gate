# Alecta (Sweden), explained

Alecta is a Swedish occupational-pension mutual, not a sovereign wealth fund and not a second Nordic-model page. The official English [Annual and Sustainability Report 2025](https://www.alecta.se/media/vdlbg0sz/alecta-annual-and-sustainability-report-2025.pdf) prints total investments of **SEK 1,404,983 million** at **31 December 2025**. Group solvency was **208 percent**. The default defined-contribution product, Alecta Optimal Pension, returned **9.6 percent** in 2025. Alecta is owned by **2.9 million** private customers and **37,000** corporate customers. Print kronor. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (English AR 2025)

Alecta Tjänstepension Ömsesidigt has administered collectively agreed occupational pensions since **1917**. The 2025 English report’s total-return table (Insurance Sweden basis) is the sourced scale:

1. **Total investments** **SEK 1,404,983 million** at **31 December 2025** (2024: **SEK 1,314,614 million**). Shares **SEK 508,192 million**; debt securities **SEK 645,894 million**; alternative assets **SEK 250,897 million**. Group total return **6.8 percent**. The cover line “SEK 1,400 million” is a translation slip — the table is the filing.
2. **Solvency** **208 percent** at year-end 2025 (2024: **202 percent**). Collective funding for defined-benefit insurance **167 percent**. Management expense ratio, Group, **0.07 percent**.
3. **Alecta Optimal Pension** (default): **9.6 percent** in 2025; five-year average **7.3 percent** in the total-return table; market value of the total AOP book **SEK 346.8 billion**. Defined-benefit insurance returned **5.9 percent**.

Alecta’s own year-end press release repeats managed capital of **SEK 1,405 billion** at **31 December 2025**. That is the same kronor pile as the table, rounded.

Do not flatten Alecta into [ATP](/atp-denmark-pension-fund/) or [NBIM](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/). ATP files kroner-adjacent Danish kroner. NBIM files Norwegian kroner and is not a pension.
