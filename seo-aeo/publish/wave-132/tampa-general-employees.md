# City of Tampa General Employees’ Retirement Fund, explained

City of Tampa General Employees’ Retirement Fund is Tampa’s civilian city pension, not leftover Tampa Fire and Police and not leftover Jacksonville Police and Fire. The official [City of Tampa FY2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.tampa.gov/sites/default/files/document/2026/fy25-final-acfr-3.25.26-optimized-w-links.pdf) is the house book. No separate audited financial statement is issued for this Fund. It prints net position restricted for pension benefits of **$929,228,201** at **30 September 2025**. Statement investments at fair value were **$929,105,595**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Gross money-weighted return was **20.09 percent**. Net money-weighted return was **10.89 percent**. Those official prints are not interchangeable. Fiduciary net position was **87.57 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

Combined City of Tampa pension-trust net position **$4,337,050,227** is both Tampa books, not this Fund. Cash and investments **$929,496,148** is not fiduciary net position. Employer-reporting GASB 68 **82.81 percent** is the prior-measurement card, not the plan **87.57 percent** card. Division A has been closed to new hires since **1 October 1981**. Division B is the open book. Do not invent a one-year time-weighted return.
