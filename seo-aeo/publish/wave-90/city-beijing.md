# Relocating to Beijing for a CIC or China owner desk

Beijing is the headquarters city of [China Investment Corporation](/china-investment-corporation-cic-explained/). Official [contact](https://www.china-inv.cn/chinainven/home/Contact_Us.shtml): **Building 3, No. 9, Dongzhimenwai Xiejie, Dongcheng District, Beijing, 100028**. CIC [relocated](https://www.china-inv.cn/chinainven/Media/2025-05/1002862.shtml) there on **3 June 2025** from New Poly Plaza, 1 Chaoyangmen Beidajie. It was founded **29 September 2007**. Overseas offices CIC lists are Hong Kong, New York and London. A move here is often a move onto the home file — not a satellite. Right to work sits with official Chinese sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit elsewhere

- [CIC](/china-investment-corporation-cic-explained/) is the People’s Republic of China’s sovereign wealth fund. Official prints live on the institution keeper. Do not invent an AUM on this page. Do not reprint leftover **$1.3 trillion**. Do not mint `/beijing-cic/`, `/safe-china/`, or `/nssf-explained/`.
- CIC International (Hong Kong), the New York representative office and the London representative office are **satellites**. If the desk is theirs, read the [Hong Kong](/careers/cities/hong-kong/), [New York](/careers/cities/new-york/) and [London](/careers/cities/london/) city pages. Headquarters is Beijing.
- [SAFE](https://www.safe.gov.cn/) is a different institution. Do not invent a SAFE AUM and stack it next to CIC.
- [GPIF](/gpif-japan-explained/) sits in [Tokyo](/careers/cities/tokyo/). Do not dollarize yen to decorate a Beijing skyline.

Do not 301 this city page onto leftover `/china-investment-corporation-cic/`. Paste the Wave 8 keeper first.

## Why people underestimate the job

A 2025 street-address change is not a change of mandate. Mandarin is the language of legitimacy even when the dealing language is English. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail. Satellites fail when the investment committee is in Beijing — the same problem our [London note](/careers/intelligence/why-london-satellites-fail/) already names, inverted.
