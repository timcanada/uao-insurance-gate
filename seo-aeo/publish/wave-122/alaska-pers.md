# Alaska Public Employees’ Retirement System, explained

The Alaska Public Employees’ Retirement System is Alaska’s public-employee pension, healthcare, and defined-contribution book, not leftover Alaska Permanent Fund. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://drb.alaska.gov/docs/reports/PERSacfr_2025.pdf) prints combined fiduciary net position of **$26,772,591 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Total investments were **$26,499,544 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Defined-benefit pension fiduciary net position was **$12,286,031 thousand**. Defined-benefit pension fiduciary net position was **71.23 percent** of total pension liability. The defined-benefit pension return was **10.10 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Alaska Permanent Fund overwrite.** Do not mint `/pers-alaska/`, `/alaska-pers-explained/`, or `/alaska-public-employees/`. Do not treat leftover Alaska Permanent Fund **$91,346,500,000** at **30 June 2026**, or leftover Alaska **$89 billion**, as this June 2025 Public Employees’ Retirement System book.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Combined fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits, postemployment healthcare benefits, and individuals** **$26,772,591 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$24,835,102 thousand**), up **7.8 percent**. Total investments **$26,499,544 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$26,867,433 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat the combined **$26.8 billion** card as a pension-only fiduciary line. Those three prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover Alaska Permanent Fund **$91,346,500,000**, leftover Alaska **$89 billion**, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books on the same statement.** Defined-benefit pension **$12,286,031 thousand**. Defined-benefit pension investments **$12,114,359 thousand** is not defined-benefit pension fiduciary net position. Alaska Retiree Healthcare Trust **$9,891,510 thousand**. Defined-contribution retirement **$3,086,596 thousand**. Occupational death and disability **$98,971 thousand**. Retiree medical **$330,063 thousand**. Remaining column **$1,079,420 thousand**. Those lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat defined-benefit pension as the combined book.
3. **Returns and funded cards.** Defined-benefit pension return **10.10 percent**. Alaska Retiree Healthcare Trust return **10.12 percent**. Defined-benefit pension total pension liability **$17,248,347 thousand**. Defined-benefit pension net pension liability **$4,962,316 thousand**. Defined-benefit pension fiduciary net position / total pension liability **71.23 percent**. Alaska Retiree Healthcare Trust fiduciary net position / total OPEB liability **136.81 percent**. Those official one-year prints and funded cards are not interchangeable.

The Alaska Permanent Fund is a sovereign savings file. This is a retirement-system file. Those two legal persons are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Alaska PERS into [Alaska Permanent Fund](/alaska-permanent-fund-explained/), [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/), or [Nevada PERS](/nevada-pers/). The Permanent Fund is the savings book. This is **one Alaska public-employee retirement file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Alaska PERS AUM” has to name the book. The **$26,772,591 thousand** line is combined fiduciary net position. The **$12,286,031 thousand** line is defined-benefit pension fiduciary net position. The **$26,499,544 thousand** line is investments. Leftover Alaska Permanent Fund **$91,346,500,000** is a different legal person.
