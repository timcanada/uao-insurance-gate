# Wyoming Retirement System, explained

The Wyoming Retirement System is Wyoming’s combined statewide pension administrator, not leftover Minnesota SBI and not leftover North Dakota Legacy Fund. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://retirement.wyo.gov/wp-content/uploads/WRS-2025-Annual-Comprehensive-Financial-Report.pdf) prints defined-benefit net position restricted for pensions of **$13,188,110,017** at **31 December 2025**. The Public Employee Pension Plan was **$11,159,949,921**. The 457(b) defined-contribution plan was **$1,269,712,195**. Total pension trust plans were **$14,457,822,212**. Those four lines are not interchangeable. The combined defined-benefit portfolio return was **17.81 percent** net of fees. Money-weighted returns on each defined-benefit plan were also **17.81 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Minnesota SBI overwrite or a second North Dakota Legacy Fund page.** Do not mint `/wrs/`, `/wyoming-wrs/`, or `/wyoming-retirement-system-explained/`. Do not mint `/north-dakota-legacy/`.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Defined-benefit net position restricted for pensions** **$13,188,110,017** at **31 December 2025** (beginning **$11,556,111** thousand on the MD&A combined card; increase **$1,631,999** thousand). Total pension trust plans, including the 457(b), **$14,457,822,212**. Defined-benefit investment assets **$12,855,121,095**. The transmittal “**$13.19 billion**” and MD&A “**$13.2 billion**” lines are the same defined-benefit book, rounded. The “**$13.1 billion**” investment-assets line is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover Minnesota SBI, leftover North Dakota Legacy Fund, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover SWIB **$160 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books.** Public Employee Pension Plan **$11,159,949,921**. State Patrol, Game and Fish Warden and Criminal Investigator **$233,011,670**. Volunteer Firefighter, Emergency Medical Technician and Search and Rescue **$144,999,404**. Paid Fire A **$141,549,945**. Paid Fire B **$306,164,469**. Judicial **$56,944,212**. Law Enforcement **$1,132,131,255**. Air Guard Firefighter **$13,359,141**. The 457(b) Plan **$1,269,712,195** is not a defined-benefit book. Those lines are not interchangeable. The eight defined-benefit plans are commingled; each plan owns an equity position — do not add the eight books a second time.
3. **Returns.** Combined defined-benefit portfolio **17.81 percent** net of fees (benchmark **13.92 percent**). Money-weighted rate of return, net of expenses, **17.81 percent** on each of the eight defined-benefit plans. Those two official **17.81 percent** prints are the same year and the same commingled pool — do not invent a third return.

Do not flatten Wyoming Retirement System into [Minnesota SBI](/minnesota-sbi/) or [North Dakota Legacy Fund](/north-dakota-legacy-fund/). Minnesota SBI is the Minnesota investment-board file. North Dakota Legacy Fund is a state oil-tax savings file. This is **one Wyoming pension-administrator file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Wyoming retirement AUM” has to name the book. The **$13,188,110,017** line is defined-benefit fiduciary net position. The **$11,159,949,921** line is the Public Employee Pension Plan. The **$1,269,712,195** line is the 457(b). Those are not the same mandate.
