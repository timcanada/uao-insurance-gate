# Pittsburgh Firemen’s Relief and Pension Plan, explained

The Firemen’s Relief and Pension Plan of the City of Pittsburgh is Pittsburgh’s sworn firefighter pension, not leftover Pennsylvania PSERS and not leftover Pittsburgh Policemen’s Relief and Pension Plan. The official [31 December 2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.pittsburghpa.gov/files/assets/city/v/1/controller/documents/annual-comprehensive-financial-report/city-of-pittsburgh-acfr-final-2025-3.pdf) prints Act 205 allocated fiduciary net position of **$348,541,109** at **31 December 2025**. Total pension liability was **$600,959,716**. Fiduciary net position was **58.00 percent** of total pension liability. The residual Firemen’s Fund column of **$70,524** is not that allocated card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Pennsylvania PSERS overwrite or a leftover Comprehensive Trust overwrite.** Do not mint `/pittsburgh-fire-explained/` or `/pgh-fire/`. Residual Firemen’s Fund **$70,524** is not allocated fiduciary net position. Comprehensive Trust **$928,543,639** is not this book. Combined GASB **56.73 percent** is not the Firemen **58.00 percent** card. OPEB firefighter money-weighted **6.9 percent** is not the pension card.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The notes net-pension-liability table is the sourced split:

1. **Allocated fiduciary net position** **$348,541,109** at **31 December 2025**. The allocation uses the method provided in Act 205. Residual Firemen’s Fund net position **$70,524** is not the allocated card. Do not treat leftover Comprehensive Trust **$928,543,639**, leftover Municipal allocated **$321,585,507**, leftover Policemen allocated **$260,940,968**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$600,959,716**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **58.00 percent**. Net pension liability **$252,418,607**. Combined city **56.73 percent** is not this card.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **11.6 percent** net of investment expense for the year ended **31 December 2025** on the Plan investments held by the leftover [Comprehensive Municipal Pension Trust Fund](/pittsburgh-cmptf/). That shared print is not a Firemen-only time-weighted card. OPEB firefighter money-weighted **6.9 percent** is not this card. This filing does not print a separate official one-year time-weighted house card for Firemen.

Do not flatten the Firemen’s Relief and Pension Plan into [Pennsylvania PSERS](/pennsylvania-psers/), [Pennsylvania SERS](/pennsylvania-sers/), [Comprehensive Municipal Pension Trust Fund](/pittsburgh-cmptf/), [Pittsburgh Municipal Pension Plan](/pittsburgh-municipal/), or [Pittsburgh Policemen’s Relief and Pension Plan](/pittsburgh-police/). The Comprehensive Trust is the investment pool. Municipal and Police are different legal persons. This is **one Pittsburgh sworn-firefighter file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Pittsburgh fire pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$348,541,109** line is Act 205 allocated fiduciary net position. The **$70,524** line is residual Firemen’s Fund cash. The **58.00 percent** card is the Firemen GASB ratio. The **11.6 percent** card is the shared Plan money-weighted return. Those are not the same mandate.
