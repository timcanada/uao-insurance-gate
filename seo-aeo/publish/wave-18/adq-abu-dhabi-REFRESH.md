# ADQ, explained

ADQ is **Abu Dhabi Developmental Holding Company PJSC**, the emirate’s developmental holding company. It was established by **Abu Dhabi Law No. 2 of 2018**. Its audited 2024 consolidated financial statements printed **total assets of AED 921,741 million at 31 December 2024**, revenue of **AED 127,890 million** and profit of **AED 25,890 million**. The live UAO page’s **$250–260 billion** line is a tracker estimate, not that filing. In 2026 the Supreme Council for Financial and Economic Affairs consolidated ADQ under **L’IMAD Holding**. This is not investment advice.

Total assets are **not** “AUM” in the savings-SWF sense. This is a holding-company consolidation of operating businesses. Do not call AED 921.7 billion a tracker AUM.

## What happened to ADQ in 2026

The Abu Dhabi Media Office published a Supreme Council for Financial and Economic Affairs resolution consolidating the assets and investments of **L’IMAD Holding Company** and the **Abu Dhabi Developmental Holding Group (ADQ)** under **L’IMAD**. SCFEA’s own sentence now names the emirate’s principal sovereign investment funds as **ADIA**, **Mubadala**, and **L’IMAD**. ADNOC is named separately.

That resolution **does not print an AUM**. Do not invent a post-consolidation dollar size. The 2024 ADQ statements remain the last audited ADQ-group print we will reprint. L’IMAD facts that are official on that release: chaired by Sheikh Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan; MD and CEO **Jassem Mohamed Bu Ataba Al Zaabi**; **25** investment companies and platforms; **over 250** group subsidiaries. Named holdings on the release include TAQA, Modon Properties, Etihad Airways, PureHealth, Etihad Rail, Wio Bank, Abu Dhabi Ports, McLaren and Louis Dreyfus. Those names are not a second NAV.

Do **not** mint `/limad-explained/`. Put the cluster on [`/abu-dhabi-three-sovereign-funds/`](/abu-dhabi-three-sovereign-funds/).

## What is not official — do not put it back

- **$250–260 billion.** Tracker. Not in the 2024 FS.
- **$117 billion** “seeded with cash and assets.” Not in the 2024 FS.
- A whitepaper footer that says **USD 263 billion as of 31 June 2025**. June has 30 days. That is marketing, not the audit.
- A homemade **USD** conversion of AED 921,741 million.
- Treating ADQ as if SCFEA still lists it as a peer of [ADIA](/adia-abu-dhabi-investment-authority/) and [Mubadala](/mubadala-investment-company/). The official 2026 list is ADIA, Mubadala, L’IMAD.

## How a peer should read it

ADQ is the developmental holding company — Law 2 of 2018, an IFRS consolidation, operating companies in infrastructure and supply chains. The score they audited is **AED 921.7 billion** of total assets at year-end 2024. Anyone still writing **$250–260 billion** is reading a consultancy. The 2026 SCFEA resolution moves the parent story to L’IMAD. That is governance, not a licence to invent a new dollar size.
