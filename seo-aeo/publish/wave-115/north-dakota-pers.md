# North Dakota Public Employees Retirement System, explained

The North Dakota Public Employees Retirement System is North Dakota’s combined public-employee administrator, not leftover North Dakota Legacy Fund. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.ndpers.nd.gov/sites/www/files/documents/about/financial/acfr-2025.pdf) prints total fiduciary net position held in trust of **$5,471,128,911** at **30 June 2025**. The Public Employees Retirement System book was **$4,782,174,476**. Highway Patrolmen’s Retirement System was **$118,915,919**. Job Service was **$84,535,941**. Those four lines are not interchangeable. The PERS money-weighted return was **12.06 percent** net. PERS fiduciary net position was **74.3 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover North Dakota Legacy Fund overwrite or a second Minnesota SBI page.** Do not mint `/ndpers/`, `/north-dakota-pers-explained/`, or `/nd-pers/`. Do not mint `/north-dakota-legacy/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Total fiduciary net position held in trust** **$5,471,128,911** at **30 June 2025**. Restricted for pensions **$5,262,887,042**. Restricted for postemployment healthcare benefits **$207,627,644**. Restricted for pretax benefits **$614,225**. Those four lines are not interchangeable. The MD&A condensed card **$5,471,129 thousand** is the same combined fiduciary book, rounded. Condensed investments **$5,420,752 thousand** are not fiduciary net position. The Uniform Group Insurance proprietary fund **$61,105,509** is an enterprise book, not a pension trust. Do not treat leftover North Dakota Legacy Fund, leftover Minnesota SBI, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover SWIB **$160 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books.** Public Employees Retirement System **$4,782,174,476**. Highway Patrolmen’s Retirement System **$118,915,919**. Job Service **$84,535,941**. Retiree Health Insurance Credit **$207,627,644**. Defined Contribution **$31,485,601**. Deferred Compensation **$245,775,105**. Pretax Benefits **$614,225**. Those lines are not interchangeable. Change in fiduciary net position **$583,367 thousand**, including a **$65.0 million** lump-sum infusion into PERS.
3. **Returns and funded status.** Annual money-weighted returns, net of investment expense: PERS **12.06 percent**. Highway Patrol **12.13 percent**. Job Service **9.12 percent**. Retiree Health Insurance Credit **13.43 percent**. Those four return lines are not interchangeable. Fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension or OPEB liability: PERS **74.3 percent**. Highway Patrol **69.6 percent**. Job Service **127.5 percent**. Retiree Health Insurance Credit **76.0 percent**.

Do not flatten NDPERS into [North Dakota Legacy Fund](/north-dakota-legacy-fund/) or [Minnesota SBI](/minnesota-sbi/). North Dakota Legacy Fund is a state oil-tax savings file. This is **one North Dakota public-employee administrator file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “North Dakota PERS AUM” has to name the book. The **$5,471,128,911** line is the combined fiduciary book. The **$4,782,174,476** line is PERS. The proprietary insurance **$61,105,509** line is not a pension mandate.
