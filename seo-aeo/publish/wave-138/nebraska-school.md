# Nebraska School Employees Retirement Plan, explained

The Nebraska School Employees Retirement Plan is Nebraska’s statewide school pension, not leftover Omaha School Employees Retirement Plan and not leftover Iowa IPERS. The official [30 June 2025 Auditor of Public Accounts report](https://auditors.nebraska.gov/APA_Reports/2026/SA851-03022026-July_1_2024_through_June_30_2025_Audit_Report.pdf) prints fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits of **$18,532,685,257** at **30 June 2025**. Investments at fair value were **$18,767,145,729**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return was **11.65 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **107.17 percent** of total pension liability. That card is a net pension asset. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover IPERS overwrite or a leftover Omaha school overwrite.** Do not mint `/npers/`, `/nebraska-npers/`, `/nsers/`, or `/nebraska-school-explained/`. `/npers/` is already reserved as the leftover Nevada PERS twin. Investments **$18,767,145,729** are not fiduciary net position. Combined School-plus-Judges-plus-Patrol is not a printed AUM. Class V Omaha members sit in leftover [Omaha School Employees Retirement Plan](/omaha-osers/).

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The School Employees column on the statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits** **$18,532,685,257** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$16,940,205,465**). The change is **$1,592,479,792**. Total investments at fair value **$18,767,145,729** is not fiduciary net position. Securities-lending collateral **$1,042,864,286** is not fiduciary net position. Cash in State Treasury **$734,110** is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover Omaha School Employees calendar-year **$1,970,571,740**, leftover Iowa IPERS **$46,740,552,174**, leftover MOSERS **$9,565,800,614**, leftover Missouri PSRS and PEERS **$63,124,090,036**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$17,292,069,116**. Net pension asset **$1,240,616,141**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **107.17 percent** (2024 **103.34 percent**). That card is an asset, not a liability. Judges **110.23 percent** and State Patrol **88.42 percent** are not this card. Actuarial additional contribution **$0** as of **1 July 2025** is not the GASB card.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **11.65 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **13.48 percent**). Judges also print **11.65 percent**. State Patrol **11.79 percent** is not this card. This filing does not print a separate official one-year time-weighted house card.

Do not flatten the Nebraska School Employees Retirement Plan into [Omaha School Employees Retirement Plan](/omaha-osers/), [Iowa IPERS](/iowa-ipers/), [MOSERS](/missouri-mosers/), [Missouri PSRS and PEERS](/missouri-psrs-peers/), or [Minnesota TRA](/minnesota-tra/). OSERS is the Omaha Class V file. IPERS is Iowa. MOSERS is Missouri state employees. PSRS and PEERS are Missouri school. This is **one Nebraska statewide school file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Nebraska school AUM” has to name the book. The **$18,532,685,257** line is School fiduciary net position. The **$18,767,145,729** line is School investments. The **107.17 percent** card is the GASB ratio. The **11.65 percent** card is money-weighted. Those are not the same mandate.
