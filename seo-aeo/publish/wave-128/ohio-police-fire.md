# Ohio Police & Fire Pension Fund, explained

Ohio Police & Fire Pension Fund is Ohio’s statewide sworn police-and-fire pension and health-care book, not leftover Ohio OPERS, leftover Ohio SERS, leftover STRS Ohio, leftover Pennsylvania SERS, and not leftover Houston fire or Houston police. The official [2025 ACFR](https://www.op-f.org/media/bd4oegx4/opf_acfr_2025.pdf) prints fiduciary net position held in trust for pension and post-employment health care benefits of **$21,256,675,219** at **31 December 2025**. Pension net position was **$20,406,816,222**. Health-care net position was **$849,858,997**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. Statement total investments were **$18,940,904,815**. Money-weighted return was **18.450 percent** net. The house **18.52 percent** portfolio card is printed gross. Pension fiduciary net position was **71.95 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The combining statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced split:

1. **Fiduciary net position held in trust for pension and post-employment health care benefits** **$21,256,675,219** at **31 December 2025** (2024 **$18,720,595,109**). Pension **$20,406,816,222**. Health care **$849,858,997**. Statement total investments **$18,940,904,815** is not fiduciary net position. Cash and short-term investments **$2,298,815,315** is not fiduciary net position. The notes’ investments grand total **$21,241,044,719** is the transmittal **$21.24 billion** card and includes cash and cash equivalents; it is not the statement investments line. Those prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover OPERS **$158,377,460,244**, leftover Ohio SERS **$21,081,732,005**, leftover Ohio STRS **$80 billion+**, leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion**, leftover Houston fire, leftover Houston police, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$28,364,303,468**. Net pension liability **$7,957,487,246**. Pension fiduciary net position / total pension liability **71.95 percent**. That ratio uses the pension book **$20,406,816,222**, not the combined **$21,256,675,219** line. Do not treat OP&F’s proportionate share of leftover OPERS as this house funded card.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **18.450 percent** net of investment expense. Transmittal and MD&A portfolio return **18.52 percent**, printed **gross**. Those official one-year prints are not interchangeable. Do not relabel **18.52 percent** net.

Do not flatten Ohio Police & Fire into [Ohio OPERS](/ohio-opers/), [Ohio SERS](/ohio-sers/), [Houston Firefighters](/houston-hfrrf/), [Houston Police](/houston-hpops/), or [Dallas Police and Fire](/dallas-police-fire/). ## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “OP&F AUM” has to name the book. The **$21,256,675,219** line is combined fiduciary net position. The **$20,406,816,222** line is the pension book. The **$849,858,997** line is the health-care book. The **$18,940,904,815** line is statement investments. The **$21.24 billion** card includes cash. The **71.95 percent** card is the pension GASB ratio. The **18.450 percent** card is the money-weighted return. The **18.52 percent** card is the gross portfolio return. Those are not the same mandate.
