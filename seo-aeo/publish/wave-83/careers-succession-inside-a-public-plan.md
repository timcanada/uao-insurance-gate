# Succession inside a public plan — the file is public

Succession inside a public pension plan is a public process. The board will be asked who was on the long list, who was conflicted, and why the winner can sit in front of a legislature. This Careers Intelligence briefing sits beside Universal Asset Owners' [fiduciary-duty](/fiduciary-duty-explained-institutional/) and [pension-fund](/what-is-a-pension-fund/) pages. It is not compensation, legal, or campaign advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second institution keeper or a pay survey.** Do not invent a house AUM or a compensation range to decorate a succession story. Do not write a people page.

## The succession is a fiduciary file

A [pension fund](/what-is-a-pension-fund/) exists to pay beneficiaries. [Asset owners](/what-is-an-asset-owner/) hold capital for their own account. The [fiduciary duty](/fiduciary-duty-explained-institutional/) does not pause because a CIO is leaving.

Public plans such as [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/) and [CalSTRS](/calstrs-explained/) are useful as *process* peers — a board, a public file, a legislature — not as a licence to reprint leftover dollars on this page. Quote an official filing on the institution page. Do not invent a league table here.

Internal successors, external searches, and interim CIOs are different products. If the board cannot say which one it is running, the market will write the story.

## What to pressure-test before the announcement

1. **The brief.** Is this a continuity hire, a change of model, or a political hedge?
2. **The long list.** Who was considered, who was conflicted, and who will minute that?
3. **Decision rights on day one.** What may the incoming CIO decide without a new IPS?
4. **The deputy.** Is the number two the successor, the runner-up, or the person who leaves?
5. **The file.** What will you refuse to put in a press release?

## For boards and search firms

A public-plan search is a public process. If you cannot defend the long list, do not start it. Owners already use [Careers](/careers/) when they are hiring. Labelled recruitment products: [Careers](/careers/).
