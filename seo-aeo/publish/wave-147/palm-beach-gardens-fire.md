# City of Palm Beach Gardens Firefighters’ Pension Fund, explained

The City of Palm Beach Gardens Firefighters’ Pension Fund is Palm Beach Gardens’ fire pension, not leftover Palm Beach Gardens Police, leftover Pompano Beach, and not leftover Boca Police and Fire. The official [30 September 2025 and 2024 City of Palm Beach Gardens Firefighters’ Pension Fund financial statements](https://www.pbgfl.gov/DocumentCenter/View/3346) print fiduciary net position of **$178,150,007** at **30 September 2025**. Investments **$177,736,937** are not that card. DROP **$12,551,890** is a component of net position, not a second AUM. Money-weighted return was **9.41 percent** net of Fund investment expense. GASB **95.68 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Palm Beach Gardens Police overwrite.** Do not mint `/pbg-fire/`, `/palm-beach-gardens-fire-explained/`, or `/ers/`. The Firefighters’ valuation / investment-activity files are not this audit.

## What is official (30 September 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position, the GASB 67 note, and the money-weighted schedule are the sourced lines:

1. **Fiduciary net position** **$178,150,007** at **30 September 2025** (beginning **$163,977,708**). Investments **$177,736,937**. Total assets **$178,474,543**. Total liabilities **$324,536**. MD&A **9 percent** is the year-over-year change, not the money-weighted card. Do not treat leftover Palm Beach Gardens Police **$160,495,301**, leftover West Palm Beach Fire **$336,235,139**, leftover Boca Police and Fire **$613,033,507**, leftover Pompano Beach Police and Fire **$313,289,538**, leftover Florida SBA **$300 billion+**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$186,203,024**. Net pension liability **$8,053,017**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **95.68 percent** (2024 **91.98 percent**).
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **9.41 percent** net of Fund investment expense for the year ended **30 September 2025** (2024 **22.06 percent**).

Do not flatten the City of Palm Beach Gardens Firefighters’ Pension Fund into [Palm Beach Gardens Police Officers’ Pension Fund](/palm-beach-gardens-police/), [West Palm Beach Firefighters’ Pension Fund](/west-palm-beach-fire/), [City of Boynton Beach Municipal Firefighters Pension Trust Fund](/boynton-beach-fire/), [City of Boca Raton Police and Firefighters’ Retirement System](/boca-raton-police-fire/), or [Pompano Beach Police and Firefighters’ Retirement System](/pompano-beach-police-fire/). Palm Beach Gardens Police is the police file. West Palm Beach Fire is a West Palm Beach firefighter file. Boynton Beach Fire is a Boynton Beach firefighter file. Boca Police and Fire is a Boca Raton file. Pompano Beach is a Pompano Beach sworn file. This is **one Palm Beach Gardens fire file**.
