# District Attorneys’ Retirement System, explained

District Attorneys’ Retirement System is Louisiana’s district-attorney pension, not leftover Louisiana TRSL, leftover LASERS, and not leftover Louisiana MPERS. The official [30 June 2025 and 2024 District Attorneys’ Retirement System financial statements](https://app.lla.state.la.us/PublicReports.nsf/0/D0D715194F871FEC86258D7E00745556/$FILE/0000989A.pdf) print fiduciary net position of **$624,823,773** at **30 June 2025**. Investments **$616,413,600** are not that card. Money-weighted return was **9.17 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. MD&A **9.15 percent** is the market card. GASB **96.47 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover TRSL overwrite.** Do not mint `/dars/`, `/louisiana-dars-explained/`, or `/mers/`. `/mers/` remains Michigan MERS.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statements of fiduciary net position, the GASB 67 schedule, and the money-weighted note are the sourced lines:

1. **Fiduciary net position** **$624,823,773** at **30 June 2025** (beginning **$578,738,448**). Total investments **$616,413,600**. MD&A cash and investments **$619,099,576** is not the investments card. Do not treat leftover Louisiana TRSL **$29,334,227,630**, leftover LASERS **$17,223,211,406**, leftover Louisiana MPERS, leftover Louisiana FRS, leftover LSERS **$2,411,550,113**, leftover Clerks of Court **$906,589,423**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$647,665,950**. Net pension liability **$22,842,177**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **96.47 percent**.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **9.17 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **9.84 percent**). MD&A market **9.15 percent** is not the notes card.

Do not flatten District Attorneys’ Retirement System into [Louisiana TRSL](/louisiana-trsl/), [LASERS](/louisiana-lasers/), [Municipal Police Employees’ Retirement System](/louisiana-mpers/), [Louisiana Firefighters’ Retirement System](/louisiana-frs/), [Parochial Employees’ Retirement System of Louisiana](/louisiana-parochial/), or [Louisiana School Employees’ Retirement System](/louisiana-lsers/). TRSL is the teacher file. LASERS is the state-employee file. MPERS and FRS are sworn statewide files. Parochial is the parish-employee file. LSERS is the school-support file. This is **one Louisiana district-attorney file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Louisiana DARS AUM” has to name the book. The **$624,823,773** line is fiduciary net position at **30 June 2025**. The **$616,413,600** line is investments. The **9.17 percent** card is money-weighted. The **9.15 percent** card is MD&A market. The **96.47 percent** card is GASB. Those are not the same mandate as TRSL or LASERS.
