# General Assembly Retirement System of Illinois, explained

The General Assembly Retirement System of Illinois is Illinois’s statewide General Assembly pension, not leftover Illinois SERS and not leftover South Carolina GARS. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://ilsrs.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/srs/gars/resources/financial-report/gars-2025annual.pdf) prints fiduciary net position restricted for pensions of **$97,435,115** at **30 June 2025**. Investments held in the Illinois State Board of Investment commingled fund were **$91,832,255**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The Illinois State Board of Investment commingled-fund return was **9.7 percent**. The System’s money-weighted return on that commingled book was **9.9 percent**. Fiduciary net position was **26.44 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Illinois SERS overwrite or a leftover South Carolina GARS overwrite.** Do not mint `/gars/`, `/illinois-gars-explained/`, or `/general-assembly-retirement-system/`. Do not mint `/sers/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Fiduciary net position restricted for pensions** **$97,435,115** at **30 June 2025** (previously presented beginning **$89,972,620**; restated beginning after GASB 101 **$89,972,434**). Investments held in the Illinois State Board of Investment commingled fund **$91,832,255** is not fiduciary net position. Total investments including securities-lending collateral **$92,863,255** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$98,580,623** is not fiduciary net position. Those four lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover Illinois SERS, leftover Illinois JRS, leftover Illinois TRS, leftover Illinois State Board of Investment as a second AUM, leftover South Carolina GARS, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$368,536,710**. Net pension liability **$271,101,595**. Fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **26.44 percent**. The MD&A funded-status card **26.4 percent** is the same ratio rounded. Those cards are not a second investment book. The Illinois State Board of Investment commingled fund is the investment vehicle, not a second General Assembly Retirement System AUM.
3. **Returns.** Illinois State Board of Investment commingled-fund overall rate of return **9.7 percent**. System money-weighted return on its investment in that commingled fund **9.9 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. The house highlights card prints that **9.9 percent** as “Investment Return.” Those two official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Illinois GARS into [Illinois SERS](/illinois-sers/), [Illinois JRS](/illinois-jrs/), [Illinois TRS](/illinois-trs/), [SURS](/illinois-surs/), [Illinois State Board of Investment](/illinois-isbi/), or [South Carolina Retirement Systems](/south-carolina-retirement-systems/). ISBI manages this book. Illinois SERS is the statewide state-employee file. JRS is the judges’ file. South Carolina GARS is a different legal person. This is **one Illinois General Assembly file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Illinois GARS AUM” has to name the book. The **$97,435,115** line is fiduciary net position restricted for pensions. The **$91,832,255** line is the ISBI commingled investment. Those are not the same mandate.
