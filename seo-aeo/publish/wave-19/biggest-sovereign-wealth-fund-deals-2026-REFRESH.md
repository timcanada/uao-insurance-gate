## How a peer should read it

A year-stamped deals URL is a trap. If the transactions are not on an official release, the page should say so and get out of the way of the institution keepers. [GIC](/gic-singapore-explained/) will not give you a denominator. [PIF](/public-investment-fund-pif/) will give you **more than US$900 billion**, not a consultancy **$925 billion**.
