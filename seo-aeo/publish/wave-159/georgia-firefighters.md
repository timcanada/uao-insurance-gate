# Georgia Firefighters’ Pension Fund, explained

The Georgia Firefighters’ Pension Fund is Georgia’s statewide supplemental firefighter pension, not leftover Georgia ERS, leftover Georgia TRS, leftover Atlanta Firefighters, leftover Illinois FPIF. The official [30 June 2025 Georgia Firefighters’ Pension Fund financial statements](https://gfpf.org/wp-content/uploads/FY2025-AUDITED-FINANCIAL-STATEMENTS.pdf) print net position restricted for pensions of **$1,396,760,413** at **30 June 2025**. Investments **$1,316,532,705** are not that card. Total pension liability **$1,897,062,210** is not AUM. The actuarial funded ratio **86.7 percent** is not the GASB **73.63 percent** card.
