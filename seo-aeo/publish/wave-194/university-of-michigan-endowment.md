# University of Michigan endowment, explained

The University of Michigan endowment is Michigan’s long-horizon book, not leftover Michigan SERS (**$15,122,140 thousand**), leftover MPSERS (**$94,365,768 thousand**), leftover Harvard **$56.9 billion**. The official [2025 consolidated financial report](https://finance.umich.edu/sites/default/files/2025-10/2025%20Consolidated%20UM%20Financial%20Report.pdf) prints endowment investments of **$21,201 million** at **30 June 2025**. The official [2025 University Endowment Fund Profile](https://publicaffairs.vpcomm.umich.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/19/2025/12/2025-Endowment-Profile.pdf) prints University Endowment Fund **$21.2 billion** and a University Long Term Portfolio return of **15.5 percent** net of fees. Those two value prints are the same card, rounded. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Michigan SERS overwrite or a leftover Harvard overwrite.** Do not mint `/university-of-michigan-endowment-explained/` or `/umich-endowment/`. **Never mint `/michigan/`.** PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP, Detroit, issued an unmodified opinion dated **16 October 2025**. The financial report does not print a one-year percent — the Profile is that card.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The financial report prints endowment investments in **millions**.

1. **University Endowment Fund** **$21.2 billion** at **30 June 2025**. Financial-report endowment investments **$21,201 million** is the named FS line for the same date. Those two official prints are the same card, rounded. Do not treat leftover Michigan SERS **$15,122,140 thousand**, leftover MPSERS **$94,365,768 thousand**, leftover Michigan State Police **$2,221,134 thousand**, leftover Harvard **$56.9 billion**, leftover Notre Dame Endowment Pool **$25,434,616 thousand**, leftover Penn **$24,808,028 thousand** as this filing.
2. **Named books are not interchangeable.** The University Endowment Fund is invested in the University Long Term Portfolio. The **15.5 percent** card is the LTP one-year net-of-fees return to **30 June 2025**. Five-year **13.7 percent**. Ten-year **9.9 percent**. Twenty-year **9.2 percent**. Do not invent a percent from the financial report alone.
3. **Payout.** Profile distribution rate **4.5 percent** of a trailing 28-quarter average, with a **5.3 percent** cap of current market value. Distribution **$0.21** per share at **30 June 2025**. Financial-report spending-rate distributions **$538 million**. Funds-functioning-as-endowment withdrawals **$454 million** are not the spending-rate card. PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP, 500 Woodward Ave, Detroit, **16 October 2025**.

Do not flatten Michigan into leftover [Michigan State Employees’ Retirement System](/michigan-sers/), leftover [Michigan Public School Employees’ Retirement System](/michigan-mpsers/), leftover [Notre Dame endowment](/notre-dame-endowment/), leftover [University of Pennsylvania endowment](/university-of-pennsylvania-endowment/), leftover [MITIMCo](/mit-investment-management-company/), or leftover [largest university endowments](/largest-university-endowments-2026/). Michigan SERS is a statewide pension file. This is **one University of Michigan endowment file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Michigan AUM” has to name the book. The **$21.2 billion** line is the University Endowment Fund at **30 June 2025**. The **15.5 percent** card is the Long Term Portfolio. The **$538 million** line is spending-rate distributions. Those are not leftover Michigan SERS **$15,122,140 thousand**.
