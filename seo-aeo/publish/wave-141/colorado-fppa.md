# Fire and Police Pension Association of Colorado, explained

The Fire and Police Pension Association of Colorado is Colorado’s statewide sworn pension administrator, not leftover Colorado PERA and not leftover Denver Employees. The official [31 December 2025 FPPA Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.fppaco.org/files/718667f51/2025+Annual+Report+-+Final.pdf) prints combined fiduciary net position of **$9,337,353,230** at **31 December 2025**. Members’ Benefit Investment Fund **$8,268,822,401** and Self-Directed Investment Fund **$1,068,530,829** are not interchangeable. Notes money-weighted return for the Long-Term Pool was **13.36 percent** net of investment expense. Transmittal time-weighted **13.34 percent** is not that card. Each plan must be separately funded. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Colorado PERA overwrite.** Do not mint `/fppa/`, `/colorado-fppa-explained/`, or `/fppaco/`. Do not invent a system-wide funded ratio.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The combining statements, the notes return print, and the January actuarial table are the sourced lines:

1. **Combined fiduciary net position** **$9,337,353,230** at **31 December 2025** (beginning **$8,288,409,280**). Combined investments **$9,023,243,529**. Members’ Benefit **$8,268,822,401** is not the Association. Self-Directed **$1,068,530,829** is not the Association. Statewide Retirement Plan combining **$5,460,097,708** is not the Association. Affiliated Local **$1,378,396,518**, Statewide Death and Disability **$723,191,543**, Colorado Springs New Hire **$706,756,023**, and Staff Health Subsidy **$380,609** are not this book. Do not treat leftover Colorado PERA Combined **$82,953,131 thousand**, leftover Denver Employees, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **Funded cards.** The house report says each plan must be judged separately. January 1 2025 actuarial value funded Statewide Retirement Plan **100.0 percent**, Statewide Death and Disability **91.4 percent**, Colorado Springs Police **88.6 percent**, and Colorado Springs Fire **84.5 percent** are not 31 December GASB cards. Statewide Retirement Plan GASB schedule fiduciary net position **$5,430,552,742** / net pension liability **$0** is not the Association and is not the combining **$5,460,097,708** line. This page does not invent a system-wide funded ratio.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return for the Long-Term Pool **13.36 percent**, Glide-Path Pool **8.20 percent**, and Short-Term Pool **8.91 percent** net of investment expense for 2025. Required supplementary Long-Term Pool **13.4 percent** is the rounded card. Transmittal time-weighted **13.34 percent** net of fees versus the **13.94 percent** policy benchmark is not the money-weighted card.

Do not flatten FPPA into [Colorado PERA](/colorado-pera/) or [Denver Employees](/denver-employees/). PERA is the Colorado statewide civilian-and-educator file. Denver Employees is a city file. This is **one Colorado sworn administrator file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Colorado fire and police AUM” has to name the book. The **$9,337,353,230** line is combined fiduciary net position. The **$8,268,822,401** line is the Benefit Investment Fund. The **$5,460,097,708** line is Statewide Retirement Plan combining. The **13.36 percent** card is Long-Term Pool money-weighted. The **13.34 percent** card is time-weighted. The January **100.0 percent** card is actuarial. Those are not the same mandate.
