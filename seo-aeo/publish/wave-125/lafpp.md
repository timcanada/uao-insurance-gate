# Los Angeles Fire and Police Pensions, explained

Los Angeles Fire and Police Pensions is the City of Los Angeles sworn fire-and-police pension and health-subsidy book, not leftover Los Angeles City Employees’ Retirement System and not leftover LACERA. The official [FY2025 audit](https://content.lafpp.lacity.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/FY2025LAFPPAuditReport.pdf) prints combined net position in trust of **$34,110,601,276** at **30 June 2025**. Pension net position was **$30,495,210,727**. Health-subsidy net position was **$3,615,390,549**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. Combined investments at fair value were **$34,674,736,764**. Pension money-weighted return was **11.26 percent**, printed **gross of investment expense**. Pension fiduciary net position was **104.74 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover LACERS overwrite, a leftover LACERA overwrite, or a leftover San José Police and Fire overwrite.** Do not mint `/la-fire-police/` or `/lafpp-explained/`. Do not treat the house **$30.50 billion** or **$3.62 billion** cards as the combined book. The condensed MD&A print **$30,495,211 thousand** is the same pension book as **$30,495,210,727**, not a second AUM.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Combined net position in trust** **$34,110,601,276** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$31,395,293,926**). Pension **$30,495,210,727**. Health subsidy **$3,615,390,549** (401(h) **$3,007,100,022** plus 115 Trust **$608,290,527**). Combined investments at fair value **$34,674,736,764** is not fiduciary net position. Pension investments **$31,011,328,365** is not pension fiduciary net position. Those prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover LACERS combined **$25,375,319 thousand**, leftover San José Police and Fire combined **$5,966,077 thousand**, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$29,116,456,371**. Pension fiduciary net position / total pension liability **104.74 percent**. Those two lines are not interchangeable with the combined book.
3. **Returns.** Pension money-weighted return **11.26 percent**, printed **gross of investment expense** (2024 **9.94 percent**). Do not relabel that print net. The house statements do not give this page a separate official time-weighted card to quote as the money-weighted substitute.

Do not flatten LAFPP into [LACERS](/lacers/), [LACERA](/lacera/), [San José Police and Fire](/san-jose-police-fire/), [Houston Firefighters](/houston-hfrrf/), [Houston Police](/houston-hpops/), [San Antonio Fire and Police](/san-antonio-fire-police/), [Jacksonville Police and Fire](/jacksonville-police-fire/), or [City of Fresno Fire and Police Retirement System](/fresno-fire-police/). This is **one Los Angeles sworn fire-and-police file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “LAFPP AUM” has to name the book. The **$34,110,601,276** line is combined net position. The **$30,495,210,727** line is the pension book. The **$3,615,390,549** line is the health-subsidy book. The **$34,674,736,764** line is combined investments. House **$30.50 billion** is the pension book rounded.
