# New York City Employees’ Retirement System, explained

The New York City Employees’ Retirement System is New York City’s municipal-employee pension, not leftover New York City Teachers and not the Comptroller’s five-system combined card. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://cms.nycers.org/app/uploads/2026/05/2025-ACFR-.pdf) prints combined net position restricted for benefits of **$94,295,072 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. The Qualified Pension Plan book was **$93,142,662 thousand**. The Correction Officers’ Variable Supplements Fund book was **$1,152,410 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. Total portfolio time-weighted return was **10.05 percent** net of management fees. The Qualified Pension Plan money-weighted return was **10.04 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combining statements of fiduciary net position are the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Combined net position restricted for benefits** **$94,295,072 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$87,931,142 thousand**). Qualified Pension Plan **$93,142,662 thousand**. Correction Officers’ Variable Supplements Fund **$1,152,410 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. Combined investments at fair value **$93,407,316 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Combined total assets **$106,863,237 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. The other four Variable Supplements Funds print a zero fiduciary net position on the combining statement. Do not treat leftover New York City Teachers, leftover New York State Common **$226.8 billion**, or the Comptroller’s FY2026 five-system **$326.3 billion** card as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards are not the combining-statement book.** Combined fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **87.66 percent** (the introductory card **87.7 percent** is that same print, rounded). Qualified Pension Plan **87.75 percent**. Combined total pension liability **$107,639,967 thousand**. Combined GASB fiduciary net position **$94,355,676 thousand** is not the combining-statement **$94,295,072 thousand**. Those four lines are not interchangeable. The actuarial-value funded card **82.2 percent** is as of **30 June 2023** — do not treat it as the 2025 GASB card.
3. **Returns.** Total portfolio time-weighted return **10.05 percent** net of management fees, against the house policy benchmark **11.66 percent**. Qualified Pension Plan money-weighted return **10.04 percent**. Correction Officers’ Variable Supplements Fund money-weighted return **8.50 percent**. Those three official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten New York City Employees into [New York City Teachers](/nyc-teachers/) or [New York City Retirement Systems](/new-york-city-crs/). The Comptroller’s FY2026 **$326.3 billion** card is a five-system combined print at **30 June 2026**. This page is the house **30 June 2025** ACFR. Those vintages are not interchangeable.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “NYCERS AUM” has to name the book. The **$94,295,072 thousand** line is the combined QPP and Variable Supplements book. The **$93,142,662 thousand** line is the Qualified Pension Plan. The **$93,407,316 thousand** line is combined investments. Those are not the same mandate.
