# Ford Foundation, explained

The Ford Foundation is a US private foundation, not a university endowment and not a second Gates or Wellcome page. The official [2025 audited financial statements](https://www.fordfoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-Audited-Financial-Statements-and-Footnotes.pdf) print investments at fair value of **$17,156,492 thousand** (**$17.2 billion**) and net assets without donor restrictions of **$16,101,005 thousand** (**$16.1 billion**) at **31 December 2025**. Grants approved were **$773,443 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second endowment-versus-foundation overwrite or a second Gates Trust overwrite.** Those keepers are `/endowment-vs-foundation-investing/` and `/gates-foundation-trust/`. Do not mint `/ford-foundation-explained/`. Do not reprint leftover Ford **$16.0 billion as of December 2022**.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The statements are in thousands. The 2025 filing is the sourced split:

1. **Investments, at fair value** **$17,156,492 thousand** at **31 December 2025** (2024: **$16,403,395 thousand**). **Total assets** **$18,578,712 thousand**. **Total net assets without donor restrictions** **$16,101,005 thousand** (2024: **$14,899,613 thousand**). Do not add those lines. Do not treat the leftover **$16.0 billion (December 2022)** line as this filing.
2. **Grants approved** **$773,443 thousand** (2024: **$770,674 thousand**). Total program activities **$881,001 thousand**. Change in net assets **$1,201,392 thousand**.
3. **Bonds payable** **$1,265,945 thousand** (net of unamortized issuance costs). That liability sits on the same statement as the investment line. It is not a second AUM.

Do not flatten Ford into [Wellcome](/wellcome-trust/) or [the Gates Foundation Trust](/gates-foundation-trust/). Wellcome is one UK charity with one portfolio. Gates is two US legal persons. This is **one US private-foundation file**.
