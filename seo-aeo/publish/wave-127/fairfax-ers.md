# Fairfax County Employees’ Retirement System, explained

Fairfax County Employees’ Retirement System is Fairfax County’s civilian employee pension, not leftover Virginia Retirement System, leftover Fairfax PORS, leftover Fairfax URS, leftover Maryland SRPS, leftover Pennsylvania SERS, and not leftover PSERS. The official [FY2025 ACFR](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/retirement/sites/retirement/files/Assets/ERS%20ACFR%20-%202025.pdf) prints net position restricted for pension benefits of **$5,387,233,149** at **30 June 2025**. Total investments were **$5,508,336,167**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. RSI money-weighted return was **6.34 percent**. Transmittal time-weighted return was **8.62 percent** net of fees. Fiduciary net position was **69.65 percent** of total pension liability. Actuarial funded **71.35 percent** is not the GASB card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

3. **Returns.** Required-supplementary and MD&A money-weighted return **6.34 percent** for fiscal 2025 (2024 **8.35 percent**). The notes’ “year ended June 30, 2025 … 8.35 percent” line matches the RSI **2024** card, not a second 2025 money-weighted print. Transmittal / investment-section time-weighted return **8.62 percent** net of fees. MD&A time-weighted print **8.88 percent** (from **9.08 percent**). Those official one-year prints are not interchangeable.
