# Relocating to Santiago for a Chile ESSF or Hacienda sovereign-fund desk

Santiago is the official-address city of Chile’s [Ministry of Finance](https://www.hacienda.cl/consultas-web/contacto-hacienda), which publishes the [Economic and Social Stabilization Fund](/chile-economic-and-social-stabilization-fund-explained/). Official Oficina de Partes: **Teatinos 120, 1° Piso, Santiago**. The ESSF was established **6 March 2007**. It is a fiscal stabilization reserve, **not** a globally diversified savings SWF, and **not** a pension. Chile’s Pension Reserve Fund is a **different** book. A move here is often a move onto that public-finance window. Right to work sits with official Chilean sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit elsewhere

- [ESSF](/chile-economic-and-social-stabilization-fund-explained/) sits under Hacienda’s international-finance stationery. Official prints live on the institution keeper. Do not invent an AUM on this page. Do not reprint leftover **$10.8 billion**. Do not mint `/essf-explained/` or `/santiago-essf/`.
- The Pension Reserve Fund is a **different** Hacienda book. Do not mash the two into one “Chile SWF” desk.
- Do not 301 this city page onto leftover `/chile-economic-and-social-stabilization-fund/` or `/chile-stabilization-fund-essf/`. Paste the Wave 11 keeper first.
- [Santiago Principles](/santiago-principles/) are a governance code named for a 2008 meeting. They are not Chile’s street address.

Do not treat this city page as a second ESSF explainer.

## Papers

## Why people underestimate the job

A stabilization reserve that can pay out is not a savings SWF with a growing AUM target. Spanish is the language of legitimacy even when the dealing language is English. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail.
