# Teachers' Retirement System of the State of Illinois, explained

The Teachers’ Retirement System of the State of Illinois is Illinois’s teacher pension, not Texas TRS and not ISBI. The official [FY2025 financial audit](https://www.auditor.illinois.gov/Audit-Reports/Compliance-Agency-List/Retirement-Systems/TRS/FY25-TRS-Fin-Full.pdf) prints Defined Benefit fiduciary net position of **$77,263,331,616** at **30 June 2025**. Combined fiduciary net position, after a **$5,268,783** other-book deficit, is **$77,258,062,833**. Those two dollar lines are not interchangeable. The investment portfolio earned **9.7 percent** net of fees. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second Texas TRS overwrite or a second ISBI overwrite.** Those keepers are `/texas-teacher-retirement-system-explained/` and `/illinois-isbi/`. Do not mint `/trs-illinois/`, `/illinois-trs-explained/`, or `/trsil/`. Do not mint `/texas-trs/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combining statement is the sourced split (dollars, not thousands):

1. **Defined Benefit fiduciary net position** **$77,263,331,616** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$71,424,713,925**). Combined fiduciary net position **$77,258,062,833**. Do not treat leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, leftover Oregon **$95 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books on the same statement.** Net investment income **$6,902,120,417**. Fiduciary net position increased **$5,837,731,788**. Investments at fair value **$76,590,555,598**. Total pension liability **$162,014,881,717**. Net pension liability **$84,751,550,101**. Fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **47.7 percent**. The funding-valuation actuarial accrued liability is **$159.1 billion**; that funded ratio is **47.8 percent**. Those GASB and funding lines are not interchangeable.
3. **Returns and benefits.** Portfolio return **9.7 percent** net of fees, **270** basis points above the **7.0 percent** assumed rate. Defined benefits and refunds **$8.6 billion**. Benefit payments **$8.5 billion** to **134,395** recipients. ISBI does not manage this book.

Do not flatten Illinois TRS into [Texas TRS](/texas-teacher-retirement-system-explained/), [ISBI](/illinois-isbi/), [SURS](/illinois-surs/), or [Illinois SERS](/illinois-sers/). Texas TRS is Texas. ISBI manages Illinois SERS, GARS, and JRS — not TRS. SURS is the public-university file. This is **one Illinois teacher file**.
