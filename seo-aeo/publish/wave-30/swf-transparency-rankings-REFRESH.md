- **What is disclosed.** Some houses publish holdings, votes, and a dated fund value. [GPFG](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/) is the exhibit. Some publish a return series and refuse AUM ([GIC](/gic-singapore-explained/), [ADIA](/adia-abu-dhabi-investment-authority/)).
- **What is not a SWF.** GPIF is a **pension**. The ranking twin files it at **$1.64 trillion** as if it were a SWF. Official GPIF is **yen** on the Wave 7 keeper — do not overwrite that URL here.
