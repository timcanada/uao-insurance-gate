# Cincinnati Retirement System, explained

The Cincinnati Retirement System is Cincinnati’s civilian pension, not leftover Ohio OPERS and not leftover Ohio Police and Fire. The official [30 June 2025 City of Cincinnati Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/retirement/assets/City_of_Cincinnati_2025_Hamilton_FINAL.pdf) prints pension fiduciary net position of **$1,800,352 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Combined pension-and-OPEB net position was **$2,399,709 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return on pension-plan investments was **10.37 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **67.20 percent** of total pension liability. Website **$2.520 billion** at **31 December 2025** is not this card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Ohio OPERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/cincinnati-crs/`, `/crs/`, or `/cincinnati-retirement-explained/`. The city ACFR is the house disclosure; the footnote says no separate CRS financial report is issued. Statement prints are in thousands. Combined investments **$2,394,528 thousand** mix pension and OPEB. This desk does not invent a pension-only investments total.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

Footnote 21 and the fiduciary statements are the sourced lines:

1. **Pension fiduciary net position** **$1,800,352 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$1,740,734 thousand**). Combined pension-and-OPEB net position **$2,399,709 thousand** is not the pension card. OPEB healthcare **$599,357 thousand** is not pension AUM. Combined investments **$2,394,528 thousand** are not pension fiduciary net position. Securities-lending collateral **$25,177 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover Ohio OPERS **$158,377,460,244**, leftover Ohio SERS **$21,081,732,005**, leftover Ohio Police and Fire **$21,256,675,219**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$2,679,041 thousand**. Net pension liability **$878,689 thousand**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **67.20 percent**. MD&A actuarial funded **68.3 percent** as of **31 December 2024** is not the GASB card. House-site **68.10 percent** at **31 December 2025** is not this card.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **10.37 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025**. House-site **14.20 percent** at **31 December 2025** is not that card. This filing does not print a separate official one-year time-weighted house card.

Do not flatten the Cincinnati Retirement System into [Ohio OPERS](/ohio-opers/), [Ohio SERS](/ohio-sers/), or [Ohio Police & Fire](/ohio-police-fire/). OPERS and Ohio SERS are statewide files. Ohio Police and Fire is the statewide sworn file. Later Cincinnati police and fire sit in leftover Ohio Police and Fire, not in this book. This is **one Cincinnati civilian city file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Cincinnati pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$1,800,352 thousand** line is pension fiduciary net position. The **$2,399,709 thousand** line is combined pension-and-OPEB. The **$599,357 thousand** line is OPEB. The **67.20 percent** card is the June GASB ratio. The **68.3 percent** card is the December 2024 actuarial ratio. The **10.37 percent** card is money-weighted. Those are not the same mandate.
