# The Universal Owner podcast — a show for the people who allocate the longest capital

The Universal Owner is Universal Asset Owners' podcast for sovereign wealth funds, public pensions, insurers, endowments and multi-generational family offices. It ships in two forms: a short daily audio of the morning brief, and a longer interview with the people who run or serve those books. It is journalism, not a tips show. Sponsors are labelled. Listen on Apple Podcasts, Spotify, or from universalassetowners.com.

## Why a podcast is part of the media company

CIOs do not only read. They walk between meetings. A ten-minute desk and a long interview are how UAO shows up in a second hour of the day — and how Apple and Spotify become search engines for this beat.

The afternoon sister show is [The Probability Desk](/listen-probability-desk/). Same rules: sourced, no retail hot take.

## What we make

| Format | Length | Job |

| Daily audio brief | ~8–12 minutes | Habit; same story as the written morning desk |
| Long-form interview | longer | Named CIOs, policymakers, managers, data people |
