# Relocating to Montréal for PSP or La Caisse

Montréal is the principal business-office city of PSP Investments and the home city of La Caisse (CDPQ). It is not Toronto, and it is not Ottawa. A move here is often a move into a Québec public-pension file, a federal public-sector mandate, and a bilingual labour market. Immigration is federal. Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Which owners sit here

- [PSP Investments](https://www.investpsp.com/en/) — Public Sector Pension Investment Board. Official headquarters is **Ottawa**; the **principal business office is Montréal**; it also lists New York, London and Hong Kong. Do not tell a candidate “Toronto” if the desk is here. Do not mint `/psp-investments/`. The keeper is [`/psp-investments-canada-explained/`](/psp-investments-canada-explained/).
- [La Caisse](https://www.lacaisse.com/en/) — Caisse de dépôt et placement du Québec, now trading as La Caisse. A public-pension and insurance asset manager for Québec depositors, not a sovereign wealth fund. The keeper is [`/cdpq-explained/`](/cdpq-explained/). Do not mint `/cdpq-canada-explained/` or `/la-caisse-explained/`.

[CPP Investments](/cpp-investments-explained/) and [Ontario Teachers'](/ontario-teachers-pension-plan-explained/) sit in [Toronto](/careers/cities/toronto/). AIMCo is Alberta-centred. Do not flatten Canada into one city.

Do not invent a house AUM on this page. Official prints live on the institution keepers and the houses’ own sites.

## Why the move has prestige — and friction

Canadian plans pay for people who can sit on both sides of a co-invest. French in the file is not a lifestyle extra. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail. Cross-border hires should start at [Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship.html), not at a dinner conversation about a visa class that may not apply to them.
