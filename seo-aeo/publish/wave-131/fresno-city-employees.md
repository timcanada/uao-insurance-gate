# City of Fresno Employees Retirement System, explained

City of Fresno Employees Retirement System is Fresno’s civilian city pension, not leftover Fresno County Employees’ Retirement Association and not leftover City of Fresno Fire and Police Retirement System. The official [FY2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.cfrs-ca.org/Resources/31949580-4bb9-4eec-b93f-e7dced41a236/FY%202025%20ACFR.pdf) prints net position restricted for pension benefits of **$1,974,211,816** at **30 June 2025**. Statement investments at fair value were **$1,972,055,839**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Time-weighted return was **11.08 percent** gross and **10.93 percent** net. Money-weighted return was **10.92 percent** net of investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **112.90 percent** of total pension liability. Actuarial funded **114.0 percent** is not the GASB card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

The combined City of Fresno Retirement Systems portfolio table **$4,317,906,747** is not this book.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position restricted for pension benefits** **$1,974,211,816** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$1,815,104,966**). The change is **$159,106,850**. Statement investments at fair value **$1,972,055,839** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$2,005,889,178** is not fiduciary net position. DROP reserves **$174,810 thousand** are not fiduciary net position. The combined CFRS custodian table **$4,317,906,747** is both city books, not this System. Do not treat leftover FCERA **$7,380,434 thousand**, leftover Fresno Fire and Police **$2,395,605,595**, leftover Sacramento City Employees **$253,354 thousand**, leftover Sacramento County **$14,577,459 thousand**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$1,748,714 thousand**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **112.90 percent** (2024 **108.37 percent**). Actuarial funded **114.0 percent** is not the GASB card. Fair-value funded **118.6 percent** is not the GASB card.
3. **Returns.** Custodian time-weighted return **11.08 percent** gross and **10.93 percent** net for the year ended **30 June 2025**. The investment letter also rounds the net print to **10.9 percent**. Notes money-weighted return **10.92 percent** net of investment expense. Those official one-year prints are not interchangeable.
