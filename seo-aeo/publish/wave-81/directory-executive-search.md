# Executive search for sovereign funds, pensions and family offices

Asset owners hire search firms for roles a board or a family cannot fill from a LinkedIn blast: CIO, deputy, head of private markets, general counsel, and the first professional hire in a family office. This hub is Universal Asset Owners' map of that category. It sits beside [Careers](/careers/), not inside the newsroom. It is not a ranking and not a placement guarantee.

A listing here is not an endorsement. Featured placements are marked paid.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## What owners are actually buying

A public pension search is a public process. The board will ask who was on the long list, who was conflicted, and why the winner can sit in front of a legislature.

A sovereign or strategic-fund search is often quieter. The client still needs a firm that knows the difference between a savings mandate and a development mandate.

A family-office search is a confidentiality problem first. The principal will not tolerate a leak. The “firm that placed the last megafund CIO” is sometimes the wrong firm for a $2 billion office.

If you sell into this room, say which of those searches you have closed. If you buy, ask for the last three mandates *like yours*, redacted, and who did the work.

## The questions a board or principal should ask

1. Who is the working partner, and will they sit in the interviews?
2. How do you search people who are not looking — without making the role a rumour?
3. Where is the conflict when you also place people at the managers we hire?
4. What will you refuse to put in the candidate pack?
