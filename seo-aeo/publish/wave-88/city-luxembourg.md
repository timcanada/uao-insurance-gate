# Relocating to Luxembourg for an owner satellite or FDC desk

Luxembourg is an AIMCo office city, not AIMCo’s head office. [Alberta Investment Management Corporation](/aimco-alberta-investment-management-corporation/) lists [11, Rue Aldringen (Le Conti), L-1118](https://www.aimco.ca/contact). The **head office is Edmonton**. The local public-pension reserve is the [Fonds de compensation](https://fdc.public.lu/en/support/contact.html) commun au régime général de pension, at **34-40, avenue de la Porte-Neuve, L-2227**. A move here is often a move onto a European vehicle or a Luxembourg statutory-pension file — or into a window whose investment committee sits elsewhere. Right to work sits with official Luxembourg sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit elsewhere

- [AIMCo](/aimco-alberta-investment-management-corporation/) — a Crown corporation that invests for Alberta public clients. Official offices: Edmonton (head office), Calgary, Toronto, London, Luxembourg. Ask whether the investment committee sits in Edmonton. If it does, you are not at the centre. Do not invent an AIMCo AUM on this page. Do not mint `/aimco-explained/` or `/luxembourg-aimco/`.
- FDC is the compensation fund for Luxembourg’s general pension scheme. It is **not** a classic sovereign wealth fund. Official prints live on fdc.public.lu. Do not invent an AUM on this page. Do not mint `/fdc-explained/` or `/fdc-luxembourg/`.
- [Calgary](/careers/cities/calgary/) is the Alberta markets satellite. [London](/careers/cities/london/) is the other AIMCo European office.

A fund-domicile postcode is not automatically the investment book.
