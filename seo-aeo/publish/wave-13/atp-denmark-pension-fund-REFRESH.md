# ATP (Denmark), explained

ATP Livslang Pension is Denmark’s **statutory lifelong supplementary pension**, not a sovereign wealth fund. ATP’s 2025 results printed members’ net assets of **DKK 694 billion** at year-end — **DKK 538 billion** of guaranteed pension obligations, a **DKK 116.5 billion** bonus potential, and a **DKK 39.5 billion** long-term supplementary provision — for **5.8 million** members. The house does **not** publish a euro AUM. This is not investment advice.

**Last updated:** 26 August 2026 · **Desk:** UAO Research · **Canonical URL:** `/atp-denmark-pension-fund/`

The live page this overwrites still says ATP manages **€85 billion** for **5.6 million** members from a **2023** annual report, then invents a **50 / 35 / 15** equity/bond/illiquid split. The euro line is a homemade conversion. Official 2025 is **DKK 694 billion** and **5.8 million** members, of whom about **1 million** are already pensioners. There is no live short twin to 301.

Do not convert DKK 694 billion into a homemade euro or dollar AUM. Do not call group **total assets DKK 887.331 billion** (the 2025 accounts’ balance-sheet total) “ATP AUM” — that line includes repos, derivatives, and other group items. The pension print ATP wants read is **DKK 694 billion** of members’ net assets.

CEO named on the English results page: **Martin Præstegaard**.

## What it is

ATP is a **collective, guaranteed lifelong pension** on top of the Danish state pension. Contributions are mandatory for most of the labour market. The investment portfolio exists to support **bonus capacity** — the room to raise the guaranteed pension — while a separate hedging/bond book is meant to make the guarantee payable “regardless of how the investment markets take shape.”

That is [LDI](/liability-driven-investing-explained/) logic, not a [total portfolio approach](/total-portfolio-approach/) equity/bond reference mix, and not a SWF.

ATP Group also administers public welfare payments. The English results page: **DKK 360 billion** a year in welfare and social-security benefits to more than **3 million** citizens, on behalf of the state, municipalities, and the social partners — “two out of three Danish kroner paid in welfare benefits.” That is an **administration mandate**, not a second pension NAV. Do not add 360 to 694.

Compare it with [USS](/uss-universities-superannuation-scheme/) or other guaranteed-income books, not with [NBIM](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/).

## How a peer should read it

- **€85 billion is dead.** If a memo still uses it, the memo is not reading the 2025 results.
- **DKK 694 billion is members’ net assets, not “AUM.”** Guarantees + bonus + supplementary provision.
- **19.5% is the investment portfolio versus the bonus potential**, not a total-fund return on 694.
- **5.8 million, not 5.6.** About one million already drawing.
- **Do not put a 50/35/15 pie back on this URL.** That split was not on the 2025 results page.
