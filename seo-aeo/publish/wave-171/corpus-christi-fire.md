# Corpus Christi Firefighters’ Retirement System, explained

The Corpus Christi Firefighters’ Retirement System is the City of Corpus Christi firefighter pension, not leftover TMRS, leftover Texas ERS, leftover Houston Fire, leftover Texas TESRS, and not leftover MassPRIM. The official [31 December 2025 and 2024 financial statements](https://www.ccfirepension.com/CCFULF/CCFDCS/CCFFRS%20Financial%20Statements%20December%2031,%202025%20and%202024.pdf) print net fiduciary position held in trust for pension benefits of **$218,380,053** at **31 December 2025**. Investments **$214,132,839** are not that card. Notes money-weighted return was **12.06 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. GASB **63.8 percent** is the notes funded card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The statements of fiduciary net position, the GASB note, and the money-weighted print are the sourced lines:

Investments at fair value **$214,132,839**. Cash and short-term investments **$3,160,248** plus investments **$214,132,839** total **$217,293,087** and are not FNP. Total assets **$218,651,299**. Total liabilities **$271,246**. Do not treat leftover TMRS **$49,405,467,577**, leftover Texas ERS **$43,165,849,614**, leftover Houston Fire **$5,799,107,368**, leftover Texas TESRS **$152,259,760**, leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion**, leftover Illinois IPOPIF **$13,743,292,730**, or leftover Illinois FPIF **$10,286,871,117** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Notes total pension liability **$342,074,772**. City’s net pension liability **$123,694,719**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **63.8 percent**. Do not treat **$342,074,772** as AUM. Discount rate **7 percent**. Investment rate of return **7.00 percent**.
3. **Returns and cash flow.** Notes money-weighted return **12.06 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **31 December 2025** (2024 **7.48 percent**). City contributions **$12,597,094**. Firefighter contributions **$6,703,242**. Total contributions **$19,300,336**. Net investment income **$23,496,177**. Benefits paid **$18,400,511**. Refund of contributions **$273,431**. Administrative expenses **$406,521**. Total deductions **$19,080,463**. GF Valdez, P.C. issued an unmodified opinion dated **18 June 2026**.
