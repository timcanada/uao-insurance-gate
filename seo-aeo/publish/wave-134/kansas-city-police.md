# Police Retirement System of Kansas City, explained

The Police Retirement System of Kansas City is Kansas City’s sworn police pension, not leftover MOSERS and not leftover Civilian Employees’ Retirement System of the Police Department of Kansas City. The official [30 April 2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://kcpers.org/media/f0zb2yev/policeannreport25_fnl.pdf) prints net position restricted for pensions of **$1,028,622,529** at **30 April 2025**. Statement investments were **$1,025,919,327**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Time-weighted return was **6.8 percent**. Money-weighted return was **6.89 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **63.87 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover MOSERS overwrite or a leftover St. Louis ERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/kcpers/` or `/kansas-city-police-explained/`. Statement investments **$1,025,919,327** are not fiduciary net position. Securities-lending collateral **$98,460,542** is not fiduciary net position. Time-weighted **6.8 percent** is not money-weighted **6.89 percent**. Year-end is **30 April**, not 30 June. Do not invent a combined Kansas City Police plus civilian AUM.

## What is official (30 April 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position restricted for pensions** **$1,028,622,529** at **30 April 2025** (2024 **$987,787,161**). The change is **$40,835,368**. Statement investments **$1,025,919,327** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$1,130,500,951** include securities-lending collateral and are not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover MOSERS **$9,565,800,614**, leftover St. Louis ERS **$937,329,534**, leftover St. Louis Firemen **$446,256,821**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion**, leftover City of Knoxville Pension System **$758,195,199**, or leftover Civilian Employees’ Retirement System of the Police Department of Kansas City **$183,870,569** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$1,610,590,464**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **63.87 percent**. Net pension liability **$581,967,935**. Those official prints are not interchangeable with a later civilian card.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **6.89 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 April 2025**. Investment-section time-weighted total-fund **6.8 percent** is not the money-weighted card. The letter’s dollar-weighted **6.89 percent** net of fees is the money-weighted print, not a second method.

Do not flatten the Police Retirement System of Kansas City into [MOSERS](/missouri-mosers/), [St. Louis ERS](/st-louis-ers/), [Firemen’s Retirement System of St. Louis](/st-louis-firemen/), or [Civilian Employees’ Retirement System of the Police Department of Kansas City](/kansas-city-police-civilian/). The civilian book is a different legal person on the same house site. This is **one Kansas City sworn-police file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Kansas City police AUM” has to name the book. The **$1,028,622,529** line is net position restricted for pensions. The **$1,025,919,327** line is statement investments. The **63.87 percent** card is the GASB ratio. The **6.8 percent** card is time-weighted. The **6.89 percent** card is money-weighted. Those are not the same mandate.
