# Maharlika Investment Corporation, explained

The Maharlika Investment Corporation is the Philippines’ statutory sovereign-wealth manager, not a leftover **$10 billion** AUM and not a second Southeast Asia pile. [Republic Act No. 11954](https://mic.gov.ph/publications) created the corporation and the Maharlika Investment Fund in **July 2023** and set authorized capital stock at **PHP 500 billion**. That authorized-capital line is not current assets. MIC’s official [2024 annual-report release](https://mic.gov.ph/news/maharlika-investment-corporation-mic-releases-2024-annual-report) prints **PHP 2.68 billion** in total comprehensive income and **PHP 1.45 billion** remitted to the National Treasury. Print pesos. Do not dollarize. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (RA 11954 and MIC 2024)

MIC’s own [transparency page](https://mic.gov.ph/transparency) says the corporation manages the Maharlika Investment Fund under Republic Act No. 11954. The statute and MIC’s own 2024 release are the sourced split:

1. **Authorized capital stock** **PHP 500 billion**. That is a statutory ceiling, not a current AUM. Do not dollarize it into a homemade rank.
2. **2024 total comprehensive income** **PHP 2.68 billion**, which MIC files as income from strategic low-risk placements during institutional build-up. The 2024 annual report includes audited financial statements for fiscal **2023–2024**.
3. **Dividends remitted to the National Treasury** **PHP 1.45 billion**, declared after that 2024 result and remitted in early **2025**.

This wake does **not** print an unofficial 2025 asset total. Unaudited quarterly or annual disclosures are not a substitute for an audited year-end AUM.

Do not flatten MIC into [Khazanah](/is-khazanah-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/) or [Danantara](/danantara-indonesia-sovereign-fund/). Those are different statutes and different house currencies.
