# Relocating to Mexico City for a Fondo Mexicano del Petróleo or Banxico fiduciary desk

Mexico City is the fiduciary city of the [Fondo Mexicano del Petróleo](https://www.fmped.org.mx/). Banco de México is the fiduciary; the Secretaría de Hacienda y Crédito Público is the fideicomitente. The fund was created by the **20 December 2013** energy constitutional reform, the law was published **11 August 2014**, and operations began **1 January 2015**. Banxico’s official [transparency window](https://www.fmped.org.mx/transparencia.html) for the fideicomiso is **Avenida 5 de Mayo 18, Colonia Centro, 06000**. It is a petroleum-revenue fideicomiso, **not** a savings SWF hiring floor. Right to work sits with official Mexican sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit elsewhere

## Why people underestimate the job

A Banxico fiduciary window is not a CIO office with a product shelf. Spanish is the language of legitimacy even when the dealing language is English. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail.
