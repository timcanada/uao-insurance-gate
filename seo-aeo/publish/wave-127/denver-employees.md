# Denver Employees Retirement Plan, explained

Denver Employees Retirement Plan is the City and County of Denver civilian employee pension and health-benefits book, not leftover Colorado PERA, leftover Phoenix COPERS, leftover Arizona PSPRS, leftover ASRS, and not leftover Denver Water. The official [2025 ACFR](https://derp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2025-DERP-ACFR.pdf) prints combined fiduciary net position restricted for benefits of **$2,994,725,181** at **31 December 2025**. Pension was **$2,905,858,839**. Health benefits were **$88,866,342**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. Investments at fair value were **$2,899,360,490**. Money-weighted return was **12.73 percent** net of fees. Pension fiduciary net position was **64.86 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Colorado PERA overwrite or a leftover Phoenix COPERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/derp/` or `/derp-explained/`. DROP and DROP II accounts **$44,488,545** are not the health-benefits book. Denver Water employees are covered by a separate retirement system.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Combined fiduciary net position restricted for benefits** **$2,994,725,181** at **31 December 2025** (2024 **$2,705,240,609**). The change is **$289,484,572**, or **10.7 percent**. Pension **$2,905,858,839**. Health benefits **$88,866,342**. Investments at fair value **$2,899,360,490** is not fiduciary net position. Investment-section “cash and investments” **$2,991,381,222** is not fiduciary net position. Those prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover Colorado PERA, leftover Phoenix COPERS **$3,913,124 thousand**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$4,480,234,341**. GASB pension fiduciary net position **$2,905,858,838**. Net pension liability **$1,574,375,503**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **64.86 percent**. The GASB pension fiduciary line is not the combined book **$2,994,725,181** and is one dollar off the combining pension column **$2,905,858,839**.
3. **Returns.** Money-weighted return **12.73 percent** net of fees. House net investment return / Total Portfolio **12.7 percent**. Those two official one-year prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat the rounded **12.7 percent** card as a second money-weighted print.

Do not flatten Denver Employees into [Colorado PERA](/colorado-pera/), leftover [Employees’ Retirement Plan of the Denver Board of Water Commissioners](/denver-water-pension/), leftover [Metro Water Recovery Defined Benefit Retirement Plan](/metro-water-recovery/), or [Phoenix COPERS](/phoenix-copers/). Colorado PERA is the statewide file. Denver Water and Metro Water Recovery are different Colorado utility files. Phoenix COPERS is a different legal person. This is **one Denver civilian-employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “DERP AUM” has to name the book. The **$2,994,725,181** line is combined fiduciary net position. The **$2,905,858,839** line is the pension column. The **$88,866,342** line is health benefits. The **$2,899,360,490** line is investments. The **$44,488,545** line is DROP / DROP II. Those are not the same mandate.
