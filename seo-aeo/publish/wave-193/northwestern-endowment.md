# Northwestern endowment, explained

The Northwestern University endowment is Northwestern’s long-horizon book, not leftover Harvard **$56.9 billion**, leftover Notre Dame Endowment Pool **$25,434,616 thousand**, leftover Columbia **$15,917,490 thousand**. The official [2025 financial report](https://www.northwestern.edu/financial-operations/annual-financial-reports/2025-financial-report.pdf) prints Long-Term Balanced Pool value of **$15,263 million** at **31 August 2025**. GAAP endowment net assets **$11,686,235 thousand** are not that card. The official [2025 endowment report](https://www.northwestern.edu/investment/reports/io_investmentreport2025_030526.pdf) prints an endowment return of **11.3 percent** net of external management fees for the twelve months ended **30 June 2025**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
