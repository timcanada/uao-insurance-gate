# SWFI vs a newsroom

SWFI and a newsroom sell different files. [SWFI’s own homepage](https://www.swfinstitute.org/) calls it a **professional subscription-based investor research platform**: profiles, deals, mandates, asset-allocation data, and membership events. A newsroom reports, attributes, and refuses to sell the conclusion. Universal Asset Owners is the second kind of room. This page is a disambiguation, not a ranking and not a data-vendor review.

**Do not treat this page as a second SWF-list explainer.** Do not mint `/swfi-explained/` or `/swfi-vs-uao/`. Do not invent a SWFI AUM total or a “largest funds” pile from a tracker.

## Two products

A research platform’s job is a login: executives, strategy notes, RFPs, and a membership list. SWFI’s homepage says that in those words. That file is useful. It is not a correspondent.

A newsroom’s job is a piece a newspaper can lift. [Ethics](/ethics/) (version 1.0, **28 May 2026**) is the test: we do not pay sources; we do not promise a particular conclusion in exchange for access; quoting AI output as source material is a firing offence. [Press](/press-desk/) is a different inbox from [Advertise](/advertise/).

If you need a dataset, buy a dataset. If you need a quote, write Press. If you mash those two into one URL, you will mis-brief the CIO and the CMO in the same sentence.

## Where Universal Asset Owners sits

We write the owner’s side. We also run labelled advertising. Those are separate. SWFI citing a ranking is **SWFI citing a tracker**. It is not a UAO ranking and not an official house AUM. Do not paste a tracker total into a lede.
