# Parochial Employees’ Retirement System of Louisiana, explained

Parochial Employees’ Retirement System of Louisiana is Louisiana’s parish-employee pension, not leftover Louisiana TRSL, leftover LASERS, leftover Municipal Police Employees’ Retirement System, and not leftover Louisiana School Employees’ Retirement System. The official [31 December 2025 audited financial statements](https://img1.wsimg.com/blobby/go/07f1d526-18a0-4f8f-a2f1-4bc942f3edad/PERS%20Audited%20financial%20statements%2012-31-2025%20F.pdf) print combined fiduciary net position of **$6,321,950,751** at **31 December 2025**. Plan A **$5,775,509,463** and Plan B **$546,441,288** are not interchangeable. Plan A money-weighted return was **13.34 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Plan B **13.87 percent** is not that card. The house report does not print one combined GASB funded ratio. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The combining statements, the Plan A and Plan B GASB 67 notes, and the money-weighted prints are the sourced lines:

1. **Combined fiduciary net position** **$6,321,950,751** at **31 December 2025** (beginning **$5,691,866,696**). Combined investments **$6,155,577,551**. Cash and investments **$6,295,507,945** is not fiduciary net position. Plan A fiduciary net position **$5,775,509,463** is not the System. Plan B **$546,441,288** is not the System. Do not treat leftover Louisiana TRSL **$29,334,227,630**, leftover LASERS **$17,223,211,406**, leftover Municipal Police **$3,133,488,618**, leftover Louisiana School Employees **$2,411,550,113**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Plan A total pension liability **$5,314,897,933** / net pension asset **$460,611,530** / **108.67 percent**. Plan B total pension liability **$492,474,505** / net pension asset **$53,966,783** / **110.96 percent**. This page does not invent a combined GASB funded ratio.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return for Plan A **13.34 percent** and Plan B **13.87 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **31 December 2025** (both plans 2024 **11.56 percent**). This page does not invent a combined money-weighted return.

Do not flatten Parochial Employees’ Retirement System into [Louisiana TRSL](/louisiana-trsl/), [LASERS](/louisiana-lasers/), [Municipal Police Employees’ Retirement System](/louisiana-mpers/), or [Louisiana School Employees’ Retirement System](/louisiana-lsers/). TRSL is the Louisiana educator file. LASERS is the Louisiana state-employee file. Municipal Police is the municipal-police file. School Employees is the school-support file. This is **one Louisiana parish-employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Louisiana parochial AUM” has to name the book. The **$6,321,950,751** line is combined fiduciary net position. The **$5,775,509,463** line is Plan A. The **$546,441,288** line is Plan B. The **$6,155,577,551** line is investments. The **13.34 percent** card is Plan A money-weighted. The **13.87 percent** card is Plan B money-weighted. The **108.67 percent** card is Plan A GASB. The **110.96 percent** card is Plan B GASB. Those are not the same mandate.
