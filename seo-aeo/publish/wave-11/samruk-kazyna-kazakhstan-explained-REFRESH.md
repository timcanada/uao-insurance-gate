# Samruk-Kazyna (Kazakhstan), explained

Samruk-Kazyna is Kazakhstan’s state holding company for strategic enterprises, not a globally diversified savings SWF. The Fund’s 2025 sustainability report prints assets under management of **KZT 44.2 trillion at the end of 2025** (KZT 41.1 trillion in 2024). KASE published PwC-audited 2025 consolidated statements on 9 June 2026. Do not invent a US-dollar AUM. This is not investment advice.

**Last updated:** 26 August 2026 · **Desk:** UAO Research · **Canonical URL:** `/samruk-kazyna-kazakhstan-explained/`

The live page this overwrites dollarizes the holding company at **roughly $88 billion at the end of 2025**. That is a tracker conversion of an operating-company balance sheet, not a liquid SWF NAV. The short twin `/samruk-kazyna-kazakhstan/` is also 200 — 301 that URL here after this overwrite, not before.

Samruk’s own 31 March 2026 interim CFS says amounts are rounded to the nearest **million tenge**. KASE labelled the same magnitude “thous. tenge.” The **44.2 trillion** sustainability print matches the KASE **44,242,913** line if that table is in millions. Use **KZT 44.2 trillion** as the Fund’s own words. Do not invent a USD headline from either table.

This is a **consolidated holding-company balance sheet** — oil, uranium, rail, power, telecoms — not GIC’s liquid book. Adding “$88 billion AUM” to a SWF league table is how the live page went wrong.

## What it is

Joint-stock company **Sovereign Wealth Fund Samruk-Kazyna**. Created by Presidential Decree No. **668 of 13 October 2008** and Government Resolution No. **962 of 17 October 2008**, by merging the Samruk holding and the Kazyna development fund. The Republic of Kazakhstan is the shareholder.

The job is to **own, run, and (over time) reform** the strategic enterprises at the centre of the Kazakh economy — and to recycle some of them toward private capital. That is closer to [Temasek](/is-temasek-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/) or [ICD](/investment-corporation-of-dubai-icd-explained/) than to [ADIA](/adia-abu-dhabi-investment-authority/).

Do not repeat the live “96% inside Kazakhstan” line unless Samruk prints it on a page you can cite. The holding-company nature is enough.

State subsidies in 2025, on the sustainability page: **KZT 64 billion**, 11% higher than 2024. That is the Fund’s own disclosure that this is not a pure commercial savings vehicle.

## How a peer should read it

- **Tenge first.** KZT 44.2 trillion of *consolidated assets*, not an $88bn savings AUM.
- **Holding company, not a portfolio manager.** The “AUM” is KazMunayGas, Kazatomprom, KTZ and the rest of the SOE stack on one IFRS balance sheet.
- **Net profit fell in tenge.** 3,213,711 vs 3,308,762 on the KASE table. Do not write a boom year the auditor did not print.
- **Two live URLs is a farm.** Keep this explained slug.
