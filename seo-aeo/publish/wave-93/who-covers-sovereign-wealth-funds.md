# Who covers sovereign wealth funds?

Sovereign wealth funds are covered by a small stack of trade papers, a data platform, a membership body, and a handful of owner-side newsrooms. [IPE](https://www.ipe.com/company-overview) is a pan-European institutional publisher established in **1997**. [Pensions & Investments](https://www.pionline.com/2025/06/25/about-pensions-investments/) calls itself the international newspaper of money management and dates its founding to **1973**. [SWFI](https://www.swfinstitute.org/) is a subscription research platform. Universal Asset Owners is an independent newsroom with a daily brief and a [podcast](/podcast/). This page is a map, not a ranking.

**Do not treat this page as a second “what CIOs read” twin.** That draft is `/publications-chief-investment-officers-read/`. Do not mint `/what-publications-do-cios-read/`. Do not invent a circulation or a “#1 SWF media” rank.

## The rooms that already exist

| Room | What it is (their words) | What it is not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IPE | Pan-European publisher for pension funds and their service providers; established **1997**; part of FD Business / FD Mediagroep | A savings-SWF AUM ledger |
| Pensions & Investments | International newspaper of money management; founded **1973**; owned by Crain Communications Inc. | A family-office society magazine |
| SWFI | “Professional subscription-based investor research platform” (homepage) | A newsroom with a firewall page |
| [IFSWF](https://www.ifswf.org/) | Membership body for funds that adopt the Santiago Principles | A daily |
| Universal Asset Owners | Independent newsroom; daily brief, Probability Desk, [podcast](/podcast-universal-owner/) | A data vendor |

A [sovereign wealth fund](/sovereign-wealth-fund/) is an owner. Coverage that treats it as a ticker is the wrong room.

## Where a quote should land

If you need a number a newspaper can lift, use the [press desk](/press-desk/) and [how newsrooms quote this publication](/how-to-get-quoted-as-an-asset-owner-publication/). If you need a dataset, SWFI sells a subscription. If you need a membership file, IFSWF is the Santiago room. Do not mash those four jobs into one URL.
