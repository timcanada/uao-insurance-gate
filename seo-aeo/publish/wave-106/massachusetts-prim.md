# Massachusetts PRIM Board, explained

The Massachusetts Pension Reserves Investment Management Board is the investment office for the Pension Reserves Investment Trust Fund, not a second CalPERS page and not leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**. The official [PRIT Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.mapension.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/PRIT-Annual-Comprehensive-Financial-Report-06302025.pdf) prints pooled net position of **$115,458,493 thousand** (**$115.5 billion**) at **30 June 2025**. The PRIT Fund returned **9.62 percent** net of fees. Net position increased **$10.2 billion**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statements are in thousands. The FY2025 ACFR is the sourced split:

1. **Pooled net position** **$115,458,493 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$105,253,934 thousand**). The change in pooled net position was **$10,204,559 thousand**. Do not treat leftover **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **Return.** The PRIT Fund returned **9.62 percent** net of fees in fiscal year 2025 (2024: **9.46 percent**). Net investment income **$10,199,405 thousand**. Official PRIM: all seven major asset classes posted positive returns for the first time in six years.
3. **Flows.** Contributions **$4,177,647 thousand**. Redemptions **$4,141,434 thousand**. Investments at fair value **$121,695,725 thousand**. That investment line sits above net position because liabilities sit on the same statement. It is not a second AUM.

Do not flatten PRIM into [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/), [WSIB](/washington-state-investment-board/), [Employees’ Retirement System of Rhode Island](/rhode-island-ersri/), or [Employees’ Retirement System of the City of Providence](/providence-employees/). CalPERS is one California PERF file. WSIB is a 40-fund Washington office. ERSRI is the Rhode Island statewide file. Providence is a Rhode Island city file. This is **one Massachusetts PRIT file**.
