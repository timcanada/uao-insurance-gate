# Louisiana School Employees’ Retirement System, explained

Louisiana School Employees’ Retirement System is Louisiana’s school-support pension, not leftover Louisiana TRSL, leftover LASERS, and not leftover Parochial Employees’ Retirement System. The official [30 June 2025 School Employees’ Retirement System audited financial statements](https://lsers.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/School-Employees-Retirement-System-06-30-2025-Final-Audit.pdf) print fiduciary net position of **$2,411,550,113** at **30 June 2025**. Investments **$2,368,176,435** are not that card. Fair-value investments **$2,317,891,118** are not the total-investments card. Money-weighted return was **7.32 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. GASB **85.20 percent** is not leftover TRSL. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover TRSL overwrite.** Do not mint `/lsers/`, `/louisiana-lsers-explained/`, or `/louisiana-school-employees/`. The June 30 2025 valuation is not this audit.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statements of fiduciary net position, the GASB 67 note, and the money-weighted schedule are the sourced lines:

1. **Fiduciary net position** **$2,411,550,113** at **30 June 2025** (beginning **$2,319,966,685**). Total investments **$2,368,176,435** (fair value **$2,317,891,118**; contract-value synthetic guaranteed investment contracts **$50,285,317**). Do not treat leftover Louisiana TRSL **$29,334,227,630**, leftover LASERS **$17,223,211,406**, leftover Parochial Employees combined **$6,321,950,751**, leftover Municipal Police **$3,133,488,618**, leftover Clerks of Court **$906,589,423**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$2,830,433,598**. Employers’ net pension liability **$418,883,485**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **85.20 percent**. This page does not treat leftover TRSL as this card.
3. **Returns.** Notes and required supplementary money-weighted return **7.32 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **8.05 percent**). The June 30 2025 valuation is not the audit and is not this card.

Do not flatten Louisiana School Employees’ Retirement System into [Louisiana TRSL](/louisiana-trsl/), [LASERS](/louisiana-lasers/), or [Parochial Employees’ Retirement System of Louisiana](/louisiana-parochial/). TRSL is the Louisiana educator file. LASERS is the Louisiana state-employee file. Parochial Employees is the parish-employee file. This is **one Louisiana school-support file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Louisiana school employees AUM” has to name the book. The **$2,411,550,113** line is fiduciary net position. The **$2,368,176,435** line is total investments. The **$2,317,891,118** line is fair-value investments. The **7.32 percent** card is money-weighted. The **85.20 percent** card is GASB. Those are not the same mandate.
