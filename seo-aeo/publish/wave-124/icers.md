# Imperial County Employees’ Retirement System, explained

The Imperial County Employees’ Retirement System is Imperial County’s 1937-Act employee pension, not leftover LACERA and not leftover SBCERA. The official [2025 audited financial statements](https://icers.imperialcounty.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/ICERS-Audit-Financial-Statements-6-30-2025.pdf) print plan net position held in trust for pension benefits of **$1,308,837,826** at **30 June 2025**. Investments at fair value were **$1,299,274,635**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The money-weighted return was **7.8 percent** net of investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **95.50 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

The house filing is the audited financial statements, not a full ACFR. Do not invent a time-weighted return. Do not treat leftover P&I **10.4 percent** as an official house print.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position held in trust for pension benefits** **$1,308,837,826** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$1,197,046,152**), up **$111,791,674**. Investments at fair value **$1,299,274,635** is not fiduciary net position. Those two prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover LACERA, leftover SBCERA, leftover SJCERA, leftover OCERS, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$1,370,521,375**. Net pension liability **$61,683,549**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **95.50 percent**. Those three lines are not interchangeable.
3. **Returns and actuarial card.** Money-weighted return **7.8 percent** net of investment expense (2024 **6.1 percent**). Actuarial funded **93.1 percent** (actuarial value of assets / actuarial accrued liability as of the **30 June 2025** valuation) is not the GASB **95.50 percent** card. The house statements do not print a time-weighted return. Do not invent one.

SBCERA is the San Bernardino **County** file. SJCERA is the San Joaquin **County** file. This is **one Imperial County employee file**.
