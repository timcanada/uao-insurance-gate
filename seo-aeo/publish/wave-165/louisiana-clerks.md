# Louisiana Clerks of Court Retirement and Relief Fund, explained

The Louisiana Clerks of Court Retirement and Relief Fund is Louisiana’s clerks-of-court pension, not leftover Municipal Police Employees’ Retirement System, leftover Firefighters’ Retirement System, leftover Louisiana School Employees’ Retirement System, and not leftover Parochial Employees’ Retirement System. The official [30 June 2025 audited financial statements](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/67c88b6e73c988dbe94e6d36/6939b8196ccd041df91e6441_LCCRRF%202025%20AUDITED%20FINANCIAL%20STATEMENTS.pdf) print fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits of **$906,589,423** at **30 June 2025**. Total investments **$903,133,415** are not that card. Notes money-weighted return was **13.34 percent** net of investment expense. GASB **90.69 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Municipal Police overwrite.** Do not mint `/lccrrf/`, `/clerks/`, or `/mers/`. `/mers/` remains Michigan MERS. Parochial Plan A also prints **13.34 percent** — that is a coincidence, not this book.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statements of fiduciary net position, the GASB note, and the money-weighted schedule are the sourced lines:

1. **Fiduciary net position** **$906,589,423** at **30 June 2025**. Beginning **$817,807,571**. Increase **$88,781,852**. Total investments **$903,133,415** (fair value **$879,732,295**; contract-value synthetic guaranteed investment contracts **$23,401,120**). Do not treat the fair-value-only line as total investments. Do not treat leftover Municipal Police **$3,133,488,618**, leftover Firefighters’ Retirement System **$2,808,100,816**, leftover Louisiana School Employees **$2,411,550,113**, leftover Parochial Employees combined **$6,321,950,751**, leftover District Attorneys **$624,823,773**, leftover Louisiana TRSL **$29,334,227,630**, leftover LASERS **$17,223,211,406**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$999,614,342**. Employers’ net pension liability **$93,024,919**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **90.69 percent** (2024 **83.09 percent**). Do not treat **$999,614,342** as AUM.
3. **Returns and cash flow.** Notes and required supplementary money-weighted return **13.34 percent** net of investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **12.56 percent**). Member contributions **$8,365,382**. Employer contributions **$25,130,753**. Ad valorem taxes **$14,587,624**. Revenue sharing **$320,553**. Net investment income **$100,477,258**. Annuity benefits **$51,922,846**. DROP benefits **$7,104,909**. The independent auditor’s report is dated **25 November 2025**, Metairie, Louisiana.

Do not flatten the Louisiana Clerks of Court Retirement and Relief Fund into [Municipal Police Employees’ Retirement System](/louisiana-mpers/), [Louisiana Firefighters’ Retirement System](/louisiana-frs/), [Louisiana School Employees’ Retirement System](/louisiana-lsers/), [Parochial Employees’ Retirement System of Louisiana](/louisiana-parochial/), or [District Attorneys’ Retirement System](/louisiana-dars/). Municipal Police and Firefighters are sworn statewide files. School Employees is the school-support file. Parochial Employees is the parish-employee file. District Attorneys is the prosecutor file. This is **one Louisiana clerks-of-court file**.
