# Relocating to Madrid for a Fondo de Reserva or Spanish public-reserve desk

Madrid is the official-address city of the [Tesorería General de la Seguridad Social](https://www.seg-social.es/wps/portal/wss/internet/Conocenos/QuienesSomos/29408), which administers Spain’s [Fondo de Reserva](https://www.seg-social.es/wps/portal/wss/internet/EstadisticasPresupuestosEstudios/FondoReserva?changeLanguage=es). Official TGSS address: **C/ Astros, 5 y 7, 28007 Madrid**. The reserve exists to meet future contributory-benefit needs when Social Security income and expenditure diverge. It is a public-reserve book, **not** a sovereign wealth fund hiring floor like PIF. A move here is often a move onto that public-finance window. Right to work sits with official Spanish sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit elsewhere

- TGSS is a common service of Social Security with its own legal personality, tutored by the Ministerio de Inclusión, Seguridad Social y Migraciones. Do not invent a Fondo de Reserva AUM on this page. Do not mint `/fondo-de-reserva-explained/`, `/madrid-swf/`, or `/tgss-explained/`.
- France’s [FRR](https://www.fondsdereserve.fr/) is a **different** legal person in [Paris](/careers/cities/paris/). Do not mash the two reserve books. Do not mint `/frr-explained/` or `/fonds-de-reserve/`.
- Confirm the legal person on the employer site. A Madrid markets floor is not automatically the reserve file.

Do not treat this city page as a minted Fondo de Reserva explainer. There is no UAO FRSS institution slug. Do not create one.

## Papers

Start at the [Secretaría de Estado de Migraciones](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones). Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Why people underestimate the job

A reserve created for contributory-benefit deviations is not a product shelf. Spanish is the language of legitimacy even when the dealing language is English. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail. Satellites fail when the investment committee is elsewhere — the same problem our [London note](/careers/intelligence/why-london-satellites-fail/) already names.
