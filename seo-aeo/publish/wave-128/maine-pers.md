# Maine Public Employees Retirement System, explained

Maine Public Employees Retirement System is Maine’s statewide public-employee retirement system, not leftover New Hampshire Retirement System, leftover Massachusetts PRIM, leftover Maryland SRPS, leftover Vermont, and not leftover Ohio PERS. The official [FY2025 ACFR](https://www.mainepers.org/wp-content/uploads/pdfs/annual-reports/2025_ACFR.pdf) prints combined fiduciary net position restricted for benefits of **$22,466,747,053** at **30 June 2025**. Combined statement investments were **$22,386,577,220**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The State Employee and Teacher Plan was **$16,888,911,440**. Defined-benefit money-weighted return was **9.3 percent** net. Time-weighted return was **9.4 percent** net of fees. The transmittal **$21.3 billion** defined-benefit portfolio card is not the combined statement. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combining statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced split:

1. **Combined fiduciary net position restricted for benefits** **$22,466,747,053** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$20,899,308,967**). Combined statement investments **$22,386,577,220** is not fiduciary net position. The notes’ investments-at-fair-value print **$22,405,112,069** is not the statement investments line and not fiduciary net position. The investment-section lagged closing fair value **$21,117 million** is not the statement. Those prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, leftover New Hampshire, leftover Maryland SRPS, leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books on the same statement.** State Employee and Teacher Plan **$16,888,911,440**. Judicial Plan **$92,775,619**. Legislative Plan **$18,658,033**. PLD Consolidated Plan **$4,401,452,107**. PLD Agent Plan **$6,918,236**. Those five defined-benefit columns sum to **$21,408,715,435**, which is the transmittal **$21.3 billion** defined-benefit portfolio rounded, not the combined book. Defined Contribution **$81,907,554**. Group Life Insurance **$10,414,329**. Group Life Retired SETP **$187,594,721**. Group Life Retired PLD **$26,671,712**. MainePERS OPEB Trust **$20,496,526**. Retiree Health Insurance Trust State **$600,531,767**. Retiree Health Insurance Trust Teachers **$130,415,008**. Those lines are not interchangeable. Do not invent a combined funded ratio.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return on all defined-benefit plan investments **9.3 percent** net of investment expenses. OPEB-plan money-weighted return **5.54 percent**. Transmittal group-life return **13.1 percent**. Investment-section time-weighted return **9.4 percent** net of investment management fees and expenses. Those official one-year prints are not interchangeable.
