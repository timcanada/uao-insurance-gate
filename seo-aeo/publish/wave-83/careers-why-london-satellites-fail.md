# Why London satellites fail — the IC is usually elsewhere

A London asset-owner seat often fails because it is a satellite: origination, public markets, or a client city for a fund whose legitimacy sits in Toronto, Abu Dhabi, Oslo or Singapore. People take the job as if the investment committee lived on the same floor. This Careers Intelligence briefing sits beside Universal Asset Owners' [London city page](/careers/cities/london/). It is not immigration, tax, or compensation advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second London city page.** Do not invent a house AUM to decorate a satellite story. UK schemes and insurers *are* centred here — do not write them out of a city that also hosts everyone else’s floor.

## The satellite is not the mandate

[Asset owners](/what-is-an-asset-owner/) hold capital for their own account. The account is usually booked at headquarters. London can be deal flow, a time zone, and a legal system. It is a bug if you thought you were joining the centre.

The [relocating umbrella](/careers/intelligence/relocating-for-an-asset-owner-role/) already says the city is part of the mandate. The [moving-into playbook](/careers/intelligence/moving-into-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/) already says people “come for a few years but don't always stay.” Satellites fail on both: the IC is elsewhere, and the household treated Mayfair as the institution.

Right to work sits with [GOV.UK visas and immigration](https://www.gov.uk/browse/visas-immigration). Sponsorship, dependents, and the gap between a “global mobility promise” and a licence are why offers collapse in week six.

## What to pressure-test before you accept

1. **Where the IC sits.** If the answer is “we dial in,” you are not at the centre.
2. **Decision rights.** Are you taking risk, or preparing decks for a committee you will not sit on?
3. **Whose book.** A Canadian pension, a Gulf fund, a UK scheme, or an insurer — those are different files.
4. **Year four.** Who is still in the London seat, and who went home?
5. **Exit.** Does the satellite have alumni, or only a lease?

## For employers and search firms

If the London role is origination, say so. If the IC is in Toronto or Abu Dhabi, write that in the brief. Owners already use [Careers](/careers/) when they are hiring. Labelled recruitment products: [Careers](/careers/).
