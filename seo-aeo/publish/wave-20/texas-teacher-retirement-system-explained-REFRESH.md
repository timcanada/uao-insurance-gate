# Teacher Retirement System of Texas, explained

The Teacher Retirement System of Texas is the state’s teacher pension. It was founded in **1937**. TRS’s own Fund Insights page prints a pension trust fund value of **$225.3 billion at 31 August 2025**, with **2,102,992** members. The financial-highlights table prints **$225.275 billion** and a **9.77%** FY2025 return. The funded ratio on the 2025 PAFR walk is **77.5%**. The live UAO page’s **$295 billion / 89.4% / 1.6 million** lines are stale. This is not investment advice.

**Last updated:** 26 August 2026 · **Desk:** UAO Research · **Canonical URL:** `/texas-teacher-retirement-system-explained/`

The live page this overwrites leads with **$295 billion**, **1.6 million** members, and an **89.4%** funded ratio as of **31 August 2023**, then ranks TRS ahead of [NYSLRS](/new-york-state-common-retirement-fund-explained/) on a homemade 2023 table. Official FY2025 numbers have moved. There is no live short twin in `twins.csv`. Do not mint `/texas-trs/` or `/teacher-retirement-system-of-texas/`.

TRS tells you it will not give you this morning’s market value. Use the fiscal year-end.

## How a peer should read it

TRS is a **teacher pension**, not a sovereign wealth fund. The score they will stand behind is the **31 August** year-end: **$225.3 billion**, **9.77%**, **2.1 million** members, **77.5%** funded. Anyone still writing **$295 billion** and **89.4%** is reading 2023. Compare it with [CalSTRS](/calstrs-explained/) and [NYSLRS](/new-york-state-common-retirement-fund-explained/), not with [ADIA](/adia-abu-dhabi-investment-authority/).
