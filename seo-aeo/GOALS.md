## Two-lane list (how 1 million subscribers does not destroy the room)

The current brief is **reviewed** and exclusive. That is how $20k–$50k premium ads stay rational.

A million names cannot all sit on that list. So the machine runs **two lanes**:

| Lane | Product | Job | Monetization |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A — Public / scale** | Open SEO newsletter + high-traffic hubs + podcast | Millions of views; 1M subscribers over time | Display, category sponsorship, podcast ads |
| **B — Institutional / reviewed** | Work-email brief, editorial review, membership | The expensive room (CIOs, principals) | Premium IOs, membership, Talent Desk |

Every page must name which lane it fills. Franchise journalism (briefs, Probability Desk) stays Lane B. Entity/Q&A/city/directory pages feed **both**: they rank (Lane A) and convert the right people into Lane B.
