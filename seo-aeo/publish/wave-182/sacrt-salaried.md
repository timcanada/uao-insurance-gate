# Sacramento Regional Transit Salaried Retirement Plan, explained

The Sacramento Regional Transit District Retirement Plan for AFSCME, AEA, and Non-Represented Employees is SacRT’s salaried pension book, not leftover SacRT ATU (**$202,076,310**), leftover SacRT IBEW (**$91,895,427**), leftover Sacramento County SCERS, leftover CalPERS. The official [financial statements as of 30 June 2025](https://www.sacrt.com/wp-content/uploads/FY-2025-Audited-Financial-Statements-for-Retirement-Plans.pdf) print Salaried net position restricted for pension benefits of **$165,467,820** at **30 June 2025**. Salaried investments **$165,265,252** are not that card. Notes and RSI money-weighted return was **11.90 percent**. Notes GASB **79.88 percent** is of the Salaried column. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statements of plan net position, the GASB note, and the money-weighted print are the sourced lines:

1. **Salaried net position restricted for pension benefits** **$165,467,820** at **30 June 2025**. Beginning **$147,310,778**. Increase **$18,157,042**. Salaried investments **$165,265,252**. ATU FNP **$202,076,310** is not Salaried. IBEW FNP **$91,895,427** is not Salaried. Combined FNP **$459,439,557** is not Salaried. Do not treat leftover SacRT ATU **$202,076,310**, leftover Sacramento County SCERS **$14,577,459 thousand**, leftover VTA ATU **$769,785 thousand**, leftover CalPERS **$637.1 billion**, leftover EBMUD ERS **$2,743,096 thousand**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion**, leftover USS **£76.8bn**, leftover PFZW **€35bn**, leftover CIC **$1.3T**, leftover Alaska **$89bn**, leftover GPIF **15.83%** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Notes Salaried total pension liability **$207,139,727**. Salaried net pension liability **$41,671,907**. Salaried plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **79.88 percent**. ATU **84.70 percent**, IBEW **84.96 percent**, and combined MD&A **82.9 percent** are not the Salaried card. Do not treat **$207,139,727** or **$41,671,907** as AUM. Discount rate **6.75 percent**. The **6.75 percent** discount is not the money-weighted card.
3. **Returns and cash flow.** Notes annual money-weighted return **11.90 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025**. RSI **11.90 percent** is the same card (2024 **10.97 percent**). Notes say assets are commingled for economies of scale — that shared-pool print is still the official money-weighted card. MD&A gross **12.38 percent** versus benchmark **12.08 percent** is not money-weighted. Combined MD&A employer contributions are not the Salaried column. Crowe LLP, Sacramento, California, dated the unmodified opinion **2 December 2025**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “SacRT salaried AUM” has to name the book. The **$165,467,820** line is Salaried fiduciary net position at **30 June 2025**. The **$459,439,557** line is combined. The **11.90 percent** card is money-weighted. The **79.88 percent** card is Salaried GASB. Those are not leftover SacRT ATU **$202,076,310**.
