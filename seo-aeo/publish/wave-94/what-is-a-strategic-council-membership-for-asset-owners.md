# What is a Strategic Council membership for asset owners?

A Strategic Council membership is Universal Asset Owners’ application-only research relationship for senior owner teams that need private briefings and custom memos, not a banner. The live [membership](/membership/) page prices it from **$175,000 a year** for up to **25 named users**. It sits above Institutional Core (**$60,000**) and Institutional Intelligence (**$100,000**). It is not investment advice, and it is not an advertising buy.

**Do not treat this page as a second advertise twin.** Media packages live on `/advertise/` and `/what-does-a-20k-a-month-media-buy-get-you/`. Do not mint `/strategic-council-explained/`, `/what-is-strategic-council/`, or `/sovereign-membership-explained/`. Do not invent a subscriber total.

## What the live membership page already prints

[Membership](/membership/) is the current public card. Lift only what it prints:

| Public tier | Starts from | Seats | How you buy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Institutional Core | **$60,000 / year** | Up to 5 named users | Annual |
| Flagship Institutional Intelligence | **$100,000 / year** | Up to 10 named users | Annual |
| **Strategic Council** | **From $175,000 / year** | Up to 25 named users | By application; invoiced |
| Sovereign / Enterprise | Custom — typically **$300,000+ / year** | Bespoke | Contract |

Strategic Council includes everything in Institutional Intelligence, plus a quarterly private executive briefing, an annual strategy workshop, a custom watchlist, a custom board-ready quarterly deck, a private scenario review after major geopolitical events, **up to 4 custom research memos per year**, and a named editorial liaison. Priority Q&A SLA on that page is same-business-day. Q&A is research clarification — not advice.

Sovereign / Enterprise is the bespoke step above Council (custom agenda, dedicated liaison, optional distribution rights). Do not invent a seat count or a discounted “founding” price on this page.

[Readership](/readership/) is the circulation file (including **98%** of SWFs and **20 of 20** largest pensions, method current as of **August 2026**). Membership does not rent that file.

## What Council is not

- A sentence in the daily brief sold as news
- A recommended-manager stamp
- Progress toward a homemade **$1 million / year** advertising total
- A substitute for [Advertise](/advertise/) (Establish authority still starts from **$20,000 / program** on that page — a different product)

[Ethics](/ethics/) (version 1.0, **28 May 2026**) still governs the room. Payment never buys a quote or a conclusion. The membership page already says nothing published is investment, legal, tax, or fiduciary advice.

## How an owner actually buys it

Public institutions can subscribe through procurement. The live page offers an Institutional Membership Procurement Brief and names **info@universalassetowners.com** for vendor onboarding. Strategic Council and Sovereign are always invoiced on a contract schedule. Multi-year terms are available for the upper tiers.
