# Oxford Endowment Fund, explained

The Oxford Endowment Fund is Oxford’s pooled long-horizon book, not leftover Cambridge CUEF (**£4,606.8 million**), leftover USS (**£76.8bn**). The official [Oxford Endowment Fund Report 2025](https://www.ouem.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/OEF-Report-2025.pdf) prints OEF assets of **£6.7 billion** at **31 December 2025**. Print pounds. Do not dollarize. The OEF returned **10.9 percent** net for the year to **31 December 2025**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Cambridge overwrite or a leftover USS overwrite.** Do not mint `/oxford-endowment-explained/` or `/oxford-endowment-ousl/`. **Never mint `/oxford/`, `/oef/`, or `/ouem/`.** The University of Oxford house financial statements **403**’d from this environment; the official OU Endowment Management report is the lock. OEF is a Charitable Unauthorised Unit Trust managed by OU Endowment Management. It is not leftover CUEF.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The report prints the OEF in **sterling**.

1. **OEF assets** **£6.7 billion** at **31 December 2025**. Print pounds. Do not dollarize. Do not treat leftover Cambridge CUEF **£4,606.8 million**, leftover USS **£76.8bn**, leftover PFZW **€35bn**, leftover Harvard **$56.9 billion**, leftover Notre Dame Endowment Pool **$25,434,616 thousand** as this filing. Do not convert these sterling lines into a homemade US-dollar AUM.
2. **Named books are not interchangeable.** Since-inception nominal **9.0 percent** and real **5.9 percent** are not the 2025 card. The **5 percent** real line is the long-term objective, not the year. Calendar year-end is **31 December**. That is not leftover Cambridge’s **31 July** NAV / **30 June** performance year.
3. **Returns and payout.** OEF net return **10.9 percent** for the year to **31 December 2025**. 2025 distribution **£253.4 million**. Distribution policy **4.25 percent** of the average of the last 20 quarters’ NAV per unit, with a **10 percent** increase cap. OU Endowment Management, authorised and regulated by the FCA.

Do not flatten OEF into leftover [Cambridge University Endowment Fund](/cambridge-endowment/), leftover [USS](/uss-universities-superannuation-scheme/), leftover [Notre Dame endowment](/notre-dame-endowment/), leftover [University of Pennsylvania endowment](/university-of-pennsylvania-endowment/), leftover [MITIMCo](/mit-investment-management-company/), or leftover [largest university endowments](/largest-university-endowments-2026/).
