# Teachers Retirement System of Georgia, explained

The Teachers Retirement System of Georgia is Georgia’s teacher pension, not leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion** and not leftover Georgia ERS. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.trsga.com/library/files/publications/TRS_ACFR2025_Protected.pdf) prints net position restricted for pensions of **$116,767,912 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Total investments were **$114,060,435 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted return was **12.2 percent**. The money-weighted return was **10.1 percent**. Fiduciary net position was **84.65 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Texas TRS overwrite or a second Georgia ERS page.** The Georgia public-employee keeper is `/georgia-ers/`. Do not mint `/georgia-trs-explained/`, `/trsga/`, or `/teachers-retirement-system-of-georgia/`. Do not mint `/texas-trs/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statements are in **thousands**:

1. **Net position restricted for pensions** **$116,767,912 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (beginning **$106,174,001 thousand**; increase **$10,593,911 thousand**). Total investments **$114,060,435 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion**, leftover Georgia ERS **$26,469,880 thousand**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio STRS **$80 billion+** / **$90 billion** / **$350 billion** as this filing.
2. **Cash flows on the same report.** Employer contributions **$3,464,704 thousand**. Member contributions **$1,031,027 thousand**. Net investment income **$12,715,602 thousand**. Benefit payments **$6,504,379 thousand**.
3. **Returns and funded status.** Time-weighted return **12.2 percent** (Daily Valuation Method). Equity sleeve **14.4 percent**. Private-equity sleeve **12.7 percent**. Fixed-income sleeve **6.0 percent**. Five-year annualized **10.1 percent**. Money-weighted return **10.1 percent**. Those return lines are not interchangeable. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **84.65 percent**.

Do not flatten Georgia TRS into [Georgia ERS](/georgia-ers/) or [Texas TRS](/texas-teacher-retirement-system-explained/). Georgia ERS is the Georgia public-employee administrator. This is **one Georgia teacher file**.
