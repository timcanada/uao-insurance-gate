# Employees' Retirement System of Georgia, explained

The Employees’ Retirement System of Georgia is Georgia’s combined public-employee administrator, not leftover Georgia TRS and not leftover Pennsylvania PSERS. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.ers.ga.gov/sites/main/files/file-attachments/ers_acfr_2025_-_protected_compressed.pdf) prints combined fiduciary net position of **$26,469,880 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Combining-statement pensions and OPEB were **$26,198,245 thousand**. The Survivors Benefit Fund was **$271,635 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The defined-benefit Employees’ Retirement System plan was **$18,637,570 thousand**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Georgia TRS overwrite or a second Pennsylvania PSERS page.** The Georgia teacher keeper is now `/georgia-trs/`. Do not mint `/ersga/`, `/georgia-ers-explained/`, or `/employees-retirement-system-of-georgia/`. Do not mint `/georgia-trs-explained/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combining statements are in **thousands**. The sourced split is:

1. **Combined fiduciary net position** **$26,469,880 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$23,683,584 thousand**). Combining-statement pensions and OPEB **$26,198,245 thousand**. Survivors Benefit Fund **$271,635 thousand**. Those two combining lines plus the combined fiduciary total are not interchangeable. Combined investments **$26,528,051 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. The proprietary-fund net position **$495,775 thousand** is not the fiduciary book. Do not treat leftover Pennsylvania PSERS, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or a skipped Georgia TRS glance line as this filing.
2. **Named books on the defined-benefit combining statement.** Employees’ Retirement System **$18,637,570 thousand**. Public School Employees Retirement System **$1,314,006 thousand**. Legislative Retirement System **$44,534 thousand**. Georgia Judicial Retirement System **$638,760 thousand**. Georgia Military Pension Fund **$52,580 thousand**. State Employees’ Assurance Department OPEB **$1,709,232 thousand**. Defined-benefit plans total **$22,396,690 thousand**. The Georgia Defined Contribution Plan **$160,570 thousand**, 401(k) Plan **$2,812,736 thousand**, and 457 Plan **$828,249 thousand** sit on the wider pensions-and-OPEB combining total. Those lines are not interchangeable. Georgia PSERS here is the Georgia public-school-employee book for members who are **not** in [Georgia TRS](/georgia-trs/). Do not flatten it onto [Pennsylvania PSERS](/pennsylvania-psers/) or [Georgia TRS](/georgia-trs/).
3. **Returns.** Time-weighted return **12.0 percent**. Money-weighted return **12.6 percent**. Equity sleeve **14.5 percent**. Those three return lines are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Georgia ERS into [Pennsylvania PSERS](/pennsylvania-psers/) or [Ohio SERS](/ohio-sers/). Pennsylvania PSERS is the Pennsylvania school file. This is **one Georgia public-employee administrator file**.
