# State Universities Retirement System of Illinois, explained

The State Universities Retirement System of Illinois is Illinois’s public-university and community-college pension, not leftover Illinois TRS and not leftover Illinois SERS. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://surs.org/wp-content/uploads/COMP-FY2025.pdf) prints combined fiduciary net position of **$25,365,032,800** at **30 June 2025**. The defined-benefit pension book was **$25,182,592,847**. Combined investments at fair value were **$25,002,957,643**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted return was **6.4 percent** net of investment management fees. Defined-benefit fiduciary net position was **45.48 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Illinois TRS overwrite or a leftover Illinois SERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/surs/`, `/illinois-surs-explained/`, or `/state-universities-retirement-system/`. Do not mint `/trs-illinois/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Combined fiduciary net position** **$25,365,032,800** at **30 June 2025** (MD&A condensed 2024 **$24,516.6 million**). Defined-benefit pensions **$25,182,592,847**. Other employee benefits **$166,653,261**. Custodial funds **$15,786,692**. Those four lines are not interchangeable. Combined investments at fair value **$25,002,957,643** is not fiduciary net position. Combined total assets **$27,941,953,476** is not fiduciary net position. The transmittal investment-assets card **$25.2 billion** is not fiduciary net position. Retirement Savings Plan assets **approximately $4.9 billion** and Deferred Compensation Plan assets **approximately $131.5 million** sit with a third-party administrator and are not included in the Statement of Fiduciary Net Position. Do not treat leftover Illinois TRS, leftover Illinois SERS, leftover Illinois State Board of Investment, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards are the defined-benefit book.** Defined-benefit fiduciary net position **$25,182.6 million**. Total pension liability **$55,372.1 million**. Net pension liability **$30,189.5 million**. Fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **45.48 percent**. Those four cards are not the combined $25.365 billion book.
3. **Returns.** Time-weighted return **6.4 percent** net of investment management fees, against the house policy benchmark **6.1 percent** and the **6.5 percent** long-term actuarial assumption. Annual money-weighted return **6.42 percent** net of fees. Those two official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten SURS into [Illinois TRS](/illinois-trs/), [Illinois SERS](/illinois-sers/), or [Illinois State Board of Investment](/illinois-isbi/). Illinois TRS is the statewide teacher file. Illinois SERS is the statewide state-employee file. ISBI does not manage this book. This is **one public-university file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “SURS AUM” has to name the book. The **$25,365,032,800** line is the combined fiduciary book. The **$25,182,592,847** line is the defined-benefit pension. The **$25,002,957,643** line is combined investments. Those are not the same mandate.
