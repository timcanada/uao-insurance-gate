# City of St. Joseph Employees’ Retirement System, explained

The City of St. Joseph Employees’ Retirement System is St. Joseph, Michigan’s cost-sharing pension, not leftover Michigan MERS, leftover Ann Arbor Employees, and not leftover Detroit GRS. The official [30 June 2025 City of St. Joseph Employees’ Retirement System annual financial report](https://www.sjcity.com/sites/default/files/fileattachments/finance_amp_utility_billing/page/2814/city_of_st._joseph_mi_employees_system_afr_2024-25.pdf) prints combined fiduciary net position of **$84,988,230** at **30 June 2025**. Mutual-fund investments **$84,714,675** are not that card. Money-weighted return was **13.53 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Combined GASB **95.31 percent** is not the City-only **95.28 percent** column. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Michigan MERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/sjers/`, `/st-joseph-ers/`, or `/st-joseph-employees-explained/`. Do not mint `/ers/`. This is Michigan, not Missouri.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position, the GASB 67 schedule, and the money-weighted schedule are the sourced lines:

1. **Fiduciary net position** **$84,988,230** at **30 June 2025** (beginning **$77,693,114**). Investments in mutual funds **$84,714,675**. The System is a cost-sharing multiple-employer book for the City of St. Joseph, the City of St. Joseph Housing Commission, and the Benton Harbor–St. Joseph Joint Wastewater Treatment Plant. City ending **$73,688,921**, Commission ending **$887,350**, and Plant ending **$10,411,959** are not the combined card. Do not treat leftover Michigan MERS Defined Benefit **$14,870,864 thousand**, leftover Ann Arbor Employees **$682,683,869**, leftover Detroit GRS, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Combined total pension liability **$89,168,714**. Combined net pension liability **$4,180,484**. Combined plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **95.31 percent**. City **95.28 percent**, Commission **96.46 percent**, and Plant **95.43 percent** are employer columns, not the combined card.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **13.53 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **11.53 percent**).

Do not flatten the City of St. Joseph Employees’ Retirement System into [Municipal Employees’ Retirement System of Michigan](/michigan-mers/), [City of Ann Arbor Employees’ Retirement System](/ann-arbor-employees/), or [General Retirement System of the City of Detroit](/detroit-grs/). MERS is a statewide local-government file. Ann Arbor Employees is an Ann Arbor city file. Detroit GRS is a Detroit city file. This is **one St. Joseph, Michigan file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “St. Joseph pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$84,988,230** line is combined fiduciary net position. The **$84,714,675** line is mutual-fund investments. The **13.53 percent** card is money-weighted. The **95.31 percent** card is combined GASB. The **95.28 percent** card is the City column. Those are not the same mandate.
