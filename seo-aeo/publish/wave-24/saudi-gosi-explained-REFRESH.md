## What this is

GOSI collects contributions and pays social-insurance benefits. Hassana invests GOSI’s book. That is a **pension / social-insurance** structure, not a petroleum SWF. Do not file GOSI next to [PIF](/public-investment-fund-pif/) as if they were the same legal job. PIF is a public investment fund. GOSI is a social-insurance scheme.

The live page’s **$16 billion** 2022 payouts, **11.2 million** 2022 members, and leftover CalPERS **$440 billion** / TIAA **$280 billion** peer lines are not Hassana’s current print.
