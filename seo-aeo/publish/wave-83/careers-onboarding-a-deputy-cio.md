# Onboarding a deputy CIO — what the first year actually is

A deputy CIO is not a CIO-in-waiting by default. You are joining someone else’s mandate, committee calendar, and succession story — or you are the hire who makes an internal office real. This Careers Intelligence briefing sits beside Universal Asset Owners' [internal-versus-external CIO](/careers/intelligence/internal-vs-external-cio-office/) note. It is not compensation, legal, or outplacement advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second OCIO definition or a pay survey.** Do not invent a house AUM or a compensation range to decorate a first-year memo.

## The deputy seat is a mandate split

[Asset owners](/what-is-an-asset-owner/) hold capital for their own account. The [fiduciary duty](/fiduciary-duty-explained-institutional/) stays with the board and the beneficiaries. A deputy who cannot say what they may decide without the CIO does not have a seat. They have a title.

The board can [staff a CIO office or rent one](/careers/intelligence/internal-vs-external-cio-office/). A deputy hire is how an internal office becomes real — or how an [OCIO](/what-is-an-ocio/) relationship keeps a named owner-side counterpart. Those are different jobs. Ask which one you are in before the welcome lunch.

Public pensions will minute the hire. Sovereign and strategic funds will still have a file. Family offices will treat a leak as the failure mode.

## What to pressure-test in month one

1. **Decision rights.** What tickets do you sign, and what goes back to the CIO or the committee?
2. **Succession.** Are you the planned successor, a complement, or a political hedge? Get it in a sentence.
3. **The pack.** What will you refuse to put in front of the board?
4. **Conflicts.** Which managers, consultants, or prior employers still sit in the book?
5. **Exit.** How did the last deputy leave, and where did they go?

## For CIOs, boards and search firms

If the deputy is a succession hire, say so in the brief. If they are not, do not let the market write the story. Owners already use [Careers](/careers/) when they are hiring. Labelled recruitment products: [Careers](/careers/).
