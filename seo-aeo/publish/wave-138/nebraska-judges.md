# Nebraska Judges Retirement Plan, explained

The Nebraska Judges Retirement Plan is Nebraska’s judicial pension, not leftover Nebraska School Employees and not leftover MOSERS Judicial Plan. The official [30 June 2025 Auditor of Public Accounts report](https://auditors.nebraska.gov/APA_Reports/2026/SA851-03022026-July_1_2024_through_June_30_2025_Audit_Report.pdf) prints fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits of **$285,310,148** at **30 June 2025**. Investments at fair value were **$287,652,761**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return was **11.65 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **110.23 percent** of total pension liability. That card is a net pension asset. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Nebraska School overwrite or a leftover MOSERS Judicial overwrite.** Do not mint `/nebraska-judges-explained/` or `/njrs/`. Investments **$287,652,761** are not fiduciary net position. School **$18,532,685,257** is not this book. Shared School money-weighted **11.65 percent** is not a Judges-only time-weighted card.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The Judges column on the statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits** **$285,310,148** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$260,499,119**). The change is **$24,811,029**. Total investments at fair value **$287,652,761** is not fiduciary net position. Securities-lending collateral **$15,980,190** is not fiduciary net position. Court fees **$5,686,190** and State appropriations **$1,459,899** are additions, not AUM. Do not treat leftover Nebraska School **$18,532,685,257**, leftover Nebraska State Patrol **$647,545,587**, leftover MOSERS Judicial Plan **$211,176,545**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$258,841,749**. Net pension asset **$26,468,399**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **110.23 percent** (2024 **105.60 percent**). That card is an asset, not a liability. School **107.17 percent** and State Patrol **88.42 percent** are not this card. Actuarial additional contribution **$0** as of **1 July 2025** is not the GASB card.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **11.65 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **13.47 percent**). School also prints **11.65 percent**. State Patrol **11.79 percent** is not this card. This filing does not print a separate official one-year time-weighted house card.

Do not flatten the Nebraska Judges Retirement Plan into [Nebraska School Employees Retirement Plan](/nebraska-school/), [Nebraska State Patrol Retirement Plan](/nebraska-state-patrol/), [MOSERS](/missouri-mosers/), or [Iowa IPERS](/iowa-ipers/). School and Patrol are different legal columns on the same audit. MOSERS Judicial is a Missouri book. This is **one Nebraska judicial file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Nebraska judges AUM” has to name the book. The **$285,310,148** line is Judges fiduciary net position. The **$287,652,761** line is Judges investments. The **110.23 percent** card is the GASB ratio. The **11.65 percent** card is money-weighted. Those are not the same mandate.
