# Norway oil-fund governance: 22,683 billion kroner, not $1.43 trillion sleeves

The governance split is real. The live dollars are not. Norges Bank Investment Management manages Government Pension Fund Global for the Ministry of Finance. Politicians set the mandate. NBIM takes the security-level decisions inside those rules. The live page still prints the fund at **approximately $1.43 trillion as of end-2023**, then invents department sleeves of **approximately $1 trillion** equities, **$280 billion** fixed income, and **$140 billion** real assets. Those dollars are not the filings.

## What the governance model can say

- **Ownership.** The fund belongs to the Norwegian people. The Ministry of Finance owns the mandate and the benchmark.
- **Manager.** NBIM is a unit of Norges Bank, not a company. Staff take security-level decisions inside the mandate.
- **Accountability.** Parliament and the Ministry hold the fund to account. NBIM publishes holdings, returns, and votes.
- **Investment rule that matters for governance.** The capital is petroleum revenue invested **only abroad**, so the mainland economy is not bid up by the oil cash. That is why this book is a [SWF](/sovereign-wealth-fund/), not Norges Bank’s FX-reserve book.

A page that needs **$1.43 trillion** and three invented dollar sleeves to explain that split is a tracker page.
