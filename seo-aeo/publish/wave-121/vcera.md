# Ventura County Employees’ Retirement Association, explained

The Ventura County Employees’ Retirement Association is Ventura County’s 1937-Act employee pension, not leftover LACERA and not leftover SBCERA. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://content.vcera.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/VCERA-ACFR-2025-Sec.pdf) prints net position restricted for pensions of **$9,160,083 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Investments at fair value were **$9,020,682 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted return was **11.80 percent** net of investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **104.57 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover LACERA overwrite or a leftover SBCERA overwrite.** Do not mint `/ventura-vcera/`, `/vcera-explained/`, or `/ventura-county-employees/`. Do not mint `/lacera-explained/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Net position restricted for pensions** **$9,160,083 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$8,292,117 thousand**). Investments at fair value **$9,020,682 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$9,228,709 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. The MD&A **$9.2 billion** card is the fiduciary line rounded. The County of Ventura fiduciary-fund print for the VCERA Pension Trust **$9,200,423 thousand** is a county combining book, not this house statement. Those four lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover LACERA, leftover SBCERA, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$8,759,939 thousand**. Net pension asset **$400,144 thousand**. Fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **104.57 percent**. The actuarial-value funded status **99.2 percent** (from **97.0 percent**) is not the GASB card. Those two funded cards are not interchangeable.
3. **Returns.** Time-weighted return **11.80 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. The transmittal **11.8 percent** card matching the policy benchmark is the same time-weighted print rounded. Money-weighted return **11.64 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Those two official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten VCERA into [LACERA](/lacera/), [SBCERA](/sbcera/), [OCERS](/ocers/), or [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/). LACERA is the Los Angeles **County** file. SBCERA is the San Bernardino **County** file. This is **one Ventura County employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “VCERA AUM” has to name the book. The **$9,160,083 thousand** line is house fiduciary net position. The **$9,020,682 thousand** line is investments. The **$9,200,423 thousand** line is the county combining print. Those are not the same mandate.
