# British International Investment, explained

British International Investment plc is the UK’s development finance institution, not a sovereign wealth fund and not a second pension page. The official [Annual Report and Accounts 2025](https://assets.bii.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/26153150/BII_Annual-Review-Accounts-2025.pdf) print total net assets of **£9,879.9 million** at **31 December 2025**. The overall portfolio was **£6,641.2 million**. Gross new commitments were **£1,823.5 million**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. Print pounds. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second development-finance-institution overwrite or a second SWF overwrite.** Those keepers are `/what-is-a-development-finance-institution/` and `/sovereign-wealth-fund/`. Do not mint `/bii/`, `/british-international-investment/`, `/british-international-investment-explained/`, or `/cdc-group/`. Do not dollarize.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The 2025 accounts are the sourced split:

1. **Total net assets** **£9,879.9 million** at **31 December 2025** (2024: **£9,869.5 million**), a rise of **0.1 percent**. That is the equity-holders line. It is not the portfolio.
2. **Total portfolio value** **£6,641.2 million** (2024: **£7,291.2 million**). The overall portfolio fell by **£650.0 million** on foreign-currency and fair-value losses. Named books: Growth **£5,636.9 million**; Catalyst **£922.5 million**; Kinetic **£29.4 million**; Ukraine **£13.3 million**. Do not add those books to invent a second AUM. At year-end BII had investments in **247** funds and **284** direct investments.
3. **Gross new commitments** **£1,823.5 million** (2024: **£1,748.8 million**). Net new commitments **£1,802.3 million** after **£21.2 million** of prior-year cancellations. The overall result is a loss after tax of **£469.6 million**, a loss of **4.8 percent** on net assets. In US-dollar terms the portfolio generated **$108.6 million**, a **1.2 percent** gain — a currency translation, not a second sterling AUM. Print pounds for the house file.

The Commonwealth Development Corporation Act **1999** granted the company exemption from UK corporation tax from **May 2003**. That statute is the legal person. It is not a current AUM.

Do not flatten BII into [what a development finance institution is](/what-is-a-development-finance-institution/). That page defines the form. This page is **one UK DFI file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “UK DFI AUM” is selling a **taxpayer-owned development-finance file**, not a leftover homepage widget. Net assets sit above the portfolio because cash and other net assets sit on the same statement. At **31 December 2025** net cash and short-term deposits were **£1,758.7 million**. Do not add cash to the portfolio and call the sum a second AUM.
