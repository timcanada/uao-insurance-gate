# Wellcome Trust, explained

The Wellcome Trust is a UK charitable foundation funded by an investment portfolio, not a university endowment and not a second foundation-versus-endowment page. The official [Annual Report and Financial Statements 2025](https://cms.wellcome.org/sites/default/files/2026-01/wellcome-trust-annual-report-and-financial-statements-2025.pdf) for the year to **30 September 2025** print an investment portfolio of **£39,933 million** and total funds of **£35,740 million**. The portfolio returned **10.2 percent** in pounds sterling, or **6.4 percent** after inflation. Charitable expenditure was **£1,916 million**. Print pounds. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (year to 30 September 2025)

Wellcome’s sole source of funds is the investment portfolio. The 2025 statements are the sourced split:

1. **Portfolio value** **£39,933 million** at **30 September 2025** (2024: **£37,564 million**). **Total funds** — portfolio less liabilities — **£35,740 million** (2024: **£33,948 million**). The two lines are not the same number. Do not add them.
2. **Return** **+10.2 percent** in GBP (2024: **+5.2 percent**); **+6.4 percent** after inflation. After **£1.4 billion** in charitable cash expenditure, the portfolio delivered a gain of **£0.9 billion**. The Board’s funding methodology sets a minimum net investment portfolio of **£29 billion** (real terms as at April 2025) to support a **£16 billion** spend plan for **2022–2032**.
3. **Charitable expenditure** **£1,916 million** (2024: **£1,577 million**). Wellcome’s own investments page says the in-house team’s work has generated around **£27.9 billion** for the mission over the last ten years, and that the portfolio has grown at an average annual rate of **11.7 percent** over the past decade.
