# MITIMCo, explained

MITIMCo is MIT’s investment office, not a second Harvard page and not a leftover homemade MIT band. MIT’s official [Report of the Treasurer for fiscal 2025](https://vpf.mit.edu/sites/default/files/downloads/TreasurersReport/MITTreasurersReport2025.pdf) prints endowment funds, excluding pledges, of **$27,366.2 million** at **30 June 2025**. The unitized pool returned **14.8 percent**. Over the ten years to that date the official [MIT News release](https://news.mit.edu/2025/mit-releases-financials-and-endowment-figures-1010) prints an annualized return of **10.7 percent**. Do not treat MIT’s **$37.7 billion** net-asset line as the endowment. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second Harvard overwrite or a second Yale-model overwrite.** Those keepers are on the Wave 17 list and `/yale-endowment-model/`. Do not mint `/mitimco/`. Do not reprint leftover homemade MIT **$35–40 billion**.

## What is official (year to 30 June 2025)

MITIMCo is a unit of MIT. It manages the Institute’s endowment, retirement, and operating funds. The Treasurer’s report and the Institute’s own news release are the sourced split:

1. **Endowment funds, excluding pledges** **$27,366.2 million** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$24,572.7 million**). That is an **11.4 percent** increase. The Treasurer’s table prints the same year-end line as **$27,366 million**.
2. **Unitized pool return** **14.8 percent**, measured using valuations received within one month of fiscal year-end. Ten-year annualized return **10.7 percent** to **30 June 2025**.
3. **Institute net assets** **$37,675.7 million** at fiscal year-end. **Investments at fair value** **$35,790.3 million**. Those are **not** the endowment. Do not file them as a second AUM.

Do not flatten MITIMCo into [Harvard](/largest-university-endowments-2026/) or [the Yale model](/yale-endowment-model/). Those are different offices and different filings.
