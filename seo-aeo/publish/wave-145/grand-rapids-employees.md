# City of Grand Rapids General Retirement System, explained

The City of Grand Rapids General Retirement System is Grand Rapids’ civilian pension, not leftover Michigan MERS, leftover Detroit GRS, and not leftover Grand Rapids Police and Fire. The official [30 June 2025 City of Grand Rapids General Retirement System financial statements](https://grpensions.org/wp-content/uploads/June-30-2025.pdf) print fiduciary net position of **$487,700,489** at **30 June 2025**. Investments **$486,847,338** are not the net-position card. Investments including securities lending **$526,106,177** mix in lending collateral **$39,258,839**. Money-weighted return was **11.76 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. GASB **81.74 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Michigan MERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/grers/`, `/grand-rapids-employees-explained/`, or `/ers/`. Do not mix this **30 June** card with the Police and Fire **31 December** card.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statements of fiduciary net position, the GASB 67 schedule, and the money-weighted note are the sourced lines:

1. **Fiduciary net position** **$487,700,489** at **30 June 2025** (beginning **$458,985,431**). Total investments **$486,847,338**. Investments including securities lending **$526,106,177** are not a second AUM. Member deposit fund **$33,749,788** is a legally required reserve, not a second book. Do not treat leftover Michigan MERS Defined Benefit **$14,870,864 thousand**, leftover Detroit GRS, leftover Grand Rapids Police and Fire **$585,054,844**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$596,650,996**. Net pension liability **$108,950,507**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **81.74 percent**.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **11.76 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **10.09 percent**).

Do not flatten the City of Grand Rapids General Retirement System into [Municipal Employees’ Retirement System of Michigan](/michigan-mers/), [General Retirement System of the City of Detroit](/detroit-grs/), [City of Grand Rapids Police and Fire Retirement System](/grand-rapids-police-fire/), or [City of Ann Arbor Employees’ Retirement System](/ann-arbor-employees/). MERS is a statewide local-government file. Detroit GRS is a Detroit city file. Grand Rapids Police and Fire is the sworn book on a **31 December** year. Ann Arbor Employees is an Ann Arbor city file. This is **one Grand Rapids civilian file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Grand Rapids pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$487,700,489** line is civilian fiduciary net position at **30 June 2025**. The **$486,847,338** line is investments. The **$526,106,177** line includes securities-lending collateral. The **11.76 percent** card is money-weighted. The **81.74 percent** card is GASB. Those are not the same mandate as the Police and Fire **31 December** file.
