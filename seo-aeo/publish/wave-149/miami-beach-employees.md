# Miami Beach Employees’ Retirement Plan, explained

The official [30 September 2025 and 2024 Miami Beach Employees’ Retirement Plan financial statements](https://www.miamibeachfl.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2025-Financial-Statements-Miami-Beach-Employees-Retirement-Plan-FY-Ending-9-30-2025-Final-Approved-by-Board-4.14.2026.pdf) print fiduciary net position of **$949,449,272** at **30 September 2025**. Investments **$942,224,197** are not that card. Cash **$7,277,757** is not investments. DROP investments **$22,886,289** are a component, not a second AUM. Money-weighted return was **10.22 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. GASB **84.36 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
