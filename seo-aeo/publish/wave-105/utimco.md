# UTIMCO, explained

UTIMCO is the University of Texas / Texas A&M Investment Management Company, not a second Texas TRS page and not a second Permanent School Fund page. The official [2025 annual report](https://www.utimco.org/media/1kib4vyb/2025-annual-report.pdf) prints Endowments of **$67.6 billion** at **31 August 2025**. The Endowments returned **10.0 percent** in the fiscal year. The ten-year annualized net return was **8.6 percent**. Distributions were **$2.7 billion** last year. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second Texas TRS overwrite or a second Permanent School Fund overwrite.** Those keepers are `/texas-teacher-retirement-system-explained/` and `/texas-permanent-school-fund-explained/`. Do not mint `/university-of-texas-imf/` or `/utimco-explained/`.

## What is official (year to 31 August 2025)

The UT System Board of Regents holds fiduciary responsibility. Day-to-day management is delegated to UTIMCO, created **March 1996**. The 2025 report’s Endowment lines are the sourced split:

1. **Endowments** **$67.6 billion** at fiscal year-end **2025**. Ten years earlier the same report prints **$25.7 billion**. Growth of **$41.9 billion** is **after** distributions.
2. **Return** **+10.0 percent** for the year to **31 August 2025** (policy benchmark **+6.7 percent**). Ten-year annualized net return **+8.6 percent** versus a passive benchmark **+7.6 percent**. Active management added **+1.0 percent** a year, which the report files as an additional **$6.1 billion**.
3. **Distributions** **$2.7 billion** last year, and **$18.8 billion** over the decade. The report’s named Endowment books are the **Permanent University Fund (PUF)** and the **UT System General Endowment Fund (GEF)**.

Do not flatten UTIMCO into [Texas TRS](/texas-teacher-retirement-system-explained/) or the [Permanent School Fund](/texas-permanent-school-fund-explained/). Those are different statutes.
