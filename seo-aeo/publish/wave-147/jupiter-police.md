The official [30 September 2025 Town of Jupiter Police Officers’ Retirement Fund financial statements](https://www.resourcecenters.com/pension%20funds/Select%20Your%20Fund/Jupiter%20Police%20Officers'%20Retirement%20Fund/26Required%20Documents%20and%20Disclosures/Audit%209-30-2025.pdf) print fiduciary net position of **$121,341,553** at **30 September 2025**. Investments **$120,441,946** are not that card. Defined-benefit restricted **$108,844,634** is not total net position. DROP **$10,055,754** and Share **$2,441,165** are restricted portions, not second AUM. Money-weighted return was **10.1 percent** net of Plan investment expense. GASB **93.98 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
