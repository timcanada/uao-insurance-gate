# The Alaska Permanent Fund, explained

The Alaska Permanent Fund is the United States’ largest sovereign wealth fund. The Alaska Permanent Fund Corporation printed a total fund value of **$91,346,500,000 at 30 June 2026**, with a **12.42%** fiscal-year-to-date return and **6.61%** over five years — **unaudited** as of **21 August 2026**. The Fund is two accounts, Principal and Earnings Reserve. The statutory POMV draw is **5%** of a smoothed market-value average. This is not investment advice.

## Two accounts, one portfolio

From [APFC fund structure](https://apfc.org/the-fund/fund-structure/) and the Fund page:

- **Principal** — constitutionally protected, non-spendable. Mineral royalties, inflation-proofing, special appropriations.
- **Earnings Reserve Account (ERA)** — statutory account for realized earnings. This is what the legislature can appropriate.

Both are invested together. The spendable rule since 2018 is **AS 37.13.140(b)**: a **Percent of Market Value** draw of **5.0%** of the average market value for the **first five of the preceding six** fiscal years, limited to what sits in the ERA.

APFC’s own sentence: the POMV draw provides more than **66%** of the state’s general-fund revenue on the Fund page (the structure page still says “more than 55%” on an older line). Quote the page you are citing. Do not invent a third percentage.

This is why Alaska is not [Norway](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/). GPFG is an accumulation book with a fiscal rule at the *budget*. Alaska’s ERA is the budget.

## What it is

A **state sovereign wealth fund** created by the 1976 constitutional amendment, managed by the Alaska Permanent Fund Corporation — a quasi-independent state agency. APFC’s May 2026 board release named **Deven Mitchell** as Executive Director / CEO and **Marcus Frampton** as Chief Investment Officer. Board Chair named there: **Jason Brune**.

It is the largest SWF **in the United States**. It is not in the same mass as [GPFG](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/) or [CIC](/china-investment-corporation-cic-explained/). Do not call it “America’s Norway.”

The dividend is a **legislative appropriation**, not a constitutional entitlement to a formula check. The 2025 $1,000 print is the Department of Revenue’s number. APFC invests the Fund; it does not set the PFD.

Three APFC benchmarks, as the performance page defines them: a **passive** stocks/bonds index (value-add test), a **performance** benchmark matching the target mix (peer/execution test), and a **return objective** of **CPI + 5%**. Do not collapse those three into one “beat the market” line.

## How a peer should read it

- **$89 billion is not the print.** Use $91,346,500,000 at 30 June 2026, and say **unaudited**.
- **POMV is 5% of a smoothed average, not 5% of today’s NAV.** FY27’s $4.0 billion sits on a $79.9 billion five-year average, not on $91.3 billion.
- **Principal is locked. ERA is the politics.** If you cannot explain the two-account split, you are not covering this fund.
- **The PFD is not the Fund’s return.** $1,000 in 2025 is a budget choice.
- **Alaska PERS is a different legal person.** Do not flatten this savings file onto [Alaska PERS](/alaska-pers/) combined fiduciary net position **$26,772,591 thousand** at **30 June 2025**.
