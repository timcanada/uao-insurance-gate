# Types of SWFs: quote the filing — GIC has no AUM and GPFG is kroner

Sovereign wealth funds are typed by **funding source** and **mandate**, not by a consultancy dollar. The live page, dated July 2026, still uses those types as a vehicle to assign Norway’s Government Pension Fund Global **approximately $1.4 trillion**, Temasek **approximately $416 billion**, GIC **approximately $810 billion**, Oman’s leftover State General Reserve Fund **approximately $20 billion**, Korea Investment Corporation **roughly $200 billion**, PIF **approximately $925 billion**, and QIA **approximately $650 billion**. Those dollars are not the filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## What the types actually are

Universal Asset Owners uses the usual IFSWF-style split. Many houses are hybrids.

1. **Stabilisation** — smooth the budget; liquidity first.
2. **Savings / future generations** — turn a depleting resource into a permanent financial book. [GPFG](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/) is the large public exhibit.
3. **Reserve investment** — a longer-horizon sleeve on assets the central bank does not need for day-to-day stability. [GIC](/gic-singapore-explained/) is the usual Singapore exhibit, and it **still will not give you AUM**.
4. **Development / strategic** — domestic industry and national champions. [PIF](/public-investment-fund-pif/) and [Temasek](/is-temasek-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/) are different legal designs of that job.
5. **Pension reserve** — pre-fund public pensions. That is **not** the same as a member pension. [GPIF](/gpif-japan-explained/) is a **pension**, not a SWF type.

A types page that needs GIC **$810 billion** and QIA **$650 billion** to illustrate the taxonomy is a tracker page.

## What is not official

- GPFG **$1.4 trillion**.
- Temasek **$416 billion**.
- GIC **$810 billion**, or “Government of Singapore Investment Corporation.”
- Oman SGRF **$20 billion** as a current standalone AUM.
- KIC **$200 billion** when the house print is **USD 232.0 billion**.
- PIF **$925 billion** as current AUM.
- QIA **$650 billion**.
