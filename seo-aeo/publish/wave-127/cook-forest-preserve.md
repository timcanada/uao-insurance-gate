# Cook County Forest Preserve Pension, explained

The Forest Preserve District Employees’ Annuity and Benefit Fund of Cook County is the Forest Preserve District’s employee pension, not leftover Cook County Pension, leftover IMRF, leftover Illinois FPIF, leftover Illinois IPOPIF, leftover Chicago Teachers, leftover Chicago Fire FABF, leftover Chicago LABF, and not leftover MEABF. The official [2025 audited financial statements](https://www.cookcountypension.com/assets/1/6/CCPF_Forest_Preserve_25_FS_Final_Secured.pdf) print fiduciary net position restricted for pensions of **$236,214,745** at **31 December 2025**. Investments were **$222,025,346**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return was **15.35 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **60.01 percent** of total pension liability. Healthcare net position prints **zero**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Fiduciary net position restricted for pensions** **$236,214,745** at **31 December 2025** (2024 **$212,371,251**). The change is **$23,843,494**, or **11.2 percent**. Investments **$222,025,346** is not fiduciary net position. Healthcare net position prints a dash. Those prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover Cook County Pension **$15,221,712,193**, leftover IMRF, leftover Chicago Teachers, leftover Chicago Fire FABF, leftover Chicago LABF, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$393,629,638**. Employer’s net pension liability **$157,414,893**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **60.01 percent** (2024 **57.07 percent**). Those two lines are not interchangeable with investments.
3. **Returns.** Money-weighted return **15.35 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense (2024 **9.07 percent**). The MD&A portfolio-return print is the same **15.35 percent** card (benchmark **15.89 percent**). The house statements do not give this page a separate official time-weighted card to quote as a second return. Do not invent one.
