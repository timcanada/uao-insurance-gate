# Employees’ Retirement System of the City of Baltimore, explained

Employees’ Retirement System of the City of Baltimore is Baltimore’s civilian pension, not leftover Maryland SRPS, leftover Montgomery County Employee Retirement Plans, leftover Howard County Retirement Plan, leftover Baltimore Fire and Police, and not leftover District of Columbia Retirement Board. The official [FY2025 ACFR](https://www.bcers.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/ERS-ACFR-Low-Res-Searchable.pdf) prints net position restricted for pensions of **$2,264,990,790** at **30 June 2025**. Statement total investments were **$2,225,547,573**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. RSI money-weighted return was **10.6 percent**. Time-weighted return was **9.5 percent**. Fiduciary net position was **80.40 percent** of total pension liability. Actuarial funded **82.0 percent** is not the GASB card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Maryland SRPS overwrite or a leftover Montgomery County overwrite.** Do not mint `/bcers/` or `/baltimore-ers/`. The transmittal “market value funded **82.0 percent**” card is the actuarial print, not the GASB card.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position restricted for pensions** **$2,264,990,790** at **30 June 2025** (beginning **$2,111,462,086**). The change is **$153,528,704**. Statement total investments **$2,225,547,573** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$2,357,503,045** include securities-lending collateral and are not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover Maryland SRPS, leftover Montgomery County Employee Retirement Plans, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$2,816,160,718**. Net pension liability **$551,169,928**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **80.40 percent** (2024 **76.50 percent**). MD&A fair-value funded **80.4 percent** is that GASB card. Actuarial funded **82.0 percent** is not the GASB card. The transmittal “market value funded **82.0 percent**” print matches the actuarial card, not market value.
3. **Returns.** Required-supplementary money-weighted return **10.6 percent** for fiscal 2025, net of investment expense. MD&A time-weighted return **9.5 percent**. Those official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Baltimore City ERS into [Maryland SRPS](/maryland-srps/), [Montgomery County Employee Retirement Plans](/montgomery-erp/), [Howard County Retirement Plan](/howard-county-retirement/), [Baltimore Fire and Police](/baltimore-fire-police/), or [District of Columbia Retirement Board](/dcrb/). Maryland SRPS is the statewide file. Montgomery County is one county house file. Baltimore Fire and Police is the sworn city file. This is **one Baltimore civilian file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Baltimore ERS AUM” has to name the book. The **$2,264,990,790** line is net position restricted for pensions. The **$2,225,547,573** line is statement investments. The **80.40 percent** card is the GASB ratio. The **82.0 percent** card is the actuarial ratio. The **10.6 percent** card is the money-weighted return. The **9.5 percent** card is the time-weighted return. Those are not the same mandate.
