# How we rank asset owners: quote the filing — do not dollarize GPFG

A ranking method is a rule about sources. The live page is a leftover dollar table. Dated 7 July 2026, it still converts [GPFG](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/) **NOK 11.46 trillion** into **approximately $1.07 trillion as of end-2023**, reprints [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/) at **$460 billion as of 30 June 2024**, and says this desk converts non-USD books at OECD year-end rates. That conversion rule is how leftover dollars get back onto the site. This desk does not do that.

## What this desk will print

- **Quote the filing.** Use the house’s own currency and the date on the print. GPFG stays in kroner. GPIF stays in yen. CPP stays in Canadian dollars.
- **Do not convert to rank.** A leftover FX print is how **$1.07 trillion** and **$1.41 trillion** both appear for the same book.
- **Say no AUM when there is no AUM.** ADIA, KIA, QIA, SAFE, and GIC do not publish AUM. Absence is not a licence to estimate.
- **Keep families separate.** A pension, a SWF, an endowment, and a manager are different legal persons. Do not add them so a method page can show a **$130 trillion** climate pile.
