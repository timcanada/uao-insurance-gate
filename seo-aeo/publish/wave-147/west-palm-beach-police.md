# West Palm Beach Police Pension Fund, explained

The West Palm Beach Police Pension Fund is West Palm Beach’s sworn pension, not leftover Palm Beach Gardens Police, leftover Boca Police and Fire, and not leftover Florida SBA. The official [30 September 2025 and 2024 West Palm Beach Police Pension Fund financial statements](http://www.wpbppf.com/docs_state/FinancialStatements/2025%20West%20Palm%20Beach%20Police%20Pension%20FS.pdf) print fiduciary net position of **$528,055,662** at **30 September 2025**. Investments **$527,525,775** are not that card. Defined-benefit restricted **$345,020,016** is not total net position. DROP **$100,962,602** and Share **$82,073,044** are restricted portions, not second AUM. Money-weighted return was **9.23 percent** net of investment expense. GASB **84.16 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$627,450,618**. Net pension liability **$99,394,956**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **84.16 percent** (2024 **86.19 percent**).
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **9.23 percent** net of investment expense for the year ended **30 September 2025** (2024 **15.7 percent**).
