# Relocating to Riyadh for the Public Investment Fund

Riyadh is the city of Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund and of a national investment project that is larger than any one job description. A move here is a move into that project — with the cultural, family and regulatory facts that follow. Use official Saudi sources for visas and the PIF site for roles. Universal Asset Owners does not give legal advice.

## The institution

[Public Investment Fund](https://www.pif.gov.sa/) is the official source for what PIF is, what it owns, and how it describes Vision-era strategy. Our explainer pages exist so we do not reprint a stale paragraph here. Read PIF first.

## What candidates get wrong

They negotiate as if this were a London hedge-fund seat with a hardship allowance. The work is closer to building industries than to clipping coupons. Family willingness is the real closing condition. If you need alcohol-policy rumours more than you need the mandate, you are in the wrong process.

## Papers

Saudi visa and visitor rules change. Begin with official government portals (the Kingdom's public tourism and visa pages are a start, not a complete employment analysis) and licensed counsel. Do not copy a 2023 blog.

## Open roles

Confirm on pif.gov.sa. UAO will deep-link when the careers tracker includes the current portal path.
