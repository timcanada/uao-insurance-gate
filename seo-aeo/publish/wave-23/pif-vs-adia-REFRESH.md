# PIF vs ADIA: official prints, not a $2.3 trillion homemade pair

Public Investment Fund’s official size line is **more than US$900 billion**. Abu Dhabi Investment Authority does **not** publish AUM. The live comparison, dated 4 July 2026, still stacks them into **combined assets exceeding $2.3 trillion**, treats PIF as **$1.15 trillion as of mid-2025**, and assigns ADIA **$1.18 trillion**. Those are tracker dollars. They are not the official filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

PIF’s US$2 trillion figure is a **2030 KPI**, not current AUM. That is already locked on `/saudi-pif-s-road-to-2-trillion-by-2030/`. Do not recycle it here as a current size.

## What each house is

**PIF** is the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia’s public investment fund and a Vision 2030 vehicle. It publishes a “more than US$900 billion” size line. It does not publish a more precise current AUM that this desk can cite.

**ADIA** is Abu Dhabi’s reserve investor, founded **1976**. It publishes long-horizon returns and does **not** publish AUM. Putting ADIA in a dollar column so the two houses can be ranked is a league-table invention.

They are not interchangeable. PIF is a Saudi public investment fund with a domestic-development mandate. ADIA is an Abu Dhabi reserve investor that refuses a public AUM. A “largest in the Middle East” frame that assigns both a press dollar is not a filing.

## What is not official

- **Combined assets exceeding $2.3 trillion.** Live lede. No official combined figure.
- **PIF $1.15 trillion as of mid-2025** and **$925 billion at end-2024** as current AUM.
- **ADIA $1.18 trillion.** ADIA does not publish AUM.
- A homemade rank that says ADIA is fourth and PIF fifth.
- Treating the **$2 trillion by 2030** KPI as current size.
