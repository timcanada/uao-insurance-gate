# City of Fort Lauderdale General Employees’ Retirement System, explained

The City of Fort Lauderdale General Employees’ Retirement System is Fort Lauderdale’s civilian pension, not leftover Hialeah Employees, leftover Miami GESE, and not leftover Florida SBA. The official [30 September 2025 City of Fort Lauderdale General Employees’ Retirement System financial statements](https://www.citypension.com/pdfs/GERS%202025%20Audit.pdf) print fiduciary net position of **$755,226,097** at **30 September 2025**. Investments **$745,570,970** are not that card. DROP **$1,790,285** is a restricted portion, not a second AUM. Money-weighted return was **6.39 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. GASB **96.88 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Miami GESE overwrite.** Do not mint `/gers/`, `/fort-lauderdale-gers/`, `/fort-lauderdale-employees-explained/`, or `/ers/`. Do not mint `/gepp/`.

## What is official (30 September 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position, the GASB 67 note, and the money-weighted schedule are the sourced lines:

1. **Fiduciary net position** **$755,226,097** at **30 September 2025** (beginning **$750,864,375**). Total investments **$745,570,970**. Defined-benefit restricted **$753,435,812**. DROP **$1,790,285** (beginning **$797,702**) is held by the plan. Investments fell while net position rose. Do not treat leftover Hialeah Employees **$814,680,820**, leftover Miami GESE, leftover Lakeland Employees **$822,834,757**, leftover Florida SBA **$300 billion+**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$779,546,235**. Net pension liability **$24,320,138**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **96.88 percent** (2024 **99.25 percent**).
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **6.39 percent** net of investment expense for the year ended **30 September 2025** (2024 **15.62 percent**).

Do not flatten the City of Fort Lauderdale General Employees’ Retirement System into [City of Hialeah Employees’ Retirement System](/hialeah-employees/), [City of Miami General Employees’ and Sanitation Employees’ Retirement Trust](/miami-gese/), [City of Lakeland Employees’ Pension and Retirement System](/lakeland-employees/), or [Florida State Board of Administration](/florida-state-board-of-administration-explained/). Hialeah is a Hialeah city file. Miami GESE is a Miami civilian file. Lakeland Employees is a Lakeland civilian file. Florida SBA is the statewide investment file. This is **one Fort Lauderdale civilian file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Fort Lauderdale pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$755,226,097** line is civilian fiduciary net position at **30 September 2025**. The **$745,570,970** line is investments. The **$753,435,812** line is defined-benefit restricted. The **$1,790,285** line is DROP. The **6.39 percent** card is money-weighted. The **96.88 percent** card is GASB. Those are not the same mandate as Miami GESE.
