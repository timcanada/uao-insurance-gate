# Anne Arundel Fire Service Retirement Plan, explained

The Anne Arundel Fire Service Retirement Plan is Anne Arundel County’s firefighter pension, not leftover Employees, leftover Police Service, and not leftover Detention Officers’ and Deputy Sheriffs’. The official [31 December 2025 Anne Arundel County Retirement and Pension System combining statements](https://www.aacounty.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/acfr-pension-trust-funds-2025.pdf) print net position held in trust for pension benefits of **$874,797,882** at **31 December 2025**. Investments **$879,861,873** are not that card. DROP lump-sum value **$20,370,304** is not a second AUM. GASB **83.2 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Police overwrite.** Do not mint `/anne-arundel/`, `/ers/`, or `/anne-arundel-fire-explained/`. Combined System **$2,934,944,458** is not a fifth book. Assets are commingled for investment; “each Plan’s assets may be used only for the payment of benefits to the members of that Plan.”

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The combining statement of fiduciary net position, the notes GASB table, and the System money-weighted print are the sourced lines:

1. **Net position held in trust** **$874,797,882** at **31 December 2025**. Beginning **$756,857,698**. Change **$117,940,184**. Investments **$879,861,873**. Employer contributions **$40,098,672**. Participant contributions **$5,894,575**. Total net investment income **$118,073,327**. Benefit payments **$45,261,337**. Administrative expenses **$865,053**. DROP lump-sum value **$20,370,304** is a component, not a second AUM. Do not treat leftover Employees **$948,193,953**, leftover Police Service **$872,886,838**, leftover Detention Officers **$239,065,785**, leftover combined System **$2,934,944,458**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Notes total pension liability **$1,051,308,018**. Net pension liability **$176,510,136**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **83.2 percent**. The notes say the source is actuarial data based on preliminary financials. Print the combining-statement dollars. Do not treat **$1,051,308,018** as AUM. Discount rate **7.00 percent**. Combined System GASB **77.9 percent** is not this card.
3. **Returns.** Notes System money-weighted return **15.4 percent** net of System expenses for the year ended **31 December 2025** (2024 **10.5 percent**). Transmittal market **15.6 percent** and the RSI schedule **15.6 percent** are not a second System card and are not a per-plan money-weighted return. This page does not invent a Fire money-weighted return.

Do not flatten the Fire Service Retirement Plan into [Anne Arundel Employees’ Retirement Plan](/anne-arundel-employees/) or [Anne Arundel Police Service Retirement Plan](/anne-arundel-police/). Employees is the civilian file. Police Service is a different sworn file. This is **one Anne Arundel firefighter file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Anne Arundel fire AUM” has to name the book. The **$874,797,882** line is this Plan’s net position. The **$879,861,873** line is this Plan’s investments. The **$20,370,304** line is DROP. The **15.4 percent** card is the System money-weighted print. The **83.2 percent** card is this Plan’s GASB ratio. Those are not leftover combined **$2,934,944,458**.
