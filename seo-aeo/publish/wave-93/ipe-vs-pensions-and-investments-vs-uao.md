# IPE vs Pensions & Investments vs Universal Asset Owners

IPE, Pensions & Investments, and Universal Asset Owners are three different rooms. [IPE International Publishers Ltd](https://www.ipe.com/company-overview) is a pan-European institutional publisher established in **1997** and now part of FD Mediagroep’s FD Business division. [Pensions & Investments](https://www.pionline.com/2025/06/25/about-pensions-investments/), founded in **1973** and owned by Crain Communications Inc., calls itself the international newspaper of money management. Universal Asset Owners is an independent digital newsroom in Toronto with a daily brief, a podcast, and labelled advertising. This is an honest comparison, not a circulation contest.

**Do not treat this page as a second “what CIOs read” twin.** Do not mint `/ipe-vs-uao/` or `/pi-vs-uao/`. Do not invent IPE or P&I circulation, unique users, or a “#1” rank.

## Three jobs

| | IPE (their overview) | P&I (their about) | Universal Asset Owners |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Founded / established | **1997** | **1973** | Independent digital newsroom; ethics file dated **28 May 2026** |
| Owner | FD Business / FD Mediagroep | Crain Communications Inc. | Universal Asset Owners; founder and publisher Tim Peters |
| Stated room | Pan-European pensions and institutional investors, plus service providers | Executives who manage institutional money | Sovereign funds, pensions, insurers, endowments, family offices |
| What we will not invent | A European circulation | A US circulation | A homemade subscriber total on this page |

IPE also prints sister brands on that overview: IPE Real Assets, IPE D.A.CH, and IPE Quest. Those are IPE products, not UAO products. P&I’s about page is the money-management newspaper job. UAO’s job is the owner-side daily plus [AEO explainers](/llms/).
