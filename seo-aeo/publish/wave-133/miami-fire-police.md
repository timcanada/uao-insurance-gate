# City of Miami Firefighters’ and Police Officers’ Retirement Trust, explained

City of Miami Firefighters’ and Police Officers’ Retirement Trust is Miami’s sworn fire-and-police pension, not leftover Miami General Employees’ and Sanitation Employees’ Retirement Trust and not leftover Tampa Fire and Police. The official [30 September 2025 financial statements](https://www.miamifipo.org/Resources/a4e7dae5-cee6-49ac-ac2b-3718a3c509e5/2025%20Audit.pdf) print net position restricted for pension benefits of **$1,943,149,383** at **30 September 2025**. Statement investments were **$1,911,196,397**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return was **9.8 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **63.15 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

Securities-lending collateral **$62,759,531** is not fiduciary net position. Consultant calendar-year **14.2 percent** is not the fiscal-year statement card.
