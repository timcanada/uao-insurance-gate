# Southern Manatee Firefighters Retirement Plan, explained

The Retirement Plan for the Firefighters of the Southern Manatee Fire and Rescue District is that district’s firefighter pension, not leftover Tampa Fire and Police, leftover Miramar Fire, and not leftover Palm Bay Police and Fire. The official [30 September 2025 firefighter-plan financial statements](https://retirement.flcities.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2025-firefighter-pension-southern-manatee-fire.pdf) print net position restricted for pensions of **$83,968,008** at **30 September 2025**. Investments **$83,968,008** equal that card; the statement prints no cash or liabilities. Notes money-weighted return was **9.11 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. GASB **101.71 percent** is a net pension **asset**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Tampa overwrite.** Do not mint `/smfr/`, `/southern-manatee-firefighters/`, or `/southern-manatee/`. The district-wide fire-rescue financial statements are not this book.

## What is official (30 September 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position, the GASB note, and the money-weighted schedule are the sourced lines:

1. **Net position restricted for pensions** **$83,968,008** at **30 September 2025**. Beginning **$76,015,132**. Change **$7,952,876**. Total investments at fair value **$83,968,008**. DROP benefits totaling approximately **$4,541,000** sit inside net position, not a second AUM. Do not treat leftover Tampa Fire and Police **$3,407,822,026**, leftover Miramar Fire **$303,762,513**, leftover Palm Bay Police and Fire **$260,496,503**, leftover Boca Police and Fire **$613,033,507**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$82,556,041**. Sponsors’ net pension **asset** **$1,411,967**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **101.71 percent**. Do not treat **$82,556,041** as AUM. Do not treat the net pension asset as a second AUM.
3. **Returns and cash flow.** Notes money-weighted return **9.11 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 September 2025**. Employer contributions **$2,981,709**. Plan-member contributions **$244,588**. State contributions **$739,805**. Net investment income **$6,387,385**. Benefits and distributions **$2,357,181**. Administrative expenses **$43,430**. The independent auditor’s report is dated **4 May 2026**, CS&L CPAs, P.A.
