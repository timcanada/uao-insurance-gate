# Public Investment Corporation, explained

The Public Investment Corporation is South Africa’s state-owned investment manager, not leftover GEPF (**R2,651,120,439 thousand** funds and reserves). The official [PIC 2025 Integrated Annual Report](https://www.pic.gov.za/) prints assets under management of **R3.049 trillion** at **31 March 2025**. The GEPF client book **R2.677 trillion** is **87.80 percent** of that AuM, not leftover GEPF stationery. Print rand. Do not dollarize. Homepage **over R3 trillion** copy is not the card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover GEPF overwrite.** Do not mint `/pic-south-africa-explained/` or `/public-investment-corporation/`. **Never mint `/pic/`.** The Auditor-General of South Africa issued an unmodified opinion on the company annual financial statements dated **27 August 2025**, Pretoria.

## What is official (31 March 2025)

The AuM table is in **R’billion**. The company annual financial statements are in **R’000**. Those two books are not interchangeable.

1. **Assets under management** **R3.049 trillion** at **31 March 2025** (beginning **R2.691 trillion**). The official client table TOTAL end-market value is **3,049** (R’billion). Asset-class page **TOTAL: R3.049 trillion** is the same card. Narrative **over R3 trillion** is that card, rounded down. Do not treat leftover GEPF funds and reserves **R2,651,120,439 thousand**, leftover USS **£76.8bn**, leftover PFZW **€35bn**, leftover CIC **$1.3T**, leftover Alaska **$89bn**, leftover GPIF **15.83%**, leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing. Do not convert these rand lines into a homemade US-dollar AUM.
2. **Named books are not interchangeable.** GEPF client book **R2.677 trillion** (**87.80 percent** of AuM; 2024 **R2.368 trillion**). UIF **R170 billion**. Compensation Commissioner Fund **R71 billion**. Compensation Commissioner Pension Fund **R62 billion**. Associated Institutions Pension Fund **R21 billion**. Smaller clients **R48 billion**. Those sleeves sum to the **R3.049 trillion** card. The GEPF client book is not leftover [GEPF](/gepf/) funds and reserves. Company annual-financial-statement total assets **R4,919,285 thousand**, equity **R4,398,993 thousand**, and profit **R511,955 thousand** are the **SOC**, not AuM.
3. **Growth.** Official AuM growth **13.30 percent** including flows and **15.94 percent** excluding flows. Inflows **R92 billion**. Outflows **R163 billion**. Market movement **R429 billion**. The Auditor-General of South Africa, Pretoria, **27 August 2025**. Board approval of the company statements **25 August 2025**.
