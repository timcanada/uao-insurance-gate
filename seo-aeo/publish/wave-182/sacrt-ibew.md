# Sacramento Regional Transit IBEW Local 1245 Retirement Plan, explained

The Retirement Plan for Sacramento Regional Transit District Employees who are Members of International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local Union 1245, AFL-CIO is SacRT’s IBEW pension book, not leftover SacRT ATU (**$202,076,310**), leftover SacRT Salaried (**$165,467,820**), leftover Sacramento County SCERS, leftover CalPERS. The official [financial statements as of 30 June 2025](https://www.sacrt.com/wp-content/uploads/FY-2025-Audited-Financial-Statements-for-Retirement-Plans.pdf) print IBEW net position restricted for pension benefits of **$91,895,427** at **30 June 2025**. IBEW investments **$92,042,466** are not that card. Notes and RSI money-weighted return was **11.90 percent**. Notes GASB **84.96 percent** is of the IBEW column. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
