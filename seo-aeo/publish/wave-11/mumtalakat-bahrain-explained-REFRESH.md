# Mumtalakat (Bahrain), explained

Mumtalakat is the Kingdom of Bahrain’s sovereign wealth fund, established in 2006 as Bahrain Mumtalakat Holding Company. Its 24 May 2026 results release does **not** print an AUM. Official score: consolidated net profit attributable to the shareholder of **BHD 592.7 million for 2025**, on consolidated revenue of **BHD 2.5 billion**. That profit beat 2024’s **BHD 316.5 million**. This is not investment advice.

**Last updated:** 26 August 2026 · **Desk:** UAO Research · **Canonical URL:** `/mumtalakat-bahrain-explained/`

Mumtalakat’s own site still describes a portfolio of local and international companies across industrial names (Alba, NBB, Beyon). That is a **holding company**, not a liquid savings book. Do not convert BHD 592.7 million into a fake-precise US dollar AUM.

The 2025 profit lift, on Mumtalakat’s own telling, came from higher revenue **and** fair-value gains on **McLaren Racing**. Quote that as the company’s explanation, not as a UAO valuation of McLaren.

## What it is

Royal Decree 64 of 2006. Closed Bahraini joint-stock company. Chairman named on the 2026 release: **Shaikh Salman bin Khalifa Al Khalifa**, Minister of Finance and National Economy. CEO: **Shaikh Abdulla bin Khalifa Al Khalifa**.

2025 operating marks the release does print: listing of **Silah Gulf** on the Bahrain Bourse; **Amana Healthcare Bahrain** with M42; **Ghitha** Food Park with Edamah; Beyon sovereign cloud region with Oracle; Hawar Resort by Mantis; Alba 5-star British Safety Council rating; McLaren Racing’s 2025 Constructors’ and Drivers’ championships.

This is a **strategic holding SWF**, closer to [Temasek](/is-temasek-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/) or [ICD](/investment-corporation-of-dubai-icd-explained/) than to [NBIM](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/). Compare it with [PIF](/public-investment-fund-pif/) only after you have dropped the invented $18bn.

## How a peer should read it

- **No official AUM in the 2025 release.** Anyone leading with $18bn is reading a tracker.
- **Profit is the print.** BHD 592.7 million on BHD 2.5 billion of revenue. 2024 is no longer the record year.
- **Alba is the industrial core.** BHD 218.7 million of the group’s year sat there. NBB and Beyon are associates, not the whole book.
- **Fair-value years cut both ways.** McLaren Racing is named. Do not invent a McLaren carrying value.
