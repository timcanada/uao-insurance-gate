# West Palm Beach Restated Employees Defined Benefit Retirement System, explained

The official [30 September 2025 West Palm Beach Restated Employees Defined Benefit Retirement System financial statements](https://www.resourcecenters.com/pension%20funds/Select%20Your%20Fund/West%20Palm%20Beach%20General%20Employees'%20Pension%20Fund/26Required%20Documents%20and%20Disclosures/2025%20WPB%20General%202025%20Audited%20Financial%20Statement.pdf) print fiduciary net position of **$45,122,439** at **30 September 2025**. Investments **$46,315,512** are not that card. Advanced contributions from the employer **$1,507,276** are a deferred inflow, not net position. Money-weighted return was **6.97 percent** net of Plan investment expense. GASB **107.85 percent** uses plan fiduciary net position **$46,629,715**, which includes those advanced contributions.
