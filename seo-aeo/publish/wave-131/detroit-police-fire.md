# Police and Fire Retirement System of the City of Detroit, explained

Police and Fire Retirement System of the City of Detroit is Detroit’s sworn fire-and-police pension, not leftover General Retirement System of the City of Detroit and not leftover Michigan Public School Employees’ Retirement System. The official [30 June 2025 financial statements](https://cms2.revize.com/revize/rscdmi/Police%20and%20Fire%20Retirement%20System%20of%20the%20City%20of%20Detroit%20-%20June%2030%202025%20Financial%20Statements.pdf) print combined fiduciary net position restricted for pensions of **$2,822,919,237**. Component II Legacy defined-benefit net position was **$2,340,553,664**. Component I Hybrid defined-benefit net position was **$475,152,502**. Income Stabilization **$7,213,071** is not those pension columns. Time-weighted return was **12.04 percent** net of fees. Money-weighted return was **11.06 percent** net. Component II fiduciary net position was **76.91 percent** of total pension liability. Component I was **94.36 percent**. Those two GASB cards are not a combined funded ratio. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Detroit General overwrite or a leftover MPSERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/dpfrs/` or `/pfrsdetroit/`. Do not add Component I and Component II into a second AUM. Do not invent a combined funded ratio. The house **$2.8 billion** card is not the statement **$2,822,919,237** line.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Combined fiduciary net position restricted for pensions** **$2,822,919,237** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$2,719,643,650**). The change is **$103,275,587**. Component II Legacy defined-benefit **$2,340,553,664**. Income Stabilization **$7,213,071**. Component I Hybrid defined-benefit **$475,152,502**. Those columns are not interchangeable. Statement investment categories (global equities, global fixed income, real assets, private equity, and diversifying strategies) totaled **$2,721,097,423**. Cash and cash equivalents **$96,486,012** are not that investment total. Do not treat leftover Detroit General pension **$1,770,424,254**, leftover MPSERS **$94,365,768 thousand**, leftover Ohio Police & Fire **$21,256,675,219**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Component II total pension liability **$3,043,093,212**. Component II net pension liability **$702,539,548**. Component II fiduciary net position / total pension liability **76.91 percent**. Component I total pension liability **$503,572,364**. Component I net pension liability **$28,419,862**. Component I fiduciary net position / total pension liability **94.36 percent**. Do not invent a combined funded ratio. Income Stabilization is not the Legacy GASB book.
3. **Returns.** Time-weighted total-fund composite **12.04 percent** net of fees and expenses for the year ended **30 June 2025**. Notes money-weighted return **11.06 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Those official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Detroit Police and Fire into [General Retirement System of the City of Detroit](/detroit-grs/), [Michigan Public School Employees’ Retirement System](/michigan-mpsers/), [Ohio Police & Fire](/ohio-police-fire/), [Baltimore Fire and Police](/baltimore-fire-police/), or [Jacksonville Police and Fire](/jacksonville-police-fire/). Detroit General is the **civilian** city file. MPSERS is the Michigan **school** file. This is **one Detroit sworn fire-and-police file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Detroit police-and-fire AUM” has to name the book. The **$2,822,919,237** line is the combined fiduciary book. The **$2,340,553,664** line is Legacy defined benefit. The **$475,152,502** line is Hybrid defined benefit. The **$7,213,071** line is Income Stabilization. The **76.91 percent** card is the Legacy GASB ratio. The **94.36 percent** card is the Hybrid GASB ratio. The **12.04 percent** card is the time-weighted return. The **11.06 percent** card is the money-weighted return. Those are not the same mandate.
