# Khazanah Nasional, explained

Khazanah Nasional Berhad is Malaysia’s strategic investment holding company and **calls itself the country’s sovereign wealth fund**. Khazanah Annual Review 2026 printed net assets of **RM105 billion** and total assets of **RM156 billion at 31 December 2025**, with a **5.2%** return for 2025 and a **6.1%** seven-year rolling return. It **does not publish a USD AUM**. The live UAO page’s **USD 44 billion as of 2023** line is a dead print. This is not investment advice.

NAV is **total assets minus liabilities**. Do not call RM156 billion “AUM.” Do not invent a USD NAV from RM105 billion. [ADIA](/adia-abu-dhabi-investment-authority/) does not publish AUM; do not put a homemade ADIA dollar next to Khazanah.

## What KAR 2026 asked you to notice

Nation-building lines on the release, not a second NAV:

- Malaysia Airports (MAHB) privatisation completed; **104.4 million** passenger movements, **+11.2%** year-on-year.
- Dana Impak / Jelawang Capital: first five fund managers, more than **10** startups, more than **RM30 million** crowded-in capital.
- **RM330 million** deployed in 2025 for capacity and community (**RM2.7 billion** cumulative since 2006).
- K-Youth: **9,934** youths trained in 2025; more than **34,000** since 2023.

Do not add those programmes to RM105 billion.

## What is not official — do not put it back

- **USD 44 billion.** Dead size. Official is **RM105 billion NAV**.
- **“As of 2023.”** KAR 2026 is a **31 December 2025** print.
- A homemade **USD** conversion of RM105 billion or RM156 billion.
- Ranking Khazanah against tracker [ADIA](/adia-abu-dhabi-investment-authority/) or [Mumtalakat](/mumtalakat-bahrain-explained/) dollars. Neither house gave UAO an official AUM in the filings we will reprint.
- Sleeve weights (infrastructure / energy / technology) that are not on the KAR 2026 release.
- Minting `/khazanah-explained/` or 301-ing this page onto the Q&A.

## How a peer should read it

Khazanah is closer to [Temasek](/is-temasek-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/) or [Samruk-Kazyna](/samruk-kazyna-kazakhstan-explained/) — a **strategic holding SWF** — than to [NBIM](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/) or [GIC](/gic-singapore-explained/). They use the SWF label on their own stationery. The score they printed is **RM105 billion NAV**, **5.2%**, and a **6.1%** seven-year. Anyone still writing **$44 billion** is not reading KAR 2026.
