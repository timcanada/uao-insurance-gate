# What is an implementation statement?

A UK implementation statement is the annual report of how trustees acted on the stewardship policies in the statement of investment principles. It is not a journey plan and not a climate-vote category. The Pensions Regulator’s general code (in force 28 March 2024) requires governing bodies that must produce a SIP to produce an implementation statement, include it in the scheme’s annual report, and make it publicly available free of charge on a website. The statement must say how, and the extent to which, the stewardship policies were followed, describe voting behaviour including the most significant votes, and state any use of a proxy voter. This page is not a ranking and not compliance advice.

## The annual vehicle, not the policy

TPR’s [stewardship module](https://www.thepensionsregulator.gov.uk/en/document-library/code-of-practice/funding-and-investment/investment/stewardship) (general code in force **28 March 2024**) and the DWP [SRD II fact sheet](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/workplace-pension-scheme-shareholder-rights-directive-ii-fact-sheet/shareholder-rights-directive-ii-fact-sheet) are the sourced definition:

1. Governing bodies of schemes that must produce a SIP **must produce an implementation statement** setting out how they acted on the stewardship policies in that SIP. The IS must sit in the scheme’s **annual report** and be **publicly available, free of charge, on a website**. Legal hook: paragraph **30(1)(ca)** of Schedule 3 to the Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Disclosure of Information) Regulations **2013**.
2. In the IS, governing bodies must set out **how, and the extent to which**, in the trustees’ opinion, the scheme’s stewardship policies have been followed during the scheme year; **describe the voting behaviour** by or on behalf of the trustees, including the **most significant votes**; and **state any use of a proxy voter**.
3. **Relevant schemes** have extra contents: how the remainder of the SIP was followed; any legally required SIP review and any changes, with reasons; or, if no review happened that year, the **date of the last review**. The DWP clock: an IS reporting against the SIP should be in any annual report produced from **1 October 2020**. The voting-behaviour block was due no later than **1 October 2021**.

A [journey plan](/what-is-a-journey-plan/) is the funding and investment bridge to a low-dependency date. Climate-vote **categories** sit on [how owners disclose climate votes](/how-do-owners-disclose-climate-votes/). This page is the **annual statement**.
