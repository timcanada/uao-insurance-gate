# What is a tripwire in scenario analysis?

A tripwire is the piece of evidence that would force a scenario weight to move — a named fact, not a vibe. On Universal Asset Owners’ Probability Desk, probabilities are attached to a named outcome over a named window and are re-scored when new evidence lands. The tripwire is that evidence, written in advance so a board can see what would change the reading. It is not a trade signal and not a forecast.

**Do not treat this page as a second Probability Desk overwrite.** Do not mint `/what-is-a-tripwire/`, `/scenario-tripwire/`, or `/probability-desk-tripwire/`. Do not invent a house AUM to decorate a weight.

## What already sits in the method

[Methodology](/methodology/) (version 1.0, **28 May 2026**) defines the Desk: discrete named outcomes, stated windows, evidence on the page, and a re-score when new evidence lands. It also says we do not publish a probability without showing what informs it, and we do not present probabilities as forecasts of the future.

That is the tripwire test. If the post cannot name the fact that would move the weight up or down, it is not ready.

Live [membership](/membership/) describes the premium Probability Desk as probability-weighted **bull, base, and bear** scenarios with **tripwires**, evidence, and portfolio implications. Weekly, plus event-driven editions. Institutional Core starts at **$60,000 / year**. Nothing on that page is investment, legal, tax, or fiduciary advice.

## How to use one in an IC pre-read

1. Name the outcome and the window (the live **22 August 2026** Desk asked whether Brent prints **$100** by **30 September**).
2. Write the current weight and the date it was scored (**58%** on that edition, from spot **$93.26**).
3. Write the tripwire as a sentence a minute-taker can read: the official statement, the shipping print, the treaty text, or the filing that would force a re-score.
4. When the tripwire hits, re-score. Do not leave a stale weight on a press page or a board pack.

How to read the whole scenario — date, outcome, window, evidence, weight — is on [how to read a Probability Desk scenario](/how-to-read-a-probability-desk-scenario/).

## What a tripwire is not

- A price target
- A recommendation to buy or sell
- A sentence sold in the daily brief
- A substitute for [ethics](/ethics/) (version 1.0, **28 May 2026**): we do not promise a conclusion in exchange for access

Boards that want the private re-score after a major geopolitical event are on [Strategic Council](/what-is-a-strategic-council-membership-for-asset-owners/), not on this explainer.
