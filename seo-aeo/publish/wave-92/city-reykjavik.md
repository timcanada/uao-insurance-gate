# Relocating to Reykjavik for an LSR or Icelandic public-pension desk

Reykjavik is the official-address city of [LSR](https://www.lsr.is/en/about-lsr/welcome-to-lsr/), the Pension Fund for State Employees. Official visit line: **Engjateigur 11, 105 Reykjavík**. The fund’s origins are **28 November 1919**, when Act no. **72/1919** created Iceland’s first real pension fund. LSR is run in three divisions: **Division A** (1997), **Division B** (closed to new members at the end of 1996), and the **Private Pension Division** (**1 January 1999**). It is a public pension, **not** a sovereign wealth fund. A move here is often a move onto that file. Right to work sits with official Icelandic sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit elsewhere

- LSR’s own welcome and overview pages: Engjateigur 11; oldest Icelandic pension fund; three legally separate divisions. Official prints live on lsr.is. Do not invent or reprint a house AUM on this page. Do not mint `/lsr-explained/` or `/reykjavik-lsr/`.
- Division B is an older, state-guaranteed rights book. Division A is the main open book. The Private Pension Division is voluntary additional savings. Do not mash the three into one “Iceland SWF” desk. Do not mint `/iceland-pension/`.
- [ATP](/atp-denmark-pension-fund/) is Hillerød / Copenhagen. [Keva](/careers/cities/helsinki/) and VER are Helsinki. [NBIM](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/) is Oslo. Do not treat a Reykjavik postcode as a Nordic savings SWF.

Do not treat this city page as a minted LSR explainer. There is no UAO LSR institution slug. Do not create one.

## Why people underestimate the job

A 1919 public-pension file is not a 2016 wealth-fund logo with a different climate. Icelandic is the language of legitimacy even when the dealing language is English. Housing, weather, and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail.
