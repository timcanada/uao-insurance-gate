# Alberta Heritage Savings Trust Fund, explained

The Alberta Heritage Savings Trust Fund is a provincial savings fund, not a second AIMCo page and not a second Alaska page. The Government of Alberta’s official [Heritage Fund page](https://www.alberta.ca/heritage-savings-trust-fund) prints fair value of net financial assets of **$31.9 billion** at **31 December 2025**, a fiscal year-to-date return of **7.7 percent**, and an initial **1976** investment of **$1.5 billion**. AIMCo is the investment manager, not a second AUM. The **$250 billion by 2050** line is a government plan, not current assets. Print Canadian dollars. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second AIMCo overwrite or a second Alaska overwrite.** Those keepers are `/aimco-alberta-investment-management-corporation/` and `/alaska-permanent-fund-corporation-explained/`. Do not mint `/alberta-heritage-fund/`. Do not reprint leftover Alaska **$89bn** or leftover AIMCo dollars.

## What is official (Alberta.ca, 2025–26 Q3)

The *Alberta Heritage Savings Trust Fund Act* creates the mandate: prudent stewardship of savings from Alberta’s non-renewable resources for current and future Albertans. The government’s own page is the sourced split:

1. **Fair value of net financial assets** **$31.9 billion** at **31 December 2025**. The fund grew by **$383 million** in the quarter. Fiscal year-to-date return **7.7 percent**. From an initial **$1.5 billion** in **1976**.
2. **Income stays in the fund** unless a withdrawal is authorized by Treasury Board — the **2022–23** amendment. The government will deposit **$2.8 billion** for **2025–26**, using surplus cash from **2024–25**; Treasury Board approved the transaction on **7 July 2025**. That deposit is reported as an amount due from the General Revenue Fund, not as a second current AUM.
3. **Investment manager** is [AIMCo](/aimco-alberta-investment-management-corporation/). The Auditor General of Alberta audits. Quarterly reports live on [Open Government](https://open.alberta.ca/publications/4993570).

The **$250 billion by 2050** line is a provincial plan in *Renewing the Alberta Heritage Savings Trust Fund*. Treat it as a KPI, the way this desk treats PIF’s **$2 trillion by 2030** line — not as today’s book.

Do not flatten the Heritage Fund into AIMCo’s Total Fund. AIMCo manages more than one client. Do not invent an AIMCo AUM here.
