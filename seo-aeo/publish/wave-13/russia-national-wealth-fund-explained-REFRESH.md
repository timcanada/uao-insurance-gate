# Russia National Wealth Fund, explained

Russia’s National Wealth Fund is a **fiscal reserve managed by the Ministry of Finance**, not a globally diversified savings SWF. MinFin’s **1 May 2026** bulletin printed a volume of **13,213,192.6 million rubles** (5.6% of forecast 2026 GDP), equivalent to **US$176,456.8 million**. Liquid assets on Bank of Russia accounts were **3,625,531.4 million rubles**, equivalent to **US$48,417.5 million**. This is not investment advice.

MinFin calculates volume at Bank of Russia official FX rates and gold accounting prices for the day **before** the reporting date. That is why the ruble and dollar lines move together and why you do not overwrite them with a homemade rate.

## What sits in the book

The 1 May bulletin is a **placement list**, not a GPFG-style public-markets portfolio. Among the lines MinFin prints:

- Bank of Russia accounts: **185,210.6 million** yuan; **145,180.1 kg** of unallocated gold; **1,320.8 million** rubles
- VEB.RF deposits: **1,321,679.5 million** rubles
- Foreign-state debt on a separate government decision, no rating floor: **US$3,000.0 million**
- Domestic infrastructure and corporate bonds (Avtodor, GTLK, Rostec, housing-territory fund, and others)
- Domestic equities, several marked to the **30 April 2026** MOEX weighted-average, including **Sberbank 3,612,895.3 million rubles** and **VTB 343,728.9 million rubles**
- Subordinated deposits at GPB, Sberbank, VTB, Sovcombank

April 2026 flows on the same bulletin include new GTLK bonds, a VEB.RF metro deposit, a VTB preferred-to-ordinary conversion of 2014 NWF stock, and small transfers into the federal budget (including **0.1 million rubles** of pension co-financing).

This is a **budget-and-domestic-finance sleeve**. It is not [NBIM](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/).

## What it is

The Fund (Фонд национального благосостояния) is run by the **Ministry of Finance**. Liquid means **balances at the Bank of Russia**. The rest is already placed — much of it in Russian issuers, state companies, and policy deposits.

Two numbers matter for a peer:

1. **Total volume** (13.2 trillion rubles / MinFin’s US$176.5 billion).
2. **Liquid** (3.6 trillion rubles / MinFin’s US$48.4 billion).

The live page’s **$185 billion** treated the 2023 stock as if it were still an international savings SWF. After 2022 the international book is not the story MinFin is telling. The liquid sleeve is **about a quarter** of the printed volume.

Do not invent a 2026 “return %” from the income lines. Do not add Sberbank’s marked equity to the liquid sleeve. Do not put this fund in a [largest SWF](/largest-sovereign-wealth-funds/) table using the October 2023 18.1 trillion line.

Sanctions and frozen-asset politics are real. They are not a license to guess AUM. Use the dated MinFin bulletin.

## How a peer should read it

- **18.1 trillion rubles is dead.** Anyone still quoting October 2023 is teaching a pre-drawdown, pre-revaluation stock.
- **$185 billion is not MinFin’s May print.** Use **US$176,456.8 million**, and say it is MinFin’s equivalent.
- **Liquid is the usable number.** US$48.4 billion on BoR accounts is not US$176 billion.
