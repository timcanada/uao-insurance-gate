# Relocating to San Francisco for an asset-owner desk

San Francisco is a city pension, a family-office city, and a window other owners staff — not the headquarters of CalPERS. [SFERS](https://mysfers.org/) is the City and County’s retirement system. [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/) and [CalSTRS](/calstrs-explained/) sit in the Sacramento corridor. A move here is often a move into a public local file or a private family file, not a Sacramento board seat. Right to work sits with official US sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which do not

- [San Francisco Employees' Retirement System](https://mysfers.org/) — the City and County’s own system. Read that site. Do not invent an SFERS AUM on this page. Do not mint `/sfers/`.
- [CalPERS](https://www.calpers.ca.gov/) and [CalSTRS](https://www.calstrs.com/) are Sacramento-corridor houses. If the desk is there, read the [Sacramento city page](/careers/cities/sacramento/).
- Family offices and multi-family offices cluster here. The job is still [a family office](/what-is-a-family-office/), not a product shelf. Do not invent a family-office AUM. Do not mint `/family-office/`.

Global pensions and endowments staff San Francisco as a *window* more often than as HQ. Ask whether you are building a book or staffing a time zone.
