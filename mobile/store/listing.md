## Description

Universal Asset Owners is the phone terminal for long-horizon capital.

The same intelligence as universalassetowners.com — The Universal Owner in the morning and The Probability Desk in the afternoon — in four formats: read, watch, listen and chart.

WHAT YOU GET

• The live tape and New York clock, so you know which desk is on
• The daily brief and the afternoon scenario book
• Charts of the day from both desks
• Video briefings and the two daily podcasts
• People, institutions and themes from the public library
• The Command Center, the same signals view as the website
• A player that starts when Universal Asset Owners goes live on YouTube
• A card in the app when the morning brief drops

WHO IT IS FOR

Sovereign wealth funds, public pensions, endowments, insurers, family offices and the people who advise them. Written for institutions that think in decades, not trading days.

SUBSCRIBE

The daily brief is complimentary. New subscribers are reviewed by the editorial desk, same as the website.

Universal Asset Owners is a Canadian publication. This app loads the public Ghost library and the same YouTube live probe as the homepage. It does not execute trades or give personalised investment advice.
