# Trinidad and Tobago HSF: official Q4 2025 NAV, not US$5.76 billion

The Ministry of Finance quarterly investment report for October–December 2025 put the Heritage and Stabilisation Fund’s net asset value at **US$6,254.9 million** at **31 December 2025**. The live page still prints **about US$5.76 billion at the end of June 2024** and a lede of **roughly US$6 billion**. Those are stale prints. They are not the current official quarterly.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## Official numbers (Ministry of Finance, Q4 2025)

| Line | Official print |
| --- | --- |
| Legal name | Heritage and Stabilisation Fund (HSF) |
| Statute | Heritage and Stabilisation Fund Act, **2007** |
| NAV at 30 September 2025 | US$6,341.3 million |
| NAV at 31 December 2025 | **US$6,254.9 million** |
| Quarterly return, Q4 2025 | **2.69%** (outperformed SAA benchmark by 0.70 percentage points) |
| Withdrawal, Q4 2025 | **US$250.0 million** under section 15 of the HSF Act (2007) |

The Q4 decline versus Q3 is the official quarterly math: market moves **plus** the **US$250.0 million** withdrawal. Print the NAV. Do not invent a newer mid-2026 speech figure in place of the quarterly.

## What this is

The HSF is Trinidad and Tobago’s petroleum-revenue sovereign fund. The 2007 Act gave it a dual mandate: stabilise the budget when energy income falls short, and save a heritage endowment. The Ministry of Finance publishes quarterly investment reports and annual accounts. Those filings are the official NAV, return, and withdrawals.

## What is not official

- **US$5.76 billion at the end of June 2024.** Live table. Stale quarterly.
- **Roughly US$6 billion** or **US$6.1 billion** (September 2024 budget talk) as current AUM.
- A homemade per-capita from “1.4 million people.”
- A later unofficial estimate in place of the 31 December 2025 quarterly.
