# Korea Investment Corporation (KIC), explained

Korea Investment Corporation is the sovereign wealth fund of the Republic of Korea, established in **2005** under the Korea Investment Corporation Act. As of the end of **December 2025** it reported about **USD 232.0 billion** of assets under management and a **13.91%** total portfolio return. It manages public funds entrusted by the Korean government and the Bank of Korea. It is **not** the [National Pension Service](/national-pension-service-korea-explained/). The live UAO page’s **$190 billion / December 2023** line is a dead print. This is not investment advice.

**Last updated:** 26 August 2026 · **Desk:** UAO Research · **Canonical URL:** `/korea-investment-corporation-kic-explained/`

The live page this overwrites (21 June 2026) still says KIC was established “with approximately **$190 billion**” and that it invests “on behalf of the National Pension Service and Korean state reserves.” Official AUM at end-2023 on KIC’s own annual table was **USD 189.4 billion** — already a year-end stock, not the founding capital. End-2025 is **USD 232.0 billion**. NPS is a **different legal person** with its own book. Wave 3 drafted `/korea-investment-corporation-kic/`; that slug **404s**. Do not mint it.

## What is not official — do not put it back

- **$190 billion as of December 2023** as if it were still current. End-2025 is **USD 232.0 billion**.
- **“On behalf of the National Pension Service.”** NPS runs [its own fund](/national-pension-service-korea-explained/) (**KRW 1,848.7 trillion** at end-May 2026). Do not add the two.
- Homemade [CIC](/china-investment-corporation-cic-explained/) **$900 billion** or NPS **$700 billion**. CIC official is **US$1.57 trillion** total / **US$1.37 trillion** net at 31 December 2024. NPS does **not** get a USD AUM on this URL.
- Founding capital of **$17 billion** as a current fact without a KIC citation on the pages above.

## How a peer should read it

On global league tables KIC sits in the **hundreds of billions**, not the trillion-dollar Gulf / Norway / China cluster. It is still a first-class counterparty: official annual reporting, a published traditional/alternatives split, and a Seoul HQ. It is a [sovereign wealth fund](/sovereign-wealth-fund/) with a national-finance overlay. It is **not** a public pension owed to named Korean workers. That is NPS.
