# Technology and data for asset owners and family offices

Asset owners buy technology for a different failure mode than a hedge fund: a process that must survive a board, a regulator, a family, or a parliament. This hub covers risk, portfolio, data, cybersecurity and the family-office stack as Universal Asset Owners sees the buying process — not as a vendor catalogue. Paid directory features are labelled. Nothing here is a product endorsement.

## How an owner buys software

The demo is the least important hour. The hours that matter are: who owns the data; who can see the family or the beneficiary file; how an investment committee extracts a number that matches the custodian; what happens when the CIO is on a plane and the board wants a look-through.

Family offices add a constraint public funds do not always advertise: a principal who will not tolerate a leak. Cybersecurity is not an IT line item. It is estate risk.

Sovereign and pension desks add another: procurement, records laws, and the inability to “just put it in a founder Slack.”

## Categories we will list

- Portfolio and order management
- Risk, liquidity and look-through
- Private-markets data rooms and commitment tracking
- Cybersecurity and identity
- Family-office operating systems
- AI tools that a fiduciary can actually defend (most cannot — say so)

## For vendors

A concentrated morning list of CIOs is not a product-led-growth playground. If you want presence, buy a labelled unit and a claimed profile. If you want a story, have a story a reporter can source.
