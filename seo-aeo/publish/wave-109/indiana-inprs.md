# Indiana Public Retirement System, explained

The Indiana Public Retirement System is Indiana’s statewide retirement office, not leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** and not a second CalPERS page. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.in.gov/inprs/files/annual-reports/INPRS-Annual-Report-FY25.pdf) prints Combined fiduciary net position of **$54,866,050 thousand** (**$54.9 billion**) at **30 June 2025**. Defined Benefit fiduciary net position is **$46,470,474 thousand**. Defined Contribution fiduciary net position is **$7,902,158 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. Consolidated defined benefit assets returned **11.0 percent** time-weighted net of all fees. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second CalPERS overwrite or a leftover MassPRIM overwrite.** Those keepers are `/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/` and `/massachusetts-prim/`. Do not mint `/inprs/`, `/inprs-explained/`, or `/indiana-perf/`. Do not mint `/indiana-trf/` as a second INPRS page.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combining statement is the sourced split (dollar amounts in thousands):

1. **Combined fiduciary net position** **$54,866,050 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$49,951,911 thousand**). That total includes defined-benefit, defined-contribution, OPEB, and custodial books. Do not treat leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, leftover Oregon **$95 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books on the same statement.** Defined Benefit **$46,470,474 thousand** (PERF DB **$16,969,651 thousand**; TRF Pre-’96 DB **$9,931,090 thousand**; TRF ’96 DB **$9,357,088 thousand**; 1977 Fund **$9,154,994 thousand**). Defined Contribution **$7,902,158 thousand**. Investment-section consolidated defined-benefit fair value **$46,548,752 thousand**. The letter of transmittal also prints **$53.0 billion** for consolidated defined-benefit assets. That letter line is not the Statement of Fiduciary Net Position and is not the Investment Section fair-value line. Do not add those lines.
3. **Returns and funding.** Consolidated defined-benefit assets **11.0 percent** time-weighted net of all fees. Long-term target **6.25 percent**. Aggregate defined-benefit funded status **83.3 percent**. GASB defined-benefit fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **83.9 percent**. Those two funded-status lines are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten INPRS into [Illinois TRS](/illinois-trs/) or [Massachusetts PRIM](/massachusetts-prim/). Illinois TRS is Illinois. PRIM is Massachusetts. This is **one Indiana statewide file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Indiana pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$54.9 billion** line is Combined fiduciary net position. The **$46.5 billion** line is the Defined Benefit book. The **$7.9 billion** line is Defined Contribution. Those are not the same mandate.
