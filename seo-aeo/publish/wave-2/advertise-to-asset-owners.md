# How to advertise to sovereign wealth funds, pensions and family offices

You advertise to universal owners by standing in the rooms they already trust — not by buying retail impressions and hoping a CIO clicks. The buyers are law firms, banks, consultants, technology vendors, search firms and event hosts whose average mandate is large enough to justify $20,000–$50,000 a month in labelled presence, or display once a site has millions of views. Universal Asset Owners sells that presence. We do not sell the brief, the subscriber file, or a recommended-vendor stamp.

## What the buyer is actually buying

A chief investment officer does not “shop” the way a consumer does. The people who pay to be in front of that CIO are selling a seven-figure relationship: counsel on a commitment, a search for a deputy, a system the board will live with, a conference the IC will attend.

That is why generic display on a business-news homepage is a weak substitute, and why IPE, Pensions & Investments, Top1000funds and Campden still have rate cards. We are building the same *kind* of room, globally, with a digital-first desk and a registry.

## What does not work

- Sending a “thought leadership” PDF and asking us to run it as news
- Buying a sentence in the daily brief (not for sale)
- Targeting “rich people” lookalikes on consumer social
- Treating a royal family office like a celebrity endorsement
