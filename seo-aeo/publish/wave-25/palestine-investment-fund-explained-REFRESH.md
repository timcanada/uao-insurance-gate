# Palestine Investment Fund: 2003, no official AUM — do not invent QIA $520 billion

The Palestine Investment Fund’s own site says the fund was established in **2003**. It is a public shareholding company registered with the Ministry of National Economy. The homepage does **not** print an official AUM. The live page, dated 10 July 2026, still uses Palestine as a foil for invented peer dollars: QIA **approximately $520 billion as of 2024**, KIA **$800+ billion**, and Oman **approximately $17 billion**.

## What this is

PIF is a Palestinian public investment company. It is not QIA, KIA, or a Gulf petroleum SWF. The live page is right that the operating constraints are different. It is wrong to prove that point with dollars those houses will not print.

QIA and KIA **do not publish AUM**. That fact is already locked on [`/qatar-investment-authority-qia/`](/qatar-investment-authority-qia/) and [`/kuwait-investment-authority-kia/`](/kuwait-investment-authority-kia/). Oman’s current print is OIA, not a leftover **$17 billion** SGRF.
