# Fire and Police Employees’ Retirement System of the City of Baltimore, explained

Fire and Police Employees’ Retirement System of the City of Baltimore is Baltimore’s sworn fire-and-police pension, not leftover Baltimore City ERS, leftover Maryland SRPS, leftover Montgomery County Employee Retirement Plans, leftover Howard County Police and Fire, leftover Ohio Police & Fire, leftover Fairfax PORS, and not leftover District of Columbia Retirement Board. The official [FY2025 ACFR](https://www.bcfpers.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2025_Acfr_v3_150_High_New.pdf) prints fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits of **$3,467,209,097** at **30 June 2025**. Statement total investments were **$3,428,496,166**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Time-weighted return was **10.9 percent**. Money-weighted return was **10.53 percent** net of investment expenses. Fiduciary net position was **73.1 percent** of total pension liability. Actuarial funded **72.4 percent** is not the GASB card. ## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits** **$3,467,209,097** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$3,241,523,777**). The change is **$225,685,320**. Statement total investments **$3,428,496,166** is not fiduciary net position. The chairman **$3.49 billion** print is rounded, not the statement. DROP **$16,466,324** and DROP 2 **$55,019,841** are not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover Baltimore City ERS, leftover Maryland SRPS, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$4,741,590,099**. Net pension liability **$1,274,381,002**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **73.1 percent** (2024 **71.8 percent**). The market funded **73.1 percent** print is that GASB card. Actuarial funded **72.4 percent** is not the GASB card.
3. **Returns.** Investment-section and MD&A time-weighted return **10.9 percent** for the year ended **30 June 2025**. Notes money-weighted return **10.53 percent** net of investment expenses. Those official one-year prints are not interchangeable.
