# Relocating to Sacramento for CalPERS or CalSTRS

Sacramento is the headquarters corridor of California’s two giant public teachers-and-public-employee plans. [CalPERS](https://www.calpers.ca.gov/) sits here. [CalSTRS](https://www.calstrs.com/) sits in West Sacramento. A move here is a move into a public, rules-heavy owner whose board can be asked to sit in front of a legislature. San Francisco is a different file. Right to work sits with official US sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## The institutions

- [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/) — official site [calpers.ca.gov](https://www.calpers.ca.gov/). Careers: [calpers.ca.gov/careers](https://www.calpers.ca.gov/careers).
- [CalSTRS](/calstrs-explained/) — official site [calstrs.com](https://www.calstrs.com/).

Read those sites before you read a recruiter brief. Do not recycle a stale AUM from a slide. The current official prints live on the institution pages. This city page is the move, not a second keeper.

[SFERS](https://mysfers.org/) is the City and County of San Francisco. If that is the desk, read the [San Francisco city page](/careers/cities/san-francisco/).

## Why the posting is different

You are joining a public pension. The [fiduciary duty](/fiduciary-duty-explained-institutional/) is a file, not a slogan. Succession and a deputy hire are public processes — see our [public-plan succession](/careers/intelligence/succession-inside-a-public-plan/) note. Housing in the corridor and a household that will not move inland are the ordinary reasons offers fail.
