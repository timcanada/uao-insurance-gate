# City of Miami General Employees’ and Sanitation Employees’ Retirement Trust, explained

City of Miami General Employees’ and Sanitation Employees’ Retirement Trust is Miami’s civilian city pension, not leftover Miami Firefighters’ and Police Officers’ Retirement Trust and not leftover Tampa General Employees. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://gese.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/FYI-FINAL-ACFR-FY-2025.pdf) prints GESE Trust net position restricted for pension benefits of **$1,138,368,633** at **30 September 2025**. Statement investments were **$1,134,090,931**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return was **8.8 percent** net of investment expenses. Fiduciary net position was **78.90 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Miami Firefighters’ and Police Officers’ overwrite or a leftover Tampa General Employees overwrite.** Do not mint `/gese/` or `/miami-ge/`. Combined managed-trust net position **$1,146,746,936** includes Staff Plan **$8,378,303** and is not the GESE Trust line. Excess Benefit Plans have **no net position**. Staff Plan money-weighted **13.0 percent** and Staff Plan GASB **102.12 percent** are not the GESE Trust cards. Do not invent a combined funded ratio.

## What is official (30 September 2025)

The statement of plan net position is the sourced split:

1. **GESE Trust net position restricted for pension benefits** **$1,138,368,633** at **30 September 2025** (2024 **$1,067,106,515**). The change is **$71,262,118**. Statement investments **$1,134,090,931** is not fiduciary net position. Combined managed-trust net position **$1,146,746,936** is GESE Trust plus Staff Plan. Staff Plan net position **$8,378,303** is not the civilian book. Do not treat leftover Miami Firefighters’ and Police Officers’ **$1,943,149,383**, leftover Tampa General Employees **$929,228,201**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** GESE Trust total pension liability **$1,442,864,508**. Net pension liability **$304,495,875**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **78.90 percent**. Staff Plan **102.12 percent** is a different legal book. Excess Benefit Plan **0.00 percent** has no assets. Do not invent a combined funded ratio.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **8.8 percent** net of investment expenses for the GESE Trust for the year ended **30 September 2025** (2024 **24.0 percent**). Staff Plan money-weighted **13.0 percent** is not the GESE Trust card. The investment-section **8.8 percent** print uses that money-weighted method. Do not invent a separate one-year time-weighted card.
