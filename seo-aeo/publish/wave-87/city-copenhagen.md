# Relocating to Copenhagen for an ATP or Danish owner desk

Copenhagen is Denmark’s capital-markets floor. It is not ATP’s head office. [ATP](/atp-denmark-pension-fund/) Koncernen’s official [hovedsæde is Hillerød](https://www.atp.dk/om-os/organisationen). A move here is often a move onto a Danish statutory-pension or labour-market file — or into a satellite of that file. ATP is a lifelong supplementary pension, **not** a sovereign wealth fund. Official prints are **kroner**. Right to work sits with official Danish sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit in Hillerød

- [ATP](/atp-denmark-pension-fund/) — ATP Livslang Pension, Denmark’s statutory lifelong supplementary pension. Official organisation page: headquarters in Hillerød; administration centres also in Vordingborg, Haderslev, Holstebro, Frederikshavn and Aarhus. Official prints live on the keeper. Do not invent an AUM on this page. Do not euroize. Do not mint `/copenhagen-atp/`. Do not 301 this city page onto leftover `/atp-denmark-explained/`.
- Labour-market pension houses and insurer investment offices sit in this city. Confirm the legal person on the employer site. Do not invent a house AUM.
- [NBIM](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/) sits in [Oslo](/careers/cities/oslo/). Do not euroize GPFG to decorate a Copenhagen skyline.

Ask whether the investment committee sits in Hillerød. If it does, you are not at the centre.

## Papers

## Why people underestimate the job

A capital postcode is not the statutory book. Danish is the language of legitimacy even when the dealing language is English. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail.
