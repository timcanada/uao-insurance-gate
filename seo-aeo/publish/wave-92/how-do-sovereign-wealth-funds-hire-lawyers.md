# How sovereign wealth funds hire lawyers

A sovereign wealth fund retains counsel through a process that is slower, more political, and more conflict-sensitive than a company buying a deal lawyer. The general counsel or a named partner usually owns the relationship; the investment team owns the commercial problem; the state or board owns the residue that will be read in a newspaper. This explainer sits beside Universal Asset Owners' [pension-counsel page](/how-pension-funds-retain-counsel/). It is not legal advice and not a recommendation of any firm.

**Do not treat this page as a second SWF definition.** Do not mint `/swf-lawyers/` or `/sovereign-wealth-fund-counsel/`. Do not invent a house AUM to decorate a panel.

## The buying centre is not the deal team

A [sovereign wealth fund](/sovereign-wealth-fund/) is an owner, not a law-firm client in the ordinary corporate sense. On a private-markets commitment, the investment team can love a firm that has closed a hundred funds. The GC may still refuse them because they represent the manager. The ministry or board may still want a name it can defend when the deal is public.

[Pension funds](/how-pension-funds-retain-counsel/) already run panels. Many SWFs do too — fiduciary/governance, investments, litigation, sanctions, employment, real assets. Getting on the panel is procurement. Staying on it is matter-by-matter conflicts and bills that look like they were written for a legislature.

The [Santiago Principles](https://www.ifswf.org/santiago-principles) are a transparency and governance file, not a counsel shortlist. Do not quote them as a UAO ranking of firms.

## What “panel” usually means here

- A public pension panel can appear in a board pack a newspaper can request.
- A savings SWF panel is often quieter and still has a file.
- A strategic holding company — [TVF](/turkey-wealth-fund-explained/), [Temasek](/temasek-holdings-explained/), [Samruk-Kazyna](/samruk-kazyna-kazakhstan-explained/) — is a corporate-law problem first and a fund-finance problem second.
- Smaller or newer funds may have a trusted partner and a fear of switching. That is a different sale.

If you are a partner trying to “get in front of CIOs,” ask who holds the panel. If you are an investment professional trying to move fast, ask what the panel *cannot* do — then budget time.

## Documents that matter more than the pitch

- The last three similar opinions (redacted)
- The conflicts system, in writing — especially manager-side work
- Who signs, and who actually drafts
- How they handle a newspaper in the sponsor capital
