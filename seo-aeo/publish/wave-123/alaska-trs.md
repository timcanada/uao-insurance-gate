# Alaska Teachers’ Retirement System, explained

The Alaska Teachers’ Retirement System is Alaska’s teacher pension, healthcare, and defined-contribution book, not leftover Alaska PERS and not leftover Alaska Permanent Fund. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://drb.alaska.gov/docs/reports/TRSacfr_2025.pdf) prints combined fiduciary net position of **$12,039,978 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Total investments were **$11,914,233 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Defined-benefit pension fiduciary net position was **$6,478,048 thousand**. Defined-benefit pension fiduciary net position was **80.65 percent** of total pension liability. The defined-benefit pension return was **10.12 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Alaska PERS overwrite or a leftover Alaska Permanent Fund overwrite.** Do not mint `/aktrs/`, `/alaska-trs-explained/`, or `/alaska-teachers/`. Do not treat leftover Alaska PERS combined fiduciary net position **$26,772,591 thousand**, leftover Alaska Permanent Fund **$91,346,500,000**, or leftover Alaska **$89 billion** as this June 2025 teacher book.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Combined fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits, postemployment healthcare benefits, and individuals** **$12,039,978 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$11,311,574 thousand**), up **6.4 percent**. Total investments **$11,914,233 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$12,077,306 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat the combined **$12.0 billion** card as a pension-only fiduciary line. Those three prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover Alaska PERS **$26,772,591 thousand**, leftover Alaska Permanent Fund **$91,346,500,000**, leftover Alaska **$89 billion**, leftover Illinois TRS, leftover Minnesota TRA, leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion**, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books on the same statement.** Defined-benefit pension **$6,478,048 thousand**. Defined-contribution retirement **$1,247,636 thousand**. Alaska Retiree Healthcare Trust **$3,891,081 thousand**. Occupational death and disability **$9,879 thousand**. Retiree medical **$100,828 thousand**. Health reimbursement arrangement **$312,506 thousand**. Defined-benefit pension investments **$6,396,652 thousand** is not defined-benefit pension fiduciary net position. Those lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat defined-benefit pension as the combined book.
3. **Returns and funded cards.** Defined-benefit pension return **10.12 percent**. Alaska Retiree Healthcare Trust return **10.13 percent**. Defined-benefit pension total pension liability **$8,032,515 thousand**. Defined-benefit pension net pension liability **$1,554,467 thousand**. Defined-benefit pension fiduciary net position / total pension liability **80.65 percent**. Those official one-year prints and funded cards are not interchangeable.

Alaska PERS is a different legal person. The Alaska Permanent Fund is a sovereign savings file. This is a teacher retirement-system file.

Do not flatten Alaska TRS into [Alaska PERS](/alaska-pers/), [Alaska Permanent Fund](/alaska-permanent-fund-explained/), [Minnesota TRA](/minnesota-tra/), or [Illinois TRS](/illinois-trs/). PERS is the public-employee book. The Permanent Fund is the savings book. This is **one Alaska teacher file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Alaska TRS AUM” has to name the book. The **$12,039,978 thousand** line is combined fiduciary net position. The **$6,478,048 thousand** line is defined-benefit pension fiduciary net position. The **$11,914,233 thousand** line is investments. Leftover Alaska PERS and leftover Alaska Permanent Fund are different legal persons.
