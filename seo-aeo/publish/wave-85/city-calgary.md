# Relocating to Calgary for an AIMCo or Alberta owner desk

Calgary is an AIMCo office city, not AIMCo’s head office. [Alberta Investment Management Corporation](https://www.aimco.ca/)’s official [head office is Edmonton](https://www.aimco.ca/contact); Calgary is Fifth Avenue Place. A move here is often a move onto an Alberta public-client mandate — or into a satellite of that mandate. Do not mint `/aimco-explained/`. Immigration is federal. Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit up the road

- [AIMCo](/aimco-alberta-investment-management-corporation/) — a Crown corporation that invests for pension, endowment, insurance and government clients in Alberta. Official offices: Edmonton (head office), Calgary, Toronto, London, Luxembourg. Ask whether the investment committee sits in Edmonton. If it does, you are not at the centre.
- Alberta government and heritage-style capital sits in the same provincial file. Do not invent a house AUM on this page. Official prints live on the institution keeper and aimco.ca.

[CPP Investments](/cpp-investments-explained/) and [Ontario Teachers'](/ontario-teachers-pension-plan-explained/) sit in [Toronto](/careers/cities/toronto/). [PSP](/psp-investments-canada-explained/)’s principal business office is [Montréal](/careers/cities/montreal/). [BCI](/bci-british-columbia-investment-management/) is British Columbia — Victoria headquarters, Vancouver office. Do not flatten Canada into Calgary.

## Why people underestimate the job

Energy-town manners are not the mandate. AIMCo’s clients are Alberta public institutions. The [fiduciary](/fiduciary-duty-explained-institutional/) file is public. Housing and a household that will not move inland are the ordinary reasons offers fail — not the quality of the investment debate.
