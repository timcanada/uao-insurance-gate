# Relocating to Abu Dhabi for a sovereign-investor role

Abu Dhabi is one of the densest sovereign-capital cities on earth: ADIA, Mubadala, ADQ and related vehicles sit on the same balance-sheet family. A move here is usually a move onto a national investment mandate, not a regional banking tour. Confirm immigration and tax with official UAE sources and your own counsel. Universal Asset Owners publishes this as career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here

Start with the institutions' own sites, not a recruiter one-pager:

- [Abu Dhabi Investment Authority (ADIA)](https://www.adia.ae/)
- [Mubadala Investment Company](https://www.mubadala.com/)
- [ADQ](https://www.adq.ae/)

Related Abu Dhabi vehicles (including technology and AI-focused platforms) appear and re-brand. Use the official site on the day you apply. Career portals change faster than encyclopedias.

## Why the posting is different

You are closer to a government owner. Confidentiality is not a preference. The time horizon can be longer than a closed-end fund, and the industrial mandate can be more explicit than a university endowment. People who arrive expecting a London hours-and-carry package, with desert weather, do not last.

Our [crossing-to-the-owner playbook](/careers/intelligence/moving-into-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/) is the prior reading.

## Papers and public rules

The UAE's official portal is [u.ae](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services). Visa classes, dependent rules and tax guidance belong there — or with licensed counsel — not in a careers paragraph that will go stale.

## Practical questions people actually ask

- Will the role sit in Abu Dhabi, Dubai, or a deal team that lives on a plane?
- Is Arabic required for *this* seat, or only for the institution's public face?
- What does “housing” mean in the offer — and what does school mean if you have children?
- How many people in the last incoming class are still there in year four?

If a search firm cannot answer those, they are filling a spec, not placing you.
