# Palm Beach Gardens Police Officers’ Pension Fund, explained

The Palm Beach Gardens Police Officers’ Pension Fund is Palm Beach Gardens’ sworn police pension, not leftover Hialeah Employees, leftover Pompano Beach Police and Fire, and not leftover Florida SBA. The official [30 September 2025 and 2024 Palm Beach Gardens Police Officers’ Pension Fund financial statements](https://www.pbgfl.gov/DocumentCenter/View/3409) print fiduciary net position of **$160,495,301** at **30 September 2025**. Investments **$158,817,883** are not that card. Money-weighted return was **10.48 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. GASB **103.61 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
