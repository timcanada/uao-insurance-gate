# Nebraska State Patrol Retirement Plan, explained

The Nebraska State Patrol Retirement Plan is Nebraska’s sworn patrol pension, not leftover Nebraska School Employees and not leftover Ohio Police and Fire. The official [30 June 2025 Auditor of Public Accounts report](https://auditors.nebraska.gov/APA_Reports/2026/SA851-03022026-July_1_2024_through_June_30_2025_Audit_Report.pdf) prints fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits of **$647,545,587** at **30 June 2025**. Investments at fair value were **$650,372,500**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return was **11.79 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **88.42 percent** of total pension liability. School **107.17 percent** is not this card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Nebraska School overwrite or a leftover Ohio Police and Fire overwrite.** Do not mint `/nebraska-patrol/`, `/nsp-retirement/`, or `/nebraska-state-patrol-explained/`. Investments **$650,372,500** are not fiduciary net position. School money-weighted **11.65 percent** is not this card. DROP investment values are not a second AUM.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The State Patrol column on the statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits** **$647,545,587** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$588,340,252**). The change is **$59,205,335**. Total investments at fair value **$650,372,500** is not fiduciary net position. Securities-lending collateral **$35,760,791** is not fiduciary net position. Member contributions **$4,323,828** and State Patrol employer contributions **$10,333,217** are additions, not AUM. Those contribution cards already reflect the LB 196 split the auditor corrected. Do not treat leftover Nebraska School **$18,532,685,257**, leftover Nebraska Judges **$285,310,148**, leftover Ohio Police and Fire combined **$21,256,675,219**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$732,345,270**. Net pension liability **$84,799,683**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **88.42 percent** (2024 **88.06 percent**). School **107.17 percent** and Judges **110.23 percent** are not this card. Benefit-term changes **$23,958,015** sit inside the liability roll-forward, not inside fiduciary net position.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **11.79 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **13.44 percent**). School and Judges **11.65 percent** are not this card. This filing does not print a separate official one-year time-weighted house card.

Do not flatten the Nebraska State Patrol Retirement Plan into [Nebraska School Employees Retirement Plan](/nebraska-school/), [Nebraska Judges Retirement Plan](/nebraska-judges/), [Ohio Police & Fire](/ohio-police-fire/), or [Ohio OPERS](/ohio-opers/). School and Judges are different legal columns on the same audit. Ohio Police and Fire is an Ohio statewide sworn file. This is **one Nebraska patrol file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Nebraska patrol AUM” has to name the book. The **$647,545,587** line is State Patrol fiduciary net position. The **$650,372,500** line is State Patrol investments. The **88.42 percent** card is the GASB ratio. The **11.79 percent** card is money-weighted. Those are not the same mandate.
