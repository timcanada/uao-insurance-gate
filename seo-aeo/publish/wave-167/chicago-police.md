# Policemen’s Annuity and Benefit Fund of Chicago, explained

The Policemen’s Annuity and Benefit Fund of Chicago is the City of Chicago sworn-police pension, not leftover Chicago Fire FABF, leftover Chicago LABF, and not leftover Chicago Teachers. The official [31 December 2025 and 2024 Policemen’s Annuity and Benefit Fund of Chicago financial statements](https://chipabf.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/final_PABF_FS_2025.pdf) print net position held in trust for pension benefits of **$4,993,424,701** at **31 December 2025**. Investments **$3,909,058,908** are not that card. Notes money-weighted return was **13.85 percent** net of investment expense. GASB **26.44 percent** is not statutory fair-value funding **26.66 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Chicago Fire overwrite.** Do not mint `/pabf/`, `/chicago-pabf/`, `/chipabf/`, or `/chicago-police-explained/`. The 31 December 2025 valuation and GASB 67/68 reports are not this audit.
