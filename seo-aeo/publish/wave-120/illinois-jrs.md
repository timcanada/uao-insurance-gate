# Judges’ Retirement System of Illinois, explained

The Judges’ Retirement System of Illinois is Illinois’s statewide judges’ pension, not leftover Illinois SERS and not leftover Illinois TRS. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://ilsrs.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/srs/jrs/resources/financial-report/jrs-2025annual.pdf) prints fiduciary net position restricted for pensions of **$1,470,253,087** at **30 June 2025**. Investments held in the Illinois State Board of Investment commingled fund were **$1,454,986,212**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The Illinois State Board of Investment commingled-fund return was **9.7 percent**. The System’s money-weighted return on that commingled book was **7.7 percent**. Fiduciary net position was **45.72 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Illinois SERS overwrite or a leftover Illinois TRS overwrite.** Do not mint `/jrs/`, `/illinois-jrs-explained/`, or `/judges-retirement-system/`. Do not mint `/sers/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Fiduciary net position restricted for pensions** **$1,470,253,087** at **30 June 2025** (previously presented beginning **$1,398,392,002**; restated beginning after GASB 101 **$1,398,391,257**). Investments held in the Illinois State Board of Investment commingled fund **$1,454,986,212** is not fiduciary net position. Total investments including securities-lending collateral **$1,458,308,212** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$1,473,870,626** is not fiduciary net position. Those four lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover Illinois SERS, leftover Illinois TRS, leftover SURS, leftover Illinois State Board of Investment as a second AUM, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$3,215,794,931**. Net pension liability **$1,745,541,844**. Fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **45.72 percent**. The MD&A funded-status card **45.7 percent** is the same ratio rounded. Those cards are not a second investment book. The Illinois State Board of Investment commingled fund is the investment vehicle, not a second Judges’ Retirement System AUM.
3. **Returns.** Illinois State Board of Investment commingled-fund overall rate of return **9.7 percent**. System money-weighted return on its investment in that commingled fund **7.7 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Those two official one-year prints are not interchangeable. The System’s return differs from the pool because cash went in and out of the commingled fund during the year.

Do not flatten the Judges’ Retirement System into [Illinois SERS](/illinois-sers/), [Illinois TRS](/illinois-trs/), [SURS](/illinois-surs/), [Illinois State Board of Investment](/illinois-isbi/), or [Illinois GARS](/illinois-gars/). ISBI manages this book. Illinois SERS is the statewide state-employee file. Illinois TRS is the statewide teacher file. SURS is the public-university file. GARS is the General Assembly file. This is **one Illinois judges’ file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Illinois JRS AUM” has to name the book. The **$1,470,253,087** line is fiduciary net position restricted for pensions. The **$1,454,986,212** line is the ISBI commingled investment. Those are not the same mandate.
