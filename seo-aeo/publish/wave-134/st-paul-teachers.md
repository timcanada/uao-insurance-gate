# St. Paul Teachers’ Retirement Fund Association, explained

The St. Paul Teachers’ Retirement Fund Association is St. Paul’s teacher pension, not leftover Minnesota TRA and not leftover Minnesota PERA. The official [30 June 2025 financial statements](https://www.osa.state.mn.us/media/kydgqmei/saintpaulteachers-retirementfundassociationfsml_25_report.pdf) print net position restricted for pensions of **$1,433,936,404** at **30 June 2025**. Statement investments were **$1,451,130,406**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return was **10.48 percent** net of fees. Fiduciary net position was **72.19 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Minnesota TRA overwrite or a leftover Minnesota PERA overwrite.** Do not mint `/sptrfa/` or `/st-paul-teachers-explained/`. Statement investments **$1,451,130,406** are not fiduciary net position. Securities-lending collateral is not fiduciary net position. The condensed **$1,433,936 thousand** print is the same statement, not a second AUM. The Minnesota State Board of Investment pool holding is inside this book, not leftover Minnesota SBI as a second AUM.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position restricted for pensions** **$1,433,936,404** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$1,335,037,000**). The change is **$98,899,404**. Statement investments **$1,451,130,406** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$1,473,242 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover Minnesota TRA **$31,261,375 thousand**, leftover Minnesota PERA **$47,438,420 thousand**, leftover TCRS **$74,910,040,763**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion**, leftover City of Knoxville Pension System **$758,195,199**, or leftover Newport News Employees’ Retirement Fund **$1,150,643,142** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$1,986,406 thousand**. Fiduciary net position **$1,433,936 thousand**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **72.19 percent** (2024 **69.06 percent**). The thousands prints are the GASB schedule, not a second statement.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **10.48 percent** net of fees for the year ended **30 June 2025**. Management-discussion market-value return of about **10.5 percent** net of fees is not the money-weighted card. Actuarial-value return **10.0 percent** is not money-weighted.
