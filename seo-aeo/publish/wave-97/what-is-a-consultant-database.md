# What is a consultant database?

A consultant database is the search file a pension or other owner uses to find external managers — not a ranking and not an OCIO. CalPERS says it searches public-markets managers through Nasdaq’s eVestment, and that a manager must be registered there with complete strategy and contact data. The same official page prints the minimum qualifications. This page is not a vendor endorsement and not investment advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second OCIO definition or a second manager-selection overwrite.** Those keepers are `/what-is-an-ocio/` and `/manager-selection-institutional/`. Do not mint `/consultant-database-explained/`, `/evestment-explained/`, or `/what-is-evestment/`. Do not invent an eVestment AUM or call the file a Universal Asset Owners product.

## What CalPERS already prints

CalPERS’ [public investment manager opportunities](https://www.calpers.ca.gov/investments/investment-manager-opportunities/public-investment-manager-opportunities) page is the sourced example. Three facts from that text:

1. CalPERS **continuously searches** for external managers of publicly traded securities **through Nasdaq’s eVestment database**.
2. Interested managers **must be registered** in that database with **updated and complete strategy and contact information**.
3. The minimum qualifications printed on that page are:

| Qualification | Global public equity | Global fixed income |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Firm history | 5 years | 10 years |
| Performance history for the proposed strategy | 5 years | 5 years |
| GIPS-compliant composites | Yes | Yes |
| Firm assets under management | $3B | $5B |
| Strategy assets under management | $500M | $1B |
| SEC-registered adviser (or equivalent non-U.S. regulator) **or** a bank, insurance company, or trust company | Yes | Yes |

Managers who miss those minima can still submit a proposal directly. That is CalPERS’ process, not a Universal Asset Owners rule.

A consultant database is the file. Manager selection is the decision. An OCIO is a rented CIO office. Flattening those three jobs is how a pitch deck gets into a board pack that cannot be minuted.

## What an owner is actually buying

A public pension buys a process the board can defend: who was in the universe, who met the screen, who was interviewed. The file is how the universe is built.

A family office or a sovereign desk may use a different file, a consultant’s own book, or no commercial database at all. Do not paste CalPERS’ eVestment rule onto a ministry or a principal.
