# Invite Universal Asset Owners — event coverage and journalism in the room

Universal Asset Owners covers conferences, roundtables and closed-door allocator meetings as a newsroom: we send a journalist, we report what is on the record, and we do not sell the story. Event hosts who want UAO in the programme should write Press or Partnerships — not Sales — and should expect the same firewall as the daily brief. Sponsored presence at an event is a labelled commercial product and a separate conversation.

## Why hosts ask

A room of CIOs wants a serious outlet in the hall, not only a livestream vendor. A quote from a named desk travels. A reporter who understands a sovereign mandate will not ask a pension CIO what ticker they like.

That is the journalistic product. It is how peers learn we are a media company.

## How to request coverage

Email info@universalassetowners.com with **Press — event** in the subject. Include:

- Date, city, and whether it is on the record
- Who is in the room (owner types, not a boast)
- Whether you want a correspondent, a podcast tape, or a written note
- Any embargo

We may say no. Capacity is finite. A “no” is not a rate negotiation.

## What you can buy (labelled)

- A branded reception or a labelled programme line — see [Advertise](/advertise/)
- A directory or newsletter adjacency in the weeks around the event
- A private briefing *for owners who are already in our membership*, not a rental of the list

You cannot buy a newsroom conclusion.

## Masthead

Correspondents are listed on the [masthead](/masthead/). Ask for the region that matches the event (Gulf, Asia, Canada, Europe, Americas).
