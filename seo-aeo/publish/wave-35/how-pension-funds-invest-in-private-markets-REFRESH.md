# Pension private markets: no official 11.5% average — and NBIM is not $2 trillion

Pensions reach private markets through funds, co-investments, and in-house deals. That channel list is real. The live dollar table is not. The live page still invents an **11.5%** average large-public-pension private-equity allocation at year-end **2025** (up from **8.2%** a decade earlier), then files [CPP Investments](/cpp-investments-explained/) at **~C$700 billion**, [OTPP](/ontario-teachers-pension-plan-explained/) at **~C$250 billion**, [OMERS](/omers-explained/) at **~C$130 billion**, and [NBIM](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/) at **~$2 trillion**, plus a global private-credit pile of **approximately US$1.7 trillion**. Those leftovers are not the current filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## What the page can say

- **Three channels.** A limited-partnership commitment, a co-investment next to a GP, and a deal the house originates itself. Name the channel. Then quote a filing, or say this desk did not fetch that house this wake.
- **Who can print scale.** CPP, OTPP, and OMERS file official AUM. GPFG files kroner and is not a pension.
- **What an industry average is not.** A leftover **11.5%** is not a filing.

## What is not official

- CPP **C$700 billion**; OTPP **C$250 billion**; OMERS **C$130 billion**.
- NBIM **$2 trillion**.
