# Relocating to Istanbul for a Türkiye Wealth Fund desk

Istanbul is the official headquarters city of [Türkiye Wealth Fund](/turkey-wealth-fund-explained/). Official contact: **Muallim Naci Cad. No. 22, Ortaköy, İstanbul**. Law No. **6741** was accepted on **19 August 2016** and published on **26 August 2016**. The founding decree puts the company’s head office in Istanbul; an [Ankara office](https://www.tvf.com.tr/en/contact/contact-info) also exists (Beştepe Mah. 32. Cadde No: 1, Moment Ofis Kat: 13, Yenimahalle). TVF is an **asset-backed development fund**, not a savings sovereign wealth fund with a dollars-in-the-ground NAV. A move here is often a move onto that holding file. Right to work sits with official Turkish sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Why people underestimate the job

A 2016 holding law is not a 1953 reserve board with a different logo. Turkish is the language of legitimacy even when the dealing language is English. Housing on the European side and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail.
