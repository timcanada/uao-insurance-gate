# PIF vs Mubadala: official prints, not $1.15 trillion versus a homemade Gulf pile

Public Investment Fund's official size line is **more than US$900 billion**. Mubadala Investment Company's official year-end 2025 print is **AED 1.4 trillion / US$385 billion**. Abu Dhabi Investment Authority does **not** publish AUM. The live comparison, dated 4 July 2026, still treats PIF as **$1.15 trillion as of mid-2025**, ADIA as **$1.18 trillion**, and invents a **$700 billion** deployment figure and a **$500 billion** NEOM cost. Those are press and consultant numbers. They are not the official filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## What each house is

**PIF** is the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia's public investment fund and a Vision 2030 vehicle. It publishes a "more than US$900 billion" size line. It does not publish a more precise current AUM that this desk can cite.

**Mubadala** is an Abu Dhabi sovereign investor. Its year-end 2025 materials print **AED 1.4 trillion / US$385 billion**. Use that pair. Do not invent a newer dollar.

**ADIA** is a separate Abu Dhabi sovereign investor. It publishes 20-year and 30-year returns (6.3 percent and 7.1 percent) and does **not** publish AUM. Putting ADIA in this comparison as a third dollar column is a league-table invention.

They are not interchangeable. PIF is a Saudi public investment fund. Mubadala is an Abu Dhabi development-and-investment house. ADIA is an Abu Dhabi reserve investor that refuses a public AUM. A "Gulf SWF vs Gulf SWF" frame that assigns all three a press dollar is not a filing.

## What is not official

- **PIF $1.15 trillion as of mid-2025.** Live lede. Press, not PIF.
- **ADIA $1.18 trillion.** Live comparison. ADIA does not publish AUM.
- **$700 billion to deploy.** Not an official PIF current-AUM or deployment filing this desk can cite.
- **$500 billion NEOM.** A widely repeated project figure. Not a PIF AUM line.
- A homemade PIF + Mubadala combined AUM.
