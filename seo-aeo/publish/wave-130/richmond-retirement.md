# Richmond Retirement System, explained

Richmond Retirement System is the City of Richmond, Virginia pension trust, not leftover Virginia Retirement System, leftover Fairfax ERS, leftover Fairfax PORS, leftover Fairfax URS, leftover Norfolk Employees’ Retirement System, leftover Maryland SRPS, leftover Pennsylvania SERS, and not leftover PSERS. The official [FY2025 ACFR](https://rva.gov/sites/default/files/2025-11/ACFR2025.pdf) prints net position restricted for pensions of **$883,849,545** at **30 June 2025**. Statement total investments at fair value were **$896,991,528**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Time-weighted return was **10.5 percent** net of fees. Money-weighted return was **10.5 percent** net of investment management expenses. Those official one-year prints are not interchangeable. Fiduciary net position was **84.9 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover VRS overwrite or a leftover Fairfax overwrite.** Do not mint `/richmond-rrs/` or `/rrs/`. The transmittal portfolio-including-cash **$901.3 million** card is not fiduciary net position.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position restricted for pensions** **$883,849,545** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$819,178,952**). Statement total investments at fair value **$896,991,528** is not fiduciary net position. The transmittal portfolio “including cash” **$901.3 million** print is not fiduciary net position and is not the statement investments line. DROP payable **$16,344,851** is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover VRS, leftover Fairfax ERS, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$1,041,237,304**. Employer’s net pension liability **$157,387,759**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **84.9 percent** (2024 **80.9 percent**).
3. **Returns.** Investment-section time-weighted return **10.5 percent** net of fees, against the custom benchmark **10.3 percent**. Required-supplementary money-weighted return **10.5 percent** net of investment management expenses. Those official one-year prints happen to match and are still not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Richmond Retirement System into [Virginia Retirement System](/virginia-retirement-system/), [Fairfax ERS](/fairfax-ers/), [Fairfax PORS](/fairfax-pors/), [Fairfax URS](/fairfax-urs/), or [Norfolk Employees’ Retirement System](/norfolk-employees/). VRS is the statewide file. Fairfax ERS, PORS, and URS are Fairfax County files. Norfolk is a different Virginia city file. This is **one Richmond city pension-trust file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Richmond Retirement AUM” has to name the book. The **$883,849,545** line is net position restricted for pensions. The **$896,991,528** line is statement investments. The **$901.3 million** card is the transmittal portfolio including cash. The **84.9 percent** card is the GASB ratio. The **10.5 percent** time-weighted card is not the **10.5 percent** money-weighted card. Those are not the same mandate.
