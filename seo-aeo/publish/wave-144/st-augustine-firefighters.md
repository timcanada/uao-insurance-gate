# City of St. Augustine Municipal Firefighters’ Pension Trust Fund, explained

The City of St. Augustine Municipal Firefighters’ Pension Trust Fund is St. Augustine’s firefighter pension, not leftover Jacksonville Police and Fire, leftover Tampa Fire and Police, leftover Miami Fire and Police, and not leftover Largo Police and Fire. The official [30 September 2025 City of St. Augustine Municipal Firefighters’ Pension Trust Fund financial statements](https://www.citystaug.com/DocumentCenter/View/9194/2025-Financial-Statements---City-of-St-Augustine-Municipal-Firefighters-Pension-PDF) print net position restricted for pension benefits of **$23,047,296** at **30 September 2025**. Investments **$23,019,246** are not that card. Money-weighted return was **14.26 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Fiduciary net position exceeds total pension liability. GASB **105.64 percent** is a net pension asset card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$21,816,714**. City’s net pension asset **$1,230,582**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **105.64 percent**.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **14.26 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 September 2025** (2024 **26.58 percent**).
