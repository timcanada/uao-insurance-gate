# Advertiser revenue — getting to $20–50k / month

## The sale in one paragraph

Universal Asset Owners is a reviewed morning room of the people who allocate permanent capital. Circulation is small by design; the desks are not. A law firm, search firm, bank, or technology vendor that lives or dies on a handful of institutional relationships already pays conference and IPE-class money. We sell **labelled presence in that room**, plus careers and directory utilities, with a readership audit available under NDA.

## Why $20–50k/month is plausible

Historical kits in this industry (Top1000funds leaderboards ~$120k; P&I cover wraps ~$90k; supplements $30–50k) already sit at or above that monthly run-rate when annualized. UAO should not discount to content-farm CPMs. We should be *clearer* than those kits: audit methodology, labelled inventory, and products a partnership can vote on.

Membership ($60k–$300k+/year) is a **parallel** revenue line for owners and sophisticated service-provider research teams. Do not confuse a bank’s research subscription with their brand advertising. Some firms will buy both.

## Firewall

- Sales does not brief reporters on what to write.
- Featured career placements are labelled.
- Directory “featured” ≠ editorial “recommended.”
- If a sponsor is in a story, the ethics page already says how that works — follow it.
