# West Virginia Consolidated Public Retirement Board, explained

The West Virginia Consolidated Public Retirement Board is West Virginia’s combined retirement administrator, not leftover Pennsylvania PSERS and not leftover Virginia VRS. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.wvretirement.com/Forms/2025-ACFR.pdf) prints Public Employees’ Retirement System net position of **$9,701,065 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Teachers’ Retirement System was **$10,809,181 thousand**. Teachers’ Defined Contribution was **$784,650 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. Combined investment assets across the ten systems were **$23,600,607 thousand**. Nine defined-benefit systems had investments of approximately **$22.82 billion**. PERS money-weighted return was **11.04 percent**. TRS money-weighted return was **11.03 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Pennsylvania PSERS overwrite or a second Virginia VRS page.** Do not mint `/cprb/`, `/wv-cprb/`, or `/west-virginia-cprb-explained/`. Do not mint `/west-virginia-pers/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The MD&A plan cards and the investment table are in **thousands**. The assets of each system are available only to that system. The Total Pension Funds column is informational only:

1. **Named books.** Public Employees’ Retirement System **$9,701,065 thousand**. Teachers’ Retirement System **$10,809,181 thousand**. State Police Death, Disability and Retirement System **$861,703 thousand**. State Police Retirement System **$412,502 thousand**. Deputy Sheriff Retirement System **$393,134 thousand**. Judges’ Retirement System **$336,037 thousand**. Emergency Medical Services Retirement System **$230,893 thousand**. Municipal Police Officers and Firefighters Retirement System **$60,536 thousand**. Natural Resources Police Officers Retirement System **$36,925 thousand**. Teachers’ Defined Contribution Retirement System **$784,650 thousand**. TDC is not a defined-benefit book. Do not add the ten books into a second house AUM. Do not treat leftover Pennsylvania PSERS, leftover Virginia VRS, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio STRS **$80 billion+** / **$90 billion** / **$350 billion** as this filing.
2. **Investments are not fiduciary net position.** Combined investment assets **$23,600,607 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (PERS **$9,730,266 thousand**; TRS **$10,757,862 thousand**; TDC **$783,667 thousand**). The letter’s nine defined-benefit systems “approximately **$22.82 billion**” line is the defined-benefit investment book, not a combined fiduciary total.
3. **Returns.** Annual money-weighted rates of return, net of investment expenses: PERS **11.04 percent**. TRS **11.03 percent**. SPDDRS **10.99 percent**. SPRS **11.08 percent**. DSRS **11.07 percent**. JRS **11.06 percent**. EMSRS **11.53 percent**. MPFRS **11.19 percent**. NRPORS **11.08 percent**. Those lines are not interchangeable. The nine defined-benefit systems “experienced annual investment returns ranging from **4.4 percent** to **22.5 percent**, net of fees.” Assumed rate **7.25 percent** net. Defined-benefit investments are commingled at the West Virginia Investment Management Board — each system owns an equity position.

Do not flatten West Virginia CPRB into [Pennsylvania PSERS](/pennsylvania-psers/) or [Virginia Retirement System](/virginia-retirement-system/). Pennsylvania PSERS is the Pennsylvania school file. This is **one West Virginia combined administrator file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “West Virginia pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$9,701,065 thousand** line is PERS. The **$10,809,181 thousand** line is TRS. The **$23,600,607 thousand** line is combined investments. Those are not the same mandate.
