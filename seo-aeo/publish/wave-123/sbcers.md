# Santa Barbara County Employees’ Retirement System, explained

The Santa Barbara County Employees’ Retirement System is Santa Barbara County’s 1937-Act employee pension, not leftover SBCERA and not leftover LACERA. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.sbcers.org/wp-content/uploads/SBCERS-2025-Annual-Comprehensive-Financial-Report-ACFR.pdf) prints combined net position restricted for benefits of **$4,904,293 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Combined investments at fair value were **$4,810,649 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted return was **9.77 percent** net of fees. Pension fiduciary net position was **90.62 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover SBCERA overwrite or a leftover LACERA overwrite.** Do not mint `/santa-barbara-sbcers/`, `/sbcers-explained/`, or `/santa-barbara-county-employees/`. Do not mint `/sbcera/` for this book. SBCERA is the San Bernardino **County** file.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Combined net position restricted for benefits** **$4,904,293 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$4,503,441 thousand**), up **8.9 percent**. Combined investments at fair value **$4,810,649 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Cash and investments on the MD&A card **$4,884,942 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$4,911,010 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. The house **$4.9 billion** card is the combined fiduciary line rounded. Those four prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover SBCERA, leftover LACERA, leftover SJCERA, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books on the same statement.** Pension Benefit Trust **$4,813,621 thousand**. OPEB Trust **$90,672 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat the pension book as the combined book. GASB pension fiduciary net position **$4,813,619 thousand** is the same pension book in a rounded GASB print, not a second AUM.
3. **Returns and funded cards.** Time-weighted return **9.77 percent** net of fees. The investment-section **9.8 percent** card is the same time-weighted print rounded. Money-weighted return **9.77 percent** net of plan investment expense. Pension total pension liability **$5,311,727 thousand**. Pension fiduciary net position / total pension liability **90.62 percent**. Those official one-year prints and funded cards are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten SBCERS into [SBCERA](/sbcera/), [LACERA](/lacera/), [SJCERA](/sjcera/), [OCERS](/ocers/), [ICERS](/icers/), or [MercedCERA](/mercedcera/). SBCERA is San Bernardino **County**. LACERA is Los Angeles **County**. This is **one Santa Barbara County employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “SBCERS AUM” has to name the book. The **$4,904,293 thousand** line is combined fiduciary net position. The **$4,813,621 thousand** line is the pension trust. The **$4,810,649 thousand** line is combined investments. Those are not the same mandate.
