# National Pension Service (Korea), explained

South Korea’s National Pension Service manages the National Pension Fund on behalf of the Minister of Health and Welfare. The official English site prints **KRW 1,848.7 trillion** of total AUM at the end of **May 2026**. The **2025** money-weighted return was **18.82%** (**KRW 231.634 trillion**). The live UAO page’s **1,438 trillion won / ~US$997 billion** line is a dead, homemade-dollar print. NPS is a **public pension**, not a sovereign wealth fund, and it is **not** [KIC](/korea-investment-corporation-kic-explained/). This is not investment advice.

**Last updated:** 26 August 2026 · **Desk:** UAO Research · **Canonical URL:** `/national-pension-service-korea-explained/`

The live page this overwrites (19 June 2026) still says **1,438 trillion won (around $997 billion USD) as of November 2025**, “crossed the $1 trillion USD threshold,” and ranks GPIF at **~$1.8 trillion** and GPFG at **~$2 trillion**. Official won prints have moved. [GPIF](/gpif-japan-explained/) official is **¥293,643.7 billion** at 31 March 2026. [NBIM / GPFG](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/) official is **22,683 billion kroner** at 30 June 2026. Do **not** invent a USD AUM for NPS.

## What is not official — do not put it back

- **1,438 trillion won / ~US$997 billion as of November 2025.** Homemade FX. Official widget is **KRW 1,848.7 trillion** at end-May 2026.
- A **USD AUM** of any kind. NPS prints **won**.
- Homemade GPIF **$1.8 trillion** or GPFG **$2 trillion**.
- Treating NPS as a [sovereign wealth fund](/sovereign-wealth-fund/), or adding it to [KIC](/korea-investment-corporation-kic-explained/).

## How a peer should read it

This is a **mandatory public pension** under the National Pension Act — contributions in, benefits out, a reserve invested to keep the system solvent as Korea ages. The won stock is large enough that sleeve shifts move markets. The 2025 **18.82%** and the 2026 YTD **26.18%** (preliminary) are **their** money-weighted prints, not a USD league-table score. Do not convert them.
