# How Universal Asset Owners audits readership

Universal Asset Owners audits readership at the subscriber, not the cookie. The live [Readership](/readership/) page is the public file: each institutional subscriber is matched to a parent organisation and scored against published rankings of the largest sovereign funds, pensions, foundations and family offices. Periodic surveys add frequency and seniority. Advertisers may see the underlying method under confidentiality. We do not invent a subscriber total.

**Do not treat this page as a second Readership overwrite.** Do not mint `/readership-audit/`, `/how-uao-audits-circulation/`, or `/readership-methodology/`. Do not invent a unique-user story to close a call.

## What the live page already prints

[Readership](/readership/) is current as of **August 2026**. Lift only what it prints:

| Public line | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **98%** of the world’s sovereign wealth funds | Coverage of the SWF universe on the list, not a homemade rank |
| **20 of 20** of the world’s largest pension funds | Largest-pension coverage, not a league table we invented |
| **10 of 10** largest charitable foundations | Same method, foundations |
| **85%** of the world’s largest family offices | Same method, family offices |
| **100+** billionaires, every morning | First-party as published |
| More than **$50 trillion** in long-horizon capital | Aggregated from the institutions on the list, using each institution’s own reporting |

Circulation is small by design. The desks it reaches are not.

## The two inputs

The live page names both:

1. **A subscriber-level audit of the full list.** Each institutional subscriber is identified and matched to its parent organisation, so coverage is measured against published rankings — not estimated from domain counts.
2. **Periodic reader surveys.** Those establish reading frequency and seniority that a list audit alone cannot show.

[Methodology](/methodology/) (version 1.0, **28 May 2026**) is the survey rule: fielding window, sample size, eligibility, response rate where it can be calculated, fielding method, incentives, and whether any party other than UAO sponsored the survey. A survey that cannot meet that standard is not published.

Assets under management on the Readership page are aggregated from the most recent published figures of the institutions represented on the list. They are reviewed as the list grows. They are not a licence to reprint a leftover dollar cell from another product.

Advertisers, sponsors and partners evaluating a placement may request the underlying audit basis and survey methodology. That file is provided on request under confidentiality. Write **info@universalassetowners.com**.
