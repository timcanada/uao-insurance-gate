## What the page can say

- **What pooling is.** Separate administering authorities put assets into regional pools so fees and access to private markets can be shared. That is a governance fact.
- **What a pool is not.** A pool is not a single legal pension. Adding 89 funds into **£350 billion** without the current official SAB line is a leftover pile.
