# Kentucky County Employees Retirement System, explained

The County Employees Retirement System is Kentucky’s county-employee pension, not leftover Kentucky TRS and not leftover Kentucky Retirement Systems. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/AgencyReports/IJC/AR/KPPA/CERS/2025%20Annual%20Comprehensive%20Financial%20Report.pdf) prints combined fiduciary net position restricted for benefits of **$20,159,914 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Combined investments at fair value were **$19,770,318 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The pension and insurance portfolios averaged **11.5 percent**. Nonhazardous pension money-weighted return was **11.64 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Kentucky TRS overwrite or a leftover Kentucky Retirement Systems overwrite.** Do not mint `/cers/`, `/kentucky-cers-explained/`, or `/kppa/`. Do not mint `/kentucky-trs-explained/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combining statements of fiduciary net position are the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Combined fiduciary net position restricted for benefits** **$20,159,914 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$18,472,783 thousand**). Nonhazardous pension **$10,625,575 thousand**. Hazardous pension **$3,822,051 thousand**. Nonhazardous insurance **$3,875,848 thousand**. Hazardous insurance **$1,836,440 thousand**. Those five lines are not interchangeable. Combined investments at fair value **$19,770,318 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Combined total assets **$20,927,705 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover Kentucky TRS, leftover Kentucky Retirement Systems, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards are not the combining-statement book.** Nonhazardous pension GASB fiduciary net position **$10,490,132 thousand** excludes 401(h) assets **$135,443 thousand** and is not the combining-statement **$10,625,575 thousand**. Nonhazardous pension fiduciary net position / total pension liability **65.34 percent**. Hazardous pension **61.24 percent**. Actuarial-value funded cards **61.42 percent** / **106.48 percent** are not those GASB cards.
3. **Returns.** Pension and insurance portfolios averaged **11.5 percent**. Pension-section time-weighted total **11.65 percent**. Money-weighted: nonhazardous pension **11.64 percent**, hazardous pension **11.69 percent**, nonhazardous insurance **11.29 percent**, hazardous insurance **11.15 percent**. Those six official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten CERS into [Kentucky TRS](/kentucky-trs/) or [Kentucky Retirement Systems](/kentucky-krs/). Kentucky TRS is the statewide teacher file. Kentucky Retirement Systems is the KERS-plus-SPRS file. Kentucky Public Pensions Authority staffs both CERS and KRS; it is not a third AUM. This is **one county-employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “CERS AUM” has to name the book. The **$20,159,914 thousand** line is the combined pension-plus-insurance book. The **$10,625,575 thousand** line is the nonhazardous pension. The **$19,770,318 thousand** line is combined investments. Those are not the same mandate.
