# Arkansas Teacher Retirement System, explained

The Arkansas Teacher Retirement System is Arkansas’s teacher pension, not leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion** and not leftover Arkansas PERS. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.artrs.gov/Publications/ATRS_2025_Annual_Report.pdf) prints net position restricted for pensions of **$23,741,135,860** at **30 June 2025**. Snapshot net position was **$23,741.1 million**. Valuation assets were **$22,806.6 million**. The investment-section Total Fund line of approximately **$23.6 billion** is not fiduciary net position. Those lines are not interchangeable. The one-year total fund return was **9.4 percent** net-of-fees. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Texas TRS overwrite or a second Arkansas PERS page.** The Arkansas public-employee keeper is `/arkansas-pers/`. Do not mint `/arkansas-trs/`, `/atrs/`, or `/arkansas-teacher-retirement-explained/`. Do not mint `/texas-trs/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position restricted for pensions** **$23,741,135,860** at **30 June 2025** (beginning **$22,359,231,384**; increase **$1,381,904,476**). The fiscal-year overview prints snapshot net position **$23,741.1 million** and valuation assets **$22,806.6 million**. Those two snapshot lines are not interchangeable with each other or with the investment-section Total Fund line of approximately **$23.6 billion**. Do not treat leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion**, leftover Arkansas PERS **$11,932,028,241**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **Cash flows on the same report.** Employer contributions **$560,279,520**. Member contributions **$214,670,034**. Total contributions **$774,949,554**. Net investment income **$2,158,534,727**. Benefits **$1,531,980,381**. The letter’s “more than **$774 million**” and “**$2.16 billion**” lines are the same books, rounded.
3. **Returns.** One-year total fund return **9.4 percent** net-of-fees. Five-year **11.0 percent**. Ten-year **8.5 percent**. The overview’s investment-rate-of-return line is the same **9.4 percent**. This filing did not print a separate money-weighted line on the pages locked here — do not invent one.

Do not flatten ATRS into [Arkansas PERS](/arkansas-pers/) or [Texas TRS](/texas-teacher-retirement-system-explained/). Arkansas PERS is the Arkansas public-employee file. Texas TRS is the Texas teacher file. This is **one Arkansas teacher file**.
