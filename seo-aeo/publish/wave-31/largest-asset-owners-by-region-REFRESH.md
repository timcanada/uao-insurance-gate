# Largest asset owners by region: no official $75 trillion APAC pile

Regions are a real way to group owners. A stacked regional AUM is not. The live page, dated 4 July 2026, still says Asia-Pacific **dominates with approximately $75 trillion**, North America **$70 trillion**, Europe **$45 trillion** — then, in the next paragraph, North America **$16 trillion**, Europe **$12 trillion**, Canada pensions **$2.5 trillion**, and [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/) **approximately $466 billion**. Those piles contradict each other. Neither is a filing.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## What the page can say

- **Who lives where.** GPIF is Japan. CalPERS is California. GPFG is Norway. CPP is Canada. That is an address and a legal-person fact.
- **What a region does not file.** There is no official “APAC asset-owner AUM.” Adding pensions, SWFs, insurers, and retail manager money into **$75 trillion** is a category error.
- **How to print scale.** Quote the filing in its own currency, or say no official combined figure.
