# First year inside a family office — what the seat actually is

The first year inside a family office is not a junior banking tour with a quieter logo. You have one client, one file, and a confidentiality problem that outranks the portfolio. This Careers Intelligence briefing sits beside Universal Asset Owners' [definition of a family office](/what-is-a-family-office/). It is not tax, estate, or compensation advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second family-office definition.** Do not mint `/family-office/`. Do not invent a family-office AUM to decorate a first-year story.

## The first year is the mandate

A [family office](/what-is-a-family-office/) is a private organisation that manages the investments, wealth and affairs of a single ultra-wealthy family. The live define page is the job: single ownership and alignment. The first year is learning whose voice is the mandate — principal, next generation, or a committee that only looks like a board.

Universal Asset Owners already publishes that it reaches **85%** of the world’s largest family offices on [Readership](/readership/). That is a circulation fact, not a licence to invent a sector AUM on this page.

[Ethics](/ethics/) still governs anything that looks like coverage. The same rule is the first-year test: if you would not put it in a minute, do not put it in a WhatsApp.

## What to pressure-test in month one

1. **Who decides.** A family meeting is not an investment committee until someone writes the rule.
2. **What you may say.** Outside the house, there is no “market colour” that is safe by default.
3. **Public versus private books.** Consolidation is the job. A founder Slack is not a custodian.
4. **The other functions.** Tax, estate, philanthropy and administration sit in the same file as the portfolio.
5. **Exit.** How do people leave this office, and who still speaks to the principal?
