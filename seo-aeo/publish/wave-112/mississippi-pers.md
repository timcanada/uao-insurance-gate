# Public Employees' Retirement System of Mississippi, explained

The Public Employees' Retirement System of Mississippi is Mississippi’s combined public-plan file, not leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion**. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.pers.ms.gov/sites/default/files/Content/CAFR/2025_ACFR.pdf) prints combined net position restricted for pension benefits of **$36,216,322 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. The PERS defined-benefit book is **$35,602,887 thousand**. The Mississippi Highway Safety Patrol Retirement System is **$468,749 thousand**. The Supplemental Legislative Retirement Plan is **$23,601 thousand**. The Municipal Retirement Systems book is **$121,085 thousand**. Those five lines are not interchangeable. The combined portfolio return was **11.66 percent** gross. The money-weighted return was **11.34 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Ohio PERS overwrite or a second Ohio OPERS page.** The Ohio OPERS keeper is `/ohio-opers/`. Do not mint `/ms-pers/`, `/mississippi-pers-explained/`, or `/pers-mississippi/`. Do not mint `/opers/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combining statement is the sourced split. The report prints these lines **in thousands**:

1. **Combined net position restricted for pension benefits** **$36,216,322 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$34,031,685 thousand**). That combined line covers PERS, MHSPRS, SLRP, and MRS. Do not treat leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books on the same report.** PERS **$35,602,887 thousand**. MHSPRS **$468,749 thousand**. SLRP **$23,601 thousand**. MRS **$121,085 thousand**. Those four named books are not interchangeable with the combined fiduciary total. Do not add MHSPRS, SLRP, or MRS to the PERS book a second time and call the sum a second AUM.
3. **Returns.** Combined investment portfolio **11.66 percent** gross. Money-weighted return **11.34 percent** net of investment expense. Those two return lines are not interchangeable. Total investments before securities lending approximated **$35.6 billion**. That investment line is not the combined fiduciary net position.

Do not flatten Mississippi PERS into [Ohio OPERS](/ohio-opers/) or [Iowa IPERS](/iowa-ipers/). Ohio OPERS is the Ohio public-employee file. This is **one Mississippi combined file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Mississippi PERS AUM” has to name the book. The **$36.22 billion** line is the combined fiduciary total. The **$35.60 billion** line is the PERS defined-benefit book. The **$35.6 billion** investments line is the pool before securities lending. Those are not the same mandate.
