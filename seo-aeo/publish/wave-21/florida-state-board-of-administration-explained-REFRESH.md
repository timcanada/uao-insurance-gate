# Florida State Board of Administration, explained

The State Board of Administration of Florida is a **constitutional investment manager**, not a single **$300 billion** pension. The Florida Constitution creates it. The trustees are the **Governor (chair)**, the **Chief Financial Officer**, and the **Attorney General**. MyFRS, the official FRS member site, prints two unaudited asset lines as of **29 May 2026**: FRS Pension Plan **$234,838,279,798.03** and FRS Investment Plan **$24,579,757,086.59**. Live UAO copy that prints **$300+ billion / $301 billion**, or an **$184 billion** FRS line, is not those trustee figures. Do not add the two plans into a homemade SBA total. Do not mint `/florida-sba/`. This is not investment advice.

## What is official

| Item | Official print | As-of | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Legal identity | State Board of Administration — Florida’s principal independent investment-management organization, created by the **Florida Constitution** | — | [SBA About](https://www.sbafla.com/about-the-sba/); [OPPAGA program 4040](https://oppaga.fl.gov/ProgramSummary/ProgramDetail?programNumber=4040) |
| Trustees | **Governor** (chair), **Chief Financial Officer**, **Attorney General** | — | Same |
| What it invests | FRS Pension Plan; administers FRS Investment Plan; Florida Hurricane Catastrophe Fund; Florida PRIME; other legislatively directed funds | — | Same. **Not one AUM** |
| FRS Pension Plan total assets | **$234,838,279,798.03** (unaudited) | 29 May 2026 | [MyFRS Plan Information / Trustee Reports](https://www.myfrs.com/PlanInformation.htm) |
| FRS Investment Plan total assets | **$24,579,757,086.59** (unaudited) | 29 May 2026 | Same |

Print those two lines as two lines. A writer who adds them, then rounds to **$300 billion**, is not quoting MyFRS.

The SBA also manages the Hurricane Catastrophe Fund and Florida PRIME. Those are **different** books. Do not fold them into an FRS total unless a dated SBA or MyFRS table does it.

## What is not official

- **$300+ billion**, **$301 billion**, or any other homemade combined SBA AUM.
- FRS Pension Plan **$184 billion** as if it were current.
- Homemade **629,000** active / **486,000** retiree counts until MyFRS or the FRS ACFR prints them on a dated page we can fetch.
- A mid-2024 sleeve mix presented as the current IPS.
- Minting `/florida-sba/` or `/florida-sba-explained/`.

## How a peer should read it

SBA is the **manager**. FRS Pension Plan and FRS Investment Plan are the **books**. Compare the pension line with [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/) and [Texas TRS](/texas-teacher-retirement-system-explained/), not with [ADIA](/adia-abu-dhabi-investment-authority/). Anyone still writing **$300 billion** is adding funds the trustee page keeps separate.
