# How to use the UAO Registry

The UAO Registry is Universal Asset Owners’ searchable database of the Top 100 long-horizon institutions and the people who run them. Use it to find a profile, a leadership move, and a confidence flag — not to lift a dollar cell as official AUM. The live [Registry](/registry/) is the product. Institution keepers, when they cite an official filing, remain the AUM source of record.

**Do not treat this page as a second Registry overwrite.** Do not mint `/uao-registry-explained/`, `/how-to-use-the-registry/`, or `/uao-database-explained/`. Do not reprint a leftover dollar total from the ranking table. Do not write a people-page from a move listed here.

## What the live page already names

On **26 August 2026** the live Registry printed:

- **The UAO Database · Top 100 + People** — 100 institutions, plus a people directory
- **8,859** people tracked
- **133** leadership moves
- Live verification: **645** of **8,859** records re-verified in the last 30 days (**6%**); median record age **67 days**; last sweep **26 August 2026**
- A full-access CTA at **$4,950 / month**
- The **UAO Influence Index** (0–100): a directional editorial score, **not a rating**
- Assets on the table carry a confidence flag: *Validated (official disclosure)* or *Estimate (public ranking)*. Institutions marked **stub** have a verified ranking entry with a fuller profile in progress

Those are product facts. They are not a licence to dollarize NBIM, GPIF, SAFE, ADIA, KIA, QIA, or GIC, or to treat a combined-assets headline as official house AUM.

## How to read a row

1. **Start with the type.** A public pension is not a sovereign wealth fund. GPIF is a pension. SAFE is a reserve manager. Do not flatten them.
2. **Read the confidence flag before the dollar.** *Estimate (public ranking)* is not an official filing. Several large owners do not publish AUM at all. Do not invent one.
3. **Prefer the institution keeper** when we have pasted an official local-currency figure (kroner, yen, Canadian dollars). A USD ranking cell can lag that filing.
4. **Leadership moves** are dated. Confirm the source link. Do not preview an unpublished people-move as journalism.
5. **Influence and transparency scores** are editorial. They are not credit ratings and not a recommended-vendor stamp.

[Methodology](/methodology/) (version 1.0, **28 May 2026**) is the ranking rule: universe, data window, sources, scoring rubric, exclusions, version, changelog. A ranking that cannot meet that standard is not published.

## What you may not do

- Lift a Registry dollar as if Universal Asset Owners had audited the fund’s books
- Call ADIA, GIC, KIA, QIA, or SAFE AUM “official” because a cell is filled
- Treat a labelled sponsor as a correspondent
- Use the [Advertise](/advertise/) form as if it were Press

[Ethics](/ethics/) (version 1.0, **28 May 2026**): we do not pay sources, and we do not promise a conclusion in exchange for access. The Top 100 job is defined on [what the UAO 100 is](/what-is-the-uao-100/).
