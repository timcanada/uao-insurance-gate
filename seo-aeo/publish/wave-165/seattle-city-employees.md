# Seattle City Employees’ Retirement System, explained

The Seattle City Employees’ Retirement System is Seattle’s civilian-employee pension, not leftover Sacramento County Employees’ Retirement System. The official [31 December 2025 Seattle City Employees’ Retirement System financial statements](https://www.seattle.gov/documents/Departments/Retirement/Publications/Auditors%20reports/2025AuditorsReport.pdf) print fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits of **$4,958,213,991** at **31 December 2025**. Investments **$4,788,416,520** are not that card. Notes money-weighted return was **15.12 percent** net of fees. GASB **83.54 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
