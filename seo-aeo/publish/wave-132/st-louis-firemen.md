# Firemen’s Retirement System of St. Louis, explained

Firemen’s Retirement System of St. Louis is St. Louis’s closed sworn firefighter pension, not leftover Employees Retirement System of the City of St. Louis and not leftover MOSERS. The System is closed to new members, and benefits have been frozen as of **1 February 2013**. The official [30 September 2025 annual financial report](https://www.frs-stl.org/25-8100-Final-Audit-Firemens-Retirement-System-of-STL.pdf) prints net position restricted for pensions of **$446,256,821**. Statement investments were **$442,731,360**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return was **12.08 percent** net of investment expenses. GASB fiduciary net position **$442,434,547** excludes the Future Benefit Fund **$3,822,274** and is not the statement. Fiduciary net position used for GASB was **98.11 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 September 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position restricted for pensions** **$446,256,821** at **30 September 2025** (2024 **$434,429,449**). Statement investments **$442,731,360** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$447,127,775** is not fiduciary net position. GASB fiduciary net position **$442,434,547** excludes the Future Benefit Fund **$3,822,274**. Those two official net-position prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover St. Louis ERS **$937,329,534**, leftover MOSERS fiduciary activities **$9,565,800,614**, leftover Tampa Fire and Police **$3,407,822,026**, leftover Jacksonville Police and Fire **$2,903,990,005**, leftover Chicago Fire FABF, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$450,965,617**. Net pension liability **$8,531,070**. GASB fiduciary net position / total pension liability **98.11 percent** (2024 **94.72 percent**). That ratio uses the **$442,434,547** print, not the statement **$446,256,821** line.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **12.08 percent** net of investment expenses for the year ended **30 September 2025** (2024 **16.79 percent**). Do not invent a one-year time-weighted card.
