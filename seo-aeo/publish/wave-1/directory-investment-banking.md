# Investment banking and capital markets for universal owners

Universal owners meet investment banks as counterparties, placement agents, advisers on sales and secondaries, and sometimes as the house that does not understand a liability-driven book. This Universal Asset Owners hub describes that relationship from the owner's side of the table. It is a directory category, not a league table, and not a solicitation.

## What the owner is listening for

A sovereign savings fund and a closed corporate pension do not want the same call.

The first may want access, geopolitics, and a house that can hold a conflict across a ministry relationship. The second may want a clean process on a risk-transfer or a private-markets secondary — and a banker who will not pitch a product the discount rate just broke.

If your coverage team cannot say which owner they are on the line with, the owner already knows.

## Honest products in this room

- Labelled desk presence for a house that lives on LP relationships
- A claimed profile that states *what you do for owners*, not your global ranking
- Recruitment for coverage roles that actually sit on allocator accounts
