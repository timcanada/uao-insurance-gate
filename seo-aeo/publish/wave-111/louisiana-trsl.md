# Teachers' Retirement System of Louisiana, explained

The Teachers’ Retirement System of Louisiana is Louisiana’s teacher pension, not [Texas TRS](/texas-teacher-retirement-system-explained/) and not leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion**. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://fluxconsole.com/files/item/1446/202846/2025%20ACFR%20final.pdf) prints Net Position Restricted for Pensions of **$29,334,227,630** at **30 June 2025**. Net investment income was **$2,770,350,275**. The market rate of return was **11.1 percent** gross of fees. The money-weighted return was **10.53 percent** net of investment expense. Those two return lines are not interchangeable. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second Texas TRS overwrite or a leftover Texas TRS $295 billion overwrite.** The Texas TRS keeper is `/texas-teacher-retirement-system-explained/`. Do not mint `/trsl/`, `/louisiana-trs/`, or `/louisiana-trsl-explained/`. Do not mint `/texas-trs/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement is the sourced split:

1. **Net Position Restricted for Pensions** **$29,334,227,630** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$27,361,441,810**). Investments at fair value **$29,134,438,729**. Those two dollar lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion**, leftover Ohio STRS **$80 billion+**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named flows on the same report.** Net investment income **$2,770,350,275**. Member contributions **$443,642,603**. Employer contributions **$1,224,369,212**. Retirement benefits **$2,460,026,650**. Do not add net investment income to Net Position Restricted for Pensions a second time and call the sum a second AUM.
3. **Returns.** Market rate of return **11.1 percent** gross of fees. Money-weighted return **10.53 percent** net of investment expense. Those two prints are not interchangeable. The letter of transmittal **11.1 percent** line is the gross investment-section print.

Do not flatten Louisiana TRSL into [Texas TRS](/texas-teacher-retirement-system-explained/) or [Kentucky TRS](/kentucky-trs/). Texas TRS is Texas. Kentucky TRS is Kentucky. This is **one Louisiana teacher file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Louisiana teacher AUM” has to name the book. The **$29.33 billion** line is Net Position Restricted for Pensions. The **$29.13 billion** line is investments at fair value. The **11.1 percent** line is gross of fees. Those are not the same mandate.
