# How does insurance close a strait?

Insurance closes a strait through a listed-area mechanism, not through a volume table. The Joint War Committee — hull-war underwriters at Lloyd’s and the International Underwriting Association — publishes geographical areas where vessels are considered at increased risk of war-related perils. Ships entering those listed areas may require additional war-risk coverage. Rating is individual negotiation; the committee plays no role in the rate. Circular JWLA-034 (29 July 2026) amended Saudi Arabia; amended the Persian/Arabian Gulf, Gulf of Oman, Indian Ocean, Gulf of Aden and Southern Red Sea (northwest boundary: Red Sea south of latitude 25.5°N, not including Egyptian territorial waters); amended Eritrea; and deleted Pakistan. This page is not a ranking and not insurance advice.

## A list, not a rate

The Lloyd’s Market Association’s [listed-areas page](https://lmalloyds.com/specialist-areas/underwriting/listed-areas/) and the [Joint War Committee](https://lmalloyds.com/committee/joint-war-committee/) are the sourced mechanism:

1. The JWC, with independent security advisers, publishes a list of geographical areas where vessels are considered at **increased risk of war-related perils**. Ships sailing into listed areas **may require additional war-risk coverage**.
2. **Rating is a matter for individual negotiation** between underwriters and brokers. The JWC **plays no role** in that rate.
3. Circular **[JWLA-034](https://lmalloyds.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/JWLA-034-Saudi-Arabia.pdf)** (**29 July 2026**) is the current list: **amended** Saudi Arabia; **amended** Persian/Arabian Gulf, Gulf of Oman, Indian Ocean, Gulf of Aden and Southern Red Sea — on the northwest, by the Red Sea **south of Latitude 25.5°N** (not including Egyptian territorial waters); **amended** Eritrea; **deleted** Pakistan. Named countries include their ports and coastal waters up to **12 nautical miles** offshore unless specifically varied. The circular says application to individual contracts is a matter for **specific negotiation**.

Barrels through Hormuz already sit on [what a chokepoint is](/what-is-a-chokepoint-for-a-universal-owner/). A listed area can make a voyage more expensive, or uninsurable on standard terms, **without** changing EIA’s transit print. An insurer general account is a different capital rule — [how insurers invest differently from pensions](/how-do-insurers-invest-differently-from-pensions/).
