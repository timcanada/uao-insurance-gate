# What “enrich” actually means

The 30–40k number is **not** 30–40k people we can email.

## The three piles (28 Aug 2026 public-only run)

| Pile | Count | What it is | Can we “just add email”? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Empty senior seats | 39,243 | A chair: “CIO of Fund X.” No name. | **No.** First find the human, then buy the email. |
| Named, not on Ghost | 470 | A real person at a real house, not already a member. | **Yes.** 1 Apollo credit each. The CSV was never saved (PII). |
| Already on Ghost | ~55k subscribed | Suppression list. | Do not mail. Do not re-subscribe. |

11,160 institutions generated those empty seats (about 3–4 titles per house: CIO, deputy, head of private markets, etc.). Most houses are PE (5,046) and asset managers (4,539), not SWFs or pensions.

## What Apollo can actually sell us

Search does **not** return emails. Match costs **1 credit** and writes a work email into `growth-agent/private/` (gitignored). Nobody is auto-added to Ghost.

Verified c-suite CIOs Apollo will sell an email for, on this seat (30 Sep 2026):

| Filter | People with a verified email for sale |
|---|---|
| Any “Chief Investment Officer” | 7,864 — includes UBS, RIAs, product CIOs |
| Pension-tagged employer | 456 |
| Sovereign-wealth-tagged | 32 |
| Endowment / foundation | 288 |
| Family office | 815 |
| Pension + SWF combined | 194 |

So: **we cannot get 30,000 new owner emails from this database.** The honest owner-desk ceiling is low thousands if we widen titles (deputy CIO, head of private markets) and buy credits. 10,000 is only possible if we accept a lot of non-owner “CIO” titles and buy ~10,000 credits.
