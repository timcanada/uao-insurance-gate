# Arkansas Public Employees Retirement System, explained

The Arkansas Public Employees Retirement System is Arkansas’s state-and-local employee pension, not leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** and not a teacher file. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://apers.org/wp-content/uploads/APERS-ACFR-FY25-web-version.pdf) prints net position restricted for pension benefits of **$11,932,028,241** at **30 June 2025**. Investments at fiscal year-end were **$12,409 million**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The combined portfolio return was **11.11 percent**. The money-weighted return was **10.52 percent** net. The DROP reserve is not AUM. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover MassPRIM overwrite or a second Arkansas teacher file.** Do not mint `/apers/`, `/arkansas-pers-explained/`, or `/arkansas-employees-retirement/`. Do not mint `/arkansas-trs/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position restricted for pension benefits** **$11,932,028,241** at **30 June 2025** (statement comparative 2024: **$11,044,310,055**). Do not treat leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion**, or leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion** as this filing.
2. **Investments versus the fiduciary book.** The investments snapshot is **$12,409 million**. That is not fiduciary net position. The Deferred Retirement Option Plan reserve is **$128,387,010**. That reserve is not a second AUM.
3. **Returns.** Combined portfolio return **11.11 percent**. The policy benchmark printed **12.43 percent**. Money-weighted return **10.52 percent** net of investment expense. Those two return lines are not interchangeable. Net investment income was **$1,149,351,361**.

Do not flatten Arkansas PERS into [Iowa IPERS](/iowa-ipers/) or [Oklahoma OPERS](/oklahoma-opers/). This is **one Arkansas public-employee file**.
