# Botswana Pula Fund, explained

The Pula Fund is Botswana’s long-term foreign-reserve portfolio, managed by the Bank of Botswana. The Bank’s own page dates the fund to **1994**; IFSWF dates the establishment to **1993** and the legal framework to the **1996 Bank of Botswana Act**. It is **not a separate legal entity**. The June 2026 monthly statement printed the foreign-assets **Pula Fund** line at **30,818,706 in P'000 at 30 June 2026**. The live UAO page’s **$6.0 billion** and **50/30/20** sleeves are not that filing. This is not investment advice.

Print the Pula Fund in **Pula**. The USD line on the same PDF is the Bank’s official conversion of **total foreign exchange reserves**. Do not run the Pula Fund line through the 0.0704 mid-rate and call that AUM.

IFSWF tells you the amount of assets under management is available on the Bank of Botswana website. The monthly statement is that print. Use the **foreign-assets Pula Fund** line as the fund size. Use the **GIA Pula Fund** line as the government’s share, not a second AUM.

## What is not official — do not put it back

- **$6.0 billion** “as of mid-2024.” Homemade USD. Not the June 2026 statement.
- **$2.7 billion in 2020** as a current comparison.
- **50% bonds / 30% equities / 20% cash.** Not on the Pula Fund page, the Act, or the monthly statement.
- Adding the **GIA Pula Fund** line to the foreign-assets Pula Fund line.
- Calling the **USD 4,161.858 million** reserve print the Pula Fund.
- A homemade **rank** against [NBIM](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/) or [GIC](/gic-singapore-explained/).

## How a peer should read it

The Pula Fund is a **central-bank long-term reserve sleeve**, not a company with its own board. Section 35 is the power; the Liquidity Portfolio is the six-month import buffer; what is left is the Pula Fund. The government owns a **share** through the Government Investment Account. The June 2026 foreign-assets line is **P30,818.706 million**. Anyone writing **$6 billion** and a **50/30/20** mix is inventing a savings-SWF that Botswana did not file.
