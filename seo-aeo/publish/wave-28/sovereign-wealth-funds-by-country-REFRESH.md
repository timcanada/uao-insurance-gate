# SWFs by country: no official $10.7 trillion pile — and SGRF is not an Emirati $15 billion book

There is no official combined AUM for sovereign wealth funds by country. The live page still prints **combined assets exceeding $10.7 trillion**, GPFG **approximately $1.32 trillion**, ADIA **roughly $950 billion**, an **Emirati State General Reserve Fund** at **$15 billion**, SAFE **$940 billion**, a homemade China consolidation **exceeding $2 trillion**, PIF **$925 billion**, KIA **$870 billion**, and a Vietnam **National Sovereign Wealth Fund** at **$23 billion (established 2021)**. Those are tracker numbers. They are not the filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

Print kroner, dirhams, and Singapore dollars as the houses print them. Do **not** add CIC to SAFE and call the sum China’s SWF AUM. Do not mint `/safe-china/` or `/vietnam-scic/` (404).

SGRF as an **Emirati $15 billion** sibling of ADIA is a category error. Oman’s leftover State General Reserve Fund is not Abu Dhabi’s third book. Abu Dhabi’s named books on this desk are ADIA, [Mubadala](/mubadala-investment-company/), and [ADQ](/adq-abu-dhabi/).
