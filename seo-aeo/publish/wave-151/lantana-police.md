Investments **$31,501,532** are not that card. DROP restricted **$1,588,528** is a component, not a second AUM. Defined-benefit restricted **$29,935,062** is not total net position. Money-weighted return was **11.14 percent** net of investment expense. GASB **99.13 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
