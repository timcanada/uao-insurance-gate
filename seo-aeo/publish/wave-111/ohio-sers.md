# School Employees Retirement System of Ohio, explained

The School Employees Retirement System of Ohio is Ohio’s non-teaching school-employee pension, not [Ohio OPERS](/ohio-opers/) and not leftover Ohio STRS. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.ohsers.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2025-ACFR.pdf) prints Total fiduciary net position of **$21,081,732,005** at **30 June 2025**. The Pension Trust Fund is **$19,771,601,672**. The Health Care Fund is **$939,812,471**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted return was **11.2 percent** net. The pension-plan money-weighted return was **10.87 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second Ohio OPERS overwrite or a leftover Ohio STRS overwrite.** The Ohio OPERS keeper is `/ohio-opers/`. Do not mint `/ohio-sers-explained/`, `/sers-ohio/`, or `/ohsers/`. Do not mint `/strs-ohio-explained/`. Do not mint `/opers/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combining statement is the sourced split:

1. **Total fiduciary net position** **$21,081,732,005** at **30 June 2025** (beginning-of-year restated **$19,520,714,290**). That total covers the Pension Trust Fund, the Medicare B Fund, the Death Benefit Fund, the Qualified Excess Benefit Arrangement, and the Health Care Fund. Do not treat leftover Ohio STRS **$80 billion+**, leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books on the same report.** Pension Trust Fund **$19,771,601,672**. Medicare B Fund **$333,234,410**. Death Benefit Fund **$36,917,804**. QEBA **$165,648**. Fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits **$20,141,919,534**. Health Care Fund / other postemployment benefits **$939,812,471**. Those lines are not interchangeable. Do not add the Health Care Fund to the Pension Trust Fund a second time and call the sum a second AUM.
3. **Returns.** Time-weighted return **11.2 percent** net, **1.1 percentage points** over the policy benchmark. Investment portfolio fair value **$20.7 billion**. Pension-plan money-weighted return **10.87 percent**. Those two return lines are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Ohio SERS into [Ohio OPERS](/ohio-opers/), [Ohio Police & Fire](/ohio-police-fire/), [Pennsylvania SERS](/pennsylvania-sers/), [Illinois SERS](/illinois-sers/), or [Cincinnati Retirement System](/cincinnati-retirement/). OPERS is Ohio’s public-employee file. Ohio Police & Fire is the statewide sworn file. Pennsylvania SERS is Pennsylvania. Illinois SERS is Illinois. Cincinnati Retirement is a city civilian file. This is **one Ohio school-employee file**.
