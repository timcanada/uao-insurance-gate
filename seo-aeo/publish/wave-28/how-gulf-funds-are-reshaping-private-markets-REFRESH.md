# Gulf funds in private markets: no official $200 billion deploy — and no PIF–KKR $40 billion filing

Gulf houses are in private markets. That is visible. The live arithmetic is not. The live page, dated 8 July 2026, still says [PIF](/public-investment-fund-pif/), [ADQ](/adq-abu-dhabi/), and [KIA](/kuwait-investment-authority-kia/) have **deployed over $200 billion** in global private markets since 2018, grows PIF from **$430 billion (2020)** to **over $925 billion (2024)**, invents a **$40 billion** PIF–KKR global PE fund, a **Giga Fund targeting $50 billion**, ADQ **$145 billion** AUM, and a combined **$120–150 billion** annual Gulf private-markets pace. Those tickets are not the filings.
