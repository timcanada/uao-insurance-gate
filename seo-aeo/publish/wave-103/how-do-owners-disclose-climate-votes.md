# How do owners disclose climate votes?

Climate-vote disclosure is a category-and-statute job, not a second proxy-voting overwrite and not a second Form PF versus N-PX page. The SEC’s 2 November 2022 Form N-PX amendments require registered funds to categorise each reported vote; the official category list includes Environment or climate, and categories are non-exclusive. In the United Kingdom, The Pensions Regulator’s general code (in force 28 March 2024) requires the statement of investment principles to state the policy on voting rights and on engagement with issuers about social and environmental impact. The Department for Work and Pensions’ SRD II fact sheet requires an implementation statement, from 1 October 2020, that describes voting behaviour, the most significant votes, and any use of a proxy voter. This page is not a ranking and not voting advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second proxy-voting overwrite or a second Form PF versus N-PX overwrite.** Those keepers are `/proxy-voting-institutional-investors/` and `/what-is-form-pf-vs-n-px/`. Do not mint `/climate-vote-disclosure/`. Do not reprint leftover CalPERS **$460bn**, leftover CalSTRS **$314bn**, leftover GPFG **$1.4T**, or leftover Vanguard **$8.2T**.

## A category in the US, a statement in the UK

Official SEC Form N-PX, TPR’s [stewardship module](https://www.thepensionsregulator.gov.uk/en/document-library/code-of-practice/funding-and-investment/investment/stewardship), and the DWP [SRD II fact sheet](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/workplace-pension-scheme-shareholder-rights-directive-ii-fact-sheet/shareholder-rights-directive-ii-fact-sheet) (updated **4 October 2019**) are the sourced split:

1. The SEC [adopted enhanced Form N-PX on 2 November 2022](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2022-198). The Commission’s [compliance guide](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/small-business-compliance-guides/enhanced-reporting-proxy-votes-registered-management-investment-companies-reporting-executive) says funds must categorise reported votes against a list that includes director elections, extraordinary transactions, Section 14A say-on-pay, shareholder rights and defenses, and **Environment or climate**, among others. Categories are **non-exclusive**; a fund must select **all** that apply. Wave 100 already covers say-on-pay and rule **14Ad-1**. This page is the **climate category**.
2. TPR’s general code (in force **28 March 2024**) requires the statement of investment principles — unless exempt — to include the policy on exercising **voting rights** and the policy on monitoring and engaging with issuers about relevant matters, including the issuer’s **social and environmental impact** and corporate governance. The annual vehicle that reports how those policies were followed is [the implementation statement](/what-is-an-implementation-statement/).
3. The DWP SRD II fact sheet is the UK transposition clock: an **implementation statement** reporting against the SIP should sit in any annual report produced from **1 October 2020** and be published online. Trustees must describe **voting behaviour** by or on behalf of the trustees, the **most significant votes**, whether the book sat in a segregated mandate, a pooled fund, or another arrangement, and any use of a **proxy voter**, in an annual report to be produced no later than **1 October 2021**.

How a house votes the shares it already owns sits on [proxy voting](/proxy-voting-institutional-investors/). Which SEC form is public sits on [Form PF vs Form N-PX](/what-is-form-pf-vs-n-px/). The annual UK vehicle sits on [what an implementation statement is](/what-is-an-implementation-statement/). This page is the **climate-specific file**.

Do not flatten “climate votes” into a stewardship-code slogan. Wave 54 already has that job.
