# City of Boca Raton Police and Firefighters’ Retirement System, explained

The City of Boca Raton Police and Firefighters’ Retirement System is Boca Raton’s sworn pension, not leftover Boca civilian, leftover Palm Beach Gardens Police, and not leftover Miami Fire and Police. The official [30 September 2025 and 2024 City of Boca Raton Police and Firefighters’ Retirement System financial statements](https://www.myboca.us/DocumentCenter/View/42248) print fiduciary net position of **$613,033,507** at **30 September 2025**. Investments **$584,701,156** are not that card. Cash and short-term investments **$28,629,392** are not investments. DROP **$44,063,868** is a component, not a second AUM. Money-weighted return was **9.72 percent** net of plan investment expense. GASB **74.47 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
