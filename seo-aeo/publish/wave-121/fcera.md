# Fresno County Employees’ Retirement Association, explained

The Fresno County Employees’ Retirement Association is Fresno County’s 1937-Act employee pension, not leftover LACERA and not leftover KCERA. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://content.fcera.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/FINAL-ACFR-2025-12.16.25.pdf) prints fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits of **$7,380,434 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Investments at fair value were **$7,176,337 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted return was **11.4 percent** net of fees. Fiduciary net position was **93.43 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover LACERA overwrite or a leftover KCERA overwrite.** Do not mint `/fresno-fcera/`, `/fcera-explained/`, or `/fresno-county-employees/`. Do not mint `/lacera-explained/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits** **$7,380,434 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$6,650,853 thousand**). Investments at fair value **$7,176,337 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Cash and cash equivalents **$339,556 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$7,948,122 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. The MD&A **$7.38 billion** card is the fiduciary line rounded. Those four lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover LACERA, leftover KCERA, leftover SBCERA, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$7,899,761 thousand**. The required-supplementary-information print **$7,899,761,247** is the same liability in full dollars, not a second book. Net pension liability **$519,327 thousand**. Fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **93.43 percent**. The actuarial funded ratio **85.94 percent** is from the **30 June 2024** valuation — it is not a 30 June 2025 funding card. Those funded cards are not interchangeable.
3. **Returns.** Time-weighted net-of-fees portfolio return **11.4 percent** versus the assumed rate **6.50 percent**. Annual money-weighted return **11.67 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. The house says those two official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten FCERA into [LACERA](/lacera/), [KCERA](/kcera/), [SBCERA](/sbcera/), [SJCERA](/sjcera/), [SBCERS](/sbcers/), [Sonoma SCERA](/sonoma-scera/), [ICERS](/icers/), [MercedCERA](/mercedcera/), or [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/). LACERA is the Los Angeles **County** file. KCERA is the Kern **County** file. SJCERA is the San Joaquin **County** file. SBCERS is the Santa Barbara **County** file. This is **one Fresno County employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “FCERA AUM” has to name the book. The **$7,380,434 thousand** line is fiduciary net position. The **$7,176,337 thousand** line is investments. The **85.94 percent** card is a 2024 actuarial print, not the 2025 GASB **93.43 percent** card. Those are not the same mandate.
