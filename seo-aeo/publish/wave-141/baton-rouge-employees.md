# Baton Rouge City-Parish Employees’ Retirement System, explained

The Employees’ Retirement System of the City of Baton Rouge and Parish of East Baton Rouge is Baton Rouge’s city-parish pension, not leftover Louisiana TRSL, leftover LASERS, and not leftover Police Guarantee Trust. The official [31 December 2025 CPERS Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.brla.gov/DocumentCenter/View/24151/2025-ACFR-Final) prints CPERS Trust fiduciary net position of **$1,439,791,602** at **31 December 2025**. Combined **$1,468,363,833** is not this card. Money-weighted return was **14.26 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Gross **14.67 percent** is not that card. GASB **67.13 percent** is not actuarial **65.0 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover LASERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/cpers/`, `/baton-rouge-cpers/`, or `/baton-rouge-employees-explained/`. Do not invent a combined Baton Rouge AUM as if it were this card.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The CPERS Trust column, the GASB 67 note, and the money-weighted schedule are the sourced lines:

1. **CPERS Trust fiduciary net position** **$1,439,791,602** at **31 December 2025** (beginning **$1,297,461,707**). Trust investments **$1,419,861,776**. Combined **$1,468,363,833** mixes this book with leftover Police Guarantee Trust **$28,572,231**. Do not treat leftover Louisiana TRSL **$29,334,227,630**, leftover LASERS **$17,223,211,406**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$2,144,715,937**. Net pension liability **$704,924,335**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **67.13 percent**. MD&A **67.1 percent** is the rounded card. Actuarial funding **65.0 percent** is not the GASB card. Police Guarantee Trust GASB **59.83 percent** is not this card.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **14.26 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **31 December 2025** (2024 **7.28 percent**). Transmittal gross **14.67 percent** / **14.7 percent** is not the RSI card. Police Guarantee Trust money-weighted **15.10 percent** is not this card.

Do not flatten the CPERS Trust into [Louisiana TRSL](/louisiana-trsl/), [LASERS](/louisiana-lasers/), or [Baton Rouge Police Guarantee Trust](/baton-rouge-pgt/). TRSL is the Louisiana educator file. LASERS is the Louisiana state-employee file. Police Guarantee Trust is a separate legal trust. This is **one Baton Rouge city-parish file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Baton Rouge pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$1,439,791,602** line is CPERS Trust fiduciary net position. The **$1,468,363,833** line is combined. The **$28,572,231** line is Police Guarantee Trust. The **14.26 percent** card is money-weighted. The **14.67 percent** card is gross. The **67.13 percent** card is GASB. The **65.0 percent** card is actuarial. Those are not the same mandate.
