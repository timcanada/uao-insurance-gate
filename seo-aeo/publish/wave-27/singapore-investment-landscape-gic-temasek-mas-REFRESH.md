# Singapore’s three books: GIC has no AUM; MAS is not $1.08 trillion

Singapore splits the jobs across three legal persons. GIC Private Limited does **not** publish assets under management. Temasek Holdings printed NPV **S$518 billion (US$401 billion)** at **31 March 2026**. The Monetary Authority of Singapore prints official foreign reserves of **S$549.3 billion / US$427.9 billion** at end-**July 2026** (preliminary). The live page still expands GIC as **“Government of Singapore Investment Company”**, a **$810 billion** global manager; Temasek as a **$403 billion** holding company; and MAS reserves as **approximately $1.08 trillion as of end-2023**. Those dollars are not the filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## Official numbers (do not stack them)

| House | Official job | Official print | What is not official |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GIC Private Limited | Professional manager of Government assets. Not the owner | No official AUM. 20-year real **3.4%** / USD nominal **5.6%** to **31 March 2026**. Established **1981** | **$810 billion**; “Government of Singapore Investment Company” |
| Temasek Holdings | Commercial holding company. Government is the **sole** equity shareholder | **S$518 billion (US$401 billion)** at **31 March 2026**. Incorporated **1974** | **$403 billion** at 31 March 2024 |
| Monetary Authority of Singapore | Central bank. Manages official foreign reserves for the exchange-rate regime | OFR **S$549.3 billion / US$427.9 billion** at end-**July 2026** (preliminary). Last update **7 August 2026, 5 PM** | **$1.08 trillion** as of end-2023 |
| Combined “Singapore sovereign AUM” | Three different legal persons | No official combined AUM | Any homemade USD stack of GIC + Temasek + MAS |

MAS’s IMF-template page is the same reserve book in another cut: official reserve assets **US$426,247.40 million** at **June 2026** (preliminary). That is **not** a second pile. Do not add it to the July monthly print.

Print the MAS number as **official foreign reserves**. It is not GIC AUM and it is not Temasek NAV.

## What the MOF page actually says

The Ministry of Finance is explicit. [MAS](https://www.mof.gov.sg/policies/reserves/who-manages-the-reserves/) manages the Official Foreign Reserves and is “the most conservative of the three investment entities.” **GIC Private Limited** is “a professional fund manager that manages Government assets.” Temasek is the third book. Those are three mandates. They are not one **$1.08 trillion + $810 billion + $403 billion** stack.

A landscape page that needs GIC **$810 billion** and MAS **$1.08 trillion** to make the contrast is consultancy arithmetic.

## What is not official

- GIC AUM in any dollar, including **$810 billion**.
- “Government of Singapore Investment Company” as the current legal name.
- Temasek **$403 billion** as the current print.
- MAS reserves of **$1.08 trillion**.
- Adding GIC, Temasek, and MAS into one Singapore sovereign AUM.
