# Texas Emergency Services Retirement System, explained

Texas Emergency Services Retirement System is Texas’ volunteer emergency-services pension, not leftover Texas ERS, leftover TMRS, and not leftover TCDRS. The official [31 August 2025 TESRS audited annual financial report](https://www.tesrs.org/_files/ugd/d3d16a_39714a2965c144ae8a6f42b259ad84e0.pdf) prints fiduciary net position of **$152,259,760** at **31 August 2025**. Investments **$148,932,352** are not that card. Cash in State Treasury **$1,070,107** is not investments. The agency governmental-fund net position is not the pension trust. Money-weighted return was **13.25 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. GASB **75.8 percent** is of this book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Texas ERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/tesrs/`, `/texas-tesrs-explained/`, or `/ers/`. The 2025 GASB 68 report is not this audit.

## What is official (31 August 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position, Schedule 1, the GASB 67 schedule, and the money-weighted schedule are the sourced lines:

1. **Fiduciary net position** **$152,259,760** at **31 August 2025** (beginning **$138,468,453**). Investments **$148,932,352**. Total assets **$152,366,507**. Total liabilities **$106,747**. MD&A **$152.3 million** is the rounded print, not the statement. Do not treat leftover Texas ERS **$43,165,849,614**, leftover TMRS **$49,405,467,577**, leftover Oklahoma OPERS Combined **$13,351,556,235**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$200,881,618**. Net pension liability **$48,621,858**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **75.8 percent**.
3. **Returns.** Notes and RSI money-weighted return **13.25 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **31 August 2025** (2024 **11.85 percent**).

Do not flatten TESRS into [Texas ERS](/texas-ers/), [Texas Municipal Retirement System](/texas-tmrs/), [Oklahoma OPERS](/oklahoma-opers/), or [Oklahoma Firefighters Pension and Retirement System](/oklahoma-firefighters/). Texas ERS is the statewide state-employee file. TMRS is the statewide municipal file. Oklahoma OPERS and Oklahoma Firefighters are Oklahoma files. This is **one Texas volunteer emergency-services file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “TESRS AUM” has to name the book. The **$152,259,760** line is pension-trust fiduciary net position at **31 August 2025**. The **$148,932,352** line is investments. The agency governmental-fund card is not the trust. The **13.25 percent** card is money-weighted. The **75.8 percent** card is GASB. Those are not the same mandate as Texas ERS or TMRS.
