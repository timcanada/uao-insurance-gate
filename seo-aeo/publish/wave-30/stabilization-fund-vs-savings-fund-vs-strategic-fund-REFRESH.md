# Stabilization vs savings vs strategic: kroner, two ISIF books, not $1.376 trillion

The mandate split is real. The live dollar table is not. Stabilization books smooth a budget when commodity revenue falls. Savings / heritage books compound a corpus. Strategic / development books take policy as well as return. The live page, dated 8 July 2026, still files [GPFG](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/) at **$1.376 trillion**, SOFAZ at **$45.3 billion**, ADIA typo’d as **“AUA, $172.4 billion”**, Temasek at **$509 billion**, [ISIF](/isif-ireland-strategic-investment-fund-explained/) at **€20 billion**, and Botswana’s Pula Fund at **$6.2 billion**. Those are tracker leftovers. They are not the filings.

## What the mandates can say

- **Stabilization.** Holds cash and liquid assets to smooth a budget. Chile’s [ESSF](/chile-economic-and-social-stabilization-fund-explained/) is the exhibit.
- **Savings.** Compounds a corpus for later generations. GPFG is the exhibit — in **kroner**, not dollars.
- **Strategic.** Takes domestic or policy objectives as well as return. [ISIF](/isif-ireland-strategic-investment-fund-explained/) is the two-book exhibit. [Temasek](/is-temasek-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/) is a holding company, not a generic “$509 billion strategic SWF.”

A page that needs **$1.376 trillion** and an **“AUA”** typo to teach that split is a tracker page.
