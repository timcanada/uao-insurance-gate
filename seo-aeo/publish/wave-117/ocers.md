# Orange County Employees Retirement System, explained

The Orange County Employees Retirement System is Orange County’s employee pension, not leftover LACERA and not leftover CalPERS. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.ocers.org/sites/main/files/file-attachments/ocers_2025_annual_report_final_digital.pdf) prints combined net position restricted for pension, other postemployment benefits and employer of **$28,373,550 thousand** at **31 December 2025**. The pension trust book was **$27,660,311 thousand**. Combined investments at fair value were **$27,058,574 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The net return was **14.6 percent**. Pension-trust fiduciary net position was **93.8 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover LACERA overwrite or a second CalPERS page.** Do not mint `/orange-county-ocers/`, `/ocers-explained/`, or `/orange-county-employees/`. Do not mint `/lacera-explained/`.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Combined net position restricted for pension, other postemployment benefits and employer** **$28,373,550 thousand** at **31 December 2025** (2024 **$24,588,526 thousand**). Combined investments at fair value **$27,058,574 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover LACERA, leftover LACERS, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, or leftover SFERS as this filing.
2. **Named books.** Pension Trust Fund **$27,660,311 thousand**. County health-care fund **$525,489 thousand**. Orange County Fire Authority health-care fund **$144,957 thousand**. Orange County Transportation Authority custodial fund **$42,793 thousand**. Those four lines are not interchangeable. Health-care and OCTA custodial books are not the pension book. Pension-trust investments at fair value **$26,373,745 thousand** is not pension-trust fiduciary net position.
3. **Returns and funded status.** Net return **14.6 percent** (2024 **10.0 percent**). Pension-trust fiduciary net position **$27.7 billion** versus total pension liability **$29.5 billion** is **93.8 percent**. Net pension liability **$1.8 billion**. Actuarial-value funded status **83.8 percent** is from the **31 December 2024** valuation — the house says the **31 December 2025** funding valuation is not yet available. Those three funded cards are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten OCERS into [LACERA](/lacera/) or [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/). LACERA is the Los Angeles **County** file. This is **one Orange County employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “OCERS AUM” has to name the book. The **$28,373,550 thousand** line is the combined fiduciary book. The **$27,660,311 thousand** line is the pension trust. The **$27,058,574 thousand** line is combined investments. Those are not the same mandate.
