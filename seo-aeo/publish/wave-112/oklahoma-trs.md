# Teachers' Retirement System of Oklahoma, explained

The Teachers' Retirement System of Oklahoma is Oklahoma’s teacher pension, not leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion** and not leftover Oklahoma OPERS. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/trs/documents/TRS.ACFR.pdf) prints combined net position restricted for pensions and OPEB of **$25,181,260,433** at **30 June 2025**. The defined-benefit pension book is **$24,669,593,730**. The OPEB book is **$511,666,703**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The portfolio net return was **10.7 percent**. The pension money-weighted return was **10.98 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Texas TRS overwrite or a second Oklahoma OPERS page.** The Texas TRS keeper is `/texas-teacher-retirement-system-explained/`. The Oklahoma OPERS keeper is `/oklahoma-opers/`. Do not mint `/oklahoma-trs-explained/`, `/oktrs/`, or `/oklahoma-teachers-retirement/`. Do not mint `/texas-trs/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combining statement is the sourced split:

1. **Combined net position restricted for pensions and OPEB** **$25,181,260,433** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$22,963,616,852**). That total covers the defined-benefit pension plan and the OPEB plan. Do not treat leftover Texas TRS **$295 billion**, leftover Oklahoma OPERS **$13,351,556,235**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books on the same report.** Defined-benefit pension **$24,669,593,730**. OPEB **$511,666,703**. Those two lines are not interchangeable with the combined fiduciary total. Do not add the OPEB book to the pension book a second time and call the sum a second AUM.
3. **Returns.** Portfolio net return **10.7 percent**. Policy benchmark **12.6 percent**. Three-year, five-year, and ten-year annualized lines **10.0 percent**, **9.9 percent**, and **7.6 percent**. Pension money-weighted return **10.98 percent** net. OPEB money-weighted return **10.9 percent** net. Those time-weighted and money-weighted prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Oklahoma TRS into [Texas TRS](/texas-teacher-retirement-system-explained/) or [Oklahoma OPERS](/oklahoma-opers/). Texas TRS is the Texas teacher file. Oklahoma OPERS is the Oklahoma public-employee file. This is **one Oklahoma teacher file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Oklahoma teacher AUM” has to name the book. The **$25.18 billion** line is the combined pension-and-OPEB fiduciary total. The **$24.67 billion** line is the defined-benefit pension. The **$13.35 billion** line is Oklahoma OPERS. Those are not the same mandate.
