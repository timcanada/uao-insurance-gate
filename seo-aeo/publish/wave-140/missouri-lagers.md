# Missouri LAGERS, explained

Missouri Local Government Employees Retirement System is Missouri’s local-government pension, not leftover MOSERS, leftover Missouri PSRS and PEERS, and not leftover Iowa IPERS. The official [30 June 2025 LAGERS Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.molagers.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/LAGERS-2025-ACFR-Website.pdf) prints LAGERS Trust fiduciary net position of **$11,341,575,751** at **30 June 2025**. Staff Retirement Plan **$34,737,540** and Staff Retiree Healthcare Supplement **$4,105,782** are not this book. Required supplementary money-weighted return was **6.38 percent** net of investment expenses. Highlights **6.4 percent** and the custodian’s modified Dietz **6.4 percent** are not that card. The **28 February 2025** actuarial **92.3 percent** card is not a June GASB funded-ratio card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The LAGERS Trust column, the required supplementary return schedule, and the February actuarial table are the sourced lines:

1. **LAGERS Trust fiduciary net position** **$11,341,575,751** at **30 June 2025** (beginning restated **$10,816,660,978**; previously reported **$10,817,417,105**). Investments at fair value **$11,274,435,571**. Staff Retirement Plan **$34,737,540** is not this book. Staff Retiree Healthcare **$4,105,782** is not this book. Do not treat leftover MOSERS fiduciary activities **$9,565,800,614**, leftover Missouri PSRS and PEERS, leftover IPERS **$46,740,552,174**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **Funded cards.** Actuarial value of assets **$11,427,874,294** and actuarial accrued liability **$12,385,498,094** at **28 February 2025** print funded **92.3 percent**. That is a smoothed actuarial card, not a 30 June GASB fiduciary-net-position-to-total-pension-liability card. LAGERS is an agent multiple-employer plan. The house report does not print one Trust-wide GASB funded ratio. Staff Retirement Plan GASB **106.04 percent** is not this card.
3. **Returns.** Required supplementary annual money-weighted return **6.38 percent** net of investment expenses for the year ended **30 June 2025**. Highlights and MD&A **6.4 percent** and the investment-section modified Dietz **6.4 percent** are not the RSI card. Assumed **7.0 percent** is not a realized return.

Do not flatten LAGERS into [MOSERS](/missouri-mosers/), [Missouri PSRS and PEERS](/missouri-psrs-peers/), or [Iowa IPERS](/iowa-ipers/). MOSERS is the Missouri state-employee file. PSRS and PEERS are the Missouri school file. IPERS is Iowa. This is **one Missouri local-government file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Missouri local pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$11,341,575,751** line is LAGERS Trust fiduciary net position. The **$11,274,435,571** line is Trust investments. The **$34,737,540** line is staff pension. The **6.38 percent** card is money-weighted. The **6.4 percent** card is the rounded / Dietz print. The **92.3 percent** card is February actuarial. Those are not the same mandate.
