# What is an Institutional Talent Desk?

An Institutional Talent Desk is Universal Asset Owners’ named careers desk for long-horizon hiring: confidential senior mandates on the employer side, and private counsel on the candidate side. It sits on [Careers](/careers/), not inside the newsroom. Featured placements are labelled. Candidate information is never shared without explicit consent. It is not a promise of a job.

**Do not treat this page as a second Careers overwrite.** Do not mint `/talent-desk-explained/`, `/institutional-talent-desk-explained/`, or `/what-is-the-uao-talent-desk/`. Do not invent a live-role count or a pay table.

## What the live Careers page already names

The live [Careers](/careers/) page is the product file. Lift only what it prints:

- **Institutional Talent Desk** — the named desk for hiring, a confidential move, or a search-firm partnership
- Inbox: **info@universalassetowners.com**
- Guides on request: Recruitment Advertising Guide; Executive Career Advisory Guide; Recruitment Partnerships Guide
- **Private by default.** No public candidate profile. **No CV shared without consent**
- Advisory services — not a promise of employment, interviews, or introductions
- **“No individually verified roles are live right now.”** Individual roles publish only once they are confirmed and source-verified
- Tracked-institution links deep-link to each employer’s own portal (including Mubadala, GIC, NBIM, CPP Investments, OTPP, Temasek, CalPERS, Future Fund, plus Allocator Jobs, CFA, SWFI, and IFSWF boards)

[Readership](/readership/) is the circulation file for the morning room those people already sit in (including **98%** of SWFs and **20 of 20** largest pensions, method current as of **August 2026**). It is not a licence to invent a subscriber total on this page.

## What the desk is not

- A second newsroom beat
- A recommended-recruiter list
- A rental of the subscriber file
- A place to preview an unpublished people-move as journalism

[Ethics](/ethics/) (version 1.0, **28 May 2026**) is the test: payment never influences editorial coverage. Featured and sponsored careers units are always labelled.

## Who should write

Employers and search firms: request the Recruitment Advertising Guide, or read [how recruiters reach sovereign-fund talent](/how-do-recruiters-reach-sovereign-fund-talent/). Category map: [executive search](/directory/executive-search/).

Senior executives: request the Executive Career Advisory Guide. If the next seat is a family-office CIO or a sovereign counsel chair, those jobs are already defined — do not invent a compensation shortcut.
