# Champaign Police Pension Fund, explained

The Champaign Police Pension Fund is Champaign, Illinois’s Article 3 police pension, not leftover Springfield Police, leftover Grayslake Police, and not leftover Illinois IPOPIF. The official [30 June 2025 Champaign Police Pension Fund financial statements](https://champaignpolicepensionfund.org/cppf/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Audit-2025.pdf) print plan net position restricted for pension benefits of **$185,698,617** at **30 June 2025**. Cash and investments **$185,981,922** are not that card. The Illinois Police Officers’ Pension Investment Fund holding **$185,893,031** is a component, not a second AUM. The latest printed total pension liability is the **30 June 2024** GASB schedule, not a 2025 AUM. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
