# City of Sarasota General Employees’ Defined Benefit Pension Plan, explained

The City of Sarasota General Employees’ Defined Benefit Pension Plan is Sarasota’s civilian defined-benefit pension, not leftover Gainesville Employees, leftover Tampa General Employees, and not leftover Florida SBA. The official [30 September 2025 City of Sarasota General Employees’ Defined Benefit Pension Plan financial statements](https://www.sarasotafl.gov/files/assets/city/v/2/cac/documents/pensions/general-pension/2025-ge-financial-statements-ada.pdf) print fiduciary net position of **$192,591,188** at **30 September 2025**. Investments **$190,935,467** are not that card. Money-weighted return was **8.74 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. The investment-manager **8.1 percent** card is not that line. GASB **87.82 percent** is not the valuation-market **86.7 percent** card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Florida SBA overwrite.** Do not mint `/sarasota-ge/`, `/sarasota-employees-explained/`, or `/sarasota-gepp/`. Do not mint `/ers/`.

## What is official (30 September 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position, the GASB 67 note, and the money-weighted schedule are the sourced lines:

1. **Fiduciary net position** **$192,591,188** at **30 September 2025** (beginning **$184,668,435**). Investments at fair value **$190,935,467**. General employees hired on or after **1 December 2021** enter the Florida Retirement System; this page is the City’s remaining defined-benefit book. Do not treat leftover Gainesville Employees **$764,938,276**, leftover Tampa General Employees, leftover Miami GESE, leftover Florida SBA **$300 billion+**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$219,310,909**. Net pension liability **$26,719,721**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **87.82 percent**. The actuarial-valuation market card **86.7 percent** is not the GASB card.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **8.74 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 September 2025** (2024 **20.20 percent**). MD&A investment-manager **8.1 percent** is not the notes card.

Do not flatten the City of Sarasota General Employees’ Defined Benefit Pension Plan into [Employees’ Pension Plan of the City of Gainesville](/gainesville-employees/), [Tampa General Employees’ Retirement Fund](/tampa-general-employees/), [City of Miami General Employees’ and Sanitation Employees’ Retirement Trust](/miami-gese/), or [Florida State Board of Administration](/florida-state-board-of-administration-explained/). Gainesville Employees is a Gainesville civilian file. Tampa General Employees is a Tampa civilian file. Miami GESE is a Miami civilian file. Florida SBA is the statewide investment file. This is **one Sarasota civilian file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Sarasota pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$192,591,188** line is fiduciary net position. The **$190,935,467** line is investments. The **8.74 percent** card is money-weighted. The **8.1 percent** card is the manager print. The **87.82 percent** card is GASB. The **86.7 percent** card is valuation-market. Those are not the same mandate.
