# Vermont State Teachers’ Retirement System, explained

Vermont State Teachers’ Retirement System is Vermont’s educator pension, not leftover Vermont State Retirement System, leftover Vermont Municipal Employees, and not leftover ERSRI. The official [30 June 2025 State of Vermont Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://auditor.vermont.gov/sites/auditor/files/Fiscal%20Year%202025%C2%A0Annual%20Comprehensive%20Financial%20Report.pdf) prints defined-benefit fiduciary net position of **$3,136,315,717** at **30 June 2025**. Money-weighted return was **10.76 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Plan-reporting net pension liability was **$1,625,927 thousand** at the **7.00 percent** discount. Employer-reporting **61.65 percent** at **30 June 2024** is not the 2025 plan card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The defined-benefit combining statements and the GASB 67 notes are the sourced lines:

1. **Defined-benefit fiduciary net position** **$3,136,315,717** at **30 June 2025** (beginning **$2,817,685,669**). Net investment income **$309,558,597**. Leftover Vermont State Retirement System **$2,944,071,052** is not this book. Leftover Municipal Employees **$1,121,818,646** is not this book. Single Deposit Investment Account **$21,140,055** is not this book. Do not treat leftover ERSRI **$14,686,484,613**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, leftover MainePERS, leftover New Hampshire, leftover Minnesota TRA, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Plan-reporting net pension liability **$1,625,927 thousand** at the **7.00 percent** discount (**6.00 percent** sensitivity **$2,203,732 thousand**; **8.00 percent** **$1,143,940 thousand**). Employer-reporting fiduciary net position **$2,817,686 thousand** / **61.65 percent** at the **30 June 2024** measurement is not the 2025 plan card. This page does not invent a 2025 GASB funded ratio that the combining statement does not print.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **10.76 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **10.23 percent**). State Employees **10.65 percent** and Municipal Employees **10.69 percent** are not this card.

Do not flatten the Vermont State Teachers’ Retirement System into [Vermont State Retirement System](/vermont-vsers/), [Employees’ Retirement System of Rhode Island](/rhode-island-ersri/), [MainePERS](/maine-pers/), or [New Hampshire Retirement System](/new-hampshire-retirement-system/). ## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Vermont teachers AUM” has to name the book. The **$3,136,315,717** line is Teachers defined-benefit fiduciary net position. The **$1,625,927 thousand** line is plan-reporting net pension liability. The **61.65 percent** card is the 2024 employer measurement. The **10.76 percent** card is money-weighted.
