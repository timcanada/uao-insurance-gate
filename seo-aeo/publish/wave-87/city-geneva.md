# Relocating to Geneva for a Swiss social-security or family-office desk

Geneva is the home city of [compenswiss](https://www.compenswiss.ch/en/home), the Confederation’s independent public-law institution that manages the OASI, invalidity-insurance and loss-of-earnings compensation funds. Official contact is **Boulevard Georges-Favon 6, 1204 Geneva**. [PUBLICA](https://www.publica.ch/en/contact), the federal pension fund, lists **Bern**. [Zurich](/careers/cities/zurich/) is the markets floor. A move here is often a move onto a Swiss social-security reserve file, a family office, or a window other owners staff. Right to work sits with official Swiss sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit elsewhere

- compenswiss is a public-law institution of the Confederation, registered in Geneva. It is a liquidity-and-reserve book for three social-security schemes, not a classic sovereign wealth fund. Official prints live on compenswiss.ch. Do not invent an AUM on this page. Do not mint `/geneva-compenswiss/` or a compenswiss explainer slug.
- PUBLICA is Bern. **Do not treat this city page as a second PUBLICA explainer.**
- Family offices and multi-family offices cluster here. The job is still [a family office](/what-is-a-family-office/), not a product shelf. Do not invent a family-office AUM. Do not mint `/family-office/`.

Global owners staff Geneva as a *window* more often than as HQ. Ask whether you are building a book or staffing a time zone.

## Papers

Start at the [State Secretariat for Migration — working in Switzerland](https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/en/home/overview-arbeit.html). Cantonal rules still apply. Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Why people underestimate the job

French is the language of legitimacy in this canton even when the dealing language is English. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail. Confidentiality is not a preference inside a family office.
