# How do public pensions publish holdings?

A public pension publishes holdings because the book is a public file — annual investment reports, financial statements, and portfolio or partnership lists — not because every owner files the same SEC form. CalPERS already posts those reports on its official Investment & Financial Reports page, including a PERF Portfolio & Partnership Report as of 31 December 2025. A family office and many sovereign funds do not publish that file. This page is not a download of anyone’s book and not investment advice.

## What CalPERS already posts

CalPERS’ [Investment & Financial Reports](https://www.calpers.ca.gov/investments/about-investment-office/investment-financial-reports) page is the sourced example. Three facts from that official index:

1. The system posts **Annual Investment Reports**, **Basic Financial Statements**, and **Annual Comprehensive Financial Reports** — the 2024–25 vintage of each is on the page.
2. It also posts **Investment Committee** program reviews (private equity, private debt, real estate, infrastructure, public equity, fixed income) and a **Quarterly Trust Level Review**.
3. Separately, CalPERS publishes a [PERF Portfolio & Partnership Report as of 31 December 2025](https://www.calpers.ca.gov/documents/perf-portfolio-partnership-report-as-of-december-31-2025/download?inline=). That file lists portfolios and partnerships. **Do not scrape it for a UAO ranking or reprint its dollar lines here.**

That is the public-pension form: the house publishes its own book. It is not Form PF (a private-fund adviser filing) and not Form N-PX (a registered-fund proxy vote report). Those are different statutes. Do not paste them onto CalPERS.

A [pension fund](/what-is-a-pension-fund/) exists to pay **named members**. A U.S. governmental plan sits outside ERISA Title I. That already sits on [what an administrator is for a pension plan](/what-is-an-administrator-for-a-pension-plan/). Public records and a board pack are why the file is public. A family office publishes nothing of the kind. Many sovereign funds publish an annual report and not a line-by-line book.

How the house then picks an external sleeve sits on [manager selection](/manager-selection-institutional/). How the search file is built sits on [what a consultant database is](/what-is-a-consultant-database/).
