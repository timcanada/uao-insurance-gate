# City of Atlanta Firefighters’ Pension Plan, explained

The City of Atlanta Firefighters’ Pension Plan is Atlanta’s sworn firefighter pension, not leftover Georgia ERS and not leftover Atlanta Police Officers’ Pension Plan. The official [30 June 2025 financial statements](https://coa.sba-inc.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/FY2025-Firefighters-Pension-Plan-Final-Report.pdf) print net position restricted for pensions of **$948,512 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Statement investments were **$938,848 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return was **11.80 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **80.57 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Georgia ERS overwrite or a leftover Atlanta Police overwrite.** Do not mint `/atlanta-fire/` or `/atlanta-firefighters-explained/`. Statement prints are in thousands. Statement investments **$938,848 thousand** are not fiduciary net position. Management-discussion overall-portfolio **11.80 percent** is not a second method from money-weighted **11.80 percent**. Do not invent a combined Atlanta sworn-plus-civilian AUM. The July 2026 newsletter and the July 1, 2025 valuation are not this audit.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position restricted for pensions** **$948,512 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$872,739 thousand**). The change is **$75,773 thousand**. Statement investments **$938,848 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$954,611 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover Georgia ERS combined **$26,469,880 thousand**, leftover Georgia TRS, leftover Atlanta Police Officers **$1,614,945 thousand**, leftover Atlanta General Employees **$1,992,291 thousand**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$1,177,304 thousand**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **80.57 percent** (2024 **70.80 percent**). Net pension liability **$228,792 thousand**. Those official prints are not interchangeable with a later police or general-employee card.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **11.80 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **11.30 percent**). Management-discussion overall-portfolio **11.80 percent** is that money-weighted print, not a second method.

Do not flatten the City of Atlanta Firefighters’ Pension Plan into [Georgia ERS](/georgia-ers/), [Georgia TRS](/georgia-trs/), [Atlanta Police Officers’ Pension Plan](/atlanta-police/), or [Atlanta General Employees’ Pension Plan](/atlanta-general-employees/). Georgia ERS is the statewide public-employee administrator. The police and general-employee books are different legal persons on the same house site. This is **one Atlanta sworn-firefighter file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Atlanta fire AUM” has to name the book. The **$948,512 thousand** line is net position restricted for pensions. The **$938,848 thousand** line is statement investments. The **80.57 percent** card is the GASB ratio. The **11.80 percent** card is money-weighted. Those are not the same mandate.
