# Illinois Firefighters’ Pension Investment Fund, explained

The Firefighters’ Pension Investment Fund is Illinois’s consolidated investment trust for Article 4 suburban and downstate firefighter pension funds, not leftover Illinois SERS and not leftover Houston Firefighters. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://ifpif.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Audit-25-Audit-FPIF.pdf) prints net position held in trust for pool participants of **$10,286,871,117** at **30 June 2025**. Investments at fair value were **$10,204,507,676**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted return was **13.3 percent** net of fees. The money-weighted return was **13.30 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Illinois SERS overwrite or a leftover Houston Firefighters overwrite.** Do not mint `/fpif/`, `/ifpif/`, or `/illinois-firefighters-pension-investment-fund/`. Do not mint `/hfrrf/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position held in trust for pool participants** **$10,286,871,117** at **30 June 2025** (beginning **$8,988,944,793**). The MD&A card **$10.287 billion** is that line rounded. Investments at fair value **$10,204,507,676** is not net position. Cash and cash equivalents **$15,206,503** is not net position. Total assets **$10,301,375,239** is not net position. Those four lines are not interchangeable. Public Act **101-0610** created this investment trust. It is not a pension plan with its own total pension liability. Do not treat leftover Illinois SERS, leftover Illinois JRS, leftover IMRF, leftover Illinois IPOPIF, leftover Houston Firefighters, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **This is a pool, not a funded-ratio card.** Member-fund contributions **$699.4 million**. Member-fund withdrawals **$604.8 million**. Net investment income about **$1.2 billion**. Those flows change pool net position. They are not a GASB funded ratio for the local Article 4 plans. The IMRF net pension liability **$325,254** on this statement is FPIF’s own staff plan, not the firefighter pool.
3. **Returns.** Time-weighted return **13.3 percent** net of fees against the house policy benchmark **12.9 percent**. Money-weighted return **13.30 percent** net of pension-fund investment expense (2024 **13.00 percent**). Those two official one-year prints are not interchangeable even where they round to the same first decimal.

Do not flatten FPIF into [Illinois SERS](/illinois-sers/), [Illinois JRS](/illinois-jrs/), [IMRF](/illinois-imrf/), [Illinois IPOPIF](/illinois-ipopif/), [Illinois State Board of Investment](/illinois-isbi/), [Houston Firefighters](/houston-hfrrf/), [Cook County Pension](/cook-county-pension/), or [Chicago Fire FABF](/chicago-fire-fabf/). IPOPIF is the Article 3 police pool. IMRF is the local-government pension. Houston Firefighters is Texas. Cook County is the Cook County employee file. FABF is the Chicago fire file, not this Article 4 pool. This is **one Illinois firefighter investment-trust file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “FPIF AUM” has to name the book. The **$10,286,871,117** line is net position held in trust for pool participants. The **$10,204,507,676** line is investments. Those are not the same mandate. This desk does not add the local Article 4 funded ratios onto this pool.
