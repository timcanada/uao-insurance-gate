# Missouri PSRS and PEERS, explained

The Public School Retirement System of Missouri and the Public Education Employee Retirement System of Missouri are Missouri’s school pensions, not leftover MOSERS and not leftover Illinois TRS. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.psrs-peers.org/docs/default-source/investments-documents/2025-acfr/psrs_peers_2025-annual-comprehensive-financial-report.pdf) prints combined net position restricted for pensions of **$63,124,090,036** at **30 June 2025**. PSRS is **$55,571,751,625**. PEERS is **$7,552,338,411**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted and money-weighted returns both printed **10.7 percent** net of all investment expenses and fees. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover MOSERS overwrite or a second Illinois TRS page.** The Missouri state-employee keeper is `/missouri-mosers/`. Do not mint `/missouri-psrs/`, `/psrs-peers/`, `/missouri-peers/`, or `/psrs-missouri/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combining statement is the sourced split:

1. **Combined net position restricted for pensions** **$63,124,090,036** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$58,996,505,874**). PSRS **$55,571,751,625**. PEERS **$7,552,338,411**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover MOSERS **$9,565,800,614**, leftover Illinois TRS, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **Commingled funds, two books.** The systems’ funds are commingled for investment. Each system owns an equity position in the pool. Do not add the PSRS book to the PEERS book a second time and call the sum a second AUM.
3. **Returns and funded ratios.** Time-weighted return **10.7 percent** net of all investment expenses and fees. Money-weighted return **10.7 percent** net of all investment expenses and fees. The filing printed the same **10.7 percent** on both lines — do not invent a difference. The gross print was **10.9 percent**. PSRS fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **90.9 percent**. PEERS **91.5 percent**. Those funded-ratio lines are not AUM.

Do not flatten PSRS/PEERS into [MOSERS](/missouri-mosers/), [Missouri LAGERS](/missouri-lagers/), [Illinois TRS](/illinois-trs/), [Nebraska School Employees Retirement Plan](/nebraska-school/), or [Omaha School Employees Retirement Plan](/omaha-osers/). MOSERS is the Missouri state-employee file. LAGERS is the Missouri local-government file. Nebraska School is Nebraska statewide school. OSERS is Omaha Class V. This is **one Missouri school-pension file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Missouri school AUM” has to name the book. The **$63.12 billion** line is the combined fiduciary total. The **$55.57 billion** line is PSRS. The **$7.55 billion** line is PEERS. Those are not the same mandate.
