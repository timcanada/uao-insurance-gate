# Tennessee Consolidated Retirement System, explained

The Tennessee Consolidated Retirement System is Tennessee’s statewide public-employee pension, not the Tennessee Retiree Group Trust and not a second CalPERS page. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://treasury.tn.gov/Portals/0/Documents/Retirement/CAFR%20Reports/2025/2025TCRSReport_Full%20Report.pdf) prints fiduciary net position restricted for pensions of **$74,910,040,763** at **30 June 2025**. Investments in the Tennessee Retiree Group Trust are **$74,784,287,394**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The portfolio time-weighted return was **10.20 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second CalPERS overwrite or a TRGT overwrite.** The CalPERS keeper is `/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/`. Do not mint `/tcrs/`, `/tcrs-explained/`, or `/tennessee-consolidated-retirement-system/`. Do not mint `/trgt/` as a second TCRS page.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combining statement is the sourced split:

1. **Fiduciary net position restricted for pensions** **$74,910,040,763** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$69,569,214,199**). The combining statement in thousands prints the same close as **$74,910,041 thousand**. Do not treat leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, leftover Oregon **$95 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books on the same statement (thousands).** Public Employee Retirement Plan **$39,821,340 thousand**. Teacher Legacy Pension Plan **$33,595,527 thousand**. Teacher Retirement Plan **$1,493,174 thousand**. Investment in the Tennessee Retiree Group Trust **$74,784,288 thousand**. TCRS owns **98.72 percent** of TRGT. Do not call a later TRGT-only line the TCRS fiduciary net position.
3. **Returns and cash flows.** Time-weighted portfolio return **10.20 percent**. Net investment income **$7,034,606,453**. Contributions **$1,833,764,195**. Benefits and refunds **$3.5 billion**. The assumed return used on the valuation is **6.75 percent**. Those return and assumption lines are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten TCRS into [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/), [North Carolina Treasurer](/north-carolina-treasurer/), [City of Knoxville Pension System](/knoxville-pension/), [Metropolitan Employees’ Benefit Trust](/nashville-employees/), or [City of Memphis Retirement System](/memphis-retirement/). North Carolina’s Treasurer book is a different legal person. Knoxville, Nashville, and Memphis are Tennessee city files. Later Knoxville teachers, later Nashville certificated teachers, and later Memphis teachers sit here if applicable; the City of Knoxville Pension System, the Metropolitan Employees’ Benefit Trust, and the City of Memphis Retirement System are different legal persons. This is **one Tennessee consolidated file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Tennessee pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$74.9 billion** line is TCRS fiduciary net position. The **$74.8 billion** line is TCRS’s investment in TRGT. Those are not the same mandate.
