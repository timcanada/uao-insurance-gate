# SWF returns: quote the official series — GIC is not $990 billion

Return series are comparable only when the house printed them. The live AUM table is not a return series. The live page still opens with GPFG at **approximately $1.4 trillion as of late 2024**, ADIA at **roughly $171 billion**, SAFE reserves **in excess of $3.2 trillion**, GIC at **approximately $990 billion**, CPP at **$406 billion** as if it were a SWF, and Angola’s FSDEA at **approximately $6.9 billion**. Those dollars are not the filings.

## What a returns page can say

- **Quote the series the house prints.** [GIC](/gic-singapore-explained/) reports a rolling 20-year **real** return, time-weighted. [ADIA](/adia-abu-dhabi-investment-authority/) prints long-horizon annualised returns and refuses AUM. [GPFG](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/) prints kroner and a dated return.
- **Do not convert AUM into a return.** A page that needs **$990 billion** to talk about GIC’s five-year communication is a tracker page.
- **Do not file pensions as SWFs.** CPP is a pension. CalPERS PERF printed **14.8%** at **30 June 2026** on the live institution page — that is a pension return, not a SWF league-table win.

This desk did not fetch a current FSDEA official print this wake. Do not reprint **$6.9 billion**.
