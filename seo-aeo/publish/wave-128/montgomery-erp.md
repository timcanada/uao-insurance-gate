# Montgomery County Employee Retirement Plans, explained

Montgomery County Employee Retirement Plans is Montgomery County, Maryland’s employees’ retirement, savings, and deferred-compensation house, not leftover Maryland SRPS, leftover Fairfax ERS, leftover Virginia Retirement System, leftover Pennsylvania SERS, and not leftover MassPRIM. The official [FY2025 ACFR](https://assets.montgomerycountymd.gov/files/2026-05/FY25_Annual_Comprehensive_Financial_Report_RAD.pdf) prints Employees’ Retirement System net position restricted for pensions of **$5,109,687,492** at **30 June 2025**. The Retirement Savings Plan was **$883,770,115**. The Deferred Compensation Plan was **$707,838,612**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. Employees’ Retirement System investments were **$5,179,117,337**. Money-weighted return was **8.87 percent** net. Time-weighted return was **9.2 percent**. Fiduciary net position was **94.2 percent** of total pension liability. Actuarial funded **95.6 percent** is not the GASB card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Maryland SRPS overwrite.** Do not mint `/mcerp/`, `/montgomery-mers/`, `/montgomery-county/`, or `/mcers/`. The statement does not print a combined total column. Do not add the three books into a second AUM.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statements of fiduciary net position are the sourced split:

1. **Employees’ Retirement System net position restricted for pensions** **$5,109,687,492** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$4,915.3 million**). Retirement Savings Plan **$883,770,115**. Deferred Compensation Plan **$707,838,612**. Employees’ Retirement System investments **$5,179,117,337** is not fiduciary net position. Those prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover Maryland SRPS **$73,240,774 thousand**, leftover Fairfax ERS, leftover VRS, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards, Employees’ Retirement System only.** Total pension liability **$5,426,796,673**. Net pension liability **$317,109,181**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **94.2 percent**. Actuarial funded **95.6 percent** (actuarial assets **$5.2 billion** / actuarial liability **$5.5 billion**) is not the GASB card. Fair-value funded **93.2 percent** is not the GASB card. The defined-contribution books do not carry that pension GASB card.
3. **Returns, Employees’ Retirement System.** Notes money-weighted return **8.87 percent** net of pension-plan investment expenses. Investment-section time-weighted return **9.2 percent**. The transmittal **10.3 percent** print is the NEPC peer-median return for U.S. public plans over **$1 billion**, printed **gross of fees**, not this house return. Those official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Montgomery County Employee Retirement Plans into [Maryland SRPS](/maryland-srps/), [Fairfax ERS](/fairfax-ers/), [Virginia Retirement System](/virginia-retirement-system/), or [Pennsylvania SERS](/pennsylvania-sers/). Maryland SRPS is the statewide file. Fairfax ERS is a Virginia county civilian file. This is **one Montgomery County house file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Montgomery County AUM” has to name the book. The **$5,109,687,492** line is the Employees’ Retirement System. The **$883,770,115** line is the Retirement Savings Plan. The **$707,838,612** line is the Deferred Compensation Plan. The **94.2 percent** card is the GASB ratio. The **95.6 percent** card is the actuarial ratio. The **8.87 percent** card is the money-weighted return. The **9.2 percent** card is the time-weighted return. Those are not the same mandate.
