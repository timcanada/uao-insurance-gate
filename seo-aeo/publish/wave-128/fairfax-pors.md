# Fairfax County Police Officers Retirement System, explained

Fairfax County Police Officers Retirement System is Fairfax County’s sworn-police pension, not leftover Virginia Retirement System, leftover Fairfax ERS, leftover Fairfax URS, leftover Maryland SRPS, leftover Pennsylvania SERS, and not leftover PSERS. The official [FY2025 ACFR](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/retirement/sites/retirement/files/Assets/PORS%20ACFR%20-%202025.pdf) prints net position restricted for pension benefits of **$1,966,417,163** at **30 June 2025**. Statement total investments were **$1,994,449,513**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. RSI money-weighted return was **5.73 percent**. Time-weighted return was **8.88 percent** net of fees. Fiduciary net position was **77.30 percent** of total pension liability. Actuarial funded **77.64 percent** is not the GASB card. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position restricted for pension benefits** **$1,966,417,163** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$1,828,703,426**). The change is **$137,713,737**, or **7.5 percent**. Statement total investments **$1,994,449,513** is not fiduciary net position. MD&A total cash and investments **$1,973,882,583** is not fiduciary net position. The investment-section / note total-investments print **$1,971,089,303** is the transmittal **$1.971 billion** card, not the statement investments line. Total assets **$2,129,963,684** is not fiduciary net position. Those prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover VRS, leftover Fairfax ERS, leftover Maryland SRPS, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$2,544,012,059**. Net pension liability **$577,594,896**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **77.30 percent** (2024 **76.95 percent**). Actuarial funded **77.64 percent** is not the GASB card.
3. **Returns.** Required-supplementary and notes money-weighted return **5.73 percent** for fiscal 2025, net of investment expense. Transmittal, MD&A, and investment-section time-weighted return **8.88 percent** net of fees. Those official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Fairfax PORS into [Virginia Retirement System](/virginia-retirement-system/), [Fairfax ERS](/fairfax-ers/), [Fairfax URS](/fairfax-urs/), [Maryland SRPS](/maryland-srps/), or [PSERS](/pennsylvania-psers/). VRS is the statewide file. Fairfax ERS is the civilian employee file. Fairfax URS is the uniformed file. This is **one Fairfax sworn-police file**.
