# Duke University endowment, explained

The Duke University endowment is Duke University’s long-horizon book, not The Duke Endowment (Charlotte philanthropy), leftover Harvard **$56.9 billion**, leftover Northwestern Long-Term Balanced Pool **$15,263 million**. The official [2024–25 financial report](https://resources.finance.duke.edu/resources/docs/Financial_Reports2024-25.pdf) prints market value of endowment of **$12,318,455 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Investments **$19,430,443 thousand** are not that card. The Long-Term Pool returned **9.0 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.
