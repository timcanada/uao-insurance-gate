# Virginia Retirement System, explained

The Virginia Retirement System is Virginia’s public-employee retirement system, not a second CalPERS page and not leftover MassPRIM. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.varetire.org/media/shared/pdf/publications/2025-annual-report.pdf) prints net position restricted for benefits of **$124,041,778 thousand** (**$124.0 billion**) at **30 June 2025**. The VRS-managed investment portfolio was **$128.6 billion**. The total fund returned **9.9 percent**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second CalPERS overwrite or a second PRIM overwrite.** Those keepers are `/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/` and `/massachusetts-prim/`. Do not mint `/vrs/`, `/vrs-explained/`, or `/virginia-retirement-system-explained/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statements are in thousands. The FY2025 ACFR is the sourced split:

1. **Net position restricted for benefits** **$124,041,778 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$114,278,237 thousand**). That is the System total across defined-benefit pension trust funds, other employee-benefit trust funds, and other custodial plans.
2. **VRS-managed investment portfolio** **$128.6 billion** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$116.5 billion**). That investment line is not the same as net position. Do not add the two.
3. **Return.** Total fund **9.9 percent** for the fiscal year ended **30 June 2025**, ahead of the custom benchmark’s **9.7 percent** and above the **6.75 percent** long-term assumed rate. Three-year return **8.6 percent**. Five-year **10.4 percent**. Net investment income **$11,472.4 million**.

Named pension books on the same file are not a second AUM: VRS plan **$114,401,121 thousand**; SPORS **$1,254,189 thousand**; VaLORS **$2,244,828 thousand**; JRS **$765,921 thousand**. Those four sit inside the System. Do not stack them on top of the **$124.0 billion** total.

Do not flatten VRS into [PRIM](/massachusetts-prim/), [OPERS](/ohio-opers/), [Fairfax ERS](/fairfax-ers/), [Fairfax PORS](/fairfax-pors/), [Fairfax URS](/fairfax-urs/), [Educational Employees’ Supplementary Retirement System of Fairfax County](/fairfax-erfc/), [Richmond Retirement System](/richmond-retirement/), [Norfolk Employees’ Retirement System](/norfolk-employees/), [Newport News Employees’ Retirement Fund](/newport-news-employees/), or [City of Roanoke Pension Plan](/roanoke-pension/). PRIM is one Massachusetts PRIT file. OPERS is one Ohio calendar-year file. Fairfax ERS, Fairfax PORS, and Fairfax URS are Fairfax County files. Fairfax ERFC is the FCPS school supplemental file. Richmond, Norfolk, Newport News, and Roanoke are Virginia city files. Later Newport News hires sit here; Newport News Employees’ Retirement Fund is a different legal person. Roanoke is still open and is not leftover VRS. This is **one Virginia System file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “VRS AUM” has to name the line. **$128.6 billion** is the managed investment portfolio. **$124.0 billion** is net position restricted for benefits. Those are not the same number.
