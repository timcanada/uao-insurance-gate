# Sacramento Regional Transit ATU Local 256 Retirement Plan, explained

The Retirement Plan for Sacramento Regional Transit District Employees who are Members of ATU Local 256 is SacRT’s ATU pension book, not leftover Sacramento County SCERS (**$14,577,459 thousand**), leftover VTA ATU, leftover CalPERS, leftover Sacramento City Employees. The official [financial statements as of 30 June 2025](https://www.sacrt.com/wp-content/uploads/FY-2025-Audited-Financial-Statements-for-Retirement-Plans.pdf) print ATU net position restricted for pension benefits of **$202,076,310** at **30 June 2025**. ATU investments **$202,561,330** are not that card. Notes and RSI money-weighted return was **11.90 percent**. Notes GASB **84.70 percent** is of the ATU column. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

ATU plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **84.70 percent**.
