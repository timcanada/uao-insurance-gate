# Accounting, tax and audit for long-horizon owners

Sovereign funds, pensions, insurers and family offices buy accounting and tax work that is shaped by liabilities, treaties, substance rules and public audit. This Universal Asset Owners hub covers the category: what desks actually purchase, what changes when a fund opens an office or a CIO relocates, and how firms should show up without confusing a directory listing for an audit opinion. Nothing here is tax advice.

## The work is not “year-end”

A public pension's financial statements are a political document as well as an accounting one. An insurer's book is a capital-charge document. A family office's stack is often a privacy document. A sovereign fund's look-through can be a diplomatic document.

Firms that treat these as a mid-market close will miss the room.

## Where tax shows up in an owner's year

- Opening or staffing an office in Singapore, Abu Dhabi, London, New York, or Toronto.
- A senior hire crossing a border (see [Careers Intelligence](/careers/intelligence/)).
- Treaty and withholding on cross-border private-market cash flows.
- Substance and the OECD BEPS project — start at the [OECD](https://www.oecd.org/tax/beps/), then use counsel.
- Pension risk transfer and insurer capital, where accounting and actuarial work sit on the same table.
