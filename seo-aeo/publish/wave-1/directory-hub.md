# The Universal Owner Directory

The Universal Owner Directory is Universal Asset Owners' public map of the firms that sit around long-horizon capital: law, accounting and tax, technology, investment banking, consulting, OCIO, custody, executive search and placement agents. It is a discovery layer for allocators and a labelled commercial surface for firms that want to be found. Editorial coverage is separate. Featured placements are marked paid.

## Why a directory belongs on a newsroom

Chief investment officers do not only read. They retain counsel on a cross-border commitment. They replace a risk system. They hire a search firm for a deputy. They take a call from a bank that understands the difference between a pension liability and a sovereign savings mandate.

Those jobs are poorly served by generic vendor lists and by firm blogs that read like pitches. They are also poorly served if a journalism site pretends the commercial world does not exist.

The Directory sits beside the newsroom, not inside it. A profile here is not a recommendation. A featured badge means someone paid for adjacency. The ethics page still governs anything that looks like coverage.

## Who it is for

**Allocators** — a short, serious list of categories you already buy, with links to explainers written in owner language.

**Service providers** — a place your future clients already are in the morning. Claim a profile. Buy labelled presence if the room is worth it. Do not expect a story in the brief as part of the package.

## How listings work

1. **Researched** — UAO may publish a short factual profile from the public record.
2. **Claimed** — a firm confirms its own facts. Still not an endorsement.
3. **Featured** — paid. Always labelled.

To claim or feature a listing, write to info@universalassetowners.com. Packages live on [Advertise](/advertise/).

## What we will not do

- Sell a “recommended” stamp.
- Let a listing rewrite a daily brief.
- Invent league tables.
- List a firm we cannot source.
