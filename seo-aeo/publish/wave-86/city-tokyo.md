# Relocating to Tokyo for a GPIF or Japan owner desk

Tokyo is the city of GPIF — the [Government Pension Investment Fund](/gpif-japan-explained/) — and a window other owners staff. GPIF is a public pension reserve, **not** a sovereign wealth fund. Official [profile](https://www.gpif.go.jp/en/about/profile.html) address: Toranomon Hills Mori Tower, 1-23-1 Toranomon, Minato-ku, Tokyo. Official prints are **yen**. A move here is often a move into that public file, or into a time zone for a house whose investment committee sits elsewhere. Right to work sits with official Japanese sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here

- [GPIF](/gpif-japan-explained/) — manages reserve funds of the government pension plans entrusted by the Minister of Health, Labour and Welfare. Established **1 April 2006**. Official prints are yen. Do not dollarize. Do not invent an AUM on this page. Do not mint `/government-pension-investment-fund-gpif/` or `/tokyo-gpif/`.
- [GIC](https://www.gic.com.sg/) and [Temasek](https://www.temasek.com.sg/) are Singapore houses. If the desk is theirs, read the [Singapore city page](/careers/cities/singapore/). Do not invent a GIC AUM.
- Korean public capital sits in Seoul, not here. Do not mint `/korea-investment-corporation-kic/`.

Do not invent a SAFE or CIC AUM to decorate a Tokyo skyline. Those prints live on the institution keepers — or they do not exist.

## Papers

Start at the [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/index.html). Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Why people underestimate the job

The city is easy to land in and easy to fail in if you treat the institution like a regional coverage job. GPIF’s first job is the public pension reserve, then return. Japanese is the language of legitimacy even when the dealing language is English. Satellites fail when the IC is elsewhere — the same problem our [London note](/careers/intelligence/why-london-satellites-fail/) already names.
