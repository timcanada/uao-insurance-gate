# What AUM means: quote the filing — there is no official $147 trillion

Assets under management is a snapshot of market value. That definition is real. The live pile is not. The live page still opens with **global AUM across all institutional managers at approximately $147 trillion in mid-2025**, then assigns BlackRock **approximately $11.6 trillion**, GPFG **approximately $1.76 trillion**, the 300 largest pension funds **over $24.4 trillion**, combined SWFs **$13 trillion**, and Temasek **S$434 billion** plus an extra **S$35 billion** mark-to-market gap. Those are leftovers. They are not the filings this desk will reprint.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

AUM is not interchangeable across legal forms. An asset **manager** reports client money it is paid to run. An asset **owner** reports the book it owns or owes. [GIC](/gic-singapore-explained/) is a manager of Government assets and **does not publish AUM**. [Temasek](/is-temasek-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/) owns what sits on its balance sheet and prints NPV. [GPFG](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/) prints a fund value in **kroner**. Do not convert those three into one homemade US-dollar league.

This page is the **definition**. The SWF methodology overwrite already sits on [`/how-sovereign-wealth-fund-aum-is-estimated/`](/how-sovereign-wealth-fund-aum-is-estimated/). **Do not 301 this URL onto that keeper.** They answer different queries. Do not mint `/aum-assets-under-management/` (404).

## What the definition can say

- **Whose money.** Manager AUM is usually client money. Owner AUM is the principal’s book. Do not add them.
- **What date.** AUM moves with markets and flows. The as-of date is part of the number.
- **What currency.** Reprint the currency the house printed. Do not dollarize GPFG or Temasek so a definition page can rank “largest.”
- **When to say no.** If the official site has no size widget, write **no official AUM**. That is the honest line for ADIA, KIA, QIA, SAFE, and GIC.

The TAI / WTW **US$24.4 trillion** top-300 figure is a **pension** study vintage already used on [`/what-is-a-pension-fund/`](/what-is-a-pension-fund/) and [`/largest-pension-funds-in-the-world/`](/largest-pension-funds-in-the-world/). It is not a global-manager pile and it is not a reason to keep **$147 trillion**.
