# City of Phoenix Employees’ Retirement System, explained

The City of Phoenix Employees’ Retirement System is Phoenix’s civilian employee pension, not leftover Arizona PSPRS and not leftover Arizona State Retirement System. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.phoenix.gov/content/dam/phoenix/coperssite/documents/COPERS%20ACFR%20FINAL%2012032025.pdf) prints fiduciary net position restricted for pensions of **$3,913,124 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Investments at fair value were **$3,818,481 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The fair-value investment return was **8.6 percent**. Fiduciary net position was **75.12 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Arizona PSPRS overwrite or a leftover Arizona State Retirement System overwrite.** Do not mint `/copers/` or `/phoenix-employees/`. Do not mint `/asrs/`. Do not treat leftover actuarial market-value **$3,913,123,278** as the statement line.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Net position restricted for pensions** **$3,913,124 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$3,615,815 thousand**), up **$297,309 thousand** / **8.2 percent**. Investments at fair value **$3,818,481 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Those two prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover Arizona PSPRS combined **$24,293,113 thousand**, leftover Arizona State Retirement System, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$5,209,416 thousand**. Net pension liability **$1,296,292 thousand**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **75.12 percent**. The discount-rate sensitivity table reprints the same **$5,209,416 thousand** total pension liability at the **7.00 percent** assumed rate; the **$5,824,031 thousand** and **$4,693,588 thousand** columns are not the primary GASB card. Those prints are not interchangeable.
3. **Returns and actuarial card.** Fair-value investment return **8.6 percent** (2024 **7.30 percent**). Money-weighted return **8.6 percent** net of investment expense (RSI **8.60 percent**). Those two official one-year prints are the same house rate, not two different performances. Actuarial funded **74.65 percent** on the smoothed actuarial value of assets is not the GASB **75.12 percent** card. Do not treat leftover valuation market-value **$3,913,123,278** as the statement fiduciary line.

Do not flatten Phoenix COPERS into [Arizona PSPRS](/arizona-psprs/), leftover Arizona State Retirement System, [Nevada PERS](/nevada-pers/), [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/), or [Denver Employees](/denver-employees/).
