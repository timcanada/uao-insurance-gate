# Employes’ Retirement System of the City of Milwaukee, explained

The Employes’ Retirement System of the City of Milwaukee is Milwaukee’s city employee pension, not leftover State of Wisconsin Investment Board. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.cmers.com/CMERS/Financial-Statements/CMERS-12-31-25-ACFR.pdf) prints combined total net position of **$6,458,509 thousand** at **31 December 2025**. Pension-restricted fiduciary net position was **$6,364,555 thousand**. Investments were **$6,514,171 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted return was **12.9 percent** net of fees. The money-weighted return was **12.8 percent** net of investment expense. Pension fiduciary net position was **77.8 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover SWIB overwrite.** Do not mint `/cmers/` or `/milwaukee-ers-explained/`. Do not treat combined **$6.46 billion** as the pension-only GASB book.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Combined total net position** **$6,458,509 thousand** at **31 December 2025** (2024 **$5,955,819 thousand**), up **$502,690 thousand** / **8.4 percent**. Pension-restricted fiduciary net position **$6,364,555 thousand** (Global Combined **$6,347,432 thousand** plus Non-Consenter **$17,123 thousand**). Employers’ Reserve / other governments **$93,954 thousand** is not pension fiduciary net position. Investments **$6,514,171 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Those prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover SWIB, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$8,185,181 thousand**. Net pension liability **$1,820,626 thousand**. Pension fiduciary net position / total pension liability **77.8 percent**. Do not treat the combined **$6,458,509 thousand** line as the pension-only GASB book.
3. **Returns.** Time-weighted return **12.9 percent** net of fees against an assumed **6.8 percent**, underperforming the benchmark by **112 basis points**. Money-weighted return **12.8 percent** net of investment expense. Those two official one-year prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Milwaukee ERS into [State of Wisconsin Investment Board](/wisconsin-investment-board-swib-explained/). SWIB is the state investment board file. This is **one Milwaukee city employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Milwaukee ERS AUM” has to name the book. The **$6,458,509 thousand** line is combined total net position. The **$6,364,555 thousand** line is pension-restricted fiduciary net position. The **$6,514,171 thousand** line is investments. Combined **$6.46 billion** is not the pension-only GASB book.
