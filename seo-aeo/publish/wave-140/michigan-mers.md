# Municipal Employees’ Retirement System of Michigan, explained

Municipal Employees’ Retirement System of Michigan is Michigan’s local-government pension, not leftover MPSERS, leftover Detroit Police and Fire, and not leftover Detroit GRS. The official [31 December 2025 MERS Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://assets.mersofmich.com/forms/AnnualFinancialReport_2025.pdf) prints Defined Benefit Plan fiduciary net position of **$14,870,864 thousand** at **31 December 2025**. Combined **$19,936,503 thousand** mixes defined benefit, defined contribution, healthcare, 457, IRA, retiree-health, and investment-services books. The notes say that Total column is not a GAAP plan net position. Money-weighted return was **15.43 percent** net on the Defined Benefit Plan. The house report says MERS does not have one funded status. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover MPSERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/mers/`, `/michigan-mers-explained/`, or `/mers-michigan/`. Statement prints are in thousands. Combined **$19,936,503 thousand** is not this card.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The Defined Benefit Plan column, the notes money-weighted line, and the funding note are the sourced lines:

1. **Defined Benefit Plan fiduciary net position** **$14,870,864 thousand** at **31 December 2025** (beginning **$13,028,271 thousand**). Investments at fair value **$14,777,322 thousand**. Defined Contribution **$1,873,271 thousand** is not this book. Health Care Savings Program **$741,634 thousand**, 457 Program **$614,191 thousand**, Individual Retirement Account **$29,686 thousand**, Retiree Health Funding Vehicle **$1,789,027 thousand**, and Investment Services Program **$17,830 thousand** are not this book. Combined **$19,936,503 thousand** is not a GAAP AUM. Do not treat leftover MPSERS **$94,365,768 thousand**, leftover Detroit Police and Fire, leftover Detroit GRS, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **Funded cards.** The house report says MERS is an agent multiple-employer system and does not have one funded status. Each municipality has its own. The **31 December 2024** actuarial print of about **$13.6 billion** actuarial assets and about **$12.4 billion** fair-value assets is not a 2025 GASB funded-ratio card. Do not invent a 2025 system-wide GASB ratio.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **15.43 percent** net of investment expenses for the Defined Benefit Plan for the year ended **31 December 2025**. The **6.93 percent** actuarial-return print is not that card.

Do not flatten MERS into [MPSERS](/michigan-mpsers/), [Police and Fire Retirement System of the City of Detroit](/detroit-police-fire/), [General Retirement System of the City of Detroit](/detroit-grs/), [City of Ann Arbor Employees’ Retirement System](/ann-arbor-employees/), or [City of St. Joseph Employees’ Retirement System](/st-joseph-employees/). MPSERS is the Michigan school file. Detroit Police and Fire and Detroit GRS are Detroit city files. Ann Arbor Employees and St. Joseph Employees are city files. This is **one Michigan local-government file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Michigan municipal pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$14,870,864 thousand** line is Defined Benefit Plan fiduciary net position. The **$14,777,322 thousand** line is Defined Benefit investments. The **$19,936,503 thousand** line is the non-GAAP combined total. The **15.43 percent** card is Defined Benefit money-weighted. Those are not the same mandate.
