# Metropolitan Employees’ Benefit Trust, explained

The Metropolitan Employees’ Benefit Trust is Metro Nashville’s open city pension, not leftover Tennessee Consolidated Retirement System and not leftover City of Knoxville Pension System. The official [30 June 2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.nashville.gov/sites/default/files/2025-12/2025_Annual_Comprehensive_Financial_Report.pdf) prints fiduciary net position of **$4,360,675,155** at **30 June 2025**. Closed County, City, and Education columns on the same combining statement are different legal books. Money-weighted return on the Metro Plan and Metro Education Plan together was **7.3 percent** net of investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **92.87 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover TCRS overwrite.** Do not mint `/mebs/`, `/nashville-metro/`, or `/nashville-employees-explained/`. Combined pension-and-other-employee-benefit trusts **$4,587,523,650** is not this book. Combined Metropolitan Employees’ Benefit Trust plus Teachers’ Retirement Plan investments **$4,726,884,275** is not fiduciary net position. Securities-lending collateral is not fiduciary net position. Later certificated teachers sit in leftover TCRS.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combining statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Restricted for pensions** **$4,360,675,155** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$4,166,503,638**). The change is **$194,171,517**. Cash and cash equivalents **$72,116,068** is not fiduciary net position. Securities-lending collateral **$36,156,365** is not fiduciary net position. Do not add closed Davidson County Employees’ Retirement **$1,796,828**, Employees’ Pension and Insurance **$8,074,026**, Teachers’ Retirement Plan **$200,258,549**, Closed City Plan **$13,381,097**, or Teachers’ Civil Service and Pension **$2,993,966** and call the sum a second AUM. Do not treat leftover TCRS **$74,910,040,763**, leftover City of Knoxville Pension System **$758,195,199**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$4,695,408,182**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **92.87 percent**. Net pension liability **$334,733,027**. Those official prints are not interchangeable with a later closed-plan card. Metro Education Plan GASB **115.29 percent** is not this book.
3. **Returns.** Notes and required-supplementary money-weighted return **7.3 percent** / **7.30 percent** net of investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025** on investments of the Metro Plan and Metro Education Plan. That combined print is not a Metro-only card. This filing does not print a separate official one-year time-weighted house card for the Metro Plan.

Do not flatten the Metropolitan Employees’ Benefit Trust into [TCRS](/tennessee-tcrs/), [City of Knoxville Pension System](/knoxville-pension/), [City of Memphis Retirement System](/memphis-retirement/), [Georgia ERS](/georgia-ers/), or [Georgia TRS](/georgia-trs/). TCRS is the later Metro teacher book. Knoxville and Memphis are different Tennessee city files. This is **one Metro Nashville open city file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Nashville pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$4,360,675,155** line is Metropolitan Employees’ Benefit Trust fiduciary net position. The **$4,587,523,650** line is combined pension-and-other-employee-benefit trusts. The **92.87 percent** card is the Metro Plan GASB ratio. The **7.3 percent** card is the combined Metro Plan and Metro Education Plan money-weighted return. Those are not the same mandate.
