# Oklahoma Public Employees Retirement System, explained

The Oklahoma Public Employees Retirement System is Oklahoma’s public-employee pension, not [Ohio OPERS](/ohio-opers/) and not leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion**. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.opers.ok.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2025_ACFR_OPERS-Final.pdf) prints Combined fiduciary net position of **$13,351,556,235** at **30 June 2025**. The pension plan is **$12,840,110,347**. The Health Insurance Subsidy Plan is **$511,445,888**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The time-weighted return was **12.49 percent** gross and **12.40 percent** net. The pension-plan money-weighted return was **12.58 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a second Ohio OPERS overwrite or a leftover Ohio PERS $46 billion overwrite.** The Ohio OPERS keeper is `/ohio-opers/`. Do not mint `/oklahoma-opers-explained/`, `/oklahoma-pers/`, or `/opers/`. Do not mint `/opers-explained/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The combining statement is the sourced split:

1. **Combined fiduciary net position** **$13,351,556,235** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$12,196,287,161**). That total covers the pension plan and the Health Insurance Subsidy Plan. Do not treat leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio STRS **$80 billion+** as this filing.
2. **Named books on the same report.** Pension-plan fiduciary net position **$12,840,110,347**. Health Insurance Subsidy Plan **$511,445,888**. Those two books are not interchangeable with the combined line. Do not add the Health Insurance Subsidy Plan to the pension plan a second time and call the sum a second AUM.
3. **Returns.** Time-weighted return **12.49 percent** gross of fees / **12.40 percent** net of fees. Pension-plan money-weighted return **12.58 percent** net. Health Insurance Subsidy Plan money-weighted return **8.18 percent** net. Those prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten Oklahoma OPERS into [Ohio OPERS](/ohio-opers/) or [Iowa IPERS](/iowa-ipers/). Ohio OPERS is Ohio. IPERS is Iowa. This is **one Oklahoma public-employee file**.
