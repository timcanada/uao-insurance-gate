# DB vs DC: IRS 2026 limits are $72,000 / $24,500 — not leftover 2025 $70,000 / $23,500

Who carries investment and longevity risk is a real design fact. The live IRS table is not. The live page still prints leftover **2025** US DC limits of **$70,000** and **$23,500** as if they were current. [IRS Notice 2025-67](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2025-49_IRB) moved those lines for **2026**.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## Official numbers (do not leave last year’s Code limits as current)

## What the page can say

- **The split.** A defined-benefit promise fixes the payout and leaves the sponsor with the risk. A defined-contribution pot fixes the contribution and leaves the saver with the risk.
- **How to print US limits.** Quote Notice **2025-67**. The leftover **$70,000 / $23,500** pair is last year’s pair.
- **What the split is not.** It is not a licence to leave 2025 Code limits as current, or to treat CRS **2021** as a 2026 census.

## What is not official

- IRS **$70,000 / $23,500** as the current 2026 limits.
- CRS **15%** as a 2026 private-sector DB-access rate.
- Any leftover GPIF dollar AUM.
