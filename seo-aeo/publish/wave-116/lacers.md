# Los Angeles City Employees’ Retirement System, explained

The Los Angeles City Employees’ Retirement System is the City of Los Angeles employee pension, not leftover LACERA. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.lacers.org/sites/main/files/file-attachments/lacers_fy25_acfr.pdf) prints fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits and postemployment health-care benefits of **$25,375,319 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. The Retirement Plan book was **$21,188,769 thousand**. The Postemployment Health Care Plan book was **$4,186,550 thousand**. Those three lines are not interchangeable. The money-weighted return was **11.5 percent** net of investment expenses. Retirement Plan fiduciary net position was **76.3 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover LACERA overwrite or a second CalPERS page.** Do not mint `/los-angeles-city-employees/`, `/lacers-explained/`, or `/la-city-employees/`. Do not mint `/lacera-explained/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits and postemployment health-care benefits** **$25,375,319 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$23,035,247 thousand**). Investments at fair value **$24,918,211 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. The transmittal portfolio card **$25.17 billion**, including cash and investments at fair value, is not fiduciary net position. Those three lines are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover LACERA, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, leftover SFERS, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books.** Retirement Plan **$21,188,769 thousand** (**83.5 percent** of the combined book). Postemployment Health Care Plan **$4,186,550 thousand** (**16.5 percent**). Those two lines are not interchangeable. Retirement Plan net pension liability **$6,535,584 thousand**. Postemployment Health Care Plan net OPEB asset **$318,317 thousand**.
3. **Returns and funded status.** Gross-of-fees portfolio return **11.16 percent**. Aggregate annual money-weighted return for the Retirement Plan and the Postemployment Health Care Plan, net of investment expenses, **11.5 percent**. Those two official one-year prints are not interchangeable. Retirement Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **76.3 percent**. Postemployment Health Care Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total OPEB liability **108.2 percent**. Combined actuarial-value funded ratio **78.4 percent**. Retirement Plan actuarial-value funded ratio **74.6 percent**. Those four funded cards are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten LACERS into [LACERA](/lacera/), [Los Angeles Fire and Police Pensions](/lafpp/), or [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/). LACERA is the Los Angeles **County** file. LAFPP is the Los Angeles **sworn fire-and-police** file. This is **one Los Angeles City civilian employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “LACERS AUM” has to name the book. The **$25,375,319 thousand** line is the combined fiduciary book. The **$21,188,769 thousand** line is the Retirement Plan. The **$4,186,550 thousand** line is the health-care plan. Those are not the same mandate.
