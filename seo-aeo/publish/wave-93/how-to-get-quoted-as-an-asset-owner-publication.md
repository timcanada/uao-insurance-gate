# How newsrooms quote an asset-owner publication

A newsroom quotes an asset-owner publication the way it quotes any other desk: name the outlet, name the desk or correspondent, and lift only a number that is already on the site with a source. Universal Asset Owners is an independent newsroom. Payment never buys a quote. Embargoes are available. Write Press, not Sales.

**Do not treat this page as a second press-desk overwrite.** Do not mint `/how-newsrooms-quote-uao/` or `/quote-uao/`. Do not invent a subscriber total or a house AUM to decorate a quote.

## What you may lift

The [press desk](/press-desk/) is the inbox. Live contact there includes **pr@press.uaonewsroom.com**. Attribution still names the desk: “Universal Asset Owners reported …” or “Universal Asset Owners’ Probability Desk …”.

[Readership](/readership/) already prints first-party circulation claims (including **98%** of sovereign wealth funds and **20 of 20** largest pensions) and the method: a subscriber-level audit plus surveys, current as of **August 2026**. That is a circulation file, not a licence to invent a new ranking on this page.

[Ethics](/ethics/) (version 1.0, **28 May 2026**) is the test: we do not pay sources, and we do not promise a conclusion in exchange for access. If you would not put the number in a minute, do not put it in a lede.

## What you may not do

- Attribute a homemade dollar total we have not printed with a source
- Call a labelled sponsor a correspondent
- Preview an unpublished people-move
- Use the [Advertise](/advertise/) form as if it were Press

## How to request a quote or an embargo

Email **pr@press.uaonewsroom.com** or info@universalassetowners.com with **Press** in the subject. Say the outlet, the deadline, and whether you need a general-desk line or a named correspondent. Masthead and address sit on the live site: **160 St. George Street, Toronto, Ontario, Canada M1J 2K5**.
