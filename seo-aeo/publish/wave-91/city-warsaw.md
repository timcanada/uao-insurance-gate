# Relocating to Warsaw for a PFR or Polish development-institution desk

Warsaw is the registered-office city of [Polski Fundusz Rozwoju](https://pfr.pl/kontakt). Official seat: **ul. Krucza 50, 00-025 Warsaw**. PFR S.A. was formed on **28 December 2012** as Polskie Inwestycje Rozwojowe and has used the PFR name since **13 April 2016**. The State Treasury is the majority shareholder. It is a development institution, **not** a savings sovereign wealth fund. A move here is often a move onto that public-development file. Right to work sits with official Polish sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit elsewhere

- PFR’s own stationery: a Polish development institution that invests on its own account and seeks a market rate of return over the long term. Official prints live on pfr.pl. Do not invent an AUM on this page. Do not mint `/pfr-explained/` or `/warsaw-pfr/`.
- Bank Gospodarstwa Krajowego is a **different** legal person and a minority PFR shareholder. Do not add the two books. Do not mint `/bgk-explained/`.
- [ÖBAG](/careers/cities/vienna/) is Vienna. [Keva](/careers/cities/helsinki/) is Helsinki. Do not treat a Warsaw postcode as a Nordic or Austrian book.

Do not treat this city page as a minted PFR explainer. There is no UAO PFR institution slug. Do not create one.

## Why people underestimate the job

A 2016 rename is not a new balance sheet. Polish is the language of legitimacy even when the dealing language is English. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail.
