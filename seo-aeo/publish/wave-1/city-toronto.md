# Relocating to Toronto for a Canadian pension-model role

Toronto is the headquarters city of the Canadian pension model people keep trying to copy: CPP Investments and Ontario Teachers' among them, with peers in Montreal and elsewhere. A move here is often a move into direct investing, governance, and a talent market that also runs London and New York books. Immigration is federal. Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Which owners sit here (and nearby)

- [CPP Investments](https://www.cppinvestments.com/) — Toronto HQ; global offices.
- [Ontario Teachers' Pension Plan](https://www.otpp.com/) — Toronto HQ; London and other offices.

PSP Investments is Montreal-centred. AIMCo is Alberta-centred. Do not tell a candidate “Toronto” if the desk is elsewhere. OMERS and others belong on the institution graph as they are profiled — link official sites, not rumours.

## Why the move has prestige — and friction

Canadian plans pay for people who can sit on both sides of a co-invest. Housing in Toronto is a real constraint. Cross-border hires should start at [Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship.html), not at a dinner conversation about “TN visas” that may not apply to them.
