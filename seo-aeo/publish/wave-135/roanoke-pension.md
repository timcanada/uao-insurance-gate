# City of Roanoke Pension Plan, explained

The City of Roanoke Pension Plan is Roanoke’s city pension, not leftover Virginia Retirement System. The official [30 June 2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.roanokeva.gov/DocumentCenter/View/23818/City-of-Roanoke-Pension-Plan-ACFR-2024-2025) prints net position restricted for pensions of **$498,374,571** at **30 June 2025**. Statement investments were **$495,999,498**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Time-weighted return was **10.8 percent**. Money-weighted return was **13.27 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **65.92 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Virginia Retirement System overwrite.** Do not mint `/crpp/` or `/roanoke-pension-explained/`. Statement investments **$495,999,498** are not fiduciary net position. The notes investment print **$496,156,811** includes Local Government Investment Pool **$157,313** that sits in cash on the statement. Time-weighted **10.8 percent** is not money-weighted **13.27 percent**. The investment-section fair-value about-**$497 million** card is rounded, not the statement. Portability agreements with leftover VRS and leftover Newport News are not a second AUM.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position restricted for pensions** **$498,374,571** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$473,057,654**). The change is **$25,316,917**. Statement investments **$495,999,498** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$498,933,251** is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover VRS **$124,041,778 thousand**, leftover Newport News Employees’ Retirement Fund **$1,150,643,142**, leftover Richmond Retirement System **$883,849,545**, leftover Norfolk Employees **$1,412,980 thousand**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$756,071,927**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **65.92 percent** (2024 **63.94 percent**). Net pension liability **$257,697,356**. Those official prints are not interchangeable with a later employer-only card.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **13.27 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **30 June 2025** (2024 **13.14 percent**). Transmittal, MD&A, and investment-section total-fund **10.8 percent** is not the money-weighted card.

Do not flatten the City of Roanoke Pension Plan into [Virginia Retirement System](/virginia-retirement-system/), [Newport News Employees’ Retirement Fund](/newport-news-employees/), [Fairfax PORS](/fairfax-pors/), [Richmond Retirement System](/richmond-retirement/), or [Norfolk Employees’ Retirement System](/norfolk-employees/). VRS is the statewide file. Newport News is a closed Virginia city file. This is **one Roanoke city file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Roanoke pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$498,374,571** line is net position restricted for pensions. The **$495,999,498** line is statement investments. The **65.92 percent** card is the GASB ratio. The **10.8 percent** card is the investment-section return. The **13.27 percent** card is money-weighted. Those are not the same mandate.
