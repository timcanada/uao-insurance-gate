# Ghana Heritage Fund: Act 815 (2011), not a 2019 founding

The Ghana Heritage Fund was established by section 10 of the Petroleum Revenue Management Act, **2011 (Act 815)**. The Bank of Ghana’s H1 2026 semi-annual report put the Heritage Fund’s closing book value at **US$1,460.98 million** at **30 June 2026**. The live page, dated 21 June 2026, still says the fund was **established in 2019** and “formally established by regulation in 2019.” That date is wrong. The statute is 2011. Allocations have been recorded since November 2011.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## What this is

The Heritage Fund is Ghana’s long-horizon petroleum savings sleeve. Section 10 of Act 815 created it to endow future generations after petroleum reserves are depleted. Section 9 created the Stabilisation Fund to cushion unanticipated petroleum-revenue shortfalls. Bank of Ghana manages both day to day. The Ghana Petroleum Wealth Fund in section 20 is a **later** consolidation that only happens after reserves are depleted. It is not a third current AUM.

The live page’s 70/30 “three stream” split and a 2019 regulation founding are not the Bank’s filing. Official BoG language: the Ghana Petroleum Funds receive **thirty percent** of petroleum revenue, split between GSF and GHF.

## What is not official

- **Established in 2019.** Live lede. The statute is **2011**.
- A homemade cedi or extra US-dollar conversion of the Bank’s US$ million prints.
- Treating GNPC or the Annual Budget Funding Amount as Heritage Fund AUM.
- A third current “Ghana Petroleum Wealth Fund” NAV. That sleeve is not open yet.
