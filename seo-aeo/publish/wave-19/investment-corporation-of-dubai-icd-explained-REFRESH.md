# Investment Corporation of Dubai (ICD), explained

The Investment Corporation of Dubai is the principal investment arm of the Government of Dubai. It was established on **3 May 2006** under **Law No. 11 of 2006** and is **wholly owned** by the Government. Its audited 2024 consolidated financial statements printed **total assets of AED 1,468,169 million at 31 December 2024**, revenue of **AED 349,636 million** and profit of **AED 67,539 million**. The live UAO page’s **“roughly $400 billion”** line is a homemade conversion, not that filing. This is not investment advice.

Total assets are **not** “AUM” in the savings-SWF sense. This is a holding-company consolidation of operating businesses, including bank assets. Do not call AED 1,468.169 billion a tracker AUM.

## How a peer should read it

ICD is Dubai’s commercial holding company — Law 11 of 2006, wholly owned by the Government, an IFRS consolidation. The score they audited is **AED 1,468.169 billion** of total assets at year-end 2024, **AED 349.636 billion** of revenue, and **AED 67.539 billion** of profit. Anyone still writing **$400 billion** is doing arithmetic, not reading the filing. Compare the job to [Temasek](/is-temasek-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/) or [ADQ](/adq-abu-dhabi/), not to a savings SWF that publishes a dollars-in-the-ground NAV.
