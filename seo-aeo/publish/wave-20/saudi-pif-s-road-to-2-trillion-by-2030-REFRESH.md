**$2 trillion by 2030 is a target. “More than US$900 billion” is the current official line.**

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

This page overwrites a live UAO stub that treated **$1.21 trillion** as end-2025 AUM, raised the 2030 target to **$2.67 trillion**, invented **$500 billion** of transfers, printed a **19.8 percent** 2023 return, and compared PIF to a homemade **$1.76 trillion** GPFG. Those lines are not PIF filings.

UAO’s institution page — [Public Investment Fund (PIF)](/public-investment-fund-pif/) — keeps the same current-language lock. This page exists only to stop the 2030 KPI from being rewritten as a 2025/2026 AUM.

## How to write the 2030 sentence

Write it as a **programme goal**: Saudi Arabia’s Vision 2030 conversation has included a $2 trillion PIF figure as a destination. Then print the fund’s own current clause. Do not add those two numbers into a “growth remaining” homemade gap unless PIF prints the gap.

Do not mint a 2031 twin. When the 2026–2030 strategy window ends, overwrite this URL.

## What is not official

- **$1.21 trillion** as end-2025 or current AUM.
- **$925 billion** as current AUM.
- **$2 trillion** or **$2.67 trillion** as current AUM.
- Homemade **$500 billion** state transfers.
- A **19.8 percent** 2023 return unless PIF reprints it on a dated page.
- A **$1.76 trillion** GPFG comparison (NBIM prints kroner; see the NBIM overwrite).
