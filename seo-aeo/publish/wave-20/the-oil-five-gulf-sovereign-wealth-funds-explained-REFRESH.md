**Five Gulf houses. Five disclosure regimes. No official combined pile.**

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

This page overwrites a live UAO stub (20 July 2026) whose meta claimed **$2.5T+**, whose body piled **$3.9T**, and whose FAQ assigned **ADIA $1.1T**, **PIF $1.21T**, **KIA $1.03T**, **QIA $557bn**, and **Mumtalakat $28bn**. None of those dollar piles is an official combined filing.

## Official table

| House | What it publishes | What it does not publish | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| ADIA (Abu Dhabi Investment Authority) | 20-year and 30-year annualised rates of return: **6.3%** and **7.1%** | AUM | ADIA public site / annual review language already locked on UAO |
| KIA (Kuwait Investment Authority) | Mandate and Santiago-principles membership | AUM | KIA public site |
| QIA (Qatar Investment Authority) | Mandate and public programme language | AUM | QIA public site |
| PIF (Public Investment Fund, Saudi Arabia) | Own 2026–2030 strategy language: **more than US$900 billion** | A current $1.21T / $925bn / $2T AUM print | [PIF 26 August 2026 board strategy release](https://www.pif.gov.sa/en/news-and-insights/press-releases/2026/chaired-by-hrh-crown-prince-pif-board-of-directors-approves-pif-2026-2030-strategy/) |
| Mumtalakat (Bahrain) | 2025 profit **BHD 592.7 million** on revenue **BHD 2.5 billion** | AUM | Mumtalakat 2025 annual report (Wave 11 lock) |

Do not add those five rows into a homemade US-dollar total.

## What "Oil Five" can mean without inventing a pile

The grouping is a journalistic cluster — five Gulf investment houses that sit on hydrocarbon-era balance sheets and sit in the same CIO conversation. That is a beat, not a filing. The honest page names each house, quotes the line each house actually prints, and stops.

PIF’s Vision 2030-era **$2 trillion** language is a **target**, not a current AUM. It belongs on the strategy page, not in a Gulf total.

## What is not official

- A **$2.5 trillion** or **$3.9 trillion** combined Gulf AUM.
- FAQ dollars: ADIA **$1.1T**, PIF **$1.21T**, KIA **$1.03T**, QIA **$557bn**, Mumtalakat **$28bn**.
- Treating PIF’s 2030 KPI as today’s size.
- Dollarizing Mumtalakat’s Bahraini-dinar profit into an AUM.
