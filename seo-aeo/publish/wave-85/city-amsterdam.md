# Relocating to Amsterdam for a Dutch pension-system desk

Amsterdam is the markets floor of the Dutch pension system. It is not automatically ABP’s home file. [ABP](/abp-netherlands-pension-fund-explained/) is the pension fund for government and education; [APG](https://www.apg.nl/) administers — APG is **not** a second AUM. [PFZW](/pfzw-netherlands-pension-fund-explained/) is a different house. A move here is often a move into a Dutch public-pension process, or into a window other owners staff. Right to work sits with official Dutch sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here

- [ABP](/abp-netherlands-pension-fund-explained/) — Stichting Pensioenfonds ABP. Read the official site and our keeper. Official prints are **euros**. Do not invent an APG client stack as ABP’s book. Ask where the board and the working teams actually sit — Heerlen is part of that file.
- [The Dutch pension system](/netherlands-pension-system-abp-pfzw/) is the franchise page. **Do not treat this city page as a second system explainer.**
- [PFZW](/pfzw-netherlands-pension-fund-explained/) is care and welfare. Do not mash it into ABP. Do not mint `/pggm/` or `/pfzw-netherlands/`.

Global owners staff Amsterdam as a *window* more often than as HQ. Ask whether you are building a book or staffing a time zone.

Do not invent a house AUM on this page. Do not euroize [GPFG](/norges-bank-investment-management-nbim/).

## Papers

Start at the [Immigration and Naturalisation Service (IND)](https://www.ind.nl/en). Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Why people underestimate the job

The 2027 scheme change is a process, not a branding exercise. Dutch is the language of legitimacy even when the dealing language is English. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail.
