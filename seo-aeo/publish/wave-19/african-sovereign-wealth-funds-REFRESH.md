# African sovereign wealth funds

Africa has several IFSWF-member sovereign wealth funds. UAO will only reprint houses with an official filing already locked: [NSIA](/nsia-nigeria-sovereign-investment-authority-explained/) (**$3,418.7 million** of total assets at **31 December 2025**), the [Pula Fund](/botswana-pula-fund-explained/) (**P30,818.706 million** on the Bank of Botswana’s **30 June 2026** foreign-assets line), [FSDEA](/angola-sovereign-fund-fsdea/) (**no current official AUM**), and [LIA](/libyan-investment-authority-explained/) (**no current official AUM**; founded **2006**). The live UAO page’s **$200 billion / 16 funds** pile is homemade. This is not investment advice.

**Last updated:** 26 August 2026 · **Desk:** UAO Research · **Canonical URL:** `/african-sovereign-wealth-funds/`

## What is not official — do not put it back

- **$200 billion / 16 funds.** Homemade pile.
- **$180–220 billion / 18 funds.** Same sin with a range.
- **$11 trillion** “global SWF assets.” Not a UAO filing.
- LIA **$66 billion** and a **2016** founding. Official founding is **2006**. Wave 18 already killed both.
- NSIA **$4.5 billion** or FAQ **~$7 billion**. Official YE2025 is **$3,418.7 million** of total assets.
- FSDEA **$4–5 billion** or **$5–8 billion**. No current official AUM.
- Pula Fund **$6.0 billion** and **50/30/20** sleeves. Official is **Pula** on the June 2026 statement.
- **PIC as a $190 billion African SWF.** PIC is a public pension manager, not a sovereign wealth fund. UAO has **no live PIC slug** (404). Do not mint one. Do not invent its AUM here.
- Morocco’s **FCP**, Ghana, Egypt, or any other house we have not locked from an official filing this wake.

## How a peer should read it

Africa is not a single pile. It is a set of **named legal persons** with **named statutes**. Some print a current size (NSIA in dollars; the Pula Fund in Pula). Some do not (FSDEA; LIA). The journalistic job is to keep those facts on their own lines. Anyone writing **$200 billion across 16 funds** is building a league table the funds did not file.
