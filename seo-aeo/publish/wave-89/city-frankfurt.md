# Relocating to Frankfurt for a German markets or public-pension desk

Frankfurt is Germany’s capital-markets floor. It is not VBL’s head office. The [Versorgungsanstalt des Bundes und der Länder](https://www.vbl.de/en/legal-notice-en) is an institution under public law at **Hans-Thoma-Straße 19, 76133 Karlsruhe**. It was [founded in Berlin on 26 February 1929](https://www.vbl.de/en/company) as the Zusatzversorgungsanstalt des Reichs und der Länder and moved to Karlsruhe in the early 1950s. A move here is often a move onto a markets window whose public-pension book sits in Karlsruhe — or into a satellite whose investment committee sits elsewhere. Right to work sits with official German sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit in Karlsruhe

- VBL is the supplementary pension institution for federal and state public-service employees. Official prints live on vbl.de. Do not invent an AUM on this page. Do not mint `/vbl-explained/`, `/frankfurt-pension/`, or `/careers/cities/karlsruhe/`.
- Supervision of VBL sits with the Federal Ministry of Finance. That is not a Frankfurt investment committee.
- Global owners staff this city as a *window* more often than as HQ. Ask whether the IC sits in Karlsruhe, Munich or Berlin. If it does, you are not at the centre.

Do not treat this city page as a minted VBL explainer. There is no UAO VBL institution slug. Do not create one.

## Why people underestimate the job

A trading-floor postcode is not the public supplementary book. German is the language of legitimacy even when the dealing language is English. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail. Satellites fail when the IC is elsewhere — the same problem our [London note](/careers/intelligence/why-london-satellites-fail/) already names.
