# Display advertising on Universal Asset Owners

Display advertising on Universal Asset Owners is labelled inventory on high-traffic public pages — explainers, hubs, directory, careers — aimed at millions of monthly views. It is a different product from Desk and Category sponsorship in the reviewed morning brief. We will not sell a fake CPM against a list we have not measured. When view counts are real, display is how a broader set of partners reach the ecosystem around universal owners.

## Two products, one firewall

**Premium (Lane B).** Newsletter strip, podcast end-board, featured directory/careers. Price is about the *room*, not the impression. See [Advertise](/advertise/).

**Display (Lane A).** Site-wide or hub-level units on pages that already rank. Price is about measured views. This page exists so that goal is explicit: the SEO/AEO machine is also a display machine.

Editorial pages do not change because a unit sold.

## What has to be true before we quote a CPM

1. Search Console and on-site analytics on the public hubs
2. A view count we will show a buyer
3. Brand-safe placements (no units inside the brief body)
4. A labelled creative policy

Until those exist, we sell Desk/Category/Platform and we *build* the traffic. We do not auction fiction.

## Who display is for

Partners who need reach across the ecosystem — not only the thirty CIOs on a premium list. Event hosts, data firms, professional-services networks, and technology vendors often want both: a Platform or Category contract *and* display on the explainers those clients already Google.
