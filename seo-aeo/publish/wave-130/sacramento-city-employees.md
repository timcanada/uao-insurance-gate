# Sacramento City Employees’ Retirement System, explained

Sacramento City Employees’ Retirement System is the City of Sacramento’s closed pre-1977 employee pension, not leftover Sacramento County Employees’ Retirement System, leftover CalPERS, leftover SDCERS, leftover LACERS, leftover Sonoma SCERA, leftover Imperial ICERS, leftover MercedCERA, and not leftover Seattle City Employees. The official [FY2025 annual financial report](https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/content/dam/portal/finance/Accounting/accounting-2025/Sacramento%20City%20Employees%27%20Retirement%20System%20-%20Annual%20Financial%20Report%20-%20FY%206.30.2025.pdf) prints net position restricted for pensions of **$253,354 thousand** at **30 June 2025**. Statement total investments were **$251,734 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. Money-weighted return was **10.33 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Fiduciary net position was **109.6 percent** of total pension liability. The City held a net pension asset. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Sacramento County overwrite or a leftover CalPERS overwrite.** Do not mint `/sacramento-city-scers/` or `/scers-city/`. Do not mint `/scers/`. The MD&A **$253.4 million** card is not the statement **$253,354 thousand** line. Current City employees sit in CalPERS.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Net position restricted for pensions** **$253,354 thousand** at **30 June 2025** (2024 **$253,497 thousand**). The change is a decrease of **$143 thousand**. Statement total investments **$251,734 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. The MD&A **$253.4 million** print is rounded, not the statement. The City contribution **$1,362 thousand** is not a second AUM. The System has been closed to new members since **1977**. As of **30 June 2025** the filing prints one active miscellaneous member and **710** plan participants. Do not treat leftover Sacramento County, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover Ohio PERS **$46 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$231,205 thousand**. City’s net pension **asset** **$22,149 thousand**. Fiduciary net position / total pension liability **109.6 percent** (2024 **101.6 percent**). The transmittal “approximately **$231.205 million**” total-pension-liability print is that thousands line in millions. The actuarial “approximate annual rate of return **12.3 percent**” card is not the money-weighted return.
3. **Returns.** Notes and required-supplementary money-weighted return **10.33 percent** for fiscal 2025, net of pension-plan investment expense. This filing does not print a separate official one-year time-weighted total-fund house card.

Do not flatten Sacramento City Employees’ Retirement System into [Sacramento County](/sacramento-scers/), [CalPERS](/calpers-explained-largest-us-pension-fund/), [SDCERS](/sdcers/), [LACERS](/lacers/), or [Sonoma SCERA](/sonoma-scera/). Sacramento County is the 1937-Act **county** file. Current City of Sacramento employees sit in CalPERS. `/scers/` stays unminted because Sacramento County and Seattle City Employees are different legal persons. This is **one closed Sacramento city file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “Sacramento SCERS AUM” has to name the legal person. The **$253,354 thousand** line is this city’s closed book. The **$14,577,459 thousand** line on [Sacramento County](/sacramento-scers/) is a different legal person. The **$251,734 thousand** line is statement investments. The **109.6 percent** card is the GASB ratio. The **10.33 percent** card is the money-weighted return. Those are not the same mandate.
