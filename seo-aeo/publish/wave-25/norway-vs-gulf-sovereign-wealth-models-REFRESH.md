# Norway vs Gulf SWF models: print kroner, not a $1.3 trillion GPFG

Norway’s Government Pension Fund Global is a kroner book. Norges Bank Investment Management put the fund’s value at **22,683 billion kroner** at the end of the first half of **2026** (**30 June 2026**). The live page, dated 13 July 2026, still leads with **$1.3 trillion AUM** and then assigns ADIA **$172 billion** and PIF **$925 billion**. Those dollars are not the filings.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

Print kroner for GPFG. Do **not** convert 22,683 billion kroner into a homemade US-dollar AUM on this URL. The model contrast is real. The dollar lede is not.

## What the models actually are

GPFG invests **only abroad** so the Norwegian economy does not overheat. NBIM executes; the Ministry of Finance sets the mandate. That separation is official. Gulf houses are not one model: [PIF](/public-investment-fund-pif/) is a Saudi public investment fund; [ADIA](/adia-abu-dhabi-investment-authority/) is an Abu Dhabi reserve investor that does not, as a matter of practice, invest in the UAE; [Mubadala](/mubadala-investment-company/) is a strategic / development investor that **does** print AUM (**AED 1.4 trillion / US$385 billion** at YE2025).

A “Gulf model” that needs ADIA **$172 billion** and PIF **$925 billion** to make the contrast is consultancy arithmetic.

## What is not official

- GPFG **$1.3 trillion** (or any other homemade dollar).
- ADIA AUM in any dollar.
- PIF **$925 billion** as current AUM. **$2 trillion** is a 2030 KPI, not a current filing.
