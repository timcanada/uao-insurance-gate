# San Joaquin County Employees’ Retirement Association, explained

The San Joaquin County Employees’ Retirement Association is San Joaquin County’s 1937-Act employee pension, not leftover LACERA, leftover OCERS, leftover SBCERA, or leftover SamCERA. The official [2025 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.sjcera.org/sites/default/files/2026-06/2025%20ACFR%20FINAL.pdf) prints fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits of **$5,286,842 thousand** at **31 December 2025**. Investments at fair value were **$5,047,120 thousand**. Those two lines are not interchangeable. The net-of-fees money-weighted return was **11.56 percent**. Fiduciary net position was **83.8 percent** of total pension liability. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover LACERA overwrite, a leftover OCERS overwrite, or a leftover SBCERA overwrite.** Do not mint `/san-joaquin-sjcera/`, `/sjcera-explained/`, or `/san-joaquin-county-employees/`. This book is a calendar year-end file, like OCERS, not a 30 June file.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line (in thousands):

1. **Fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits** **$5,286,842 thousand** at **31 December 2025** (2024 **$4,659,216 thousand**). Investments at fair value **$5,047,120 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Total assets **$5,346,568 thousand** is not fiduciary net position. Liabilities **$59,726 thousand**. The house **$5.3 billion** / **$5.287 billion** card is the fiduciary line rounded, not a second AUM. Those four prints are not interchangeable. Do not treat leftover LACERA, leftover OCERS, leftover SBCERA, leftover SamCERA, leftover CalPERS **$460 billion**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB funded cards.** Total pension liability **$6,312,524 thousand**. Net pension liability **$1,025,682 thousand**. Fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **83.8 percent**. The **31 December 2024** valuation (not the 2025 GASB card) prints market-value funded **76.5 percent** and actuarial-value funded **77.9 percent**. Those 2024 valuation cards are not 2025 funding prints. Those funded cards are not interchangeable.
3. **Returns.** Net-of-fees / money-weighted return **11.56 percent** against an assumed rate of **6.75 percent**. Three-, five-, and ten-year net returns **10.1 percent**, **7.3 percent**, and **7.7 percent**. Those official prints are not interchangeable.

Do not flatten SJCERA into [LACERA](/lacera/), [OCERS](/ocers/), [SBCERA](/sbcera/), [SBCERS](/sbcers/), [Sonoma SCERA](/sonoma-scera/), or [SamCERA](/samcera/). LACERA is the Los Angeles **County** file. OCERS is the Orange **County** file. SBCERA is the San Bernardino **County** file. SBCERS is the Santa Barbara **County** file. Sonoma SCERA is the Sonoma **County** file. SamCERA is the San Mateo **County** file. This is **one San Joaquin County employee file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “SJCERA AUM” has to name the book. The **$5,286,842 thousand** line is fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits. The **$5,047,120 thousand** line is investments at fair value. The **76.5 percent** card is a 2024 market-value funded print. Those are not the same mandate.
