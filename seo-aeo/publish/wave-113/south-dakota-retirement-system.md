# South Dakota Retirement System, explained

The South Dakota Retirement System is South Dakota’s statewide pension, not leftover Minnesota SBI and not leftover Iowa IPERS. The official [FY2025 financial statements](https://www.sd.gov/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=1ab2b4ee47f5f610701588ff336d43aa) print fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits of **$15,271,517,603** at **30 June 2025**. The money-weighted return was **5.44 percent** net. The time-weighted return was **5.45 percent** net. Those two return lines are not interchangeable. The Supplemental Retirement Plan and Special Pay Plan books are not the SDRS pension book. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover Minnesota SBI overwrite or a second Iowa IPERS page.** Do not mint `/sdrs/`, `/south-dakota-sdrs/`, or `/south-dakota-retirement-system-explained/`.

## What is official (30 June 2025)

The statement of fiduciary net position is the sourced line:

1. **Net position restricted for pension benefits** **$15,271,517,603** at **30 June 2025** (2024: **$14,922,309,487**). Investments at fair value were **$15,210,238,019**. That investment line is not fiduciary net position. Do not treat leftover Minnesota SBI Total Funds **$155,859.3 million**, leftover Iowa IPERS **$46,740,552,174**, or leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion** as this filing.
2. **Named books that are not the pension trust.** The Supplemental Retirement Plan (457) ended at **$827,341,151**. The Special Pay Plan (401(a)) ended at **$85,307,597**. Those two lines are not the SDRS pension book. Do not add them to the **$15,271,517,603** line and call the sum a second AUM.
3. **Returns, cash flows, and funded status.** Money-weighted return **5.44 percent** net of investment expenses. Time-weighted return **5.45 percent** net. Both prints sit below the assumed **6.50 percent**. Employee contributions **$176,641,822**. Employer contributions **$178,517,895**. Benefits **$761,846,186**. The MD&A’s “**$762 million**” and “**$355 million**” lines are those same books, rounded. Net pension asset **$8,505,183**. Fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **100.06 percent**. That funded-ratio line is not AUM.

Do not flatten SDRS into [Minnesota SBI](/minnesota-sbi/) or [Iowa IPERS](/iowa-ipers/). Minnesota SBI is a board book. Iowa IPERS is the Iowa public-employee file. This is **one South Dakota statewide pension file**.

## What an owner is actually buying

Counsel selling “South Dakota pension AUM” has to name the book. The **$15.27 billion** line is fiduciary net position restricted for pension benefits. The **$827.3 million** line is the 457 plan. The **$85.3 million** line is the 401(a) plan. Those are not the same mandate.
