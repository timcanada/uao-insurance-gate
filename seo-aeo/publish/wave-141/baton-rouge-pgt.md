# Baton Rouge Police Guarantee Trust, explained

The Police Guarantee Trust is Baton Rouge’s separate sworn-guarantee pension trust, not leftover CPERS Trust, leftover Louisiana TRSL, and not leftover LASERS. The official [31 December 2025 CPERS Annual Comprehensive Financial Report](https://www.brla.gov/DocumentCenter/View/24151/2025-ACFR-Final) prints Police Guarantee Trust fiduciary net position of **$28,572,231** at **31 December 2025**. Combined **$1,468,363,833** is not this card. Money-weighted return was **15.10 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense. Gross **15.32 percent** is not that card. GASB **59.83 percent** is not actuarial **54.9 percent**. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

**Do not treat this page as a leftover CPERS Trust overwrite.** Do not mint `/pgt/`, `/police-guarantee-trust/`, or `/baton-rouge-pgt-explained/`. The house report calls PGT a separate legal trust. Do not invent a combined Baton Rouge AUM as if it were this card.

## What is official (31 December 2025)

The Police Guarantee Trust column, the GASB 67 note, and the money-weighted schedule are the sourced lines:

1. **Police Guarantee Trust fiduciary net position** **$28,572,231** at **31 December 2025** (beginning **$23,663,100**). Trust investments **$28,566,939**. Combined **$1,468,363,833** mixes this book with leftover CPERS Trust **$1,439,791,602**. Do not treat leftover Louisiana TRSL **$29,334,227,630**, leftover LASERS **$17,223,211,406**, leftover MassPRIM **$73 billion**, or leftover NYSCRF **$226.8 billion** as this filing.
2. **GASB cards.** Total pension liability **$47,755,610**. Net pension liability **$19,183,379**. Plan fiduciary net position as a percentage of total pension liability **59.83 percent**. MD&A **59.8 percent** is the rounded card. Actuarial funding **54.9 percent** is not the GASB card. CPERS Trust GASB **67.13 percent** is not this card. Discount **5.75 percent** is not leftover CPERS Trust **7.00 percent**.
3. **Returns.** Notes money-weighted return **15.10 percent** net of pension-plan investment expense for the year ended **31 December 2025** (2024 **9.03 percent**). Transmittal gross **15.32 percent** is not the RSI card. CPERS Trust money-weighted **14.26 percent** is not this card.

Do not flatten the Police Guarantee Trust into [Baton Rouge City-Parish Employees’ Retirement System](/baton-rouge-employees/), [Louisiana TRSL](/louisiana-trsl/), or [LASERS](/louisiana-lasers/). CPERS Trust is the city-parish file. TRSL is the Louisiana educator file. LASERS is the Louisiana state-employee file. This is **one Baton Rouge sworn-guarantee file**.
