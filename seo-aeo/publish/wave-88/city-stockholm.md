# Relocating to Stockholm for a Swedish AP-fund desk

Stockholm is the city of the remaining Stockholm-based AP buffer funds. [AP3](https://www.ap3.se/en/kontakta-ap3) lists a visiting address at **Regeringsgatan 28**. [AP4](https://www.ap4.se/aktuellt/) lists **Jakobsbergsgatan 16**. [AP1 ceased at the end of 2025](https://www.ap4.se/en/about-ap4/about-ap1/); its assets and liabilities transferred to AP3 and AP4 as of **1 January 2026**. [AP2](https://ap2.se/en/contacts/) sits in **Gothenburg**. A move here is often a move onto a Swedish income-pension buffer file — not onto a fund that no longer exists. Right to work sits with official Swedish sources. This is Universal Asset Owners career intelligence, not advice.

## Which owners sit here — and which sit in Gothenburg

- AP3 and AP4 are buffer funds in the Swedish income pension system. Official prints live on ap3.se and ap4.se. Official prints are **kronor**. Do not invent an AUM on this page. Do not recycle leftover AP1 **SEK 446 billion**.
- AP1 is not a live house. Do not mint `/ap1-explained/` or `/stockholm-ap1/`. Do not treat a 2025 AP1 stock as a 2026 desk.
- AP2’s official visiting address is Kyrkogatan 48, Gothenburg. Do not mint `/careers/cities/gothenburg/`. If the desk is AP2, say Gothenburg in the brief.

The [Nordic pension model](/nordic-pension-model-explained/) is the franchise page. **Do not treat this city page as a second system explainer.** [ATP](/atp-denmark-pension-fund/) is Hillerød / Copenhagen, not here.

## Papers

Start at the [Swedish Migration Agency](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en.html). Universal Asset Owners does not give immigration advice.

## Why people underestimate the job

A buffer-fund merger is a statute, not a branding exercise. Swedish is the language of legitimacy even when the dealing language is English. Housing and a household that will not move are the ordinary reasons offers fail.
