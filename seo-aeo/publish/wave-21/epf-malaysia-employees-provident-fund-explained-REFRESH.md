# EPF Malaysia (Employees Provident Fund), explained

The Employees Provident Fund is Malaysia’s mandatory retirement-savings scheme. It was established in **1951** and now sits under the **Employees Provident Fund Act 1991 (Act 452)**. KWSP’s own 2025 dividend release prints investment assets of **RM1,409 billion at 31 December 2025**, up from **RM1,250 billion**. Membership was **18,088,387**, of which **10,588,648** were active. The 2025 dividend was **6.15%** on both Simpanan Konvensional and Simpanan Shariah. Live UAO copy that prints **RM950 billion / ~USD 190 billion / 15–17 million** members is stale. Do not dollarize the ringgit book. This is not investment advice.

## What is not official
