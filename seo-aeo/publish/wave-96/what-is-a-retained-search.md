# What is a retained search?

A retained search is an exclusive consulting assignment: the firm is engaged and paid to run the mandate from the first brief through onboarding, whether or not a hire closes next week. AESC member firms operate on a retained and exclusive basis. A contingent recruiter is paid if a résumé they sent is hired. Those are different contracts. This page is not a fee schedule and not a recommendation of any firm.

**Do not treat this page as a second CIO-mandate how-to.** That keeper is `/how-do-search-firms-work-for-cio-mandates/`. Do not mint `/retained-search-explained/`, `/what-is-retained-executive-search/`, or `/contingent-vs-retained-search/`. Do not invent a percentage of first-year cash.

## What AESC already prints

The Association of Executive Search and Leadership Consultants’ [Client Bill of Rights](https://www.aesc.org/standards/aesc-client-bill-of-rights/) (dated **15 October 2025**) says:

- Executive search firms are **specialized management consultants** who serve as talent and leadership advisors
- AESC member firms operate on a **retained and exclusive basis** for executive search
- They provide expert advice **from the beginning to the conclusion** of each assignment
- The engagement should be **in writing**, including fees and the payment schedule, guarantees, off-limits, and conflicts
- A member **will not undertake an assignment for which it is unqualified**
- Follow-through continues after the offer: onboarding and integration

That is the retained form. Exclusivity is the point: the candidate is not floated to a dozen desks at once.

A contingent process is the opposite commercial shape: many assignments at once, paid on a hire from a résumé presented. AESC’s own standards page is written for the retained side. Do not flatten them.

## What an owner is actually buying

A public-pension CIO search is a process the board can minute. A sovereign or strategic-fund search is often quieter. A family-office search is confidentiality first. The contract form can still be retained in all three rooms. The failure mode is a leak, a conflicted long list, or a firm that also places people at the managers you hire and never said so.
