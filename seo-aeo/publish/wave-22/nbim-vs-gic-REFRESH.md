**Kroner on one side. No AUM on the other. No homemade dollar rank.**

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

This page overwrites a live UAO compare (4 July 2026) that prints NBIM as **~$1.3 trillion** and GIC as **~$1 trillion** in the lede, then **$688 billion** in the body. Those dollars are not filings. The live page also uses the retired expansion “Government of Singapore Investment Corporation.” The legal name is **GIC Private Limited**.

## Official table

| House | Legal job | Official scale | Official return | Source / as-of |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NBIM / GPFG | Sovereign wealth fund. Not a member pension. | **22,683 billion kroner** | H1 2026 **9.4%** | [NBIM fund value](https://www.nbim.no/en/investments/the-funds-value/) at **30 June 2026** |
| GIC | Professional manager of Singapore government assets. Not the owner of them. | **Does not publish AUM** | 20-year real **3.4%** / USD nominal **5.6%** | [GIC Report 2025/26](https://www.gic.com.sg/uploads/2026/07/GIC-Report-2025-26-News-Release.pdf) to **31 March 2026** (released 24 July 2026) |

Print kroner next to “no AUM.” Do not invent a US-dollar winner.

## What the comparison can say without inventing a pile

NBIM invests a petroleum-era savings book for the Norwegian state. The Ministry of Finance sets the mandate. GIC invests a slice of Singapore’s reserves under the Ministry of Finance’s reserves framework, alongside [MAS](https://www.mas.gov.sg/) and [Temasek](/is-temasek-a-sovereign-wealth-fund/). Singapore’s MOF is explicit: GIC **manages**; it does not own the assets on its own balance sheet the way Temasek does.

A writer who assigns GIC **$688 billion** or **$1 trillion** so the two books can be ranked is not quoting GIC.

## What is not official

- NBIM as **$1.3 trillion**, **$2 trillion**, or any other homemade dollar AUM.
- GIC as **$688 billion**, **$796 billion**, **$875 billion**, or **$1 trillion**.
- “Government of Singapore Investment Corporation” as the current legal name.
- A homemade ethics-versus-opacity morality play that depends on those dollars.
