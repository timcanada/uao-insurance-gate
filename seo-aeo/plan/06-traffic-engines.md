# Traffic engines

Every engine has a job and a conversion. Vanity followers are not a job.

## 1. Classic search

- Defend franchise URLs (universal owner, named institutions, largest-lists).
- Grow graph layers (people, cities, firms, moves).
- Technical: Ghost already emits sitemaps and basic WebSite schema. Add Person/Organization/FAQ where templates ship. Fix tag duplication. Fast LCP on briefs (the flipbook repo is unrelated — do not confuse the two).
- International: one English site, global entities. Do not spin up thin translated doorway pages in Wave 1–2. If a market deserves a language, it deserves a correspondent.

## 2. Answer engines

See `05-aeo-citation-system.md`. This is discovery for people who never type a news domain.

## 3. Email (the real home)

The brief is complimentary and **reviewed**. That review is product, not friction — it keeps the room expensive.

SEO pages should use the same work-email module, same promise (“five minutes, five days”). Do not add a second consumer list. **Do** add a careers alert list (roles + moves) for the talent flywheel.

## 4. Audio and video

Two daily shows already exist. Use them as distribution for graph nodes:

- 60-second people-move at the end of the morning show.
- City/relocation as a Saturday conversation.
- Show notes must contain the canonical URL (this is how podcast apps and YouTube become search).

## 5. LinkedIn and X

- Named correspondents share *their* bylines.
- Brand account (`@universalowners`) posts the chart and the pressable number.
- No engagement-bait. No “agree?” stickers on a fiduciary topic.

## 6. Careers and alerts

Every tracked employer portal is a traffic source we already deep-link. Add:

- Indexable institution career pages (Wave 2).
- Verified individual roles when the desk can confirm them (empty state today — fill it).
- Labelled featured mandates as a revenue line.

## 7. Directory

Utility traffic: “family office lawyers Singapore,” “pension fiduciary counsel UK.” Those queries are how vendors find us *and* how owners find vendors. Label paid placements.

## 8. Public relations

One finding per week, embargoed if needed, to beat reporters who already write SWF/pension/family-office stories. Success is “Universal Asset Owners” in a dateline, not a press-release dump on BusinessWire.

## 9. Partnerships (earned, not paid links)

IFSWF, ILPA, CFA societies, university endowments forums, local pension associations. Exchange: we cover their public documents; they cite the Registry or UAO 100.

## 10. Events and private briefings

These feed Strategic Council / Sovereign, not SEO. Mention them; do not build 200 event microsites.

## Paid media (narrow)

Acceptable later: LinkedIn sponsored to *job titles* that match the brief, and recruitment products. Unacceptable: Google Ads on “best stocks,” tabloid native, Facebook lookalikes of retail investors.

## Measurement (do not invent)

Use Ghost + the analytics already on site. Add Search Console property coverage for new URL prefixes (`/directory/`, `/careers/cities/`). AEO panel is manual or a lightweight sheet until a vendor is chosen. Never fabricate session counts in a plan or a media kit.
