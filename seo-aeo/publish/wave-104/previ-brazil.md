# Previ (Brazil), explained

Previ (Caixa de Previdência dos Funcionários do Banco do Brasil) is Brazil’s largest closed complementary-pension entity, not a sovereign wealth fund and not a second Latin American AFP page. The official [2025 annual report](https://relatorio.previ.com.br/2025/) prints assets under management of the order of **R$ 302 billion**. Plano 1 closed 2025 with **R$ 258.2 billion** in patrimônio and a surplus of **R$ 12.5 billion**. Previ Futuro surpassed **R$ 42 billion**. The fund was founded in **1904** and is headquartered in Rio de Janeiro. Print reais. This page is not a ranking and not advice.

## What is official (Relatório Anual 2025)

Previ is an **entidade fechada de previdência complementar (EFPC)** for Banco do Brasil staff, Previ’s own staff, and their families — about **200 thousand** associates. The 2025 report and the official summary PDF are the sourced split:

1. **Assets under management** of the order of **R$ 302 billion** at year-end 2025. Plano 1 patrimônio **R$ 258.2 billion**; Previ Futuro **R$ 42.1 billion**; Previ Família **R$ 392.9 million**. Do not add those three as a homemade fourth AUM — the official total-assets line is the **R$ 302 billion** order.
2. **Plano 1** reversed the 2024 accumulated deficit and closed 2025 with a surplus of **R$ 12.5 billion**. The report records a **R$ 21 billion** equity-book divestment aligned with Plano 1’s shift toward a liability-matching portfolio.
3. **Founded 1904**, two decades before Brazil’s official social-security system. Official summary address: Praia de Botafogo, **501**, Rio de Janeiro. That is a Rio HQ, which is why this desk does not mint a São Paulo city career page for Previ.

Do not flatten Previ into [Chile’s multifondos](/how-do-latam-pension-afps-invest/) or [an Afore](/what-is-an-afore/). Those are different statutes.
