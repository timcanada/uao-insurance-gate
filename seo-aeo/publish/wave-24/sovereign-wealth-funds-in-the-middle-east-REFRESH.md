# Sovereign wealth funds in the Middle East: no official $3 trillion pile

There is no official combined AUM for Middle East sovereign wealth funds. The live page, dated 8 July 2026, still prints **combined AUM exceeding $3 trillion**, then assigns **ADIA $171 billion** (and a **2007** founding by merger), **PIF $925 billion**, and **KIA $716 billion** (and a **2003** founding). Those dollars and dates are wrong. ADIA was founded in **1976** and does **not** publish AUM. KIA traces to the **1953** Kuwait Investment Board and **Law No. 47 of 1982**. PIF’s official size line is **more than US$900 billion**.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

## What this page can say

The region’s large official houses are not one book. PIF is a Saudi public investment fund. ADIA is an Abu Dhabi reserve investor. KIA is Kuwait’s sovereign investment manager. [Mubadala](/mubadala-investment-company/) prints **AED 1.4 trillion / US$385 billion**. [QIA](/qatar-investment-authority-qia/) does not publish AUM. [Mumtalakat](/mumtalakat-bahrain-explained/) prints profit, not AUM.

A “Middle East $3 trillion” lede is a consultancy pile. So is a 2007 ADIA merger story and a 2003 KIA founding.

## What is not official

- **$3 trillion** combined.
- **ADIA $171 billion** / **$150–200 billion** / a **2007** founding.
- **PIF $925 billion** as current AUM, or **$500 billion NEOM** as an AUM line.
- **KIA $716 billion**, or a **2003** founding.
- **$15 billion** ADIA annual private-markets deployment as an official current AUM substitute.
