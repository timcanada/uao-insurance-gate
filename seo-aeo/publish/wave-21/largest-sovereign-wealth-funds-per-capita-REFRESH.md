**Per-capita SWF dollars are a writer’s ratio, not a filing.**

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

This page overwrites a live UAO list (8 July 2026) that leads with Norway at **$296,000 per capita**, GPFG at **$1.7 trillion** (December 2023), UAE at **$180,000**, ADIA at **$180 billion**, and GIC at **$170,000 per capita**. None of those per-citizen dollars is an official filing.

Twin `/sovereign-wealth-funds-per-capita/` still returns **200**. 301 that URL here after paste. Do not mint a 2027 year-stamp.

## Official table

| House | What it publishes | What it does **not** publish | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| GPFG / NBIM | **22,683 billion kroner** at **30 June 2026** | A US-dollar AUM, or a per-Norwegian dollar | [NBIM fund value](https://www.nbim.no/en/investments/the-funds-value/) |
| ADIA | 20-year **6.3%** / 30-year **7.1%** | AUM, therefore no official per-capita | ADIA public review language |
| GIC | 20-year real **3.4%** / USD nominal **5.6%** (24 Jul 2026) | AUM, therefore no official per-capita | GIC Report 2025/26 |
| PIF | **More than US$900 billion** | A per-Saudi dollar | PIF 2026–2030 strategy language |

A per-capita ratio that divides a homemade dollar by an OECD headcount is not a third official number. It is two unofficial steps.

## What a per-capita sentence can say

It can say that GPFG is large relative to Norway’s population **as a qualitative fiscal fact** — the fund exists because petroleum revenues were saved. It cannot assign **$296,000** to each resident unless NBIM prints that ratio.

It cannot add ADIA + Mubadala + ADQ + a pension authority into a “UAE SWF ecosystem” and divide. Wave 18 already killed the **$800–900 billion** Abu Dhabi pile. ADQ’s official print is **dirhams** (total assets AED 921,741 million at 31 December 2024), not a per-Emirati dollar.

## What is not official

- Norway **$296,000** per capita, or GPFG **$1.7 trillion** at December 2023.
- ADIA **$180 billion**, or any ADIA AUM.
- UAE **$180,000** per capita, or a consolidated UAE SWF pile.
- GIC **$170,000** per capita, or any GIC AUM.
- A 5.5 million population denominator presented as if NBIM printed it.
